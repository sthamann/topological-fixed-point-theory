# The first R113 source needed is the joint empty Gram

The Atlas and the main fundamental plan still put the primitive weighted
source upstream of the clock identification, physical family index,
local four-dimensional net and gravity. The native diagnostic-domain
run of `r113` returns `BLOCKED`, with no original blocked fibre packet,
physical weighted endpoint form, or bound absolute empty reference.
The later phase and current calculations do not supply those inputs.

## A direct reconstruction on the original charged two-window chart

Use the native R032 conditional charged84 transfer, the same colour
centre projector and the original frozen-sea Yukawa insertion. Fix
the equal window width `2/5`, momentum `1/4` and five positive Higgs
radii `1/4,3/10,7/20,2/5,11/20` on the same real field axis. These are
specified test coordinates. They are not a claim about the support of
the physical field measure.

Let `K0[i,j]` and `KY[i,j]` be the unmarked and late kernels on each
ordered pair of radii with the same free charged reference. The early
mark is `u[i]=r[i]^2`. For a joint mass matrix `M`, the weighted empty
Gram on the two sets of radius indicators is `Q0[i,j]=M[i,j] K0[i,j]`.
Every `K0[i,j]` is positive. Thus the single matrix `Q0` recovers `M`
entrywise in this class. In particular the mixed mark follows directly
as `sum Q0[i,j] u[i] KY[i,j]/K0[i,j]`. Neither the late mark nor the
mixed mark needs an independent fitted parameter once this joint
empty form has been evaluated. The reduction holds at fixed times,
reference and conditional fermion dynamics.

There is a direct unmarked call for every matrix entry if the original
source exposes independent quadratic insertions in the two windows.
Use `Q(m,n)` with insertion `exp(-8m U_early -8n U_late)` for `m,n=0..4`
and keep the same absolute reference. Then `Q=V Q0 V^T` with
`V[m,i]=exp(-8u[i])^m`. The five nodes are distinct, so Vandermonde
inversion gives the full Gram. Symmetry reduces this to 15 scalar calls
in the reflected class. The executable inverse recovers both witness
Grams and their mixed marks using only these unmarked inputs. This calls
for source-bound quadratic insertions, which the current installed
primitive caller does not evaluate.

## Why the diagonal data cannot substitute for that matrix

The audit constructs two symmetric, strictly positive mass matrices
with all of the following held fixed:

* The original charged kernels, fixed colour projection and reference.
* The whole bare and weighted one-window radial marginal on each side.
* `Z0`, `ZU` and `ZY`.
* Reflection positivity of the radius-indicator Gram at this cut.

The exact reason is that an off-diagonal mass variation on edge `(i,j)`
can be compensated at `(i,i)` and `(j,j)` to preserve the bare
marginals. On the resulting ten-dimensional edge space the five
weighted marginal constraints and one late-mark constraint have rank
six. Adjoining the mixed mark raises the rank to seven. Arb certifies
a nonzero minor on the native couplings. The signed maximal minors of
its first six rows give an exact null direction for the six constraints.
Adding and subtracting a small multiple yields the two mass matrices.
Both stay positive definite by the symmetric row norm bound relative
to the least eigenvalue of the common baseline `(I+11^T)/30`.

The reflected charged kernel `K0` is a Gram of the separate exterior
heat factors, with the scalar sea factors on their respective sides.
The common centre projector commutes with these heat factors, and the
ordinary trace on the selected sector is their Hilbert–Schmidt inner
product. The Schur product with either positive mass matrix preserves
positivity on the two-window indicator space. This does not assert
graded OS positivity for the entire physical R113 field algebra or
projective consistency at all physical cutoffs.

The two mixed marks differ by `9.34364762991164e-10` relative to the
fixed charged reference. Their connected marks differ by
`4.36847647916590e-6`. The analytic constraints agree exactly, with
the quoted gap certified by interval evaluation. Independently using
the full `Q0` entrywise reproduces each distinct mixed mark.

The direct source task therefore concerns the joint reflected empty
form, including its cross terms, on the first original block. A
one-window law, a single absolute vacuum scalar or the separate lower
marks leaves that task incomplete even in this restricted native
example. The original physical measure, selector and reference must
be carried by that evaluation. For the full field theory the charged
reduction also needs its physical dictionary and the other sectors.
