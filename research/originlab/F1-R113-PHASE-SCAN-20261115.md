# Relative Higgs phase reads the late R113 mark

This result concerns the charged 84 coordinate R032 two-window kernel,
with the original sea factor held fixed under the late Yukawa insertion.
Work in the same weak and charge chart at both windows. The early and
late Higgs fields lie on the fixed complex weak axis. Write their radii
as `a,b>0`, with relative phase `phi`. A scan rotates the late *fermion
transfer* by `theta` relative to the early transfer. It keeps the
underlying histories and weights fixed. Rotating both windows together
leaves the relative phase unchanged.

For each charged Yukawa singular block the determinant-one two-state
transfer has trace `A+B cos(phi−theta)`. This follows by writing its
Hamiltonian as the Pauli vector with third coordinate `p` and in-plane
length equal to the Yukawa singular value times the Higgs radius.
Taking the trace of the two heat matrices gives the dot product of
these vectors. The neutral lepton factors and the identity colour
sector use `A+2+B cos(phi−theta)`. The two nontrivial colour centre
sectors use `A−1+B cos(phi−theta)` on quark pairs after conjugate
particle and antiparticle factors are combined. Here `A≥2` and `B>0`.
The sea factor depends only on the radii. The same global colour
projector acts over all the retained momenta.

Thus with `N` distinct retained momenta the unmarked exterior kernel
is a polynomial of degree `L=42N` in the relative cosine. Each power
has a strictly positive coefficient. The Fourier coefficient `c_k`
is strictly positive for every `|k|≤L`, by expanding
`cos(x)^j=(e^(ix)+e^(−ix))^j/2^j`. The late marked kernel has Fourier
coefficients `y_k` in the same band. It is the derivative of the late
exterior heat factor with respect to logarithmic Yukawa amplitude,
times the original factor `−1/window_width`. The sea is undifferentiated.

At a fixed pair of radii, write `m_k=∫e^(ik phi) dmu(phi)`. A uniform
scan of the unmarked response `F_0(theta)` has Fourier data `c_k m_k`.
Exactly `2L+1` equally spaced values suffice for inversion. Keeping the
radii frozen, taking successively wider momentum cuts and supplying the
scan at each cut determines the whole angular measure. Trigonometric
polynomials are dense in the continuous functions on the circle.

The late mark can be reconstructed directly from the scan using
`y_k/c_k` as the Fourier multiplier on `F_0`. This avoids first
inverting the phase measure, an operation which is ill conditioned at
high harmonics. The Euclidean norm of the scan error is amplified in
the grid of late marks by at most `max_k |y_k/c_k|`, since the discrete
Fourier transform is unitary after a common normalization. At a single
off-grid phase the root-mean-square scan error may be amplified by
at most `sqrt(2L+1) max_k |y_k/c_k|`, by Cauchy–Schwarz.

At fixed early radius the early mark is a scalar multiple of `F_0`.
For the two-window convention with `U=a²`, multiplying the late
response by `a²` yields the mixed mark. With varying radius use the
phase scan resolved by radius and apply the multiplier before the
radial integral. A single scan already averaged over unknown radii
does not justify using the multiplier of one fixed radius. A common
reference factor passes through the linear operation.

The OriginLab `source_bridge` replays the two real orientations using
the existing native kernel. With native radii `7/20,11/20`, window
width `2/5` and momenta `1/4` and `−3/4`, the two cuts require 85 and
169 samples. Arb interval arithmetic certifies the inverses on a
declared test distribution and predicts a withheld late response
directly. The condition scales for the full angular inverse are about
`10^87.45` and `10^176.62`. The direct late-mark multipliers are bounded
above by 111 and 222, respectively.

For a positive angular measure, a shorter grid of `M=2K+1` phases also
gives a certified late mark. Every moment has modulus at most `m_0`.
Aliasing adds only coefficients above order `K`, each to one residue
class. The late mark error at any phase is bounded by
`2m_0(sum_(k>K)|y_k| + max_(k≤K)|y_k/c_k| sum_(k>K)c_k)`.
The measured grid mean bounds `m_0` from above after division by
`c_0−2 sum_(j≥1)c_(jM)` when that denominator is positive. For the
one and two momentum examples, 25 and 33 phases respectively give
error bounds below `4×10^−10` times the unmarked scan mean, before
any observation error. The replay verifies this bound at a withheld
angle using the complete kernel.

An independent 84 coordinate evaluation assembled the original Higgs
matrices at four nonreal relative phases and contracted the colour
projected exterior determinant and its matrix exponential derivative.
Both kernels agreed with the phase polynomial to relative error below
`3×10^−14` in this numerical check.

## Equal radii on the full96 carrier

With equal radii and equal window widths, there is also an algebraic
extension including the neutral Majorana block. Use the exterior heat
transfer `A` at the common radius and the weak phase action `W(theta)`.
The full96 Majorana term acts on weak singlets and commutes with this
action. The two windows therefore have exterior transfers `A` and
`W A W*`. Both preserve the colour centre sector. In a weight basis
for `W`, the coefficient of the unmarked trace at phase order `k` is a
sum of `|A_ij|²` over admitted pairs of the corresponding weight
difference. This follows directly by taking the trace of `A W A W*`
and using `A=A*`. Differentiating only the late transfer replaces one
factor of `A` in each summand by its derivative. Hence if the unmarked
coefficient vanishes, the marked coefficient vanishes too. The same
phase multiplier reconstructs the late mark on the nonzero harmonics.

The single-particle weights of the weak phase on the full96 carrier
are `+1` and `−1` each with multiplicity 24, and zero with multiplicity
48. On the exterior algebra this bounds the weight differences by
48 per momentum. Thus `96N+1` phases suffice at a cut with `N`
momenta. The native full96 builder independently reproduces this weight
count and the rotation of the heat matrix with the Majorana term present.
The sum-of-squares argument
uses the identical early and late heat factors and the centre
projected trace. It leaves unequal radii and the physical outer
selector in the full96 theory to be checked independently.

The scan requires a physically bound relative weak rotation and a
common original ensemble, selector and absolute empty reference.
The charged R032 model permits the conditional rotation used in the
calculation. The corresponding whole-source R113 operation, including
the neutral Majorana channels and physical regional transport, is open.
