Original class
Use the six anchored four-cycle permutation histories of
F1-HISTORY-GRAM-AND-ALL-D4-KMS-NO-GO-20261116, in a fixed order. The
seven invariant Hermitian coordinates satisfy three independent trace
preservation constraints. Their exact null space gives four directions
A,B,C,D. The center is I6/6. The family includes genuinely complex
interference. Every direction in the channel has zero output partial
trace and the center has output partial trace I4. Trace of the history
matrix K itself is not the trace preservation condition.

At integer denominator h, search K=(hI+aA+bB+cC+dD)/(6h) with all four
numerators between -h and h. The computed characteristic polynomial
of 6hK has scalar roots h-2a, h+a-b (double), h+a+b-2c and a quadratic
with trace 2h-a+3b+2c and determinant h*(h-a+3b+2c)-2*(a+c)^2-8d^2.
Hermiticity gives real roots. Nonnegative scalar roots, quadratic trace
and determinant are equivalent to PSD, including boundary points.
All checks run in integer arithmetic. Rank six of the history embedding
W makes K -> W K W* injective in the fixed marked basis. The four probe
slopes below are also independent. D4 acts trivially on this invariant
family. Counts refer to marked operators, with no assumed native
quotient of observational equivalence.

At denominator 2h the old grid is precisely the all-even quadruples.
The program reuses their results and only tests the added tuples. The
observation fingerprint binds the reuse and each round records the grid
and candidates. The continuous affine system is solved separately to
distinguish grid absence from an inconsistent or nonpositive solution.

Identifying responses
With zero-based row-major Choi coordinates and normalized
(a,b,c,d) divided by the search denominator, the four readings are:
  Re J[1,1] = (2-a-c)/6
  Im J[1,2] = d/3
  Re J[1,4] = (b-c)/6
  Re J[1,6] = (1-2a)/6
They recover a from the last, d from the second, c from the first and b
from the third. This gives four concrete mixed responses to look for
on a physically bound history block. A complete R113 binding requires
the same actual marked histories, weighted reflected Q0, outer P_adm
and absolute empty reference. The history matrices by themselves all
preserve the scalar and standard sectors and cannot select a unique
vacuum across them.
