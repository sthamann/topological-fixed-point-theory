# A minimal protected quantum memory as an origin candidate

## Principle and first free choice

Start from tensor product quantum systems with identical local Hilbert dimension
q, an isometric encoding of one logical q-level system, and protection against
one arbitrary unknown local error. Minimize the number n of physical sites and
require perfect packing: the orthogonal error sectors fill the physical Hilbert
space. These are explicit choices of task and admissible model class. They do
not assume a TFPT boundary kernel, five sites, a marked partition, four marks,
E8, or a numerical TFPT seed.

There are no fitted continuous constants. Quantum mechanics, tensor locality,
an isometric code, one logical subsystem, the error model, minimization, and
perfect packing are assumptions. The logical state, its physical preparation,
an action, a clock, a recovery rate, and the physical units are not determined.

## Exact derivation

One arbitrary local error requires code distance at least three, hence correction
of any two erasures. The quantum Singleton bound is

\[
n-k\ge 2(d-1).
\]

With k=1 and d>=3, n>=5. For this case, the same obstruction can be explained
directly: at n=4, two disjoint pairs could each reconstruct the unknown logical
state, contradicting no cloning. A concrete n=5 code exists, so the minimum is
attained. This lower bound is a theorem, not a finite search inference.

For a nondegenerate perfect one-error code,

\[
q^n=q\,[1+n(q^2-1)].
\]

At the independently obtained minimum n=5, the saturation equation becomes

\[
q^4-5q^2+4=(q^2-1)(q^2-4)=0.
\]

For an integer local Hilbert dimension q>=2, this forces q=2. Thus the two-state
alphabet follows from the stated combination of minimality and saturation.
Dropping saturation removes this selector: five-site one-error codes also exist
for larger alphabets. Saturation is a substantive assumption, not free physics.

An explicit representative is generated by XZZXI, IXZZX, XIXZZ, ZXIXZ. The
script checks rank four, commutation, and distance three. It constructs rational
logical codewords with 16 nonzero amplitudes +/-1/4 each and checks the full
Knill-Laflamme Gram condition for I and all 15 one-site Pauli errors. The 32
error images form an exact orthonormal basis of the physical 32-dimensional
Hilbert space.

The following are correlated consequences of this one code:

* five physical sites;
* sixteen error syndromes, not sixteen logical matter states;
* any three sites can recover the logical qubit;
* any two sites contain no information about that logical qubit;
* adjoining a logical reference gives an AME(6,2) state, verified by all twenty
  three-site reductions being I/8.

## A decisive obstruction to a complete P2 derivation

The constructed code and both logical basis states are invariant under a
cyclic permutation of the five sites. This C5 action is transitive. A subset
chosen canonically from the unmarked code must be invariant under every
automorphism. Therefore its size must be zero or five. There is no invariant
two-site or three-site subset.

The threshold statement “every three recover, every two are blind” is therefore
not TFPT's marked 3+2 partition. In the present symmetric model an intrinsic
marked 3+2 selector is impossible. A chosen subsystem, boundary condition,
measurement outcome, environment or order parameter would add the missing
marking, and its selection would still need derivation.

## Why the sixteen is not yet the SM half-spinor

The physical Hilbert space has even and odd computational parity sectors of
dimension sixteen. After a fermionic Fock identification, the even sector may
be written as Lambda-even C5. But that identification, fermionic grading and
the relevant Spin(10) action are extra structure. The protected code contains
only one state in the even sector and one in the odd sector: its logical
dimension is two. The sixteen syndromes label error alternatives.

An equality of these dimensions is not an intertwiner. Nothing here constructs
the SM hypercharge operator, its marked three versus two eigenvalue spaces,
the quotient gauge group, or three families. Jordan-Wigner transformations
also change the meaning of locality. The error locality initially postulated
cannot silently become fermionic interaction locality.

## Baseline

The finite comparison class consists of all binary Pauli seed words of a fixed
length n. Generate their stabilizer candidates by cyclic shifts, retain
commuting rank n-1 spans, and then test all one-site syndromes for uniqueness.
All words at the same n have the same literal word length. This is a structural
baseline, not a scan of decimal expressions or a probability distribution over
physical theories.

| n | All seeds | Commuting seeds | Commuting rank n-1 | Correcting seeds | Distinct subspaces |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 4 | 4 | 1 | 0 | 0 |
| 2 | 16 | 16 | 3 | 0 | 0 |
| 3 | 64 | 40 | 9 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | 256 | 160 | 48 | 0 | 0 |
| 5 | 1024 | 304 | 75 | 30 | 2 |
| 6 | 4096 | 1408 | 216 | 0 | 0 |

At n=5 the raw passing fraction is 30/1024=15/512. Conditional on commuting
rank-four candidates it is 30/75=2/5. The two passing stabilizer subspaces are
equivalent by a permutation of physical sites. This is uniqueness only inside
the enumerated cyclic single-seed family. The n=6 zero is only a property of
this restricted family; it does not exclude general six-site codes.

These fractions are not a significance level for TFPT, and conditioning on
the exact correction requirement makes correction unsurprising. The useful
result is the input-free derivation of the site count within an explicit
operational problem, alongside an exact identification of the missing
marking. The sixteen and recovery threshold must not be counted as independent
numerical confirmations.

## Verdict

KANDIDAT, stage 1, for an operational origin of a five-site structure. The
marked P2 carrier remains OPEN, with the symmetric selector in this model
ruled out. P1 is not generated: positivity of a Gram kernel would be easy,
but orientation, unit winding, normalization 1/(8*pi), a physical state, and
time do not follow. No claim about E8, gravity, the continuum or 3+1 follows.

Strongest objection: the five-site recovery threshold is unmarked, whereas
TFPT needs a specific marked partition with matter representation semantics.

Most informative next test: give an operational, automorphism-equivariant
construction of a quantum variable carrying the choice of a two-site subset,
and test whether a unique selected state supplies the required hypercharge
operator without adding a choice of cut. A deterministic invariant selector
on the unmodified symmetric code is already excluded and should not be
searched again.

## Additional exact bridge: the syndrome spread and its canonical A5 core

The three nonidentity single-site errors at each site have syndromes u, v,
u+v in F2^4. They are a projective line. The five sites thus give five disjoint
lines partitioning the fifteen nonzero syndrome vectors:

```text
{5,8,13}, {1,10,11}, {3,4,7}, {6,9,15}, {2,12,14}.
```

The labels are coordinates in the chosen stabilizer-generator basis; changing
that basis conjugates the configuration. `syndrome_spread.py` exhausts every
one of the 20,160 invertible linear maps of F2^4. Exactly 360 preserve the
five-line set. Their induced action on the five lines is the full S5, with
linewise kernel C3, verified by its element orders 1,3,3:

\[
1\longrightarrow C_3\longrightarrow G_{\rm spread}
\longrightarrow S_5\longrightarrow1.
\]

The code computes the derived subgroups without recognizing or presupposing
their names. Their orders are

\[
360\longrightarrow180\longrightarrow60\longrightarrow60.
\]

The terminal perfect subgroup has trivial intersection with the kernel and
its image is exactly the sixty even permutations of the five lines. Thus it
is canonically A5. No arbitrary selection of an A5 subgroup was necessary:
the perfect core is defined intrinsically by the derived series.

This is an exact algebraic connection worth retaining. However, the complete
syndrome incidence group is not automatically a physical code automorphism
group. Pure physical qubit permutations preserving the stabilizer have order
ten, with element-order counts 1 identity, 5 involutions, and 4 elements of
order five, the dihedral group D5. Both the syndrome S5/A5 actions and this
physical D5 action are transitive; none supplies a marked 3+2 partition.

One could next study the universal projective realization of this canonical
A5 and its binary central lift. That construction needs a physical reason,
an actual representation, and an invariant lattice/norm to connect to E8.
Neither the binary lift nor E8 has been constructed by these scripts. This
is not yet a second independent TFPT success and does not raise the stage.

## Decisive negative bridge: the sixteen syndromes are not the even Fock labels

A direct test of the tempting 16 = 1+10+5 identification fails. On the sixteen
syndrome vectors, both the full 360-element spread group and its canonical A5
core have orbit sizes **1+15**. On the even subsets of five spread lines, the
same induced A5 action has orbit sizes **1+10+5**, classified by subset size
zero, two, and four.

These are different four-dimensional representations over F2. In particular,
the canonical A5 core is the natural SL2(F4) action on F4^2 and is transitive
on its fifteen nonzero vectors; its action cannot be replaced by the deleted
permutation module of A5 without adding a different representation.

The script constructs the even-subset module in the basis e_i+e_4, i=0..3,
and solves every equation L G = D L over F2. The equations have rank sixteen
on sixteen matrix coefficients:

\[
\dim_{\mathbb F_2}\operatorname{Hom}_{A_5}
(V_{\rm syndrome},V_{\rm even\ subset})=0.
\]

Thus there is no nonzero linear intertwiner, and the orbit mismatch already
rules out any equivariant bijection of the underlying label sets.

There is also an obstruction over the complex numbers that survives arbitrary
unitary phases on a monomial syndrome basis. Let P be the five-place
permutation matrix for an element of A5. The trace on the even exterior space
is obtained exactly from the even coefficients of

\[
\det(I+tP)=\prod_{\text{cycles }c}(1-(-t)^{|c|}).
\]

The script checks every element of the canonical A5 core:

| Element order | Fixed syndrome labels | Trace on Lambda-even C5 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 16 | 16 |
| 2 | 4 | 0 |
| 3 | 1 | 4 |
| 5 | 1 | 1 |

At order three, a unitary monomial matrix with this syndrome permutation has
only one potentially nonzero diagonal entry. Its trace therefore has modulus
at most one, independent of the assigned phases. The desired even exterior
representation has trace four. A phase-adjusted monomial representation of
the natural syndrome permutation cannot be equivalent to Lambda-even C5.

This negative result is narrowly scoped to the natural syndrome action and
its monomial lifts. It does not exclude a different physical representation,
an enlarged Hilbert space, or a new construction of a fermion algebra. Those
would be new bridges that need to be supplied, not consequences of the shared
number sixteen. The passage from the canonical A5 to an E8 construction must
also respect this representation obstruction.
