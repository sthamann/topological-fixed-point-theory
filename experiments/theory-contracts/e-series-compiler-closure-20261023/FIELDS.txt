2. Actual Chevalley/Tits operators

Let e_alpha=E_alpha and f_alpha=eps(alpha,-alpha)E_-alpha so that
[e_alpha,f_alpha]=h_alpha. Define
  n_alpha=exp(ad e_alpha) exp(-ad f_alpha) exp(ad e_alpha).
In the level-one lattice realization this is the root SL2 Weyl operator.
Its exact action on every root vector is computed from the original epsilon.
For m=(alpha,lambda) and k=eps(alpha,-alpha), its phase is
  m=0:1;
  m=1:-k eps(-alpha,lambda);
  m=-1:eps(alpha,lambda);
  m=+/-2:-k.
Its lattice action is s_alpha. These formulas follow by expanding the
nilpotent root SL2 exponentials; for m=+/-1 they are the two-dimensional
SL2 string, and for m=+/-2 the adjoint string. In every case
  n_alpha^2 E_lambda=(-1)^((alpha,lambda)) E_lambda.

Because alpha and beta are orthogonal, their two root SL2s commute. Put
  T_alpha=n_alpha n_beta.
Then
  T_alpha^2=D_kalpha,
  kalpha(lambda)=(alpha+Jalpha,lambda) mod2.                 (2.1)
This character is independent of which root in the Gaussian line was
chosen: replacing alpha by Jalpha changes alpha+Jalpha by -2alpha,
which pairs evenly with every lambda in L. Sign reversal is similar.
The character is nonzero. For alpha=e0+e1 it is pairing with2e1, hence
is negative on all128 half-roots and positive on the112 integer roots.
The complete60-line replay finds exactly128 negative roots for each line.
Thus the raw Tits pair has order4 on V_L, although r has order2 on L.
This is the standard distinction between Weyl action and its actual lift.

3. Full dictionary to the original ordered lattice lift

Let S_r be the generator-normalized lift in the existing ordered basis:
it sends each E_bi to E_(r bi). If
  product_i E_bi^ni=c_B(n) E_lambda,
then
  S_r E_lambda=eta_r(lambda)E_(r lambda),
  eta_r(lambda)=c_(rB)(n)/c_B(n).
This is exactly the original ordered generator construction used by the
existing marked-monodromy and global G31 lift studies. No replacement
of the epsilon convention is made.

Two lifts of the same lattice isometry differ by a lattice character.
Consequently there is a unique input character chi_alpha such that
  T_alpha=S_r D_chi_alpha,                                (3.1)
where D_chi E_lambda=(-1)^chi(lambda)E_lambda. It is determined by the
eight generator phases. The script computes every chi_alpha and checks
equation(3.1) on all240 roots for all60 lines. Equality on the eight
generators, together with the common action on L and the group product,
proves the equality on EVERY lattice vector, not merely the root shell.

For an arbitrary specified original lift R_r=S_r D_a the exact matching
correction is T_alpha D_(chi_alpha+a). Thus the dictionary compares to
every originally specified lift, not an arbitrarily assumed unique one.
The coordinate masks depend on the ordered basis and oriented alpha;
they are explicit conventions, not conjugacy invariants.

4. Involutive lifts commuting with the fixed quarter lift

Keep the original generator-normalized lift Jhat of J. Correct on the
input side: R_psi=T_alpha D_psi. The involution equation is
  psi+psi composed r=kalpha.
The commutator with Jhat supplies the second explicit linear equation.
Both are equations in A=Hom(L,F2). For every60 lines the complete256
character census gives exactly8 solutions. Their differences are
A^<r,J>, of dimension3, so the solutions form a torsor for this fixed
character subgroup. All solutions and the actual generator masks are
stored in field-lift-results.json.

This does not split the global G31 extension. The previously proved
global obstruction remains: per-reflection choices cannot satisfy all
G31 group relations simultaneously. The original nontrivial extension
must be carried along when operations are composed.

5. Complete field and native4/5/20 diagram

A lattice lift acts by
  R[h1(-n1)...hk(-nk) E_lambda]
   =(rh1)(-n1)...(rhk)(-nk) phase(lambda) E_(r lambda).
The Heisenberg action and lattice-group action determine the full field
automorphism; the original cocycle identity preserves every vertex product.
The checker verifies all13680 nonzero root brackets per line (13440
root-sum brackets and240 opposite-root Cartan brackets). Higher lattice
and oscillator products follow from the displayed defining action.

All D_chi act trivially on the neutral Cartan Fock sector. Therefore
every matching correction preserves the actual native diagram:
  V4=the J-positive complex Cartan oscillator space;
  P5=the actual Pauli-fixed symmetric quartic space;
  W20=span(partial_a f_i), the actual neutral erasure derivatives.
The actions there are respectively r, Sym4(r) restricted to P5, and
Sym3(r) restricted to W20, with the given erasure map equivariant.
In particular the8 distinct corrected field lifts agree pointwise on
this neutral diagram while differing on charged sectors. This W20 is
not the distinct charged current20 that earlier work found mismatched.
