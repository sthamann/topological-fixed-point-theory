1. What the standard fermionization theorem requires
-----------------------------------------------------

Smith, Lin, Tachikawa and Zheng, "Classification of chiral fermionic CFTs of
central charge <= 16", arXiv:2303.16917v2, provide the relevant standard:

  * Section 2.1, equations (2.1) to (2.4): fermionization of a bosonic theory
    T_B requires a nonanomalous internal Z2 symmetry g, a spin-invertible
    factor Q with phase

      (-1)^q(a)=(-1)^[Arf(q+a)-Arf(q)],

    and diagonal Z2 gauging. The spin structure q is explicit data.

  * Section 4.2.1, equations (4.15) and (4.16): the E8 class represented by
    x=omega_7/2 is nonanomalous and has invariant algebra D8. The other
    conjugacy class has invariant algebra E7+A1 and is anomalous. The action
    on every vertex operator is

      Gamma_alpha -> exp(2 pi i (x,alpha)) Gamma_alpha. (3)

  * Section 5.1, equations (5.3) to (5.6): both untwisted and defect-inserted
    partition functions along the spatial and temporal cycles enter. The
    result is the spin theory of 16 Majorana-Weyl fermions.

  * Appendix A: the E8 lattice splits as D8 root lattice plus one spinor
    coset. The even sublattice is generated explicitly, and

      Z_E8=chi_1^D8+chi_S^D8.                           (4)

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16917

Equations (3) and (4) give the decisive comparison criterion. A TFPT Z2 must
have the same action on the charged root fields, not merely the same order,
the same number of labels, or the same abstract group name.

11.2 Why the two signs of the Pin- lift do not restore R

The two lifts r and -r have the same square,

  (-r)^2=r^2=-I,                                       (24)

so both give (23). More generally, the two Pin- structures on the Moebius
band differ by the nontrivial flat Z2 line on the base. Its pullback to the
orientable double is trivial: the generator of the double-cover core projects
to twice the base generator, and twice a Z2 holonomy is zero. Thus both Pin-
structures induce the same NS spin structure on the orientable double.

The flat-line twist used in the previous revision of this report was instead
the nontrivial flat Z2 line directly on the annular double. It changes the
cover spin structure from NS to R, but it does not descend from a change of
the Pin- lift on the Moebius base. Equivalently, it changes (22) from -I to
+I. It therefore preserves the thin A2/A3 data

  two sheets, Moebius base, wind(U_Gamma)=1,
  |SF|=1, Delta_Gamma=2 pi,

but it does not preserve A4. It is not a counterexample to the complete
A1--A4 package.

The corrected classification is consequently:
