CANONICAL SEAM FERMIONIZATION: LATTICE AND SOURCE FRAME
=======================================================

Question
--------

The marked compiler lattice is

  K=D5+A3 subset E8,
  E8/K=<g+K> isomorphic to Z4.

Its unique order-two subgroup determines one canonical intermediate even
lattice.  Is that marked intermediate lattice, in the already fixed O/M
dictionary and without another Weyl rotation, exactly the D8 charge lattice
of the eight folded components of the one CAR source?

Verdict
-------

SCOPED FALSE.

The order-two intermediate of the marked D5+A3 glue is exactly a D8.  The
folded CAR even lattice is also exactly a D8.  In the common target frame
fixed by the existing O and M, however, they are different embedded D8
sublattices of E8:

  canonical marked D8 root set:       112 roots,
  M(folded CAR D8) root set:          112 roots,
  intersection:                        48 roots,
  canonical only:                      64 roots,
  folded only:                         64 roots.

Their Cartan defect characters and their actions of the actual quarter-time
vector also differ.  Equality of Dynkin type, determinant and root count is
therefore insufficient in the simultaneously fixed O/M marking.

This is not a coordinate-invariant TFPT no-go.  A Weyl transformation is a
legitimate change of coordinates if it transports every marked datum at
once: the D5+A3 flag, the O frame, the time vector, the source embedding,
the field words and their cocycle/locality data.  What fails here is the
identity comparison after the two existing dictionaries O and M have both
been fixed.  Rotating only one D8 to align it with the other while leaving
the remaining data fixed would introduce a new dictionary.  No such fitted
Weyl rotation is made here.

Because this first equality gate fails, the proposed common identification
of the nonisotropic vector coset, the odd neighbour Z8 and the E8 defect
sector called v+c is not performed.  Those names belong to the discriminant
group of a specified D8 embedding and cannot be carried across these two
frames by identity.

Status split
------------

  * the unique order-two glue intermediate is the canonical target D8;
  * O maps the original Gaussian E8 roots bijectively to the fixed target
    E8 frame and maps that intermediate to the standard target D8;

  * this is the exact source preimage of the equal-energy target root line
    already recorded in FOLD-COVARIANCE-TO-NATIVE-FIELD-RESPONSE.txt.

  O(D8_halfway)=M(D8_CAR)

in the existing marked O/M dictionary.

CONDITIONAL ABSTRACT FACT, NOT A CROSS-FRAME IDENTIFICATION:

  For any fixed copy of D8, D8*/D8 has one nonisotropic vector class and
  two isotropic spinor classes.  Adjoining the vector class gives the odd
  unimodular neighbour Z8; adjoining either spinor class gives an E8
  neighbour.  This standard discriminant-form fact does not say which class
  of one embedded D8 is the named class of another embedded D8.

OPEN AND NOT TESTED AFTER THE FAILED GATE:

  * a primitive source reason selecting a common Weyl presentation in which
    the D5+A3 flag, O, M, time, source and fields are transported together,
    or selecting a new E8 automorphism which aligns the two D8s while the
    other markings are retained;
  * a common microscopic identification of the CAR odd vector coset with a
    canonical compiler spinor or defect sector;
  * a local odd-sector field construction.  The known global re-fielding

1. The canonical order-two intermediate
-----------------------------------------

Use the fixed standard target coordinates in which A3 is the usual D3 root
lattice on the last three coordinates.  Then

  K={z in Z8:
       sum_(i=0)^4 z_i is even,
       sum_(i=5)^7 z_i is even}.

Choose the marked Z4 glue generator

  g=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2).

It has order four modulo K: 2g=(1,...,1) is not in K, whereas
4g=(2,...,2) is in K.  A cyclic group of order four has one subgroup of
order two, namely <2g>.  Its intermediate lattice is

  L_2=K union (K+2g).

For an integral vector z, membership in K means that the first-five and
last-three coordinate sums are both even.  Membership in K+2g means that
both sums are odd.  Their union is therefore

  L_2={z in Z8: sum_i z_i is even}=D8.                    (1)

Equation (1) proves the full lattice identity.  It is not inferred from the
number of roots.

On roots, use doubled coordinates as in the original programs.  The marked
glue degree is

  deg(r)=sum_(i=0)^4 r_i mod 4.

The exact E8 root counts in degrees 0,1,2,3 are

  52,64,60,64.

The roots in the order-two subgroup are degrees 0 and 2.  Their total is
52+60=112, and their set is exactly

  R_can={+/-e_i +/-e_j: i<j},                             (2)

the standard D8 root set in physical target coordinates.

The original map in tfpt_explorer/cartan_source.py is

  O=T S^(-1)/sqrt(2),                                     (3)

where S is the original Gaussian simple-root matrix and T is the displayed
target simple-root matrix.  Exactly O^T O=I.  If r is an original norm-four
Gaussian root and n=S^(-1)r is its integral simple-root coordinate, then

  O(r/sqrt(2))=Tn/2.                                      (4)

The checker verifies that (4) maps all 240 original roots bijectively to the
fixed standard target E8 roots.  Applying the marked even glue grades gives
precisely (2).  Thus no unmarked classification is being substituted for the
actual O dictionary.

2. The folded CAR D8 in the same target frame
----------------------------------------------

The eight folded CAR charge components have integer charge lattice Z8.  Its
even part is

  D8_CAR={p in Z8: sum_i p_i is even},

with the same standard root set R_can in the folded component frame.

The existing fold dictionary does not identify that component frame with
the target axes.  It uses the fixed matrix M stored in

  v16-operator-pullback-20261014/fold-results.json.

M is orthogonal, has determinant one and permutes all 240 E8 roots.  The
actual folded even root set in the target frame is therefore

  R_fold=M R_can.                                         (5)

Exact set comparison gives

  |R_can|=|R_fold|=112,
  |R_can intersect R_fold|=48,
  |R_can minus R_fold|=|R_fold minus R_can|=64.            (6)

A smallest witness in the fixed coordinates is

  r_can=(-1,0,-1,0,0,0,0,0) in R_can minus R_fold.

Its M^T preimage is

  (-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,1/2,-1/2,1/2,-1/2),

an E8 spinor root and not a CAR D8 root.  Conversely,

  r_fold=(-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2)

lies in R_fold minus R_can, and

  M^T r_fold=(-1,-1,0,0,0,0,0,0)

is an actual CAR D8 root.  This is a direct charge-vector mismatch.  It is
stronger than comparing root-system types.

The earlier fold audit already stated the same structure in a coarser form:
the M-transported deck-fixed D8 contains 48 roots which are integer in the
old target frame and 64 which are spinor roots in that frame.  Equation (6)
now identifies those 48 as the exact intersection with the marked
order-two intermediate.

3. Cartan action
----------------

Both D8s have full rank, so both use the same eight-dimensional target
Cartan vector space.  This does not make their charged root actions equal.

The canonical marked D8 is the +1 root sector of

  h_can=e_0,
  sigma_can(E_r)=exp(2 pi i h_can.r) E_r.                 (7)

Integer D8 roots have phase +1 and the 128 spinor roots have phase -1.

The folded D8 transported by M is the +1 root sector of

  h_fold=M e_0=(1/2,1/2,0,0,0,1/2,1/2,0),
  sigma_fold(E_r)=exp(2 pi i h_fold.r) E_r.               (8)

Both (7) and (8) fix all eight Heisenberg Cartan currents, as every torus
character does.  On the 240 charged root currents their joint sign counts
are

  (sigma_can,sigma_fold)   root count
        (+1,+1)                48
        (+1,-1)                64
        (-1,+1)                64
        (-1,-1)                64.                        (9)

Thus the charged action differs on 128 roots.  The two character vectors
are not congruent modulo the self-dual E8 lattice.  Their difference has
mixed integral and half-integral coordinates and is not an E8 vector.

At the current level the actual dictionaries are correspondingly

  H_target=O H_Gaussian,
  H_target=M j_fold.

M is a half-integral Hadamard-type E8 automorphism, not a signed permutation
of the eight elementary CAR charges.  Replacing M by a new rotation which
makes (5) equal (2) would change the marked dictionary being tested.

3a. Coordinate equivalence versus a simultaneous marking
----------------------------------------------------------

Let W be an E8 Weyl automorphism.  If one replaces every target datum by its
W transport,

  R_can -> W R_can,       R_fold -> W R_fold,
  O -> W O,               M -> W M,
  d -> W d,

and transports the D5+A3 flag, charged field labels, cocycle choices and
local source inclusion by the same W, then all inner products, OPEs and time
pairings are unchanged.  This is a passive coordinate change.  In
particular, the statement R_can=R_fold is invariant under such a common
transport: if it is false before W, it remains false after W.

A different operation chooses W so that W R_fold=R_can while O, the marked
D5+A3 flag or d is kept at its old value.  That changes the relative
position of source and compiler data.  Such a map certainly exists here:
W=M^T sends R_fold=M R_can back to R_can when it is applied to the source
side alone.  This does not prove that the original O/M dictionary contains
that re-marking or that the source fields and time intertwine after it.  The
negative result in (6) is exactly this relative, simultaneously marked
statement.  It is independent of the common global coordinate convention
and it does not exclude TFPT as a whole.

4. The actual quarter-time vector
---------------------------------

In the folded CAR component frame the source shift is

  delta_(alpha,k)=(k+alpha)/8-1/2.

The existing dictionary transports it, without a fit, to

  d_alpha=M delta_alpha.                                  (10)

For the two actual quarters this gives

  alpha=1/4:
    delta=(-15,-11,-7,-3,1,5,9,13)/32,
    d=(-1/2,0,1/16,-1/2,0,-3/16,-1/4,-1/4);

  alpha=3/4:
    delta=(-13,-9,-5,-1,3,7,11,15)/32,
    d=(-1/2,0,3/16,-1/2,0,-1/16,-1/4,-1/4).              (11)

Orthogonality of M gives, for every CAR root beta,

  d_alpha.(M beta)=delta_alpha.beta.                      (12)

The checker verifies (12) root by root.  Hence the phase multiset on
R_fold is exactly the original folded-source time multiset.

The same d_alpha has a different phase multiset on R_can.  A short part of
the exact mod-one census, identical in these displayed multiplicities for
both quarter choices, is

  phase       on R_can       on R_fold
    0             10              0
    1/2           18              8
    1/4           17              8
    3/4           17              8
    3/8            0              8
    5/8            0              8.                     (13)

The full phase tables are emitted by the checker.  On the two witness roots,

  alpha        d.r_can        d.r_fold
   1/4            7/16           13/16
   3/4            5/16           11/16.                  (14)

Equations (11)-(14) rule out rescuing the equality by retaining only the
same eight-dimensional Cartan space or the same abstract D8 type.  The
actual source time sees the embedding difference.

There is also a precise zero-energy spinor line which must be retained in
any state/time comparison.  In the folded source coordinates set

  p=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2).             (15)

It is an E8 root: p^2=2 and it has four minus signs.  With
s=(1/2,...,1/2),

  p-s=(0,0,0,0,-1,-1,-1,-1) in D8,

so p lies in the standard even-parity spinor neighbour D8+s.  It is not an
integer CAR charge.  It is also not in the coset called c in
ODD-SECTOR-REFIELDING-AND-NAHT.txt, where c was deliberately represented as
m+s with sum m odd.  That file uses the other spinor convention.

For either alpha=1/4 or 3/4, the relation

  delta_(k+4)=delta_k+1/2

gives

  delta.p=(1/2)[sum_(k=0)^3 delta_k-sum_(k=4)^7 delta_k]
         =-1,
  p^2/2+delta.p=1-1=0.                                  (16)

The fixed dictionary M sends this vector to

  q=M p=(1,0,0,1,0,0,0,0)=e_1+e_4                     (17)

in one-based target notation.  By orthogonality and d=M delta,

  q^2/2+d.q=p^2/2+delta.p=0.                             (18)

The root q belongs to R_can but not to R_fold=M(D8_CAR).  Thus this same
vector simultaneously exhibits the frame distinction and the extra sector
introduced by extending the integer CAR charge lattice to E8.

Thus the M-transported zero-time-ground line is the lattice charge line

  C e^(e_1+e_4).

This is the same equal-energy target root primary already recorded in
FOLD-COVARIANCE-TO-NATIVE-FIELD-RESPONSE.txt, not a newly discovered
degeneracy.  Equation (15) supplies its exact source-spinor preimage.  The
bare folded CAR integer charge lattice does not contain p, whereas the full
E8 extension does.  Consequently an E8 extension adds a zero-energy charge
line at the actual quarter background.  A claim that the extended theory
has the same source state and time therefore needs an explicit sector or
ground-state selection rule.  This is a state/time compatibility condition,
not a rejection of the E8 extension.

5. Why the odd-sector continuation stops here
----------------------------------------------

For one fixed D8, write its four discriminant classes as

  0, v, c, v+c.

The quadratic form makes v the unique nonisotropic class and c,v+c the two
isotropic spinor classes.  The usual neighbour statements are

  D8 union (D8+v) is the odd unimodular lattice Z8,
  D8 union (D8+c) and D8 union (D8+v+c) are E8 neighbours.

This is the standard discriminant-form/overlattice correspondence.  Nikulin's
classification of integral symmetric bilinear forms supplies the general
theorem that even overlattices correspond to isotropic discriminant
subgroups.  Here equations (1) and (2) prove the required special case
directly.

The known ODD-SECTOR-REFIELDING-AND-NAHT result starts with the CAR D8 and
defines a global unitary which is the identity on that even module and is
parity-dependent on its odd vector coset.  It already proves that the pulled
back target Cartan observable is not local in the unchanged CAR inclusion.
The new comparison adds an earlier obstruction: the even module fixed by
that construction is M(D8_CAR), not the canonical compiler intermediate
O(D8_halfway).  Therefore its labels v,c,v+c cannot be read as canonical
compiler labels by identity.

An E8 Weyl automorphism can of course conjugate isomorphic D8 subsystems.
Existence of such a conjugacy is not the requested marked equality and does
not preserve the O/M dictionary automatically.  Selecting one from P1 or
from the original seam data remains open.
