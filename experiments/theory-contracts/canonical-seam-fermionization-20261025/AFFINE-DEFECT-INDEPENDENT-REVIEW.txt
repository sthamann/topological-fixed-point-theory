1. The four sector parent and the affine map
--------------------------------------------

The dual of D8 is the union

  D8*=D8 union(D8+v) union(D8+s) union(D8+c)
     =Z8 union(Z8+s).                                    (4)

The conformal quadratic form is

  q(0)=0,       q(v)=1/2,       q(s)=q(c)=1  modulo Z.    (5)

Consequently the direct sum of all irreducible V_D8 modules has an abelian
intertwining algebra whose abelian 3 cocycle is determined, up to cochain
gauge, by (5). This is the standard lattice module theorem. The nonintegral
pairings in D8* are exactly why the whole sum is not an ordinary local
lattice VOA.

The cyclic coordinate permutation R preserves D8 and each discriminant
class. Translation by e0 represents fusion with v. Hence (1) gives (3) and
is a bijection of D8*. On the finite energy basis

  oscillator descendant tensor |p>,       p in D8*,

Gamma(R) permutes all Heisenberg oscillators and the lattice label, while
T_e0 shifts the label. Once the cocycle phases have been chosen, both maps
are unitary and preserve the algebraic finite energy core. Their product is
therefore a unitary on its Hilbert completion.

On Z8=D8 union(D8+v), choose the ordered CAR cocycle and the already fixed
normalization. Then (2) is exactly the old source W1, including its Klein
signs. This is a restriction statement, not a reconstruction from matching
dimensions.

2. Exact Ward identity on all four sectors
------------------------------------------

Use the retained folded source energy

  K_alpha(p,N)=|p|^2/2+delta.p+N,
  delta_k=(k+alpha)/8-1/2,       k=0,...,7.               (6)

Take R(p_0,...,p_7)=(p_7,p_0,...,p_6). The oscillator level N is unchanged.
Direct expansion gives

  |Rp+e0|^2/2-|p|^2/2=p_7+1/2,

and

  delta.(Rp+e0-p)
    =(p_0+...+p_6)/8-7p_7/8+alpha/8-1/2.

Therefore, for every real p and hence for every p in D8*,

  K_alpha(A_1 p,N)-K_alpha(p,N)
     =(Q(p)+alpha)/8,       Q(p)=sum_k p_k.               (7)

Equation (7) proves the proposed Ward increment on the Ramond cosets as
well as the NS lattice. No new energy formula is fitted to those sectors;
it is the same quadratic conformal energy restricted to the four standard
D8 modules. Absolute Ramond ground energies are fixed by (5).

3. Delta(e0,z) is the corresponding inner twisted functor
----------------------------------------------------------

For a CFT type VOA V and semisimple h in V_1 with rational h(0) spectrum,
Li's operator is

  Delta(h,z)=z^(h(0)) exp(sum_(n>0) h(n)/(-n) (-z)^(-n)). (8)

Lam and Shimakura, Proposition 3.1, state that

  Y_M^(h)(a,z)=Y_M(Delta(h,z)a,z)                         (9)

turns a sigma twisted module M into a sigma_h sigma twisted module, where

  sigma_h=exp(-2 pi i h(0)).                              (10)

The zero mode and energy become

  x_0^(h)=x_0+<h,x>,
  L_0^(h)=L_0+h(0)+<h,h>/2.                              (11)

For V=V_E8 and h=e0, the automorphism in (10) is +1 on the D8 root fields
and -1 on the spinor root fields. Its unique twisted module is the shifted
lattice module

  V_(E8+e0)=H_v direct-sum H_c.                           (12)

The sign h=-e0 may be used instead if the opposite Delta convention is
chosen. Since the automorphism has order two, both signs give the same g,
although they reverse the displayed charge shift.

For a Heisenberg current x and an E8 charge alpha, equations (8) and (9)
give

  x(z) -> x(z)+<e0,x>/z,
  Y(e^alpha,z) -> z^(<e0,alpha>)Y(e^alpha,z).             (13)

Composing with Gamma(R) replaces x and alpha by Rx and Ralpha. This is the
field version of p -> Rp+e0. The coordinate characters e_i all define the
same E8 involution because e_i-e_j lies in D8 subset E8 and E8 is self dual.
Thus R normalizes this g and the composition remains the same g twisted
functor.

Equation (13) also gives the precise formal locality statement. On any
simply connected interval avoiding z=0, choose one branch of the fractional
power. The twisted Jacobi identity supplies the correct monodromy. If the
field has already been constructed as a smeared operator, multiplying its
test function by 1/z or the chosen analytic power does not enlarge support.
Constructing that smeared charged intertwiner, proving strong convergence
on a common core and obtaining a bounded conformal net defect still require
energy bounds and a defect localization theorem. The Delta proposition by
itself is a VOA module theorem.

This analytic distinction is substantive. Dong and Xu construct conformal

4. The decisive lift phase
--------------------------

Order the four sectors as (0,s,v,c). The geometric torus character is

  t_geo=exp(2 pi i e0.P)=(+,-,+,-).                      (14)

It is + on both integral sectors 0,v and - on both half integral sectors
s,c. Since W_aff pairs 0 with v and s with c, it commutes with t_geo.

The standard E8 defect lift is different. Smith, Lin, Tachikawa and Zheng,
equation (5.3), obtain

  Z[E8]^a=chi_0-chi_s,
  Z[E8]_a^a=chi_c-chi_v.                                 (15)

Thus their chosen lift on the four sectors is

  g_hat=(+,-,-,+).                                       (16)

It anticommutes with W_aff on both pairs in (3):

  g_hat W_aff=-W_aff g_hat.                              (17)

Equations (14) and (16) differ by the character

  d_defect=(+,+,-,-),                                    (18)

which is -1 on the complete g twisted space H_v+H_c. This is the defect or
quantum Z2 lift, not another torus character. Omitting (18) would incorrectly
identify the spatial NS/R sector sign with fermion parity.

The modular S and T convention fixes the relative v versus c sign in (15).
The fermionization formula additionally chooses the spin invertible theory
and the Arf sign. Stacking with Arf exchanges the two Ramond parity labels,
as stated after equation (5.5) of the primary paper. Hence this phase is
standard and consistent, but it is not derived from the original NS CAR
operator alone.

5. What fixes the R sector action of W_aff
------------------------------------------

The relevant intertwining spaces are one dimensional. Once the abelian 3
cocycle and one nonzero intertwiner in every fusion channel are normalized,
associativity and braided locality determine all products. Rescaling those
intertwiners by a normalized 2 cochain gives an equivalent abelian
intertwining algebra. Absolute phases of individual spinor vertex operators
are therefore gauge, while monodromy, conformal weight and the signs in a
fixed modular convention are invariant data.

The ordered CAR cocycle fixes W_aff on H_0+H_v. Extending the R lift while
preserving the full four sector algebra still permits multiplication by the
discriminant character that is + on 0,v and - on s,c. Equivalently, the two
extensions agree on the entire NS theory and differ by one sign on the
entire Ramond theory. If only a direct sum Hilbert space unitary were asked
for, an arbitrary common U(1) phase on the Ramond block would be possible;
compatibility with all intertwining products reduces it to this character
choice.

The standard defect convention (15), a chosen Arf theory and a chosen basis
of Ramond ground intertwiners select one representative. The source NS
winding, equation (7), and its neutral Cartan word do not distinguish the
two representatives. A claimed equality with one physical source operator
must therefore cite the original spin/defect lift or calculate the mixed NS
and Ramond cocycle explicitly.

7. Original source phase audit and exact gauge resolution
---------------------------------------------------------

This section corrects the provisional physical reading in lines 65--77 and
240--303 above.  The two Ramond signs are distinct after one fixes a complete
four-sector intertwining representative.  The reviewed originals do not fix
that complete representative.  With every field transported, the two signs
are isomorphic descriptions of the same two already fixed subtheories.

Write every charge uniquely as

  p=n+c0 s,        n in Z^8, c0 in {0,1},

and let Q(n)=sum_j n_j.  On the four sectors define the diagonal unitary

  U_tau |n+c0 s> = tau(n+c0 s)|n+c0 s>,
  tau(n+c0 s)=(-1)^(c0 Q(n)).                             (19)

Its sector signs in the order (0,v,s,c) are (+,+,+,-).  Hence U_tau is the
identity on both complete supplied local subextensions:

  H_0+H_v, the ordered-CAR NS theory;
  H_0+H_s, the even E8 lattice VOA.                        (20)

In particular it fixes the old W1 input data on integer charges, every E8
state, the E8 vacuum, and the spinor state e^s itself.

Let epsilon_* be the common four-sector cocycle chosen in
SAME-SOURCE-AFFINE-DEFECT.txt.  The basis change (19) gives

  epsilon_*'(p,q)
    =tau(p) tau(q) tau(p+q)^(-1) epsilon_*(p,q).            (21)

Equation (20) implies that (21) restricts EXACTLY to the same ordered-CAR
cocycle on Z^8 and to the same fixed E8 cocycle on D8 union(D8+s).  It changes
only the cross-sector intertwiner coefficients.  Thus those two restrictions
do not determine the full four-sector cocycle representative.

For the affine operator of the same-source construction,

  W_kappa |n+c0 s>
    =eta_R(n) kappa^c0 |Rn+e0+c0 s>,
  kappa in {+i,-i},                                       (22)

one has Q(Rn+e0)=Q(n)+1 and therefore

  tau(Rn+e0+c0 s)/tau(n+c0 s)=(-1)^c0,
  U_tau W_kappa U_tau^(-1)=W_(-kappa).                    (23)

This is the explicit equivalence that is invisible on both original local
subtheories.

The decisive mixed word can also be written exactly.  Put X=E_s for the
ground-state spinor charge shift in the chosen common charge algebra.  Its
relations are

  X E_e0 X^(-1)=i E_e0,
  Rhat X Rhat^(-1)=kappa X,
  W_kappa=E_e0 Rhat.

They give

  W_kappa X W_kappa^(-1) X^(-1)=(-i kappa) I.             (24)

The adjoint ordering W_kappa^* X W_kappa X^* has the same sign because the
right hand side is +1 or -1.  Thus, with the COMPLETE operator X held fixed,
kappa=+i and kappa=-i are distinguished by +I and -I.  Rephasing X by one
constant cannot alter (24).

This does not yet make (24) an original observable.  Under the full basis
transport (19), the spinor shift becomes

  X' = U_tau X U_tau^(-1),
  X'|n+c0 s> = (-1)^Q(n) X|n+c0 s>.                       (25)

It agrees with X on the E8 vacuum representation 0+s and changes sign only
on its action between v and c.  Conjugating every factor shows

  M(W_(-kappa),X')=U_tau M(W_kappa,X)U_tau^(-1).           (26)

Hence the scalar in (24) is unchanged under simultaneous transport.  The
apparent sign change occurs only if W is changed while the previously
unfixed v/c action of X is frozen by hand.  This proves the exact distinction:

  * fixing a full marked AIA, including X on v+c, makes the sign measurable;
  * fixing only the supplied CAR and E8 subtheories leaves the two signs
    related by (19)--(26), so there are not yet two physical sources.

For an actual vertex field Y_s(z), rather than the unitary charge translation
X on the algebraic core, the displayed star word additionally requires a
