1. Local net preservation is exact
-----------------------------------

Let

  B_a psi_n=(n+a)psi_n,       0<a<1,
  u=M_z,                      z(theta)=exp(i theta),

with sea covariance C=1_(-infinity,0)(B_a). For a proper interval I define
the CAR field algebra

  F(I)=C*{c(f),c(f)* : supp(f) subset I}.               (2)

The one-particle multiplier u and its inverse do not change support. Hence
the implementer W1, fixed by

for every I. This is a net-preserving automorphism. Equation (4) does not

Here A_0 is the total-charge-neutral observable net. It is smaller than the
fermion-parity-even D8 subnet used later in the sixteen-Majorana
fermionization. This distinction matters for time covariance.

2. Time cocycle and the generator anomaly
-----------------------------------------

Use

  tau_t(c(f))=c(exp(i t B_a)f).

The one-particle Ward relation is

  exp(i t B_a) u exp(-i t B_a)=exp(i t)u.               (6)

Since c(f) is anti-linear in f,

  tau_t Ad(W1)(c(f))
    =exp(-i t) Ad(W1) tau_t(c(f)).                      (7)

Let gamma_t be the gauge action gamma_t(c(f))=exp(-it)c(f). Then on the
full field net

  tau_t Ad(W1)=gamma_t Ad(W1) tau_t.                    (8)

On A_0, gamma_t is the identity, and consequently

  tau_t Ad(W1)|A_0=Ad(W1) tau_t|A_0.                    (9)

This proves the claimed time commutation on the neutral observer net. It is
false on the whole parity-even D8 field subnet: an even field can carry
nonzero charge, and then the phase in (8) need not cancel.

Let Q be the normally ordered charge and H the positive Fock generator with
H Omega=Q Omega=0. On the common finite-energy core the recovered source
relations are

  W1* Q W1=Q+I,
  W1* H W1=H+Q+a,
  [H,W1]=W1(Q+a).                                      (10)

Thus

  exp(i t H)W1 exp(-i t H)=W1 exp(i t(Q+a)).            (11)

The scalar exp(ita) and the adjoint action of exp(itQ) disappear on A_0,
which is the implementer form of (9). On charged sectors, Q+a is the exact
generator cocycle. It is the anomaly that must not be hidden by saying that
W1 commutes with time globally.

3. Current density, normal ordering, and the shifted state
----------------------------------------------------------

Changing to normal ordering relative to the shifted sea would recenter this
