1. Fixed inputs and exact object
--------------------------------

Let

  D8 = { n in Z^8 : Q(n)=sum_j n_j is even },
  s  = (1/2,...,1/2),
  v  = e0 + D8,
  c  = e0+s+D8.

Every charge in D8* has a unique expression

  p = n + c0 s,       n in Z^8, c0 in {0,1}.

The four D8 sectors are therefore

  0 : c0=0 and Q(n) even,
  v : c0=0 and Q(n) odd,
  s : c0=1 and Q(n) even,
  c : c0=1 and Q(n) odd.

The finite-energy core and its Hilbert completion are

  H_fin = direct_sum_(mu=0,v,s,c)
          M(1)_fin tensor C[D8+mu],

  H_*   = completion(H_fin).

Here M(1) is the rank-eight Heisenberg Fock representation with the standard
positive Hermitian form.  This is one source Fock system with four charge
sectors.  It is not an eight-copy construction.

The full sum H_* is the standard abelian intertwining algebra attached to
the discriminant form of D8.  It is not an ordinary lattice VOA because D8*
is not integral.  Its discriminant quadratic form is

  q(0)=0, q(v)=1/2, q(s)=q(c)=1 modulo Z.

This nontrivial quadratic form supplies the abelian 3-cocycle and the
fractional monodromy.  The two local subextensions used here are

  H_0+H_s, the bosonic E8 lattice VOA,
  H_0+H_v, the odd-lattice vertex superalgebra or complex CAR NS theory.

The E8 vacuum representation is the even extension

  V_E8 = H_0 direct_sum H_s.

The integer fermionic NS space and the Ramond space are

  H_NS = H_0 direct_sum H_v,
  H_R  = H_s direct_sum H_c.

The inputs R, e0, M and delta are the already fixed source fold, charge carry,
source-to-target dictionary and quarter-time vector.  No new Weyl rotation is
chosen here.


2. Exact common charge cocycle
------------------------------

On Z^8 retain the original ordered-CAR cocycle

  eps_Z(n,m) = (-1)^(sum_(i>j) n_i m_j).

Adjoin X=E_s to its charge algebra with relations

  X E_n X^(-1) = i^Q(n) E_n,
  X^2            = -E_1,

where 1=(1,...,1)=2s.  These relations are consistent because conjugation by
X^2 gives (-1)^Q(n), and conjugation by E_1 in the ordered-CAR charge algebra
gives the same sign.

Use the section

  E_(n+c0 s) = E_n X^c0.

Its normalized cocycle is

  eps_*(n+c0 s,m+d0 s)
    = i^(c0 Q(m)) eps_Z(n,m)                         if c0 d0=0,

  eps_*(n+s,m+s)
    = -i^Q(m) eps_Z(n,m) eps_Z(n+m,1).

Associativity of this charge multiplication follows directly from the
presented associative charge algebra.  The checker also verifies its
2-cocycle identity on a generating set and all generator products.  This
ordinary charge 2-cocycle does not trivialize the abelian 3-cocycle of the
four-sector intertwining algebra.  The latter remains fixed by q and is the
categorical source of the fractional field monodromy.

Two restrictions are exact, rather than merely cohomologous:

  (a) On Z^8, eps_* is eps_Z.

  (b) If Q(n) and Q(m) are even, eps_* equals the previously fixed E8
      cocycle

      eps_E8(n+c0 s,m+d0 s)
        = (-1)^(c0 Q(m)/2+c0 d0)
          eps_Z(n,m) eps_Z(n+m,c0 d0 1).

Thus the construction simultaneously retains the original CAR Klein factors
and the already fixed even E8 lattice cocycle.  Fields from the E8 spinor
coset and the odd integer vector coset are not thereby made mutually ordinary
local.  Their joint use belongs to the sector-changing or twisted
intertwining system described below.


3. The cyclic lift and its forced mu4 phase
-------------------------------------------

Let R be the cyclic permutation

  R(n0,n1,...,n7)=(n7,n0,...,n6).

The exact ordered-CAR phase is

  eta_R(n)=(-1)^(n7 sum_(j<7) n_j).

It obeys

  eta_R(n+m) eps_Z(n,m)
   = eta_R(n) eta_R(m) eps_Z(Rn,Rm).

To extend the lift to X one must set

  R_hat X R_hat^(-1) = kappa X.

Since eta_R(1)=-1 and X^2=-E_1, covariance of X^2 forces

  kappa^2=-1,
  kappa in {+i,-i}.

These are the two mu4 representatives of the cyclic lift on the Ramond
sectors.  They will be shown below to differ only by an intertwiner-basis
gauge.  They do not constitute an additional physical choice.  This basis
gauge must still be distinguished from the two genuinely different
operators obtained by choosing the geometric Cartan lift or the standard
g-defect lift, and from any finite compiler Arf word.

For either kappa define

  eta_*(n+c0 s)=eta_R(n) kappa^c0.

Then

  eta_*(p+q) eps_*(p,q)
   = eta_*(p) eta_*(q) eps_*(Rp,Rq)

for all p,q in D8*.  Hence R_hat is a unitary cocycle-compatible lift on the
whole four-sector charge algebra.

Define the diagonal basis change

  U_tau |n+c0 s> = (-1)^(c0 Q(n)) |n+c0 s>.

It is pointwise the identity on both already supplied local subtheories:

  * c0=0 on the whole CAR NS theory 0+v;
  * c0=1 and Q(n) even on the E8 spinor sector s, so it is also the identity
    on the whole E8 vacuum theory 0+s.

Since T1 changes Q(n) by one,

  U_tau W_kappa U_tau^* = W_(-kappa).

Thus kappa=+i and kappa=-i are isomorphic descriptions of the same extension
up to the normalization of the remaining v-to-c intertwiner.  In particular,
they cannot be counted as an independent physical phase after intertwiner
bases are quotiented out.

For the marked spinor intertwiner X=E_s, conjugation by U_tau changes the
sign only on the v<->c channel.  Therefore every mixed word is unchanged if
W and X are transported together.  If X is instead held fixed as an extra
marking, then

  W_kappa X W_kappa^* X^* = -i kappa I.

This scalar distinguishes the two representatives only relative to that
additional fixed action of X.  It is not an invariant of the unmarked
four-sector extension.


4. Explicit affine defect operator
----------------------------------

Define the affine charge map

  T1(p)=Rp+e0.

On the finite-energy basis set

  W_ext [ a1(-m1)...ar(-mr) |n+c0 s> ]

    = eta_R(n) kappa^c0
      (Ra1)(-m1)...(Rar)(-mr)
      |Rn+e0+c0 s>.

The translation factor eps_*(e0,Rp) is identically one in this chosen ordered
section, so this is exactly E_e0 R_hat, not an abbreviation which drops a
Klein phase.

The map of charge labels is bijective, R is orthogonal on every oscillator
mode, and all displayed coefficients have absolute value one.  W_ext is
therefore an isometry of H_fin with dense inverse, and extends uniquely to a
unitary on H_*.

For c0=0 the formula is precisely the old ordered-CAR W1 action.  In
particular it retains the actual CAR phase eta_R(n); the extension has not
replaced W1 by a charge-only permutation.

The sector action is

  0 <-> v,
  s <-> c.

Consequently

  0+s  -> v+c,     E8 vacuum representation to g-twisted representation,
  0+v  -> 0+v,     fermionic NS sum preserved,
  s+c  -> s+c.     Ramond sum preserved.

Eight iterations give the actual integer carry, including its cocycle action:

  W_ext^8 = E_1.

On a charge state |p>, the right hand side acts as

  E_1 |p> = eps_*(1,p) |p+1>.

This is an infinite-lattice statement.  It is not a replacement by a finite
eight-state register.


5. Exact charge and quarter-time Ward identities
------------------------------------------------

Let

  delta_k=(k+alpha)/8-1/2,
  K_delta=N+p^2/2+delta.p,
  H=8 K_delta,

with alpha=1/4 or alpha=3/4.  A direct polynomial calculation gives, for
every real p and hence for every integer or half-integer charge,

  K_delta(Rp+e0)-K_delta(p)=(Q(p)+alpha)/8.

Therefore on H_fin,

  W_ext^* Q W_ext = Q+1,
  W_ext^* K_delta W_ext = K_delta+(Q+alpha)/8,
  W_ext^* H W_ext = H+Q+alpha.

The half-charge sectors require no new energy prescription.  As the smallest
quarter-state check, for

  p_*=(1,1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1)/2

one has

  K_delta(p_*)=0,
  K_delta(T1 p_*)=alpha/8,

exactly as for p=0 and T1p=e0.  Thus the extra E8 half-charge zero-energy line
is transported by the same Ward law.  This calculation does not decide which
zero-energy line the physical state selects.


6. Vertex transport and the twist
----------------------------------

For beta=m+d0 s define

  lambda_kappa(beta)=eta_R(m) kappa^d0,
  C_*(u,v)=eps_*(u,v)/eps_*(v,u).

On the common algebraic domain, conjugation of the lattice exponential field
is

  W_ext Y(e^beta,z) W_ext^(-1)

    = lambda_kappa(beta) C_*(e0,R beta)
      z^(-(R beta).e0) Y(e^(R beta),z),

with the Heisenberg oscillators transported by R.  If gamma=R beta, the
monomial is exactly z^(-gamma.e0).

For D8 roots the exponent is integral.  For E8 spinor roots it is
half-integral.  The latter branch is the required sector change from 0+s to
v+c.  It is the lattice realization of the Li Delta(-e0,z) twist.  Reading
this formula as an ordinary local automorphism of V_E8 would erase the
half-integral monodromy and is false.

The construction is therefore a global defect or representation
intertwiner.  It is not itself a point-local vertex insertion.  On a simply
connected punctured chart the chosen branch has the standard formal twisted
locality.  If a smeared field has independently been constructed, multiplying
its test function by the displayed analytic monomial preserves support.
Li's formal Delta theorem alone does not prove strong convergence on a common
operator core or produce a bounded localized conformal-net defect.


7. Two different lifts of the same untwisted E8 character
---------------------------------------------------------

There are two relevant involutions on the four sector labels.

The direct Cartan lift is

  G_Cartan(n+c0 s)=(-1)^c0.

In sector order (0,v,s,c) its signs are

  (+,+,-,-).

It equals exp(2 pi i e0.p).  It is +I on the entire integer NS charge space
0+v and commutes with W_ext.  Therefore the original retained Cartan
four-word stays

  W1^* U_k W1 U_k^* = +I.

The standard spin-sector lift is

  G_std(n+c0 s)=(-1)^(Q(n)+c0).

Its signs are

  (+,-,-,+)

in sector order (0,v,s,c), equivalently (0+,s-,v-,c+) in the order often used
for the four D8 modules.  It anticommutes with W_ext, so its corresponding
four-word is -I.

This is the lift convention encoded by equation (5.3) of Smith, Lin,
Tachikawa and Zheng:

  Z[E8]^a   = chi_0-chi_s,
  Z[E8]_a^a = chi_c-chi_v.

The accompanying fermionization convention also fixes a spin invertible
theory and an Arf sign.  Those are extra defect-junction data; they are not
contained in the original NS winding operator.

Both lifts restrict to the same E8 g-character on the untwisted E8 vacuum
representation 0+s: sector 0 has sign + and sector s has sign -.  They differ
by the discriminant or Klein character

  chi_v=(+,-,+,-),

which is invisible on the choice of untwisted E8 character but changes the
lift on the twisted module v+c.  Hence the E8 character alone does not fix the
twisted-module phase.  The original Cartan word and the standard spin-sector
word are both mathematically valid, but they are different operators once W1
and the source Klein factors are fixed.

The following data must therefore remain typed separately:

  1. eta_R, the fixed ordered-CAR Klein lift;
  2. kappa=+i or -i, the two gauge-equivalent mu4 representatives of the
     cyclic R lift on X=E_s;
  3. chi_v, the Z2 choice of lift on the g-twisted E8 module;
  4. any finite compiler Gaussian or Arf word.

The kappa representatives are identified by U_tau, while no equality between
the remaining typed operators follows from the present construction.  In
particular the old Cartan +I word must not be replaced by the -I word of
G_std merely because both extend the same untwisted E8 character.

----------------------------------------

The source result is transported by the already fixed orthogonal dictionary

  D8_F = M(D8_CAR),
  R_F  = M R M^(-1),
  h_F  = M e0
       = (1/2,1/2,0,0,0,1/2,1/2,0),
  d    = M delta.

Then the actual target-frame affine map is

  q -> R_F q+h_F,

and the transported charge is

  Q_F(q)=(M 1).q.

For

  K_F(q)=N+q^2/2+d.q

one has identically

  K_F(R_F q+h_F)-K_F(q)=(Q_F(q)+alpha)/8.

All charge, oscillator, cocycle and sector words above transport together by

It does not assert

  D8_F = canonical compiler D8.

The canonical compiler D8 remains a distinct marked audit frame.  Its
previously proved fixed-frame inequality with D8_F is neither used nor turned
into a physical obstruction here.


9. What is closed and what remains open
---------------------------------------

Closed mathematically:

  A. A unitary W_ext exists on the full four-module D8 abelian intertwining
     algebra and its Hilbert completion.

  B. It restricts exactly to the old CAR W1 on NS states, including phases.

  C. Its eighth power, sector exchange, E8 twist, cocycle transport and Ward
     identities are exact.

  D. The construction lives in the actual fixed M frame and needs no global
     odd Phi map.

  E. The Cartan and standard spin-sector lifts and their four-word signs are
     explicitly separated, while the apparent kappa freedom is removed by
     the intertwiner-basis gauge U_tau.

  F. Consequently the finite-energy four-sector continuation in the actual
     M frame is unique up to intertwiner basis, with no remaining kappa
     choice in the unmarked mathematical object.

Still required for a physical TFPT source statement:

  1. Primitive P1 or the original seam data must select the four-module
     extension H_* and identify its local observable net with the physical
     source.  The present theorem starts once H_* is admitted.

  2. The physical construction must select the geometric Cartan operator or
     the standard defect lift on v+c if a compiler Gaussian or Arf word is to
     be compared with it.  NS data and the untwisted E8 character do not make
     those two operators equal.  No separate physical selection of kappa is
     required unless a particular v-to-c action of X is additionally marked
     and held fixed.

  3. A local defect field, if required, needs a localization theorem beyond
     this global finite-energy intertwiner.

  4. The physical state must decide how the additional E8 half-charge
     zero-energy line participates.  Ward covariance alone does not select a
     state.

  5. The earlier P1-to-QWZ and QWZ-to-this-specific-CAR-fold selection remains
     the upstream source question.  The present result proves the affine
     completion after that specific CAR fold has been selected.

Thus the smallest precise conclusion is:

  The full affine W1 defect is mathematically closed on the stipulated
  same-source four-module D8 system.  Physical P1 selection and the operational
  choice of geometric versus standard twist lift remain separate inputs.
