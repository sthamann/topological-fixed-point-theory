MOBIUS QUARTER OPERATOR DICTIONARY
===================================

Question
--------

Does the A4-selected NS spin structure on the orientable Moebius double
already produce the two native quarter domains alpha=1/4 and alpha=3/4,
without choosing a free boundary holonomy? How does the winding-one seam map
act on those domains, and is the original per-sheet factor d=2 already the
same operator index statement?

Verdict
-------

PROVED:

  The full Pin-minus Moebius monodromy is cJ, where J combines the
  transverse reflection with the pinor fibre action. Its two J-eigenspaces
  have longitudinal shifts 1/4 and 3/4. After passage to the orientable
  double and reduction to the longitudinal core, the NS cover operator
  decomposes as

    B_cov = 2 (B_1/4 direct-sum B_3/4).                 (1)

  The two quarters are the +i and -i characters of the A4 order-four lifted
  monodromy. They are not freely chosen holonomies. Changing the sign of the
  Pin lift exchanges their labels but leaves the unordered pair fixed.

PROVED:

  In a common periodic coefficient basis the winding-one cover multiplier is

                  [ 0       u_base ]
    u_cov  =      [                  ],                 (2)
                  [ I          0    ]

  so it exchanges the two quarter domains, while

    u_cov^2 = diag(u_base,u_base).                      (3)

  Consequently

    SF_cov(u_cov)=1,
    SF_cov(u_cov^2)=2,
    SF_1/4(u_base)=SF_3/4(u_base)=1.                    (4)

  Here u_cov is the multiplier on the seam of the orientable double. It
  exchanges the two reduced longitudinal quarter subspaces. On the full
  Moebius pinor space, scalar multiplication by u_cov changes c to -c at
  fixed J, so it is not by itself an endomorphism of one fixed descended Pin
  lift. This is the correct location for the original A3 operator, which is
  defined on the cut orientable double.

PROVED SOURCE CONSEQUENCE:

  A4 selects the full NS cover, equivalently the complete pair of quarter
  character sectors. It does not select one quarter sector as the primitive
  CAR channel. The primitive cover pair is (B_1/2,u_cov) with spectral flow
  one. Restricting to one quarter and using u_base instead describes a block
  of u_cov squared. Any identification of one quarter with the independent
  P2 quarter clock therefore still requires an explicit map.

SCOPED GAP:

  The original formula d SF=2 has exactly the same index value as either

    SF_cov(u_cov^2)=2

  or the sum of the two quarter indices. The original source does not state
  the operator map which makes one of these equalities its intended
  per-sheet construction. The quarter sectors are deck-character sectors,
  not literal position sheets. A deck-invariant sheet trace may be changed
  to this character basis, but u_cov itself is off diagonal there. Thus the
  value 2 matches exactly while the original operator attribution remains
  unstated.

1. Geometry and the two coordinates
-----------------------------------

Normalize the lifted core of the orientable double by

  theta in [0,2 pi],
  f(theta+2 pi)=-f(theta).                              (5)

The minus sign is the A4 result r^2=-I derived in
ORIGINAL-SPIN-AND-SEAM-DICTIONARY.txt. On this reduced longitudinal cover the
half-deck translation is

  (T_pi f)(theta)=f(theta+pi),
  T_pi=exp(i pi B_cov),
  B_cov=-i partial_theta.                               (6)

The coordinate on the base core is

  x=2 theta mod 2 pi.                                   (7)

For a fixed x, the two values theta=x/2 and theta=x/2+pi are the two cover
sheets. Diagonalizing the full A4 lifted monodromy gives the quarter
characters below. This character transform must be distinguished from the
two position sheets themselves. In the full Moebius pinor space the
half-deck operation also contains the transverse reflection and its pinor
fibre action; T_pi in (6) is the resulting longitudinal character operator,
not a replacement for that full action.

2. Full Pin-minus monodromy and the longitudinal reduction
------------------------------------------------------------

For the flat Moebius band, write the one-step deck transformation as

  (s,w) -> (s+1,-w).

On transverse pinor data let

  P phi(w)=phi(-w),
  J=sigma_1 P,
  K_j=ker(J-jI),                 j in {+1,-1}.          (F1)

For Pin-minus the two lifts have c=+i or c=-i, and an equivariant section
satisfies

  Psi(s+1,.)=c J Psi(s,.),
  Psi(s+2,.)=-Psi(s,.).                               (F2)

Thus the orientable double is NS. On K_j the one-step scalar phase is cj.
For c=+i this gives shift 1/4 on K_+ and shift 3/4 on K_-; for c=-i the two
assignments are exchanged. Hilbert-valued Fourier expansion and Parseval
give the orthogonal and complete decomposition. The same decomposition
holds on the geometric Sobolev domains because weak longitudinal and
transverse derivatives respect the two-sector coefficient characterization.

This is precisely the full version of the scalar coefficient model below.
Equations (10)--(16) suppress K_+ and K_- after their orthogonal projections;
they do not infer the phases from the fibre matrix sigma_1 alone. A fibre
eigenvalue determines a phase only after the transverse reflection parity is
included in J.

3. Hilbert spaces, domains, and unitary longitudinal decomposition
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Use normalized measures dtheta/(2 pi) and dx/(2 pi). Let

  H_cov = {longitudinal L2 sections satisfying (5)},
  Dom(B_cov) = H1 sections satisfying (5).              (8)

Let K=L2(S1_x) with periodic Sobolev domain H1_per, and define

  D=-i partial_x,
  B_a=D+a,                    a in {1/4,3/4}.            (9)

Define maps W_0,W_1:K -> H_cov by

  (W_0 g)(theta)=exp(i theta/2)  g(2 theta),
  (W_1 g)(theta)=exp(3 i theta/2)g(2 theta).             (10)

They obey the NS boundary condition because both phases change sign after
theta -> theta+2 pi. Their Fourier images are respectively

  W_0 exp(i m x) = exp(i(2m+1/2)theta),
  W_1 exp(i m x) = exp(i(2m+3/2)theta).                 (11)

These are the even-n and odd-n parts of the complete NS basis
exp(i(n+1/2)theta). They are orthogonal and their union is complete.

The norm is preserved without an extra square root:

  integral_0^2pi |g(2theta)|^2 dtheta/(2pi)
    = integral_0^2pi |g(x)|^2 dx/(2pi),                 (12)

because x=2theta traverses the base circle twice and the factor from
dx=2dtheta cancels that multiplicity. Hence

  W=W_0 direct-sum W_1

is unitary from K direct-sum K onto H_cov, including the stated H1 domains.
Tensoring the two summands with the corresponding K_j coefficient spaces in
(F1) gives the full Fourier and Sobolev decomposition just described.

Differentiating (10) gives

  B_cov W_0 = 2 W_0 B_1/4,
  B_cov W_1 = 2 W_1 B_3/4,                              (13)

which proves (1) as an unbounded-operator identity with domains.

The equivalent quasiperiodic-domain presentation uses

  H_a={h:h(x+2pi)=exp(2pi i a)h(x)},
  Q_a=-i partial_x on H1_a.                             (14)

Multiplication G_a g=exp(i a x)g is unitary K -> H_a and

  Q_a G_a = G_a B_a.                                   (15)

Thus the two summands in (1) are exactly the alpha=1/4 and alpha=3/4
domains, with boundary phases +i and -i. No alpha was fitted or added.

4. Half-deck action
-------------------

Equations (10) and periodicity of g give

  T_pi W_0=+i W_0,
  T_pi W_1=-i W_1.                                     (16)

Therefore T_pi^2=-I on both sectors, exactly the A4 order-four lift. The two
quarter domains are its complete reduced longitudinal character
decomposition. In the full geometry these are the cj characters of cJ in
(F2). The signs +i and -i belong to one selected Pin-minus lift; they are not
two competing spin structures.

5. Winding-one action
---------------------

Let

  (u_cov f)(theta)=exp(i theta)f(theta),
  (u_base g)(x)=exp(i x)g(x).                           (17)

Both preserve the corresponding Sobolev domains. Direct substitution in
(10) gives

  u_cov W_0 g = W_1 g,
  u_cov W_1 g = W_0(u_base g).                          (18)

In the ordered coefficient basis (1/4,3/4), this is exactly (2). Squaring
gives (3).

In the quasiperiodic gauge (14), the off-diagonal arrows carry multiplication
by exp(i x/2). Formula (2) is the same operation after both sectors are
written in their common periodic coefficient basis. Omitting this gauge
distinction would incorrectly replace a domain-changing map by an ordinary
periodic multiplier.

The scalar multiplier u_cov is defined on the orientable-double seam. Under
the one-step Moebius relation it changes c to -c while leaving J fixed. Thus
it exchanges the two reduced longitudinal quarter subspaces in (18), but it
does not preserve one fixed descended Pin lift by itself. A full descended
endomorphism with this longitudinal factor would also need a specified
operator anticommuting with J. No such extra operator is needed for the
present A3 comparison because the original seam multiplier is stated after
cutting the orientable double. This distinction keeps the full monodromy cJ
separate from its reduced longitudinal coefficient action.

6. Spectral flow and the non-double-counting rule
-------------------------------------------------

The NS cover spectrum is

  spec(B_cov)={n+1/2:n in Z}.                           (19)

The path implementing u_cov is B_cov(t)=B_cov+t,
0<=t<=1. Only n=-1 crosses zero, at t=1/2, so

  SF_cov(u_cov)=1.                                      (20)

This is the half-shifted correction of the A3 family. It retains the original
winding-one spectral flow exactly.

The square implements B_cov(t)=B_cov+2t. The modes n=-1 and n=-2 cross, so

  SF_cov(u_cov^2)=2.                                    (21)

By (3), the same index is the direct sum of the two base-quarter paths
B_a(t)=B_a+t:

  SF_1/4(u_base)+SF_3/4(u_base)=1+1=2.                 (22)

Equations (20)--(22) impose the required bookkeeping rule:

  * u_cov is a full-cover winding-one operator and has SF=1;
  * it exchanges the quarter sectors, so it is not a within-sector winding;
  * u_base has SF=1 on each individual quarter domain;
  * the sum of those two indices describes u_cov squared, not u_cov.

Therefore one must not identify the full-cover SF=1 of u_cov with two
simultaneous within-quarter SF=1 contributions. That would count the cover
degree twice.

7. Comparison with the original per-sheet factor d=2
-----------------------------------------------------

The later original source states

  J=d J_1,
  J_1=SF,
  d=2,
  SF=1,                                                 (23)

under the premise of identical sheet contributions. It also proves the
separate integral identity for a deck-invariant source,

  integral_(M_tilde) J = d integral_M J.                (24)

That passage does not restrict its U_Gamma to one position sheet. Conversely,
the d=2 lemma calls SF the per-sheet normalization but does not define a
per-sheet boundary operator or the Fourier transform (10).

There is a precise route to equality. A deck-invariant local source which is
the same on both position sheets has a two-dimensional sheet trace. Changing
from the position-sheet basis to the T_pi character basis is unitary, so its
trace becomes the sum of the 1/4 and 3/4 blocks. If its seam insertion is
u_cov^2, equation (22) is exactly the original d=2 sum.

What is not yet in the source is the premise in the last sentence: the
original response functional is not identified with u_cov^2 or with
diag(u_base,u_base). Since u_cov itself is off diagonal in (2), its SF=1
cannot simply be called SF=1 on each sheet or each quarter.

The smallest remaining index-map obligation is therefore:

  exhibit the original deck-invariant response on the two-sheet seam fiber
  and prove that, under W, its Fredholm or spectral-flow class is

    [u_cov^2]=[u_base] direct-sum [u_base],              (26)

  or give the different explicit per-sheet operator whose two copies produce
  (23). No new geometry or free alpha is needed.

8. Scope
--------

This result derives the two quarter domains and their exact winding action
from the local A4 NS cover. It retains the direct full-cover source
(B_1/2,u_cov), for which 0<1/2<1 and the rank-one spectral-flow construction
needs no quarter choice. It does not identify either quarter character with
the internal E8/P2 quarter time, construct the marked D8/CAR local-net
intertwiner, or supply the global spin-surface quadratic refinement and
diagonal Arf gauging.
