INDEPENDENT REVIEW: ORIGIN OF THE QUARTER PAIR
================================================

25 October 2026. Read only review of
MOBIUS-QUARTER-OPERATOR-DICTIONARY.txt and its exact checker. This review does
not change an original source, the Atlas, the ledger, or the contract index.

Verdict
-------

ACCEPTED for the longitudinal core, with one sign clarification and one
necessary scope interpretation.

The operator identity

  B_cov = 2 (B_1/4 direct-sum B_3/4)

is exact, including the self-adjoint H1 domains. The two shifted summands are
the two characters of one Pin-minus order-four half-deck action. They are not
two separately selected sources. The sign of the Pin lift exchanges the labels
1/4 and 3/4 and leaves the unordered pair fixed.

The primary geometric result supports precisely this statement once the
contract is read as a longitudinal core reduction. On the full Moebius pinor
space the one-step operator is not scalar translation alone. It is cJ, with

  J phi(w) = sigma_1 phi(-w).

The shifts 1/4 and 3/4 belong to the J=+1 and J=-1 reducing spaces. Thus the
contract's scalar H_cov is the reduced core after this J decomposition. The
bare equation f(theta+2 pi)=-f(theta) determines the half-integer cover
spectrum, but the assignment of the two quarter shifts to full pinor
components additionally uses J. The contract states this in its verdict; it
must remain part of every later use of the dictionary.

Primary comparison:

1. Exact Fourier and domain calculation
---------------------------------------

Put normalized measure on both circles and write

  e_m(x)=exp(i m x),
  W_0 e_m=exp(i(2m+1/2)theta),
  W_1 e_m=exp(i(2m+3/2)theta).

These are disjoint even and odd parts of the full antiperiodic Fourier basis.
For each j,

  ||W_j g||^2
    = integral_0^(2pi) |g(2 theta)|^2 dtheta/(2pi)
    = ||g||^2.

The second equality includes the two traversals of the x circle. Hence W_0 and
W_1 are isometries with orthogonal, jointly complete ranges, and

  W=W_0 direct-sum W_1

is unitary. Fourier weights also show that W maps H1_per direct-sum H1_per
onto the antiperiodic H1 domain. Direct differentiation gives

  B_cov W_0 = 2 W_0(D+1/4),
  B_cov W_1 = 2 W_1(D+3/4).

Thus the equality is an unbounded self-adjoint operator identity, not merely a
spectral list. W_0 and W_1 have norm one. Multiplication by exp(i theta),
multiplication by exp(i x), and half translation all have norm one and preserve
the stated Sobolev domains.

The orientation repair B_cov=-i partial_theta is consistent with the printed
eigenvalues n+1/2. It should be understood as the repaired orientation of the
older circle formula, not as a literal use of an older i partial_theta formula
with unchanged eigenvalue signs.

2. Half-deck and winding action
-------------------------------

On the reduced core,

  T_pi=exp(i pi B_cov),
  T_pi W_0= i W_0,
  T_pi W_1=-i W_1,
  T_pi^2=-I.

Changing the Pin lift sign swaps the two eigenspace labels. It supplies no
canonical choice of one of them.

For u_cov=exp(i theta), direct substitution gives

  u_cov W_0 g=W_1 g,
  u_cov W_1 g=W_0(u_base g),

and therefore, in the common periodic coefficient basis,

            [ 0  u_base ]
  u_cov  =  [           ],
            [ I    0    ]

  u_cov^2=diag(u_base,u_base).

This matrix is correct. It also makes the selection boundary especially
clear: each quarter space reduces B_cov and T_pi, but neither quarter space is
invariant under the original winding-one u_cov. Only u_cov squared preserves
the two quarter spaces separately. Choosing one quarter as the operational
source therefore still needs an additional source, state, or OS time rule.

3. Spectral flow and Fredholm sign
----------------------------------

With the path convention used in the dictionary,

  B_cov(t)=B_cov+t,

the crossing counts are

  SF(u_cov)=+1,
  SF(u_cov^2)=+2,
  SF_1/4(u_base)=SF_3/4(u_base)=+1.

There are no endpoint crossings, so no endpoint convention enters. The
non-double-counting conclusion is correct: the sum of the two base quarter
flows represents u_cov squared, not u_cov.

The word "index" needs a sign convention whenever it means the compressed
Toeplitz operator. For the positive spectral projection P_+ and the multiplier
u used here,

  ind(P_+ u P_+)=-1,
  ind(P_+ u^2 P_+)=-2,

whereas the displayed spectral flows are +1 and +2. Thus

  SF(B to u^* B u) = - ind(P_+ u P_+)

in this convention. The dictionary's numerical equations are correct as
spectral-flow equations. Phrases such as "the same index value" and
"Fredholm or spectral-flow class" must not be read as equality with the same
signed Fredholm index. The original d SF=2 comparison is a comparison of the
positive spectral-flow normalization unless an explicit sign convention says
otherwise.

4. What is established and what remains
---------------------------------------

Established:

  * the Pin-minus lift produces the unordered pair {1/4,3/4};
  * the unitary Fourier split, domains, deck characters, and winding matrices
    are exact;
  * one cover winding has spectral flow one, while its square is the direct
    sum of the two quarter windings and has spectral flow two;
  * the two quarters are one source-cover decomposition and must not be counted
    as independent physical sources.

Still separate:

  * a rule selecting one reducing quarter channel;
  * identification of B_cov with the original OS or physical time generator;
  * identification of the original d=2 response insertion with u_cov squared
    or with the diagonal base operator;
  * any equality between this geometric deck clock and an internal E8 clock;
  * the full transverse pinor, boundary, local-net, and Arf data.

The exact checker correctly verifies the finite Fourier-label identities and
crossing counts. The unitary and unbounded-domain statements above are the
analytic argument behind those finite checks. No additional holonomy, second
source copy, or boundary model is required for the quarter-pair origin.
