Use the old q, r from the five-packet source, q = M^t(e8-e7),

2. Exact fixed-label composition and its Ward test

Write a scalar cover mode as N=8n+k, 0<=k<8. The original tangential
frequency is p=N+alpha. Define T_a e_N=e_(N+8a_k) for uniform integral or
half-integral a. Let Pi act on the labels by k->k+4 mod 8, and let eps(a)
be 0 in the integral case and 1 in the half-integral case. Then
  T_a T_d = T_(d+Pi^eps(d) a).
This follows by reading the residue after the first translation. The
test is exactly periodic in N, so the eight residue checks suffice.

The pinned data, coordinates in label order 0,...,7, are
  q = (-1, 1, 1,-1,-1, 1, 1,-1)/2,
  r = ( 1, 1, 1,-1, 1,-1, 1, 1)/2.
Pi q=q, Pi r differs from r. Set b=r-q and b_wrong=Pi r-q. Thus
  T_-q T_r = T_b,  T_r T_-q = T_b_wrong,
  T_q^-1 = T_-q,   T_r^-1 = T_(-Pi r).
The old current root generators of weights -q and r commute since their
inner product is zero and neither sum nor difference is a root. A cocycle
phase cannot change the resulting Fourier destinations.

M b = e5+e6+e7-e8, with (Y,T3)=(+1/2,-1/2);
M b_wrong = -e5+e6+e7-e8, with (Y,T3)=(-1/2,+1/2).
Both are color singlets and electrically neutral. If the original charged
endpoints carry these Cartan actions, any common hypercharge-invariant
form makes the wrong branch orthogonal to the intended branch. Indeed a
constant hypercharge rotation multiplies the cross form by exp(i theta);
invariance for every theta forces zero. This conditional Ward zero
concerns the wrong branch, not the physical q/r response.

The individual bare half shifts also fail the source Cartan test before
any ordered comparison. For the original residue phase D(theta), their
conjugation permutes the phases by Pi. An implementer transforming by a
pure scalar character would commute with D(theta) at the CAR
automorphism level. Changing a scalar Klein phase cannot alter that
conjugation. In the fixed p<0 sea, the residue occupation differences
are integer, namely (0,1,1,0,-1,0,0,-1) for T_q and
(1,0,1,1,0,0,0,-1) for T_r. They are not the old half charges.
The canonical graph Fock representation has periodic same-face Cartan
rotations; an intertwiner carrying the required half-charge needs the
opposite action of a 2pi residue rotation between its source and target
sectors. The already supplied old AIA has this action, R070/R071.
This test excludes a same-representation identification, not the
sector-changing defect intertwiners of that parent.

3. What the original graph can and cannot distinguish

R074 gives beta=4pi, C(p)=1/(1+exp(beta*p)) on the second face, and
G(p) the orthogonal projection on (1,u), u=exp(-beta*p/2).
For an integral shift a within the residues the absolutely convergent
signed trace of C(p)-C(p+8a_k) along the kth ladder is a_k by telescoping.
In particular it agrees with the signed reference-sea difference.
From R074 eqs. (7)-(8) applied separately to ladders the full geometric
Hilbert-Schmidt defect squared of the bare simultaneous two-face shift is
  sum_k 2 a_k tanh(2 beta a_k).
Finite shift profiles, including the two half shifts, have finite
commutator norm by the exponential tails of G(p) in both directions.

Both endpoints have four entries of magnitude 1, so both yield
8 tanh(2 beta). The exact filled-sea relative energy in each quarter is
sum_k [4a_k^2+(k+alpha-4)a_k]. For both paths this is 29/2 at alpha=1/4
and 31/2 at alpha=3/4. Neither scalar test sees the hypercharge sign.
These are geometric graph and one-face energy computations with the
stated sea subtraction. They are not R113 measure factors.

The canonical exact graph transport for p->p' is F(p') F(p)^*, where
F(p) has columns (1,u),(-u,1) normalized by sqrt(1+u^2).
It intertwines the two G blocks and their complements. Frame products
telescope, so this graph-preserving lift still obeys the composition
law of section 2 and permutes the Cartan labels. Its polarization
preserving Fock lift keeps the doubled G vacuum up to phase. Geometric
transport therefore supplies neither a pure half-charge operator nor
the required marked endpoint response.

4. Forced charge correction and old field test

The unique integral residue-diagonal translation closing the bad product
is kappa=b-b_wrong=r-Pi r=(0,1,0,-1,0,-1,0,1). It has M kappa=2e5,
Y=1, T3=-1 and squared norm four. Exactly,
  T_kappa T_r T_-q = T_b = T_(Pi r) T_-q.
The last expression exhibits the corresponding moving chart rule. Using
it as a physical field map requires that the charge action and the state
be carried with the chart. The correction is not a neutral phase.

In the old level-one lattice field algebra r and -Pi r are orthogonal
roots, both with Y=+1/2, T3=-1/2, and their regular normally ordered
product has charge kappa up to the fixed cocycle. The integer field
V_kappa has two creation and two annihilation legs in the folded CAR
description. In the actual eight-point Fourier unfold its coefficient
on creation arcs 0,1 and annihilation arcs 2,3 has absolute value 1/32
from the two Fourier minors. The two quarter phases affect this
coefficient only by unit modulus. Thus that field has a separated-arc
term, and cannot be a field supported on just one cover arc.

The permutation equality does not imply a vertex mode equality. Test
it independently on the existing old Fold with the already fixed five
q packets x_i, of charge q and oscillator grade three. Compare
  A_i = (V_b)_0 x_i,
  C_i = (V_kappa)_0 (V_b_wrong)_0 x_i.
These are lattice L0 zero modes. The two final momenta agree exactly.
The intermediate momenta Pi r and r have equal squared length and
kappa has zero pairing with the original clock drift k+alpha-4.
The correction thus leaves the original H energy unchanged.
Neither calculation replaces a CAR permutation by a vertex identity.
This tests the natural field-level candidate corresponding to the
charge repair, with no oscillator or cocycle fitted to force equality.

The three modes have zero net oscillator grade shift, since each
charge a on its input momentum p has a^2/2+a.p=0. Expand the existing
five packets in orthonormal oscillator coordinates. The vertex
coefficients use exp(sum_n (a.h_-n) z^n/n) and contract deleted
creation factors with -a. The Hermitian oscillator form is diagonal in
the orthonormal monomials with factorial weights. All computations are
exact in the old coefficient field. The omitted fixed-momentum
cocycle phases have unit modulus and do not change the tested ranks or
squared overlaps. The first correct norm divided by the input norm
agrees with the independent old value 19017/4277 in both quarters.

The correct and corrected images each have rank five; the cross Gram
has rank five; the union has rank ten in both quarters. Therefore even
arbitrary mixing of the five corrected outputs cannot yield a nonzero
correct output. Each corrected packet has strictly positive squared
normalized overlap with its intended packet, ranging from approximately
0.218 to 0.692, and all are strictly below one. Charge and time were
repaired, but the simple ordered correction is not the field transport.
The Gram and separation are statements about the old Fold, not about
their possible projections in a physical weighted quotient.
