data fixed. On a patch with nonzero constant original doublet Phi0,
set Phi_alpha(x)=exp(i alpha(x))Phi0, with alpha smooth and supported
inside the patch. It can be chosen seam even by using the corresponding
reflected pair of patches. More generally use the actual weak/quaternion

 |D Phi_alpha|^2-|D Phi0|^2=u0 |d alpha|^2                (2)

in the flat, A=0 local chart and the displayed phase convention. The
right side is positive for a nonconstant alpha. The source's actual

replaced by a scalar phase on every fermion species.

Equation(1) and the fixed connection are identical for these configurations.
Thus the hypothetical complete Laplace operator

A gauge transformation of Phi together with the transformed gauge

where Phi_F=Phi_F* is the entire original finite internal endomorphism,
with its Yukawa, conjugate, family and Majorana entries as supplied. The
Euclidean gamma matrices are Hermitian, gamma5 anticommutes with gamma^mu,

 D_Phi^2=Dslash^2+Phi_F^2

Other gamma and connection conventions move signs or i factors between

Higgs coupling, oscillator, field metric or representation is chosen here.
If a source uses a more general Dirac potential, square that actual
