FINITE ORIGINAL SOURCE: CONSTRUCTIVE BEREZIN INVERSE AND ADMISSIBLE TESTS
3 November 2026. Readonly source review, workspace proof note.

Result

On an actually supplied finite Grassmann field chart, complementary source
insertions reconstruct every coefficient of the original unnormalized
density. A single saturated insertion extracts its zero fermion body,
including at zeros of the Pfaffian. Compact group invariance lets this
inverse use invariant composites without requiring individual charged
letters to be physical observables. Applicability to the full original
Padm source still requires the saturated composite's actual field/contact
representation and admissibility. Neither is inferred from its degree.

1. Exact coefficient inverse, including the sign

Use the ORIGINAL finite regulator, ordered independent odd coordinates
theta_1,...,theta_N with N even, base integration dnu(b), and its given
orientation. Put tau=theta_1...theta_N and int Dtheta tau=1. Here b denotes
every remaining original variable, not a newly chosen boson background.
Let

 w(b,theta)=sum_(I subset {1,...,N}) w_I(b) theta^I,
 L[A]=int dnu(b) int Dtheta A(b,theta) w(b,theta).        (1)

All words theta^I have increasing indices. If J=I^c, define

 sigma(I)=(-1)^(#{(j,i):j in J, i in I, j>i}).

Thus theta^J theta^I=sigma(I)tau. Every other coefficient either has
a repeated index or fails to saturate all indices, so

 L[f(b) theta^(I^c)]=sigma(I) int dnu(b) f(b) w_I(b).     (2)

Since sigma(I)^2=1, the same sign inverts the formula. This identifies
the coefficient distributions against every actually allowed test f.
For an even density, only even I occur and all complementary insertions
are even. No odd observable access is required by this inverse.

In particular

 tau w=tau w_empty,
 L[f tau]=int dnu f w_empty.                            (3)

This is an explicit inverse operation on the source. It does not infer
an unknown value of L[f tau] from lower moments or from reflection positivity.

2. The bare body and Pfaffian zeros

On an actual chart with w=exp(-S_B) exp(-theta^T M_b theta/2),

 L[f]=int dnu f exp(-S_B) Pf(-M_b),
 L[f tau]=int dnu f exp(-S_B).                          (4)

The sign in the Pfaffian is fixed here by the displayed exponent and
orientation. A convention absorbing that sign into its M gives Pf(M).
Equation(3), and therefore body extraction, is unchanged. It requires
neither an inverse of M nor division by Pf(M), so it survives singular
fermion fibers. Higher even fermion interactions also leave(3) unchanged.

If N counts only matter fermions, w_empty still contains the original
ghost and other odd variables. A pure boson density requires saturation
of every integrated odd direction at that integration stage. This is
not automatic in a physical BRST quotient. Conversely, ghosts already
eliminated in the original caller are not integrated a second time.

On a fixed nonzero body branch, all coefficients from(2) give the action
by a finite algebraic logarithm. Write w=w_empty(1+n), with n nilpotent:

 S=-log(w_empty)-sum_(k=1)^N (-1)^(k+1)n^k/k.            (5)

Terms vanish once their Grassmann degree exceeds N; for an even n the
sum terminates already at k<=N/2. The chosen branch and every original
boundary, determinant, eta and reference factor remain part of this
same caller. On a zero body stratum retain w itself. No logarithmic
representation is asserted there.

If only normalized omega=L/L[1] is known, (2) reconstructs w/L[1]. In
particular omega(f tau)/omega(tau), when its denominator is nonzero,
is the normalized body functional. The missing absolute scalar is not
free: the original Z_rel[0] and reference caller fix it when supplied.
No normalization is reset in these statements.

3. Compact invariant quotients require fewer probes

Let the ACTUAL compact structural group G act by even automorphisms on
this chart, preserve the COMPLETE original joint pairing
B(A,w)=int dnu(b) Dtheta A w, and leave the joint density w invariant.
At fixed b a purely Berezin pairing is valid only for actions fixing b.
If gauge transformations move b, all its original density/Jacobian data
are transported in the joint pairing. Its normalized Haar average R_G obeys

 B(R_G A,w)=int_G B(g A,w)dg=B(A,w).                    (6)

Thus the averaged complementary insertions R_G(theta^(I^c)) reproduce
the same coefficient functionals. More intrinsically, the restricted
pairing separates all invariant densities: if invariant delta w pairs
to zero with all invariant A, then it pairs to zero with every A by(6),
and Berezin nondegeneracy implies delta w=0. This separates invariant joint densities; it does not assert invariance
of a conditional density at each fixed b. It applies to the even
subalgebra as well when N is even. It does not require every individual
charged field to preserve the physical Hilbert space.

For an original paired complex chart transforming by

 psi -> U psi,       barpsi -> barpsi U^(-1),

the determinant character of the combined odd representation is
det(U)det(U^(-1))=1. Its saturated top monomial is therefore invariant
under this action, for any invertible U. Gauge and color center neutrality
of this particular paired top probe follows directly. A genuine Majorana
chart instead has its actual determinant/orientation character, which
must be checked; it is not replaced by the paired formula.

4. Exact restriction criterion and determinant line bookkeeping

At a fixed b, let D be the permitted linear density class and A_adm the
linear space of actual admissible composite tests. Define

 N_adm={delta w in D: B(A,delta w)=0 for every A in A_adm}.

The body is identifiable from admissible source data if and only if

 N_adm is contained in ker(delta w -> delta w_empty).   (7)

Proof: two densities with identical data differ by N_adm; their body
values agree exactly when(7) holds. In finite dimension the equivalent
dual criterion is that the body probe tau belongs to A_adm+D^perp.
Here D^perp denotes the annihilator of D under B. The invariant theorem
above verifies this criterion when the actual reduction is precisely
the stated compact invariant class. It is not a theorem for every
unrelated spectral restriction.

Under a variable dependent chart theta'=R(b)theta,

 Dtheta'=det(R)^(-1)Dtheta,
 w'(b,theta')=det(R)w(b,R^(-1)theta').

Hence w'_empty=det(R)w_empty. A global top test has the local form
T=kappa(b)tau, with kappa'=det(R)^(-1)kappa. The reconstructed
kappa w_empty is then invariant. These are density and determinant
line transformations, not an extra phase or measure choice. Use the
original trivialization/eta/orientation when it exists; do not choose
a new frame to make the extracted body take a desired value.

5. Applicability to the original physical source

The original complete source and its given field/contact dictionary must
realize L[f theta^(I^c)] as the relevant ordered source derivatives.
In particular the represented saturated word must preserve Ran(Padm)
together with its adjoint. It cannot be declared physical merely because
it is even or gauge invariant. A top Grassmann insertion is not in general
a CAR occupation projector: coherent symbol, time ordering and coincident
contacts belong to the original map.

The original whole Padm includes a spectral P_gap as well as seam, color
and determinant selectors. Gauge neutrality from section3 supplies no
commutator with P_gap. If the original caller instead supplies an actual
independent reduced Grassmann chart on its physical field domain, apply
the inverse directly in that chart. No ambient CAR saturation is required.
The notation integral_Hadm alone does not construct such a chart.

A nonmultiplicative selector kernel must remain in the full original
kernel. Equation(3) then extracts the body of that kernel. Calling it the
unselected exp(-S_B) would require the selector's actual field action.

First remaining original premise: a concrete admissible composite or
source coefficient that realizes this top probe on the actual common
field chart, with its prescribed contact, determinant and reference data.
The inspected original passages do not compute that source value or
prove its full Padm admissibility. No primitive omega value, actual bare
boson weight, or full source equality has been obtained by this note.
