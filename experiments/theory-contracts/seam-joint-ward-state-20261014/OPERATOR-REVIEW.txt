8. WINDING FOUR IS A GENUINE ORIGINAL-CAR TRANSPORT, BUT NOT THE E8 ROOT

There is a constructive source operator behind the proposed affine map.  In
the original chiral Fourier basis

  f_(ell,k)(theta)=exp(i(8 ell+k+alpha) theta),  k=0,...,7,

  (ell,k) -> (ell,k+4)       for k=0,...,3,
  (ell,k) -> (ell+1,k-4)     for k=4,...,7.

It preserves the quarter boundary phase because exp(4 i theta) is periodic.
In the eight-residue charge coordinates, with the convention in which the
four crossings of the polarized sea add charge, this is exactly

  T(p)=Pp+a,
  P(k)=k+4 mod 8,
  a=(1,1,1,1,0,0,0,0)=s+r0.

The opposite Fourier shift gives T^-1 and reverses the charge convention; P
itself is not orientation reversal.  Orientation reversal would send the
Fourier index to its negative and reverse oscillator modes, whereas P merely
adds four modulo eight.

Since Pa=q-a, the affine identities hold for every charge vector:

  T^2(p)=p+q,
  Q(Tp)=Q(p)+4.

For every integer n, including negative n,

  T^n(0)=n s+epsilon_n r0,

where epsilon_n is 0 for even n and 1 for odd n.  For example
T^-1(0)=a-q=-s+r0, so the same parity formula covers negative powers.

The time coincidence is also exact rather than numerical.  With

  K_alpha(p)=|p|^2/2+delta.p,
  delta_k=(k+alpha)/8-1/2,

one finds

  K_alpha(Tp)-K_alpha(p)=Q(p)/2+3/4+alpha/2.

particular K_alpha(a)=K_alpha(s)=3/4+alpha/2, namely 7/8 or 9/8 for the two
quarter twists.  The equality occurs because

  |a|^2/2=2,  |s|^2/2=1,  delta.(a-s)=delta.r0=-1.

It is therefore possible for source charge, squared clock and full physical
time to agree while the conformal/OPE operator is different.

The fine clock detects the difference on the odd CAR algebra.  Put
mu_k=(k+alpha)/8.  Relative to translation by s, the clock exponent of T is

  mu.(Tp-p-s)=r0.p-1.

For p in Z^8 this contributes (-1)^Q(p).  Hence T and the s translation agree
on the even current algebra and under C=g^2, but differ by fermion parity on
