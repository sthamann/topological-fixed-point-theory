========================

------

the first tensor power carrying a nonconstant POVM with the actual six Ovoid
label action is n=3.  At n=1 and n=2 every covariant six-effect POVM is the

The result covers arbitrary linear Hermitian effects, followed by positivity
and normalization.  It is not restricted to projective or rank-one guesses.

Why covariance becomes an intertwiner question
-----------------------------------------------

Let F_a, a=0,...,5, be effects with sum F_a=I and with the actual Ovoid
permutation pi_g:

    rho_n(g) F_a rho_n(g)* = F_{pi_g(a)}.

Write X_a=F_a-I/6.  Then sum X_a=0.  The six labels therefore contribute
their centered five-dimensional permutation representation.  A nonconstant
POVM exists only if this target five occurs in End(V4 tensor n).  Conversely,
at n=3 the checker supplies positive effects explicitly.  This reduces the
minimality statement to a finite exact Hom computation.

Arity one
---------

The Ovoid labels are fixed by the normal Pauli kernel N64.  Hence every F_a
must commute with N64.  The sixteen phase-free Pauli matrices span End(V4),
and their exact simultaneous commutant has dimension one.  Thus every F_a is
scalar.  The transitive S6 action makes the six scalars equal, and normalization
gives

    F_a = I4/6.

Arity two: the decisive outer-action mismatch
---------------------------------------------

On V4 tensor V4, the sixteen vectors vec(P)/2, for the sixteen phase-free
Paulis P, form an orthonormal Bell basis.  The checker diagonalizes every
P tensor P in that basis and finds sixteen distinct joint characters.  The
full N64-invariant operator algebra is therefore exactly the span of the
sixteen Bell projectors.  Its dimension is 16.

Transpose parity splits these projectors canonically:

    16 = 10 symmetric + 6 antisymmetric.

Every one of the sixty actual root reflections preserves this split.  Its
cycle types are

    actual Ovoid6 labels       : (2,1,1,1,1),
    symmetric Bell10 labels   : (2,2,2,1,1,1,1),
    antisymmetric Bell6 labels : (2,2,2).

Thus the antisymmetric six is precisely the outer six-point S6 action.  It is
not the actual Ovoid six.  The fifteen target transpositions together with
their Bell actions generate 720 paired permutations.  Exact character inner
products over those 720 elements give

    Hom(target5, Bell10) = 0,
    Hom(target5, Bell6)  = 0.

The Bell10 centered character has norm one and dimension nine.  The Bell6
centered character has norm one and dimension five, but its inner product
with the target five is zero.  Equal dimension five therefore does not supply
the needed dictionary.  The complete N64 commutant contains no target five,
so

    F_a = I16/6

for every covariant six-effect POVM at n=2.

Mixed-orientation control
-------------------------

The checker does not infer the result for V4 tensor conjugate(V4) from the
ordinary tensor square.  It separately reconstructs the similarity action

    vec(P) -> vec(R P R*)

on all sixteen Pauli Bell lines.  It separately diagonalizes every
P tensor conjugate(P) and again finds sixteen distinct joint characters, so
the mixed N64 commutant is exactly the span of these sixteen projectors.  The
identity line is fixed, and the nonzero
fifteen-label character decomposes exactly as

    15 = 1 + 9 + outer5.

The paired root permutations again generate all 720 elements.  The inner
product of this permutation character with the actual Ovoid target five is
zero.  Thus this alternative orientation also supplies only the uniform
six-effect Ovoid POVM.  This is a separate exact check and does not identify
the two tensor orientations.

Arity three: the existing original carrier
-------------------------------------------

From the original quartic word supports the checker reconstructs the same
64 by 20 isometry W used by the erasure contract.  It proves exactly

    W*W = I20,
    WW* = Pi20 = the three-slot symmetrizer,
    image(W) = Sym^3(V4),
    R4 tensor3 W = W [conjugate(R4) tensor C5]

for all sixty native root reflections.  For all sixteen phase-free Pauli
kernel matrices it also proves

    P tensor3 W = W [conjugate(P) tensor I5].

Consequently the N64 commutant on this carrier is

    I4 tensor End(H5).

The actual quartic H5 is the actual Ovoid standard five by the independently
reconstructed all-sixty bridge.  Over the generated S6, if chi is its
character, the checker verifies the exact character decomposition

    End(H5) = 1 + 5 + 9 + 10

with pairwise orthonormal characters.  Equivalently,

    <chi, chi^2> = 1.

The target five occurs exactly once on the carrier.  The known centered Ovoid
effects E_a-I5/6 are a nonzero copy of it.  Therefore every covariant
six-effect POVM restricted to Sym^3(V4) has the form

    A_a(t) = I4 tensor [t E_a + (1-t) I5/6]

for one real t.  This is a classification, not only an example.

Full G31 character count
------------------------

The carrier calculation alone would still allow another target five on the
44 dimensional complementary sector.  The checker closes that possibility
by an independent full representation character count.

It chooses one of the actual native four-dimensional root matrices above each
of the fifteen Ovoid transpositions.  A breadth-first word construction gives
one matrix G_pi for every one of the 720 permutations pi in S6.  The checker
then verifies all multiplication data needed for a complete transversal:

* all sixty original root lifts lie in the expected Pauli coset;
* the fifteen chosen lifts normalize the projective Pauli16;
* every one of the 720 times 15 right-generator products lands in the expected
  next coset;
* the matrices P G_pi give exactly 11,520 distinct projective elements.

The four central phases of G31 do not change |tr|.  Hence the full G31
multiplicity equals the projective average

    m_n = (1/11520) sum_(pi in S6, P in Pauli16)
          chi5(pi) |tr(P G_pi)|^(2n).

The exact unnormalized sums for n=1,2,3 are

    0, 0, 11520,

so

    m_1=0, m_2=0, m_3=1.

Thus the target five occurs exactly once in the entire End(V4 tensor3), not
once per chosen sector.  The already constructed centered family is nonzero,
so it spans that unique full Hom space.  Every full covariant POVM is therefore

    F_a(t) = I64/6 + t W[I4 tensor (E_a-I5/6)]W*

or, equivalently,

    F_a(t) = W[I4 tensor (t E_a+(1-t)I5/6)]W*
             + (I64-Pi20)/6.

The uniform action on the 44 dimensional complement and the support of the
informative term on Sym^3 are consequences of full G31 covariance.  They are
not an extra bosonic-sector choice for this POVM classification.

Each E_a has eigenvalues 5/6 on its Ovoid ray and zero on the orthogonal
four-space.  The two eigenvalues of the bracket are therefore

    (1+4t)/6, with multiplicity 1,
    (1-t)/6,  with multiplicity 4.

Positivity is equivalent to

    -1/4 <= t <= 1.

At t=1 this is the existing rank-four readout on the twenty-dimensional
carrier.  At t=0 it is uniform.  At t=-1/4 the Ovoid ray is excluded and the
carrier effect has rank 16.

The checker constructs t=1 as six actual 64 by 64 matrices.  They sum to I64,
each has rank 48 and spectrum

    5/6 with multiplicity 4,
    1/6 with multiplicity 44,
    0   with multiplicity 16.

All 60 times 6 covariance identities are certified exactly in factored form:
the checker verifies the decoder intertwiner, all six H5 effect identities,
and invariance of Pi20 for each of the sixty roots.  Multiplying these three
identities gives the displayed 64 dimensional covariance identity.  Hence
n=3 is attainable, while n=1 and n=2 are excluded, proving minimality.
