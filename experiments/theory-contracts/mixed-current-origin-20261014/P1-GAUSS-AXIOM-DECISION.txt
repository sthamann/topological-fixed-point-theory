4. Concrete physical difference of the two local nets

Old net: Z is an even LOCAL register observable; F_[0,a] is a string.
New net: W=i^b product_x tau^z_x is a GLOBAL Wilson loop; tau^x_a is local.
The relation F Z=-Z F is preserved as tau^x W=-W tau^x. Products and
adjoints are not the issue; support is changed.

More explicitly, assume the register cell is disjoint from a candidate
endpoint interval O. If a support-preserving faithful comparison retained
both Z and a nonzero local F in those disjoint regions, ordinary even
locality would require [Z,F]=0. The actual identity gives [Z,F]=2ZF,
which is nonzero for unitary F. Hence the existing E cannot be a local-net
equivalence with this old assignment fixed. This requires no E8 premise.

The same change affects matter insertions. An old local even hopping
c_x* c_(x+1) changes sign under G_x and G_(x+1). In the existing gauge
description its local observable representative includes tau^z_x;
cross-region bilinears require the corresponding Wilson path. Calling
the undressed old hopping the same gauge-invariant local generator would
change the operator rule. This is a consequence of (3.1), not a new model.

Conditional consequence: WITH THE OLD FULL LOCAL REGISTER NET FIXED,
identifying this E as a support-preserving re-expression is incompatible.
This does not establish incompatibility with EVERY primitive P1 source,
