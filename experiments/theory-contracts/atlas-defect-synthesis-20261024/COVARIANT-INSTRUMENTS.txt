COMPLETE COVARIANT INSTRUMENTS FOR THE NATIVE SIX-SIMPLEX POVM
================================================================

Scope
-----

This theorem stays on the original native H5 and uses the six rank-one ovoid
effects and the sixty marked G31 actions already reconstructed by
doily_quartic_bridge.py.  An instrument means six completely positive maps

    I_a : End(H5) -> End(H5)

whose effects are the fixed operators E_a and which obey the actual outcome
covariance

    I_{pi_g(a)}(rho_g X rho_g^dagger)
      = rho_g I_a(X) rho_g^dagger.

The result classifies every such finite instrument.  It does not add a new
carrier, select a physical value of its parameter, or turn the instrument into
physical time evolution.

Why rank one forces measure and prepare
---------------------------------------

For one outcome let J_a be the positive Choi matrix of I_a.  Its input
partial trace is E_a^T.  Since E_a has rank one, positivity forces J_a to have
support only on

    support(E_a^T) tensor H5.

Indeed, for x in the kernel of E_a^T,

    Tr[(|x><x| tensor I) J_a] = <x|E_a^T|x> = 0.

A positive matrix with zero expectation on that whole subspace has no row or
column there.  Therefore there is a unique density matrix sigma_a such that

    J_a = E_a^T tensor sigma_a,

or equivalently

    I_a(rho) = Tr(E_a rho) sigma_a.

This is a theorem for every completely positive operation with the fixed
rank-one effect.  It is not a chosen Kraus ansatz.

Exact stabilizer commutant
--------------------------

Write

    P_a = (6/5) E_a.

Each P_a is a rank-one orthogonal projector.  The sixty original marked
actions are transitive on the six labels.  For a fixed label a, forty marked
reflections fix a; their outcome permutations contain the ten transpositions
of the other five labels.  Solving the full rational commutator system on
End(H5) gives rank 23 and hence

    dim_C Comm(Stab(a)) = 2.

Both I5 and P_a lie in this commutant and are independent, so they form its
complete basis.  Covariance makes sigma_a stabilizer invariant.  Positivity
and trace one therefore give exactly

    sigma_a(p) = p P_a + (1-p)/4 (I5-P_a),
    0 <= p <= 1.

Transitivity forces the same p for all six outcomes.  Conversely the checker
verifies this family against every one of the sixty native actions.  Thus the
displayed interval is the complete covariant instrument space.

Kraus ranks and the extra minimality rule
-----------------------------------------

Because J_a=E_a^T tensor sigma_a, the minimal Kraus rank of one outcome is
rank(sigma_a):

    p=1:       rank 1,
    p=0:       rank 4,
    0<p<1:     rank 5.

With the classical outcome retained, the six blocks have total minimal Kraus
ranks 6, 24, and 30 respectively.  Requiring one Kraus operator per outcome,
pure prepared output, or minimal tagged environment dimension selects p=1
uniquely.  Each is an additional instrument principle.  None is implied by
the word primitive in the P1 source.

Channel after forgetting the outcome
------------------------------------

Let Phi_p=sum_a I_a and let Phi_L be the square-root Lueders channel of the
same POVM.  Since E_a=(5/6)P_a and P_a rho P_a=Tr(P_a rho)P_a,

    Phi_L(rho) = sum_a Tr(E_a rho) P_a.

Expanding sigma_a(p) now gives the exact identity

    Phi_p = (5p-1)/4 Phi_L + (1-p)/4 Tr(rho) I5.

The existing Lueders decomposition of End(H5) has dimensions 1+5+19 and
eigenvalues 1,4/5,0.  The replacement term acts only on the identity line.
Consequently

    spec(Phi_p) = 1^1, (p-1/5)^5, 0^19.

For 0<=p<=1 the second eigenvalue lies in [-1/5,4/5].  The unique stationary
density is therefore I5/5 for every member of the classified family.

The Choi matrix of the forgotten-outcome channel is

    J_p = (5p-1)/4 J_L + (1-p)/4 I25.

For this real symmetric rank-one POVM the numeric J_L array equals the
Lueders Liouville array, although their tensor-index meanings differ.  Its
exact Choi spectrum is

    p^1, ((15p+1)/20)^5, ((1-p)/4)^19.

Thus the forgotten-outcome channel has Choi rank 6 at p=1, rank 24 at p=0,
and rank 25 for 0<p<1.  These ranks differ from the tagged instrument ranks
6,24,30 because forgetting the classical label can merge Kraus spans.

Repeated outcome statistics
---------------------------

After observing a, the prepared state is sigma_a(p).  A second use of the
same POVM is therefore the six-state Markov chain

    Pr(b=a | a) = 5p/6,
    Pr(b!=a | a) = (6-5p)/30.

The transition matrix is doubly stochastic, so its stationary distribution
is uniform.  The stationary two-step law has

    Pr(a,a) = 5p/36,
    Pr(a,b) = (6-5p)/180,  a!=b.

At p=1 these numbers are 5/36 and 1/180, the same numbers obtained from the
Omega state in RELATIONAL-CHANNEL.txt.  Here they arise from a separable
measure-and-prepare time sequence.  Equality of these two tables is therefore
not evidence for entanglement or for a common physical preparation.

Exact failure of state tomography
---------------------------------

The six effects span a six dimensional subspace of the full 25 dimensional
operator space.  Repeating the instrument does not recover the missing
coherences.  This is witnessed by two explicit positive states in the real
isometric H5 chart.

Let

    X = i(|0><1| - |1><0|),
    rho_+ = I5/5 + X/10,
    rho_- = I5/5 - X/10.

X is Hermitian and traceless.  Both density matrices have the exact spectrum

    {1/10, 1/5, 1/5, 1/5, 3/10},

so they are distinct strictly positive states.  Every E_a is real symmetric,
while X is imaginary antisymmetric.  Therefore

    Tr(E_a X) = 0

for all six outcomes and

    Tr(E_a rho_+) = Tr(E_a rho_-) = 1/6.

The measure-and-prepare classification gives the probability of every finite
outcome word directly:

    Pr(a_1...a_n | rho)
      = Tr(E_{a_1} rho)
        product_{k=2}^n T_p[a_{k-1},a_k].

The two states have the same first factor, and the transition matrix is fixed
by p and the previous outcome.  Induction in n proves that rho_+ and rho_-
have identical probabilities for every finite outcome word.  Thus this
instrument family is not informationally complete and cannot reconstruct the
unmeasured GNS coherences or a complete GNS crossword from its outcome history.
This is an explicit counterexample, rather than a dimension-only inference.

Rational endpoint Choi certificates
------------------------------------

The checker also represents the same native H5 as the zero-sum subspace of
Q^6.  This is a redundant rational frame for the existing six rays, not a new
Hilbert space.  With

    Q = I6 - (1/6) 11^T,
    u_a = e_a - (1/6)1,

one has exactly

    Gram(u_a) = Gram(q_a)/12,
    E_a = u_a u_a^T,
    P_a = (6/5)u_a u_a^T.

All endpoint Choi entries are consequently rational.  Positivity follows
from the projector factors in

    J(I_a) = E_a^T tensor sigma_a.

Explicit endpoint Kraus descriptions are

    p=1: M_a = sqrt(6/5) u_a u_a^T,

and, for any orthonormal basis v_{a,1},...,v_{a,4} of the complement of u_a
inside H5,

    p=0: M_{a,r} = (1/2) v_{a,r} u_a^T.

The checker verifies rationality, ranks, trace five, and both H5 partial
traces for the total endpoint Choi matrices without floating point arithmetic.

Exact boundary of the result
----------------------------

The theorem removes all finite instrument freedom except p once the native
H5, its fixed six effects, and actual G31 outcome covariance are accepted.
It does not derive p, one-Kraus minimality, physical time, transition rates,
spacetime propagation, an entanglement source, complete state tomography,
unmeasured GNS coherences, or any P2 or D5 carrier map.
