COMMON GLUE AND CLOCK DISCRIMINANT
24 October 2026

Question

Can the already established E8 glue extension and the native quarter-clock
twisted sectors be recovered from the same marked object, with actual
quadratic forms rather than matching counts?

Category and inputs

Fix the positive definite root lattice Q=D5 direct-sum A3, its unitary
same-conformal VOA U, and its marked diagonal simple-current generator
h=(1,1). The canonical diagonal extension is V_E8. The prior SOURCE06
inner-conjugacy theorem identifies its dual glue clock kappa with the native
Jhat after transporting Cartan and all other marks. This transported category
is essential; a fixed old Gaussian Cartan is not preserved by that conjugacy.

1. One sixteen-element quadratic module

The discriminant group of Q is A_Q=C4 x C4, with

    q(a,b)=(5a^2+3b^2)/8 mod1,
    B((a,b),(c,d))=(5ac+3bd)/4 mod1.

This A_Q is NOT the different group A_J=E8/(1-J)E8=F2^4 in the doily theorem.
They both have sixteen elements; their additive groups and pairings differ.
Their connection is through the same V_E8 and its clock, not a group
isomorphism between C4^2 and F2^4.

The determinant of diag(5,3) modulo4 is a unit. Thus B is nondegenerate.
All q-isotropic subgroups are

    {0}, <(2,2)>, H+={(k,k)}, H-={(k,-k)}.

The two Lagrangians H+ and H- describe the two oriented E8 extensions over
fixed U. Their common subgroup is <(2,2)>, the D8 intermediate extension.
This reproduces the earlier extension classification rather than introducing
a new choice of lattice.

2. The clock sectors are the four fibers of this same module

For x=(a,b), braiding with h has character

    B(x,h)=(a-b)/4 mod1.

Hence the quotient/monodromy flux is f=a-b mod4. In the Li convention with
kappa=exp(+2 pi i v(0)), v=(1/2^5,0^3), the kappa^r twisted lattice is
E8-rv and has U labels (k-r,k). Thus f=-r; the two orientations must not be
silently interchanged.

Restriction of each twisted module to U is the sum of the four simple U
modules in its flux fiber. One may see this directly by partitioning the
shifted lattice into Q cosets. This proves the branching at every conformal
grade, rather than inferring it from ground-state dimensions.

D5 level-one lowest weights are (0,5/8,1/2,5/8), with top dimensions
(1,16,10,16). A3 weights are (0,3/8,1/2,3/8), with top dimensions
(1,4,6,4). Adding the two weights in each fiber gives

    r           0         1             2                    3
    ground h    0         3/8           1/2                  3/8
    top         1         (1,4)         (10,1)+(1,6)          (1,bar4)
    dimension   1         4             16                   4.

The names 4 and bar4 use the T chart of CLOCK-TWISTED.txt. An A3 discriminant
label called 1 in another chart can denote the opposite fundamental; the
listed U labels and f=-r formula are the actual orientation contract.

3. Explicit match to cyclic orbifold theory

Set phi(i,j)=(3i+j,2i+j) mod4. Its determinant is1, flux(phi(i,j))=i, and

    q(phi(i,j))=i^2/8+ij/4 mod1.

This is exactly the discriminant form for a cyclic order-four automorphism
of type t=2. In the notation of van Ekeren, Moller and Scheithauer,
t=4^2 rho mod4=2 for rho=3/8. The match is an explicit isometry of marked
quadratic modules, not just a census of sixteen states. Twisted-eigenspace
labels allow a root-of-unity normalization, as in their Theorem5.13.

4. What the anomaly actually says

There is no isotropic subgroup K of order4 with K intersect H+={0}.
Equivalently, there is no local extension selecting exactly one summand from
each of the four flux fibers. This is the obstruction to a transverse local
orbifold completion relative to this marked H+.

There DO exist holomorphic local extensions: H+ and H-. No conclusion that
all local completions are impossible follows from the obstruction.

5. Full modular phases

For the sixteen simple U modules, set

    S_xy=(1/4) exp(-2 pi i B(x,y)),
    T_x=exp(2 pi i(q(x)-8/24)).

The normalized quadratic Gauss sum equals

    (1/4) sum_(a,b) zeta8^(5a^2+3b^2)
      =(2 zeta8^5)(2 zeta8^3)/4=1.

The exact cyclotomic computation verifies S^2=(ST)^3=C, where C sends x to
-x. The quadratic form has level8. Including the c=8 Casimir term gives
T of exact order24. These are matrices on the labeled module space; distinct
ordinary characters can agree as scalar functions.

This modular level is not a statement about the degree of an earlier C12
base cover and does not identify conformal rotations with physical time.

6. Reproduction and review

    python3 finite_quadratic_clock.py
    python3 -OO finite_quadratic_clock.py

All equalities are over integers, rationals or Z[zeta24], using
Phi24(X)=X^8-X^4+1. Normal and optimized results are byte-identical. An
independent review checked orientation, all isotropic supports, the explicit
orbifold isometry, Gauss sum and both modular relations. CLOCK-TWISTED.txt
independently checks actual shifted E8 weights and native oscillator data.

Verdict

Exact common marked glue/clock structure within the previously reconstructed
unitary conformal realization. This removes an independent choice of twisted
sector data in that class. It does not select P1's microscopic source,
spacetime, a coupling normalization outside this realization, or a physical
state of the universe.
