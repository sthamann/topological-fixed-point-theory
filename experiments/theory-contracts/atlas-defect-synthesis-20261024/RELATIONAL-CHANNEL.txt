RELATIONAL CHANNEL ON CONJUGATE(H5) TENSOR H5
==============================================

Scope and additional contract
-----------------------------

The doily quartic theorem supplies the original native quartic representation
H5, its exact positive form, its six ovoid effects, and all sixty marked G31
actions.  This file adds one explicit kinematic contract:

    the state space contains conjugate(H5) tensor H5,

with diagonal action conjugate(rho_5(g)) tensor rho_5(g).

The presence or dynamical population of this tensor sector is not derived from
the compiler.  No coupling strength, transition rate, P2 carrier, or D5 carrier
identification is asserted.  Everything below follows exactly once this
additional sector contract is accepted.

Isometric use of the existing bridge
------------------------------------

In the centered ovoid basis the metric is H=I5+J5.  In the native unnormalised
quartic word basis the primitive positive form is

    G = diag(1,3,3,3,6).

For the existing primitive bridge T, the checker verifies

    T^T (G/12) T = H.

Thus T is an exact isometry after the displayed fixed normalization.  Changing
the common scale of T and G does not change any ray, projector, probability,
or channel below.

Invariant vector and singlet Choi construction
-----------------------------------------------

Let e_1,...,e_5 be a G orthonormal basis and define

    |Omega> = (1/sqrt(5)) sum_i conjugate(e_i) tensor e_i.

In the native word basis the same ray is vec(G^-1).  The checker solves the
full simultaneous fixed space for all sixty vector actions and obtains
dimension one.  Therefore |Omega> is the unique invariant vector ray in the
contracted relational sector.  This vector statement does not make its density
the unique invariant density operator.

Its projector has rank one, trace one, and both reduced density matrices are

    rho_left = rho_right = I5/5.

If the state is |Omega><Omega|, the unnormalised operator
5 |Omega><Omega| has input partial trace I5, is positive, and has rank one.
Under the canonical conjugate input identification it is exactly the Choi
operator of the identity channel on the native H5.  This is an operational
construction inside the declared relational sector.  It does not identify H5
with another five dimensional carrier.

Six ovoid effects and their exact joint law
-------------------------------------------

The six primitive native ovoid rays q_a obey

    q_a^T G q_a = 10,
    q_a^T G q_b = -2 for a != b.

The effects

    E_a = q_a q_a^T G / 12

are positive rank one operators for G and sum to I5.  All sixty native quartic
actions permute them exactly.

Conditional on the state |Omega><Omega|, measuring the conjugate effect on the
first half and the native effect on the second half gives

    p(a,b) = <Omega| conjugate(E_a) tensor E_b |Omega>
           = Tr(E_a^dagger E_b)/5.

The complete exact table has

    p(a,a) = 5/36,
    p(a,b) = 1/180 for a != b,
    p(a) = p(b) = 1/6.

Consequently

    p(second outcome = a | first outcome = a) = 5/6.

The joint table sums to one.  It is invariant under all sixty marked actions.

Conjugation convention
----------------------

The first tensor factor is the conjugate representation.  The identity used
throughout is

    <Omega| conjugate(A) tensor B |Omega> = Tr(A^dagger B)/5.

There is no transpose substitution.  The checker contains a complex control
with A=i E_00 and B=E_00.  The direct tensor expectation and adjoint formula
both give -i/5, while Tr(A^T B)/5 gives +i/5.  The wrong convention therefore
fails explicitly.

Invariant relational operator and exact spectrum
--------------------------------------------------

Besides the singlet projector, the effects give

    R = sum_a conjugate(E_a) tensor E_a.

R is Hermitian in the real isometric chart, nonzero, non-scalar, and invariant
under all sixty actions.  The checker proves the annihilating polynomial

    R (R - (5/6) I25) (R - (2/3) I25) = 0

and the three exact nullities.  Hence

    spec(R) = (5/6)^1, (2/3)^5, 0^19.

The one dimensional largest eigenspace is precisely the Omega ray.  Therefore
Rayleigh Ritz gives a sharp conditional selection statement: if one adds the
rule that the realized normalized state maximizes Tr(rho R), the unique
maximizer is |Omega><Omega| and the maximum is 5/6.  Choosing that variational
rule, or choosing the Hamiltonian -R and a ground-state rule, is an additional
state postulate.  Neither follows from G31 covariance or from the POVM.

Symmetry alone gives a direct counterexample.  The density I25/25 is invariant
under all sixty actions and has the same two local marginals I5/5.  For that
state

    Tr((I25/25) R) = 1/6,
    p(a,b) = 1/36 for every a,b.

Thus invariant densities need not have the Omega correlations.  The correlated
law 5/36 on the diagonal and 1/180 off the diagonal belongs specifically to
the Omega state.

Normalized Lueders channel
--------------------------

Each existing effect E_a has rank one and sole nonzero eigenvalue 5/6.  Its
positive square root is therefore

    K_a = sqrt(E_a) = sqrt(6/5) E_a.

The checker verifies

    sum_a K_a^dagger K_a = I5,
    sum_a K_a K_a^dagger = I5.

Consequently the square-root instrument gives a trace-preserving, unital
channel.  With column vectorization in the real isometric chart its Liouville
matrix is

    L = sum_a conjugate(K_a) tensor K_a = (6/5) R,

so its exact spectrum is

    spec(L) = 1^1, (4/5)^5, 0^19.

The fixed operator space is one dimensional and contains I5.  Hence the unique
normalized stationary density is I5/5.  Here stationary means fixed by this
specified channel.  The POVM determines the channel only after adopting the
Lueders square-root instrument; it does not supply a physical time evolution,
a coupling strength, or a rate.

Liouville and Choi index roles
------------------------------

R is an operator on conjugate(H5) tensor H5.  After multiplication by 6/5 its
indices give the Liouville matrix L of the Lueders channel.  The Choi matrix is
constructed separately as

    J_L = sum_a vec(K_a) vec(K_a)^dagger,
    vec(K)[input*5 + output] = K[output,input].

The checker verifies all 625 entries of the reshuffle

    J_L[(i,a),(j,b)] = L[(b,a),(j,i)].

Thus R must not be called a Choi matrix merely from its tensor notation.  A
special algebraic coincidence occurs here: every K_a is a real symmetric rank
one outer product, so the reshuffle fixes the summed array and J_L and L have
the same 25 by 25 entries.  Their index meanings remain different.  The Choi
construction independently has rank six, trace five, is positive, and has both
partial traces I5.

What is and is not selected
---------------------------

Within the additional tensor-sector contract, symmetry fixes exactly one
vector ray.  It does not choose that ray as the state because invariant mixed
densities also exist.  The identity-channel Choi construction and the displayed
correlated law follow after specifying the Omega state.  Maximizing the already
given R selects Omega uniquely, but the maximizing rule remains extra.  The
Lueders channel follows after specifying the square-root instrument.  None of
these statements selects a local pure state or one preferred ovoid, and none
supplies physical dynamics, preparation probability, or interaction strength.
