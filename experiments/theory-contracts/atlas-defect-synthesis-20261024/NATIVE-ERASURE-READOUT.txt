1. Original decoder, reconstructed rather than assumed
--------------------------------------------------------

Let S_i, i=0,...,4, be the five original quartic word supports from
tfpt_explorer/process.py:242-258.  Their sizes are

    |S_i| = (4,12,12,12,24).

After erasing tensor slot x0, define the gauge-first isometry

    W : C4_gauge tensor H5 -> C4 tensor C4 tensor C4

by

    W[(x1,x2,x3),(a,i)] = 2/sqrt(|S_i|)

when (a,x1,x2,x3) belongs to S_i, and zero otherwise.  In integer row and
column indices this is

    row = 16*x1 + 4*x2 + x3,
    column = 5*a + i.

The checker reconstructs all 64 nonzero entries.  Exact algebra gives

    W* W = I20,
    Pi20 = W W*,
    rank(Pi20) = 20,
    Pi20^2 = Pi20.

Thus the actual decoder on its physical carrier is U_dec=W*.  The earlier
original checker uses columns 4*i+a, hence H5 tensor C4 order.  The present
columns 5*a+i are the requested C4 tensor H5 order and differ only by the
explicit factor-swap permutation.  The complete sparse W and U_dec matrices
are stored in native_erasure_readout_results.json.

This exactly reuses the original constructor in
experiments/theory-contracts/joint-native-algebra-selection-20261016/

2. Six effects on H5 and H20
----------------------------

In the unnormalised original word basis the invariant metric and six rays are

    G = diag(1,3,3,3,6),
    q_r^T G q_r = 10,
    E5_native,r = q_r q_r^T G / 12.

The normalized original quartic code basis is obtained with

    D = diag(1,sqrt(3),sqrt(3),sqrt(3),sqrt(6)).

Consequently the orthonormal H5 effects are

    E5_r = D E5_native,r D^-1
         = (D q_r)(D q_r)^T / 12.

They obey

    sum_r E5_r = I5,
    rank(E5_r) = 1,
    E5_r^2 = (5/6) E5_r.

The requested twenty-dimensional effects are the explicit matrices

    E20_r = I4 tensor E5_r.

Therefore

    sum_r E20_r = I20,
    rank(E20_r) = 4.

On the actual three-register physical carrier they are

    Ephys_r = W E20_r W*.

All six 20 by 20 matrices and all six sparse 64 by 64 physical matrices are
stored in the result JSON.  Their physical sum is Pi20, which is the identity
on H20 inside the ambient C64.


3. Exact probabilities on the original erasure output
------------------------------------------------------

For an arbitrary symbolic 5 by 5 matrix rho5, define the original erasure
state in the physical three-register space by

    rho_out = W (I4/4 tensor rho5) W*.

Using W*W=I20 gives, for every r,

    Tr(rho_out Ephys_r)
      = Tr[(I4/4 tensor rho5)(I4 tensor E5_r)]
      = Tr(rho5 E5_r).

The checker verifies this as a polynomial identity in all 25 symbolic entries
of rho5 for each of the six outcomes.  Positivity or unit trace is not needed
for the algebraic check, so the result covers every density matrix by
specialisation.


4. Actual native action on all twenty output directions
-------------------------------------------------------

For each of the 60 original Gaussian reflections, let R4 be its exact native
four-dimensional matrix and C5 its action on the normalized original quartic
code.  The checker verifies the full 64 by 20 identity

    R4^tensor3 W = W [conjugate(R4) tensor C5].

Thus the gauge-first twenty-dimensional action is the actual matrix

    rho20(g) = conjugate(R4(g)) tensor C5(g).

It is not inferred from matching dimensions.  It is checked entry by entry
against the explicit W for all 60 reflections.  The six effects obey

    rho20(g) E20_r rho20(g)* = E20_{g.r}

for all 60 times 6 cases.  The result JSON records every induced permutation,

The C5 matrices are independently reconstructed from the fourth tensor power

5. The 120-word stabilizer average
----------------------------------

The 60 native reflections induce the 15 transpositions of the six ovoids,
each four times.  Selecting the first native representative for each distinct
transposition gives 15 actual reflection generators.  Exact breadth-first
closure produces all 720 elements of S6 together with a word in those native
reflection indices.

For each ray r, its stabilizer has exactly 120 elements.  Averaging their
original H5 matrices in the native word basis gives

    (1/120) sum_{g in Stab_S6(r)} C5_native(g)
       = q_r q_r^T G / 10
       = (6/5) E5_native,r.

After the fixed D conversion this is precisely

    P_r = (6/5) E5_r.

So P_r is the invariant projector onto the one-dimensional fixed space of the
actual S5 stabilizer.  This is the representation-theoretic normalization of
the doily effect, not merely a rank or trace coincidence.  The JSON contains
all six lists of 120 group words.  Each word is a list of original native
reflection indices; the certificates found with the 15 transposition
generators have maximum length four.

----------------------------
