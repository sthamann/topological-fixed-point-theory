1. The four exact shifted-lattice ground spaces

In the Li Delta-operator convention sigma_h=exp(-2 pi i h(0)), kappa uses
h=-v. Hence the kappa^a-twisted conformal Hamiltonian on a charge beta is

    L^(a)(0)=L(0)-a v(0)+a^2 (v,v)/2,
    h_a(beta)=|beta-a v|^2/2.

The checker enumerates the actual doubled-coordinate E8 condition: all eight
coordinates have one parity and their sum is divisible by4. It obtains

    a   min |beta-a v|^2   ground h   multiplicity   D5 x A3
    0          0               0           1          (1,1)
    1          3/4             3/8         4          (1,4)
    2          1               1/2        16          (10,1)+(1,6)
    3          3/4             3/8         4          (1,bar4).

For a=1 the four shifted vectors have zero first five coordinates and last
three coordinates

    (1/2,1/2,1/2), (1/2,-1/2,-1/2),
    (-1/2,1/2,-1/2), (-1/2,-1/2,1/2).

The existing D3-to-A3 isometry T maps them exactly to the four permutations
of (3/4,-1/4,-1/4,-1/4), the weights of the A3 fundamental. For a=3 the
opposite parity gives the antifundamental. For a=2 the ten doubled vectors
supported in the first five coordinates are the D5 vector weights and the six
supported in the last three are the D3=A3 vector weights. Thus the middle
ground space is exactly (10,1)+(1,6), not a dimension-only match.

This also identifies the 5/8+3/8 glue arithmetic. The unshifted glue state has
the D5 half-spinor contribution 5/8 and A3 fundamental contribution3/8.
Kappa spectral flow subtracts the D5 charge v and leaves the A3 fundamental
as the four-dimensional twisted ground space of weight3/8.

2. Native oscillator and inner-shift characters

For J with four +i and four -i Heisenberg eigenvectors, the normalized twisted
oscillator character is

  4 q^(1/24) product_(n>=0)
      (1-q^(n+1/4))^-4 (1-q^(n+3/4))^-4.              (2.1)

For the inner-conjugate kappa description it is

  Theta_(E8-v)(q) / eta(q)^8.                          (2.2)

With x=q^(1/4), after removing their common factor x^(1/6), the checker
computes both sides independently through x^24. Every integer coefficient is
equal. This finite coefficient certificate is a check of the exact character
identity supplied by the already proved conformal-vector-preserving inner
conjugacy; it is not used to infer that conjugacy from finitely many terms.

3. Three different phase statements

The twisted ground rotation without the Casimir shift is

    exp(2 pi i rho),  rho=3/8,

and has order8. With c=8, the normalized top T phase is

    exp(2 pi i (3/8-8/24))=exp(2 pi i/24)=zeta_24.

All homogeneous weights are3/8+k/4. Their normalized phases have exponents

    1/24, 7/24, 13/24, 19/24,

and all have exact order24. T^4 is the common scalar zeta_6. The uninserted
twisted trace is not by itself a zeta_24 eigenfunction: modular T changes the
insertion (kappa,1) to (kappa,kappa). Diagonal phase statements apply to
homogeneous states or normalized fixed-point-subalgebra summands.

The cyclic orbifold type is

    4{16 rho mod4}=4{2}.

For n=4 and type t=2, the discriminant-form level is
N=n^2/gcd(t,n)=8. Since c=8 is not divisible by24, the cyclic-orbifold trace
theorem gives congruence level lcm(3,8)=24. This is the precise mathematical
level24 statement. It is not a new cyclic group of order24.
