DOILY QUARTIC BRIDGE
====================

Statement
---------

Let L be the Construction A E8 lattice used by the original compiler and let
J be its fixed integral complex structure.  Put

    A = L/(1-J)L.

The Bakalov Kac commutator form makes A a four dimensional symplectic F2
space.  Its fifteen nonzero vectors and fifteen isotropic planes form the
doily W(3,2).  Let B be the 15 by 15 point line incidence matrix.

The original native quartic five is G31 equivariantly isomorphic to the
point side kernel ker(B^T).  This kernel is the centered permutation module
on the six ovoids.  It is not the centered module on the six spreads, and no
sign twist of either module works.

This is an exact finite representation theorem.  It does not identify the
native four with the J twisted ground space, does not identify conformal
states, and does not identify this five with a P2 or D5 carrier by dimension.

Exact construction
------------------

The checker imports only the tracked constructors

    tfpt_explorer/process.py
    tfpt_explorer/twisted_source.py

and reconstructs every further object.

The deterministic Darboux order is (e1,f1,e2,f2).  The JSON certificate lists
all fifteen points, all fifteen lines, all six ovoids, all six spreads, the
five quartic word supports, and every one of the sixty roots and action
matrices.  Therefore the displayed intertwiner has no hidden relabeling.

For an ovoid O define the point vector

    v_O = 3 1_O - 1.

The six such vectors obey

    B^T v_O = 0,
    sum_O v_O = 0,
    (v_O,v_O') = 36 delta_O,O' - 6.

They have rank five and span ker(B^T).  The incidence calculation is

    rank(B) = 10,
    spectrum(B B^T) = 9^1, 4^9, 0^5.

Thus the orthogonal projector onto the ovoid five is both

    P5 = (1/36) sum_O v_O v_O^T

and the incidence polynomial

    P5 = ((B B^T - 9 I)(B B^T - 4 I))/36.

All sixty marked reflections
-----------------------------

For every Gaussian ray z from process._hamming_rays(), the checker converts z
back to its actual eight dimensional E8 root.  twisted_source then computes
the induced action on A.  Every action is a symplectic transvection.

On the six ovoids every root reflection is one transposition.  Hence its
centered five dimensional trace is 3.  On the six spreads it is a product of
three transpositions and the centered trace is -1.

The native quartic action is rebuilt without importing another checker.  If
r is the native four dimensional reflection, the checker applies (2r)^tensor4
to the five unnormalised equal coefficient word sums.  The result is an
integral numerator N_r, with quartic action N_r/16.  For all sixty roots,

    N_r^2 = 256 I,
    tr(N_r/16) = 3.

Solving all simultaneous intertwiner equations over Q gives

    candidate              constraint rank   Hom dimension
    ovoid standard               24                 1
    ovoid sign twist             25                 0
    spread standard              25                 0
    spread sign twist            25                 0.

In the centered ovoid basis f_j=e_j-e_5 and the listed unnormalised quartic
word basis, the primitive generator of the one dimensional Hom space is

        [ 3  3  3  0  3]
        [-1  1  1  0 -1]
    T = [-1 -1  1  0  1]
        [-1  1 -1  0  1]
        [ 1  1  1  2  1].

Its determinant is -96.  The checker verifies separately for every root that

    N_r T = 16 T D_r,

where D_r is the centered ovoid permutation.  Since the sixty reflections
generate G31, this is the full marked equivariance statement.

The interface is precisely

    G31/N64 = Sp(4,2) = S6,

where

    N64 = Z4 central-product 2^(1+4).

Here 2^(1+4) is the extraspecial two-qubit Pauli group of order 32 and
its central involution is identified with the involution in Z4.  Thus N64
has order 64.  It is not a direct product of mu4 with a sixteen-element
Pauli label set; those sixteen labels describe the projective quotient.

Both A and the native quartic five kill the kernel.  The quartic five is the
standard S6 representation supplied by the six actual A ovoids.

Why the four dimensional identification remains false
------------------------------------------------------

The checker also reconstructs the native Clifford conjugation action on the
four Pauli labels.  Over F2 it solves

    X rho_A(g) = phi_native(g) X

for all sixty roots.  The Hom space has dimension zero, so there is no
invertible marked identification between A and the native Pauli label module.
They remain related by the S6 outer duality.

The independent spectral obstruction is retained as well.  For a twisted
ground transvection, the sixteen Clifford lifts have squared trace magnitude
8 twice and 0 fourteen times.  A native complex reflection has squared trace
magnitude 4.  All 60 times 16 comparisons therefore fail exactly.  The new
five dimensional theorem does not repair or reinterpret the failed native
four bridge.

What this supplies for state and selection
------------------------------------------

The ovoid construction does supply canonical representation level selection
data:

1. P5 is a basis independent rational projector selecting the unique point
   side incidence kernel.
2. The six operators

       E_O = v_O v_O^T / 36

   are positive rank one operators, are permuted by G31, and sum to P5.  They
   form a canonical six outcome covariant resolution on the selected channel.
3. The invariant positive form on the native quartic five is unique up to a
   positive scalar.  In the unnormalised word basis its primitive matrix is

       diag(1,3,3,3,6).

   This is also the form inherited from the tensor Hilbert space because the
   five word support sizes are 4,12,12,12,24.

Transporting the six centered ovoid rays through T gives the following six
primitive rays directly in the native quartic word basis:

    ( 1,-1,-1,-1, 0)
    ( 1, 1,-1, 1, 0)
    ( 1, 1, 1,-1, 0)
    (-2, 0, 0, 0, 1)
    ( 1,-1, 1, 1, 0)
    (-2, 0, 0, 0,-1).

Each has squared norm 10 for G=diag(1,3,3,3,6).  The native operators

    E_a = q_a q_a^T G / 12

are positive with respect to G, are permuted exactly by all sixty native
quartic actions, and sum to I_5.  Thus the bridge supplies a concrete
six-outcome covariant POVM on the original quartic five, not merely an
abstract dimension match.

The exact symmetric form calculation has dimension one.  Representation
theory therefore leaves only its overall positive scale.  The equivariant
intertwiner is likewise unique up to a nonzero scalar.  Requiring it to be an
isometry fixes the modulus, leaving a complex phase, or only a sign over R.

The full commutant is one dimensional, and there is no nonzero vector fixed
by all sixty reflections.  Consequently the
geometry does not select one pure state or one ovoid.  After choosing the
invariant form and trace normalization, the only fully invariant density
operator is the maximally mixed state I_V5/5.  Selecting one of the six rank
one effects needs an additional symmetry breaking datum, such as an anchored
quadratic refinement.  That datum is not supplied by A, J, and incidence
alone.
