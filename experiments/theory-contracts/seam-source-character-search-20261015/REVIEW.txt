INDEPENDENT REVIEW, 15 October 2026

Reviewed artifact: PROOF.txt for UR.SEAM.SOURCE_CHARACTER_SEARCH.01,
with a read-only replay of search.py and a fresh local Lean check.

VERDICT

The bounded lattice/character result is mathematically sound, and the proof
keeps the decisive physical boundary: it does not claim that P1 selects the
constructed order-four character. Registration should wait for two material
corrections below: the four successful representatives are labelled in the
wrong order in PROOF.txt, and the advertised executable replay currently
fails. Three smaller wording corrections would make the scope unambiguous.

This review does not promote the conditional E8 marking to a common physical
source, a P2 carrier identification, a state selection, or an event law.

1. CONFIRMED MATHEMATICS

1.1 Saturation and completeness

The saturation argument in section 3 is correct. For h=A d+B r in the E8
lattice, coordinates 2 and 5 exclude the half-integral coset; coordinates 7
and 8 give A=4k; coordinate 3 gives B=l-k/4; and even coordinate sum gives
k even. Hence

  V intersect L = Z lambda1 + Z r,
  lambda1=8d-r/2=(-4,0,0,-4,0,-2,-2,-2).

It follows that the order-dividing-four characters in the source plane are
exactly (u lambda1+v r)/4 for u,v modulo 4. The 16-candidate family is therefore
the full 4-torsion of this stipulated source torus, not a numerical cutoff.

Small notation correction: line 60 should say V/(V intersect L), not V/L,
because L is not a subgroup of the two-dimensional real vector space V.

1.2 Neutral-root moment

The root counts and moment reconstruction in section 4 are correct. For the
D5+A3 cases, the neutral-root moment has eigenvalue 16 on the D5 span and 8
on the orthogonal A3 span, so P5=(M0-8I)/8 is the rank-five orthogonal
projector. This recovers the subspace from the neutral roots and does not put
the desired coordinate projector into the candidate definition.

Material correction: with the representative order printed on lines 82-83,
the character association on line 84 is reversed. The correct association is

  (2,3)=O t,  (2,7)=t,  (6,1)=-O t,  (6,5)=-t.

Equivalently, retain the phrase "t, O t, -t, -O t" and reorder the displayed
representatives to (2,7),(2,3),(6,5),(6,1).

1.3 Full lattice/oscillator operator

Section 5 correctly evaluates the operator on the full stipulated lattice
module, including oscillator level. Since N+p.p/2 is integral for p in E8,

  exp(4 pi i K_a)

has torus vector 2d_a. The charge factor has vector -a q/4=-a r/2. Their
product therefore has vector

  t=2d_a-a r/2,

independent of a for the displayed d_a. Oscillator excitations acquire no
additional phase. This is a valid conditional operator identity in the given
lattice representation.

The limitation on lines 119-123 is necessary and correct: [u_Sigma]=1 is a
winding/index statement, not the operator identity identifying P1's seam
action with W_a. Positive clock orientation can select t over -t only after
that missing identification has been supplied. Thus m=2 does not follow from
P1 as presently recorded.

For precision, line 41 should call q/4 the torus character of the source
charge-quarter operator Z_q=exp(i pi Q/2) on the E8 extension. Calling it the
"physical full-circle quarter character" can conflate it with the separately
recorded fold clock and its a-dependent deformation, which section 2 otherwise
keeps correctly distinct.

1.4 Sheet transport and pullback to elementary fields

The exact covariance O t=t+r/2=t+q/4 is correct, as is the conclusion that the
sheet map transports the conditional marking to W_(1-a) Z_q rather than fixing
one rank-five marked subspace.

The M^T pullback in section 6 is also correct: each successful character gives
two copies of each phase 1,i,-1,-i on the eight original complex fermions, and
the neutral bilinear shell has 20 roots and semisimple rank 7. Because section
4 classifies nonorthogonality-connected components, line 145 should describe
these as

  A3 plus four A1 components, with the four A1 components groupable into two
  coordinate D2=A1+A1 blocks,

rather than simply "two D2 blocks". The latter is isomorphic as a root system
but is not the connected-component decomposition used by the classifier.
The conclusion remains correct: the elementary bilinears do not yield the
E8 neutral system D5+A3; the additional roots use the stipulated spinor
extension.

2. EXECUTABLE AND FORMAL CHECKS

The fresh Lean command succeeds with Lean 4.29.1. Its printed dependencies are
only the standard Mathlib quotient/classical axioms propext, Classical.choice,
and Quot.sound for the listed theorems; the check produced no error.

The result replay does not currently succeed:

  python3 search.py verify --output results.json
  ValueError: Result replay mismatch: source_generated_group

The recomputed values agree, but source_generated_group.order_counts is built
with integer dictionary keys, while JSON reloads those keys as strings. The
canonical encoder sorts integer keys numerically before serialization and
string keys lexicographically after reload, so the byte encodings differ
(notably 1,2,4,8,16 versus 1,16,2,4,8). Normalize these keys to strings before
hashing/comparison, or compare recursively normalized JSON values, then
regenerate results.json and its source manifest. Until that is done, the
executable receipt is not replay-clean even though this mismatch does not
change the mathematical enumeration.

3. BOUNDARY OF THE PROPOSED OPERATOR COVARIANCE

The proposed next datum U* B U=B+I is a useful exact target only when its
operator core and domain transport are part of the statement. By itself it
does not force B to be the scalar Fourier generator or make the source unique.

The smallest valid scalar-circle theorem needs all of the following extra
hypotheses: the rank-one Hilbert space L2(S1); U acting by multiplication by
exp(i theta); B a local selfadjoint first-order differential operator with
normalized principal symbol +1; and the appropriate periodic selfadjoint
domain. Then B has the form

  B=-i d/dtheta + A(theta),

with real scalar A. A periodic scalar gauge removes the nonconstant part of A,
leaving a constant equal to its holonomy average modulo an integer. Thus the
conclusion is gauge equivalence to a constant connection, not literal equality
or selection of its holonomy. With multiplicity, matrix-valued connections
retain nonabelian holonomy and the shift covariance is even less selective.

PROOF.txt currently avoids the stronger claim: lines 175-182 call the
covariance a missing operator target and explicitly require multiplicity and
domain control. That wording should remain. Deriving this datum from the actual
P1 collar/source, rather than appending it as an axiom, is still the first
source-selection obligation.

FINAL BOUND

After correcting the representative labels, the quotient/root-component
wording, and the replay normalization, the artifact supports the following
bounded statement: within the stipulated E8 lattice realization, the full
4-torsion of the source-generated two-torus has exactly four D5+A3 characters,
and the displayed time-charge composition realizes one inverse pair while the
sheet map exchanges the two rank-five placements. It does not establish that
the original P1/P2 source chooses that composition or supplies the remaining
field, state, and event map.

3. The new section 4a is a valid blind source-frame control in its declared

blind_source_classification() has no M argument, reads no file, and contains no

  (u,v)=(1,0),(1,2),(3,0),(3,2),

all with reconstructed projector rank five. calculate() calls this function
before reading the fold file or M. Only after both classifications have
finished does it apply M and compare M P_source M^T with the separately
reconstructed target-frame moment projector; all four comparisons pass.

The fixed coordinate P5 is likewise used only in that post-classification
audit, to record which transported placement equals the standard coordinate
choice. It does not affect candidate generation, root-type acceptance, or the
reconstructed projector. The control is therefore blind to the target frame
and target projector, though deliberately not blind to the stated abstract
success criterion D5+A3. PROOF.txt correctly retains the stipulated E8 source
extension as an input and does not present this control as its derivation.
