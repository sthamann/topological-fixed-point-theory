1. Target and result type

The target is the actual common P1/P2 source, its charged field action,
state and time, with quarter phase retained. The preceding E8-character
classification is complete in its scope and is not repeated here.
We examine the first inference from a seam winding CLASS to an actual
operator acting on the same Calderon data. A constructive reconstruction
theorem is proved under explicit operator hypotheses, and its first tests
are automated in a rational-symbol class. Those hypotheses are not derived
from P1/P2. No full physical source or event law is claimed.

The rational diagnostic class assumes scalar Hardy data and CAR when fields
are discussed. It is not a classification or countermodel of every admissible
TFPT bordism. The abstract reconstruction in section 5 does not initially
assume a circle; that representation follows from its stronger hypotheses.

3. The first operator identity, before any spectrum fitting

Let C be an orthogonal projection, U a unitary on the same Hilbert space,
and S=C U C restricted to C H. Direct multiplication gives

 C-S* S = C U* (1-C) U C = ((1-C) U C)* ((1-C) U C).

Thus S is an isometry precisely when (1-C) U C=0. This says that the
actual seam action leaves the positive Cauchy-data subspace invariant.
It is a relation between two operators, stronger than a winding number.
Lean proves the algebraic corner identity and the equivalence S*S=C iff
the leakage expression vanishes. Hilbert positivity and the norm-zero
implication are the elementary analytic argument above, not Lean output.

Fredholm index -1 determines dim ker S - dim ker S* = -1 only. Once S is
known to be an isometry, it implies dim ker S*=1. It does not itself prove
isometry or exclude a unitary summand in the Wold decomposition.

4. Exact diagnostic and full continuous scalar-symbol classification

Use periodic trivialization H=L2(S1), B_alpha=N+alpha, N=-i d/dtheta,
alpha=1/4 or 3/4, and C e_n=e_n for n>=0, zero for n<0. This is equivalent
to the usual alpha-twisted boundary domain. The quarter transition is
exp(2pi i B_alpha)=exp(2pi i alpha) I and is kept throughout.

For |a|<1 write b_a(z)=(z-a)/(1-conj(a)z). A finite rational unimodular
symbol is a phase times a quotient of finite products of these factors.
Cancel common factors first. Its winding is numerator degree minus
denominator degree. For S=C M_u C, zero leakage implies M_u 1=u belongs to
H2. Conversely analyticity and boundedness of u preserve H2 and, since
|u|=1, multiplication is an isometry there. A noncancelled denominator
factor has a pole in the disk. Hence in this rational class:

 zero leakage <=> no denominator after cancellation.

Together with winding one this forces exactly ONE Blaschke factor, times
a constant phase. This classifies the full finite-rational-symbol family,
not just a finite root grid. Real rational roots in the executable are
exact diagnostics; the argument also covers complex roots in the disk.

In fact this classification extends to EVERY continuous scalar unimodular
symbol u on the circle. Zero leakage implies that its negative Fourier
coefficients vanish. Analytic Fejer sums then converge uniformly to u,
so u extends to the disk algebra A(D). Since |u|=1 on the boundary,
continuity gives a zero-free collar of that boundary; all zeros are in a
compact interior subset and there are finitely many. Divide out their
Blaschke factors. The quotient v and 1/v are both in A(D), with unit
boundary modulus. The maximum principle applied to each forces |v|=1
inside; hence v is constant. By the argument principle, winding one gives
one Blaschke factor. This is an analytic theorem about the whole scalar
Hardy class; a finite executable grid is only its diagnostic check.

An exact orientation-preserving control is u_a=z^2/b_a for real 0<a<1/3.
It is smooth, unimodular and has winding one. Its phase derivative is
2-(1-a^2)/(1-2a cos(theta)+a^2), with positive lower bound
2-(1+a)/(1-a). At a=1/4 that bound is 1/3.
For m>=1 its negative Laurent coefficient is

 [z^(-m)]u_a=(1-a^2) a^(m+1).

Consequently ||(1-C)u_a e_0||^2=a^4(1-a^2), exactly 15/4096 at a=1/4.
The same quarter domain, C and B are retained. Therefore even a smooth,
strictly oriented winding-one representative need not satisfy the first
operator condition. This tests the inference winding -> isometry; it is
not a demonstration of all TFPT postulates in this diagnostic source.

For a single b_a, isometry holds and its defect is one-dimensional with
normalized vector k_a(z)=sqrt(1-|a|^2)/(1-conj(a)z). Indeed b_a H2 is the
kernel of evaluation at a, whose orthogonal complement is span{k_a}.
Purity follows because a function in every b_a^n H2 has a zero of every
order at a and is identically zero.

Nevertheless for the GIVEN original B_alpha=N+alpha,

 M_ba* B_alpha M_ba = B_alpha + (1-|a|^2)/|e^(i theta)-a|^2.

The increment is the constant one only when a=0. A matching Wold shift
does not identify its reconstructed time with the supplied source time.

5. Constructive reconstruction without initially inserting a circle

Assume all of the following about the ACTUAL source pair on one Hilbert space:
(i) C orthogonal, U unitary and (1-C) U C=0;
(ii) S=U|CH is Fredholm with index -1;
(iii) S is pure: intersection_n S^n CH={0};
(iv) U is its minimal unitary dilation, span{U^n CH:n in Z} dense in H.

Set W=CH orthogonally minus U CH. By (i),(ii), dim W=1. Wold decomposition gives
CH=orthogonal_sum_(n>=0) U^n W. Minimality supplies the negative powers,
so H=orthogonal_sum_(n in Z) U^n W. Choosing a unit vector w in W gives
e_n=U^n w. Only a common scalar phase depends on this choice. Therefore
(C,U) is unitarily equivalent to (Hardy projection, bilateral shift).
This derivation uses Wold, not an assumed equality of dimensions.

Now require the ACTUAL selfadjoint B on the common invariant finite-span
core to satisfy U* B U=B+1 and B w=alpha w. These domain statements are
part of the premise, not inferred from bounded matrices. Iteration in both
directions gives B e_n=(n+alpha)e_n. The diagonal operator is essentially
selfadjoint on this core, so its selfadjoint closure is fixed. Thus

 B=N+alpha, C=1_[0,infinity)(B), exp(2pi i B)=exp(2pi i alpha) I.

Lean certifies the integer-index recurrence at the scalar level. The
infinite-domain and spectral statements are analytic parts of this proof.
The positive spectral projection follows for 0<alpha<1; it is not a
separate choice after these hypotheses.

For the diagnostic b_a family the bilateral basis is k_a b_a^n, n in Z.
It is complete and orthonormal: change variables by the circle diffeomorphism
b_a, with |k_a|^2 equal to its positive phase derivative. Its nonnegative
span is H2, so the resulting unitary F_a commutes with C. The operator
B_a=F_a(N+alpha)F_a* has the required covariance and quarter transition.
For a!=0 it is not the supplied B_alpha in section 4. Replacing B_alpha
by B_a proves a conditional reconstruction, not an original-source match.

6. Actual conditional field, sea-state and time action

If the physical field algebra is CAR(H), unitarity of the derived map
F:delta_n -> U^n w from the reference orthonormal basis induces its CAR
isomorphism: products, adjoints and all
anticommutators are preserved, not just spectra. CAR is an additional
field-statistics premise; neither P1 reflection positivity nor Wold alone
implies it. With negative sea occupation Q_occ=1-C, the pure gauge-invariant
quasifree state is fixed by omega(c_m* c_n)=delta_mn 1_(n<0) and Wick's rule.
The physical identification with this quasifree state is also a premise.
Do not use the symbol Q_occ for the relative charge operator below.

On the algebraic finite-excitation sea core define

 Q = sum_n :c_n* c_n:,
 H_alpha = sum_n (n+alpha) :c_n* c_n:.

Each particle at n>=0 costs n+alpha; each hole at n<0 costs -(n+alpha).
Thus H_alpha is positive with a unique zero-energy sea vector. Its closure
is the positive free-Fock excitation Hamiltonian. The field time satisfies
exp(i t H_alpha)c_n exp(-i t H_alpha)=exp(-i t(n+alpha)) c_n.

The unit winding action is beta_U(c_n)=c_(n+1), not a four-periodic mode
permutation. Its change of polarization has rank one. In the charged sea
Fock representation it is implementable, up to scalar phase, by a unitary R,
with convention beta_U(A)=R A R*: R c_n R*=c_(n+1), equivalently
R* c_n R=c_(n-1).
Equivalently R shifts each occupation configuration, including the whole sea;
the CAR ordering fixes relative fermionic signs. Hence on the common core

 R* Q R=Q+1,       R* H_alpha R=H_alpha+Q+alpha.

The additive constant is the newly occupied n=0 sea mode. In particular
R Omega is a charge-one state of energy alpha; R^(-1) Omega has charge -1
and energy 1-alpha. Generally the charge-k shifted sea has energy
k(k-1)/2+alpha k, for every integer k. These formulas follow by summing
the finite occupation differences and hold beyond any numerical cutoff.

On every finite-excitation vector H_alpha-alpha Q is an integer. Therefore
exp(2pi i H_alpha)=exp(2pi i alpha Q): the quarter phase survives on charged
fields and states. The charged winding R, the representation equivalence F,
and the quarter transition are three distinct operators. R changes the sea
charge; requiring it to preserve the vacuum would contradict this unit flow.
No event rate, stochastic instrument or preferred measurement is inferred
from the existence of R.

The rank-one wandering space counts a primitive charged shift channel.
It is not the five-slot P2 interface, a spacetime dimension, or a D5
spinor. Mapping this reconstructed channel to the actual P2 carrier and
the earlier E8 extension remains a separate physical identification.

6a. A same-source variational selection, without changing B

Nonzero leakage does NOT make a seam transformation physically invalid.
It prevents the pure-isometric Wold route just examined. In the given CAR
source any unitary multiplication U=M_u still defines the quasifree state
with occupation U Q_occ U*. For smooth u its off-diagonal polarization
blocks are Hilbert-Schmidt, so it is also implementable in the sea Fock
representation. Thus non-inner candidates must not be discarded as impossible
physics merely because they fail the isometry test.

There is a stronger constructive comparison using the SAME B_alpha and
the same original sea representation and reference vector Omega. The
transformed state is R_u Omega with covariance U Q_occ U*, not Omega.
Let u(theta)=exp(i phi(theta)) have winding w and
square-integrable phase derivative p=phi'. Write u_k for its Fourier
coefficients. The transformed diagonal occupation is

 occupation_u(n)=sum_(m<0) |u_(n-m)|^2.

Subtract the original sea occupation. A single Fourier shift k has charge
k and relative energy E_k=k(k-1)/2+alpha k by section 6. Therefore

 <Q>_u=sum_k k |u_k|^2=w,
 <H_alpha>_u=sum_k [k(k-1)/2+alpha k] |u_k|^2
            =1/2 <p^2> + (alpha-1/2) w,

where <.> denotes normalized integration over the circle. These identities
follow directly from the CAR covariance, not from an inserted cost function.
The charge sum is absolutely convergent when sum k^2|u_k|^2 is finite.
For energy, all E_k are nonnegative for 0<alpha<1, allowing the positive
particle/hole sums to be interchanged; the same H1 condition gives finiteness.
Parseval and u* (-i partial_theta)u=p give the displayed integral expressions.
For H1 u the implementability condition is also visible explicitly:
||[C,U]||_HS^2=sum_k |k| |u_k|^2 < infinity.

At w=1 this reduces to the exact nonnegative remainder

 <H_alpha>_u = alpha + 1/2 <(p-1)^2>.

Equality holds precisely when p=1 almost everywhere, hence
u=e^(i chi) z. Thus the original time and sea select a UNIQUE lowest-energy
representative of winding one, up to scalar phase, among all smooth scalar
symbols (and the stated H1 class). This uniqueness is within scalar
multiplication operators, not the full restricted unitary group: additional
block-diagonal vacuum symmetries can produce the same minimal state energy.
This is stronger than selecting a winner
among the finite diagnostic grid and does not change B to fit U.

For real Blaschke parameters, <P_a P_b>=(1+ab)/(1-ab), from the absolutely
convergent Fourier series P_a=sum_(k in Z) a^|k| exp(i k theta).
Hence the program computes <p^2> exactly for every signed factor list.
Both b_a and z^2/b_a have energy excess a^2/(1-a^2). At a=1/4 it is 1/15,
and their alpha=1/4 energy is 19/60, compared with 1/4 for z. They differ
in leakage despite having the same energy. A noncentered Blaschke isometry
creates a charge-one wavepacket, not the lowest original-time charge-one
state; choosing its adapted B_a would hide this excess.

The variational THEOREM is established in the given source. The SELECTION
RULE that the actual P1 seam must minimize this charge-one excitation energy
is not stated or derived in the original postulates. Moreover the theorem
does not select the original B, field algebra or state used to define that
energy. Jointly conjugating (U,B,C) preserves the comparison; minimizing
inside one chosen source is not a proof that it is the physical source.

7. What the automation establishes and does not establish

The program classifies and compares operator representatives, reconstructs
the conditional source, and checks exact charge/state/time identities. It
must distinguish a given B from B newly conjugated to fit U. It records
unproved provenance obligations, rather than granting them from passing
operator diagnostics or a user-supplied Boolean. The original source remains
unidentified until these obligations are derived on the same actual fields.

The complete continuous scalar-symbol result removes an infinite
representative search: admissible inner degree-one symbols are one
Wold-equivalence class.
For a fixed original D_alpha, covariance selects its centered representative.
Neither fact proves that the original TFPT Calderon pair belongs to this
class. On the Wold route the first original-source obligation is the leakage
identity, followed by pure/minimal generation, actual B covariance and the
field/state premises. The variational route in section 6a does not require
zero leakage: it compares all stated scalar symbols in one fixed source.
Its additional selection obligation is to derive the minimum-energy rule
from P1, as well as the original field, state and time used in that rule.
The finite diagnostic grid is not advertised as exhaustion of P1/P2 sources.

The original minimal-defect proof must therefore be supplemented by a
classification that separates the actual operator tuples in its minimal
fiber. Existence of a minimal integer vector cannot substitute for that work.
