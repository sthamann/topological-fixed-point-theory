3. Analytic completeness of the candidate family

Use the connected source torus with Lie algebra V=span_R{d,r}, d=d_(1/4).
It is a two-dimensional rational plane, not an arbitrary E8 Cartan search.
For h=A d+B r, coordinates 2 and 5 vanish. If h belongs to L, it cannot
be in the half-integer coset. Integrality of coordinates 7 and 8 forces
A=4k, k integer. Coordinate 3 then forces B=l-k/4, l integer. Coordinate
sum is 2l-7k, whose evenness forces k=2s. Conversely these conditions suffice.
Thus the full lattice in the plane is

 V intersect L = Z lambda1 + Z r,
 lambda1=8d-r/2=(-4,0,0,-4,0,-2,-2,-2).

Consequently every character in V/(V intersect L) of order dividing 4 is uniquely

 h_(u,v)=(u lambda1+v r)/4 mod L,  u,v in {0,1,2,3}.

This derives a complete 16-candidate search from a continuous family.
Twelve have exact order 4. It is not a finite parameter cutoff.
For comparison the finite group generated by d and r/8 has 64 elements,
isomorphic to Z16 x Z4; 8d-4(r/8)=lambda1 is its nontrivial short relation.
All its 4-torsion is exactly the set above. Enumerating both ways provides
an independent completeness check, rather than a second physical assumption.

to an existing candidate to make it pass.
