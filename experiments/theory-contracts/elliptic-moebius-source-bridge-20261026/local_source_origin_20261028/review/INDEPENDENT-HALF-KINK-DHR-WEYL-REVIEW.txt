1. Exact positive result
------------------------

On the stipulated quotient net

    A_8(I) = CAR(L^2(I,C^8))^even

the smooth half kink is a genuine localized automorphism.  Let a_I be a
smooth real phase which is 0 on one component of I' and 2 pi on the other,
and let

    g_I = exp(i a_I/2)

act simultaneously in the eight complex fermion planes.  Outside I this is
+1 on one component and -1 on the other.  The sign -1 acts as fermion parity,
hence trivially on the even algebra.  Changing the square root branch changes
g_I globally by -1 and therefore gives the same automorphism of A_8.  This is
exactly the odd pseudolocalization mechanism used by Böckenhauer for the even
selfdual CAR net.

The sector label requires the stipulated D8 bosonization chart in addition to
odd pseudolocality.  In that chart the charge is

    s = q/2 = (1/2,...,1/2),
    s^2 = 2,
    h_s = s^2/2 = 1.

Thus the report has a correctly typed spinor sector once the fixed q and its
chirality convention are retained.  Böckenhauer's theorem by itself proves the
specific spinor endomorphism constructed from his odd pseudolocalized
Bogoliubov operator.  Odd pseudolocality alone does not choose between the two
spinor labels.  Here that remaining choice is supplied by the explicit D8
charge chart, not by a new fermion gauge interaction.


2. Current normalization, Weyl line, and cocycle
------------------------------------------------

The folded current definition is consistent:

    J_tot,n = J_cover,8n,
    [J_tot,n,J_tot,m] = 8 n delta_(n+m,0).

Multiplying the diagonal current charge by 1/2 gives effective squared charge

    8 (1/2)^2 = 2,

which is the same s^2 as above.  For two smooth kinks the difference

    b_IJ = (a_I-a_J)/2

has a pullback b_F with modes only at multiples of 8.  If

    sum_(n>0) 8 n |b_n|^2 < infinity,

then W(-b_F) is a bounded Weyl unitary in the given current Fock
representation and intertwines the two localized automorphisms.  This is a
real transporter, not a formal exponential.

The cocycle factor is also correct.  The level 8 diagonal current combined
with the half coefficient produces the factor 2 in the symplectic form.  It
therefore reproduces the charge s cocycle rather than the level 8 cocycle
without the half factor.

The zero mode must remain part of the Weyl operator.  It commutes with the
source Hamiltonian and creates no oscillator time phase.  It is invisible in
the displayed charge zero Hall kernel matrix elements, but it still acts on
charged fields.  The reviewed report does not need to set it to zero and
should not do so in a later compression.

The strongest algebra statement directly established by these sources is:

* W(-b_F) is a bounded implementer in the source current representation;
* its adjoint action is localized on the quotient net;
* in the Haag dual vacuum net, Böckenhauer's argument places the transporter
  in the corresponding local von Neumann algebra.

Calling it an element of the norm quasilocal even C star algebra would require
a separate norm inclusion argument.  The current kernel alone proves the
bounded representation operator and localized adjoint action, which is enough
for the DHR transporter statement used here.

5. Material correction: the sharp bilocal
------------------------------------------

For a sharp step, the Fourier coefficients behave as 1/n, hence

    sum_(n>0) n |b_n|^2

diverges logarithmically.  Consequently there is no bounded Weyl operator
limit.  The reviewed report states this correctly.  The same calculation,
together with the level 8 and half charge, fixes the standard current vertex
short distance exponent 2.

Those facts do not yet prove the stronger sentences in
CLOSED-HALF-KINK-SOURCE-WEYL-LINE.txt which assert an unqualified unique
renormalized form limit on the full finite energy domain and a closable
smeared bilocal operator.  The original ordered current contract proves exact
matrix elements for smooth Weyl operators and controls the already defined
native normal ordered products.  It explicitly does not establish arbitrary
new coincident composite distributions.  Böckenhauer's localized
endomorphism theorem also does not supply the missing vertex operator energy
bounds.

What is established from the present sources is the narrower statement:

1. smooth approximants have an exact Gaussian self energy divergence;
2. after removing that scalar divergence, their matrix elements between the
   controlled finite degree source states have the standard vertex limit;
3. the exponent, cocycle, i0 ordering, and relative normalization convention
   are fixed by the current kernel and the condition T_II=1;
4. no bounded sharp Weyl transporter exists.

To promote this to a unique closable operator valued distribution one still
needs either a cited lattice or current VOA vertex operator construction with
the relevant energy bounds, or a direct proof of smoothing independence,
dense domain invariance, and closability in this representation.  Until then,
the sharp bilocal paragraph should be marked conditional or restricted to the
controlled renormalized matrix elements.  This correction does not weaken the
smooth DHR transporter theorem.
