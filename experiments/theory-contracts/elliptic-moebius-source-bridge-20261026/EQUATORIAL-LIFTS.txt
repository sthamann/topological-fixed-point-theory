Question
========

The previous elliptic calculation used the eight anti-holomorphic maps

  h^a Q^b kappa,

on

  E: y^2=x^4-1,
  h(x,y)=(x,-y),  Q(x,y)=(i x,y),
  kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y).

It found the dividing pair hQkappa and hQ^3kappa. The original P1 source,
however, also contains the mark-fixing base mirror x -> 1/x and the pointwise
fixed equatorial Real form x -> 1/bar x. The decisive question is whether
their lifts add a missing original seam geometry, and whether the same
Q-invariant spin line is compatible with it.

Verdict
=======

[E] There are exactly two deck-related lifts of the equatorial base Real
form:

  j_+(x,y)=(1/bar x, +i bar y/bar x^2),
  j_-(x,y)=(1/bar x, -i bar y/bar x^2)=h j_+(x,y).       (1)

Both square to one and both have two ovals. Their quotients are annuli. The
quarter Q exchanges them. They arise without a new cover or a chosen Real
form: if

  S(x,y)=(1/x, i y/x^2),                                (2)

then S^2=h and

  j_+=S kappa,  j_-=hS kappa.                           (3)

[E] Adding S completes the old base D4 action on the concrete curve. The
sixteen anti-holomorphic lifts split exactly into the two old v510/v519 base
families:

  h^a Q^b kappa       -> x |-> i^b bar x       family D,
  h^a Q^b S kappa     -> x |-> i^b/bar x       family A. (4)

Among these sixteen maps, twelve are strict Real involutions and four have
order four. The previous eight-map census was correct inside <h,Q,kappa>,
but it was not the full marked D4 lift census. In the full census there are
two different dividing orbits: the old OS midpoint pair
{hQkappa,hQ^3kappa}, and the new equatorial pair {Skappa,hSkappa}.

[E] This does not identify the two slots. v519 says explicitly that family A
defines the Euclidean seam section and that its equatorial member fixes the
seam pointwise, with no OS half-space decomposition. Family D supplies the
OS reflection and inverts the clock. Thus the old pair remains the correct
lift of the two mark-swapping family-D midpoint axes. The new pair supplies
the missing lift of the pointwise fixed family-A equator.

[E] The smallest source-identification test produces a real obstruction to
calling one new oval the original full seam. If Gamma is the unit circle,
which contains all four mu4 branch marks, then

  p^{-1}(Gamma)=Fix(j_+) union Fix(j_-),  p(x,y)=x.       (5)

In the square uniformization this is two horizontal plus two vertical
circles. They meet at the four ramification points. The union is a connected
graph with V=4, E=8 and b1=5; its complement consists of four disks. Each
individual oval maps two-to-one only onto one alternating quarter arc of
Gamma. Therefore the original full pointwise fixed seam does not pull back
to one ordinary boundary circle or to the boundary of one of the two
annuli.

[E] As an unlinearized complex flat line class, the existing Q-fixed
nontrivial elliptic spin class alpha is invariant under the full marked lift
group. On each of the four equatorial ovals its restriction is NS. At the
four-valent graph vertices, however, a one
dimensional boundary Dirac operator needs additional vertex matching data.
The sources checked here provide no such matching rule and no unitary taking
the original circle kernel to this graph.

[O] The exact remaining source step is consequently smaller and sharper
than before: identify whether the original operational seed uses the base
circle Gamma itself, a chosen family-D OS cut acting on Gamma, or a specified
smooth push-off of p^{-1}(Gamma), and supply its boundary trace and state
transport. The curve and its spin line now carry both required Real-form
slots. They do not by themselves choose the smooth carrier on which the
original quarter Hardy or NS kernel acts.

2. Exact lift and group calculation
====================================

2.1 The holomorphic mirror lift
--------------------------------

For S in (2),

  (i y/x^2)^2=(1/x)^4-1

after using y^2=x^4-1. Hence S preserves E. A second application gives

  S^2(x,y)=(x,-y)=h(x,y).                               (8)

Thus S has order four, while its base map x -> 1/x has order two. The two
possible lifts of that base map are S and hS.

Together with h and Q,

  h^2=1,  Q^4=1,  S^2=h,
  h central,
  SQS^-1=hQ^-1.                                         (9)

The holomorphic marked lift group therefore has sixteen elements

  h^a Q^b S^c,   a,c in Z2, b in Z4.                   (10)

2.2 The equatorial anti-holomorphic lifts
------------------------------------------

For x'=1/bar x, the conjugate curve equation gives

  x'^4-1=-(bar y)^2/(bar x)^4.

Therefore y' must equal either sign of i bar y/bar x^2. This proves both
existence and completeness of (1). Direct composition gives

  j_+^2=j_-^2=1,       Qj_+Q^-1=j_-.                   (11)

Their fixed sets can be found without uniformization. Put

  x=e^(i theta),  y=e^(i theta) w.

Then the curve gives

  w^2=2i sin(2 theta).                                  (12)

For j_+, fixedness says w=i bar w, hence sin(2 theta)>=0. The two intervals
[0,pi/2] and [pi,3pi/2], with the two sheets joined at the branch endpoints,
give two circles. For j_-, fixedness says w=-i bar w, hence
sin(2 theta)<=0, and the complementary two intervals give two circles.
Both Real curves are therefore dividing and their quotients are annuli.

2.3 Full anti-holomorphic lift census
--------------------------------------

Conjugation satisfies

  kappa Q kappa=Q^-1,       kappa S kappa=S^-1.         (13)

The anti-holomorphic coset of the full marked lift group has sixteen maps.
The first eight are the old family-D lifts

  d_(a,b)=h^a Q^b kappa.

All eight square to one. Their fixed-component census remains

  (1,0,1,0; 1,2,1,2),                                  (14)

with the semicolon separating a=0 and a=1.

The other eight are the family-A lifts

  a_(a,b)=h^a Q^b S kappa.

Their exact squares are

  a_(a,b)^2=h^b Q^(2b).                                 (15)

Consequently b=0,2 gives four further Real involutions. The two b=0 maps
are j_+ and j_- and each has two ovals. The two b=2 maps project to
x -> -1/bar x and are fixed-point free. For b=1,3 the square is hQ^2,
so the four maps have order four and no fixed points. This proves the count

  12 anti-holomorphic involutions + 4 order-four maps.  (16)

It also reproduces v519's family-A square dichotomy on the actual curve:
mu=+1 is equatorial, mu=-1 is fixed-point free, and mu=+-i is order four.

3. The full equatorial pullback is a graph
===========================================

This is the smallest decisive source test.

Away from a branch mark, a point over |x|=1 satisfies exactly one of the two
fixed equations in (1), according to the sign of sin(2 theta). At each of
the four marks y=0, it satisfies both. Therefore (5) holds exactly.

In the square uniformization E=C/(Z+iZ), use O=(0,i), T=(1+i)/2 and the
normalization already used by the C2 calculation:

  Qz=iz,
  hz=-z+T,
  kappaz=-i bar z+T.

For the lift S in (2), S*z on the holomorphic differential is i and the
corresponding affine map is

  Sz=iz+1/2.                                             (17)

Hence

  j_+z=bar z+i/2,
  j_-z=-bar z+1/2.                                      (18)

The fixed sets are

  Fix(j_+): Im z=1/4 or 3/4,
  Fix(j_-): Re z=1/4 or 3/4.                            (19)

Every horizontal circle meets every vertical circle once. The four
intersection points solve hz=z and are exactly the four branch marks. Each
of the four circles is divided into two edges. Thus

  V=4, E=8, connected, b1=E-V+1=5.                     (20)

The four rectangular complementary regions are disks. Equivalently,
Euler's formula on the torus gives F=E-V=4.

This makes the source issue visible. A fixed oval of j_+ or j_- is smooth,
but its projection folds two-to-one over one alternating quarter arc. The
whole original equatorial seam requires all four ovals and the four branch
vertices. It is not the boundary of either annulus E/j_+ or E/j_-.

A small radial push-off |x|=r with r<1 or r>1 avoids the branch points. Its
inverse image consists of two unbranched circles because it encloses zero or
four branch points, hence has even covering monodromy. The original source
does not choose such a push-off, its side, or the transport back to the
marked seam. Using it as the physical carrier would therefore be an extra
source map, not a consequence of (1).

4. Spin compatibility and its exact limit
==========================================

The existing source calculation identifies the unique Q-fixed nontrivial
elliptic spin class as

  alpha=O(T-O),  T=(1+i)/2.

See

  experiments/theory-contracts/original-admissible-source-closure-20261025/
  ORIGINAL-BASES-AND-ELLIPTIC-SPIN.txt:60-65,72-101
  SHA256 bf6b2336e24a39aef26b9907c24f033a47a8a1318f8f19ae561e4c477680ce67.

As a flat character on the square torus this is

  chi_alpha(1)=chi_alpha(i)=-1.                         (21)

Both Q and S pull the elliptic differential dx/y to i dx/y. Their integral
H1 action is the quarter rotation, so alpha is invariant under both. It is
also invariant under h and under the anti-holomorphic maps above as a
two-torsion class.

By (19), every j_+ boundary oval represents the A cycle and every j_-
boundary oval represents the B cycle. Equation (21) therefore gives

  hol_alpha(Fix(j_+) component)=-1,
  hol_alpha(Fix(j_-) component)=-1.                     (22)

Thus all four smooth oval components carry NS spin. Q exchanges the two
horizontal and vertical presentations without a new spin choice.

This is compatibility, not yet the original kernel theorem. At each branch
vertex p^{-1}(Gamma) has four incident half-edges. The flat spin line does
not select a self-adjoint vertex condition, a Calderon graph subspace, or a
state. Also, v519's OS anti-linear operator is family D, whereas j_+/- are
family A. Separate Real linearizations are required for the two roles. The
old source has not supplied their common Fock or boundary-domain transport.
