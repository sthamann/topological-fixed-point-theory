SPIN TRACE OF THE ELLIPTIC SZEGO KERNEL AT THE FOUR BRANCH MARKS
26 October 2026

Question and verdict
====================

Let

  p:E -> P1,  E:y^2=x^4-1,

and retain the actual Q-invariant nontrivial spin line alpha and its NS--NS
Szego Green kernel. The coefficient pushforward p_*alpha has the familiar
local deck exponents 0 and 1/2. The ramified spin trace contains an additional
half-density Jacobian. At every simple branch mark its two exponents are

  -1/4 and +1/4,

with spin-frame monodromy -i and +i. Thus the old quarter pair occurs
naturally as the LOCAL conjugacy class of the spin-trace kernel in the
central spin lift of the branch meridian. It was invisible in the raw
coefficient pushforward because that calculation deliberately computed the
ordinary rank-two coefficient bundle, not the ramified spin frame.

This does not yet identify the old smooth quarter circle. The actual marked
equator pullback is the ramified V4/E8 graph, and the spin trace has four
regular-singular branch points. The old B_1/4 direct-sum B_3/4 system is a
smooth circle domain with one global quarter holonomy. The two systems are
conjugate on a punctured neighborhood of one mark, but no bounded smooth
conjugacy extends through all four marked vertices. The missing datum is now
precise: an original trace or vertex-domain map selecting a smooth carrier,
not a missing quarter phase.

1. Raw coefficient trace versus spin trace
===========================================

Near one branch point a in mu4 choose local coordinates

  X=x-a=q^2.                                             (1)

The flat line alpha is locally trivial on E because a small loop in the q
disk contracts. A local coefficient section has the unique form

  f(q)=f_0(X)+q f_1(X).                                  (2)

For raw coefficient functions, the two summands in (2) have exponents

  0, 1/2

and monodromy +1,-1 around X=0. This is the order-two connection previously
computed for p_*alpha.

A chiral spinor on E is instead

  f(q)(dq)^(1/2).

In the base half-density frame,

  (dq)^(1/2)=(dq/dX)^(1/2)(dX)^(1/2)
             =(2q)^(-1/2)(dX)^(1/2).                   (3)

Equations (2)-(3) give the two spin-trace frames

  e_-(X)=q^(-1/2)(dX)^(1/2),
  e_+(X)=q^(+1/2)(dX)^(1/2).                            (4)

Up to the harmless constant 2^(-1/2), their exponents in X are -1/4 and
+1/4. Analytic continuation once around X=0 sends q to -q and therefore

  e_- -> -i e_-,
  e_+ -> +i e_+.                                        (5)

No scalar phase was inserted. The quarter phases are forced by the simple
ramification and the spin half-density.

There is an essential type distinction here. The raw pushforward has

  M_coeff=diag(1,-1),       M_coeff^2=I,

whereas the frames (4) have

  M_spin=diag(-i,+i),       M_spin^2=-I.                 (5a)

Thus M_spin is not an ordinary representation of the order-two branch
stabilizer. It is its central order-four spin lift, equivalently a projective
or ramified spin-frame action. On the punctured q disk the factor q^(-1/2)
itself changes sign around q=0, even though alpha is locally trivial there.
It must not be described as a single-valued gauge of the ordinary flat bundle
p_*alpha.

The underlying ordinary holomorphic bundle statement remains unchanged:

  p_*alpha=O(-1) direct-sum O(-1).

Factoring the base spin line K_P1^(1/2)=O(-1) is a local calculation on the
punctured base. Equations (4)-(5a) describe the ramified spin trace in the
central extension of the branch group. They do not replace the ordinary flat
connection on p_*alpha by a new connection with order-four holonomy.

2. Exact pushed-forward local Cauchy kernel
===========================================

Write Y=r^2 at the second point. The singular part of the cover Szego kernel
is

  S_E(q,r)=c (dq)^(1/2)(dr)^(1/2)/(q-r)+regular,         (6)

where c=i/(2pi) in the normalization of ELLIPTIC-GREEN-RP.txt. Choose the two
sheet half-density factors

  h_+(q)=(2q)^(-1/2),
  h_-(q)=i(2q)^(-1/2).                                  (7)

The factor i is not fitted: it is the analytic square root of
d(-q)/dX=-1/(2q). In the ordered sheet basis (+,-), pushing (6) to the base
is an exact operation on the actual elliptic covariance. More explicitly,
write

  S_E(u,v)=s_E(u,v)(du dv)^(1/2).

Then the complete local pushforward is

  K_full(X,Y)_(epsilon,eta)
    =h_epsilon(q) h_eta(r) s_E(epsilon q,eta r)
      (dX dY)^(1/2),                                    (7a)

for epsilon,eta in {+1,-1}, with the square-root branches fixed by (7).
Its universal singular part is the rank-two matrix

  K_sheet(X,Y)=c/[2 sqrt(qr)]
    [[1/(q-r),  i/(q+r)],
     [-i/(q+r), 1/(q-r)]] (dX dY)^(1/2)
    +regular.                                            (8)

The constant unitary which diagonalizes

  [[0,i],[-i,0]]

turns (8) into

  K_spin(X,Y)=c/(X-Y)
    diag((X/Y)^(1/4),(Y/X)^(1/4)) (dX dY)^(1/2)
    +regular.                                            (9)

Indeed,

  1/(q-r)+1/(q+r)=2q/(X-Y),
  1/(q-r)-1/(q+r)=2r/(X-Y).

Equation (9) is the smallest decisive kernel identity. Away from X=0 its
diagonal singularity is the ordinary identity Cauchy kernel. Moving one
argument around the branch point produces the two phases +i and -i. The
regular part of the actual s_E in (7a) changes global correlations but cannot
change these local exponents.

3. Comparison with the old quarter circle
==========================================

On a punctured disk, after passing to the central spin lift of the branch
meridian, the two diagonal factors in (9) are the standard regular-singular
quarter systems

  d+(1/4)dX/X,
  d-(1/4)dX/X.                                           (10)

Their scalar Cauchy kernels have the same local form

  K_a(X,Y)=c (X/Y)^a (dX dY)^(1/2)/(X-Y),

with a=+1/4 and a=-1/4. This is precisely the pair displayed in (9), up to
the order of the two diagonal entries.

This is the exact positive local match to the old B_1/4 and B_3/4 conjugacy
class in the ramified spin category. It is stronger than matching the numbers
+i,-i: the Cauchy principal part, the half-density normalization and the
central-lift monodromy are all carried together. It is not an isomorphism of
the ordinary flat bundles, because M_coeff has order two and M_spin has order
four.

The global domains are nevertheless different.

  * The old quarter pair lives on one smooth circle. Its +i,-i are the
    holonomies of that circle generator.

  * The elliptic spin trace lives on P1 minus the four branch marks. Each
    branch meridian has the local pair (-i,+i). The product of the four local
    monodromies is compatible with the trivial total loop on P1.

  * The original marked equator |x|=1 passes through all four branch points.
    Its full inverse image is the connected V4/E8 graph of local valence four,
    not a smooth circle. At a vertex, smoothness in q imposes the canonical
    regular-singular domain (2)-(4) on the four incident germs.

Consequently a punctured local gauge identifies (9) with a quarter kernel,
but it becomes singular as X^(+/-1/4) at the branch mark. A smooth bounded
circle conjugacy across that mark does not exist. Replacing the graph by one
normalized component, a radial push-off or a chosen vertex channel would be
an additional source selection unless it is supplied by the original trace
map.

4. Quarter clock and loop typing
================================

The geometric clock Q:x->ix permutes the four branch marks and transports
their local kernels. It does not turn a small branch meridian into the old
longitudinal circle generator. Therefore the following statements have
different scopes:

  proved: every marked cusp carries the natural spin quarter pair;
  proved: Q transports the four cusp kernels and their regular-singular
          domains;
  open:   one original Q-compatible trace map identifies a selected global
          graph channel with the old smooth quarter-circle domain.

This resolves the apparent coefficient mismatch. The raw p_*alpha
monodromy (+1,-1) is correct for coefficient functions. The ramified
spin-frame trace has the central-lift monodromy (-i,+i). Neither result
replaces the other, and the latter does not erase the global V4/E8 graph
obstruction or prove that the same ordinary connection is preserved.

5. Consequence for the holomorphic source kernel
================================================

The rank-two kernel (8) is obtained by an exact pushforward of the already
given elliptic Szego half-form, including its square-root Jacobian. Therefore
the holomorphic two-point kernel, and algebraically the determinant Wick
expressions built from that kernel, transport to the quasipermutation,
ramified spin-trace chart on the punctured base with the cover-induced domain
(4). The central lift and its phase are inherited from the original cover
spinor rather than selected independently.

This local half-form calculation does not by itself prove a positive
equal-time CAR covariance on a base Hilbert space. Such a claim additionally
requires a specified real slice and one-particle inner product, a closed trace
operator T across all branch vertices, and the operator statements

  C_base=T C_E T^*,       C_base=C_base^*,       0<=C_base<=I,

with the relevant isometry or coisometry property of T. None of those
Hilbert-space statements follows merely from the bilinear holomorphic formula
(7a). In particular, a square-root Jacobian gives the correct tensorial
transformation law but is not on its own a proof of unitarity or positivity.

The strongest positive statement proved here is therefore narrower:

  the same elliptic Szego half-form has the old quarter Cauchy principal part
  at every marked branch point, including its central spin phase.

It still does not supply a single periodic old-circle field. The first
remaining source equation is a Q-compatible global trace or vertex-domain
intertwiner from the actual ramified equator graph to the old smooth carrier.
Without that equation and the Hilbert-space conditions above, local quarter
conjugacy cannot be promoted to equality of CAR state, time or winding
domains.
