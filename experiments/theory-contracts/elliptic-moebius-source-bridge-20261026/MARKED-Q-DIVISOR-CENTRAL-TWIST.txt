1. THE FINITE-POINT LINE IS A NATURAL BOUNDARY DETERMINANT

On

    C: eta^2=1-w^6,

write q_a=-omega^a, p_a=omega^a.  The source-selected spin line is

    S_0=O_C(D_q-K_C),       S_0^2=K_C.                          (3)

Because D_q is a reduced divisor of length three,

    H^0(D_q,S_0|D_q)=direct_sum_a S_0|q_a,
    B_q=det H^0(D_q,S_0|D_q).                                  (4)

The exact evaluation sequence

    0 -> S_0(-D_q) -> S_0 -> S_0|D_q -> 0                     (5)

makes B_q a canonical boundary term in determinant functoriality.  In fact,
by (3), S_0(-D_q)=O_C(-K_C).  Thus (4) is not an arbitrary one-dimensional
character appended to the theta module.

There are two closely related lines that must be distinguished:

    B_q = det H^0(D_q,S_0|D_q),

    N_q = B_q tensor det H^0(D_q,O_Dq)^(-1)
        = tensor_a S_0|q_a.                                    (6)

N_q is the ordinary norm or unordered tensor line.  Under a permutation of
the points, B_q contains the determinant sign; N_q cancels that sign against
the same permutation on O_Dq.  Both have the same beta action because beta
fixes every branch point.  Their alpha actions differ by one minus sign
because Q swaps q_1 and q_2.  Their alpha squares are identical.  Formula
(1) uses N_q only to avoid making an irrelevant ordering choice.


2. THE q ORBIT CARRIES THE REQUIRED +i CHARACTER

The original quarter map is

    Q:(w,eta) |-> (1/w,-i eta/w^3),
    h=Q^2:(w,eta) |-> (w,-eta).                                (7)

At q_0=-1, eta is a local coordinate and Q sends eta to +i eta.
For the selected spin lift alpha this gives

    alpha^2=beta,       beta|S_0,q_0=+i.                        (8)

The cubic map c:(w,eta)|->(omega w,eta) cycles the three q points and
commutes with h.  Let gamma be an order-three lift of c to S_0.  The
commutator of gamma and beta is a scalar zeta.  From gamma^3=I and
beta^2=-I one obtains

    zeta^3=1,       zeta^2=1,

so zeta=1.  Hence beta has the same +i fiber action at every q_a.  Taking
the determinant or norm over the three points gives

    beta|B_q=beta|N_q=i^3=-i,
    beta|N_q^(-1)=+i.                                          (9)

The normalized c lift acts trivially on N_q: it cyclically permutes three
fibers, the cycle is even, and the product of its fiber maps is gamma^3=1.
Rescaling gamma by a cube root of unity also changes the product by its
third power and therefore changes nothing.  The twist preserves the cubic
operator rather than fitting a new one.

The ordinary theta space

    H_Theta=H^0(Jac(C),O(2 Theta))

has beta=+I under its canonical even linearization.  Combining this with
(9) proves the first relation in (2).  For alpha, Q fixes q_0 and exchanges
q_1,q_2.  Whether one uses B_q or N_q, functoriality gives

    alpha^2|N_q=beta|N_q=-i,
    alpha^2|N_q^(-1)=+i.                                      (10)

Replacing alpha by -alpha multiplies its action on the three-fiber line by
-1, so alpha itself is still determined only up to the already known global
sign.  Its square in (10) is sign independent.  Since alpha^2 acts as +I on
H_Theta, equations (1) and (10) prove the other two relations in (2).


3. THE s-EXCHANGED POSITIVE LINE HAS THE CONJUGATE CHARACTER

The same calculation is not symmetric after retaining the marked Q
orientation.  At p_0=+1, formula (7) sends eta to -i eta.  Cubic covariance
then gives

    beta|S_0,p_a=-i       for all a,
    beta|N_p=(-i)^3=+i,
    beta|N_p^(-1)=-i,                                         (11)

where D_p=p_0+p_1+p_2 and N_p is defined as in (6).

The source involution s:(w,eta)|->(-w,eta) exchanges D_q and D_p and obeys

    s Q s=Q^(-1).                                               (12)

It therefore exchanges the two twisted spaces

    W_q=H_Theta tensor N_q^(-1),
    W_p=H_Theta tensor N_p^(-1),                               (13)

on which beta has the conjugate central characters +i and -i.  A
complex-linear operator cannot conjugate the scalar +i I on one fixed
four-dimensional space to -i I on that same space.  An antiunitary
identification or a changed complex structure would be extra data and is not
introduced here.

This does not damage the q candidate.  The accepted (4,3,2) triangle and
v117 coefficient source already select the negative q packet; s is not a
symmetry of that isolated A0* object.  If one demands the earlier six-line
s covariance, the correct object is the paired system W_q direct_sum W_p,
with s exchanging its two conjugate central-character blocks.  That paired
eight-dimensional system is not being identified with the native four-
dimensional module.


4. THE SOURCE-SELECTED SPIN NORMALIZATION MAKES THE FAMILY LINE CONSTANT

The preceding central-character calculation must be checked over the whole
Jacobian A.  A Poincare bundle is defined only up to tensoring by a line
pulled back from A.  Normalizing it at q_0 produces a misleading flat factor.
Indeed, for a q_0-normalized Poincare bundle P one obtains

    Norm_Dq(P)=mathcal P_delta_q,
    delta_q=D_q-3q_0=q_1-q_2 not_equal 0 in Jac(C)[2].          (14)

The original c moves this nonzero vector through the three nonzero elements
of V_q.  Thus the q_0-normalized one-copy line is not c invariant.  This is
an obstruction to that point normalization, not an intrinsic obstruction to
the source family: using q_0 as the Poincare rigidification was an additional
choice, even though q_0 itself was already source marked.

The source has already selected a reference of the correct type, namely the
degree-one spin line S_0.  From (3) and S_0^2=K_C,

    O_C(D_q)=S_0 tensor K_C=S_0^3.                              (15)

Let

    mathcal R=<P,S_0>

be the Deligne pairing over A, and define the spin-normalized Poincare family

    P_spin=P tensor pr_A^*(mathcal R^(-1)).                     (16)

This tensor factor comes from the base, so P_spin represents the same family
of degree-zero line bundles on C.  Since deg(S_0)=1, bilinearity first gives

    <P_spin,S_0>=<P,S_0> tensor mathcal R^(-1)=O_A.

Norm functoriality and bilinearity of the Deligne pairing then give a
canonical cancellation:

    Norm_Dq(P_spin)
      =<P,O_C(D_q)> tensor mathcal R^(-3)
      =<P,S_0^3> tensor <P,S_0>^(-3)
      =O_A.                                                      (17)

Consequently the evaluation norm of

    E_spin=S_0 tensor P_spin

is the constant equivariant line

    mathcal N_q(E_spin)=N_q(S_0) tensor O_A.                    (18)

The same statement holds for D_p because O(D_p)=O(D_q)=S_0^3.  Thus the
q and p blocks remain a full-family s-exchanged pair with the conjugate
central characters computed in section 3.

This normalization does not change the ordinary theta determinant.  A line
pulled back from A enters determinant of cohomology with exponent
chi(S_0 tensor L)=0, so the factor mathcal R^(-1) has weight zero there.
It changes the length-three evaluation norm with exponent three, exactly as
needed in (17).

The construction is c and Q covariant because S_0 and D_q were already
selected invariant objects, and the S_0-normalized universal family is unique
up to its fixed rigidification.  The remaining scalar ambiguity in a lift of
S_0 does not reintroduce a phase: scaling the second entry of the Deligne
pairing acts with exponent deg(P_spin|C)=0.  Therefore (17) is independent of
that scalar and obeys the same cocycles as the selected source lifts.

The direct one-copy family correction is hence genuinely

    O(2 Theta) tensor N_q(S_0)^(-1),                            (19)

with no moving Pic^0 factor.  It retains the four-dimensional theta space,
the original c action, and the central relations (2).  No new point or flat
character has been selected; the normalization uses the unique
c-invariant source spin line S_0.

One distinction remains.  If one first imposes vanishing at D_q via (5) and
then forms the usual two-theta line, the evaluation factor is squared:

    (D tensor mathcal N_q(E_spin)^(-1))^(-2)
      =D^(-2) tensor N_q(S_0)^2.                               (20)

Its beta phase is (-i)^2=-1, not +i.  Thus determinant exactness alone does
not select the one-copy insertion (19).  The remaining physical question is
precise: does the original puncture, boundary, sewing or conformal-block
construction attach one inverse copy of the marked evaluation norm directly
to the O(2 Theta) module?  The geometry supplies that line canonically, but
the inspected source has not yet supplied this insertion rule.
