   T_net=(A+2I)/4 acts on nine affine nodes. L0 acts on an infinite conformal

   Connection and holonomy may be contained in its definition and domain.
   The absence of the printed symbol delta in an abstract boundary tuple
   does not prove the absence of those data. The formula dGamma(|B_Sigma|)
   requires the appropriate free field and polarization reconstruction.
   It does not follow for an arbitrary P1 field measure from the tuple alone.

4. The eight-step staircase has the exact A7 decomposition

     delta=-rho_A7/8+(2a-1)q/16,
     rho_A7=(7/2,5/2,3/2,1/2,-1/2,-3/2,-5/2,-7/2),
     q=(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1).

   Its denominator is the A7 Coxeter number eight. Labelling it the D8 or

   Coxeter numbers are fourteen and thirty. In particular p*=omega4(A7)
   obeys rho_A7.p*=8 and |p*|^2=2. Its H-zero value is an exact property
   of the unchanged affine grading, not evidence that this grading is the
   physical OS generator or that it should be removed.

   Its cyclic vector does not select that functional from topology alone.
   The established free C2 two-boundary kernel and its pure CAR state remain
   valid in their stated setting. The identification with the original P1
