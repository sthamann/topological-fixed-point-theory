Question

  delta_j=(j+a)/8-1/2,  j=0,...,7,

and how does its distinguished zero-grade root p* sit in the complete E8 root

Verdict
-------

Coxeter denominator eight:

  rho_A7=(7/2,5/2,3/2,1/2,-1/2,-3/2,-5/2,-7/2),

Consequently the folded source generator has the exact decomposition

  H8=8L0-rho_A7.a0+(2a-1)Q/2.                         (1)

This corrects the proposed identification of the staircase with a D8 or E8
Coxeter direction.  The denominator eight and rho_A7 are intrinsic to the
ordered eight-coordinate ramp.  This statement does not identify physical

elsewhere in the theory.

The complete E8 root census by Q is

  Q       -4   -2    0   +2   +4
  count    1   56  126   56    1.

The 126 neutral roots are exactly the E7 subsystem orthogonal to q.  Under
its maximal-rank A7 subsystem they split as

  126 = 56 + 70
      = roots(A7) + weights(Lambda^4 C^8).              (2)

  p*=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2)

2. Exact offset decomposition
-----------------------------

The j-th entry of -rho_A7/8 is

  -(7-2j)/16.

Adding the uniform component (2a-1)/16 gives

  -(7-2j)/16+(2a-1)/16
    =(j+a)/8-1/2
    =delta_j.

Since L0=N+p^2/2 and Q=q.a0, multiplication by eight gives (1) exactly.
The parameter a occurs only in the uniform U1 direction.  Every Q-neutral
state is therefore independent of a under the nonuniform grading.

For a level-one root state J_(alpha,-1)|0>, the basic commutator is

  [H8,J_(alpha,-1)]
    =(8-rho_A7.alpha+(2a-1)Q(alpha)/2) J_(alpha,-1).    (3)

For alpha=p*, rho_A7.p*=8 and Q(p*)=0, so the coefficient in (3) vanishes.
This is the precise source of the old zero grade.  It is an A7 principal
grade cancellation, independent of the Wilson-line parameter a.

Among all 126 neutral E7 roots, p* is the unique root with zero coefficient
in (3).  The opposite root has coefficient 16.  The complete neutral base
grade multiplicities for grades 0 through 16 are

  1,2,4,6,9,10,13,14,8,14,13,10,9,6,4,2,1.

3. Full root census and the E7 cut
----------------------------------

Use the standard E8 realization with 112 integer D8 roots

  +/-e_i +/-e_j

and 128 half roots (1/2)(+/-1,...,+/-1) with an even number of minus signs.

For the integer roots, Q=0 consists exactly of e_i-e_j and its negatives:
2*C(8,2)=56 roots.  The remaining integer roots give 28 roots at each of
Q=+2 and Q=-2.

For a half root with m minus signs,

  Q=4-m.

The allowed even values m=0,2,4,6,8 yield multiplicities

  1,28,70,28,1.

Adding both root types gives the five-charge census above.  At Q=0 the 70
half roots have four plus and four minus signs.  Choosing the four plus
positions identifies them exactly with the 70 weights of Lambda^4 C^8.
Together with the 56 A7 roots they form the 126-root, rank-seven,
reflection-closed E7 subsystem q-perp inside E8.  This is the standard
adjoint branching

  133 of E7 -> 63 of A7 + 70,

with the common seven Cartan directions added to the root counts in (2).
