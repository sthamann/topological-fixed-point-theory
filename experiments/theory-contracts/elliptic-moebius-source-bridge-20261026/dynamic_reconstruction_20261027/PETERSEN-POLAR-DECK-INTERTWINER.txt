PETERSEN POLAR DECK INTERTWINER
================================

27 October 2026

Question
--------

Does the signed Petersen radial transfer from v808 admit a natural marked
intertwiner with the actual lazy family operator from v486/v487 when the shell
charges and the existing deck involution are carried along?

Answer
------

Yes for the positive transfer, and no for the literal v487 deck marking.

The reversible Petersen operator has a canonical polar decomposition

      P = J_P |P|.

There is an explicit isometry W with

      W |P| W^{-1} = M_lazy.

It maps the stationary shell mark to the family vacuum, the hypercharge mode
to the 2/3 even family mode, and the third v808 mode to the 1/3 odd family
mode.  But under this same forced spectral mapping,

      W J_P W^{-1} = K = Z Delta,

where Delta is the v487 deck swap and

      Z = diag(1,-1,-1)

is the canonical reflection across the Perron line.  Consequently no static
or time dependent isometric intertwiner can carry both the positive transfer
and the literal deck marking Delta.  The obstruction already occurs on the
one dimensional 2/3 eigenspace.

There are two exact surviving constructions:

1. a signed static correspondence

      W P W^{-1} = K M_lazy;

2. a period two correspondence to the positive lazy walk,

      W_n = K^n W,
      W_{n+1} P W_n^{-1} = M_lazy.

The period two frame transports the hypercharge sign rather than discarding
it.  Equivalently, the canonical bipartite double cover turns P into the
positive lazy walk by a fixed six dimensional isometry.  Its deck exchange is
transported to a K twisted exchange, not to the plain v487 deck.

This is stronger than the old equality after six powers.  The two transfers
are exactly equivalent at every even time.  Their odd steps differ by K.
The transported shell multiplication operator is not conserved: its exact
one step defect has rank two.  Also, Z has an older source candidate: v409
sets the same matrix as the seam sheet involution.  What remains absent is a
source intertwiner proving that this involution and the v487 deck use the same
marked basis.

2. Reversible radial Hilbert space
----------------------------------

The shell sizes are 1, 3 and 6.  The radial Markov operator is

          [ 0    1    0  ]
      P = [1/3   0   2/3 ],
          [ 0   1/3  2/3 ]

with stationary measure

      pi = (1,3,6)/10.

Detailed balance holds entry by entry:

      pi_i P_ij = pi_j P_ji.

Thus P is selfadjoint on H_pi = R^3 with scalar product

      <f,g>_pi = sum_i pi_i f_i g_i.

The three source marked eigenfunctions from v808 are

      f0 = (1,1,1),                 eigenvalue  1,
      q  = (6,-4,1),                eigenvalue -2/3,
      r  = (3,1,-1),                eigenvalue  1/3.

The second vector is literally the shell hypercharge observable evaluated on
the charge pure classes e^c, u^c and Q.  Their squared H_pi norms are 1, 9 and
9/5 respectively.  Hence

      b0 = f0,
      bY = q/3,
      br = sqrt(5) r/3

is an orthonormal marked basis.

Conjugating by D^(1/2), D = diag(pi), gives the ordinary symmetric matrix

               [ 0       1/sqrt(3)       0       ]
      P_sym =  [1/sqrt(3)    0         sqrt(2)/3 ],
               [ 0        sqrt(2)/3       2/3     ]

with the same marked spectrum.

3. Canonical polar decomposition
--------------------------------

Since P is invertible and selfadjoint on H_pi, its polar factors are unique.
Interpolation on the three simple eigenvalues gives coordinate free formulas

      |P| = (4/5)P^2 - (1/15)P + (4/15)I,
      J_P = -(6/5)P^2 + (8/5)P + (3/5)I.

In the original shell coordinates these are

            [ 8/15  -1/15   8/15]
      |P| = [-1/45  32/45  14/45],
            [ 4/45   7/45  34/45]

and

            [ 1/5    8/5   -4/5]
      J_P = [ 8/15  -1/15   8/15].
            [-2/15   4/15  13/15]

Direct multiplication gives

      J_P^2 = I,       J_P |P| = |P| J_P,       P = J_P |P|.

Both factors are selfadjoint for the pi weighted scalar product.  Their action
on the marked basis is

             b0    bY    br
      |P|     1    2/3   1/3,
      J_P     1     -1    1.

The negative sign is therefore attached canonically to the hypercharge mode.

4. Concrete intertwiner to M_lazy
---------------------------------

Write the target family basis as

      e0 = (1,0,0),
      e+ = (0,1,1)/sqrt(2),
      e- = (0,1,-1)/sqrt(2).

Then

      M_lazy e0 = e0,
      M_lazy e+ = (2/3)e+,
      M_lazy e- = (1/3)e-.

Define the isometry W by

      W b0 = e0,       W bY = e+,       W br = e-.

This definition transports all three v808 markings and fixes W up to the
irrelevant simultaneous sign conventions of the one dimensional marked
lines.  It proves immediately that

      W |P| W^{-1} = M_lazy.

For a literal matrix, first send H_pi to the standard Euclidean shell frame by
D^(1/2).  In that frame the orthogonal matrix U of W is

      [ 1/sqrt(10),       sqrt(30)/10,                sqrt(15)/5              ]
      [ 1/2+sqrt(5)/5,   -2sqrt(15)/15+sqrt(3)/6,   -sqrt(6)/6+sqrt(30)/30 ]
      [-1/2+sqrt(5)/5,   -2sqrt(15)/15-sqrt(3)/6,    sqrt(30)/30+sqrt(6)/6 ].

It satisfies U U^T = I and the claimed conjugation exactly.

The shell charge multiplication operator is

      Y_shell = diag(6,-4,1).

It is not discarded.  In the target frame it becomes

                  [0,          3sqrt(2)/2,             3sqrt(2)/2          ]
      Y_target =  [3sqrt(2)/2,  3/2+5sqrt(5)/3,        -7/6                ].
                  [3sqrt(2)/2, -7/6,                    3/2-5sqrt(5)/3    ]

This matrix has the original eigenvalues 6, -4 and 1.  The marked hypercharge
function itself maps more simply as W(q/3) = e+.

5. Exact deck obstruction
--------------------------

The v487 deck operator swaps the surviving pair:

                  [1  0  0]
      Delta =     [0  0  1].
                  [0  1  0]

Its spectral marks are

      Delta e0 =  e0,
      Delta e+ =  e+,
      Delta e- = -e-.

The transported Petersen polar sign is instead

                   [1  0   0]
      K = W J_P W^-1 = [0  0  -1]
                       [0 -1   0],

so that

      K e0 =  e0,
      K e+ = -e+,
      K e- =  e-.

Equivalently,

      K = Z Delta,       Z = diag(1,-1,-1) = 2|e0><e0| - I.

The extra Z is not fitted.  It is the canonical reflection across the unique
Perron line of M_lazy.

This proves the minimal contradiction.  Any isometry V satisfying

      V |P| V^-1 = M_lazy

must map the simple 2/3 eigenspace span(bY) to span(e+).  J_P is -1 on the
first line, while Delta is +1 on the second.  Therefore

      V J_P V^-1 != Delta

for every such V.  Rephasing one dimensional eigenspaces cannot change an
involution eigenvalue.

The same obstruction holds for a time dependent sequence of isometries V_n
with

      V_(n+1) P V_n^-1 = M_lazy.

Indeed, taking adjoint products forces

      V_n |P|^2 V_n^-1 = M_lazy^2.

Positivity and the simple spectrum then force

      V_n |P| V_n^-1 = M_lazy

at every n, so the preceding deck contradiction applies at every time.

6. Signed and time dependent correspondences
---------------------------------------------

Although the literal deck marking fails, the transport itself has an exact
one step dictionary:

      W P W^-1 = K M_lazy = M_lazy K.

Hence for every n >= 0,

      W P^(2n) W^-1     = M_lazy^(2n),
      W P^(2n+1) W^-1   = K M_lazy^(2n+1).

The old sixth power match is the n=3 member of an exact even time theorem.

Define alternating frames

      W_n = K^n W.

Then

      W_(n+1) P W_n^-1 = M_lazy

for every n.  The hypercharge mark is carried as

      W_n(q/3) = (-1)^n e+.

Thus the sign of the Petersen hypercharge mode becomes orientation data of the
time frame.  The full charge multiplication operator is transported as

      Y_n = K^n Y_target K^n,

which has period two.  A fixed positive family transfer and a fixed charge
matrix cannot both be obtained from this dictionary without retaining that
orientation bit.

7. Canonical bipartite double cover
-----------------------------------

The same result can be made static by retaining the orientation sheet.  On
H_pi direct sum H_pi define

                 [0  P]
      P_hat =    [P  0].

This is the radial action induced by the canonical bipartite double cover:
every step exchanges the two orientation sheets.  Let

                 [I   0 ]
      G =        [0  J_P].

Using P J_P = J_P P = |P| gives

                 [ 0   |P|]
      G P_hat G = [|P|  0  ].

Therefore

      W_hat = diag(W,W) G

is a fixed isometry with

                              [ 0       M_lazy]
      W_hat P_hat W_hat^-1 = [M_lazy    0     ].

So the orientable double cover does remove the signed one step obstruction to
positive transport.  It does not preserve the plain deck mark.  If tau is the
sheet exchange, then

      W_hat tau W_hat^-1 = [0 K; K 0],

while a plain target sheet exchange would contain I, and the v487 internal
deck would contain Delta.  The exact transported deck is the product of sheet
exchange, native deck and Perron reflection:

      tau tensor K = tau tensor (Z Delta).

8. Scope and consequence
-------------------------

The strongest source based conclusion is positive and precise:

* signed Petersen transport and the lazy walk are the same reversible
  contraction after retaining a canonical period two orientation frame;
* all even powers agree under one fixed marked isometry;
* the bipartite double cover makes the equivalence static;
* the shell charge and all three marked modes are explicitly transported.

The literal identification of the Petersen polar sign with the v487 deck is
excluded.  Their nontrivial parity assignments are opposite.  The smallest
additional source statement that would close the natural route is therefore:

      geometric deck = orientation-sheet exchange times Z Delta,

or an independently derived reason that the OS or clock lift includes the
Perron-complement reflection Z.  The inspected source supplies Delta but does
not supply this extra reflection as part of the deck action.  Interpreting the
period two frame as physical time or promoting Y_target to a charged local
field remains a separate identification.

9. Operator test: the shell charge is transported but not conserved
--------------------------------------------------------------------

The preceding formula transports the multiplication operator attached to the
literal shell hypercharge

      Y_shell = diag(6,-4,1).

It does not turn that observable into a conserved charge of the Markov step.
Directly,

                         [  0      10       0   ]
      [Y_shell,P] =      [-10/3     0     -10/3 ]
                         [  0      5/3      0   ],

which has rank two.  In the alternating target frames let

      Y_n = K^n W Y_shell W^-1 K^n.

Using W_(n+1) P W_n^-1 = M_lazy gives the exact defect identity

      Y_(n+1) M_lazy - M_lazy Y_n
        = W_(n+1) [Y_shell,P] W_n^-1.

For even n the right hand side is

                [    0        -5 sqrt(2)/2   -5 sqrt(2)/2 ]
                [ -5 sqrt(2)/2  -5 sqrt(5)/3       0       ],
                [ -5 sqrt(2)/2       0         5 sqrt(5)/3 ],

and for odd n it is its negative.  In either case its rank is two and its
squared Frobenius norm is 700/9.  Therefore the period two charge frame does
not satisfy

      Y_(n+1) M_lazy = M_lazy Y_n.

This is a deliberately narrow conclusion.  v808 describes carrier
representation geometry, not an isolated particle dynamics.  The calculation
only says that one Petersen Markov walk, even with the exact alternating
frame, is not by itself a charge conserving physical time transport.  A
compensating sector or interaction would have to come from an actual source
operation; this calculation supplies none.

10. The Perron reflection already has a named source candidate
---------------------------------------------------------------

The matrix Z was not first invented by the polar decomposition.  The original
v409 lemma RES.COXETER.SYMMETRY.01 explicitly sets

      sigma = diag(1,-1,-1)

and calls it the seam sheet involution and the universal prime two parity
factor.  In the common target slot convention used above, this is exactly Z,
and hence

      K = sigma Delta.

Moreover sigma, Delta and M_lazy commute pairwise.  Algebraically, the missing
sign is therefore precisely the older marked prime two parity multiplied by
the v487 family deck, rather than an arbitrary new reflection.

The checked source does not yet identify their bases.  v409 sets sigma as a
three dimensional sheet involution but imports neither the v487 family space
nor M_lazy.  The earlier v97 bridge places the same numerical matrix SIG in
the generation coordinate basis.  Its actual cusp eigenvectors are

      e_0=(0,0,1),  e_(1/3)=(0,1,2),  e_(2/3)=(1,0,0),

and cusp conjugation in that basis is

      T_A = [[0,1,0],[1,0,0],[2,-2,1]],

not SIG.  v97 also labels the identification between the generation grading
and the A3 discriminant grading as the remaining proposed dictionary.  Thus
the exact equality K=sigma Delta is source backed only after choosing the
common slot convention; the original chain still lacks the intertwiner that
proves v409 sigma and v487 Delta act on that same marked three dimensional
space.

No stronger identification was found in the inspected classical Z3 or SU3
transfer lifts.  A pure Z3 clock has no nontrivial element of order two, and
the reviewed quantum lifts establish the transfer or unistochastic data but
do not name 2 Pi_Perron-I as a half period.  In their uniform basis the
canonical Perron reflection is

      2 Pi_uniform - I = (2/3) 11^T - I,

which becomes diag(1,-1,-1) only after an orthogonal eigenbasis choice.  This
is consistent with v808's explicit spectrum-not-basis scope and cannot supply
the missing source intertwiner by itself.
