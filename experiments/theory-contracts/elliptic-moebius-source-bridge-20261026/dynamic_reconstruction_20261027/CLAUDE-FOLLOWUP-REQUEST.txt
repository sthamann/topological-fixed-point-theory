106: for $D_+$ on $\Sigma$, and define the boundary polarization operator
107: \[
108: \iC:=2P_C-1

127:     \qquad
128:     D_{\mathrm{rel}}:=\frac12\bigl(D-\taudbl^{\ast}D\taudbl^{\ast}\bigr);
