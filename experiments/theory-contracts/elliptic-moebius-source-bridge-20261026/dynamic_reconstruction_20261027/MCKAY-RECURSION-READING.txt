MCKAY RECURSION: A SOURCE BASED READING OF THE EXISTING NETWORK STEP
27 October 2026

Scope. This is a synthesis of the already proved v219/v299 McKay data and
the actual v395 network update. It adds no P1 selection theorem and does
not identify a representation index with a physical particle or spacetime.

The original v219 constructs the binary icosahedral group from 120 unit
quaternions and obtains the affine E8 adjacency A from tensoring with its
natural two dimensional representation V. The irreducible dimensions are
the existing marks d=(1,2,3,4,5,6,4,2,3), in the v395 ordering.
They satisfy A d=2d and sum d_i^2=120.

The actual recursive operator is T=(A+2I)/4. With D=diag(d), its canonical
positive eigenvector transform is

  P=D^-1 T D,
  P_ij=(A_ij+2 delta_ij)d_j/(4d_i).

This is entrywise nonnegative and each row sums to one. Its stationary law
is pi_i=d_i^2/120, and pi_i P_ij=pi_j P_ji follows from A_ij=A_ji.
Thus the mark vector and the Plancherel equilibrium are two normalizations
of the same existing recursion, not two independently chosen states.

There is also an exact representation meaning. Tensor an irreducible i
with R=V direct-sum 1 direct-sum 1, and select summand j with probability
proportional to its multiplicity times d_j. R has dimension four and
multiplicity matrix A+2I, so the resulting tensor-product Markov chain is
precisely P. Its lazy weight was already fixed by v395; the representation
description does not derive that choice anew from P1.

Primary literature check: Benkart, Diaconis, Liebeck and Tiep, Tensor Product
Markov Chains, arXiv:1810.00409, equation (1.1) and Theorem 1.1, give the
general multiplicity-times-dimension transition rule and its Plancherel

The exact rational calculation is saved as mckay-doob-exact.json. All rows,
positivity, detailed balance and normalization were checked without numeric

Meaning for the requested route. The hypergraph fixed point is already tied
to tensor fusion of representations and to the binary double cover. It need
not be interpreted as an arbitrary classical crystal. However the Markov
chain describes representation populations. It does not specify coherent
intertwiners, their phases, a physical field state, an electromagnetic
interaction or the physical clock. Promoting it to those objects would
repeat the source identification problem. This reading should be reused as
a finite structural connection, not as a new independent derivation of E8
or a complete quantum dynamics.
