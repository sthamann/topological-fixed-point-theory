The second answer proposes Z=R_tau restricted to a shell space. That is not
accepted as derived. Four different identifications remain unproved:

1. No embedding of the three-dimensional Petersen shell space into the P1
   Cauchy-data space has been given. Writing P_C restricted to shells is
   not a specified finite matrix problem until that embedding and its
   inner product are supplied. A compression is generally not a projector.

2. The source p*=omega4(A7) is an eight-dimensional lattice charge, and its
   field is neutral under Q=sum a0_j. The Petersen q=(6,-4,1) is a function
   on three charge classes, not a definite-charge field state. Equal minus
   signs do not identify these lines, representations or operators.

3. The sewing step alpha has alpha^2=beta and order eight; the alternating
   Petersen frame K has order two. A quotient functor relating them must
   specify what happens to the nontrivial beta holonomy. It is not supplied
   by saying that both constructions use two objects. Likewise complex
   conjugation on a determinant line is an antilinear operation; the real
   matrix Z is a linear involution. A real-structure identification is
   required before equating them.

4. A direct test of the already available graph-Calderon formula rejects
   the naive single-face identification. For the given positive invertible
   M=M_lazy define

     C=M^2(I+M^2)^-1, X=M(I+M^2)^-1,
     P_M=[[I-C,X],[X,C]].

   P_M is an orthogonal rank-three projector on the doubled six-dimensional
   space. Its single-face compression has eigenvalues

     {1/2,4/13,1/10},

   all strictly between zero and one. It is not the rank-one Perron
   projector {1,0,0}. The other face is likewise full rank. The calculation
   uses the exact existing finite graph formula, not a newly fitted source.
   It excludes the proposed identification for this natural inclusion;
   it does not exclude a different source-derived invariant embedding.
   An arbitrary mixed embedding would simply add the missing choice.
