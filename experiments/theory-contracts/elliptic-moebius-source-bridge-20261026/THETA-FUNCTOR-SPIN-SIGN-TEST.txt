THE THETA FUNCTOR FORGETS THE SELECTED SPIN CENTRAL SIGN
Smallest decisive test for the geometric theta to native Pauli bridge
2026-10-26

VERDICT

PROVED FOR THE STANDARD DETERMINANT-OF-COHOMOLOGY THETA FUNCTOR.  The
source-selected spin line S_0 and its projective hyperelliptic lift beta obey

    beta^2=-I on S_0.

Nevertheless, the induced lift on the theta determinant line squares to
+I.  The reason is exact and does not depend on a basis: the relevant Euler
characteristic is zero.  Passing to O(2 Theta) cannot recover the lost sign.
With the standard symmetric linearization, inversion acts as +I on all four
second-order theta functions.

Thus the existing geometric theta functor does not derive

    beta  |->  J=i I_4.

The already verified Pauli quotient and finite lift remain compatible.  To
obtain the native central phase one still needs a marked mu_4 theta-group or
metaplectic linearization whose central character is specified separately.
Projectively this distinction disappears; linearly it is exactly the
missing datum.


1. THE DETERMINANT LINE AND ITS SCALAR WEIGHT

Let A=Jac(C), and let S_0 be the unique source-selected even theta
characteristic from SOURCE-SELECTED-THETA-SPIN.  Over L in A consider

    E_L=S_0 tensor L,

and the determinant-of-cohomology line, in one standard convention,

    D_L=det R Gamma(C,E_L)
       =det H^0(C,E_L) tensor det H^1(C,E_L)^(-1).              (1)

Changing to the dual convention reverses every exponent below and changes
nothing because that exponent is zero.  Since deg(S_0)=g-1=1 and g=2,

    chi(E_L)=deg(E_L)+1-g=0.                                  (2)

A scalar lambda I on E_L acts functorially on (1) by

    lambda^(h^0(E_L)-h^1(E_L))=lambda^chi(E_L)=1.              (3)

This also holds on the theta divisor, where both cohomology groups can be
nonzero: Riemann-Roch still gives h^0=h^1.  Formula (3) is therefore a
fiberwise identity on the determinant line, not a generic-point argument.

Use the Poincare bundle on C x A, normalized along {q_0} x A at the marked
fixed point q_0, to form this family over A.  The normalization does not hide
another central character.  An isomorphism between the relevant normalized
Poincare pullbacks is unique after its value on the rigidifying fiber is
fixed.  Dropping that rigidification would allow one to multiply the lift by
an arbitrary scalar, but doing so is precisely the additional phase choice
at issue, not part of the canonical functor.

The determinant line is O(-Theta) in convention (1), or O(Theta) after
dualizing.  This sign convention has no bearing on (3).


2. APPLYING THE TEST TO THE SELECTED SPIN LIFT

Let h=Q^2 be the hyperelliptic involution.  It induces inversion [-1] on A.
The already proved source-selected lift

    beta:h^*S_0 -> S_0

satisfies beta^2=-I.  Functoriality of determinant of cohomology produces a
lift beta_D of [-1] to D.  Its square is the determinant action of beta^2:

    beta_D^2
      =det R Gamma(beta^2)
      =det R Gamma(-I)
      =(-1)^chi(E_L)
      =+I.                                                       (4)

Equivalently, if alpha_D is the determinant lift induced from the selected
order-eight Q lift alpha, then

    alpha_D^4=det R Gamma(alpha^4)=det R Gamma(-I)=+I.           (5)

This cannot equal the already constructed native linear candidate, whose
fourth power is -I.

So the order-four spin lift becomes an involution on the theta determinant
line.  The selected local +i phase at q_0 is real information on S_0, but
the ordinary determinant functor cancels it between H^0 and H^1.

This is the decisive functorial obstruction.  It is stronger than observing
that projectivization forgets scalars: the scalar has already disappeared
on the determinant line before taking projective sections.


3. O(2 THETA) DOES NOT RESTORE THE PHASE

Squaring or dualizing beta_D still gives a lift whose square is +I.  In the
standard analytic theta structure, a basis of H^0(A,O(2 Theta)) consists of
the four second-order theta functions

    Theta[epsilon](tau,z),       epsilon in (Z/2)^2.

Each is even in z.  Hence the standard symmetric lift of [-1] acts by +I on
the whole four-dimensional space, and the associated Kummer map factors
through A/{+1,-1}.  This agrees with (4) and (5).

One may multiply this linear lift by i and thereby make it square to -I and
act as i I_4.  That multiplication is a new choice of mu_4 central
character.  It is not selected by determinant of cohomology, by S_0 alone,
or by beta after (3).  In projective space +I and iI have the same image, so
the Kummer representation cannot decide this choice.

Mumford's theta group supplies exactly the correct general home for such a
linear phase: its central G_m acts with its chosen central character in the
Schrodinger representation.  The existence of that theta group does not
identify its mu_4 with the native G31 center.  Such an identification is
additional marked theta-structure or metaplectic data.
