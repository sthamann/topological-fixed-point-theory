2. Why the two extensions have trivial intersection over C
===========================================================

Over C the old root cover is simply

  D/C: H^4=w.                                             (3)

The function w has two simple zeros, at (w,eta)=(0,+1) and (0,-1), and two
simple poles, the two points at infinity of the even hyperelliptic model.
Thus (3) is a cyclic C4 cover totally ramified at exactly four points.
Its only proper nontrivial intermediate cover is the quadratic cover
obtained from H^2.  That cover is ramified at the same four valuations.

By contrast, Y_q/C is the connected unramified V4 cover defined by the
three two-torsion lines.  Let E be the intersection of the function fields
of D and Y_q over C.  Since E is a subfield of Y_q, E/C is unramified.  Since
it is also a subfield of the cyclic C4 extension D/C, it can only be C, the
ramified quadratic intermediate, or D.  The latter two are ramified.
Therefore

  C(D) intersect C(Y_q) = C(C).                         (4)

This proves linear disjointness, connectedness of the normalized fiber
product, and

  [X:C]=4 times 4=16,       Gal(X/C)=C4 times V4.       (5)

The argument uses ramification type rather than coprime degrees.  Both
relative covers have degree four, so a degree count alone would not prove
(4).

3. Absolute group and the shared cubic clock
=============================================

Both D and Y_q are Galois over the z sphere:

  Gal(D/P1_z)=C12,
  Gal(Y_q/P1_z)=A4.

Their common intermediate C has

  Gal(C/P1_z)=C3.

The quotient C12 -> C3 is the action on w=H^4.  The quotient A4 -> C3 has
kernel V4 and is the same marked rotation w->omega w.  Therefore the deck
group of X over z is the fiber product

  G = C12 times_C3 A4,       |G|=12*12/3=48.            (6)

This is not C12 times A4 of order 144.  The common cubic motion is identified
once, rather than copied twice.

The Chinese remainder isomorphism C12 congruent C3 times C4 gives an exact
marked isomorphism

  G -> A4 times C4,
  (n,a) |-> (a,n mod 4),                                  (7)

where n mod 3 is the A4/V4 class of a.  Given (a,k), the inverse chooses the
unique n mod 12 satisfying

  n=q(a) mod 3,       n=k mod 4.

Thus

  Gal(X/P1_z) congruent A4 times C4.                     (8)

The C4 factor in (8) is the relative root deck of D/C.  It is not a second
family C3 clock and does not duplicate the A4/V4 quotient.

4. Minimality in the requested object class
============================================

The phrase “carries the original g” is already strong enough to require D.
Indeed, on C one has

  g=H^3,       w=H^4,       H=w/g.                      (9)

Hence C(C,g)=C(D).  A connected cover carrying w and the given g coordinate
therefore factors through D.

Likewise, a connected cover on which all three flat coefficient characters
become trivial has fundamental group contained in the intersection of their
kernels.  That kernel is precisely the connected V4 cover Y_q.  Equivalently,
the cover contains two independent Kummer roots h_0,h_1, with h_2 fixed by
h_0 h_1 h_2=1, and therefore factors through Y_q.

Any connected cover satisfying both requirements contains both function
fields.  It consequently contains their compositum and maps to X.  By (4)
its degree is at least 16 over C and 48 over z.  The normalization (1)
attains both bounds.  Minimality holds among all connected covers, not only
among Galois or cyclic candidates.

5. Genus
========

The map X -> Y_q is the base change of D -> C.  Since Y_q -> C is unramified
of degree four, each of the four branch points of D -> C has four preimages
on Y_q.  Thus X -> Y_q is cyclic of degree four and is totally ramified at
16 points.  Since g(Y_q)=5, Riemann-Hurwitz gives

  2g(X)-2
    =4(2*5-2)+16(4-1)
    =32+48
    =80.

Therefore

  g(X)=41.                                               (10)

Equivalently, for X -> C of degree 16, each of the four branch points has
four points above it with inertia C4.  This gives

  16(2*2-2)+4*4*(4-1)=32+48=80.

6. The actual quarter lift on the compositum
==============================================

On C the existing geometric quarter is

  Q_C:(z,w,eta) |-> (i z,w^(-1),-i eta/w^3).            (11)

It has order four and its square is the hyperelliptic involution over w.
On D it lifts as

  Q_D:(z,H) |-> (i z,i/H),                              (12)

so that w=H^4 goes to w^(-1) and g=H^3 goes to -i/g.

On Y_q the accepted marked lift is represented by

  (epsilon_0,epsilon_1,epsilon_2)=(1,-1,-1),             (13)

up to the already recorded V4 frame change.  It restricts to (11), has order
four, and on the three coefficient frames has square (+,-,-), up to ordering.

Because (12) and (13) have the same restriction to C, their pair defines an
automorphism of the normalized compositum:

  Q_X=(Q_D,Q_Y):X -> X.                                  (14)

It covers z->iz.  Both components have fourth power one, so Q_X^4=1; since
its base action has exact order four, Q_X itself has exact order four.  This
is one diagonal quarter action on the common field.  Taking two independent
quarter generators would change the object and is neither required nor
canonical.

7. Why this is not the joint compiler C12 clock
================================================

Let delta be the deck generator of D/P1_z,

  delta(H)=zeta_12 H.

The geometric quarter (12) satisfies

  Q_D delta Q_D^(-1)=delta^(-1).                        (15)

In particular, for the cubic deck sigma=delta^4,

  (Q_D sigma)^2=Q_D^2,

and Q_D sigma still has order four.

The compiler cover in COVERS.txt is a different root problem,

  s^12=x^4-1.

There the plain base quarter Q_12 commutes with Delta_12, and

  R_12=Q_12 Delta_12^4

has order twelve, with R_12^4=Delta_12^4 and R_12^9=Q_12.  Equations
(15) and the commuting compiler relation are inequivalent.  The present
compositum must not turn Q_X into R_12 or claim that the new C4 factor is a
second compiler clock.

What does match is narrower and exact: the common C3 quotient in (6) is the
already marked w rotation, and the q coefficient packet carries its actual
C4 covariance.  A total compiler field action would require an additional
proved map between these two root problems.  No such map is introduced here.
