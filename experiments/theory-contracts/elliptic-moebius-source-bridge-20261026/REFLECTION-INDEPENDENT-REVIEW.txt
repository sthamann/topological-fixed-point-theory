INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE CHECKERBOARD REFLECTION WITNESS
=========================================================

26 October 2026. Exact CAR sign, gauge scope and intrinsic Ward test.
No Atlas, original source or canonical contract was changed.

Verdict
=======

The negative neutral witness in GRAPH-CUT-REFLECTION-TEST.txt is valid for
the declared free theta_3 Gaussian functional and either one fixed global
reflection S or hS. The decisive point is relative and therefore insensitive
to an overall Wick or Spin convention: the already proved same-half all-word
formula fixes the sign of the normal-ordered bilocal word. Moving only its
psi test function to the opposite Schottky half changes exactly one reflected
one-particle block from positive to negative. The charged Clifford minus,
word reversal and normal-ordering prescription remain unchanged.

The witness is neutral under the one global U(1) present in the source. It is
not invariant under a separately postulated U(1) on each disconnected face,
and it is odd on each face although globally even. Thus an independent-face
gauge rule or facewise-even observable algebra removes the witness, but
neither rule is part of the tested source. The report scopes this exception
correctly.

The exact intrinsic boundary metric supplies an additional narrow negative
test. On one checkerboard face the four edge lengths add to L=2, and the
pushforward of its uniform arclength generator is

  B_push=-i[a(theta) partial_theta+a'(theta)/2],
  a(theta)=4 K(-1) sqrt(|sin(2 theta)|).

Consequently

  [B_push,exp(i theta)]=a(theta) exp(i theta),

which is not a constant spectral shift. This excludes the literal base
winding exp(i theta) as the uniform intrinsic winding operator. It does not
exclude an intrinsic uniform multiplier transported through an actual source
unitary.

1. Geometry and localization
============================

For rho=hS, recentering t by tau=t-1/4 makes rho(tau,s)=(-tau,s). Its two
Schottky halves are A_+={0<tau<1/2} and A_-={-1/2<tau<0}. The set

  Omega=p^(-1)({|x|<1})

has two disk components, one in each half. Smooth bumps may therefore be
chosen with

  supp(f_+) compactly contained in Omega intersect A_+,
  supp(g_-) compactly contained in Omega intersect A_-.

Their supports avoid the equator graph and all four branch vertices. Hence
the sign test is independent of the graph trace metric, a vertex domain or a
choice of radial push-off. Quarter rotation transports the same geometry and
witness to S.

2. The fixed Clifford sign and the relative Wick argument
==========================================================

Keep exactly the source convention

  Theta psi(t,s)=-barpsi(-t,s),
  Theta barpsi(t,s)=-psi(-t,s),

with antilinearity and reversal of word order. On A_+ the two reflected
charge kernels are the positive Gram forms

  K_bar(t,u)=-S_E(-t-u)=X_t^*X_u,
  K_psi(t,u)= S_E( t+u)=Y_t^*Y_u.

For tau=-r and u=-v in A_-, 0<r,v<1/2, the same global reflection gives

  K_psi(-r,-v)=S_E(-r-v)
               =-X_r^*X_v.                            (1)

No field phase has been changed between these formulas. In particular, the
two minus signs in Theta occur once in the reflected neutral bilocal and
cancel each other. They cannot cancel the branch sign in (1).

Choose the normal-ordered word

  F=:barpsi(f_+) psi(g_-): .                           (2)

Normal ordering removes the contractions internal to F and internal to
Theta(F). Gauge invariance separates the remaining cross contractions into
one bar charge determinant depending only on f_+ and one psi charge
determinant depending only on g_-. For one test function in each block these
are one-by-one determinants.

An absolute sign calculation is unnecessary and would depend on where one
writes the common source-order phase. The existing same-half theorem fixes
that convention: if both tests lie in A_+, the expectation of the word (2)
is the product of the two positive Gram forms. Moving only g to A_- leaves
the Wick permutation, the Clifford minus and the f block unchanged, while
(1) reverses the g block. Therefore

  omega(Theta(F)F)=q_bar(f_+) q_psi(g_-)<0,             (3)

provided the two Gram vectors are nonzero. Such bumps exist: take a product
bump in a small rectangle in each checker face. A nonzero spatial bump has a
nonzero NS Fourier coefficient, and a nonnegative time bump makes its real
exponential Laplace coefficient nonzero.

This relative proof also shows why a constant Real-spin linearization phase
cannot repair (3). It multiplies the same-half and opposite-half calculations
identically, and neutral words cancel the two charge phases.

3. Global charge versus independent face rules
===============================================

Under the source's single global gauge action

  psi -> exp(i lambda) psi,
  barpsi -> exp(-i lambda) barpsi,

the word (2) has charge zero. It therefore belongs to the global even,
gauge-invariant CAR algebra on Omega.

If one instead declared a product gauge group for the two components, with
parameters lambda_+ and lambda_-, then (2) would acquire

  exp[-i lambda_+ + i lambda_-]

and would be removed. The same happens if observables are required to be
even on each face separately: (2) contains one odd field on each face. These
are genuine ways to avoid the counterexample, but they change the observable
algebra. Disconnected support alone does not create either rule, and the
sources used by the tested Gaussian functional specify only the global
charged field and its global reflection.

4. Intrinsic length and the narrow Ward obstruction
====================================================

From the actual square-torus uniformization

  dx/dz=-i c y,       |c|=2 sqrt(2) K(-1),

and x=exp(i theta), y^2=exp(4i theta)-1, the flat arclength on every open
equatorial edge is

  d ell/d theta
    =1/(|c| |y|)
    =1/[4 K(-1) sqrt(|sin(2 theta)|)].                 (4)

The integral of (4) between two adjacent marks is 1/2. The boundary of one
inside checker face consists of four such edges, so its intrinsic perimeter
is exactly L=2.

Let B_ell=-i partial_ell on the uniform intrinsic half-density space. On the
core of smooth functions compactly supported away from the four vertices,
the unitary change from d ell to d theta multiplies coefficients by
sqrt(d ell/d theta). Setting

  a(theta)=d theta/d ell
          =4 K(-1) sqrt(|sin(2 theta)|),

gives

  B_push=-i[a(theta) partial_theta+a'(theta)/2].        (5)

The half-density connection term commutes with multiplication, so

  [B_push,M_exp(i theta)]
    =M_{a(theta) exp(i theta)}.                         (6)

Because a vanishes at the marks and varies along every edge, (6) is not a
constant ladder relation. It excludes only the word-for-word identification
of the original base multiplier exp(i theta) with a uniform intrinsic
winding for this face boundary. A multiplier exp(2 pi i ell/L), after an
actual source identification of the cyclic intrinsic coordinate and its
Spin domain, would obey a constant relation. Equation (6) neither supplies
nor excludes that transported operator.

5. Status of the reviewed claim
================================

PROVED for the named free theta_3 Gaussian functional:

  * one fixed S or hS is not reflection positive on the full global even CAR
    algebra of both inside checker faces;
  * the compactly supported neutral word (2) is a strict negative witness;
  * graph vertex conditions and boundary metric choices cannot affect it;
  * literal base winding is not the uniform intrinsic boundary ladder.

NOT proved, and not claimed by this review:

  * failure after imposing independent face gauge invariance or facewise
    parity;
  * failure for a source-supplied piecewise reflection;
  * failure of a transported intrinsic uniform winding;
  * a no-go theorem for another graph state or for the primitive P1 source.
