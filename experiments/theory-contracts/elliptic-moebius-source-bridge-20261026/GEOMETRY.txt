DIVIDING REAL FORMS OF THE ORIGINAL ELLIPTIC C2 COVER
26 October 2026

Question
========

The previous kernel audit tested only

  kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y)

on the existing curve

  E: y^2=x^4-1.

That real form has one nondividing oval.  Does the already given group
generated by

  h(x,y)=(x,-y),       Q(x,y)=(i x,y),       kappa

contain a dividing real form, a genuine bordered half and the same bond
reflection that passed the original v519 free NS reflection-positivity test?

Verdict
=======

Yes.  The omission of the deck factor h in the previous real-form test was
decisive.

Among all eight existing antiholomorphic involutions

  rho_(a,b)=h^a Q^b kappa,       a in {0,1}, b in Z/4,

exactly

  rho_+ = h Q kappa,
  rho_- = h Q^3 kappa                                      (1)

have two ovals.  They are the two dividing real forms.  Their quotients are
conformally identical annuli of modulus 1/2.  Q exchanges the two annulus
charts.

On the marked base they induce

  x -> +i bar x,       x -> -i bar x,                       (2)

and hence exchange the mu4 marks in adjacent pairs with no fixed mark.  This
is exactly the mu=+i and mu=-i midpoint-axis orbit in v519, not a resemblance
by eigenvalues.  The normal v519 checker confirms that this clock-inverting
family has square +1 spin/Fock lifts and that its bond placement is
reflection positive, while the site placement is indefinite.

There is a second exact structural closure.  The existing word

  delta = h Q^2                                               (3)

is the unique fixed-point-free holomorphic involution among h, Q^2 and
hQ^2.  It commutes with rho_+ and rho_-.  On either annulus it descends to
the free orientation-reversing map

  (t,s) -> (1/2-t,s+1/2),                                    (4)

whose quotient is a Moebius band.  Thus the same old curve contains the
full topological tower

  closed elliptic Schottky double E
      -- quotient by rho_+ or rho_- --> bordered annulus A
      -- quotient by delta          --> Moebius band M.       (5)

The three roles stay separate:

  rho_+/-  is the OS/Schottky reflection;
  delta    is the free Moebius deck on the annulus;
  Q        is the quarter clock and exchanges the two cut charts.

This repairs the too-narrow statement in ELLIPTIC-KERNEL-TEST.txt that the
existing elliptic geometry supplies no positive half.  Kappa alone does not;
the full already given h,Q,kappa group does.

The repair does not identify the finite-annulus Calderon projector with the
old one-circle csc covariance.  The actual annulus Cauchy data form a
two-boundary graph with fixed finite-width terms.  That distinction is
computed below.

1. Uniformization fixed by the original marked points
======================================================

Use O=(0,i) as the elliptic origin and T=hO=(0,-i).  The original quarter
automorphism fixes O and T.  On

  E = C/(Z+iZ)

the maps therefore have the affine expressions

  Q z       = i z,
  h z       = -z+T,
  kappa z   = -i bar z+T,
  T         = (1+i)/2.                                    (6)

The formula for kappa is forced by kappa(O)=T, kappa(T)=O and by its action
on dz.  It satisfies kappa^2=1 and kappa Q kappa=Q^-1.

Modulo the square lattice, all eight real forms are

  a b     rho_(a,b)(z)                         real locus
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  0 0     -i bar z + T                         1 oval, nondividing
  0 1      bar z + T                           empty
  0 2      i bar z + T                         1 oval, nondividing
  0 3     -bar z + T                           empty
  1 0      i bar z                             1 oval, nondividing
  1 1     -bar z                               2 ovals, dividing
  1 2     -i bar z                             1 oval, nondividing
  1 3      bar z                               2 ovals, dividing.       (7)

This also shows why integral H1 action alone was insufficient.  For example
Q kappa and h Q^3 kappa have the same linear reflection type up to the D4
frame, but the affine T shift makes the first fixed-point free and the second
dividing.

2. Direct algebraic fixed-point and infinity census
====================================================

A fixed point of rho_(a,b) can be written

  x=e^(i b pi/4)t,       y=i^a v,       t,v real.

The curve equation becomes

  v^2=(-1)^a[(-1)^b t^4-1].                              (8)

For (a,b)=(1,1) or (1,3), this is

  v^2=t^4+1.

Its positive and negative graphs close separately at the two points at
infinity, giving two ovals.  For completeness, if

  u=1/x,       eta=y/x^2,

then the two infinity points are P_+ and P_- with eta=+1 and -1.  The real
form acts there by

  eta -> (-1)^(a+b) bar eta.                              (9)

Thus P_+ and P_- are individually fixed exactly when a+b is even.  In the
two dividing cases each graph closes at one fixed infinity point.  For
(a,b)=(0,1),(0,3), equation (8) has no finite real point and (9) swaps the
infinity points, so these real forms are genuinely fixed-point free.

In the integral cycle basis A=1, B=i, let

  J=[[0,-1],[1,0]],
  K=[[0,-1],[-1,0]].

Then

  Q_*=J,       kappa_*=K,
  rho_(a,b)*=(-I)^a J^b K.                               (10)

Every rho is anti-symplectic.  For the dividing pair,

  rho_+* = diag(-1,+1),      Fix H1 = Z B,
  rho_-* = diag(+1,-1),      Fix H1 = Z A.               (11)

The fixed homology classes are exactly the directions of their two real
ovals.  Q sends A to B and conjugates rho_+ to rho_-.

3. The annulus and its exact modulus
=====================================

For rho_+=-conjugation, a fundamental bordered half is

  A_+={z=t+i s: 0<=t<=1/2, s in R/Z}.                    (12)

Its boundary circles are t=0 and t=1/2.  For rho_-=conjugation, the
corresponding half is the rotated strip

  A_-={z=s+i t: 0<=t<=1/2, s in R/Z}.                    (13)

With the square flat conformal metric, both have

  circumference L=1,
  width W=1/2,
  conformal modulus m=W/L=1/2,
  round-annulus radius ratio r=exp(-2 pi m)=exp(-pi).     (14)

There is no fitted length in (14).  Overall rescaling cancels in W/L.  The
Schottky double has modulus 2 i m=i, exactly the given elliptic curve.

The quarter clock does not act inside one chosen half:

  Q rho_+ Q^-1=rho_-.

It rotates the vertical two-boundary cut into the horizontal one.  Q^2
preserves each cut and reverses its boundary-circle orientation.  Full
quarter equivariance therefore belongs to the supplied pair (A_+,A_-), not
to a newly declared translation around one boundary component.

4. The missing Moebius operation is already in the group
=========================================================

On the algebraic curve,

  delta=hQ^2: (x,y)->(-x,-y).                            (15)

It has no fixed point: x=0 would require y=0 although y^2=-1, and it swaps
the two infinity points.  In the uniformization,

  delta z=z+T,

so delta is the nonzero two-torsion translation.  The other holomorphic
involutions h and Q^2 have fixed points.  Hence delta is the unique free
involution in this existing three-element involution set.

Because delta commutes with rho_+ and rho_-, it descends to both annuli.  On
A_+, reduction back to the strip (12) gives exactly

  delta_A(t,s)=(1/2-t,s+1/2).                            (16)

It swaps the two boundary circles and has no fixed point.  Its quotient is
the standard Moebius strip.  The same statement on A_- is obtained by Q
rotation.

Equation (16) is an exact realization of the original orientable-double
axiom inside the already existing C2 data.  Identifying this realization
with the full physical carrier still requires the field map discussed below.
It also prevents an old conflation: delta_A is the Moebius deck, while
rho_+ is the OS reflection across the boundary of the Schottky half.  The
v519 result that the deck itself is not the OS reflection is respected.

5. Spin lift square and the zero-mode test
==========================================

The Q-fixed nontrivial spin line alpha has flat character

  chi(m+n i)=(-1)^(m+n).                                (17)

It is invariant under h,Q and kappa.  Write an affine real form as

  rho(z)=c bar z+d.

For the normalized anti-linear lift on alpha, the square is the character
of the lattice translation made by rho^2:

  j_rho^2=chi(c bar d+d).                                (18)

For a=0, d=T and the four translations in (18) are

  0, 1, 1+i, i,

so the lift squares are

  +1, -1, +1, -1.                                       (19)

For a=1, d=0 and all four lift squares are +1.  In particular,

  j_(h Q kappa)^2=j_(h Q^3 kappa)^2=+1.                 (20)

The two fixed-point-free anti-linear Real lifts j_(Qkappa) and
j_(Q^3kappa) are instead quaternionic on alpha, with square -1.  The deck
factor h therefore simultaneously
creates the dividing fixed set and removes the Kramers obstruction.  This
is the cover-level counterpart of v519's Kramers-free family D.

The spin line itself passes the kernel test:

  h0(E,alpha)=0.                                         (21)

On A_+, both boundary components run in the B direction; on A_- they run
in the A direction.  Equation (17) gives holonomy -1 in either direction.
Thus both annulus boundaries carry the bounding NS spin structure, with

  spec B_Sigma={2 pi(n+1/2):n in Z},
  ker B_Sigma=0.                                         (22)

The anti-linear Real lifts j_rho in (18)-(20) must now be kept separate from
the complex-linear pullbacks on the full two-component Dirac coefficients.
Denote the latter by U_rho, U_delta and U_g.  On the universal square cover
write

  rho z=-bar z,              delta_E z=z+T,
  T=(1+i)/2.                                             (23a)

Although rho and delta_E commute on E, their displayed universal lifts do
not commute literally:

  rho delta_E(z)=-bar z-bar T,
  delta_E rho(z)=-bar z+T,

and the two right hand sides differ by the A-cycle 1.  Because the NS--NS
character (17) is invariant, the complex-linear coefficient pullbacks obey

  U_rho^2=+I,
  U_delta^2=chi(2T)=chi(1+i)=+I,
  U_rho U_delta=-U_delta U_rho.                          (23b)

Thus the bare half-period translation on the closed torus has square +I.
The order-four element is instead the combined Schottky glide.  Choose the
representative

  g(z)=delta_E rho(z)=-bar z+T.                          (23c)

It obeys

  g^2(z)=z+i,
  U_g^2=chi(i)=-I,                                      (23d)

equivalently (U_rho U_delta)^2=-I by (23b).  This is the
central-commutator mechanism missing from the earlier argument.  It uses
both NS holonomies, chi(1)=chi(i)=-1; a single core-holonomy number would
not determine it.

A concrete two-component boundary realization makes the Pin statement
equally explicit.  With gamma_t=sigma_t, gamma_s=sigma_s and

  sigma_t=[[0,1],[1,0]],       sigma_s=[[0,-i],[i,0]],
  D=sigma_t partial_t+sigma_s partial_s,

the reflected complex-linear pullback U_g flips the t symbol.  Hence

  A=sigma_s U_g                                          (23e)

is Dirac covariant, and (23d) gives A^2=-I.  Its two eigenspaces have
eigenvalues c=+i and c=-i.  On either eigenspace the actual quotient descent
operator c^-1 A has square +I.  The fiber matrix c sigma_s also has square
-I, but that algebraic square is not U_delta^2 and must not be used to
replace the central computation (23b)-(23d).

The corresponding Real lift j_g is anti-linear.  Since sigma_s is imaginary,
(sigma_s j_g)^2=+I in this convention; an anti-linear operator has no complex
+/-i eigenspace decomposition.  Therefore the c=+/-i sectors above belong
only to the complex-linear full-Dirac operator A.  They are not eigensectors
of the OS Real structure.

Equations (23b)-(23e) are precisely compatible with the original A4
order-four gluing declaration.  They do not identify the flat alpha lift of
Q with the independent Z8 spin-carrier matrix used in v519.  Establishing
that carrier identification would require the still-open field map.

The convention label Pin plus or Pin minus depends on the Clifford sign.
The invariant statement used here is the explicit square A^2=-I together
with the involutive descended quotient sector.  It concerns neither the OS
Real lift in (20) nor the quarter clock Q.  Bare Schottky rho fixes the two
annulus boundaries; the free glide delta_A swaps them.  Hence the cut Real
structure, the Moebius deck and the quarter clock occupy three distinct
operator slots.

The normally twisted bundle alpha tensor p*O(1) still has its previously
proved two holomorphic zero modes and QD=-DQ.  It is therefore not the
bundle used for the nullmode-free annulus kernel.  No zero-mode projector is
silently inserted here.

6. The actual bordered Calderon graph
======================================

The correct operator for the half A_+ is the two-boundary Cauchy-data or
Calderon projector, not the closed-torus Green kernel evaluated on one
circle.

On the product cylinder (12), let B_Sigma e_n=lambda_n e_n with

  lambda_n=2 pi(n+1/2).

A harmonic chiral spinor for D=partial_t+B_Sigma has boundary values related
mode by mode by

  f_1,n=exp(-W lambda_n) f_0,n,
  W=1/2.                                                  (24)

The orthogonal projector onto this Cauchy graph is therefore

                  1
  C_n = -------------------------
        2 cosh(W lambda_n)

        [[ exp(W lambda_n), 1 ],
         [ 1, exp(-W lambda_n) ]],                       (25)

with

  W lambda_n=pi(n+1/2).

This formula uses the product trivialization to identify the two boundary
spin lines.  Reversing the outward-normal convention conjugates (25) by a
fixed diagonal unitary.  It does not change (26), the mode weights or the
nonzero magnitude in (27).

Direct multiplication gives

  C_n^2=C_n,       C_n*=C_n,       spec C_n={0,1}.       (26)

Thus the existing geometry supplies a canonical positive one-particle
polarization on the two boundary circles, with no free temperature or width.
The off-diagonal boundary transport is

  (C_n)_01=1/[2 cosh(pi(n+1/2))],                        (27)

which is nonzero at the fixed square modulus.  The diagonal entries are the
corresponding Fermi factors.  These smoothing terms are the boundary form of
the finite theta correction found in ELLIPTIC-KERNEL-TEST.txt.

In the long-cylinder limit W->infinity, (25) tends to complementary Hardy
spectral projections on the two ends.  At the actual W=1/2 it is a coupled
two-boundary graph.  Therefore

  canonical annulus Calderon polarization: YES;
  equality to the pure one-circle csc covariance: NO.    (28)

This is a refinement, not a conflict.  The earlier calculation compared the
closed elliptic Szego kernel with a flat one-circle kernel.  Equations
(24)-(27) compute the correct bordered operator and retain the elliptic
finite-width information.

7. Exact connection to the original v519 reflection
====================================================

The deck h is invisible on the base coordinate x.  Therefore the dividing
forms (1) induce the two base reflections

  Theta_+(x)= i bar x,
  Theta_-(x)=-i bar x.                                  (29)

On the ordered marks (1,i,-1,-i), their permutations are

  Theta_+ : [1,0,3,2] = (1 i)(-1 -i),
  Theta_- : [3,2,1,0] = (1 -i)(i -1).                  (30)

They fix no mark.  Their circle cut points solve e^(2 i theta)=+i or -i,
so they lie at the silver midpoints.

This is literally v519 family D with mu=+i and mu=-i.  The current replay of
the original checker returned 34 passed, 0 failed.  In particular it proves
on its stated free carrier:

  * Theta^2=+1 and Theta clock Theta=clock^-1;
  * compatibility with the full Z8 spin tower;
  * a normalized Fock lift with square +1;
  * positive-definite bond-cut Grams for mu=i;
  * exact indefiniteness of the site cut;
  * the deck/antipode is not the OS reflection.

The checker also verifies the mu=1 mark-axis family at its corresponding
half-offset placement.  It therefore does not by itself select mu=i over
mu=1.  Selection of the midpoint orbit here comes from the independent
eight-real-form census (7): precisely hQkappa and hQ^3kappa are dividing.
The checker then proves that this geometrically selected orbit is RP
positive on the free carrier.

The new geometric content is that the original C2 cover selects the same
midpoint orbit without adding a real form.  Among the eight words, only
rho_+ and rho_- satisfy all of

  existing h,Q,kappa word,
  dividing fixed set,
  Kramers-free alpha lift,
  clock-inverting base action,
  mark-swap midpoint orbit.                               (31)

The two choices in (31) are exchanged by Q, so they are one quarter-clock
orbit rather than a new binary parameter.

What is not yet proved is an equality between the v519 pure 16-site state
and the finite-annulus projector (25), or the transport of the original
interacting field measure to alpha-valued spinors on A.  v519 explicitly
keeps the interacting algebra and OS reconstruction open.  The present
result supplies the missing geometric half and its canonical one-particle
Cauchy graph; it does not promote that remaining field map by name.
