ELLIPTIC NS GREEN REFLECTION POSITIVITY AND THE ANNULUS GAUSSIAN STATE
26 October 2026

Question and verdict
====================

Take the actual square elliptic curve E=C/(Z+iZ), its Q-invariant NS--NS spin
line alpha and the dividing real form

  rho(z)=-bar(z).

On the half 0<t<1/2, z=t+i s, use

  L=partial_t+B,  B=-i partial_s,

with antiperiodic conditions in t and s. The full free charged Gaussian Green
functional of L is reflection positive for both charge blocks and hence for
all polynomial CAR words supported in the open half. Its time-zero covariance
is exactly the second-face compression

  C=(1+exp(B))^-1

of the Calderon graph already derived on this same elliptic annulus. Thus the
Green KMS state is the labeled boundary marginal of that graph, while the
full two-face graph is its canonical purification. They are one free Gaussian
source construction rather than two one-particle formulas with matching
spectra.

This is a theorem for the explicitly named free Gaussian CAR functional. It
does not turn a two-point identity into an interacting field theorem and does
not identify this state with the primitive physical P1 kernel.

1. The actual elliptic Green kernel
===================================

Let

  e_n(s)=exp(2pi i(n+1/2)s),
  B e_n=lambda_n e_n,
  lambda_n=2pi(n+1/2).

There is no zero mode. For 0<t<1 the inverse of L is

  S(t)=exp(-tB)(1+exp(-B))^-1,                           (1)

and for -1<t<0 it is

  S(t)=-exp(-(t+1)B)(1+exp(-B))^-1.                     (2)

The two branches have jump I at zero and obey S(t+1)=-S(t). They therefore
give the inverse on the actual temporal NS domain. Although B has both signs,
each displayed product is bounded on its stated open interval.

This spectral Green operator is exactly the elliptic Szego kernel, including
normalization. Put w=(s-s')+i t and use Jacobi theta functions with periods
1 and i. For 0<t<1,

  S_E(w)=i/(2pi)
         theta_3(w|i) theta_1'(0|i)
         /[theta_3(0|i) theta_1(w|i)].                  (3)

Indeed, (3) has residue i/(2pi w), the fundamental singularity for

  L=-2i partial_bar_w,

and is antiperiodic under w->w+1 and w->w+i. The difference between (3) and
the Fourier kernel of (1) is a holomorphic section of alpha. Since
H0(E,alpha)=0, the difference vanishes. This proves the theta identity
without fitting a coefficient or a thermal parameter.

2. Reflection positivity for both charges
==========================================

The Schottky reflection is

  rho(t,s)=(-t,s).

For 0<t,u<1/2, set a=t+u. The first reflected charged kernel is

  K_bar(t,u)=-S(-a)
            =exp(aB)(1+exp(B))^-1
            =X_t^* X_u,                                (4)

  X_t=exp(tB)(1+exp(B))^-1/2.

The conjugate charge block is

  K_psi(t,u)=S(a)
            =exp(-aB)(1+exp(-B))^-1
            =Y_t^* Y_u,                                (5)

  Y_t=exp(-tB)(1+exp(-B))^-1/2.

Both X_t and Y_t are bounded for 0<t<1/2. Spectral calculus therefore gives

  integral <f(t),K_bar(t,u)f(u)> dt du
     =||integral X_u f(u)du||^2 >=0,

and the analogous identity with Y for the second charge. The statement holds
for every spatial mode and arbitrary superpositions, not only for a finite
mode sample.

In the charged field convention used in the previous full-cover proof, define
the anti-linear order-reversing reflection first on unsmeared generators by

  Theta psi(t,s)    =-barpsi(-t,s),
  Theta barpsi(t,s)=-psi(-t,s).                         (6)

The constant minus is the reduced normal-Clifford and source-order phase. It
is essential for the first sign in (4), and Theta^2=1. Moving this minus into
the opposite source-order convention is equivalent; omitting it while
keeping the displayed contractions is not.

For a gauge-invariant free Gaussian functional, Wick expansion of reflected
normal-ordered words gives exterior Gram determinants of (4) and (5).
Consequently every finite polynomial F in the positive-half CAR algebra
satisfies

  omega(Theta(F)F)>=0.                                  (7)

Equation (7) is the requested all-word result for this free Gaussian source.
It makes no claim for an interacting deformation with the same two-point
function.

3. The same state as the annulus Cauchy graph
==============================================

For the strip 0<=t<=W with W=1/2, the Cauchy relation for L is

  f_1=exp(-WB)f_0.

Its graph projector has second-face compression

  C_A=(1+exp(2WB))^-1=(1+exp(B))^-1.                    (8)

This is precisely the covariance in (4). The first-face compression is
I-C_A, precisely the covariance in (5). The off-diagonal graph block gives
the canonical purification of these two charge and face marginals.

Thus, after applying the ordinary CAR quasifree functor, the torus Green
functional has exactly the labeled boundary marginal of the annulus Cauchy
graph and the same reflected word functional on that marginal algebra. The
full graph is the canonical two-face purification fixed by the same solution
operator. No additional Araki-Wyss purification or chosen temperature is
inserted. The inverse temperature is the geometric beta_s=1 for B,
equivalently beta_angle=2pi for B_cov=B/(2pi).

4. Winding domains and the relative source
===========================================

The original winding multiplier

  u(s)=exp(2pi i s)

preserves H1_NS and obeys on that domain

  B u=u(B+2pi).                                         (9)

Because Theta is anti-linear and rho leaves s fixed,

  Theta u Theta^-1=u^-1.                                (10)

Thus the actual OS word reflection reverses the winding, as required. The
state is not claimed to be invariant under u. Rather, (9) gives its spectral
flow and the already proved relative covariance determinant

  det_F[(1-z u C_A u^*)(1-z C_A)^-1]=1-z,

for |z|<1. This determinant remains a relative Gaussian source response. It
is not automatically the physical EM or Quillen functional.

5. Quarter clock transport between the two cuts
================================================

The geometric quarter map Qz=iz does not translate one chosen boundary. It
maps the vertical half A_+ to the horizontal half A_-. In their natural
coordinates,

  z=t+i s  on A_+,
  z=s'+i t' on A_-,

one has

  t'=t,  s'=-s.                                         (11)

Therefore its fixed spin lift U_Q satisfies

  B_- U_Q=-U_Q B_+,
  U_Q C_+ U_Q^-1=I-C_-,
  U_Q u_+=u_-^-1 U_Q.                                  (12)

The constant spin-lift phase of U_Q cancels in (12) and in every neutral
Gaussian expectation. It is the already fixed alpha lift, not a new fitted
phase. Equations (11)-(12) show exactly how Q transports the two OS halves:
it exchanges the charge covariances and reverses winding. It is not an
internal time translation of either annulus.

6. What has and has not been closed
====================================

PROVED: the actual NS--NS elliptic Szego kernel equals the spectral Green
kernel of partial_t+B with the normalization (3).

PROVED: the Schottky reflection rho=-bar(z), together with the fixed constant
field phase in (6), is positive for both charged blocks and all free Gaussian
CAR words supported in 0<t<1/2.

PROVED: the full free Gaussian Green state has the labeled marginal of the
same finite-width Calderon graph already found on this elliptic annulus, and
that graph supplies its canonical two-face purification.

PROVED: winding preserves the actual Sobolev domain, is inverted by Theta,
and Q transports the paired cuts by B->-B, C->1-C and u->u^-1.

OPEN: identification of this elliptic free Gaussian source with the primitive
P1 kernel, the interacting measure, the old Hardy W8 state or physical OS
time. The theorem supplies a concrete same-E candidate for that remaining
map; it does not assume the map.
