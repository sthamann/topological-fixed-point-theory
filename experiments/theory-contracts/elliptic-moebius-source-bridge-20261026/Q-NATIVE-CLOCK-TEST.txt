1. THE ACTUAL Q ACTION ON THE GENUS TWO TWO TORSION

    L_a^q=O_C(q_a-q_(a+1)),                  a in Z/3,

with L_0^q+L_1^q+L_2^q=0 in Jac(C)[2].  The marked geometric lift is

    Q:(z,w,eta) |-> (i z,1/w,-i eta/w^3).

The divisor calculation already proved

    Q^* L_a^q = L_(-a-1)^q.                                    (1)

Thus Q exchanges L_0^q and L_2^q and fixes L_1^q.  In the basis

    e_0=L_0^q,   e_1=L_1^q,   L_2^q=e_0+e_1,

the columns of the reduced action are Qe_0=e_0+e_1 and Qe_1=e_1:

    A_Q  =   [1 1]    over F2.                                  (2)

Hence

    A_Q != I,   A_Q^2=I,   rank(A_Q-I)=1.                       (3)

The identical calculation applies to the positive packet L_a^p.  The accepted
six line result gives

    Jac(C)[2]=V_q direct_sum V_p,
    Q_bar=diag(A_Q,A_Q).                                        (4)

Therefore Q_bar is a nonidentity involution with a two dimensional fixed
space.  This does not conflict with the fact that Q has order four on C:

    Q^2:(z,w,eta) |-> (-z,w,-eta)

is the hyperelliptic involution.  It induces [-1] on Jac(C), which becomes the
identity on Jac(C)[2].

3. THE DECISIVE INTERTWINER TEST

Assume that a proposed literal source dictionary supplied an invertible map

    phi:Jac(C)[2] -> V_E8

and required phi Q_bar=J_bar phi.  By (7),

    phi Q_bar=phi.

Multiplication by phi^(-1) gives Q_bar=I, contradicting (2) and (4).  Hence

    #{invertible phi with phi Q_bar=J_bar phi}=0.                (8)

The failure is conjugacy invariant.  A change among the 720 symplectic frames
cannot turn the nonidentity element Q_bar into the identity.

    h^*(dw/eta)=-dw/eta.                                        (9)

It acts trivially only after reduction to two torsion.  If an h invariant
theta characteristic kappa with kappa^2=K_C is selected together with a spin

    h_tilde(s)=+i s  or  -i s.                                  (10)

Such a spin lift has order four although h has order two on C.  This is the
same type of central spin cocycle mechanism already present in the older A4
spin lift result: an order two base action can lift with square -I and produce

Equation (10) is only a surviving typed candidate for the native central J.

Those are the three required source premises.  Without them, identifying J
with the spin lift of Q^2 would be a new choice rather than a reconstruction.
