ORIGINAL PRINCIPLE RELEVANCE OF THE ELLIPTIC C2 ROUTE
=====================================================
26 October 2026

Question
========

Does the concrete geometry

  E: y^2=x^4-1,
  h(x,y)=(x,-y),
  Q(x,y)=(i x,y),
  kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y)

together with its Q-invariant NS spin line actually follow from the original
TFPT principles?  Does the original Moebius matching select the dividing
real-form orbit and the quotient by delta=hQ^2, or has a compatible geometry
only been recognized after the fact?

Verdict
=======

The result has two sharply different levels.

First, the elliptic object is not a freely fitted curve.  Once the already
given marked base P1 minus mu4 and the already given all-J peripheral
monodromy are supplied, its parity character forces the connected cover

  E: y^2=x^4-1.                                           (1)

Then h is its deck map, Q is a lift of the given base quarter rotation, and
kappa is the real structure of the displayed real equation.  Inside this
fixed object, the dividing real forms are not selected by an extra mark.
They form the unique unordered orbit

  {rho_+,rho_-}={hQkappa,hQ^3kappa},                     (2)

and Q exchanges its two members.  Keeping both annulus charts therefore
removes the apparent single-cut choice.  Among the three involutions in the
marked subgroup generated by h and Q, there is a unique free operation
compatible with the annulus double:

  delta=hQ^2.                                             (3)

Second, the primitive P1 and A4 sources do not construct (1), do not form
its closed Schottky double, and do not identify their physical orientable
double with either quotient E/rho_+ or E/rho_-.  The old A4 source specifies
a Moebius base, its orientable annular double and an order-four spin lift.
It gives no elliptic modulus and no map to the compiler parity cover.  The
later seam result identifies the physical and conformal circle kernels and
their base group dictionary, but explicitly leaves the covering-level
identification open.

The first additional premise is consequently one concrete equivariant
transport map, not a choice of a new curve or a preferred member of (2):

  I_cover:
    original orientable-double annulus, seam, deck and spin lift
      -> the Q-paired annulus groupoid {E/rho_+,E/rho_-},

with the original seam circle, full boundary domain, Moebius deck, Pin lift
and quarter action transported in their separate operator slots.            (4)

Given (4), the rest is rigid.  The original order-four spin condition and Q
equivariance force the unique flat spin character (-,-), hence the line
alpha used in the elliptic calculation.  Its absence of zero modes follows;
it is not an additional no-zero assumption.  The Moebius deck is forced to
be delta_A, and the two cuts remain one Q-orbit.

Thus this is a strong canonical reconciliation of two pre-existing TFPT
structures.  It is not yet an original-principle derivation of their common
physical realization.

There is also a full two-dimensional modulus obstruction to a stronger
identification.  The existing concrete flat Pin model has full orientation
annulus modulus 1, whereas the elliptic dividing annulus has modulus 1/2.
The latter realizes the broad topological A4 axiom, but it is not the same
fixed full Dirac source by a change of units.  Only its boundary circle
operator can be aligned by an overall unit conversion without further input.

2. Intrinsic selection inside the supplied C2 object
====================================================

2.1 The dividing orbit is a group invariant
--------------------------------------------

All antiholomorphic involutions generated by the supplied marks are

  rho_(a,b)=h^a Q^b kappa,
  a in {0,1}, b in Z/4.                                 (5)

Their complete fixed-locus census has exactly two dividing members, those
in (2).  Q and kappa exchange them and h preserves each.  Hence the set (2),
viewed as an unordered pair or two-object groupoid, is invariant under the
whole marked group.

There is no invariant choice of one member, and none is required.  A single
chart is a presentation.  Full quarter transport uses both:

  Q: A_+=E/rho_+  ->  A_-=E/rho_- .                    (6)

This is stronger than choosing a favorable real structure after inspecting
the answer.  Dividing type is an intrinsic topological property, and the
complete eight-element census leaves one group orbit.

2.2 The Moebius deck is conditionally forced
---------------------------------------------

In the square uniformization E=C/(Z+iZ), with T=(1+i)/2,

  h z=-z+T,
  Q^2 z=-z,
  delta=hQ^2: z -> z+T.                                (7)

The first two involutions have fixed points.  The third is a nonzero
two-torsion translation and is fixed-point free.  It commutes with both
members of (2).  On A_+ it descends to

  delta_A(t,s)=(1/2-t,s+1/2),                           (8)

which is free, swaps the two annulus boundaries and has Moebius quotient.

Thus, among the involutions in the supplied marked h,Q action, the original
orientable-double requirements select delta uniquely.  A4 alone does not
select (7) among arbitrary abstract annulus presentations.  The uniqueness
becomes applicable only after (4) says that the original deck is represented
inside this marked action.

The three relevant operations remain distinct:

  rho_+/-      Schottky real structures fixing the annulus boundaries;
  delta_A      free Moebius deck swapping those boundaries;
  Q            quarter transport exchanging the two annulus charts.         (9)

The original collar reflection and its boundary trace are not automatically
rho, delta_A or their product.  Map (4) must state that operator slot and its
domain explicitly.

3. Spin minimality
==================

3.1 The four flat characters
----------------------------

Let A=1 and B=i be the primitive cycles of E.  A flat spin line is specified
by

  (epsilon_A,epsilon_B),   epsilon_A,epsilon_B in {+1,-1}. (10)

Because Q maps A to B and B to -A, Q equivariance gives

  epsilon_A=epsilon_B.                                  (11)

There are therefore only two Q-equivariant flat spin characters:

  (+,+), the trivial odd spin line with one holomorphic zero mode;
  (-,-), the nontrivial even spin line alpha with no zero mode.              (12)

No nullity preference has been used in obtaining this pair.

3.2 The original order-four condition chooses alpha after genuine transport
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

For rho=rho_+=-conjugation and delta_E(z)=z+T, the annulus glide is represented
on the square cover by

  g(z)=delta_E rho(z)=-bar z+T,
  g^2(z)=z+i=z+B.                                       (13)

On the complex-linear full-Dirac coefficient space let U_g denote the
character lift of this pullback.  Then

  U_g^2=epsilon_B.                                      (14)

Use the fixed Euclidean two-component Clifford convention

  sigma_t=[[0,1],[1,0]],
  sigma_s=[[0,-i],[i,0]],
  sigma_s^2=+I,
  A_g=sigma_s U_g.                                      (15)

The coordinate reflection flips the t symbol, so A_g is Dirac covariant and

  A_g^2=epsilon_B.                                      (16)

If (4) is an actual Spin or Pin bundle transport, the original A4 lift and
A_g may differ only by the central sign +/-1.  A4 requires the transported
glide lift to have square -I.  Equations (11) and (16) then force

  epsilon_B=-1,
  epsilon_A=-1,
  alpha=(-,-) uniquely.                                 (17)

The genus-one parity theorem now gives h0(E,alpha)=0.  Zero-mode absence is a
consequence of Q equivariance plus the transported A4 relation, not a third
selection rule.

This conclusion must not be overstated.  If (4) were only a projective
complex operator identification allowing an independent phase i, one could
replace A_g by i A_g and manufacture square -I even for (+,+).  The original
source does not provide the C2 Spin-bundle transport which forbids that
extra phase.  Spin minimality is therefore exact once the missing genuine
bundle map is supplied, and it is not proof that the source already supplied
that map.

The anti-linear Real lifts are a different slot.  Their squares classify
real versus quaternionic structures, but an anti-linear lift has no complex
+/-i eigenspace decomposition.  The c=+/-i pair belongs to the complex-linear
operator A_g in (15), not to the OS Real structure.

4. The first additional assumption, stated minimally
=====================================================

The missing premise is not "choose the midpoint cut", "choose alpha", or
"choose tau=i" independently.  It is the single transport assertion (4).
Written as a checkable tuple, it must provide:

  I_top:   the original orientable double of the Moebius base is identified
           with A_+ or A_- as one object in the Q-paired groupoid;

  I_seam:  the original seam core and its two cut faces are mapped to the
           corresponding annulus core and traces, rather than merely to a
           homotopic circle or to the Schottky fixed boundary;

  I_deck:  the original free Z2 deck maps to delta_A;

  I_spin:  its genuine order-four Pin lift maps to A_g up to the central
           real sign, with no independent U(1) phase;

  I_clock: Q transports the complete field, domain, state and observables
           between A_+ and A_-, not only the base points;

  I_metric: if the target is the later fixed flat full Dirac source, the map
            must derive the elliptic modulus 1/2 from that source or supply
            an explicit conformal/operator transport from its modulus 1;

  I_op:    the original B_Sigma, U_Gamma and Calderon or OS operators retain
           their actual domains and are not replaced by algebraic h, delta
           or rho merely because the group orders agree.                   (18)

This is one load-bearing equivariant source map with several preservation
obligations.  The obligations prevent a structural match from being promoted
to a physical dictionary without operator action.

I_metric is vacuous only when the claim is the broad topological A4
realization.  It is essential for equality with the already fixed full
two-dimensional flat Pin operator.  A circle-unit conversion does not meet
that obligation.

The modulus tau=i is already fixed on the compiler parity cover by the
harmonic mu4 cross ratio.  It is not an extra continuous fit after (1).
Nevertheless A4 by itself does not fix that modulus, and tau=i is not
B_Sigma, a Euclidean time scale or a transfer kernel.  Those identifications
belong to I_op.

5. Smallest decisive test
=========================

The complete decision can be compressed to four exact tests:

  T1  Eight-real-form census:
      dividing(rho_(a,b)) iff (a,b)=(1,1) or (1,3).

  T2  Group transport:
      Q rho_+ Q^-1=rho_-, so {rho_+,rho_-} is canonical but neither member
      alone is Q invariant.

  T3  Marked deck:
      h and Q^2 have fixed points; hQ^2 is the free translation by T and
      descends to the Moebius glide (8).

  T4  Spin character:
      Q invariance gives epsilon_A=epsilon_B; genuine A4 transport and
      g^2=B give epsilon_B=-1; hence alpha=(-,-) uniquely.                  (19)

T1 through T4 pass on the displayed existing data.  Full-source identity does
not: the fixed full annulus moduli are 1 and 1/2.  The abort test is also
precise.  If no map satisfying (18), including I_metric for the fixed flat
source, exists, the construction remains a canonical compiler geometry with
a matching Moebius and RP pattern, not the original physical P1 seam
realization.
