2. THE SMALLEST OBSTRUCTION AND ITS MINIMAL REPAIR

Use the normalized Kummer equations

    (h_a^q)^2=(w+omega^a)/(w+omega^(a+1)),
    (h_a^p)^2=(w-omega^a)/(w-omega^(a+1)),
    product_a h_a^q = product_a h_a^p = 1.                (5)

Direct substitution gives

    s^*((h_a^q)^2)=(h_a^p)^2,
    s^*((h_a^p)^2)=(h_a^q)^2.                             (6)

Equivalently,

    s^*L_a^q=L_a^p,       s^*L_a^p=L_a^q.                 (7)

This is the exact obstruction for the isolated q packet: no s equivariant
structure can exist on it because its set of isomorphism classes is carried
to a disjoint set.  Adding the p orbit is both sufficient and minimal.
It does not replace the q packet in the v117 triangle problem.  The p packet
still has the reversed marked passport (2,3,4); it enters only as the image
required by s.

Equations (5) also construct a lift rather than merely count dimensions.
One may choose

    s^*h_a^q=h_a^p,       s^*h_a^p=h_a^q,                 (8)
    c^*h_a^q=h_(a-1)^q,   c^*h_a^p=h_(a-1)^p.             (9)

These substitutions preserve every defining equation and give s^2=c^3=1
and sc=cs.  Hence T=sc has order six on the joint cover.  Other lifts differ
from (8) by a deck transformation.  This deck torsor is the exact residual
sign ambiguity; the six point permutation by itself does not choose a
preferred lift.


3. THE TWO PLANES FILL Jac(C)[2]

For a genus two hyperelliptic curve, a nonzero two torsion class is represented
by an unordered pair of the six Weierstrass points, with even subsets taken
modulo complement.  The three nonzero elements of the q plane are

    {q_0,q_1}, {q_1,q_2}, {q_2,q_0},

and the three nonzero elements of the p plane are the analogous p pairs.
No q pair equals a p pair, even after taking the complement.  Thus

    V_q intersect V_p = 0,
    dim_F2 V_q = dim_F2 V_p = 2,
    Jac(C)[2] = V_q direct-sum V_p.                       (10)

The Weil pairing is nondegenerate on each plane: two distinct nonzero pairs
inside the same three point orbit meet in one point.  The two planes are
orthogonal because a q pair and a p pair are disjoint.  Therefore (10) is an
orthogonal sum of two symplectic planes.

The six lines in (2) are only six of the fifteen nonzero classes.  Their
tensor products generate the remaining nine mixed classes.  They must not be
identified with the six Weierstrass points themselves.  The two six element
sets carry matching T cycles, but they have different mathematical types.

Let Y_16 be the normalization obtained by adjoining two independent q roots
and two independent p roots from (5).  By (10),

    Gal(Y_16/C) = (Z/2)^4,
    [Y_16:C]=16.                                          (11)

The cover is unramified, so Riemann-Hurwitz gives

    g(Y_16)=1+16(g(C)-1)=17.                              (12)

Because the four characters span Jac(C)[2], Y_16 is the maximal connected
elementary abelian two cover of C.  This is a canonical cover.  A chosen lift
of s or T remains a torsor under its deck group, as stated after (9).

The exact native G31 calculation in E8.ST31.01 gives a normal subgroup N64
and quotient data

    G31/N64=Sp4(2)=S6,
    |Z(G31)|=4.                                           (15)

E8.CLIFFORD2Q.01 restates the same four dimensional carrier as

    |G31| = 4 * 16 * 720,

with the 16 projective Pauli translations and the full symplectic action.
This has the abstract shape of a genus two theta or Heisenberg normalizer.
The new cover supplies only a group of the required abstract 16 type: by the
Weil pairing, its deck characters form a symplectic F2^4.  No map to the
native Pauli 16 is implied by that statement.

For a principally polarized genus two Jacobian, the standard theta group of
O(2 Theta), after the corresponding theta data are chosen, provides a
Heisenberg central extension and its four dimensional Schrodinger module.
That general construction is not the missing theorem.  Two source-specific
identifications are still required:

  1. a marked central extension of this Jac(C)[2] by the same mu4 as the
     native N64, including the commutator pairing and its four dimensional
     Schrodinger representation;

  2. the full Sp4(2) action.  The old curve automorphisms used here generate
     only <c,s> congruent C6 on the six marked objects.  Even the full
     automorphism action of this fixed hexagonal branch set lies in its
     dihedral D12, far below Sp4(2) congruent S6.

The Atlas contains two related results, but neither supplies these missing
maps for the present cover.  CURVE.CODE.2TORSION.01 finds only a bare
