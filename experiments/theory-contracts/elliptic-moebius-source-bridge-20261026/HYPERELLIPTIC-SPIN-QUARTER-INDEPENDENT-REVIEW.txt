VERDICT

ACCEPTED WITH ONE TERMINOLOGICAL BOUNDARY.

For a fixed Weierstrass point W, every L in Pic^1(C) has the unique form
O(W) tensor M with M in Jac(C).  Since the hyperelliptic involution h fixes W,
acts as inversion on Jac(C), and K_C is O(2W), the asserted identity

  h^*L isomorphic K_C tensor L^(-1)

holds on all of Pic^1(C).  Applying it to S^2 isomorphic K_C proves that all
sixteen theta characteristics are h invariant as isomorphism classes.

After fixing alpha:S tensor S -> K_C, any isomorphism beta:h^*S->S can be
rescaled to intertwine alpha with the differential action of h on K_C.  The
normalization leaves exactly beta and -beta.  At a Weierstrass fixed point,
choose u with h(u)=-u.  The canonical action on du is then multiplication by
-1, so compatibility forces the fiber scalar lambda of beta to satisfy
lambda^2=-1.  Since beta composed with h^*beta is a global scalar automorphism
of S, evaluation at that fixed point proves

  beta composed with h^*beta = -I_S.

Thus beta is an order-four projective spin lift of the order-two geometric
involution.  It is not an honest C2-linearization, whose cocycle condition
would require square +I.  The main report is correct when it calls beta a
compatible spin lift.

The stated scope is also correct.  The argument selects neither a theta
characteristic S nor one of the signs beta and -beta.  It supplies no
equivariant identification with the native Gaussian E8 module and does not
remove the separately proved obstruction to identifying the literal Q action
on Jac(C)[2] with the native central J.
