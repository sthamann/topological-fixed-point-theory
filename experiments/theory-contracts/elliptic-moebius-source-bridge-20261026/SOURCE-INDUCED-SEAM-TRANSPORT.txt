Question and answer
===================

On the existing curve

  E: y^2=x^4-1,       p(x,y)=x,
  h(x,y)=(x,-y),      Q(x,y)=(i x,y),

take the already fixed Q invariant nontrivial spin line alpha with flat
character chi_alpha(A)=chi_alpha(B)=-1. Does its flat connection turn the
ramified pullback of the original seam into one canonical circle operator and
one canonical vertex rule?

It gives more than a bare monodromy list, but less than that conclusion.

PROVED:

  1. The two one sided resolutions have no free U(2) matrix. In a common
     Q compatible sheet frame their local flat transports are exactly

       T_out=I_2,             T_in=P,
       P=[[0,1],[1,0]].                                      (1)

     Reversing all orientation conventions exchanges the two names. The
     invariant statement is T_out^(-1)T_in=P.

  2. In a separately normalized local frame, a full positive or negative loop
     around any one branch point has flat alpha monodromy P, because
     P^(-1)=P. Its moving tangent Spin phase is respectively +i or -i. These
     are distinct pieces of data:

       M_flat,local^ccw=M_flat,local^cw=P,
       M_tangent^ccw=+i P,   M_tangent^cw=-i P.               (2)

     In one common global frame the four flat peripheral matrices are instead
     (P,-P,P,-P). Their product is I. The signs are the actual alpha
     translation character and cannot be discarded when comparing vertices.

  3. The two radial push offs are both exact self adjoint realizations of the
     pulled back scalar operator. Unmarked, they are unitarily equivalent and
     have two copies of the integer spectrum. As Q and deck marked objects
     they differ by P. The outer quarter action is the inner quarter action
     times the deck exchange.

     On the full flat alpha line, however, the genuine deck and quarter Spin
     pullbacks anticommute. Thus Q exchanges the two deck eigensectors. No
     single deck eigenline is a global Q preserved circle carrier.

  4. At the ramification point itself the vector field lifting
     -i partial_theta has a 1/z pole. The regular flat alpha connection does
     not cancel it. Therefore neither radial realization extends as a regular
     first order operator on all smooth alpha spinors of the ramified graph.

  5. Keeping both push offs gives the canonical pair of smooth resolutions.
     It does not give a single self adjoint four edge vertex domain. Smooth
     ambient continuity is only a one dimensional trace condition, whereas a
     self adjoint first order graph vertex requires a two dimensional
     Lagrangian trace space.

Thus the existing spin and complex choices canonically fix the two radial
resolutions and leave one exact Z2 relative cocycle between them. They do not
select a self adjoint vertex condition from the full mathematical family,
identify the original matching operator U_Gamma, or produce the old quarter
twisted Hardy domain. No free vertex matrix is inserted here.

1. Local branch calculation
============================

Write the four marks as

  a_j=i^j,             theta_j=j pi/2,             j=0,1,2,3,

and use the same local coordinate at every mark,

  w_j=(x-a_j)/a_j=z_j^2.                               (3)

For x=r exp(i(theta_j+s)),

  w_j=r exp(i s)-1=(r-1)+i s+O(s^2).                  (4)

This normalization is Q equivariant because

  w_(j+1)(Qx)=w_j(x).

The sheet deck is z_j -> -z_j. Let the incoming and outgoing root frames be

  z_- = exp(-i pi/4)sqrt(|s|),
  z_+ = exp(+i pi/4)sqrt(|s|).

For r>1, the path in (4) passes to the right of w=0 and its argument changes
by +pi. Analytic continuation sends z_- to z_+, hence T_out=I. For r<1 it
passes to the left and the continuously chosen argument changes by -pi. It
sends z_- to -z_+, hence T_in=P. This proves (1) at all four marks at once.

The labels in (1) depend on the displayed endpoint roots. Changing a sheet
frame conjugates both matrices and may exchange which one is written as I.
Their ratio P is invariant. It is the monodromy of a full small base loop.

1.1 Flat alpha transport
------------------------

The lift of a small branch disk is an ordinary z disk. Alpha is flat and
locally trivial there, so its parallel transport contributes scalar 1 on
each lifted path. The full base loop is not a closed path on one sheet. It
joins z to -z and therefore acts in that local frame on the rank 2 pushforward
by P. Clockwise and counterclockwise give P and P^(-1), which are equal. This
is a branchwise normalized statement. Transporting all four local frames to
one base fiber introduces the alpha character signs computed in Section 1.3.

There is no hidden sign from alpha itself. The closed radial components for
r<1 contract to the two points over x=0, and those for r>1 contract to the
two points over x=infinity. Consequently alpha has holonomy +1 around every
one of these four component loops.

1.2 Moving tangent Spin phase
------------------------------

A positive full base loop lifts to a half turn z -> -z. Its tangent rotates
by +pi and the Spin lift contributes exp(+i pi/2)=+i. A negative loop gives
-i. This proves the second line of (2). For the two half detours in (1), the
lifted tangent rotates by plus or minus pi/2, so a standard endpoint tangent
frame gives

  H_out=zeta_8 I,       H_in=zeta_8^(-1) P,
  zeta_8=exp(i pi/4).                                  (5)

Their fourth products are both -I. This is the bounding Spin sign of a
positively oriented contractible component. Their relative transport is

  H_out^(-1)H_in=-i P.                                 (6)

Equations (1), (5) and (6) must not be merged. Equation (1) is flat alpha
parallel transport. Equation (5) expresses the same paths in a moving tangent
Spin frame. The individual eighth root phases change with endpoint tangent
frames; the full loop statement (2) and the total sign -I are invariant.

There are two further common traps:

  * The complex coordinate factor (2z)^(-1/2) changes phase around the branch
    and is not a single valued gauge of p_*alpha.

  * The metric half density |2z|^(-1/2) is positive and contributes no complex
    phase at all.

The ramified base orbifold has local order 2, while a pi tangent rotation has
Spin lifts plus or minus i. It therefore does not define an ordinary Spin
quotient downstairs. The honest object is the upstairs alpha line together
with its central Spin extension.

1.3 The global flat pushforward and all four peripherals
--------------------------------------------------------

Let Lambda=Z A plus Z B with A=1 and B=i, and write t_lambda for translation
by lambda. In the affine torus coordinate used above,

  h z=-z+T,       T=(1+i)/2,

and the four branch reflections can be represented by

  r_lambda=t_lambda h,       lambda in {0,A,A+B,B}.       (6a)

Use the Q cyclic order

  lambda_0=0, lambda_1=A, lambda_2=A+B, lambda_3=B.

After naming the first fixed point x=1, this is the branch order
1,i,-1,-i. A cyclic relabeling changes none of the relations below.

Since r_a r_b=t_(a-b), the global peripheral relation is

  r_0 r_A r_(A+B) r_B
    =t_(-A+A+B-B)=1.                                    (6b)

Now induce the actual flat alpha character

  chi_alpha(A)=chi_alpha(B)=-1

to the two sheets. In the common sheet basis,

  rho(t_lambda)=chi_alpha(lambda) I,       rho(h)=P.

Therefore the four peripheral matrices are exactly

  (M_0,M_1,M_2,M_3)=(P,-P,P,-P),
  M_j^2=I,       M_0 M_1 M_2 M_3=I.                     (6c)

This is the requested common global frame. It also shows that the induced
flat system is reducible. In the character basis e_plus=(1,1) and
e_minus=(1,-1), P becomes Z=diag(1,-1), and the two global rank one
characters have peripheral values

  (+1,-1,+1,-1),       (-1,+1,-1,+1).                  (6d)

The decomposition does not yield a Q invariant line. Conjugation by Q sends

  lambda -> -A+i lambda       modulo 2 Lambda,

which cycles 0,A,A+B,B. The possible discrepancy by 2 Lambda is invisible
to chi_alpha. In the character basis one may take the raw Q intertwiner to
be X=[[0,1],[1,0]], so

  X M_j X^(-1)=M_(j+1),       X Z=-Z X.                 (6e)

Thus Q exchanges the two characters. With the holomorphic Spin Jacobian,

  Q_hat=zeta_8 X,       h_hat=i Z,

one obtains Q_hat^4=-I, h_hat^2=-I and the same central anticommutator as in
(7d) below.

The half form Jacobian around a branch supplies a scalar c_j with c_j^2=-1.
For oriented peripheral generators its lifted matrices have the form

  M_tilde_j=c_j M_j.                                   (6f)

The punctured sphere product relation requires product_j c_j=1. Q covariance
requires c_(j+1)=c_j. Hence there are exactly two coherent Q equivariant
choices, c_j=+i for every j or c_j=-i for every j. For the latter, the local
diag(-i,+i) convention, the common character frame gives

  (M_tilde_0,M_tilde_1,M_tilde_2,M_tilde_3)
   =(diag(-i,+i),diag(+i,-i),diag(-i,+i),diag(+i,-i)),
  product_j M_tilde_j=I.                               (6g)

The choice c=+i negates all four matrices and matches h_hat=i Z. Both choices
satisfy the product relation and Q covariance. Therefore the local half
density factor diag(-i,+i) does not by itself choose a global lift. The
isomorphism class of alpha fixes (6c) and (6d); choosing between (6g) and its
negative is the remaining deck Spin linearization or orientation convention.
The explicitly normalized source operator D fixes such a convention on its
stated twisted section carrier, up to the unavoidable overall sign. It does
not turn either character line into a Q invariant line.

GLOBAL-SPIN-KERNEL.txt performs the further normalized Szego calculation.
It shows that the actual alpha kernel fixes the unordered pair of these two
choices and diagonalizes globally, while Q exchanges their labels. Thus no
extra rank two kernel is needed, although no single Q invariant line results.

2. The two radial push offs and Q
=================================

Along x=r exp(i theta), the quantity x^4-1 traces, during one quarter turn in
theta, a circle centered at -1.

  * For r<1 that circle does not enclose zero. A continuous square root
    returns to itself after one quarter. Q preserves each radial component.

  * For r>1 it encloses zero once. Its continuous square root changes sign
    after one quarter. The algebraic Q keeps y fixed, so it exchanges the two
    continuously labelled radial components.

Let S be the quarter shift theta -> theta+pi/2 and let q be the fixed Spin
lift scalar, q^2=i. Thus q=plus or minus zeta_8 and q^4=-1. In the continuous
component frames,

  Q_in=q S,             Q_out=q P S,
  Q_out=P Q_in.                                         (7)

Both lifts have fourth power -I and eighth power I. Formula (7) is the exact
relation between the two Q equivariant radial shards. Here P is the local
sheet permutation. It records the existing branch deck on component labels,
but is not by itself the full global deck Spin pullback on alpha.

This distinction is forced globally on the actual square torus. With

  T=(1+i)/2,       h z=-z+T,       Q z=i z,

one has

  hQz=-iz+T,       Qhz=-iz+iT,       hQz-Qhz=1.          (7a)

The given flat alpha character obeys chi_alpha(1)=chi_alpha(i)=-1. Therefore
the raw pullbacks on alpha satisfy the exact central relation

  U_h U_Q=-U_Q U_h.                                     (7b)

Adding the genuine holomorphic Spin derivative factors does not remove it.
For the standard source normalization

  h_hat=i U_h,       Q_hat=zeta_8 U_Q,
  h_hat^2=-I,        Q_hat^4=-I,                         (7c)

and hence

  h_hat Q_hat=-Q_hat h_hat.                              (7d)

Consequently Q exchanges the plus i and minus i eigenspaces of h_hat. A
single deck quarter eigensector cannot be selected as a globally Q invariant
circle. This central sign comes from the already fixed alpha character and
the actual affine h,Q actions; it is not an auxiliary Spin or sheet factor.

In the common quarter interval frame used in (1), define piecewise constant

  G_j=P^j        on (theta_j,theta_(j+1)).               (8)

It removes the four P jumps because P^2=I and P^4=I. Hence it unitarily maps
the two unmarked radial operators to each other. Under the quarter clock it
changes by P. Therefore it is not an equivalence preserving the same Q mark.
The two resolutions are spectrally equal and marked differently.

3. Which operator is literally the pullback of -i partial_theta?
================================================================

For r different from 1, p restricts on each radial component with degree one.
In a flat parallel alpha frame, the literal pullback is

  B_r=-i d/dtheta                                      (9)

on two copies of H1(S1) with periodic coefficients. Equivalently, in the
common cut frame it acts on piecewise H1 C2 valued functions obeying

  Psi(theta_j+)=T_j Psi(theta_j-),

with all T_j=I on one resolution and all T_j=P on the other. These are
self adjoint domains because the T_j are unitary.

The all mode proof is immediate. On each open quarter an eigenfunction is
exp(i lambda theta)v. Going around all four quarters gives

  exp(2 pi i lambda) (product_j T_j)v=v.

Both products equal I. Hence

  lambda in Z, with multiplicity 2,                           (10)

for either radial side. The gauge (8) gives the explicit unitary equivalence.

If coefficients are written in the moving tangent frame, they are
antiperiodic because the total tangent Spin holonomy is -1. The same pulled
back connection is then

  B_pull,mov=-i d/dtheta+1/2                              (11)

up to the orientation convention. It still has the integer spectrum (10).
The naive operator -i d/dtheta on that antiperiodic domain is a different
intrinsic bounding circle Dirac operator and has spectrum Z+1/2.

Neither reading gives the old quarter spectrum Z+1/4. The order 8 Q lift in
(7) is a quarter rotation symmetry. It is not parallel transport through a
full circle and cannot be substituted for a boundary condition. A quarter
twist must still come from the original phi=pi/2 domain declaration or from
an actual source intertwiner.

3.1 Failure at the ramified seam itself
---------------------------------------

At r=1, use x=a_j(1+z^2). Since dx/dtheta=i x,

  dz/dtheta=i(1+z^2)/(2z),

and therefore

  p^*(-i partial_theta)=(1+z^2)/(2z) partial_z.          (12)

The coefficient has a pole. For a smooth local alpha section

  f(z)=f_0+f_1 z+f_2 z^2+...,

the leading result is f_1/(2z). In the base dtheta measure its square is not
locally integrable. Regularity forces the odd first jet to vanish and, for a
full invariant domain, removes the deck odd local branch. The regular flat
alpha connection has local connection form zero and cannot cancel (12).

The intrinsic Dirac operator using the smooth z coordinate is regular, but
it is a different operator. It is not the pullback of the written base
-i partial_theta. This is the exact operator obstruction at the branch.

4. Why both resolutions do not make one graph vertex rule
==========================================================

The full pullback of the marked equator has four valence 4 ramification
vertices. At one vertex the first order Green boundary form has signature
(2,2) on the four half edge traces. A self adjoint local graph domain must
choose a two dimensional Lagrangian subspace.

The outer and inner push offs each choose such a pairing. They are the two
smooth resolutions (1). Requiring a smooth ambient alpha section merely says
that all four limiting values equal the one value in alpha at z=0. That trace
space is one dimensional. It is isotropic but not maximal, so it is not a
self adjoint graph Dirac vertex condition.

Keeping both resolutions therefore gives a canonical pair, not a canonical
single vertex operator. Choosing an arbitrary U(2) scattering matrix would be
unjustified, and none is introduced here. The remaining decision is whether
the original cut problem names one face, retains both as a two face Calderon
space, or supplies a matching operator between them.
