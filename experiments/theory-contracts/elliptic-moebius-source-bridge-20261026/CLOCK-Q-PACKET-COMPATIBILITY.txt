CLOCK AND NEGATIVE SIGN PACKET
Exact compatibility of the geometric quarter lift with the v117 coefficient clock
2026-10-26

VERDICT

PROVED at the flat coefficient level.  The geometric quarter map preserves the
selected negative Weierstrass orbit.  It does not exchange that orbit with the
positive one.  Its unique order-four lift at the t=0 branch acts on the three
negative sign lines with square (+,-,-), up to their ordering, and is conjugate
to the original v117 clock U.  With the old source convention, the positive
t=0 monodromy is U^(-1).

This proves actual C4 equivariance of the coefficient local system.  It does
not yet construct the action on charged CAR fields, their state, or local
operator support.


1. THE CORRECT GEOMETRIC LIFT

Set

    f(z)=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1),
    w^3=f(z),
    eta=z(1-w^3),
    C: eta^2=1-w^6.                                                (1)

A direct calculation gives

    f(i z)=1/f(z).                                                  (2)

Therefore a lift of the quarter map z->i z has w' equal to a cube root of
w^(-3).  The marking used in the September source is

    Q:(z,w,eta) |-> (i z, 1/w, -i eta/w^3).                       (3)

The eta factor is not optional.  It follows from eta=z(1-w^3):

    eta' = i z(1-w^(-3)) = -i eta/w^3.                             (4)

For q_a=-omega^a one has

    1/q_a = -omega^(-a) = q_(-a).                                 (5)

Thus Q preserves the negative orbit {q_0,q_1,q_2}.  It also preserves the
positive orbit separately.  The apparent q to p exchange came from dropping
the minus sign in the reciprocal of q_a.

Applying (3) twice gives

    Q^2:(z,w,eta) |-> (-z,w,-eta),                                 (6)

which is the hyperelliptic involution of C over the w-sphere.  In particular
Q has order four on C.


2. ACTION ON THE SELECTED TWO-TORSION LINES

The exact coefficient packet is

    L_a^q=O_C(q_a-q_(a+1)),
    h_a^2=H_a(w):=(w+omega^a)/(w+omega^(a+1)),
    h_0 h_1 h_2=1.                                                 (7)

Pullback of the divisor gives

    Q^* L_a^q
      = O_C(q_(-a)-q_(-a-1))
      = (L_(-a-1)^q)^(-1)
      = L_(-a-1)^q.                                                (8)

The last equality uses two-torsion.  Hence Q fixes L_1^q and exchanges
L_0^q with L_2^q.  This already proves invariance of the unordered coefficient
packet.

The Kummer functions give the sharper lift.  From (7),

    H_a(1/w) = omega^2 / H_(-a-1)(w).                              (9)

Consequently every lift has the form

    Q^* h_a = epsilon_a omega / h_(-a-1),
    epsilon_0 epsilon_1 epsilon_2=1.                               (10)

The product condition is forced by h_0 h_1 h_2=1.  Formula (10) also shows why
the bare permutation of the three line classes does not yet distinguish the
order-two and order-four lifts.


3. LOCAL t=0 INERTIA SELECTS THE ORDER-FOUR LIFT

The quotient coordinate is

    t=z^4=((1+w^3)/(1-w^3))^2.                                    (11)

At q_0=-1, write delta=w+1.  Then t is a nonzero constant times delta^2.
The cover Z_q -> P1_w defined by (7) is ramified at q_0, so delta is a nonzero
constant times a local square s^2 on Z_q.  Therefore

    t = constant times s^4.                                       (12)

The positive t=0 inertia must act by s->i s.  Its square acts by s->-s and is
the nontrivial V4 inertia above q_0.

For a=0, equation (10) gives

    Q^2 h_0 = (epsilon_0/epsilon_2) h_0.                           (13)

The local condition (12) therefore forces

    epsilon_0/epsilon_2=-1.                                       (14)

Together with the product condition, this gives epsilon_1=-1.  Up to the
single diagonal V4 change of the exchanged h_0,h_2 frames, take

    (epsilon_0,epsilon_1,epsilon_2)=(1,-1,-1).                     (15)

The alternative with epsilon_0=epsilon_2 has order two above q_0 and cannot
be the inertia of the marked degree-four branch.  Thus the order-four lift is
selected by the actual local geometry, without importing a target matrix.


4. EXACT COEFFICIENT CLOCK

Choose flat character frames e_a for L_a^q after the canonical identifications
with their duals.  With the order-four lift (15), one may take

    Q e_0=e_2,
    Q e_1=e_1,
    Q e_2=-e_0.                                                    (16)

Its matrix is

    A = [[0,0,-1],
         [0,1, 0],
         [1,0, 0]],

    A^2=diag(-1,1,-1),
    A^4=I,
    det(A)=1,
    spec(A)={1,i,-i}.                                              (17)

The fixed-line sign in (16) is selected by the determinant-one coefficient
representation already proved in the original triangle source.  The other
choice is the ordinary standard S4 representation with determinant equal to
the sign character; the old source excludes it.

In the F basis printed in the September source,

    R=F^dagger U F
     = [[1,0,0],
        [0,0,1],
        [0,-1,0]].                                                 (18)

The constant signed permutation

    K = [[0,1,0],
         [1,0,0],
         [0,0,-1]]                                                 (19)

satisfies

    K A K^(-1)=R.                                                  (20)

This is a comparison after the lift has already been selected by (12), not a
definition of A from R.  Since pullback transports monodromy contravariantly,
the positive loop around t=0 acts on flat solutions by A^(-1).  Equation (20)
therefore gives exactly

    K A^(-1) K^(-1)=R^(-1)=F^dagger U^(-1) F,                     (21)

which is the positive-loop convention stated in the old source.

On C, Q^2 is the hyperelliptic involution (6).  On the coefficient cover Z_q,
which lies over the w-sphere, the same square is the nontrivial V4 deck element
shown in (17).  On their fiber product Y_q it carries both pieces.  This is why
Q^2 can be geometrically nontrivial even though each two-torsion line class is
fixed after two applications.


5. SCOPE

The q packet is therefore not merely compatible with the four A4 puncture
monodromies.  It descends equivariantly through the original C4 clock, and the
order-four action, its square, determinant, spectrum, and positive-loop
orientation all agree with the source U.

What is now exact:

  * Q preserves the selected negative line packet;
  * its local t=0 lift has order four;
  * Q^2 gives the required nontrivial V4 sign pattern;
  * the determinant-one character sum is the original v117 coefficient
    clock, with positive monodromy U^(-1).

What remains open is the next type change.  No source yet identifies these
flat coefficient frames with charged CAR field operators, their state, or
local support.  The present result supplies the coefficient clock that such a
field intertwiner must respect; it does not assume that intertwiner.
