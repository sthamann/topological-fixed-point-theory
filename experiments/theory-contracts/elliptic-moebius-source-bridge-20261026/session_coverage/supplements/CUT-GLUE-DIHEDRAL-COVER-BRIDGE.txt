CUT, GLUE AND THE EXISTING DIHEDRAL COVER CARRIER
26 October 2026

Question
========

Can the original Moebius cut and Pin gluing be connected to the already
present marked P1 and C2/C12 covers without choosing a new bundle, a new
state, or a global Cartan field on the compact normal S2?

Verdict
=======

Yes, at one precise and useful level.  The pair of Pin quarter sectors on
the existing NS orientation cover and the polarized real first cohomology of

  C2: y^2=x^4-1

carry the same real two dimensional marked dihedral representation.  The
map x:C2->P1 is already the double cover branched at the four flavor marks
mu4.  Thus there is an exact monodromy carrier from the original Pin cut and
gluing to the marked flavor P1.  It does not pass through the normal S2 and
does not require a global Cartan choice there.

This closes a representation and marking bridge.  It does not yet identify
the full two dimensional Green covariance with the original P1 boundary
state or Hardy covariance.  That state, time and field map remains open.

The changed premise
===================

The earlier search often asked for a global map from the compactified normal
S2, with its degree one determinant line, to the flavor P1.  The original
paper does not ask for such a map.  It constructs two outputs from the seam:

  * the compact normal S2 with L2=O(1), and
  * the compact seam quotient P1 with four mu4 marks.

The smallest common object required for the current question is therefore
the representation carried by the cut and gluing operators.  It need not be
a common base surface.  This weaker premise is sufficient for the exact
bridge below and introduces no geometry.

Corrected source boundary: deck reflection is not the Calderon polarization
============================================================================

The archived source starts from D_+=gamma_n(partial_n+B_Sigma) and constructs

  M_tilde=M_+ union_Sigma M_+^op,
  Sigma=Fix(tau_dbl),
  D=D_+ union_Sigma D_+^op,

It then claims on the Cauchy data

  i_C=2P_C-I=R_tau_dbl.                                (0)

This equality is not a positive bridge.  It was already disproved in the
scoped 15 October contract.  On a compact product Dirac collar the local
geometric deck lift conjugates the two-face graph projector instead of being
2P_C-I.  More generally, the principal symbol of 2P_C-I depends on tangential
cotangent direction, while a local deck lift is pointwise in the bundle.
Defining R_tau:=2P_C-I makes the equation tautological and loses the claimed
geometric identity.

The corrected operator slots are therefore separate:

  * geometric deck tau_dbl,
  * operational reflection Theta,
  * Calderon polarization P_C.

The full two-face Cauchy graph remains positive and reconstructs the signed
boundary operator B.  It does not identify any of these three slots.  No new
requirement Theta_x=R_tau is made here.

Positive bridge, prioritized test: NS Pin pair and polarized C2 cohomology
============================================================================

On the existing NS orientation cover the original Pin operator is

  A=J T1,        A^2=-I.

The two Pin sectors are the A eigenlines with eigenvalues +i and -i.  The
linear reflection R satisfies

  R A R=-A=A^{-1}.

Hence, in the ordered (+i,-i) basis,

  A=diag(i,-i),       R=[[0,1],[1,0]].                 (1)

This is the standard real D4 representation after complexification.

Now use only the existing parity cover C2 and its already marked base map:

  p:C2->P1_x,   p(x,y)=x,
  Q2(x,y)=(i x,y),
  kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y).

The branch divisor of p is exactly mu4.  Infinity has two unramified points.
For the holomorphic differential eta=dx/y,

  Q2* eta=i eta,
  Q2* bar(eta)=-i bar(eta),
  kappa eta=bar(eta),
  kappa Q2* kappa=(Q2*)^{-1}.                           (2)

Equations (1) and (2) give the explicit intertwiner

  Pin +i line  -> C eta,
  Pin -i line  -> C bar(eta),
  A            -> Q2* on H1(C2,R),
  R            -> kappa on H1(C2,R).

This is stronger than matching two eigenvalues.  In the real basis

  a=Re(eta),       b=Im(eta),

write the intersection form, quarter rotation and real reflection as

  Omega=[[0,1],[-1,0]],
  Q=[[0,1],[-1,0]],
  K=[[1,0],[0,-1]].

Then, exactly,

  Q^T Omega Q=Omega,
  K^T Omega K=-Omega,
  K Q K=Q^{-1}.

Thus Q preserves the symplectic intersection pairing, while K is
the expected anti-symplectic reflection.  For the positive Hodge L2 metric
g, normalized to the identity in this basis,

  Q^T g Q=g,          K^T g K=g.

So the full real carrier is positively polarized and the two generators are
Hodge isometries.  There is one essential type distinction.  The linear
pullback K swaps H^{1,0}=C eta with H^{0,1}=C bar(eta).  It does not preserve
the positive holomorphic line by itself.  If C denotes coefficient
conjugation, the anti-linear operation C K fixes each of those quarter
eigenlines.  This reproduces the two different NS facts: the linear Green
Clifford reflection swaps the quarter sectors, while the anti-linear
reflection preserves each sector.  It still does not identify their Green
covariance or Fock state with the Hodge polarization.

The comparison is made in the category of real representations.  Pullback by
kappa is complex linear on complex-valued cohomology and swaps the two Hodge
types; the separate coefficient conjugation is anti-linear.  Neither is
being confused with the linear Clifford factor in the Green positivity form.
On the four marked points, kappa acts as z->1/z,
because every mark lies on the unit circle.  Thus it has exactly the same
marked permutation as the paper's D4 reflection.  Globally, one is an
antiholomorphic real structure and the other is the holomorphic Moebius map
z->1/z.  The proved dictionary is the marked real representation and not an
identity of those two maps on all of P1.

This is more than a count of four points.  It preserves the quarter
eigenvalues, the reflection conjugation law, and the actual branch marking.
It also explains why one fixed Pin eigenline is insufficient: reflection
exchanges the two conjugate cohomology lines just as the Green reflection
exchanges the two Pin sectors.

The same reflection also inverts the existing C12 compiler clock
===================================================================

On the already forced common cover

  C12: s^12=x^4-1

retain the previously fixed marked clock

  R12=Q12 Delta12^4,   Q12(x,s)=(i x,s).

Complex conjugation is a canonical real structure of this same equation:

  kappa(x,s)=(bar x,bar s).

It inverts both factors and therefore

  kappa R12 kappa=R12^{-1}.                            (3)

The old power relations remain unchanged:

  ord(R12)=12,
  R12^4=Delta12^4,
  R12^9=Q12.

Under C12->C2, y=s^6, the factor Delta12^4 acts trivially on y.  Thus R12
descends to Q2 and equation (3) descends to the reflection law in (2).  This
adds reflection to the already established clock dictionary.  It does not
identify cover degree 12 with clock order 12 and does not add a new cover.

Cut and gluing picture
======================

Cut the Moebius band along its core.  The orientation cover has two boundary
copies related by the Pin gluing.  Applying the gluing twice produces the NS
sign, so the gluing operator has square -I and quarter eigenvalues +i,-i.
The elliptic cover C2 packages exactly those two conjugate quarter lines as
eta and bar(eta).  Its projection forgets the sheet and leaves the four
branch values mu4 on P1.  Reflection swaps the two quarter lines and inverts
the rotation.  This is the whole proved geometric content of the bridge.

The compact normal S2 remains the separate normal index surface.  Its
degree one line selects the weak doublet, but no source passage makes it the
base of C2, and none is needed for the representation result above.

Cable and knot terminology
===========================

No cable construction is needed for either bridge.  A literal audit of the
original paper corpus found zero technical occurrences of cable, cabling,
knot, torus-knot, mapping-torus, orientation-double and Kabel.  German
"Knoten" occurrences denote graph vertices or spectral zeros.  The original
vocabulary is "oriented double cover", "cut geometry", "gluing" and "spin
lift".  The maps used here are the existing cyclic branched covers and their
induced cohomology actions.
Calling them a cable would add a knot or solid torus structure that has not
been supplied by the seam sources.  The useful cabling intuition is only the
elementary one that repeated gluing records powers of one monodromy.  The
proof uses the displayed cover maps and operator relations instead.

Status
======

PROVED: the exact real D4 representation intertwiner between the NS Pin pair
and H1(C2,R), including the mu4 branch marking.

PROVED: reflection inversion of the fixed C12 marked clock and its descent
to C2.

EXCLUDED IN GENERAL: geometric deck reflection equals 2P_C-I.  The archived
formula is retained only as a corrected source claim.  The three reflection,
deck and polarization slots remain distinct.

NOT REQUIRED: a global Cartan choice or a map from the normal S2 to flavor
P1 for these representation statements.

OPEN: an operator map identifying the full NS Green covariance, time,
support and field algebra with the original P1 boundary kernel.  The present
bridge supplies one exact polarized monodromy and reflection carrier.  Its
selection as the original physical carrier, and the state and field map,
remain open.
