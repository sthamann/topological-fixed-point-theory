This does not revise the earlier Atlas criterion, which already allowed local

The construction does not yet identify this package with the old quarter Hardy

the next source question. The compact H dynamics is exactly periodic. This
rules out real time mixing, but the archived clustering clause does not state
that it means mixing for tau_H. It therefore remains a typing question, not a
failure of the seed axiom.

5. The finite channel rotation test

The relative open channel partition function proved for this exact cover is

  Z_rel=2 product_(n>=1)(1+exp(-n pi))^2.

The factor two is the transverse zero mode. After dividing only this ground
degeneracy and writing q=exp(-pi),

  Z_rel/2 = 1+2q+3q^2+6q^3+... .

The coefficient 2 of q is already enough: at the finite retained cylinder the
channel amplitude contains excited closed channel contributions. Rotating the
annulus therefore does not identify the state with a single Hardy vacuum.
Boundary conformal field theory says the same structurally: the open trace is
equal to propagation between boundary states in the closed channel. Vacuum
projection follows only in a long channel limit, not merely from exchanging
the two directions at a finite modulus.

The original tau=i datum does not change this conclusion. At a modular fixed
point the modulus returns to itself, while spin structures, boundary states and
characters can still transform nontrivially. The nonzero q coefficient is an
operator level witness that fixed geometry alone has not selected a fixed
vacuum vector.

This also explains why the exact v526 cut state is useful but cannot supply the
missing equality. v526 proves that a pure NS circle state can restrict to a
faithful thermal half algebra and constructs its standard form. Its own T4 test
shows that the geometric clock transfer is not its modular flow. Moreover its
one particle modular energies

  2 log cot(pi/48),  2 log cot(7pi/48)

are not the present nonzero modular ladder n pi. The two results share the
cut to KMS mechanism, not one time generator.

The other requested originals agree with this typing. v519 proves purity of

6. Smallest next test for the W8 source map

The next claim should be local and operator specific. It is enough to construct
one compatible family of maps

  iota_I:A_W8(I) -> CAR(L2(I,C2))''

for proper w intervals, compatible under inclusion, such that the chosen W8
field has the same reflected correlations and iota intertwines its declared
source time with the H dynamics. No global one particle unitary and no equality
of the entire Hardy and KMS representations is required.

The first decisive test is to derive a candidate iota(W8) from an actual field
dictionary through this same Dirac field, including the b boundary and Pin
cocycle, and compare its two point function in C with the already computed old
Hardy W8 two point function. If the q coefficient or the zero mode contribution

does not preserve the source state. Equality is only a necessary test. It gives
at most an isometry of the linear one insertion GNS span; it does not supply a
local field map, products or higher word correlations. Those require the
operator dictionary first and then all compatible n point functions. Merely
matching annulus partition functions, one two point function or abstract type
III local factors would not pass this test.
