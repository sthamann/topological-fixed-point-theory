Verdict
-------

There is a real reduction of the origin problem.

1. A full free CAR source is not needed to reconstruct the P1 Hilbert space,
   state and positive collar time.  The stronger operational seed already
   contains a local Euclidean algebra, its time translations, reflection and
   reflection positive state.  The Osterwalder Schrader quotient reconstructs
   the cyclic Hilbert space and its positive time semigroup directly.

2. A global unitary from a preselected Fock space onto the whole P1 Hilbert
   space is not needed.  A state, reflection and time preserving local star
   homomorphism on the cyclic subalgebra generated by the marked E8 fields is
   sufficient.  Equality of its OS word kernel then produces the unitary
   intertwiner on that cyclic closure automatically.

3. A global Cartan basis and five physical CAR modes are not needed to obtain
   the internal P2 packet.  For the already derived rank five carrier
   E=E3 direct sum E2, the half spin module Lambda_even(E) and the Clifford
   action are basis free consequences of the natural E plus E* pairing.
   This does not make those five internal slots physical spacetime fermions.

The earliest still missing operator is therefore much smaller than the former
"P1 equals a complete free CAR theory" demand.  It is one localized map of the
marked extension generator, together with the already fixed base current net,
into the actual operational seed algebra.  The original source does not yet
write this map.  Its primitive spectral action also does not define a field
dependent D_rel[Phi] from which the map could be differentiated.

This is a scoped origin advance.  It neither identifies the present NS cover
Green function with P1 nor derives a physical E8 field from c3 alone.

2. The first genuine P1 operator relation
------------------------------------------

Let A_+ be the positive time part of the actual operational seed algebra.  In
the source convention the OS form is

  <[F],[G]>_OS = omega(Theta(F) G),

with the adjoint included in Theta as declared there.  In the convention in
which reflection and adjoint are written separately it is

  <[F],[G]>_OS = omega(Theta(F*) G).                       (2.1)

Reflection positivity says that this is positive semidefinite.  Quotient its
null space N and complete:

  H_OS = closure(A_+ / N),       Omega = [1].              (2.2)

The actual Euclidean translation has the operator relation

  T_s [F] = [tau_s(F)],          T_(s+t)=T_s T_t.           (2.3)

Under the regularity and null stability conditions used in OS reconstruction,
T_s is a strongly continuous symmetric contraction semigroup and hence

  T_s = exp(-s H_OS),            H_OS >= 0.                 (2.4)

Equations (2.1) to (2.4) are the first operator relations furnished by the
P1 operational seed itself.  They are independent of a CAR presentation,
quasifree state, Cartan basis or global Fock vacuum.

This is precisely the type of theorem proved for a Euclidean C star net by
Schlingemann, From euclidean field theory to quantum field theory,
arXiv:hep-th/9802035.  Lemma 3.1 constructs the contraction semigroup and its
positive generator from a regular reflection positive functional.  Theorem
3.4 reconstructs positive energy Poincare covariance under the stronger
Euclidean covariance hypotheses.  Theorem 3.7 reconstructs the local net when
the time zero condition is available.  The original Osterwalder Schrader
sources are Commun. Math. Phys. 31 (1973) 83 to 112 and 42 (1975) 281 to 305.
The distinction matters here: the present operational tuple is enough for the
collar Hilbert space and time semigroup asserted by its own theorem, while a
complete Minkowski Haag Kastler net still requires the stated covariance,
regularity and time zero hypotheses.

The new whole NS orientation cover calculation gives a concrete realization
of (2.1), not a replacement for it.  Its exact factorization

  K(t,s) = B_t* B_s

identifies the OS quotient with the closure of the ranges of B_t and proves
positivity for both charge blocks and all transverse modes.  It still does
not say that its free Dirac field is the local insertion algebra of P1.

Suppose there is a localized star homomorphism on the needed cyclic algebra

  iota : B_8 -> A_loc                                             (3.1)

such that for all positive time words X,Y

  iota(XY)=iota(X)iota(Y),       iota(X*)=iota(X)*,                (3.2)
  iota(Theta_8 X)=Theta iota(X),                                  (3.3)
  iota(alpha_s X)=tau_s iota(X),                                  (3.4)

Then

  <V_0[X],V_0[Y]>_P1
    = omega(Theta(iota(X)) iota(Y))
    = omega_8(Theta_8(X) Y)
    = <[X],[Y]>_8.                                                (3.7)

Thus null vectors map to null vectors.  Completion extends V_0 uniquely to a

on this cyclic subspace.  No independently selected global unitary is an

This also avoids demanding a free CAR origin.  CAR is one realization of the

------------------------------------

The fixed Ext_H(U) theorem says that the extension is generated over

by the marked weight one sector M_h with h=(s,f).  Therefore it is not
necessary to start by mapping all 248 currents or a complete free fermion

  localized operator X_h(f) in A_loc carrying the marked h sector; verify
  its normalized Q system product with U, its adjoint sector, and the three

If (4.1) holds, the unique marked extension generates the rest, and (3.6) to
(3.8) reconstruct its cyclic representation and time without a global Fock

The source test is immediate and decisive.  The original operational seed
names A_loc but does not exhibit iota_U or X_h.  The definition of the local

      + (i pi/2) Delta eta_Sigma,                                 (4.2)

for a declared profile f.  It does not give D_rel[Phi], D_ref[Phi], or a

local algebra and state from the start.


5. P2 already gives the internal Clifford packet without a Cartan choice
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The carrier source states

  E = E3 direct sum E2,           rank E = 5,
  S+ = Lambda_even(E),            dim S+ = 16.

See

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--02_carrier_source/sections/
  001-subsection-carrier-algebraic-normal-form.tex, lines 17 to 32 and
  238 to 252, and
  002-subsection-internal-carrier-stabilizer-and-one-family-pa.tex,
  lines 197 to 330.

This construction is intrinsically Clifford.  For v in E and lambda in E*,
let epsilon(v) be exterior multiplication and iota(lambda) contraction on
Lambda(E).  They obey

  {epsilon(v),epsilon(w)}=0,
  {iota(lambda),iota(mu)}=0,
  {iota(lambda),epsilon(v)}=lambda(v) I.                           (5.1)

Hence

  c(v+lambda)=epsilon(v)+iota(lambda)                             (5.2)

represents the Clifford algebra of E plus E* with its canonical split
quadratic form.  The even Clifford algebra preserves Lambda_even(E), whose
dimension is 2^(5-1)=16.  Equations (5.1) and (5.2) use only the evaluation
pairing E* tensor E -> C.  An adapted basis gives the familiar five CAR or
Jordan Wigner matrices, but that basis is a coordinate description of the
module, not an additional origin premise.  A Hermitian metric is useful for a
unitary real form; it is not needed for the algebraic Clifford module itself.

Riemann Roch can determine a five dimensional section space in the declared
geometric setting, and P2 separately fixes the 3 plus 2 split.  Neither fact
identifies these internal sections with physical boundary fermion modes.  The
correct gain is narrower and useful: no global Cartan choice and no physical
five mode CAR vacuum are required to construct the internal 16.


6. The second idea and why it stops earlier
-------------------------------------------

A tempting direct P1 route is to define local insertions as spectral action
jets.  For a supported bounded zeroth order perturbation V one would formally
obtain

  delta S_prim(V)
   = chi_seed^(-1) Tr(f'(D_rel/chi_seed) V_rel)
     - chi_seed^(-1) Tr(f'(D_ref/chi_seed) V_ref)
     + delta eta,                                                 (6.1)

with divided differences replacing f' in higher noncommuting derivatives.
An eighth jet along a correctly typed E8 invariant perturbation would be a
direct candidate source for a weight eight observable, without first choosing
free CAR fields.

This route fails its first premise in the current original.  No map from a P2
carrier field or marked h sector to V_rel and V_ref is defined, and the test
profile f is declared rather than derived.  Consequently (6.1) is not yet an
operator in the actual local source algebra and its eighth derivative cannot
be identified with W8.  Pursuing higher jets before constructing that first
supported perturbation would rebuild the missing dictionary under another
name.  The cyclic OS route in Sections 2 to 4 is therefore the stronger path.
