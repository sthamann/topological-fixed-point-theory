LOCAL E8 COMPLETION, ORIGINAL SOURCE TIME AND CLUSTERING
27 October 2026

Verdict: no completion satisfies the four requirements below simultaneously.
This is a theorem for a fixed comparison class, not a no-go for P1 or TFPT.
It replaces an unjustified appeal to uniqueness of the whole H ground space
by a concrete local correlation test.

1. The exact comparison class

Retain these four requirements together:

C1. The source D8 current embedding and its unitary level-one lattice
    realization are retained. Complete by a local even unimodular lattice
    extension on the same eight Cartan channels. The two possibilities are
    Ls=D8 union(s+D8) and Lc=D8 union(c+D8).

C2. The actual source time on the completed algebra remains
    H=8(L0+delta.a0), delta_j=(j+a)/8-1/2, 0<a<1,
    and Omega is the original neutral vacuum. The clustering parameter is
    explicitly the positive Euclidean transfer exp(-tH).

C3. The transported source charge remains q=(1,...,1) and q maps to twice
    an E8 root, as in the existing marked dictionary. All completed local
    E8 currents are included in the tested observable net, rather than
    retaining only D8 observables on an enlarged field Hilbert space.

C4. For bounded local observables X, the connected vacuum correlation
    <Omega,X* exp(-tH) X Omega>-|<Omega,X Omega>|^2 tends to zero.

The complete source bridge has not established C2 and the full observable
part of C3 from P1. C1 and the charge dictionary in C3 are also retained
comparison premises, not a claim of their primitive physical derivation.

2. Why the marked extension fails the clustering test

The twice-root condition implies q/2 is a lattice vector. It belongs to Ls
but not Lc. Thus C1 and C3 select Ls independently of the target frame.

The old all-charge calculation gives exactly two H zero states in Ls:

  Omega=|0>, psi=|p*>, p*=(1,1,1,1,-1,-1,-1,-1)/2.

In particular |p*|^2=2, delta.p*=-1, Q(p*)=0, and L0(psi)=1.
The zero energy of psi is not a second conformal vacuum.

With conventional level-one root modes,

  J_(p*),-1 Omega=psi, [H,J_(p*),-1]=0.

Choose a smooth current test function supported in a proper interval whose
relevant Fourier coefficient is nonzero. The hermitian combination of the
p* and -p* current smearings is F, with <psi,F Omega> nonzero.
This is an actual local current in the stipulated E8 affine net. In this
net the smooth hermitian current is essentially self-adjoint and its
unitaries X_lambda=exp(i lambda F) belong to the interval algebra.

Since Omega is in the domain of F,

  d/dlambda <psi,X_lambda Omega>|_(lambda=0)=i<psi,F Omega> != 0.

Some arbitrarily small nonzero lambda therefore gives a BOUNDED local
observable X with <psi,X Omega> != 0. This removes any dependence on using
an unbounded field as the test observable.

Let P0 be the orthogonal projection onto ker H. The spectral theorem gives
strongly exp(-tH) -> P0. Consequently

  lim_(t->infinity) [<Omega,X* exp(-tH) X Omega>
                     -|<Omega,X Omega>|^2]
   = ||P0 X Omega||^2-|<Omega,X Omega>|^2
   = |<psi,X Omega>|^2 > 0.

This contradicts C4. The original Cartan Ward values select Omega among
normal ground densities, but do not remove psi or this local matrix element.

3. Why switching chirality does not satisfy the same requirements

In Lc the same H has a unique zero state Omega. Thus exp(-tH) tends strongly
to |Omega><Omega| and the displayed Euclidean correlation clusters for
every bounded X. However q/2 is not in Lc. No lattice isometry can both map
Lc to E8 and map q to twice a root: inverse linearity would put q/2 in Lc.

The two extensions exhaust C1. Hence none obeys C1 to C4 together.
This conclusion uses full charge-lattice minima, not a finite root census.

4. A sector energy penalty does not preserve the same local time

H_epsilon=H+epsilon P_s, epsilon>0, removes the extra zero spectrally.
P_s is the global s-sector projector. In the fixed irreducible, multiplicity
one local order-two simple-current extension B of A_D8, an automorphism
fixing A_D8 pointwise acts on the s intertwiner with a scalar lambda_s.
The nonzero fusion channel s times s to 0 forces lambda_s^2=1. The relative
automorphism group is therefore the discrete dual Z2.

For two strongly continuous local time evolutions with the same interval
motion, the same action on A_D8 and the same vacuum representation, their
pointwise relative automorphism is a continuous path in this Z2 starting
at the identity. It is identically the identity. A nonzero sector energy
penalty cannot furnish another such local time evolution.

For example exp(it epsilon P_s) assigns the phase exp(it epsilon) to
vacuum-to-s vectors. This cannot obey the local s times s product rule for
all real t unless epsilon=0. A modified D8 dynamics or a different local
field construction is outside this statement.

5. What this settles, and what it leaves as the actual source question

This exact comparison route is closed. Adding the marked local E8 fields
while leaving the original source time and its neutral vacuum unchanged
cannot also supply clustering on all those fields. Equal spectra, a chosen
initial density, or a global energy shift on s do not repair the route.

The already derived even CAR/D8 observable algebra has no such transition:
every one of its operators preserves the D8 superselection sectors, so
<psi,X Omega>=0. Neutral total charge is not trivial D8 sector charge.

P1 defines its physical admissible local net by restriction of source
operators that, together with their adjoints, preserve H_adm. It does not
define that net by arbitrary global compression, nor has it supplied the
local spin field or identified its physical transfer with this H.

The next origin calculation must therefore derive which local operators
are admissible and how the original OS transfer acts on them. In
particular it must decide whether the E8 charged fields belong to the
physical observable net with this time, or to an internal/field structure
with a different, source-derived time relation. A new relation must be
derived from the original data; it cannot be introduced just to evade the
test. The geometric E8 and previously proven clock and cover results are
unaffected in their existing scopes.
