2. ACTION OF THE FROZEN NATIVE CUBIC ON THE PAULI QUOTIENT

The frozen sigma is an order-three Clifford unitary with complex trace one.
Its conjugation action on End(C^4) consequently has trace

  tr(Ad_sigma)=tr(sigma) conjugate(tr(sigma))=1.

In the Pauli basis this adjoint action is a signed permutation.  A projective
Pauli fixed by an order-three element cannot acquire sign -1: cubing such an
action would still give -1, contradicting sigma^3=I.  Every fixed Pauli thus
contributes +1 to the adjoint trace.  Since the identity is already fixed and
the trace is one, no nonidentity Pauli is fixed.  Equivalently,

  dim_F2 (N64/mu4)^sigma=0,
  chi_sigma(x)=(x^2+x+1)^2.

Jac(C)[2]=V_q direct-sum V_p.  The two order-three actions belong to the same
fixed-point-free symplectic conjugacy class.
