1. THETA CHARACTERISTIC AND PARITY

For every Weierstrass point r on C, 2r is a canonical divisor.  Hence

  S_0=O_C(q_0+q_1+q_2-K_C)

has degree one and 2S_0 is isomorphic to K_C.  Moreover

  div((w^3+1)/eta)=q_0+q_1+q_2-p_0-p_1-p_2,

so exchanging the q and p sides leaves S_0 unchanged.

The even-parity argument is valid.  An effective degree-one theta
characteristic on a genus-two hyperelliptic curve is O_C(r) for a Weierstrass
point r.  If S_0 were one of these six classes, use the q expression when r is
a q point and the equivalent p expression when r is a p point.  Cancelling r
and replacing K_C by twice another point in the same triple would make the
difference of two distinct points principal.  That would define a degree-one
map to P1, impossible for a genus-two curve.  Thus h^0(S_0)=0 and S_0 is even.

2. UNIQUENESS FROM THE CUBIC SOURCE SYMMETRY

The automorphism c cycles each of the q and p triples, so it fixes S_0.  On

  Jac(C)[2]=V_q direct-sum V_p

its restriction to either two-dimensional F2 plane has order three and no
nonzero fixed vector.  Therefore Jac(C)[2]^c=0.  Since theta characteristics
form an affine Jac(C)[2] torsor, S_0 is the unique c-invariant theta
characteristic.  This uses the already selected source action rather than an
additional theta marking.

3. Q LIFT AND PULLBACK CONVENTION

The marked map Q preserves S_0 and fixes q_0.  At q_0, eta is a local
coordinate and

  Q^*eta=i eta,       Q^*deta=i deta.

For a square-root-compatible map alpha:Q^*S_0->S_0, its fiber scalar lambda at
q_0 therefore obeys lambda^2=i.  The fourth pullback iterate has value
lambda^4=-1 at the fixed point and is a global scalar, proving

  alpha^4=-I,         alpha^8=I.

The composition

  beta=alpha composed with Q^*alpha:(Q^2)^*S_0->S_0

is type-correct.  Replacing alpha by -alpha leaves beta unchanged.  In the
stated pullback convention its fiber value at q_0 is lambda^2=+i, and
beta^2=alpha^4=-I.  Thus Q selects the sign-independent order-four projective
spin lift of h=Q^2.  This is not an honest C2-linearization, whose cocycle
square would be +I.

4. SCOPE

The proof now selects the even spin line S_0, the Q lift up to an overall sign
that cancels in beta, and the induced local +i phase of beta.  It does not construct an
equivariant identification of the resulting theta or spin module with the
native four-dimensional Gaussian E8 module or its central generator J.  The
separate obstruction to the literal identification with the Gaussian code
quotient V=L/(1+i)L therefore remains in force.  That quotient labels Pauli
contexts, not Pauli translations.  It is not an obstruction to the correctly
typed theta target Jac(C)[2] -> N64/mu4.  For that target the source cubic and
the frozen native sigma both have zero-dimensional fixed space; the marked
symplectic and Heisenberg intertwiner remains to be constructed.
