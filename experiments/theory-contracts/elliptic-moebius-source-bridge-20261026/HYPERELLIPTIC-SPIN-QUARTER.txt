1. ACTION OF THE HYPERELLIPTIC INVOLUTION ON Pic^1

Choose a Weierstrass point W.  It is fixed by h and the hyperelliptic fiber at
W is 2W, so

    K_C isomorphic O_C(2W).                                      (1)

Every degree one line bundle has a unique expression

    L=O_C(W) tensor M,       M in Jac(C).                         (2)

The hyperelliptic involution induces [-1] on Jac(C).  Hence

    h^*L = O_C(W) tensor M^(-1)
          = K_C tensor L^(-1).                                  (3)

Equivalently, for every degree one divisor class D,

    hD is linearly equivalent to K_C-D.                          (4)

This argument takes place on all of Pic^1.  It does not assume that D is an
effective point divisor.


2. ALL SIXTEEN THETA CHARACTERISTICS ARE INVARIANT

Let S be any theta characteristic:

    S^2 isomorphic K_C.                                          (5)

Equations (3) and (5) give

    h^*S isomorphic K_C tensor S^(-1) isomorphic S.               (6)

There are 2^(2g)=16 such line bundles at genus two.  Therefore h fixes every
theta characteristic as an isomorphism class.  This is stronger than the
modulo two statement that h acts trivially on Jac(C)[2]: it identifies each
member of the affine theta characteristic torsor with its pullback.


3. THE COMPATIBLE LIFT SQUARES TO -I

Fix an isomorphism

    alpha:S tensor S -> K_C.                                     (7)

Because of (6), choose an isomorphism beta:h^*S->S.  The two maps from
(h^*S)^2 to K_C obtained from alpha, beta, and the differential action of h
on K_C differ by a nonzero complex scalar.  Rescaling beta by a square root
makes the diagram compatible with (7).  Exactly two compatible choices
remain, beta and -beta.

Now evaluate at any Weierstrass fixed point.  Take a local coordinate u with

    h(u)=-u.                                                      (8)

Then h^*(du)=-du.  If beta acts on the one dimensional spin fiber at that
point by the scalar lambda, compatibility with alpha gives

    lambda^2=-1,
    lambda=+i or -i.                                             (9)

The square beta composed with h^*beta is a holomorphic automorphism of S and
therefore a constant scalar on the connected compact curve.  Its value at the
fixed point is lambda^2=-1.  Consequently

    beta composed with h^*beta = -I_S,                            (10)

so each compatible lift has order four.  Changing beta to -beta changes its
quarter phase sign but leaves (10) unchanged.
