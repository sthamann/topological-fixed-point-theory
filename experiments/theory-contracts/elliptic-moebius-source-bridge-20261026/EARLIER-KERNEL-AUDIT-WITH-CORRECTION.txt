Verdict

It provides a canonical nullmode-free ELLIPTIC Szego kernel for the bare

No flat Moebius comparison is needed to establish these statements.  That

1. Which elliptic spin structure is actually fixed
===================================================

Write E=C/(Z+iZ), with a and b the two primitive cycles.  A spin structure
has signs (epsilon_a,epsilon_b) in {plus or minus 1}^2.  The quarter rotation
z->iz exchanges the two cycle signs.  Its fixed spin structures are

  (+,+), the trivial odd spin with one holomorphic zero mode;
  (-,-), the nontrivial even spin with no holomorphic zero mode.

The earlier exact divisor calculation identifies the second class as

  alpha=O(T-O),       O=(0,i), T=(0,-i).

It is also kappa-real because kappa exchanges O and T and alpha has order
two.  Thus q, kappa and the demand of a nontrivial fixed spin class do select
one bare spin line.  No additional theta characteristic is chosen here.

For this nonsingular characteristic the normalized genus-one Szego kernel is

  S_alpha(z,w)
    = theta_3(z-w|i)/theta_3(0|i)
      * theta_1'(0|i)/theta_1(z-w|i).                  (1)

It is the unique meromorphic section with the alpha multipliers, unit residue
on the diagonal and no spin zero-mode ambiguity.

This is a complex geometric Cauchy kernel.  Its existence does not by itself
declare H1(E) to be the original one-particle Hilbert space or choose a CAR
measure.

2. First decisive test: monodromy and boundary spectrum
========================================================

From the theta transformation laws, (1) obeys

  S_alpha(z+1,w)=-S_alpha(z,w),
  S_alpha(z+i,w)=-S_alpha(z,w).                        (2)

Consequently a circle cut along either primitive cycle has

  spec B_alpha={n+1/2:n in Z},
  ker B_alpha=0.                                       (3)

This agrees exactly with the LOCAL A4-selected bounding NS spectrum.  It
does not agree with either original quarter multiplier:

  K_1/4(t+1)= i K_1/4(t),
  K_3/4(t+1)=-i K_3/4(t).                              (4)

The finite automorphism q cannot be substituted for translation around the
cut.  It rotates the a cycle into the b cycle.  The later full-group census
does find existing bordered channels: hQkappa and hQ^3kappa define two
annuli, and q exchanges them.  This supersedes the prior absence statement,
but it does not turn q into translation around either boundary circle.  An
actual field transport from the original seam into the annulus pair remains
absent.

Therefore the elliptic spin fixes NS, while the quarter phase remains a
separate lift/marking action.  This is the same operator distinction already
visible in the original A3/A4 audit.

3. Second decisive test: the theta correction is nonzero at tau=i
=================================================================

The local diagonal behavior of (1) is universal:

  S_alpha(z,w)=1/(z-w)+regular terms.

It therefore has the same short-distance singularity as a flat Hardy
kernel.  That local fact does not identify the global kernels.  At finite
tau=i,

  S_alpha(t,0)
    = pi/sin(pi t)
      * [1+2 exp(-pi)(cos(2pi t)-1)+O(exp(-2pi))].      (5)

An exact value at t=1/2 makes the mismatch normalization independent:

  S_alpha(1/2,0)=pi theta_4(0|i)^2,                    (6)
  pi csc(pi/2)=pi.

The Jacobi series gives

  theta_4(0|i)=1+2 sum_(n>=1)(-1)^n exp(-pi n^2)
               =0.9135791381561168...,

so

  S_alpha(1/2,0)=2.6220575542921198... != pi.          (7)

The correction is not a fitted parameter.  It is forced by the fixed square
modulus.  The trigonometric NS kernel appears only in a long-cylinder/nodal
degeneration Im(tau)->infinity.  The original curve has tau=i, and no source
operation sends it to that degeneration.  Taking the limit would discard
the fixed j=1728 geometry rather than derive the original kernel from it.

4. Reflection and the absence of a canonical positive half
============================================================

The real structure kappa preserves alpha and gives the usual reality law for
(1).  This is not yet Osterwalder-Schrader positivity on the original seam.

The fixed locus of kappa on y^2=x^4-1 has x,y real and x^4>=1.  Since the
quartic has only the two real branch points plus or minus 1, this real locus
is one oval.  For a real elliptic curve one oval is the nondividing case:
E minus Fix(kappa) is connected.  Thus kappa does not split E into a positive
half and its reflected copy whose boundary Szego projector could be read as
the original Hardy space.

For kappa itself there is no simultaneous q-invariant cut:

  kappa q kappa=q^{-1},

and q sends a point with real x to one with imaginary x.  It moves the
kappa-fixed oval to the fixed oval of a conjugate reflection.

The stronger old conclusion is superseded.  The conjugate real structures
hQkappa and hQ^3kappa are already words in the given group, not new fitted
data, and each is dividing.  Their positive halves are annuli exchanged by
q.  What the group still does not supply is one q-invariant half or the
physical P1 field/state map into this paired bordered channel.

5. The actual normal twist has zero modes
===========================================

The earlier exact normal pullback did not use alpha alone.  It used

  alpha tensor L,        L=p*O(1),
  H0(alpha tensor L)=span(1,u),
  u=(y+i)/x^2.                                           (8)

Hence the corresponding dbar/Dirac operator has a two-dimensional
holomorphic kernel.  A Green or Szego inverse requires a zero-mode
subtraction or a complementary projector.  Such a projector is not selected
by the curve alone.

Moreover the genuine deck and quarter lifts on (8) satisfy

  D=[[0,-1],[1,0]],       D^2=-I,
  Q=a diag(1,-1),
  QD=-DQ.                                                (9)

There is no common invariant one-dimensional kernel line.  Selecting one
zero mode would break either the deck or quarter action.  This excludes the
normally twisted two-mode kernel as a canonical source of the rank-one
quarter Hardy polarization.

6. What survives positively
============================

The elliptic geometry supplies three useful and exact facts:

  * a unique q-fixed nontrivial even spin class alpha;
  * its nullmode-free global elliptic Szego kernel (1);
  * the local A4 NS spectrum (3), with no zero mode.

It therefore gives a canonical finite-modulus NS comparison kernel.  It does
not supply the original quarter channel, a one-sided Hardy projector, the
original seam time, or the charged Gaussian measure.  The existing full NS
cover Green construction remains a valid positive free realization under
its stated field premise, but it cannot be replaced by (1) without an actual
field, support, time and cut map.

The smallest remaining positive test is now precise: an original source map
would have to send the original seam circle into a specified bordered
channel of E, transport its CAR field and reflection, and explain why either
the finite theta correction in (5) is physically retained or why a
source-derived degeneration removes it.  It must also transport q as the
quarter marking without turning q into circle translation by declaration.
