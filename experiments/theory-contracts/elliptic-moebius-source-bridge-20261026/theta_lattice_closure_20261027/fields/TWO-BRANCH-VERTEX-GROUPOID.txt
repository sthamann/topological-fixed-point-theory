1. FIXED STANDARD COCYCLE
=========================

Let B be the integral basis produced by check_theta_lattice.py and let G be its
even unimodular Gram matrix.  The checker fixes the standard bimultiplicative
cocycle

  epsilon(m,n)=(-1)^(m^T F n),                                (6)

where

  F_ii=G_ii/2 mod 2,
  F_ij=G_ij mod 2 for i>j,
  F_ij=0 for i<j.

Thus

  F+F^T=G mod 2,
  epsilon(m,m)=(-1)^(<m,m>/2).                                (7)

No cocycle is fitted to the desired group relations.  The same rule is used
on both branch coordinate frames.

The mod-two calculation is a complete all-charge calculation, not a finite
shell test.  Both epsilon(m,n) and every sign carry eta_A(m) depend only on the
classes of m,n in Lambda/2Lambda.  Hence the 256 charge classes and the 65536
ordered charge pairs exhaust the cocycle identities for every m,n in Lambda.
The exact integral matrix identities A^T G A=G and the geometric relations of
J,V,sigma are checked separately over Z by check_theta_lattice.py before this
parity reduction is used.

For a lattice isometry A define

  D_A=A^T F A+F mod 2,
  q_A^0(m)=sum_(i<j) (D_A)_ij m_i m_j mod 2.                  (8)

The checker proves exhaustively on Lambda/2Lambda that

  q_A^0(m+n)+q_A^0(m)+q_A^0(n)=m^T D_A n.                    (9)

Consequently

  Ahat(e^m)=(-1)^(q_A^0(m)) e^(A m)                          (10)

is an automorphism of the twisted lattice group algebra.  On the full lattice
VOA it acts by

  Ahat[h_1(-n_1)...h_r(-n_r)e^m]
    =(Ah_1)(-n_1)...(Ah_r)(-n_r)(-1)^(q_A^0(m))e^(Am).        (11)

Because A is orthogonal and the phase has modulus one, (11) is unitary.  It
fixes the vacuum, preserves the conformal vector and intertwines every vertex
operator.  The calculation on the group algebra together with the ordinary
isometry relations on the Heisenberg generators therefore proves the
relations on all vertex fields, not only on the root shell.

Multiplication by a sign character

  (-1)^(t^T m),              t in (F_2)^8                     (12)

changes the lift section but not epsilon, A, the lattice, or the vertex
algebra.  The optional strict census enumerates exactly these 256 choices per
marked generator.


2. WHY THE TWO OBJECT CONSTRUCTION NEEDS NO SPLITTING
=====================================================

The identities in (3)-(5) are formal consequences of one existing C12 lift
and one invertible VOA isomorphism T.

First,

  alpha_10 alpha_01
    =beta_0 T^(-1)T
    =beta_0,

  alpha_01 alpha_10
    =T beta_0 T^(-1)
    =beta_1.                                                   (13)

Since beta_0 and beta_1 have order four, alpha has exact order eight.

For the cubic mark, the first arrow obeys

  T sigma_0
    =sigma_1^(-1) T,                                         (14)

because sigma_1^(-1)=T sigma_0 T^(-1).  On the return arrow,

  beta_0 T^(-1) sigma_1
    =beta_0 sigma_0^(-1) T^(-1)
    =sigma_0^(-1) beta_0 T^(-1),                             (15)

using only [beta_0,sigma_0]=1.  Equations (14),(15) are precisely the two
components of alpha sigma alpha^(-1)=sigma^(-1).

Conjugation transports (1) to beta_1.  The inverse in the definition of
sigma_1 does not change its order, and beta_1 still commutes with sigma_1.
Equation (5) follows.

The actual geometry also matches.  In branch coordinates T has matrix V.
The return arrow has matrix

  J V^(-1)=J V=iV,                                           (16)

because the geometric matrix V is an involution.  Likewise

  V sigma^(-1) V^(-1)=sigma,

so the transported branch-one cubic has the same geometric matrix sigma.
Only its cocycle section has been transported, as it must be.

The exact checker deliberately uses a valid standard-cocycle lift T for which

  T^2 != 1,                 [T,beta_0] != 1.                  (17)

Nevertheless every identity (13)-(15) passes on all 256 residue fields.
Thus no hidden same-frame split is being used.

The choice of T is unique only up to character gauge.  If T'=chi T, transport
by chi conjugates all branch-one data beta_1,sigma_1,c_1 and the return arrow.
It produces an isomorphic two-object vertex groupoid.  Thus the field family
is canonical only up to this ordinary groupoid equivalence; the proof does not
promote one character section to a physical observable.


3. OPTIONAL STRICT ORDER 24 SECTION
===================================

For completeness, the checker also asks the stronger question posed in the
task.  For every A in {J,V,sigma} it enumerates

  eta_A^t(m)=(-1)^(q_A^0(m)+t^T m),       t in (F_2)^8,       (18)

and tests the complete relations on all m mod 2.

Power-normalized candidate counts are

  J^4=1:       256,
  V^2=1:        16,
  sigma^3=1:    16.                                          (19)

Exactly 256 triples additionally satisfy

  [V,J]=1,
  [sigma,J]=1,
  V sigma V^(-1)=sigma^(-1).                                 (20)

The lexicographically first witness has character masks

  t_J     =0x03,       support {0,1},
  t_V     =0x35,       support {0,2,4,5},
  t_sigma =0x2a,       support {1,3,5}.                       (21)

The generated group of vertex automorphisms has exactly 24 elements.  For

  c=J sigma

the checker proves

  order(c)=12,             c^4=sigma,          c^9=J.         (22)

No triple in the complete census has t_J=0.  Therefore the zero-on-basis
standard J lift does not extend to this strict same-frame C4 x S3 section.
This is not an obstruction to the groupoid construction in section 2.  It is
the exact reason the transported two-object construction is the safer result
when an earlier J or C12 lift must remain fixed.

The number 256 counts raw sign-character sections of the fixed standard
cocycle.  It is not a count of physical choices or inequivalent theories.
Character gauge and further marked centralizers can identify sections.  No
quotient census is needed for the existence theorem proved here.


4. SCOPE
========

PROVED:

  * the actual standard cocycle on the theta E8 basis admits a unitary lift of
    the lattice isomorphism Lambda -> zeta_8 Lambda;

  * given the existing faithful branch-zero C12 lift, transport along that one
    arrow produces coherent beta, sigma and C12 actions on branch one;

  * alpha has exact order eight on the direct sum groupoid representation,
    squares to the branchwise beta and reverses sigma on all vertex fields;

  * the optional same-frame subgroup C4 x S3 also splits after a compatible
    sign-character choice and has exact order 24.

NOT CLAIMED:

  * no splitting of the full G31 lattice extension;

  * no alteration of the established nonsplitting result for that larger
    extension;

  * no single alpha-invariant lattice or single local VOA containing both
    branches;

  * no physical P1 sewing, state or field-measure identification;

  * no claim that the optional masks (21) equal a previously frozen source
    linearization.  They are a concrete compatible section of the fixed
    cocycle extension.

The groupoid result needs none of the optional masks.  It preserves any given
faithful C12 lift on branch zero and defines every branch-one mark by transport.
The executable certificate instantiates beta_0,sigma_0 with the displayed
strict witness so that every identity can be replayed entrywise.  The theorem
it certifies is stronger in the relevant direction: for any already fixed
faithful c_0, set beta_0=c_0^9 and sigma_0=c_0^4, and equations (2),(3) prove
the same groupoid identities for every cocycle lift T.  The instantiation is
not used to replace the original c_0 or J linearization.

Individual lattice-isometry lifts exist by (8)-(11).  Nothing here constructs
or assumes a simultaneous lift of every element of G31.
