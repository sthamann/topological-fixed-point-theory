1. THE GROUPOID ALGEBRA IS TYPE CORRECT

The arrows have the exact source and target types

  alpha_01=T:V_0 -> V_1,
  alpha_10=beta_0 T^(-1):V_1 -> V_0.

Therefore

  alpha_10 alpha_01=beta_0,
  alpha_01 alpha_10=T beta_0 T^(-1)=beta_1.

Since beta_0 has exact order four, so does beta_1, and alpha has exact order
eight on V_0 direct_sum V_1.

The cubic relation is equally formal.  From

  sigma_1=T sigma_0^(-1) T^(-1)

one gets

  T sigma_0=sigma_1^(-1)T.

For the return arrow, commutation of beta_0 with sigma_0 gives

  beta_0 T^(-1) sigma_1
    =sigma_0^(-1) beta_0 T^(-1).

These are precisely the two components of

  alpha sigma alpha^(-1)=sigma^(-1).

Transport preserves the order of beta and sigma and their commutation.  Thus

  c_j=beta_j sigma_j

has exact order twelve on both branches, with c_j^4=sigma_j and
c_j^9=beta_j.

The proof begins with the existing branch-zero lift

  beta_0=c_0^9,    sigma_0=c_0^4.

It does not derive or replace that source marking.  This is the right logical
direction for preserving the earlier nonsplitting and clock results.


2. THE MOD TWO CHECK IS AN ALL CHARGE PROOF

For the integral theta-E8 basis, the fixed standard cocycle is

  epsilon(m,n)=(-1)^(m^T F n),

where F+F^T equals the Gram matrix modulo two and the diagonal gives
epsilon(m,m)=(-1)^(<m,m>/2).

For an exact lattice isometry A, the cocycle quotient is controlled by

  D_A=A^T F A+F mod 2.

It is symmetric with zero diagonal.  The checker constructs q_A with

  q_A(m+n)+q_A(m)+q_A(n)=m^T D_A n mod 2.

Hence

  e^m -> (-1)^(q_A(m)) e^(Am)

is a lift of A on the twisted lattice group algebra.  Both epsilon and the
sign correction depend only on m,n modulo 2.  Exhausting the 256 residue
classes and all 65536 ordered pairs therefore proves the cocycle identity for
all lattice charges.  It is not merely a finite root probe.

The exact integer matrix isometries and their geometric relations are checked
separately in the imported theta-lattice certificate.  Oscillator modes are
transported by the same orthogonal A, so the group-algebra calculation extends
to every finite-energy lattice-VOA field and its adjoint.
