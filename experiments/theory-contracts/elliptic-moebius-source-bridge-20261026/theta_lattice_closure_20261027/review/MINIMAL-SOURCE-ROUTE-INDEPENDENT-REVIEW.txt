INDEPENDENT REVIEW: THE FULL P1 TO C3 BULK MAP IS SUFFICIENT, NOT NECESSARY
27 October 2026

VERDICT

AKTUELLER-STAND-DE.md overstates the next necessary source step in two places.
The requested map from the original inner Dirac problem and all Cauchy data to
the genus two three-sheet spin source is one sufficient realization.  It is
needed if one wants to identify the complete original Calderon space with the
C3 Hardy space.  It is not necessary for either

  1. the already constructed finite native module, or
  2. the conditional local E8 reconstruction from the marked C4 generator.

Likewise, the dual determinant line does not need a new physical amplitude
choice in order to exist.  Once the Hardy shift M_z and its Hilbert adjoint are
given, its Fredholm line

  delta(M_z)=det ker(M_z) tensor det coker(M_z)^(-1)

and the duality det(V)^(-1)=det(V)^* are canonical.  What remains physical is
whether the original source functional actually evaluates this contravariant
line.  That is a membership and readout question, not another choice required
to construct the mathematical module.

The weakest useful next source theorem is therefore local.  It must place the
already existing marked CAR and simple-current sector inside the primitive
source as a state and adjoint preserving local sector.  A global equality of
the primitive bulk Dirac operator with the genus two operator is stronger than
this and should not be presented as mandatory.


1. WHAT IS ALREADY CONSTRUCTED

The finite geometric theorem constructs

  W_q=H^0(Jac(C),O(2 Theta)) tensor N_q(S_0)^(-1)

with its Heisenberg group N, cubic gamma and quarter lift alpha.  It proves an
intertwiner with the native four-dimensional N64 module, unique up to the
listed Heisenberg frame, alpha sign and native centralizer orbit.  See
SPIN-RIGIDIFIED-THETA-MODULE.txt, lines 7 to 58 and 216 to 240.

The later Hardy theorem derives the inverse point contribution from the same
original seam multiplier z.  On the selected spin Hardy space M_z is injective
with three-dimensional cokernel V, hence delta(M_z)=det(V)^(-1).  No state or
bulk Dirac identification is used to define this line.

There is one exact typing qualification.  On a single ordered spin cable,

  delta(M_z)=N_q(S_0)^(-1) tensor B_O^(-1).

The ordering line changes alpha to -alpha and carries odd Fredholm parity.
After forgetting the super grading this is the same classified native complex
module.  The literal permutation-free representative N_q^(-1), or an even
graded relative sewing line, still requires the established norm
normalization or the two-cable ratio.  Therefore the sentence

  W_q=H^0(2 Theta) tensor delta(M_z)

is correct for the ungraded native module class, but is not a literal equality
of the fixed signed and graded sewing representatives without this
qualification.


2. CAR ADJUNCTION DOES NOT ADD A SECOND FREE CHOICE

For the inherited negative sea, the original positive winding fills V.  The
charged vacuum ket is

  Xi=a^*(v_1)a^*(v_2)a^*(v_3) Omega in det(V)

and beta acts on it by -i.  Hilbert adjunction sends this line canonically to
its continuous dual det(V)^*, on which beta acts by +i.  The Hardy Fredholm
line is exactly this dual.  This is the content of
PROJECTOR-CAR-DEFECT-LINE-REVIEW.txt, lines 70 to 128.

Thus there is no extra choice between two unrelated mathematical objects.
Ket and dual are canonically paired once the Hilbert CAR representation and
its adjoint are fixed.  Projector positivity fixes the CAR state and all its
word expectations, but those expectations are phase blind.  They do not by
themselves say that a separate physical boundary response is the bra rather
than the ket.

The corrected physical question is therefore:

  Does the already defined original sewing or response functional restrict to
  the canonical adjoint pairing with Xi, equivalently to delta(M_z)?

This is a concrete equality of functionals.  It must not be reformulated as a
new freely chosen amplitude orientation.  If the original functional is only
the vector state <Xi,A Xi>, no central line character is observable there.  If
it is a charged boundary covector, the inverse line follows by adjunction.


3. THE LOCAL E8 THEOREM NEEDS LESS THAN A BULK IDENTIFICATION

The same-conformal reconstruction theorem has a smaller first premise.  It
requires a connected local unitary realization of

  U=V_D5 tensor V_A3

with the same conformal vector and one occurrence of the marked charged module
M_h.  That occurrence forces support H=<h>, the unique E8 extension, its
vacuum, local products, vacuum correlations and conformal dynamics.  See
SIMPLIFICATION.txt, lines 100 to 111 and 162 to 168, and THEOREMS.txt, lines
19 to 47.

No genus two bulk Dirac operator appears in this implication.  The local
realization and the marked generator are the hypotheses.  Consequently, a
direct primitive-source realization of U and M_h closes the chiral E8 step
even if the complete primitive Cauchy space is not isomorphic to the C3 Hardy
space.

Conversely, an abstract four-dimensional theta-module isomorphism alone does
not establish this local VOA premise.  It supplies neither the local fields
nor the common conformal vector.  The correction therefore removes an
unnecessary global hurdle without weakening the actual local requirement.


4. THE OLD AFFINE CAR DEFECT ALREADY SUPPLIES THE ALGEBRAIC OPERATOR

SAME-SOURCE-AFFINE-DEFECT.txt proves that the old ordered-CAR W1 extends to a
unitary finite-energy operator W_ext on all four D8 modules.  It has the exact
Klein phases, sector exchange, Ward identities and E8 twisted-module action.
This result uses the actual fold frame and no global odd-field map.  See lines
9 to 27.

Its boundary is equally explicit.  W_ext is a global defect or representation
intertwiner, not yet a point-local vertex insertion.  Formal Li transport does
not prove strong smeared fields on a common local core.  See lines 310 to 333.

This pins down the smallest missing operator statement.  The primitive source
need not recreate W_ext from a new genus two bulk.  It must realize the already
fixed same-source CAR/AIA sector locally and show that the marked field and its
adjoint act on the physical cyclic source space with the inherited stress
tensor and support.


5. WEAKEST SUFFICIENT ORIGINAL ROUTE

Let A_mark(I) be the local algebra generated by the already fixed CAR currents,
the relevant D5 x A3 subtheory and one marked charged field in M_h on an
interval I.  A sufficient source theorem is an injective local star morphism

  iota_I:A_mark(I) -> A_P1(I_source)

with compatible maps as I varies, such that on the common finite-energy core:

  a. iota preserves products, adjoints and the cyclic positive source state;
  b. iota identifies the common conformal vector and positive-energy grading;
  c. iota carries the old winding W1 to the actual seam operation and retains
     its charge, Klein and quarter marks;
  d. the image contains one nonzero local vector in the marked module M_h;
  e. if the inverse norm is used as a physical response, the original sewing
     functional restricted to this sector is the canonical adjoint Fredholm
     pairing for M_z.

The extension generated by this image must be connected, local and unitary;
these are the exact categorical hypotheses of the reconstruction theorem.
Under that stated hypothesis, conditions a to d are enough for the local CAR
and same-conformal E8 route.
Condition e is needed only for the claimed determinant-line readout.  Full
transport of the inner Dirac operator, all Cauchy data, all P1 fields and the
entire genus two Hardy space is not required by these conclusions.

If the physical claim instead identifies the complete original Calderon
polarization with the C3 Hardy polarization, then the stronger bulk and
closed-domain map in AKTUELLER-STAND-DE.md is indeed necessary for that
stronger claim.  It should be labelled as that realization route, rather than
as the unique next source theorem.


6. ACTIONABLE NEXT TEST

The next original-source check should target one local charged field, not the
whole bulk geometry:

  1. Take the already fixed finite-energy CAR/AIA core and its W_ext.
  2. Locate in the primitive boundary source the candidate local operator for
     the weight-one (16,4) generator of M_h.
  3. Verify on one proper interval that the operator and its adjoint preserve
     the source domain, have the required CAR/cocycle products, share the
     source stress tensor, and transform under the original winding exactly as
     W_ext prescribes.
  4. Evaluate the original seam functional on the corresponding three-mode
     transition once.  If it is the adjoint matrix element, the dual line
     delta(M_z) follows canonically.  If it is a state expectation, retain its
     phase blindness and do not infer the determinant character.

Success at steps 2 and 3 triggers the marked same-conformal uniqueness theorem
and reconstructs the local E8 extension.  Step 4 types the physical readout.
Only failure of this direct local route makes the stronger P1 to C3 bulk map
the next justified construction.


CORRECTION TO AKTUELLER-STAND-DE.md

Lines 25 to 27 should be read as one sufficient route.  The phrase that the
next proof must connect the original inner Dirac problem to the three-sheet
spin source should be weakened to:

  The next source proof must realize the already constructed marked local
  CAR/simple-current sector in the primitive source.  A bulk Cauchy-data map to
  the C3 Hardy source is one sufficient realization.  A local state and
  adjoint preserving embedding with the same conformal vector and the marked
  generator also suffices for the E8 reconstruction.

replaced by:

  The dual Fredholm line is canonically constructed from M_z and CAR
  adjunction.  A physical determinant response requires proof that the
  original sewing functional evaluates this canonical dual pairing.  This is
  separate from the existence of W_q and from the CAR state itself.
