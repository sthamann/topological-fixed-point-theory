VERDICT

ACCEPTED in the stated mathematical scope.

The checker proves an actual integral reconstruction, not a dimension or
character coincidence.  Starting from the q norm completed theta module, a
marked level two Schrodinger frame, its affine stabilizer supports and the
declared primitive Gaussian normalization, the support weighted vectors
produce 240 norm two vectors.  Their integer span has an even integral
unimodular Gram matrix of rank eight.  The explicit orthogonal coordinate map
then sends the entire generated shell bijectively to the standard 112 plus 128
E8 shell.

The conclusion is precisely an E8 lattice after choosing the theta frame and a
unit of the q norm line, and an E8 isometry or complex homothety class after
forgetting those choices.  The result does not establish a unique embedded E8
lattice among all lattices compatible with some Heisenberg action, nor does it
fix a physical metric scale from P1.

The actual source spin lift alpha is correctly typed as a transition between
two lattice copies.  It is not an automorphism of the constructed single
lattice.  The old C12 operator c=i sigma remains an honest automorphism of
each copy.


1. ROOT CONSTRUCTION AND E8 IDENTIFICATION

The relevant source module is

  W_q=H_theta tensor N_q^(-1).

The bare theta space H_theta is beta neutral.  The inverse q norm line supplies
beta=+i, so the source relation alpha^2=i belongs to W_q.  A unit nu of that
one-dimensional norm line is part of the displayed Schrodinger coordinates.

For support dimension d=0,1,2 the checker then uses the Gaussian weights

  pi^(2-d),    pi=1+i,

and the common physical factor 1/sqrt(2).  A support contains 2^d entries.
Its squared norm is therefore

  (1/2) |pi|^(4-2d) 2^d=2.

The allowed central units absorb every Gaussian associate.  After requiring
the numerator to be a primitive element of Z[i], the three squared norms
force pi^2, pi and 1 up to those units.  Equal norm, support size and the
central i alone would still allow arbitrary continuous phases on the rays.
Thus the primitive Gaussian integral recipe is genuine additional arithmetic
input.  Z[i] is the smallest integer coefficient ring containing the marked
mu4, but the construction does not derive the uniqueness of every integral
form on the underlying complex module.  It does not import E8 coordinates.

The exact support counts are

  d=0: 16 vectors,
  d=1: 96 vectors,
  d=2: 128 vectors.

All 240 vectors have physical norm two.  Hermite normal form gives an
eight-dimensional basis B with |det B|=16 in unscaled Gaussian coordinates.
Thus B/sqrt(2) has covolume one.  Its Gram matrix

  G=B^T B/2

is integral, even and has determinant one.

The shell comparison is decisive.  On every Gaussian coordinate the real
orthogonal map is, in doubled standard coordinates,

  a+ib -> (a+b,a-b).

After the corresponding factor of two is interpreted, it preserves the
physical norm and maps the generated 240 vectors exactly onto the standard
E8 roots.  Since both lattices are generated by those roots, this also maps
the whole generated lattice to the standard E8 lattice.  No classification
argument or hidden E8 basis is needed for this last step.


2. CLIFFORD COVARIANCE AND ITS LIMIT

The checker verifies integral Gram isometries for

  X1,X2,Z1,Z2,S1,S2,CNOT,
  zeta8^(-1)H1 and zeta8^(-1)H2.

These generators prove covariance under the compensated Clifford frame
changes used in the report.  The compensation is legitimate for a projective
theta coordinate change because two odd changes differ on composition only
by the central unit -i.

This does not turn an actual fixed operator into a frame gauge.  The source
operator

  alpha=zeta8 V,    V^2=I,

has alpha^2=iI.  Removing zeta8 would change its square to I.  The report
therefore correctly refuses that rephasing for the physical alpha.

The construction is canonical only relative to the marked theta structure,
Schrodinger polarization, central Gaussian unit, primitive Z[i]
normalization, root norm convention and a chosen unit nu of N_q^(-1).
Changing nu rotates or rescales the embedded lattice.  Without the theta frame
and norm-line unit, only the abstract E8 isometry class and the complex
homothety class descend.  The finite irreducible module alone fixes an
invariant Hermitian form only up to positive scale; the normalization to root
norm two fixes that remaining homothety after the stated choices.


3. TWO LATTICES, NOT ONE

The checked operators satisfy

  sigma Lambda=Lambda,
  i Lambda=Lambda,
  alpha Lambda=zeta8 Lambda,
  alpha(zeta8 x)=iVx in Lambda.

Thus alpha exchanges the two objects and alpha squared is multiplication by i
on either one.  This is exactly the claimed spin square-root action on W_q.  It
must not be reassigned to the beta-neutral bare theta space.

The obstruction to merging the branches is stronger than failure of closure
of their union.  In physical coordinates

  Lambda subset 2^(-1/2) Q(i)^4.

If a nonzero vector lay in both Lambda and zeta8 Lambda, some nonzero Gaussian
coordinate would imply zeta8 belongs to Q(i), which is false.  Hence

  Lambda intersect zeta8 Lambda={0}.

Their additive span has Z rank sixteen in an eight-dimensional real vector
space and is not discrete.  No common discrete overlattice in this fixed
embedding can contain both full-rank lattices.  This proves that neither the
union nor its generated group is a lattice.

This obstruction remains scoped.  It does not prove that no differently
embedded E8 lattice can be invariant under a conjugated or otherwise changed
order-eight operator.  The checker itself correctly reports only failure for
the constructed lattice.


4. CLOCK AND FIELD SCOPE

The retained cubic sigma and central quarter J=iI preserve both branches.
Their product

  c=i sigma

has exact order twelve and satisfies c^4=sigma and c^9=iI.  The old C12 clock
therefore acts inside either E8 lattice.  Alpha is an additional spin lift
above J and reverses sigma; it is not the old C12 compiler.

An orthogonal lattice isometry Lambda -> zeta8 Lambda admits a lattice VOA
isomorphism.  The revised report correctly states the remaining qualification:
the lift through the lattice central extension is unique only up to character
gauge, and the finite checker neither chooses that cocycle lift nor identifies
it with an already marked source field operator.  It therefore proves no
single alpha invariant VOA, no two-branch local extension and no primitive P1
field realization.

The alternative same-spin Fredholm representative contains the additional
factor B_O^(-1).  It changes the retained sign of alpha and keeps the odd
Fredholm parity.  Forgetting that parity yields the same ungraded lattice
comparison; a graded physical sewing statement may not discard it.


5. COVER MINIMALITY

For the actual fixed matrix image F, membership in the two Clifford cosets
defines the character

  chi:F -> Z/2.

Its kernel preserves each branch and alpha has chi(alpha)=1.  The associated
index-two cover is therefore sufficient and minimal for this fixed two-object
system.  This is a statement about the displayed representation and its two
branches.  It is not a universal minimality theorem over all possible lattice
embeddings or physical source constructions.

FINAL ASSESSMENT

The new result closes the abstract integral step that was previously absent:
the marked theta stabilizer data reconstruct an E8 lattice similarity class,
and the native C12 clock acts integrally on it.  The spin square root alpha
does not preserve that embedded lattice and must be represented as a
transition between Lambda and zeta8 Lambda.  The construction does not by
itself select a physical P1 metric, a unique embedded lattice, a cocycle lift
of alpha on fields or a local two-branch source theory.
