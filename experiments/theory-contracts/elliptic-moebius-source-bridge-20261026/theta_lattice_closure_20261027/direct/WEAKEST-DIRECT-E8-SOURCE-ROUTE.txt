VERDICT
=======

The physical identification of the primitive P1 Cauchy problem with the
three sheeted genus two Dirac problem is not needed for either of the two
mathematical conclusions that are presently used:

  1. the reconstruction of the finite native N64, cubic and quarter marked
     complex module;

  2. the reconstruction of the canonical chiral E8 lattice field and its
     vacuum inside the already stated local marked extension class.

The two conclusions have different sources and should not be forced through
one physical C3 operator map.

The genus two curve is sufficient as an auxiliary algebraic representation
space.  The original circle supplies the oriented winding one multiplier.
The already selected P2 cover pulls this multiplier to a forced three sheet
cable.  Its same spin Fredholm defect, followed by the theta functor, gives
the exact finite native marked module.  This does not say that the primitive
P1 bulk Dirac operator is the Dirac operator of the curve.

The full chiral field follows by a separate and stronger theorem.  Put

  U=V_D5 tensor V_A3,       h=(s,f) in C4 x C4.

If the primitive source realizes U inside any connected local unitary VOA
extension W with the same conformal vector, and the marked charged module
M_h occurs in W, then W is forced to be the unique marked V_E8 extension.
Support, multiplicities, holomorphicity, vacuum, local products, conformal
Hamiltonian and vacuum correlation functions are consequences.  A physical
C3 bulk realization is absent from the hypotheses.

There is one remaining distinction.  The canonical VOA vacuum is thereby
fixed as a mathematical chiral state.  The claim that the raw RP P1 seam
state is this vacuum remains the open parent SEAM.EQUIV.01.  A physical C3
Dirac map would be one possible sufficient route to that parent statement,
but it is not a logically necessary premise of the field reconstruction.


1. THREE TARGETS THAT MUST NOT BE IDENTIFIED
============================================

The source question separates into three arrows.

  A. Finite marked module

       original winding mark plus P2 cover
         -> (N64, C4, sigma, alpha) on a four dimensional complex space.

  B. Chiral local field and mathematical vacuum

       fixed U plus a local occurrence of M_h
         -> V_E8, its vacuum, vertex products, L0 and conformal net.

  C. Physical source identification

       raw RP P1 seam state
         -> the vacuum representation of that V_E8 net.

Arrow A is now reconstructed by the auxiliary curve.  Arrow B is the marked
generator theorem.  Arrow C is the unconditional SEAM.EQUIV.01 claim and is
still open.  Requiring a physical P1 to C3 bulk Dirac intertwiner before
using A or B made a sufficient construction look like a necessary source
axiom.

3. WHY THIS DOES NOT NEED A PHYSICAL C3 DIRAC MAP
=================================================

The only analytic information used in (2.12) is the original circle Hardy
orientation and its multiplier z.  The cable is a functorial algebraic base
change along the already marked P2 map.  The genus two curve serves the same
role as a splitting field in algebra: it exposes the finite action without
claiming that physical propagation occurs on that field.

This distinction is decisive because the two possible claims have different
domains.

  Auxiliary claim:
    the determinant of the pulled back multiplier realizes the finite marked
    representation.

  Physical claim:
    the primitive P1 bulk harmonic Cauchy data are unitarily identical to the
    spin Dirac Cauchy data on the three sheeted curve.

The first claim follows from (2.2) through (2.12).  The second needs the
missing bulk operator and domain intertwiner.  It is stronger and is not
used in the proof of the first.

There is also no hidden choice of three copies.  The degree of pi is three,
c permutes the three fibers transitively, and the same original M_z acts on
all of them.  Nor is there a new physical polarization.  Treating the
holomorphic pullback as an auxiliary determinant functor fixes the complex
Hardy space algebraically.  Claiming it is the physical Calderon range would
be the additional assertion that remains unproved.

5. HOW THE FINITE MARKS ACT ON THE FULL FIELD
==============================================

The finite module is not being used here to choose the E8 lattice.  The

narrower: it proves that the auxiliary geometric marks belong to the same
finite native N64, sigma and quarter class.

recovers the abstract E8 lattice class.  Theta frame changes preserve its
complex homothety class.  The actual source alpha lies in zeta8 G31 and
exchanges Lambda with zeta8 Lambda, so it does not select one alpha invariant
embedded lattice.  This strengthens the auxiliary branch without changing

On the field side an epsilon preserving lattice lift has the actual action

  ghat[h_1(-n_1)...h_r(-n_r)e^lambda]
    =(gh_1)(-n_1)...(gh_r)(-n_r)
      eta_g(lambda)e^(g lambda).                            (5.1)

this is a lattice VOA automorphism.  Therefore

  ghat Y(v,x) ghat^(-1)=Y(ghat v,x),
  ghat 1=1,
  ghat omega=omega,
  [ghat,L_n]=0.                                            (5.2)

Thus the lattice form, vertex products, locality, vacuum and conformal
grading are preserved by the actual native lifts.  The finite intertwiner in

It is not used to pull an integral lattice or a field algebra back to the

This avoids the earlier circular move.  The E8 lattice and fields come from
