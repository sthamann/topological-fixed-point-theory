VERDICT
=======

There are two different questions, and the old wording made the second one a
condition for the first.

1. Abstract module construction.  Once the original negative-sea convention

     C=I-P_H,                 U C U^*=C+Pi_V,

   has produced the three-dimensional charged defect V, the two relative
   vacuum lines

     K=det(V),                K^*=det(V)^(-1)                 (1)

   and their perfect CAR pairing are functorial.  No eta term and no separately
   selected physical state are needed to construct K^*.  For the existing
   marks,

     beta|K=-i,               beta|K^*=+i,
     c|K=c|K^*=1.                                      (2)

   The Q character includes the already proved q_1,q_2 transposition, or,
   equivalently, the B_O ordering factor in the family formula.  Thus the
   dual line required by the native complex module already exists as a
   consequence of the CAR defect and adjunction.  Asking the P1 eta term to
   choose it is an unnecessary extra condition for the abstract module
   reconstruction.

2. Physical source readout.  In the strongest original formulas checked here,
   the P1 eta and sewing data are scalars, phases, positivity forms, or index
   data.  None is written as a complex-linear outgoing boundary covector with
   values in K^*.  Therefore those formulas do not yet prove that the physical
   P1 response evaluates the charged ket through the dual line.  This is a
   typing gap for the physical amplitude, not a gap in the construction of the
   dual representation.

The exact positive conditional statement is small.  If the original outgoing
seam amplitude is a covector on the shifted negative-sea sector, then it lies
in K^*, pairs with the charged ket in K to a scalar, and has beta character +i.
No reversal of the occupied projection is needed.  The sources read in this
audit do not state that outgoing-covector typing.


1. THE CAR ADJOINT CONVENTION AND THE DUAL LINE
===============================================

Use a complex Hilbert product conjugate-linear in the first variable and
linear in the second.  Creation a^*(v) is linear in v and annihilation a(v) is
conjugate-linear in v.  For an orthonormal frame v_1,v_2,v_3 of V,

  Xi=a^*(v_1)a^*(v_2)a^*(v_3)Omega

spans K=det(V).  The Hilbert adjoint gives the bra

  R(Xi): K -> C,             R(Xi)(xi)=<Xi,xi>.                (3)

For fixed Xi, (3) is complex-linear in xi, so R(Xi) belongs to K^*.  The Riesz
map R:K->K^* is conjugate-linear.  Under a unitary symmetry g,

  g|K=det(g|V),              g|K^*=det(g|V)^(-1).              (4)

This is the precise reason that beta=i I_V gives -i on K and +i on K^*.

The often used annihilation expression

  <Omega,a(w_3)a(w_2)a(w_1)Xi>                               (5)

is conjugate-linear in the vector arguments w_j with the convention above.
It is the Hermitian adjoint form of the pairing.  It should not silently be
called a complex-linear element of det(V)^*.  The fully algebraic
complex-linear statement uses covectors phi_j in V^* and the canonical
contractions

  det(V)^* tensor det(V) -> C,
  (phi_1 wedge phi_2 wedge phi_3, v_1 wedge v_2 wedge v_3)
     |-> det(phi_i(v_j)).                                      (6)

Equations (3) and (6) agree after the anti-linear Riesz identification
V ~= V^*.  They prove the existence and character of the dual line.  They do
not select a preferred nonzero section of that line.  A ray or positive state
also cannot select such a section, because its overall phase is absent from
all state expectations.  This phase fact is irrelevant to the abstract module,
which needs the line and its group action rather than a distinguished vector.

The full family version is the already checked identity

  K=B(E)=N_q(S_0) tensor B_O,
  K^*=B(E)^(-1)=N_q(S_0)^(-1) tensor B_O^(-1).                 (7)

Both are odd superlines.  Dualization reverses the central complex character
but preserves the odd parity.  Dropping B_O is permitted only in the already
scoped ungraded module comparison where its effect is the residual alpha sign;
it is not an equality of graded determinant lines.

2.2 The primitive eta contribution is a scalar action factor
------------------------------------------------------------

The later primitive action is

Its contribution to exp(-S_prim) is therefore

  a=exp(-i pi Delta eta/2).                                    (8)

With the already checked Dai-Freed conventions and vanishing zero modes,

  a^2=tau_rel^(-1),                                            (9)

would additionally need the real structure, gluing orientation and kernel
correction.  The source writes (8) as a complex scalar in the action.  It does
not type it as a section of K or K^*, and it contains none of the beta,c,Q

The scalar is compatible with a hidden evaluation pairing K^* tensor K to C,

Gamma_+,Gamma_- labels in fullv21 are therefore the right candidate
incoming-outgoing orientation, but the source does not extend them to a

3. C3 NEED NOT BE A PHYSICAL BULK SOURCE
========================================

The already proved auxiliary construction uses the existing curve

  C: eta^2=1-w^6,
  z=eta/(1-w^3),              div(z)=D_q-D_p,

and the selected spin family S_0.  On the three unramified discs over |z|<1,
multiplication by z has cokernel

  V=direct_sum_a S_0|q_a

and Fredholm line det(V)^(-1).  This construction is a determinant functor on
the auxiliary marked family.  A determinant functor does not require C to be
literal physical spacetime or a physical bulk.  For the abstract complex
module, the C3 curve is allowed to be the functorial coefficient geometry
which realizes the already fixed line and its c,Q,beta action.

The stronger physical statement needs only a local natural transformation,
not an identification of all P1 and C3 Hilbert spaces:

  actual cyclic P1 source sector
      -> three-sheet S_0 Hardy source sector,

intertwining the actual seam shift with M_z and carrying the resulting
relative determinant functor to K^*.  Equivalently, one can specify directly a
sewing covector

  A_Sigma in Hom(K,C)=K^*                                     (11)

with the established beta,c,Q marks.  Either construction makes the auxiliary
curve a coefficient and determinant-line realization.  Neither requires it to
be a new physical boundary component or bulk field.

What remains absent from the original formulas checked here is exactly this
natural transformation or the typed covector (11).  Requiring a physical C3
bulk source would be stronger than necessary.  Claiming the original P1
amplitude already equals (11) would be stronger than the source.

4. SHARP CONCLUSION
===================

For module reconstruction the requested inverse line is already canonical:

  negative-sea CAR defect V
      -> charged ket line det(V)
      -> CAR dual line det(V)^(-1)
      -> beta character +i and the established c,Q actions.

The eta term is not needed to select this line.  It must also not be identified
with tau_rel: its source factor is the square root in (8),(9), subject to the
actual Pfaffian data.

For a physical P1 transition amplitude, the smallest remaining premise is:

  the outgoing seam response is a complex-linear covector on the charged
  negative-sea defect, or is naturally carried to that covector by the local
  cyclic source-sector map.                                      (12)

Under (12), the original shift fills V, the response annihilates those three
added modes, and the scalar transition is the canonical pairing K^* tensor K
to C.  This gives the dual orientation without changing the sea convention and
without promoting C3 to a physical bulk source.  The exact original formulas
audited above provide the winding, eta scalar, positivity and determinant
phase, but they do not state (12).
