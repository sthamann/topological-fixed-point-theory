THE CUBIC CAUCHY RANGE: HARDY CLOSURE AND THE REMAINING OPERATOR DOMAIN
============================================================================
27 October 2026. Source audit and exact local branch calculation.
No Atlas or paper source was changed.


QUESTION AND VERDICT
====================

On

  C: eta^2=1-w^6,

the original marked function

  z=eta/(1-w^3),       w^3=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1)                 (1)

defines a three sheeted map pi:C->P1_z. The chosen spin line S_0 satisfies
S_0^2=K_C and is the unique c invariant even theta characteristic. Does the
actual primitive boundary operator have Cauchy range

  H2(pi^(-1)D,S_0)=H2(D) direct-sum H2(D) direct-sum H2(D), (2)

with the old seam shift represented by multiplication by z?

The smallest decisive test has two exact outcomes.

  1. CONDITIONAL ANALYTIC THEOREM. If the supplied operator is the standard
     chiral spin Dirac on the normalization of pi^(-1)D, with its induced
     half form norm, then its closed L2 Cauchy range is canonically (2). No
     sheet metric, U(3) mixing, or boundary profile is selected. The cubic
     branch exponent is exactly -1/3 and is L2 integrable.

  2. SOURCE AND DOMAIN OBSTRUCTION. The smooth ambient spinor domain is not
     the direct sum of three unrelated H1 circle domains. At each of the four
     branch values it retains a six half edge matching condition. That
     condition disappears only after L2 closure. The original TFPT boundary
     source supplies an abstract self-adjoint B_Sigma on a smooth product
     collar, but gives neither its formula nor a domain map to this cubic
     boundary. The older D_Gamma and U_Gamma supply the circle generator and
     winding, but not this missing map. Therefore the actual operator arrow
     is not yet proved by the original source.

This is a type level decision, not a new model. Standard Calderon theory can
transport the result after a marked collared spin intertwiner is given. It
cannot construct that intertwiner or replace the missing B_Sigma by a newly
chosen Dolbeault operator.


1. THE OPEN PULLBACK IS THREE DISKS
===================================

Let D={|z|<1}. The only ramification values of pi are

  z_j in {1,-1,i,-i}.                                      (3)

They lie on the boundary. Hence pi is unramified over D. Formula (1) has
three holomorphic cubic roots over D, so

  pi^(-1)D = D_0 disjoint-union D_1 disjoint-union D_2,     (4)

and pi restricts to a biholomorphism D_a->D on every component. The centers
are q_a=-omega^a above z=0. The source symmetries act by

  c:(w,eta,z)->(omega w,eta,z),
  Q:(w,eta,z)->(1/w,-i eta/w^3,i z).                       (5)

Thus c cyclically permutes the three disks, while Q covers the quarter turn
z->i z and sends q_a to q_(-a). These are inherited marked actions, not a
choice of a three channel frame.

On D_a choose the holomorphic half form e with

  e^2=dz.                                                   (6)

It is unique up to sign. For psi=f(z)e the induced spin norm is

  ||psi||^2_boundary = integral_|z|=1 |f(z)|^2 |dz|.       (7)

Therefore the standard chiral spin kernel on the normalized disks has
orthogonal boundary range H2(D)^3 and orthogonal projector

  P_norm=P_H direct-sum P_H direct-sum P_H.                (8)

The scalar presentation H2(D)^3 is defined up to the three harmless frame
signs. The norm, closed subspace, and orthogonal projector in (7) and (8) are
intrinsic. No frame sign creates a metric fit or changes the range.


2. THE EXACT CUBIC BRANCH EXPONENT
==================================

At a branch point over z_j=+/-1 use u=w. At a branch point over z_j=+/-i use
u=1/w. In either case (1) gives, after choosing the appropriate value z_j,

  z=z_j(1+u^3+(1/2)u^6+O(u^9)),
  dz=3z_j u^2(1+O(u^3))du.                                (9)

Write the boundary as z=z_j exp(i s). The six incident germs are

  u_(a,+)(s)=omega^a exp(i pi/6)s^(1/3)(1+O(s)),   s>0,
  u_(a,-)(s)=omega^a exp(-i pi/6)|s|^(1/3)(1+O(s)), s<0.   (10)

Let an ambient smooth spinor germ be

  psi=F(u)(du)^(1/2),
  F(u)=F_0+F_1u+F_2u^2+O(u^3).                            (11)

In the base frame e=(dz)^(1/2), the coefficient is

  f(z)=F(u)(du/dz)^(1/2)
      =(3z_j)^(-1/2)u^(-1)(F_0+F_1u+F_2u^2+O(u^3)).       (12)

Consequently

  f_(a,+/-)(s)=kappa_(j,a,+/-)|s|^(-1/3)
      [F_0+F_1u_(a,+/-)(s)+F_2u_(a,+/-)(s)^2+O(s)].       (13)

Its squared norm has leading behavior

  |f(s)|^2 ds = O(|s|^(-2/3))ds,                          (14)

which is integrable. The four branch values have measure zero. Hence the
half form trace extends to L2 on all three normalized boundary circles.

This is the cubic analogue of the already proved square root exponent
-1/4 in TRACE-METRIC-AND-DOMAIN.txt. The change from -1/4 to -1/3 is forced
by dz approximately u^2du and is not a fitted weight.


3. WHY THE CLOSED RANGE IS H2^3
================================

The smooth ambient core retains equation (13). In particular all six
half-edge germs at one branch value arise from the same coefficients F_k.
Its closure in a specified Sobolev or graph norm retains exactly the traces
controlled by that norm. Ordinary intrinsic H1 on the six edges retains the
common vertex value F_0; it does not retain the complete Taylor series.
Higher Sobolev orders retain the corresponding finite jet. None of these
finite conditions reduces the closed L2 analytic range.

There is a direct density proof at every fixed finite regularity. For m>=1,
multiplication by

  a_m(z)=(1-z^4)^m                                         (15)

forces as many boundary jets to vanish as required once m is large enough.
Therefore triples a_m p_a, with p_a polynomials, satisfy the matching needed
for any one fixed Sobolev order after m is chosen for that order. The
polynomial 1-z^4 has no zero in D and only boundary zeros, hence it is outer.
Outer cyclicity implies

  closure{(1-z^4)^m p:p polynomial}=H2(D).                (16)

For a literal C-infinity core one may use one explicit flat outer factor.
Choose 0<rho<1 and the principal branch

  G(z)=exp(-(1-z^4)^(-rho)).                                (17)

Since Re(1-z^4)>0 in D, this branch is holomorphic and zero-free. Near every
fourth root z_j of one, log|G| is of order -|s|^(-rho), which is integrable.
Thus G is outer. It and every derivative tend to zero at each z_j from inside
the disk. The intrinsic spin coefficients u G(z(u))p_a(z(u)) are therefore
flat at every vertex. They admit compatible smooth extensions through the
outside wedges by the ordinary smooth extension and partition construction;
holomorphy is required only on the inside disks. Polynomial multiples of G
are dense in H2 by outer cyclicity.

Applying either finite-order construction, or (17) for the smooth core,
independently on the three disks proves that the L2 closure of the compatible
analytic core is all of H2(D)^3. This proves (8) even though a chosen strong
domain remains coupled.

The result is precisely about the closed Cauchy range. It does not identify
an oblique Plemelj kernel, a finite-temperature covariance, or an interacting
state with P_norm.


4. THE H1 DOMAIN DOES NOT SPLIT ON THE SINGULAR BOUNDARY
========================================================

There are two different boundary objects.

  * On the normalization, the boundary is three disjoint smooth circles.
    Its standard first-order domain is H1(S1)^3. This gives (8) directly.

  * In the ambient curve, the same boundary image has four vertices, each
    with six incident half edges. Its smooth core obeys the common Taylor
    relations (13). The common vertex value survives in its intrinsic H1
    closure. Higher jets survive only in correspondingly stronger norms.

The naked constant coefficient circle generator does not preserve the
natural traced ambient smooth core. Indeed an allowed leading coefficient
f(s) approximately s^(-1/3) is sent by -i partial_s to a term of order
s^(-4/3), which is not L2. The intrinsic smooth operator in the u coordinate,
when expressed through s approximately u^3, necessarily acquires a
degenerate variable coefficient and the spin connection term. Thus the
following three claims cannot be combined without another domain theorem:

  (i) the constant generator -i partial_theta,
  (ii) the ambient smooth six-edge matching,
  (iii) a self-adjoint graph operator on that singular boundary.           (18)

A self-adjoint six-edge vertex condition may exist, but it has to be stated
and proved as the graph closure of a named core. It is not automatically the
abstract B_Sigma of the smooth P1 seam. Choosing the normalization instead is
mathematically clean, but it deliberately separates the vertices. L2 closure
alone does not decide between these operator domains. The derivative test
above therefore rules out only the naive constant-generator identification
on the natural ambient trace core. It is not a no-go theorem for every
possible primitive boundary operator.

There is also a framing distinction. In the holomorphic frame e^2=dz, the
coefficient number operator is N=-i partial_theta with integer modes. In an
adapted bounding-spin frame the geometric tangential Dirac can be N+1/2, up
to the boundary orientation, with Neveu-Schwarz spectrum Z+1/2. Both have the
same Hardy sign range after the corresponding convention is fixed, and both
satisfy the same one-step commutator with M_z. The original source must say
which framed operator and domain its B_Sigma denotes.


5. THE CONDITIONAL MARKED OPERATOR DICTIONARY
==============================================

On the normalized three-disk space, the natural candidate dictionary is

  boundary Hilbert space     -> L2(S1)^3,
  positive Cauchy range      -> H2(D)^3,
  seam multiplier U_Gamma   -> M_z,
  cubic symmetry c           -> cyclic sheet permutation,
  quarter map Q              -> z->i z with its S_0 spin lift.             (19)

In the holomorphic coefficient convention,

  [N,M_z]=M_z,
  c M_z c^(-1)=M_z,
  Q^* M_z (Q^*)^(-1)=i M_z,
  Q^* c (Q^*)^(-1)=c^(-1).                                (20)

The Q lift on S_0 contributes the already proved square-root phase. Equations
(19) and (20) are exact on the named normalized candidate. They become facts
about the primitive source only after a unitary domain intertwiner is given.


6. WHAT THE ORIGINAL SOURCES ACTUALLY PROVIDE
==============================================

TFPT45 starts from

  T_bound=(A_+,H_+,D_+,J,Gamma,B_Sigma),
  D_+=gamma_n(partial_n+B_Sigma),                            (21)

where B_Sigma is assumed self-adjoint on the smooth compact boundary of a
reflection-regular product collar. It then constructs the Calderon projector
from that supplied datum. The source gives no explicit B_Sigma formula or
domain on the cubic curve.

The older fullv21 source separately takes

  D_Gamma=i d/dtheta,
  U_Gamma(theta)=exp(i theta),                              (22)

and computes spectral flow one. This fixes a circle representative and a
one-step winding. It does not identify D_Gamma with B_Sigma, identify its
circle with the normalization of pi^(-1)D, or transport the six-edge domain.

The distinction is decisive because the hypotheses of (21) are a smooth
boundary and a product collar. The ambient cubic boundary in Section 4 is a
singular graph. The normalized boundary is smooth, but passing to it discards
the H1 vertex matching. Neither interpretation follows merely from (21).

Standard results on Calderon projectors are functorial for a supplied smooth
Dirac operator and its boundary domain. They do not manufacture an
identification between two source problems. See:

  C. Baer and W. Ballmann, Boundary value problems for elliptic differential
  operators of first order, arXiv:1101.1196;

  C. Guillarmou, S. Moroianu and J. Park, Bergman and Calderon projectors for
  Dirac operators, arXiv:1009.3179, for the natural spin Calderon
  construction used by the earlier source audit.


7. THE DIRECT PUSHFORWARD IS NOT THE OLD RANK THREE ANCHOR
==========================================================

The direct pushforward supplies a useful exclusion without choosing a new
bundle. Let E=pi_*S_0. Since pi is finite,

  rank E=3,
  chi(E)=chi(S_0)=deg(S_0)+1-g(C)=1+1-2=0.                (23)

On P1 this gives deg E=-3. The selected even theta characteristic is
non-effective, so

  H0(P1,E)=H0(C,S_0)=0.                                  (24)

Every Birkhoff-Grothendieck summand therefore has degree at most -1. Their
sum is -3, hence

  pi_*S_0 isomorphic O(-1) direct-sum O(-1) direct-sum O(-1).              (25)

The original v117/P2 anchor O(-2) direct-sum O(-1)^2 is therefore not the
direct pushforward of this C3 spin source. An elementary modification at
z=0 could change (25) to that splitting without choosing one q point, but it
would be an additional modification at a regular point. No checked original
source requires it, and it may change the connection by an apparent pole.
It is not inserted here.


8. THE SMALLEST REMAINING PREMISE
==================================

The actual source arrow would be proved by one of the following two precise
data sets.

  NORMALIZED OPTION:

    W:H_boundary -> L2(S1)^3,
    W Dom(B_Sigma)=Dom(B_spin)^3,
    W B_Sigma W^(-1)=B_spin direct-sum B_spin direct-sum B_spin,
    W U_Gamma W^(-1)=M_z,                                  (26)

  with W equivariant for c and the marked Q spin lift, together with either
  an intertwiner of the full one-sided interior Dirac problems and their
  boundary traces, or an independent proof that W maps the actual harmonic
  Cauchy data onto the normalized analytic range. A local isometric embedding
  onto the relevant reducing sector can suffice. Global surjectivity onto all
  three channels is a stronger sufficient condition, not a necessary one.

  SINGULAR GRAPH OPTION:

    W maps the actual source to a specified six-edge Dirac problem whose
    vertex relations are the H1 closure of the ambient smooth core in (13),
    and it intertwines the same seam and symmetry marks. That graph problem
    must separately be proved self-adjoint and elliptic, and its harmonic
    Cauchy range must separately be identified.               (27)

A tangential relation in (26) alone does not imply

  W P_C W^(-1)=P_norm.                                      (28)

The Calderon range depends on the full interior operator, not only on its
adapted tangential operator. Equation (28) follows when the full one-sided
Dirac problem and boundary trace are intertwined, because W then maps the
boundary values of harmonic interior solutions. It may also be established
directly by proving equality of those Cauchy-data ranges.

Standard smooth Calderon functoriality does not apply automatically to the
singular graph in (27). The H2(D)^3 density theorem in Sections 2 and 3 is a
theorem about the particular ambient analytic core. It is not a theorem that
every self-adjoint six-edge vertex condition has that Cauchy range.

No checked original source supplies the strengthened data following (26) or
the graph theorem in (27). This is now the first missing datum. It is narrower
than a new field model and stronger than a dimension or winding match.

FINAL SCOPE
===========

The cubic geometry itself now supplies a canonical three-Hardy closed range
and the exact branch exponent. It also shows why this L2 fact does not settle
the source domain: normalization and ambient H1 matching are different
operator problems. The actual TFPT primitive B_Sigma remains an input with no
formula tying it to either one. The next proof must construct the marked
full interior and Cauchy-data intertwiner accompanying (26), an independent
proof of the actual Cauchy range, or the explicit graph theorem (27).
Replacing these data by a convenient Dolbeault operator would change the
source and is not done.
