VERDICT

The corrected report is accepted.  The local branch analysis, the L2 Hardy
closure on the normalization and the pushforward splitting are correct under
the stated massless chiral spin Dirac hypothesis.  They prove a useful
conditional theorem.  They do not identify the primitive P1 Cauchy range.

The final SHA correctly repairs the two material scope issues found in the
earlier draft:

  1. an intertwiner of the tangential boundary operator B_Sigma alone does
     not imply an intertwiner of the global Calderon projectors;

  2. the cited smooth-boundary Calderon theorems do not automatically apply
     to a singular six-edge graph domain.

It now requires a full interior/Cauchy-data transport or a separate range
proof, and it treats the graph case as a new operator theorem.  The source
gap is therefore neither hidden nor enlarged into an unnecessary global
surjectivity demand.


1. LOCAL HALF FORM AND INTEGRABILITY ARE CORRECT

At every ramification point the local map has

  z-z_j approximately z_j u^3,
  dz approximately 3 z_j u^2 du.

For an ambient smooth spinor F(u)(du)^(1/2), its coefficient in the base half
form e=(dz)^(1/2) is

  f(z)=F(u)(du/dz)^(1/2) approximately const u^(-1).

Since the boundary variable satisfies |u| approximately |s|^(1/3),

  f approximately |s|^(-1/3),
  |f|^2 ds approximately |s|^(-2/3) ds.

The trace is therefore L2.  Its derivative in the base parameter has order
|s|^(-4/3) and is not L2, so the report correctly rejects the naive constant
generator on the natural ambient smooth trace core.  This does not reject a
different named self-adjoint graph operator.


2. THE COMPATIBLE SMOOTH CORE IS L2 DENSE IN H2(D)^3

The finite regularity factors (1-z^4)^m vanish to order 3m in the intrinsic u
coordinate at each branch point and enforce every fixed finite jet condition
when m is large enough.

For the C-infinity core, 1-z^4 maps the disk to the right half-plane.  For
0<rho<1 its principal negative rho power has positive real part and an
integrable boundary singularity.  The function

  G=exp(-(1-z^4)^(-rho))

is zero-free outer and is flat, with all derivatives, at all four branch
values.  Outer cyclicity makes its polynomial multiples dense in H2(D).

Independent triples G p_a have zero Whitney jet at each common
vertex.  The three interior u sectors are separated by exterior sectors, so
the triples admit an ambient C-infinity extension by the standard local
extension and partition-of-unity construction.  No holomorphic extension to
the exterior is needed or claimed.  This supplies the small missing sentence
behind the density argument and proves

  closure_L2(compatible analytic smooth core)=H2(D)^3.

The scalar identification with H2(D)^3 uses a choice of sign for the half
form on each simply connected disk.  The half-form Hardy subspace, its norm
and its projector are canonical; only the displayed scalar frames are
sign-dependent.


3. THE STRONG DOMAIN REMAINS DIFFERENT

The L2 result does not split every strong domain.  In the intrinsic u edge
coordinate, the H1 closure of the ambient smooth core retains the common
spinor value at the six incident half edges.  It does not retain all Taylor
coefficients.  Higher finite jets require higher Sobolev order.  The report's
separation of L2 closure, H1 matching and C-infinity germs is therefore
correct.

This also answers the global trace concern.  Arbitrary Hardy triples need not
be literal traces of globally holomorphic spinors on the compact curve.
They are L2 limits of chiral solutions on the three interior disks whose
boundary germs possess ambient smooth extensions.  That is exactly the
conditional Cauchy range statement and no stronger global holomorphic claim.


4. THE DIRECT PUSHFORWARD SPLITTING IS EXACT

For E=pi_*S_0,

  rank(E)=3,
  chi(E)=chi(S_0)=0,
  deg(E)=-3.

The selected even degree-one theta characteristic is non-effective, so
H0(E)=H0(S_0)=0.  Every Birkhoff-Grothendieck summand has degree at most -1;
three such degrees summing to -3 must all equal -1.  Hence

  pi_*S_0=O(-1) direct-sum O(-1) direct-sum O(-1).

This does not equal the old O(-2) direct-sum O(-1)^2 anchor.  An elementary
modification would be additional data, as the report states.


5. CORRECTED SCOPE: BOUNDARY B DOES NOT DETERMINE CALDERON P

The final normalized option asks for a boundary unitary W satisfying

  W B_Sigma W^(-1)=B_spin direct-sum B_spin direct-sum B_spin

and explicitly says this alone does not yield W P_C W^(-1)=P_norm.  That is
correct: it does not follow from the boundary operator alone.
The Calderon range depends on the bulk first-order operator, its collar
extension and the interior Cauchy problem.  Two bulk operators can have the
same tangential boundary operator and different Cauchy data spaces.

A sufficient premise is a marked unitary intertwiner of the full
bulk Dirac or Poisson/Cauchy trace problems, whose boundary restriction gives
the displayed W.  A weaker sufficient premise may directly intertwine the
closed Cauchy trace operators.  Merely intertwining B_Sigma, its sign or its
functional calculus is insufficient.

Surjectivity onto the entire primitive P1 Hilbert space is not necessary.  A
reducing marked source sector and an isometric intertwiner of its complete
Cauchy problem are enough for the scoped source arrow.


6. CORRECTED SCOPE: THE GRAPH NEEDS ITS OWN THEOREM

The singular graph option cannot invoke the cited smooth-boundary Calderon
functoriality without another theorem.  One must define the first-order graph
operator, its six-edge vertex condition, adjoint and self-adjoint domain, and
then prove that its Poisson range has the claimed L2 closure.  An arbitrary
self-adjoint vertex condition need not have the ambient analytic core used in
the density proof and need not produce H2(D)^3.

The density theorem proves the closure for the named compatible
analytic core.  It is not by itself a Calderon theorem for every graph
realization of that boundary.  The final report now states the singular option
as an additional operator premise and does not present it as an immediate
application of Bär-Ballmann or Guillarmou-Moroianu-Park.


7. SOURCE SCOPE

The original sources still do not provide any of the following:

  * a marked bulk Dirac or Cauchy-trace intertwiner to the normalized C3
    disks;

  * a proved reducing source sector with this complete Cauchy range;

  * a named self-adjoint six-edge operator whose analytic core is the one
    used above;

  * an identification of the source B_Sigma time and domain with either
    candidate.

Accordingly the mathematical L2 Hardy theorem is positive and exact, while
the P1 source arrow remains conditional.  No new Dolbeault or graph model is
needed merely to state that first missing premise.
