PROJECTOR CAR DEFECT LINE REVIEW
The full CAR state fixes all words but its ket line is dual to the Hardy Fredholm line
2026-10-27

VERDICT

The full projector covariance theorem applies and gives a genuine positive
result: once the original three-sheet boundary covariance is the shifted
projection, positivity fixes the complete charged CAR state and every CAR
word.  No further Gaussian or higher-moment choice remains.

The proposed determinant-line identification has the opposite orientation
for the already fixed source winding.  In the old source convention the
occupied covariance is the negative sea C, while the positive Hardy
projection is its complement P_+=I-C.  For the same ambient unitary U=M_z,

  U C U^*=C+Pi_V,
  P_+-U P_+ U^*=Pi_V.                                         (1)

Thus the source shift fills the three-dimensional defect V.  Its shifted
vacuum ket lies in det(V), with beta character -i.  The Hardy Fredholm line
is det(V)^(-1), with beta character +i.  It is the dual bra or anomaly line,
not the shifted ket line itself.

The state functional is phase blind.  Its complete CAR correlations therefore
do not choose between these two dual lines.  A direct connection to the
inverse q norm is valid only if the original sewing or response functional
uses the dual bra, determinant or Pfaffian orientation.  The choice between
covariant ket and contravariant dual is not supplied merely by projector
covariance and positivity.


1. THE TWO PROJECTION FORMULAS HAVE OPPOSITE ROLES

On the old oriented circle, with 0<a<1,

  C=1_(-infinity,0)(B_a),
  P_+=I-C,
  u psi_n=psi_(n+1).

The checked source identity is

  u C u^*=C+Pi_0,                                             (2)

so u adds the formerly empty mode psi_0 to the filled sea.  Taking
complements gives

  P_+-uP_+u^*=Pi_0.                                           (3)

Equation (3) is the Toeplitz or Hardy cokernel formula.  It does not say that
P_+ is the occupied covariance.  Calling the Hardy projection P and then
using it as the occupation covariance reverses the existing particle and
hole convention.

The three-sheet lift has the identical structure.  Let V be the three
dimensional q evaluation space.  With P_H the source Hardy projection and
C=I-P_H the inherited filled-sea covariance,

  U C U^*=C+Pi_V,
  P_H-U P_H U^*=Pi_V.                                         (4)

Choosing instead an occupied covariance P_H would give P'=P_H-Pi_V and a
three-hole state.  That is a mathematically valid charge-conjugate
polarization, but it is not the existing source orientation unless an
explicit source map reverses the old time, charge and adjoint conventions.


2. POSITIVITY FIXES THE SHIFTED STATE AND ALL ITS WORDS

Choose an orthonormal basis v_1,v_2,v_3 of V.  In the Fock representation of
C, the shifted state from (4) is

  Xi=a^*(v_1)a^*(v_2)a^*(v_3) Omega                             (5)

up to one phase and the corresponding orientation convention.  CAR gives

  ||Xi||=1,
  covariance(Xi)=C+Pi_V.                                      (6)

Because C+Pi_V is a projection on the full one-particle Hilbert space, the
projector-state rigidity theorem applies.  Any normalized positive CAR state
with this full covariance is the unique pure gauge-invariant quasifree state
of that projection.  Every finite CAR word is therefore its Wick contraction.

This is stronger than a two-point fit.  It supplies all neutral and charged
CAR correlations allowed by the algebra once the complete covariance
identity (4) has been derived on the actual source.  It does not fix the
overall phase of Xi, because no state expectation can observe that phase.


3. KET, BRA AND FREDHOLM LINE HAVE DUAL CHARACTERS

Let g preserve the source data and act on V.  With the conventional linear
creation operators, the ket (5) transforms by

  det(g|V).

Its dual bra transforms by det(g|V)^(-1).  The same conclusion follows from
the relative semi-infinite wedge: adding the occupied subspace V multiplies
the ket vacuum line by det(V), while the dual vacuum line is det(V)^(-1).

For the source-selected hyperelliptic lift beta,

  beta|V=i I_3.

Therefore

  beta|ket=i^3=-i,
  beta|dual bra=(i^3)^(-1)=+i.                                (7)

The cubic c cyclically permutes the three fibers and has determinant +1.
For Q, the determinant includes the transposition of q_1 and q_2, exactly the
B_O ordering sign already isolated in the Hardy review.  In the full family,

  ket line=B(E)=N_q(S_0) tensor B_O,
  dual line=B(E)^(-1)=N_q(S_0)^(-1) tensor B_O^(-1),           (8)

where E=S_0 tensor P_spin and Nm_Dq(P_spin) is canonically trivial.

Multiplication by z on the Hardy space is injective with cokernel V.  Hence
its Fredholm determinant line is

  delta(M_z)=det ker(M_z) tensor det coker(M_z)^(-1)
            =det(V)^(-1).                                     (9)

Equations (7)-(9) prove the orientation mismatch.  They also show the exact
positive bridge: the Hardy line is the dual charged-vacuum line of the actual
three-mode CAR defect.

Both lines are odd superlines because dim(V)=3.  Dualizing reverses their
central phase but not their parity.  No additional fermionic parity choice is
needed once the full CAR source and its orientation are fixed.  What remains
to be selected is whether the source amplitude uses the ket or its dual.


4. WHY ALL CORRELATIONS DO NOT SELECT THE INVERSE LINE

The vector state is

  omega_Xi(A)=<Xi,A Xi>.

Under Xi -> exp(i phi)Xi, the bra and ket phases cancel.  The same cancellation
occurs in every covariance and every higher CAR word.  Projector-state
rigidity therefore determines the ray and all state correlations, but it
cannot decide whether a separate charged boundary amplitude is represented
covariantly by the ket line or contravariantly by its dual.

This is also visible in the old rank-one calculation.  The source winding
creates c_0^* Omega, while the directed relative Fredholm determinant can be
taken in either order and gives 1-z or (1-z)^(-1).  The state fixes the
rank-one covariance defect; the response contract fixes which directed
determinant is read.

Consequently the full projector theorem removes every independent
higher-correlation or Gaussian assumption.  It does not remove the typed
boundary-amplitude orientation needed to identify the CAR defect with
N_q^(-1).


5. SMALLEST REMAINING SOURCE TEST

No new covariance or model is required.  The exact remaining question is:

  Does the original boundary, sewing, eta or Pfaffian functional pair with
  the shifted CAR vacuum as a bra and therefore take values in
  det(V)^(-1), or does it use the ket line det(V)?

If the original amplitude is a covector on the shifted charged sector, then
(9) gives the desired inverse norm line, its beta phase +i and its odd
Fredholm parity directly from the actual CAR defect.  If the source only
supplies the positive state omega_Xi, its phases cancel and no norm-line
character follows.

An explicit reversal that declares the occupied projection to be P_H would
also produce a hole ket in det(V)^(-1), but that reversal is not licensed by
the inherited source winding.  It would have to be proved by an actual
orientation-changing source map rather than introduced to match the desired
line.

Thus the new CAR connection is real and useful: it identifies the Hardy
Fredholm line as the dual of the unique charged three-mode defect and fixes
all its correlation functions.  It does not yet prove that the physical
source reads that dual rather than the ket.
