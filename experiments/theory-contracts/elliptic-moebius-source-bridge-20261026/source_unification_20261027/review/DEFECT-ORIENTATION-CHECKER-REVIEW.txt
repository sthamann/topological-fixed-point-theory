INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF check_defect_orientation.py
2026-10-27

VERDICT

The finite CAR calculation is correct and its saved scope is properly
conditional.  It proves the orientation statement for the given
three-dimensional defect representation.  It does not prove that the
physical P1 source supplies this C3 Hardy covariance or its operator map.

The checker uses the complete eight-dimensional exterior algebra.  Its
Jordan-Wigner signs satisfy all 72 basis CAR tests.  With

  Q=zeta_8 times the transposition (q_1 q_2),
  c=(q_0 q_1 q_2),
  beta=i I_3,

the induced exterior actions obey

  c^3=I,
  Q^2=beta,
  beta^2=(-1)^F,
  Q c Q^(-1)=c^(-1).

The code checks Q and c covariance for every creation operator on every
exterior basis vector.  Since beta=Q^2 and the actions are unitary signed
permutations, the corresponding beta and annihilation covariance follow.

On the filled three-mode ket it obtains, in zeta_8 exponents,

  ket:   c=0, Q=7, beta=6, parity odd,
  dual:  c=0, Q=1, beta=2, parity odd.

Thus beta acts by -i on the ket and +i on the dual.  This agrees with the
analytic determinant calculation and preserves odd parity under duality.
The empty and filled annihilation relations checked at the end, together
with CAR, give the expected zero and identity covariances on the defect
space.

ONE WORDING CORRECTION

The source comment

  "Relative density matrices see no ray phase, but CAR charged words do."

is too broad.  Every expectation in the vector state is invariant under a
common phase of the ket, including expectations of charged words.  The phase
can appear in charged transition amplitudes between different sectors or in
a separately oriented bra, ket or determinant line.  The executable checks,
the JSON verdict and PROJECTOR-CAR-DEFECT-LINE-REVIEW use the correct narrower
statement, so this comment does not invalidate any computed result.
