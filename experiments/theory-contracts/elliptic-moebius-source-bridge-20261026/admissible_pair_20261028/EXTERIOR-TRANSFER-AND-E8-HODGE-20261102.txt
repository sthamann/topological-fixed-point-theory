1. Exact source-tensor identity
------------------------------

Let E=C^5 with its given orientation, H=Lambda^2 E, and use the five original
signed epsilon matrices C_m of hodge_amplitudes(). They are bilinear maps
H -> H*, not endomorphisms with an implicit identification of H* and H.
For ANY complex 5x5 matrix A put D=Lambda^2 A. In the original oriented
duad bases, the fully transported bilinear maps obey

   D^T C_m D = sum_n adj(A)_(n,m) C_n.                         (1)

Here T is transpose, NOT Hermitian adjoint. The adjugate is polynomial and
(1) also holds for singular A. Proof: expanding both sides on four original
vectors is the cofactor identity

 epsilon(e_m,A u,A v,A w,A z)
   = sum_n adj(A)_(n,m) epsilon(e_n,u,v,w,z).

Thus a correctly typed common A on both wedge legs keeps the original marked
epsilon bracket in its original 5-dimensional bracket span. For invertible A
the two spans are equal; for singular A the span can shrink. It does not say
that a given physical or interacting transfer actually has this common A, nor
that the source implements the necessary tensor-product representation.

An independently selected two-mode particle/hole transfer is not an A on E.
The R087 two-mode diagonal transfer must not be inserted into (1) by dimension
matching. The family leg, source scalar, reference, phase, domain, original
field insertion, reflection and time transport all require the actual map.

2. Original finite 5-cycle and the trace condition
-------------------------------------------------

In the marked carrier chart take the cyclic slot permutation A e_j=e_(j+1).
Then A^5=I, (1) rotates the original five C_m, and the ordinary Kraus effect
sum_m K_m^* K_m=3I for K_m=D^T C_m D. Therefore the given Hodge CPTP map
constructed with K_m/sqrt(3) agrees with the original one. This is the
carrier's algebraic five-cycle; it is not an identification with physical time.

For an arbitrary supplied A, put B=adj(A). The original 25 matrices C_n^* C_q
are linearly independent, while sum_n C_n^* C_n=3I. Consequently the five
equally weighted K_m give a trace-preserving map after ONE common scalar
rescaling iff B B^*=r I_5 for some r>0. For invertible A this is equivalent
to A A^*=s I_5 with s>0. Thus preserving the Hodge span alone does not ensure
that a naive unweighted carrier operation is TP. A family-dependent positive
environment metric, more channels or an actual original normalization could
alter this conclusion, so this is not a no-go for the full TFPT source.

An exact diagnostic, not a proposed physical source: for A=diag(1,1,1,1,1/2),
the effect sum K_m^*K_m/3 equals 1/2 on duad |01> and 1/4 on |04>. No single
normalizing scalar makes both one. This distinguishes source span from source
weight and is compatible with the more general R086 joint family theorem.

3. The precise interaction term that would need evaluation
-----------------------------------------------------------

On a finite regularized common Fock representation, suppose the ACTUAL source
generator could be written H= dGamma(h)+V, with V retaining all its original
field couplings and vertices. This decomposition itself is a premise that
must be derived from the original source; it is not asserted for S_adm here.
For a creator a*(f), put f_s=exp(-s h) f. Differentiation of

   F(s)=exp(-(ell-s)H) a*(f_s) exp(-sH)

gives the exact identity

 exp(-ell H)a*(f) - a*(exp(-ell h)f)exp(-ell H)
   = - integral_0^ell exp(-(ell-s)H) [V,a*(f_s)] exp(-sH) ds. (2)

Equation (2) says what must be evaluated before a free creator relation can be
applied to the interacting generator. Vanishing of the integral is a condition;
it need not follow from one-particle data, nor does nonzero V alone prove that
every integral or matrix element is nonzero. For time-dependent external fields
the ordered propagator and its actual source insertions must replace the
constant-h formula. The two equations concern different mathematical levels:
(1) is a finite carrier bracket identity; (2) is a conditional Fock generator
identity. They are not yet an identified common physical process.

The checker applies (2) to the already recorded R087 two-mode control with
ell=1, h=diag(log 4,log 64) and V=-(log 2)n1 n2. The full transfer is
diag(1,1/4,1/64,1/128), and for the first creator the exact defect in the
|second> to |pair> matrix entry is 1/256 on BOTH sides of (2). The same
matrix is the previous counterexample, not a source-selected interaction.
