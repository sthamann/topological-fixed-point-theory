THE REAL TRACE METRIC AND DOMAIN OF THE ELLIPTIC SPIN KERNEL
============================================================
26 October 2026. Exact L2 trace, branch weights and positivity test.

Question and verdict
====================

The preceding global calculation gives the complete holomorphic rank two
kernel on

  E: y^2=x^4-1,

diagonalized on the punctured base as

  C_+=g P_H g^(-1),       C_-=g^(-1) P_H g,
  g(x)^4=(x^2-1)/(x^2+1).                              (1)

Does the flat NS--NS Spin metric turn (1), on Gamma: |x|=1 or its radial push
offs, into the positive equal time covariance of the already proved elliptic
Gaussian state?

The exact answer has two parts.

  1. POSITIVE RANGE RESULT. The flat metric gives a closed unitary two sheet
     half density trace with no chosen U(2). Both operators in (1) are bounded
     idempotents. Their closed ranges are exactly the ordinary Hardy space,
     because g and g^(-1) are outer. Hence the orthogonal projection onto the
     actual inside analytic L2 trace range is canonically

       P_orth=P_H direct-sum P_H.                       (2)

     The exterior result is the corresponding P_- direct sum P_-.

  2. NEGATIVE STATE IDENTIFICATION. Under the same induced metric, the
     operators C_+ and C_- themselves are not selfadjoint. Thus the bilinear
     closed surface Szego restriction is an oblique Cauchy jump projector,
     not the positive covariance (2). The alternative diagonal weights that
     make (1) orthogonal are not the metric produced by the unitary two sheet
     half density trace. Using them would change the trace/state.

The old Hardy RANGE therefore reappears without a free channel choice, but
the old Hardy STATE and the previously proved finite annulus KMS state do not
follow from this fact. The latter lives on the smooth dividing annulus cut.
No original unitary propagation map from that cut to the ramified equator
graph is present. Consequently an identity

  C_base=T C_E T^*,       0<=C_base<=I,                 (3)

is proved for the orthogonal radial analytic range projector if C_E denotes
that radial Calderon projector. It is not proved for the already existing
elliptic KMS covariance. Defining C_E anew to make (3) true would be a state
change and is not done here.

1. The metric fixed by the actual square torus
================================================

Use the period-one square uniformization

  E=C/(Z+iZ),
  x(z)=sn(c z|-1),       c=2 K(-1)(1+i),
  dx/dz=-i c y.                                          (4)

Thus the flat metric is

  ds_E=|dz|=|dx|/(|c| |y|),       |c|=2 sqrt(2) K(-1).  (5)

For the radial circle Gamma_r, x=r exp(i theta), r different from 1, each of
the two lifted components has exact arclength measure

  dnu_r(theta)
   =r dtheta/[|c| |r^4 exp(4i theta)-1|^(1/2)].         (6)

This is the L2 measure for coefficients in a unit flat Spin frame upstairs.
It is not selected to repair (1); it is the restriction of (5).

Let a chiral half density on sheet epsilon be

  psi_epsilon=f_epsilon(z)(dz)^(1/2).

Define its base trace by the actual Jacobian

  (T_r psi)_epsilon(x)
    =f_epsilon(z_epsilon(x))
       (dz_epsilon/dx)^(1/2) (dx)^(1/2).                (7)

Then change of variables gives exactly

  sum_epsilon integral_Gamma_r |(T_r psi)_epsilon|^2 |dx|
   =sum_epsilon integral_p^-1(Gamma_r)
       |f_epsilon(z)|^2 |dz|.                           (8)

Therefore T_r extends uniquely to a closed unitary

  T_r:L2(p^-1 Gamma_r,alpha;|dz|)
        ->L2(Gamma_r,C^2;|dx|).                         (9)

The sheet character transform J from GLOBAL-SPIN-KERNEL.txt is constant and
unitary. Hence the Hermitian metric after diagonalization remains the identity
matrix in the standard half density measure |dx|. No channel dependent weight
appears in (9).

Equivalently, if the Jacobian in (7) is left out, the same Hilbert space has
the raw weighted measure (6). These two descriptions are unitarily identical;
one may not use the Jacobian and the weight simultaneously.

2. The ramified limit and the actual branch domain
===================================================

At a mark a_j=i^j write theta=theta_j+s. Since

  y^2=exp(4is)-1=4 i s+O(s^2),

equation (6) has the integrable singularity

  dnu_1(s)=[1/(2|c|)] |s|^(-1/2)(1+O(s)) ds.           (10)

The half density trace factor in (7) has modulus

  |dz/dx|^(1/2)
    =[1/sqrt(2|c|)] |s|^(-1/4)(1+O(s)).                (11)

Thus T_1 remains an L2 isometry in the graph limit. The vertices have measure
zero and do not obstruct the closed L2 map.

They do matter for the operator domain. In the Q compatible local coordinate

  (x-a_j)/a_j=z_j^2,

the four seam germs are

  z_j=+/- exp(+i pi/4) sqrt(s)       for s>0,
  z_j=+/- exp(-i pi/4) sqrt(|s|)     for s<0.           (12)

If one smooth upstairs Spin coefficient is

  f(z_j)=f_0+f_1 z_j+O(z_j^2),

its four traced half edge coefficients have the forced form

  F_e(s)=kappa_e |s|^(-1/4)
          [f_0+f_1 z_e(s)+O(|s|)],                     (13)

where kappa_e are the fixed square root Jacobian phases. Formula (13), with
one common f_0 and one common f_1 before inserting the four roots (12), is the
actual smooth branch matching. A free U(2) vertex matrix does not occur.

The closed L2 trace range forgets this point matching because values at four
points have zero L2 measure. The H1 or graph operator domain retains it. In
particular, the pulled back base generator has

  p^*(-i partial_theta)
    =(1+z_j^2)/(2z_j) partial_(z_j),                    (14)

so it does not preserve all smooth germs (13). The natural L2 trace therefore
does not by itself produce the old smooth circle domain or one selfadjoint
four edge Dirac vertex domain.

3. The smallest selfadjointness countertest
============================================

On |x|=1,

  |g(exp(i theta))|^2=|tan(theta)|^(1/2)=:w(theta),
  |g|^(-2)=w^(-1).                                     (15)

Both w and w^(-1) are Muckenhoupt A2 weights: at each of the four marks their
power is +1/2 or -1/2, strictly between -1 and 1. Hence the weighted Hardy
theorem makes both similarities in (1) bounded on standard L2. They obey

  C_+^2=C_+,       C_-^2=C_-.                           (16)

Bounded idempotence is insufficient for a CAR covariance. Positivity would
require selfadjointness.

The failure is exact. Multiplication by g is unitary from L2(w dtheta) to
standard L2, and transports the weighted Riesz projection P_H to C_+. If C_+
were selfadjoint, H2 and H2_- would be orthogonal in L2(w dtheta). But

  <1,exp(-2i theta)>_w
    =(1/(2pi)) integral_0^(2pi)
        |tan theta|^(1/2) exp(-2i theta) dtheta
    =-1/sqrt(2),                                        (17)

while 1 lies in H2 and exp(-2i theta) lies in H2_-. The imaginary part
vanishes by symmetry. The real integral follows from

  integral_0^(pi/2) tan(theta)^(1/2) cos(2theta)dtheta
    =-pi sqrt(2)/4.

Therefore

  C_+^* != C_+.

Replacing w by w^(-1) changes the sign in (17), so C_- is not selfadjoint
either. Consequently neither C_+ nor C_- satisfies 0<=C<=I in the actual
standard trace Hilbert space (9).

One can make C_+ orthogonal by declaring the target weight |g|^(-2), and C_-
by declaring |g|^2. But after (7)-(9) the actual induced metric is the identity
in both character channels. The diagonal replacement

  diag(|g|^(-2),|g|^2)                                  (18)

is therefore an additional nonunitary metric change. It is not supplied by
the flat NS--NS Spin metric and cannot be used to identify the state.

This is also the conceptual distinction between the three kernels:

  * The algebraic Szego half form gives the bilinear two point function.

  * Its boundary Plemelj operator gives the oblique Cauchy idempotents (1).

  * The orthogonal Szego or Calderon projection uses the Hermitian boundary
    metric and projects orthogonally onto the closed analytic trace range.

Only the third object is automatically a positive one particle covariance.

4. Why the Hardy range nevertheless returns canonically
========================================================

The function g is analytic and nonzero in |x|<1. Its boundary logarithm is
integrable and it has no inner factor. Both g and g^(-1) lie in H2 because
their four boundary powers are only +/-1/4. Hence both are outer.

Outer cyclicity gives the domain-safe statements

  closure{g p:p a polynomial}=H2,
  closure{g^(-1) p:p a polynomial}=H2.                 (19)

Every displayed polynomial vector is fixed by the corresponding idempotent:
C_+(g p)=g p and C_-(g^(-1)p)=g^(-1)p. Conversely every vector in either
range is the L2 boundary value of an analytic Smirnov function and hence,
because that boundary value is in L2, belongs to H2. Since bounded
idempotents have closed ranges, (19) therefore sharpens to

  Ran(C_+)=Ran(C_-)=H2.                                 (20)

For r<1 the statement is even simpler: g and g^(-1) are bounded analytic and
boundedly invertible on the closed radial disk, so equality in (20) holds
before taking a closure. At r=1, outer cyclicity supplies the exact limit.

Therefore the orthogonal projection onto the complete two channel inside
trace range, using the actual metric (9), is exactly (2). No U(2), profile or
new metric is selected. For r>1 the same argument in the exterior chart gives

  P_orth,out=P_- direct-sum P_-.                        (21)

This is the precise positive recovery of the Hardy object. It is a statement
about the closed L2 analytic RANGE. It does not retain the pointwise branch
matching (13), and it does not say that the oblique correlator (1) equals the
orthogonal projection (2).

5. Comparison with the proved elliptic Gaussian state
======================================================

The existing positive theorem uses the dividing Real form rho(z)=-bar(z), the
smooth half annulus 0<t<1/2 and

  L=partial_t+B,       B=-i partial_s.

On its two smooth boundary circles the orthogonal Cauchy graph has marginal

  C_A=(1+exp(B))^(-1),                                 (22)

and the full free Gaussian functional is reflection positive for all charged
CAR words. This is a proved positive state on that named cut.

The unitary T_1 in (9) has a different source: it maps the two sheet trace on
p^-1(|x|=1) to the ramified base graph. It does not map the two smooth annulus
boundary circles of (22) to that graph, and it does not intertwine their
normal evolutions. Restricting the holomorphic bilinear Green kernel to the
graph produces (1), whose failed selfadjointness is the concrete witness that
such an equal time identification has not occurred.

The old full collar result says exactly what would be sufficient: retain both
labeled endpoint Hilbert spaces, their actual fiber comparison, the positive
normal length and the complete orthogonal graph projector. Those data recover
the signed B and its KMS marginal. The ramified equator restriction presently
supplies none of that normal product structure.

This also fixes the notation issue. The earlier source uses P_C or G_ell for
the orthogonal L2 projection onto a closed Cauchy graph. In the present radial
problem that role is played by P_orth in (2), after the side and analytic range
have been specified. The oblique operator C_+ direct-sum C_- in (1) cannot be
substituted for that P_C merely because both have the same closed range.

Thus there are two valid positive operators with different scopes:

  [E] P_H direct-sum P_H is the orthogonal projector onto the inside radial
      analytic L2 range fixed by the actual trace metric.

  [E] (1+exp(B))^(-1) is the positive mixed covariance of the existing smooth
      elliptic OS annulus.

There is currently no original unitary intertwiner identifying these states.
The global holomorphic kernel alone is not that intertwiner.

6. Exact status
===============

  [E] The full global Spin kernel yields bounded Cauchy idempotents in the
