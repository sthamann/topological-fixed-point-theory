THE GLOBAL RANK TWO SPIN KERNEL ON THE ELLIPTIC DOUBLE
======================================================
26 October 2026. Exact algebraic continuation and full kernel diagonalization.
Atlas audit frame: revision c76c7d969cabaa790d397657. No Atlas write.

Question and verdict
====================

On the already fixed curve and spin line

  E: y^2=x^4-1,       Q(x,y)=(ix,y),       alpha nontrivial and Q fixed,

does the unique Q invariant branch partition give only the previously proved
local quarter principal part, or the complete global pushed forward Szego
kernel?

It gives the complete kernel. Put

  A(x)=sqrt(x^2-1),       B(x)=sqrt(x^2+1),       y=A B,
  g(x)=sqrt(A(x)/B(x)),   g(x)^4=(x^2-1)/(x^2+1).       (1)

Then the actual normalized alpha Szego kernel is

  S_alpha(p,q)=sqrt(dx dz)/(2(x-z))
    [g(x)/g(z)+g(z)/g(x)].                              (2)

The square roots in (1) are sections on E, with their sheet signs retained.
In the two sheet pushforward, one constant unitary J diagonalizes the full
matrix kernel, not only its Cauchy principal part:

  J^* K_sheet(x,z) J
   =sqrt(dx dz)/(x-z)
      diag(g(x)/g(z),g(z)/g(x)).                        (3)

There is no omitted elliptic correction in (3). The two diagonal entries are
the global quarter local systems selected as an unordered pair by the actual
alpha spin structure. Q exchanges them. Thus the original elliptic data give
a concrete global holomorphic source kernel without a fitted profile or a
free vertex matrix.

This does not turn the four marked punctures into the old smooth quarter
circle. Nor does a holomorphic bilinear kernel alone prove positivity of an
equal time CAR covariance. Those two operator statements remain separately
typed.

1. The partition is the actual alpha spin structure
====================================================

Let R_a denote the ramification point over a and let infinity_+, infinity_-
be the two points over infinity. The divisor

  D_A=R_1+R_(-1)-infinity_+-infinity_-                  (4)

satisfies

  2 D_A=div(x^2-1).                                    (5)

It is nonprincipal. Indeed, if it were principal, a function with at most
simple poles at the two infinities would vanish at both R_1 and R_(-1).
But

  H0(E,O(infinity_++infinity_-))=span(1,x),

and no nonzero affine function of x vanishes at x=1 and x=-1.

Likewise define

  D_B=R_i+R_(-i)-infinity_+-infinity_-.

Since D_A+D_B=div(y), one has D_B~-D_A~D_A. The quarter map Q swaps the two
parts {1,-1} and {i,-i}, so the unordered partition

  {1,-1} | {i,-i}                                      (6)

is Q invariant. The other two nontrivial partitions are exchanged by Q.
Thus O(D_A) is the unique Q fixed nontrivial two torsion spin line. It is the
same alpha already fixed by the original elliptic spin audit. Since alpha is
nontrivial of degree zero,

  H0(E,alpha)=0.                                       (7)

No new spin characteristic is selected in (6).

2. Direct genus one Szego proof
===============================

For p=(x,y_x) and q=(z,y_z), define

  S_part(p,q)=
    [A(x)B(z)+B(x)A(z)]/[2(x-z)]
    (dx/y_x)^(1/2) (dz/y_z)^(1/2).                     (8)

The signs of A and B are chosen coherently so that A B=y on E. Formula (8)
has the defining properties of the alpha Szego kernel:

  * It is a half form in each variable with spin multipliers D_A.

  * At p=q, its only pole has unit residue because both ratios tend to one.

  * At q=h p, the apparent x=z pole cancels between the two numerator terms.

  * It has no other poles.

If S_part and the normalized Szego kernel differed, their difference for
fixed q would be a holomorphic section of alpha. Equation (7) makes that
difference zero. Hence (8) is the actual NS--NS elliptic Szego kernel.

Now use y=A B. The two terms in (8) simplify exactly as

  A(x)B(z)/sqrt(y_x y_z)=g(x)/g(z),
  B(x)A(z)/sqrt(y_x y_z)=g(z)/g(x).                    (9)

Equations (8) and (9) prove (2). This is a direct genus one argument from
pole, multipliers and H0(E,alpha)=0. It does not import a genus two theorem.

For comparison, the same algebraic shape for a hyperelliptic even spin
partition is displayed in E. D'Hoker, M. Hidding and O. Schlotterer,
"Cyclic products of Szego kernels and spin structure sums", JHEP 05 (2023)
073, arXiv:2211.09069, equation (2.26):

  https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.09069

That reference treats genus two. It is a primary literature check of the
partition formula, not the proof of the genus one identity used here.

3. Exact two sheet matrix and constant diagonalization
=======================================================

Set

  a=g(x)/g(z),       b=g(z)/g(x)=a^(-1).

Use the analytic square root of the Jacobian on the second sheet, so the
second half density differs by i. In the common ordered sheet frame the
complete pushforward of (8) is

  K_sheet(x,z)=sqrt(dx dz)/[2(x-z)]
    [[a+b,       i(a-b)],
     [-i(a-b),   a+b   ]].                              (10)

The diagonal entries reconstruct the same sheet kernel (2). The off diagonal
numerator a-b vanishes when x=z, which is the explicit cancellation of the
false pole at the conjugate point.

Let

  J=1/sqrt(2) [[i,-i],[1,1]].                           (11)

Then J is unitary and direct multiplication gives

  J^* K_sheet J=sqrt(dx dz)/(x-z) diag(a,b).            (12)

This proves (3). In particular, the regular part left unspecified by the
earlier local calculation is not an additional matrix mixing term. The full
global kernel splits into the two character kernels

  K_+(x,z)=sqrt(dx dz)/(x-z) g(x)/g(z),
  K_-(x,z)=sqrt(dx dz)/(x-z) g(z)/g(x).                 (13)

Their average reconstructs the cover kernel because the inverse character
transform has the usual factor 1/2.

4. Peripheral monodromy and Q transport
========================================

The common factor

  t(x)=(x^4-1)^(-1/4)=y^(-1/2)

turns A t and B t into g and g^(-1). Therefore no independent fourth root
has been fitted. Around x=1 and x=-1, a positive meridian gives

  (g,g^(-1)) -> (i g,-i g^(-1)).                       (14)

Around x=i and x=-i it gives the inverse pair

  (g,g^(-1)) -> (-i g,+i g^(-1)).                      (15)

In the cyclic branch order 1,i,-1,-i, the four peripheral matrices are

  diag(i,-i), diag(-i,i), diag(i,-i), diag(-i,i),       (16)

and their product is I. These are precisely the global Spin Jacobian lifts
of the raw flat matrices P,-P,P,-P after passing to the character basis.
Each matrix squares to -I, as a central Spin lift should.

The quarter map acts on (1) by

  g(ix)^4=g(x)^(-4).                                   (17)

Thus Q exchanges K_+ and K_- and transports (14) to (15). The unordered
rank two kernel is Q invariant, while neither diagonal line is. The two
abstract coherent choices of local square root sign found from the product
relation amount here to swapping the two character labels. The normalized
kernel, its residue and the unordered partition fix the full rank two object;
they do not canonically name one of its two lines.

5. Exact finite replay
======================

An inline SymPy replay checked

  J^*J=I,
  J^* [[a+b,i(a-b)],[-i(a-b),a+b]] J /2=diag(a,a^(-1)),
  (P)(-P)(P)(-P)=I,
  product of the four matrices in (16)=I,
  every matrix in (16) has square -I,
  [(x^2-1)/(x^2+1)] at x->ix equals its reciprocal.

The pole check is also exact: as z approaches x, a and b approach one, so
(12) has identity residue. The conjugate sheet numerator a-b vanishes to
first order and removes the other apparent pole.

As an independent diagnostic, the square torus uniformization was evaluated
at 80 decimal digits using

  x(z)=sn(c z|-1),       c=2 K(-1)(1+i),
  y(z)=i cn(c z|-1) dn(c z|-1),
  A(z)=i sqrt(1-x(z)^2),       B(z)=y(z)/A(z).

At three generic complex point pairs, the algebraic value

  -i c [A(z)B(w)+B(z)A(w)]/[2(x(z)-x(w))]

agreed with the normalized NS--NS theta kernel

  pi theta_3(pi(z-w)) theta_1'(0)
       /[theta_3(0) theta_1(pi(z-w))]

to absolute errors 8.5e-81, 1.3e-80 and 9.5e-81. This is a diagnostic of
normalization and spin characteristic. The proof remains the pole, multiplier
and H0(alpha)=0 argument in Section 2.

6. Scope and consequence
========================

  [E] The Q invariant partition (6) is the already fixed alpha spin class.

  [E] The complete rank two spin trace diagonalizes as (12), with no

This is a constructive global source connection in the holomorphic sense:
