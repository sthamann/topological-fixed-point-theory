REFLECTION, HALF KINK AND THE ACTUAL FOLDED SOURCE
Corrected independent source and type review, 2026-10-28

Question
========

Does the already present Pin and theta reflection transport the established
half winding sector rho_s and its bounded Weyl transporters on the actual
folded quarter source?  The test must retain the quarter holonomy, the
original positive winding convention, and the original K_alpha time.


Corrected verdict
=================

The geometric quarter switch, sector charge, and K_alpha time are mutually
compatible.  The existing sources do not yet prove that their OS word map
acts on the bounded current Weyls.  That missing typed intertwiner prevents
an unconditional reflection formula for T_IJ.  Two corrections and this
remaining gap are essential.

First, reflection of the eight fold fibres is not the bare permutation
r -> -r mod 8.  Crossing the quasi periodic seam contributes the quarter
phase.  With P the bare arc reversal and

  S_alpha = diag(1,e^(-2 pi i alpha),...,e^(-2 pi i alpha)),              (1)

the exact linear quarter switch is S_alpha P, not P.

Second, the actual v519 checker supplies an anti linear, order reversing
rule on the tested normal ordered disjoint words.  It is not

  A -> Rhat A^* Rhat^-1                                  (false),         (2)

when Rhat is antiunitary.  The map in (2) is linear.  The scalar test already
separates them:

  Theta_OS(i I) = -i I,
  Rhat (i I)^* Rhat^-1 = +i I.                            (3)

For a single disjoint even Majorana word, the phase and reversal sign in the
v519 rule cancel.  This does not prove an operator algebra identity on the
completed even CAR algebra and does not justify exponentiating to W(phi).
The candidate current relation that would close the gap is

  Theta_OS(J(phi))=J(phi o r),
  Theta_OS(W(phi))=W(-phi o r).                         (4, conditional)

Finally, reflection reverses the winding orientation.  For the standard
positive oriented kink profiles,

  b_IJ^+ o r = -b_(rI,rJ)^+.                              (6)

If the missing current relation (4) is supplied, the established smooth
transporter obeys

  T_IJ^+ = W(-b_IJ^+ o F),
  Theta_OS(T_IJ^+) = T_(rI,rJ)^+.                     (7, conditional)

There is no adjoint in (7).  An adjoint appears only if one keeps the raw
negative oriented reflected profile b_raw=b_IJ^+ o r instead of converting
back to the standard positive orientation:

  Theta_OS(T_IJ^+) = W(+b_raw o F)
                    = W(-b_raw o F)^*.                (8, conditional)

The exact sector and time dictionary remains

  alpha -> 1-alpha,
  q     -> -q,
  s     -> -s,
  I     -> rI,
  t     -> -t.                                            (9)

Here [s]=[-s] in the D8 discriminant group, while s and -s remain distinct
oriented representatives before passing to the even observable net.

Separately, the local normality conclusion survives unchanged.  It applies
to every quotient interval I whose closure is compactly contained in the
complement of the cut mark.  On such I the actual folded covariance is a
smooth local U(8) gauge transform of eight NS covariances.  No additional
local normality assumption is needed there.  This supplies no global vacuum,
time, theta, or primitive P1 identification.


1. The three reflection roles
=============================

The original sources contain three typed operations.

1. The linear Green reflection on the paired orientation cover is

     R_G = sigma_1 R_x,
     R_G P_i R_G = P_-i.                                  (10)

   It exchanges the two Pin lifts.  The paired cover is essential for the
   positive full NS Green form.  A single fixed quarter fails its temporal
   zero mode test.

2. The antiunitary one particle symmetry

     Theta_Pin = K R_x                                    (11)

   preserves each fixed Pin domain.  Complex conjugation compensates the
   deck eigenvalue inversion.  It commutes with the full Dirac operator and
   preserves its spectral sea.  It does not perform (10).

3. The v519 OS field reflection is an anti linear, order reversing word
   operation on the normal ordered, disjoint Majorana monomials used in its
   RP Gram matrices.  It is implemented there as

     theta(g_i1 ... g_ik)
       = eta^k s_ik ... s_i1 g_r(ik) ... g_r(i1),
     eta=i.                                               (12)

   It conjugates scalar coefficients and reverses word order.  The same
   checker separately constructs the normalized antiunitary Fock real
   structure Rhat=U_r K and proves clock inversion.  It does not prove that
   the word operation equals Ad_Rhat on the completed current algebra.

These facts are compatible.  The linear Green map exchanges Pin lifts,
K R_x preserves a fixed lift, and the OS map acts on field words.


2. The quarter holonomy in the folded chart
===========================================

The exact fold matrix is

  U_alpha(r,k)=exp(2 pi i(k+alpha)r/8)/sqrt(8),
  r,k in Z/8.                                             (13)

Let P reverse the arc index modulo 8.  Bare P fails at every r other than
zero:

  (P U_alpha)(r,k)
    = exp(2 pi i alpha)
      exp(-2 pi i(k+alpha)r/8)/sqrt(8),  r=1,...,7.       (14)

At alpha=1/4, r=1, k=0, this has the unwanted factor i.  Thus the earlier
bare permutation formula was false.

The correct map follows directly from the quasi periodic source function

  f(theta+2 pi)=exp(2 pi i alpha) f(theta),
  (F_alpha f)_r(x)=f((x+2 pi r)/8)/sqrt(8).               (15)

For the linear source reflection R_x f(theta)=f(-theta),

  (F_(1-alpha) R_x F_alpha^-1 eta)_0(x)
    = eta_0(-x),

  (F_(1-alpha) R_x F_alpha^-1 eta)_r(x)
    = exp(-2 pi i alpha) eta_(8-r)(-x),
      r=1,...,7.                                          (16)

Equivalently,

  S_alpha P U_alpha(:,k)=U_(1-alpha)(:,7-k).             (17)

The antiunitary fixed Pin symmetry has the conjugate seam phase:

  (F_alpha K R_x F_alpha^-1 eta)_0(x)
    = conjugate(eta_0(-x)),

  (F_alpha K R_x F_alpha^-1 eta)_r(x)
    = exp(+2 pi i alpha) conjugate(eta_(8-r)(-x)),
      r=1,...,7,                                          (18)

and hence

  S_alpha^* P conjugate(U_alpha(:,k))=U_alpha(:,k).       (19)

Equation (17) is the quarter switch.  Equation (19) is fixed quarter Pin
invariance.  Both carry the required seam phase.

The existing E8 source chart agrees with the sector part.  With M the fixed
source to E8 isometry and R the source coordinate reversal,

  g=-M R M^-1,
  g d_(1/4)=d_(3/4),
  g s=-s.                                                 (20)

The exact rational vectors are

  s       =(0,0,1,0,0,1,0,0),
  g s     =(0,0,-1,0,0,-1,0,0),
  d_(1/4) =(-1/2,0,1/16,-1/2,0,-3/16,-1/4,-1/4),
  g d_(1/4)
           =(-1/2,0,3/16,-1/2,0,-1/16,-1/4,-1/4)
           =d_(3/4).                                     (21)


3. Type check of the actual OS map
===================================

The false formula (2) fails before any dynamics is considered.  For
antiunitary Rhat,

  A -> Rhat A^* Rhat^-1

is a linear order reversing map because the adjoint and antiunitarity each
conjugate scalars.  By contrast, v519 R1.3 tests its anti linear, order
reversing rule on normal ordered monomials with distinct supports and uses
conjugate(eta) eta=1 in theta^2.

On an even Majorana word of length k=2m, the reversal sign and eta phase in
(12) cancel:

  eta^(2m) (-1)^((2m)(2m-1)/2)
    =(-1)^m (-1)^m=1.                                   (22)

Thus on each single disjoint even monomial the displayed phase and reversal
sign cancel.  This is not an algebra proof.  The v519 helper theta_mono is
deliberately called only on monomials with distinct indices.  If one tries
to extend its generator rule to the full Clifford algebra, then

  theta(g_a^2)=theta(g_a)theta(g_a)=-1,
  theta(1)=1,                                             (23)

because eta=i and g_a^2=1.  The two disagree.  Therefore theta_mono is the
OS normal ordered word operation used for the RP form, not a demonstrated
bounded operator anti automorphism of Cl(16).  The separate Rhat=U_r K is a
genuine antiunitary, but v519 does not identify its adjoint action with
theta_mono on current exponentials.

The original Green source convention is

  Theta psi=-barpsi o r,
  Theta barpsi=-psi o r,                                  (24)

with reversed normal ordered word order.  Formally this sends the neutral
current to

  Theta_OS(J(phi))=J(phi o r).                            (25)

For real phi, anti linearity would then send W(phi) to W(-phi o r), and the
Weyl cocycle would be compatible because reflection reverses the current
symplectic form.  The original reports never prove the required passage
from their Green source word rule to the bounded W(phi) in the folded Hardy
representation.  Equations (4), (7), and (8) remain conditional on exactly
this one intertwiner.


4. Positive kink orientation and rho_s
======================================

Choose a_I^+ with the fixed positive half winding convention used in the
closed half kink report.  Orientation reversal gives, up to a constant that
cancels in differences,

  a_I^+ o r=-a_(rI)^+ + constant.                         (26)

For b_IJ^+=(a_I^+-a_J^+)/2 this proves (6).  If the source current
intertwiner (4) holds, combining it with (6) proves (7).  The geometry fixes
the sign unambiguously; the missing point is whether the actual OS word map
has been extended to the bounded folded Weyls.

The charge label itself transforms by (20):

  Theta_cov rho_(s,I) Theta_cov^-1
    is equivalent to rho_(-s,rI).                         (27)

Because 2s lies in D8, [s]=[-s].  Therefore the reflected endomorphism is
the same D8 sector of the even net.  Equation (27) does not identify the two
oriented charged field representatives.  That representative change uses
the existing q=2s vacuum lattice equivalence.


5. K_alpha time
===============

The actual source evolution is

  U_alpha(t)=exp(-it K_alpha),
  K_alpha=(Lcal_0^alpha-ground)/8.                        (28)

For every charge and every integer mode, the original exact formula

  c_n^alpha(q)
    =nq/8+n(n-1)/16+alpha n/8                            (29)

satisfies

  c_(-n)^(1-alpha)(-q)=c_n^alpha(q).                     (30)

Consequently the quarter exchanged reflection intertwines the ground
subtracted generators and reverses source time,

  Rhat K_alpha Rhat^-1=K_(1-alpha),
  Rhat U_alpha(t) Rhat^-1=U_(1-alpha)(-t),                (31)
  Theta_OS tau_t^alpha
    =tau_(-t)^(1-alpha) Theta_OS.                         (32)

This is K_alpha time, not the unrescaled cover Hamiltonian.  It remains
distinct from the still open primitive P1 OS time.

On the shifted ground rays, up to their irrelevant phases,

  Rhat |d_alpha>=|d_(1-alpha)>,
  omega_(1-alpha)(Rhat A Rhat^-1)
    =conjugate(omega_alpha(A)).                           (33)


6. Local normality on the actual folded state
==============================================

This conclusion is independent of the corrected reflection phases and
follows directly from the existing folded covariance.

In the diagonal fold channels,

  C^-_mu(x-y)
    =exp(i(mu-1/2)(x-y)) C_NS(x-y),
  mu_k=(k+alpha)/8.                                      (34)

Set delta_k=mu_k-1/2 and let I be a quotient interval whose closure is
compactly contained in the complement of the cut mark.  Then

  G_I(x)=U_alpha diag(exp(i delta_k x)) U_alpha^*.        (35)

The exact matrix kernel identity is

  C_fold(x,y)=G_I(x) C_NS^8(x,y) G_I(y)^*.               (36)

The diagonal singularity is therefore the standard Hadamard 1/(x-y)
singularity in every arc component.  The off diagonal arc kernels are
smooth because their source arguments remain separated by a nonzero
multiple of 2 pi/8.  Equivalently,

  [exp(i delta_k(x-y))-1] C_NS(x-y)                       (37)

extends smoothly across x=y.

Because I is proper, G_I has a smooth periodic U(8) extension G that is the
identity outside a slightly larger interval and has zero winding.  Its
Fourier coefficients obey

  sum_n |n| ||G_n||_HS^2 < infinity,

so [P_NS,G] is Hilbert Schmidt.                           (38)

The Shale and Araki criterion implements this local Bogoliubov
transformation.  Hence the actual folded quasifree representation and the
eight fermion NS vacuum representation are locally quasi equivalent on
A(I).

The equivalent covariance argument is also direct.  The compactly
restricted kernel C_fold-C_NS^8 is smooth and therefore trace class.
Powers Stoermer gives

  ||C_fold^(1/2)-C_NS^(1/2)||_2^2
    <=||C_fold-C_NS||_1,                                  (39)

and the same estimate for 1-C.  These are the quasifree local quasi
equivalence hypotheses.

Thus the established rho_s and its smooth bounded Weyl transporters are
normal in the actual folded GNS representation on such I.  No separate
local normality assumption is needed.

This is not global.  The unextended G_I retains the quarter holonomy.  Its
determinant changes by exp(2 pi i(alpha-1/2)) around the full quotient
circle.  Therefore (36) supplies no global vacuum unitary, no global
K_alpha intertwiner, and no global theta identification.


7. Exact correction checks
===========================

The following smallest checks were performed.

* Bare arc reversal counterexample:

    alpha=1/4, r=1, k=0

  gives an extra factor exp(2 pi i alpha)=i.  Multiplication by
  exp(-2 pi i alpha) in S_alpha removes it exactly.

* Antiunitary type counterexample:

    Theta_OS(iI)=-iI,
    Rhat(iI)^*Rhat^-1=+iI.

  Therefore (2) cannot be the v519 OS map.

* Even word check:

    i^(2m)(-1)^((2m)(2m-1)/2)=1

  for every m.  This proves cancellation on one disjoint even monomial.  It
  does not prove multiplication or exponentiation.  The control
  theta(g_a^2)=-1 != theta(1) shows why that extrapolation is invalid.

* Time check:

    c_(-n)^(1-alpha)(-q)-c_n^alpha(q)=0

  identically.


8. Scope and surviving obstruction
===================================

Proved for the given paired Pin cover and actual folded source:

* the holonomy corrected linear quarter switch (16) and (17);
* fixed quarter antiunitary Pin invariance (18) and (19);
* the correct OS word map type and its exact limitation in v519;
* the winding sign required for a positive oriented reflected kink;
* sector reflection [s] -> [-s]=[s];
* the exact K_alpha time reversal;
* local normality of the actual folded state on intervals I whose closure
  is compactly contained in the complement of the cut.

Not proved or claimed:

* equality of the positive and negative oriented charged representatives;
* an OS word to bounded current Weyl intertwiner;
* unconditional reflection covariance of T_IJ in the actual folded Hardy
  representation;
* global equivalence to eight independent NS vacua;
* identification of K_alpha with primitive P1 OS time;
* a primitive P1 rule selecting rho_s or its response.
