1. DETERMINANT WEIGHT AND COCYCLE

For E_L=S_0 tensor L on the genus-two curve, deg(E_L)=g-1=1 and therefore
chi(E_L)=0 for every L in Jac(C).  A scalar lambda on E_L acts on

  det R Gamma(E_L)=det H^0(E_L) tensor det H^1(E_L)^(-1)

with weight lambda^(h^0-h^1)=lambda^chi=1.  This remains true on the theta
divisor because Riemann-Roch gives h^0=h^1 fiberwise; it is not a generic-fiber
argument.

The normalized Poincare bundle supplies the required family statement.  The
hyperelliptic involution fixes q_0 and induces inversion on Jac(C).  An
isomorphism between the corresponding Poincare pullbacks is unique after its
value on {q_0} times Jac(C) is fixed.  Its square is therefore the identity by
the same rigidification.  Consequently functorial composition gives

  beta_D^2=det R Gamma(beta^2)=det R Gamma(-I)=+I,
  alpha_D^4=det R Gamma(alpha^4)=+I.

There is no untracked scalar in these equations.  Removing the rigidification
would reintroduce precisely an additional phase choice.

2. STANDARD SECOND-ORDER THETA SECTIONS

The determinant line is O(-Theta) in the convention used in the report, with
the dual convention changing no zero Euler exponent.  Since S_0 is even and
non-effective, H^0(S_0)=H^1(S_0)=0.  The determinant fiber at the origin is
therefore canonically C and its induced involution is +1.  This pins the
standard symmetric linearization rather than its negative.

For O(2Theta), the four standard second-order theta functions are individually
even under z->-z.  Hence the normalized inversion lift acts as +I on all of
H^0(Jac(C),O(2Theta)).  This agrees with the determinant calculation and with
the factorization of the Kummer map through A/{+1,-1}.

Multiplying this lift by i would produce square -I and scalar iI_4, but that is
an extra mu4 central-character choice.  It is not recovered from the ordinary
determinant functor, S_0, or the local beta phase.

3. CONSEQUENCE AND SCOPE

The source-selected spin relation beta^2=-I and its +i fiber value do not by
themselves imply beta->J=iI_4 on the ordinary four-dimensional second-order
theta module.  Thus the finite Pauli and Clifford candidate remains compatible
but conditional on an additional phase-sensitive extension dictionary.

This is not a no-go for a Pfaffian, metaplectic, or otherwise spin-refined
construction.  Such a refinement can retain square-root phase data, but none
is supplied by the ordinary determinant and Kummer functors checked here.
The next positive bridge would have to construct that refinement from the
original source and compare its central character and full Heisenberg action
with native N64 and J.
