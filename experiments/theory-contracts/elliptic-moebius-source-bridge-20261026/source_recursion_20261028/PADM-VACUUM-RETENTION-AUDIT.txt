VERDICT

The positive color-center result does not yet extend to the original full
projector

    P_adm = P_prim P_sing P_Theta.

The obstruction is now precise and source based.

* P_sing keeps the stipulated E8 vacuum Omega and removes the only other H8
  ground state p_*.  This was proved in CLUSTERING-ADMISSIBLE-SECTOR.txt.
* The original P_prim definition does not say that it keeps Omega.  Its gap
  factor keeps the spectrum of D_prim^* D_prim above a positive threshold.
  Hence, if an identification sent Omega to a D_prim zero mode, the displayed
  original formula would remove it.
* The determinant data determine P_Theta as part of the closed branch, but no
  source formula specifies Q_Theta Omega.  Thus neither P_Theta Omega=Omega nor
  P_Theta Omega=0 follows in the H8 lattice representation.
* The sources prove commutation of D_adm with the admissibility factors.  They
  do not place H8 on the same Hilbert space or prove [P_adm,H8]=0.

Therefore the one-dimensional kernel and positive gap on Ran(P_sing) are
exact, while an H8 clustering theorem on Ran(P_adm) remains conditional on one
typed image and reduction test.  No further projector choice is needed or
allowed; the missing datum is an intertwiner between two already fixed source
representations.


1. THE DECISIVE P_PRIM TEST

The original primitive Hilbert space is

    H_prim = H_Seam tensor H_t,

and its seam selector is P_Seam,+ = (1+iC)/2.  The source then defines

    K_g = sqrt(lambda_gap)
          1_[0,epsilon_adm^2/4)(
             P_Seam,+ D_prim^* D_prim P_Seam,+),

    P_prim = Pi_ker Delta_prim
           = P_Seam,+ P_prim,gap,

    P_prim,gap
      = 1_[epsilon_adm^2/4,infinity)(
          P_Seam,+ D_prim^* D_prim P_Seam,+).

Thus P_prim selects seam-even vectors in the gapped spectral subspace.  For a
seam-even vector xi in the displayed operator domain,

    D_prim xi = 0  implies  P_prim xi = 0.

This is the opposite of an inference that a geometric zero mode must be the
retained vacuum.  It does not prove that P_prim removes the H8 vacuum, because
the original source never supplies a map taking the H8 lattice vector Omega
to a vector xi in H_prim.  It proves that retention cannot be inferred from the
word vacuum or from zero energy.  The actual question is the image identity

    P_prim W Omega = W Omega

for a source-defined isometry W between the H8 representation and the
boundary or OS representation.  W is absent.

This agrees with the already registered Atlas finding

    finding:original-admissibility-requires-image-membership-not-positive-half-or-commutation.

Its source-reviewed contract identifies the direct test P_adm R0=R0 and warns
that commutation alone is insufficient.  The present H8 vacuum is subject to
the same type requirement.


2. THE DECISIVE P_THETA TEST

The full admissibility source defines

    P_Theta = (1+Q_Theta)/2,
    Q_Theta^2=1,
    Q_Theta^*=Q_Theta.

The carrier source fixes the determinant class

    (c1(L2),c1(L3))=(1,0)

and says that P_Theta is thereby determined.  This determines the later
determinant-sector selector inside its own Hilbert factor.  It does not give a
representation of Q_Theta on the stipulated E8 lattice module and nowhere
states

    Q_Theta Omega = +Omega.

The retention value is therefore unspecified, not negative.  No original
definition found in the audited sources explicitly excludes Omega through
P_Theta; equally, none proves that it survives.


3. WHY THE H8 COMMUTATOR DOES NOT FOLLOW

The exact color-center computation gives

    [P_sing,H8]=0

because both operators are diagonal Cartan actions in the stipulated E8
lattice representation.  The full source instead states that D_adm commutes
with iC, P_sing, and Q_Theta.  This is a statement about D_adm on

    H_Seam tensor H_c tensor H_Theta tensor H_t.

It is not a statement about H8.  Neither H8 nor an operator unitarily
equivalent to H8 occurs in the definition or proof of the full admissibility
projector.  Consequently

    [P_adm,H8]=0

is not a well-typed source theorem before an intertwiner W is given.

The later OS/CAR theorem cannot repair this gap.  It assumes clustering on
P_adm and then reconstructs a semigroup exp(-t H_adm) with vacuum Omega.  It
does not identify H_adm with H8, and clustering is an input of that theorem.
Using it to prove H8 clustering would therefore be circular.


4. STRONGEST CORRECT CONDITIONAL SEMIGROUP STATEMENT

Let W embed the stipulated H8 E8 module into the Hilbert space on which the
fixed original P_adm acts.  Suppose the following three image and reduction
identities are proved from the source:

    P_prim W Omega  = W Omega,
    P_Theta W Omega = W Omega,
    P_adm reduces W H8 W^*.

The previous color-center theorem already supplies

    P_sing W Omega = W Omega

and removes the second zero state.  On Ran(P_adm), the restricted spectrum is
then a subset of the spectrum on Ran(P_sing).  Hence its kernel is the single
line C W Omega and its gap g obeys

    g >= 3/2  for alpha=1/4,
    g >= 2    for alpha=3/4.

For bounded admissible operators A and B preserving this reduced space, the
connected Euclidean-time matrix element satisfies

    | <Omega,A exp(-tH8) B Omega>
       - <Omega,A Omega><Omega,B Omega> |

      <= exp(-g t)
         || A^* Omega ||
         || (1-|Omega><Omega|) B Omega ||.

This is exponential transfer-time clustering for that reduced H8 semigroup.
It is not spatial clustering, a full OS reconstruction, or an identification
of H8 with the source's H_adm.  If P_prim removes W Omega, if Q_Theta has
eigenvalue -1 there, or if P_adm does not reduce W H8 W^*, the statement does
not apply.


5. THE ONE MISSING TEST

The smallest decisive continuation is not another zero-state census.  It is
one typed diagram:

    H_E8 --W--> H_Seam tensor H_c tensor H_Theta tensor H_t

with the three displayed identities for Omega and H8.  A global unitary is
stronger than necessary.  An isometry on the cyclic H8 subspace generated by
the intended admissible algebra suffices.  The source must fix W; inserting a
new identification would merely choose the desired answer.

Until that diagram is supplied, the exact conclusion remains:

    ker(H8 | Ran P_sing) = C Omega,

while retention by P_prim and P_Theta, and therefore clustering on the actual
full P_adm space, is not proved and is not explicitly ruled out.
