CLUSTERING ON THE ORIGINAL COLOR-CENTER ADMISSIBLE SECTOR
28 October 2026

VERDICT

The old second-ground-state obstruction is real on the stipulated full E8
lattice field module, but it does not survive the color-center selector already
present in the original full admissibility complex.

For both quarter marks alpha=1/4 and alpha=3/4, the unchanged affine time

    H8 = 8 K_alpha,
    K_alpha = L0 + delta_alpha.H0,
    delta_(alpha,j) = (j+alpha)/8 - 1/2,

has exactly the two lattice ground charges 0 and

    p_* = (1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2).

The fixed rational Weyl dictionary sends p_* to e1+e4 in the actual first-five
carrier slots.  With the original 3+2 mark this root lies in (3,2)_(1/6), has
color triality one, and therefore has Z_c eigenvalue omega.  Consequently

    P_sing p_* = (1+omega+omega^2)p_*/3 = 0,
    P_sing Omega = Omega.

No Hamiltonian correction is used.  Since H8 and Z_c are commuting Cartan
actions, the center-neutral subspace is H8 invariant.  On that subspace the
H8 kernel is the single vacuum line.  The exact first positive gaps are 3/2
for alpha=1/4 and 2 for alpha=3/4.

This removes the earlier ground-doublet argument as an independent obstruction
to clustering of the later center-neutral observable restriction.  It does not
remove the obstruction on the full graded E8 field net, and it does not by
itself identify H8 with the physical OS transfer Hamiltonian.


1. WHAT WAS ALREADY KNOWN

The quotient-locality contract proved by coordinatewise convexity that the
stipulated E8 extension with unchanged K_alpha has precisely two ground
charges, 0 and p_*.  It also checked the fixed rational equality

    M p_* = e1+e4

and the vanishing of the transported source U(1) charge on that root.  The
contract explicitly scoped its exclusion to the unchanged charged source and
left a selected local observable completion open.

The later carrier-action contract then supplied the missing charge type:

    E_(e1+e4) = epsilon_1 epsilon_4,
    [Y,E_(e1+e4)] = E_(e1+e4)/6,

and the six roots e_i+e_a, i=1,2,3 and a=4,5, form (3,2)_(1/6).  It already
warned that neutrality under the transported source U(1) is not P2
hypercharge or color neutrality.

The original full admissibility complex, which was not applied in the old
full-field ground comparison, defines

    P_sing = (1+Z_c+Z_c^2)/3

after the carrier theorem has supplied the color factor.  The source calls
this the color-center singlet projector.  It is a center-neutrality projector,
not the Haar projector onto a full connected SU(3) singlet.


2. THE SMALLEST DECISIVE ACTION TEST

Use the actual ordered carrier frame E=C^3 direct-sum C^2.  The scaled
hypercharge mark is

    X=6Y=(-2,-2,-2,3,3,0,0,0).

For q=M p_*=e1+e4,

    X.q = 1,              Y(q)=1/6,
    Q_source.q = 0.

The SU(3) center exponent in this frame is the coweight pairing

    t(q) = q1+q2-2q3 mod 3.

It equals one on e1+e4.  Thus Z_c acts by omega.  Reversing the convention for
the fundamental would replace omega by omega^2 and leaves the projector result
unchanged.  The vacuum has t=0 and survives.

On the two-dimensional full ground space the exact actions are therefore

                  Omega       |p_*>
    H8              0            0
    Z_c             1          omega
    P_sing          1            0.

This also explains the observable statement without treating P_sing as a
global compression.  For a center-neutral field A, so that [A,Z_c]=0,

    <p_*|A|Omega>
      = <p_*|Z_c^{-1} A Z_c|Omega>
      = omega^{-1}<p_*|A|Omega>
      = 0.

The same vanishing follows directly from the original local-net definition for
any A for which A and A* preserve the center-neutral subspace, because A Omega
then lies in a subspace orthogonal to p_*.  The colored root current may remain
an intersector field in the graded field net.  It is not an operator of the
restricted center-neutral local observable net because it sends the vacuum out
of the admissible subspace.


3. COMPLETE ZERO AND GAP CLASSIFICATION

For a lattice charge p and oscillator level N,

    K_alpha(p,N) = N + ||p||^2/2 + delta_alpha.p.

Because |delta_(alpha,j)|<1/2, every nonzero integer coordinate contributes

    n^2/2 + delta_j n = n(n/2+delta_j) > 0.

Hence the integer D8 coset has the unique minimum p=0.  On the half-integral
coset, strict coordinatewise convexity uniquely chooses +1/2 on the first four
coordinates and -1/2 on the last four.  This is p_*, it satisfies the D8 parity
condition, and its energy is zero.  Positive oscillator level adds at least one
to K_alpha.  These facts prove that 0 and p_* are all lattice ground charges;
the root census is not being used as a cutoff proof of that statement.

For the first positive center-neutral energy there is a short global bound.
For both quarters,

    ||delta_alpha||^2 = 85/128 < 49/64,
    ||delta_alpha|| < 7/8.

The E8 lattice is even.  Every nonzero vector that is not one of its 240 roots
has norm at least two.  Cauchy-Schwarz therefore gives

    K_alpha(p,0)
      >= ||p||^2/2 - ||delta_alpha|| ||p||
      > 2 - 7/4 = 1/4

for every non-root.  The exact census of the 112 integer and 128 half-integral
roots is therefore complete for the gap at or below 1/4.  Applying the same
fixed M and the center coweight to all 240 roots gives

    alpha       dim ker K       dim ker K on P_sing       gap K       gap H8
    1/4              2                    1                 3/16         3/2
    3/4              2                    1                 1/4          2.

The checker records the first minimizing charge and its transported carrier
weight in each case.  It also checks all 240 center characters exactly over
the rationals.


4. WHAT THIS CHANGES

The previous statement

    "the same H8 gives a second ground state in the E8 extension"

remains correct for the full lattice field module.  The stronger inference

    "therefore the physical admissible observable sector has a degenerate
     vacuum and cannot cluster"

does not follow.  On the branch where the original P_sing is operative, the
only extra zero state has nontrivial color triality and is removed.  The
unchanged H8 already has a one-dimensional center-neutral kernel and a positive
spectral gap.

This is a real scope correction to the old obstruction, not a new source or a
fitted energy offset.  Other parts of the old unchanged-source verdict remain
separate, including the missing raw P1 field map, the actual quarter-source
operator realization, and the half-Cartan/current locality questions.


5. PRECISE LIMITS

The theorem proved here is:

    On the stipulated E8 lattice extension, with its fixed M dictionary and
    the original later color-center action, ker(H8|Ran P_sing)=C Omega and the
    displayed positive gaps hold.

Four additional identifications are still needed before calling this a
physical clustering theorem.

1. P_sing is not part of primitive P1.  The original source introduces it only
   after the carrier theorem has identified the color factor.  The result is
   therefore about the closed admissible branch, not the primitive source by
   itself.

2. Intersecting with P_prim and P_Theta cannot recreate p_*, but the present
   calculation does not independently prove that both projectors retain the
   vacuum line.  Unique physical vacuum means one-dimensional only after that
   retention is checked.

3. The original physical local net is defined by restriction to operators for
   which both A and A* preserve H_adm.  This calculation identifies the center
   selection on the ground states.  It does not construct the full invariant
   local net.

4. Spectral uniqueness and a gap imply the usual transfer clustering only
   after H8 is proved to be the reconstructed physical OS transfer generator
   on that same net.  No such identification, no full SU(3)-invariant physical
   Hamiltonian, no graded-field-net clustering, and no four-dimensional mass
   gap is asserted here.
