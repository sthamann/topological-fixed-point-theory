VERDICT

ACCEPTED.  The finite evaluation line has the stated central phase, and the
source-selected spin normalization removes the moving two-torsion factor over
the full Jacobian family.  The construction is functorial under c and Q.  It
still does not prove that the physical boundary or conformal-block functor

precisely.

1. FIXED-FIBER CHARACTER

For D_q=q_0+q_1+q_2 the permutation-free norm line is

  N_q = tensor_a S_0|q_a.

The selected beta phase is +i at q_0.  If gamma is the normalized order-three
lift of c, gamma beta gamma^(-1)=zeta beta implies zeta^3=1, while beta^2=-I
implies zeta^2=1.  Thus zeta=1 and beta has phase +i on all three q fibers.
Consequently

  beta|N_q = i^3 = -i,
  beta|N_q^(-1) = +i.

The canonical even action on H^0(O(2Theta)) is +I.  Hence the fixed-fiber
candidate has beta=alpha^2=+i I and alpha^4=-I.  These square relations do not
depend on the remaining sign alpha -> -alpha.  The cubic cycle has positive
permutation sign, and gamma^3=1 makes its normalized action on N_q trivial.

At p_0 the local derivative has phase -i.  The same argument gives beta=-i
on every p fiber, beta=+i on N_p, and beta=-i on N_p^(-1).  The relation
sQs=Q^(-1) therefore exchanges conjugate q and p blocks.  This is compatible
with a paired eight-dimensional system and does not identify that pair with
one native four-dimensional module.

2. SPIN-RELATIVE POINCARE NORMALIZATION

Let P be any Poincare family on C x A and put

  R=<P,S_0>,
  P_spin=P tensor pr_A^*(R^(-1)).

Because deg S_0=1, Deligne-pairing bilinearity gives a canonical
trivialization

  <P_spin,S_0>=O_A.

This condition removes the usual freedom to tensor a universal family by a
line from A.  Indeed, such a tensor changes the pairing by that base line to
the first power.  It therefore defines the Poincare family relative to the
already selected spin line, without choosing a new point or a flat
character.

The divisor identity used next is exact.  From

  S_0=O(D_q-K_C),  S_0^2=K_C

one obtains O(D_q)=S_0^3.  Hence

  Norm_Dq(P_spin)
    =<P_spin,O(D_q)>
    =<P_spin,S_0>^3
    =O_A.

An isomorphism O(D_q)->S_0^3 is unique only up to a nonzero scalar.  That
scalar acts in this pairing with exponent deg(P_spin|C)=0, so the displayed
trivialization is independent of it.  The same proof applies to D_p because
O(D_p)=O(D_q).

At the origin this normalization does not change N_q(S_0) or its beta phase.
Over the family it gives

  mathcal N_q(S_0 tensor P_spin)=N_q(S_0) tensor O_A.

It also leaves the ordinary theta determinant unchanged: a line pulled back
from A enters determinant of cohomology with exponent
chi(S_0 tensor L)=0.  Thus the norm changes with the required divisor length
three while the theta line has exponent zero.

3. FUNCTORIALITY AND THE EARLIER P_DELTA TERM

For a Poincare bundle normalized at q_0 one instead finds

  Norm_Dq(P)=P_delta_q,
  delta_q=D_q-3q_0=q_1-q_2.

Delta_q is a nonzero element of V_q and c moves it through that plane.  The
resulting failure of c invariance is real for the q_0-normalized family, but
it is not intrinsic to the source family.  It disappears under the
spin-relative normalization above.

The c and Q actions preserve the classes of S_0 and D_q.  Pulling P_spin back
by either action gives another universal family with the same trivialized
pairing with S_0.  Uniqueness of this normalized universal family supplies
the comparison isomorphism.  Any scalar ambiguity in the chosen lift of S_0
acts on the pairing with exponent deg(P_spin|C)=0 and disappears.  An
automorphism of P_spin preserving its pairing trivialization is itself the
identity because deg S_0=1.  These facts make the comparisons satisfy the
group cocycles, rather than giving only equality of Picard classes.

This also sharpens the rigidification distinction.  A scalar change of a
q_0 rigidification cannot alter P_delta_q.  The successful construction is a
different canonical normalization supplied by S_0 itself.  It is not a
scalar repair of the point-normalized bundle.

4. DETERMINANT EXPONENT AND SCIENTIFIC SCOPE

For the evaluation exact sequence, imposing vanishing first changes the
determinant line D to D tensor N_q^(-1).  Taking the inverse square that
produces the ordinary two-theta line gives

  (D tensor N_q^(-1))^(-2)=D^(-2) tensor N_q^2.

Its beta phase is (-i)^2=-1, not +i.  The full-family one-copy candidate

  H^0(O(2Theta)) tensor N_q(S_0)^(-1)

has the desired +i phase, but determinant exactness alone does not select
that one-copy insertion.  The report correctly leaves as open the original
field, boundary, sewing, Calderon, or conformal-block rule that would attach
this inverse norm line to the four-dimensional theta module.
