GRAPH CUT REFLECTION TEST FOR THE ACTUAL EQUATOR
================================================

26 October 2026. Independent analytic review. No Atlas, original source, or
canonical contract was changed.

Question
========

Let

  E=C/(Z+iZ),
  Omega=p^(-1)({|x|<1}),

for the fixed elliptic double E:y^2=x^4-1 with its Q invariant NS spin line
alpha. The boundary of Omega is the full equatorial graph

  p^(-1)({|x|=1})=Fix(S) union Fix(hS),

where, in z=t+i s coordinates,

  S z=bar z+i/2,
  hS z=-bar z+1/2.

The open set Omega consists of two opposite checkerboard faces. Does either
one of the fixed global reflections S or hS make the already constructed
theta_3 free Gaussian functional reflection positive on the CAR algebra of
their union?

Verdict
=======

No. Each reflection is positive on one of its two annular Schottky halves,
exactly as proved in ELLIPTIC-GREEN-RP.txt. The checkerboard union Omega is
not contained in either half. It contains one face in each of the two
opposite annuli.

This is not only a mismatch of pictures. In the full gauge invariant CAR
algebra of the disconnected union there is an explicit neutral bilocal Wick
word whose reflected norm is strictly negative. The witness uses the actual
theta_3 Szego Green kernel and the already fixed charged Clifford minus. It
has compact support in the interiors of the two checkerboard faces, so no
choice of graph vertex condition, boundary trace measure, or branch point
domain enters the calculation.

The conclusion applies to the named free Gaussian functional and either
single global reflection S or hS. It does not exclude a separately supplied
piecewise reflection with compatible vertex data, nor a restriction to the
smaller algebra generated by operators that are even on each face
separately. Neither extra structure is supplied by the current equatorial
pullback.

1. The checkerboard is not a Schottky half
==========================================

Use rho=hS and recenter the first coordinate by

  tau=t-1/4.

Then

  rho(tau,s)=(-tau,s),

and its fixed circles are tau=0 and tau=1/2 modulo one. Its two Schottky
halves are the annuli

  A_+={0<tau<1/2},
  A_-={-1/2<tau<0}.

The four edges of Fix(S) cut each annulus into two rectangular disks. Since
rho covers x -> 1/bar x, it sends Omega to p^(-1)({|x|>1}). The open disk
{|x|<1} contains no branch value, so its inverse image has two components
exchanged by the deck map h. In the centered coordinates h sends tau to
-tau and hence exchanges A_+ with A_-. Therefore the two components of
Omega are opposite checkerboard disks, one contained in A_+ and one
contained in A_-. In particular Omega is not a subset of A_+ or A_-.

The same statement for S follows by the quarter rotation Q, which exchanges
the vertical and horizontal presentations. This is the exact geometric
difference from the previous annulus theorem: that theorem chose one whole
component of E\Fix(rho), whereas the actual graph side takes one disk from
each component.

2. The actual theta_3 kernel and its two signs
===============================================

On the translated rho chart the NS Green operator is the same translation
invariant kernel as in ELLIPTIC-GREEN-RP.txt. With

  B=-i partial_s,
  spec(B)=2 pi (Z+1/2),

write S_E(a) for the Green kernel of partial_tau+B. It is exactly

  S_E(w)=i/(2 pi)
    theta_3(w|i) theta_1'(0|i)
    /[theta_3(0|i) theta_1(w|i)],

with the already proved Fourier branches

  S_E(a)=exp(-aB)(1+exp(-B))^(-1),       0<a<1,
  S_E(-a)=-exp((a-1)B)(1+exp(-B))^(-1), 0<a<1.

For 0<tau,u<1/2, the fixed charged reflection convention gives

  K_bar(tau,u)=-S_E(-tau-u)=X_tau^* X_u,
  K_psi(tau,u)= S_E( tau+u)=Y_tau^* Y_u,

where

  X_tau=exp(tau B)(1+exp(B))^(-1/2),
  Y_tau=exp(-tau B)(1+exp(-B))^(-1/2).

This is the previous positive annulus factorization. On the opposite half,
put tau=-r and u=-v with 0<r,v<1/2. The same global reflection and the same
Clifford sign give

  K_psi(-r,-v)=S_E(-r-v)=-X_r^* X_v.                  (1)

Thus the reflected one particle form of this charge block is strictly
negative on suitable nonzero test functions in A_-. A constant Real spin
linearization phase cannot alter the relative sign between the two halves.
For a neutral word its two charge phases cancel in any case.

This use of S_E is typed narrowly. GLOBAL-SPIN-KERNEL.txt proves the full
holomorphic alpha Szego kernel and its rank two pushforward, but does not by
itself define a positive equal time CAR covariance. The present test uses
the declared free Gaussian Green functional of ELLIPTIC-GREEN-RP.txt, where
the theta kernel, CAR covariance, word reversal, and Clifford sign were
already fixed together.

3. A compactly supported neutral negative witness
=================================================

Choose real smooth product bumps f_+ and g_- with

  supp(f_+) compactly contained in Omega intersect A_+,
  supp(g_-) compactly contained in Omega intersect A_-.

They may be chosen so that the factorized vectors below are nonzero. For
example, take nonnegative time bumps and any nonzero spatial bump supported
inside the corresponding checker face. At least one NS Fourier coefficient
of the spatial bump is nonzero, and its time Laplace integral is then
nonzero. Equations above give

  q_bar(f_+)=|| integral X_tau f_+(tau) d tau ||^2 >0,

  q_psi(g_-)=-|| integral X_r g_-(-r) d r ||^2 <0.    (2)

Now take the normal ordered bilocal word

  F=:barpsi(f_+) psi(g_-): .                           (3)

It has total U(1) charge zero and is even. Its support is contained in the
two component open set Omega. Gaussian Wick expansion with the same order
reversing reflection used in the annulus theorem gives the one by one
exterior Gram factors

  omega(Theta_rho(F) F)=q_bar(f_+) q_psi(g_-)<0.       (4)

There is no unlisted sign in (4): with both supports in A_+, the identical
word convention is precisely the product of the two positive charged Gram
forms used in the proof of all word positivity. Moving only the psi test
function to A_- changes its factor by the exact NS sign (1).

Equation (4) is the smallest decisive test in the full gauge invariant CAR
algebra of the disconnected checkerboard union. It is stronger than a
charged one field counterexample and does not appeal to a freely chosen
projector or covariance.

4. The second original reflection and the exact scope
=====================================================

The quarter map Q exchanges S and hS and preserves the unordered alpha
kernel. Rotating f_+,g_- by Q therefore gives the same witness for S. Hence
neither fixed original reflection supplies reflection positivity on all of
Omega, although each supplies it on its own annular Schottky half.

The obstruction is already interior. The induced measure on the graph,
closed trace spaces, and self adjoint branch vertex conditions cannot repair
(4), because the test functions avoid the graph and all four vertices.

There are two precise ways to evade the witness, neither presently follows
from the source:

  1. Use a piecewise reflection that acts by different Real structures on
     the two checker faces and prove its compatibility at the graph edges
     and branch vertices.

  2. Replace the global even CAR algebra of Omega by the smaller algebra
     generated by operators that are even on each connected face. The word
     (3), which has one odd factor on each face but is globally even, is then
     removed by an additional facewise parity superselection rule.

The result is therefore a scoped negative answer to the current question:
the already proved annulus RP does not descend unchanged to the union of the
two actual inside checker faces under one fixed S or hS reflection. It is not
a no go theorem for another graph quantization or for the primitive P1
source.
