2. WHAT THE PREMISE MEANS

For an extension algebra B with support H, v_H is the restriction of the
extended vacuum character to U.  Requiring its span to be closed under the
torus S move says that the extension has no other chiral sector into which its
vacuum character can transform.  In this pointed category this is precisely
the Lagrangian, or holomorphic, condition.

Therefore "a single chiral vacuum line" is substantial global input.  It is not
ordinary sewing consistency of an arbitrary rational theory.  Non-holomorphic
extensions have consistent multi-component modular representations, and the
vacuum component is allowed to mix with the others.

The criterion is also not derivable from a scalar doubled partition function.
The diagonal matrix M=I obeys

  S^* M S=M,  T^* M T=M

for the whole 16-sector theory.  Its rank is 16, so this invariance supplies no
rank-one chiral vacuum line.  Likewise tau=i is a fixed point of S; equality of
one specialized numerical value at tau=i does not establish (1) as a vector
identity.

Charge resolution is load-bearing for the full support and the marked
embedding.  The leading vacuum coefficient of an unrefined S-transformed
character still gives the necessary equation |H|/4=1, because every nonvacuum
U module has positive lowest weight.  Thus it detects the required cardinality
four inside the already classified four-extension list.  Coincident unrefined
q-series can nevertheless hide the rest of the support.  The safe object for
the complete statement is the labelled modular tensor-category basis e_x, or
Jacobi characters with independent charge variables.  Scalar specialized
characters cannot distinguish Hplus from Hminus and need not retain the full
16-dimensional action faithfully.


3. COMPARISON WITH THE MARKED-GENERATOR THEOREM

The two hypotheses are not logically equivalent on the classified set:

  Hom_U(M_(1,1),W) nonzero       holds only for Hplus;
  one chiral S-stable vacuum line holds for Hplus and Hminus.

Thus modular sewing is strictly less marked.  It is sufficient for the
unlabelled VOA isomorphism class V_E8, because Hplus and Hminus differ by the A3
diagram reversal.  It is insufficient for the literal fixed-U extension and
for every later dictionary that distinguishes (1,1) from (1,-1).

Conversely, the new condition is exactly holomorphy within this classified
extension class, whereas the local marked-generator theorem starts only from
occurrence of one charged module and proves saturation.  Calling the modular
premise weaker would therefore be wrong.  It is less marked as a test for the
unlabelled endpoint, while making a global statement about all
superselection sectors.

The local-extension premise remains.  The classification used here begins
with a connected local unitary extension sharing U's conformal vector.  A
formal vector satisfying Sv=v does not by itself construct the extension,
prove locality, or map the primitive P1 source into it.

5. SMALLEST DECISIVE ORIGINAL-SOURCE TEST

Before naming an extension, construct from the primitive source one of the
following equivalent charge-resolved data:

  (a) its torus chiral conformal-block vector
      Z_ch=sum_(x in A) n_x chi_x(tau,z),
      with n_0=1 and n_x nonnegative integers, and prove the source sewing map
      sends Z_ch to the same one-dimensional line under S;

  (b) the boundary net's mu-index and prove it equals one;

  (c) the torus conformal-block, equivalently topological state, space of the
      associated modular theory and prove it has dimension one, without using
      an E8 K matrix or E8 lattice already chosen downstream.

Item (c) never means the ordinary vacuum-energy eigenspace of a chiral CFT.
D8 also has a unique ordinary vacuum vector; that fact does not make its
modular category holomorphic.

Within the established extension classification, any one of these tests gives
H=Hperp and therefore the abstract E8 extension.  To recover the original
marked embedding as well, one further charge-resolved datum must distinguish

  n_(1,1)=1, n_(1,3)=0

from the reversed choice.  That last bit is exactly the surviving orientation
or marking information.  It is smaller than constructing the local (16,4)
generator explicitly, but it cannot be obtained from an unrefined character or
from modular invariance alone.

CHIRAL-SEWING-SELECTION.txt proof are exact in their stated class.  They give a
