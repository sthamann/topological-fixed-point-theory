SECTOR TRANSPORT, LOCALITY AND THE CLAUDE PROPOSAL
27 October 2026

VERDICT

The original winding does not create a Ramond spinor from an NS state.
This follows from its exact original action, not from a numerical analogy.
The proposed square-root shortcut and membership in the unchanged NS CAR
algebra fail. Local spinor intertwiners in an appropriate extension remain
a viable conditional route. No conclusion against all Ramond constructions
or against categorical reconstruction is drawn.

1. THE ACTUAL FOUR SECTORS

Use the retained D8 modules in the order (0,v,s,c):

  H_NS=H_0+H_v, H_R=H_s+H_c,
  H_E8=H_0+H_s, H_E8,twisted=H_v+H_c.

These are two DIFFERENT decompositions of the same supplied parent space.
The exact original operator W_ext=E_e0 Rhat has sector action

  W_ext:(0 v)(s c).

It maps the E8 module to its g-twisted module while preserving NS and R
separately. If P_R projects onto H_s+H_c, then [P_R,W_ext]=0. Since W_ext
is unitary and P_R reduces it, spectral calculus gives

  [P_R,f(W_ext)]=0

for every bounded Borel function f, including every chosen spectral branch
of a square root. Thus P_R f(W_ext) Omega_NS=0. A non-functional square root
would be an additional operator whose source origin and locality are not
provided by W_ext.

The global charge shift X=E_s has a different sector action (0 s)(v c).
It is already available on the four-sector parent; its global existence
is not the missing local source theorem. The full affine W_ext includes
charge translations and cocycles, so its four-sector permutation must not
be substituted for its entire infinite-dimensional operator.

2. CORRECTION OF OUR OWN WORDING

The previous WEAKEST-DIRECT-E8-SOURCE-ROUTE.txt contained the phrase
'its half integral powers' after discussing W_ext. That phrasing invited an
incorrect interpretation. The proved identity is

  W_ext Y(e^beta,z) W_ext^-1
   = lambda(beta) C(e0,R beta) z^(-(R beta).e0) Y(e^(R beta),z).

It is the exponent of the FIELD COORDINATE z that is half integral for
spinor roots. W_ext itself sends 0+s to v+c. There is no statement here
about W_ext^(1/2) generating s from 0. The original SAME-SOURCE proof
states both facts correctly; the later summary is repaired in place and
its old version is retained with this audit.

3. THE CORRECT LOCAL OBJECT

Take v=e1 and s=(1/2,...,1/2). Then exp(2 pi i v.s)=-1, whereas s has
integral pairing with all D8 roots. Thus the s-charge field is local
relative to the D8 current theory and gives the bosonic E8 extension
V_D8+H_s. It is not mutually local with the vector fermions of the NS CAR
theory. Combining v and s in one ordinary local or graded-local algebra
is not justified by this construction.

In the unchanged NS representation the Spin(10) kernel fixes the vector
fermions and vacuum, hence its whole generated algebra. It cannot produce
an operator transforming in the spinor M_h on that same representation.
Bounded affiliation with the same local von Neumann algebra is membership
and does not evade this obstruction. Unbounded affiliated operators also
cannot manufacture a disjoint target sector from that unchanged algebra.

A well-typed target is a localized charged intertwining field relative to
the even D8 current net on an appropriate sector Hilbert space, or a field
of the local E8 extension. Smeared vertex fields generally are closable and
energy bounded rather than bounded. Energy bounds, domains, strong locality
and the original source identification must be stated separately. A bounded
global defect implementer is a different object from a smeared primary field.

Once a full marked s-sector is realized, its ground space already contains
(16,4)+(bar16,bar4); no extra arbitrary choice of a single vector is needed
for the extension theorem. Likewise the existing common Ramond lift sign is
intertwiner-basis gauge, not a reopened physical parameter. Extra original
markings matter only when actually fixed by the source contract.

4. FURTHER CORRECTIONS TO THE FIRST EXTERNAL REPORT

For {psi0^a,psi0^b}=delta_ab the chirality involution uses gamma_a=sqrt(2)psi0^a:

  Gamma=i^8 gamma_1...gamma_16=2^8 psi0^1...psi0^16.

Its square is one; the unscaled product has square 2^-16. The full complex
zero-mode module has dimension256, each half-spin module128, and each
marked ground component64. A fusion-intertwiner space of dimension1 is a
space of operator families, not any of those ground spaces.

A single chiral theory of16 real fermions still has all120 D8 currents.
Spacetime chirality and the internal D8 Lie algebra are different labels.
Complete Dirac vector Ward charges must be compared by an explicit map,
not equated with the internal chiral currents by name.

Unit spectral flow phi:0->2pi returns to the same spin boundary condition
and changes occupation. Half flow changes the NS/R boundary condition.
The free orientable-double deck of a Moebius strip has no fixed point;
a Ramond puncture is not automatically an existing deck fixed point.

Finite determinant/theta spaces are not identical to the full field module.
Their dimensions alone do not exclude a reconstruction functor. The
existing lattice/VOA construction is one conditional such reconstruction.
These corrections reject overclaims in Claude's first proposal and leave
the properly typed alternative routes open.

5. THE FIRST TEST MUST TEST THE RIGHT TRANSITION

For a claimed spinor field X_s the minimal sector test is

  P_s X_s P_0 != 0,

with its D8 charge determined on the retained current algebra. In contrast,

  P_s W_ext P_0 = 0.

Equation32 of NEUTRAL-NET-AND-DEFECT localizes the affine winding action;
it is not, by itself, a test that a proposed operator creates the s-sector.
The two localization questions are distinct. The existing global E_s passes
the sector test but still needs the genuine local field/source construction.
This prevents spending a new localization proof on the wrong charged object.

Claude accepted the six principal corrections in its second answer. Its
last section nevertheless conflates these targets again and proposes an
unsupported exclusive A3-reading fork. The second independent review records
those residual errors. Neither the full OS reconstruction nor a scalar
source determinant determines a rank16 block-pairing matrix without the
missing block dictionary. The paired M=I example remains only a logical
control. No blanket theorem against a future P1 map is asserted.

6. WHAT IS REUSED AND WHAT THE SOURCE STILL MUST SUPPLY

The same-conformal extension theorem, four-sector AIA, complete affine
winding, lift-sign gauge and theta-to-lattice-to-VOA pair are already
available with their stated assumptions. None is reclassified as missing.
The original physical P1 source must supply either a charged local sector
map in that class, or an equivalent independently justified global
extension-selection property. Selection is not proved merely by giving a
standard extension model or by localizing an operator in the wrong algebra.
The alternative chiral-sewing test is developed in the adjacent report.
