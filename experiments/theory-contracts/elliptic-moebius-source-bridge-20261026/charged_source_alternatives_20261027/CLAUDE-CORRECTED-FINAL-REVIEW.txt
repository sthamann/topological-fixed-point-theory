VERDICT

The main corrections are accepted.  The report now correctly separates
W_ext from Ramond transport, retracts the functional square root, distinguishes
D8-current locality from NS-CAR membership, fixes Clifford normalization and
fusion-space dimension, and withdraws the dimension-only exclusion of a
categorical reconstruction functor.

Five material residual corrections remain.

1. Equation (32) is not the local M_h test

Section D again calls equation (32) the local charged-generator route and says
its global version is W_ext.  That equation tests localization of the affine
winding W_ext, whose sector action is (0 v)(s c).  It preserves NS and R and
does not supply the s charge.  A local M_h route instead needs the distinct
s-sector intertwiner of type (0 s)(v c), represented globally by E_s, or the
corresponding vertex field relative to the D8 current net.  Localizing W_ext
would solve an important source-defect problem, but would not by itself trigger
the marked-generator theorem.  The target should be an energy-bounded charged
field, or a bounded implementer in a larger extension algebra, not a "bounded
affiliated generator" in the unchanged NS algebra.

2. A3 does not decide an exclusive two-route fork

The scalar-versus-spinorial reading of D_Gamma is relevant source typing, but
neither reading selects exactly one of the two proposed routes.  Reading 2 does
not construct the sixteen-fermion s module or its localized field.  Reading 1
does not prove a genus-one chiral block functor and does not exclude some other
local charged-source construction.  It is therefore not a minimal decider
between "local field" and "chiral sewing"; each route still needs its own
explicit source map.

3. No generic OS rank-16 conclusion

OS or Schlingemann reconstruction does not, without an additional conformal
block dictionary, produce the 16 by 16 pairing matrix M=I.  M=I is a precise
logical countercontrol showing that scalar modular invariance need not be a
single chiral line.  It is not the generic output of the original P1 source.
Consequently failure to obtain a rank-one block does not force the local-field
route, and no rank-16 alternative may be inferred before the block map exists.

Moreover an S-stable rank-one vacuum vector selects Hplus or Hminus, not Hplus
alone.  The original charge marking is required to choose the plus embedding.

4. Solved gauge questions must remain closed

Once the full selected marked s module is realized, its ground space already
contains both 64-dimensional U modules, including M_h.  No arbitrary vector or
component selection is required.  The common Ramond lift sign has already been
shown to be intertwiner-basis gauge.  An Arf convention or frozen mark is an
extra physical datum only if the actual source contract fixes it; it is not a
generic prerequisite for the extension theorem.

5. Two remaining type/count corrections

W_aux is a four-dimensional theta/Heisenberg module tensored with a
one-dimensional determinant line; it is not itself "a determinant/Heisenberg
line".  Its direct identification with M_h fails, while a reconstruction
functor remains open as Claude now correctly states.

Sixteen chiral Majorana fermions generate the full weight-one D8 current
algebra of dimension 120: 112 root currents plus eight Cartan currents.  Saying
only "all 112 roots" omits the Cartan part, although it does correctly refute
the earlier claim that spacetime chirality discards half the internal algebra.

FINAL STATUS

Accepted after these residual corrections: the representation-level Ramond
identification, the formal four-sector AIA, and the exact modular S-vector
criterion.  Still open and logically independent are the localized marked
s-sector source map and the primitive charge-resolved chiral sewing map.  The
source evidence currently chooses neither route, and the two should not be
forced into a false dichotomy.
