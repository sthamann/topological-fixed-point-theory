CHIRAL SEWING AS AN ALTERNATIVE E8 SELECTION TEST
27 October 2026

DECISION

Within the already classified connected local unitary same-conformal
extensions of U=V_D5 tensor V_A3, preservation of the single chiral vacuum
block by exchange of the two torus cycles selects precisely the two marked
E8 extensions. The original h=(1,1) marking selects the plus embedding once
that marked field identification is supplied.

This is a standard finite Fourier consequence of the existing extension
classification. It supplies an alternative source test; it does not derive
the cycle-exchange premise from P1 and is not claimed as a new general CFT
classification theorem. A scalar paired partition function, a pure CAR
state, or a clock action at the fixed square torus does not provide that
premise.

1. THE EXISTING EXTENSION CLASS

Retain the exact class of SIMPLIFICATION.txt:

  U=V_D5 tensor V_A3,
  A=U-lattice discriminant group=C4 x C4,
  q(a,b)=(5a^2+3b^2)/8 mod1,
  b((a,b),(c,d))=(5ac+3bd)/4 mod1.

W is a connected local unitary VOA extension with the SAME conformal vector.
The old theorem derives finite support and multiplicity one. Its support
H is one of exactly four isotropic subgroups:

  H0={(0,0)},
  H2={(0,0),(2,2)},
  Hplus={(a,a):a in C4},
  Hminus={(a,-a):a in C4}.

They realize U,D8,E8plus,E8minus. No assumption of a pre-existing marked
spinor generator is made in this four-way class.

2. CYCLE EXCHANGE ON THE ACTUAL DISCRIMINANT DATA

Use the full labelled character/conformal-block space C[A], or retain Cartan
chemical potentials if scalar characters are used. This avoids confusing
conjugate or otherwise equal unrefined characters.

For Cartan-refined characters, transform the background by z -> z/tau and
retain the common Jacobi anomaly factor before extracting the finite matrix.
The lattice modular S action on the labelled blocks is the finite Fourier transform

  S_(x,y)=|A|^(-1/2) exp(-2 pi i b(x,y))
         =(1/4) exp(-2 pi i b(x,y)).

Let v_H=sum_(h in H) e_h be the branching vector of the extension vacuum.
Character orthogonality gives the exact identity

  S v_H=(|H|/4) v_(H-perp).                              (1)

Indeed its x coordinate is (1/4) sum_(h in H) exp(-2 pi i b(x,h)).
This sum is |H|/4 for x in H-perp and zero otherwise.

Require that the single chiral vacuum line is preserved: S v_H=lambda v_H.
Unitarity gives |lambda|=1, while its vacuum coordinate is lambda=|H|/4>0.
Consequently |H|=4, lambda=1 and H=H-perp. The converse is immediate.

  support      vacuum coefficient after S     single chiral block fixed
  H0                         1/4                         no
  H2                         1/2                         no
  Hplus                      1                           yes
  Hminus                     1                           yes

Thus the cycle-exchange condition is equivalent, within this class, to the
extension being holomorphic. It selects E8 up to the two labelled embeddings.
It is not logically weaker than selecting the relevant extension. Its value
is that global sewing may be the quantity directly specified by a source,
whereas an individual charged generator may not be directly given.

Because all four supports are isotropic, the relative twist matrix T0 has
T0 v_H=v_H. The common gravitational phase exp(-2 pi i c/24), c=8, is retained
in the physical modular T transformation; it is not set to one.

For an unrefined character function the same necessary cardinality follows
from the leading vacuum coefficient after S, since all nonvacuum U modules
have positive lowest weight. Unrefined functions do not distinguish the two
E8 embeddings; the labelled formulation retains that distinction explicitly.

3. WHY A SCALAR TOTAL ANSWER IS NOT ENOUGH

Let chi be the column of the sixteen U characters. The paired scalar

  Z_pair(tau,bar tau)=chi(tau)^* M chi(tau), M=I16

is invariant under S and T, has nonnegative integral coefficients and vacuum
multiplicity M00=1. It pairs all sixteen modules. Its pairing matrix has
rank16, while a factorized single chiral vacuum amplitude would have the
rank-one form v_H v_H^*.

The diagonal unitary full CFT is a standard example of this distinction.
It has both chiralities. It is not a countermodel satisfying all original
P1 requirements and is not a proposed replacement TFPT source. It disproves
only the implication 'scalar modular invariant answer implies a single
holomorphic chiral vacuum block'.

Likewise the equality of a scalar value before and after tau=i -> -1/i is
tautological. It is not an intertwining statement on the full labelled block
space, nor a modular identity for an entire family.

Purity of a positive CAR state concerns a covariance on a one-particle space.
Rank one of the pairing matrix M concerns a genus-one conformal-block space.
No map equating these two notions is furnished by the CAR projector theorem.

4. DECISIVE ORIGINAL SOURCE CHECK

The original spectral-bordism category in tfpt45 specifies tuples
(A_+,H_+,D_+,J,Gamma), reflection positivity, finite relative action and a
nonzero seam index. Its morphisms are unitary intertwiners of those data.
The cited definition supplies no torus conformal-block functor or cycle
exchange operator on the sixteen U modules.

The original primitive completion imports the carrier packet and family
data into a discrete classification. It does not define the chiral vacuum
branching vector or prove equation (1) describes a physical P1 sewing map.
The earlier minimality audit already records the missing algebra-fiber
classification; its absence is reused, not presented as a new discovery.

The exact new source question is therefore:

  Does the actual original P1 sewing functional, after its explicit map to
  the local U theory, define a single chiral vacuum block v_H and transport
  it to itself under the exchange of the two seam cycles?

To answer yes one must supply the block map, retained charge marking and
actual cycle-exchange action. The original source time
H=8[N+p^2/2+delta.p] must not silently be replaced by conformal L0; its
chemical background and time normalization require their actual transport.
A full paired norm, determinant scalar, eta
phase, fixed-torus quarter symmetry, or pure CAR covariance alone does not
answer it. If the answer is yes, the existing finite classification closes
the extension to E8 without a separate search for the (16,4) generator.

5. HISTORICAL ROUTE REUSED

The general holomorphic completion route is already present in the older
papers. The July20 v490 entry explicitly records mu_pre=4 and theta_v=1,
while the condensation mu=4 -> 1 is a cited conditional theorem and the
original seam equivalence remains open. The later celestial/twistor account,
steps8-10, records the same 16 -> 4 -> 1 index chain and distinguishes
lattice witnesses from continuum theorems.

The present calculation is the explicit discriminant-group S-vector form
of that selection problem. It connects the old conditional completion to
the newer exhaustive four-extension classification. It is not a newly
discovered holomorphy route, and no old lattice witness is promoted to the
actual original P1 conformal-block functor. An ordinary unique CFT vacuum
also is not a one-dimensional torus conformal-block space.

6. REPRODUCIBLE CHECK

check_modular_sewing.py computes the exact 16x16 S and relative T matrices,
checks unitarity and S^2=charge conjugation, proves (1) on all four already
classified supports, and checks the rank16 paired control. It uses exact
Gaussian rationals and algebraic phases, with explicit exceptions rather
than Python assert statements.

The finite computation certifies the finite representation identities. The
source statement in section4 is an audit of the cited original passages;
the computation does not prove a physical P1 map.
