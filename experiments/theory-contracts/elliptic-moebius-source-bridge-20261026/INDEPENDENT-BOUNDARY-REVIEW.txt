INDEPENDENT FINAL REVIEW OF THE BOUNDARY AND MODULAR SOURCE REPORTS
==================================================================

26 October 2026. Read only review of TRACE-METRIC-AND-DOMAIN.txt and
MODULAR-ORBIFOLD-SOURCE.txt. No Atlas or original source was changed.

Verdict
=======

Both reports are accepted in their stated scope. The trace report needs one
domain precision in its outer function argument, but its range theorem and
its negative state identification remain correct. The modular report has the
correct marked monodromies, passport, finite versus full modular distinction,
and elliptic Belyi data. It does not identify either construction with the
old quarter operator or state.

1. Trace metric and domain
==========================

The change of variables is correct:

  |dz|=|dx|/(|c||y|),

so including the half density Jacobian once makes T_r unitary from the two
lifted L2 traces to standard L2(Gamma_r,C^2;|dx|). The raw weighted measure
and the Jacobian description are equivalent presentations and must not be
used simultaneously. At a branch mark, |y| is asymptotic to
2|s|^(1/2), giving the stated |s|^(-1/2) measure and |s|^(-1/4) half density
factor. Both are locally L2 compatible. The four point branch matching is
invisible after L2 closure but remains genuine H1 domain data.

On |x|=1 one has

  |g|^2=|tan(theta)|^(1/2).

The weights |g|^2 and |g|^(-2) are A2 weights. Thus the densely defined
similarities g P_H g^(-1) and g^(-1) P_H g extend to bounded idempotents.
The displayed Fourier coefficient is also correct:

  (1/(2 pi)) integral |tan(theta)|^(1/2) exp(-2i theta)dtheta
    =-1/sqrt(2),

with the opposite sign for the inverse weight. Hence the idempotents are not
selfadjoint in the induced standard trace metric and cannot themselves be
positive CAR covariances.

The only required precision concerns the notation

  closure[g H2]=H2.

At the ramified boundary M_g is unbounded on H2, so gH2 is not a naive image
of every H2 vector. Define instead

  D_g={h in H2: g h lies in H2}.

Then

  g D_g is contained in Ran(g P_H g^(-1)) and in H2

on the natural similarity core. For the bounded extension, approximation by
that core gives

  Ran(g P_H g^(-1)) is contained in closure(g D_g) inside H2.

Conversely the range contains g times every analytic polynomial, while the
range is closed because the extended operator is a bounded idempotent. Since
g is outer, those polynomial multiples are dense in H2. Therefore

  Ran(g P_H g^(-1))=H2.

The identical argument with g^(-1) proves the second channel. This supplies
the rigorous domain version of the report's equation (19) and preserves

  P_orth=P_H direct-sum P_H.

The report correctly keeps this orthogonal range projector separate from the
oblique Plemelj idempotent, the smooth annulus covariance
(1+exp(B))^(-1), and the old quarter Hardy state. No forbidden state or
operator identity is asserted.

2. Modular pullback
===================

With

  a=M^2,   b=M U^(-1),   c=U,

the exact matrices satisfy

  a^3=b^2=c^4=abc=I.

For Phi(t)=-(t-1)^2/(4t), the marked local monodromies are precisely

  u_0       =c=U,
  u_1       =a^2=M,
  u_(-1)    =b^2=I,
  u_infinity=b c b^(-1)=(U M)^(-1).

Their product in the displayed Hurwitz order is I. Writing only
d=(UM)^(-1), as the report does, is correct; the conjugation formula above
makes the path marking explicit.

The composition with t=x^4 has degree eight and passport

  [2,2,2,2], [2,2,2,2], [4,4].

Its ramification contribution is 14=2*8-2. The four surviving peripheral
operators are U^k M U^(-k), and their product is the v117 relation. Q:x->ix
is the source deck transformation. It is not thereby the modular S path or
the marked fiber operator U.

The finite image qualification is necessary and correctly stated. In
PSL(2,Z/4)=S4 the cusp image has order four, so the points over zero and
infinity become apparent after the fourth power. For the full modular local
system the cusp T has infinite order and T^4 remains at both points. Hence
the same map does not close on P1 minus mu4 for the full PSL(2,Z) system.

3. Elliptic degree four source
==============================

The Tate calculation checks out. For

  E_b: y^2+x y+b y=x^3+b x^2,

the discriminant is b^4(1-16b). The critical collision equation has factors
(16b-9)(16b-1). The branch b=1/16 is singular, while b=9/16 is smooth. At
the latter value the normalized function has passport [4],[3,1],[4] and

  j=2*47^3/3^8=207646/6561.

An independent enumeration gives 24 transitive ordered branch triples of
the stated cycle types. They form one simultaneous S4 conjugacy orbit of
size 24, confirming the unique ordered Nielsen class. The source therefore
is not the fixed j=1728 ellipse. The sign twist needed to turn the traceless
permutation representation into the v117 character remains an additional
marked representation datum, exactly as the report states.

Final scope
===========

The reviewed results establish a canonical positive Hardy range for the
ramified trace and an exact finite modular origin for the v117 relations.
They do not supply a propagation unitary from the smooth annulus state to the
graph, choose the old quarter state, identify Q with modular S, or promote
the finite Level 4 result to the faithful full modular local system.
