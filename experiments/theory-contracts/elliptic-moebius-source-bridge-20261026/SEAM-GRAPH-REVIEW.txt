INDEPENDENT REVIEW: EQUATOR LIFT, REAL FORMS, AND THE SEAM GRAPH
================================================================

26 October 2026. Exact analytic review plus independent affine checker. No
Atlas, paper, ledger, or earlier contract was changed. This review tests one
new geometric identification only. It introduces no field model and makes no
phenomenological claim.

Question
========

On

  E: y^2=x^4-1

let

  Q(x,y)=(i x,y),
  h(x,y)=(x,-y),
  kappa(x,y)=(bar x,bar y),
  r(x,y)=(1/x,i y/x^2).

The base equator is fixed by x -> 1/bar x. Its lift is S=r kappa. Does the
full inverse image of that marked equator coincide with the previously used
smooth P1/NS seam circle? What group and real-form census actually results
after adjoining r?

Verdict
=======

The proposed affine formulas and the enlarged census are exact:

  r^2=h,
  r(z)=i z+1/2,
  S(z)=bar z+i/2,
  hS(z)=-bar z+1/2                       mod Z+iZ.

The marked equator does not lift to one smooth seam circle. Its complete
inverse image is

  pi^(-1)(|x|=1)=Fix(S) union Fix(hS),

namely two horizontal and two vertical ovals. They meet at the four branch
points of pi:E->P1. The resulting embedded torus graph has

  V=4, E=8, F=4, b1=5.

Therefore the old smooth one-circle NS source and the full marked equator
pullback are different geometric objects. Choosing one oval, normalizing the
nodal graph, or smoothing its four crossings adds a choice that is absent
from the full pullback.

The group correction is equally precise. The old holomorphic group
<Q,h> has order eight and is C4 x C2, hence is abelian and not D4. Adjoining
the actual lift r gives a holomorphic group of order sixteen. It is the
central deck extension of the base D4 action. After adjoining kappa the full
group has 32 elements, split into 16 holomorphic and 16 antiholomorphic maps.

Among the 16 antiholomorphic maps, 12 are real involutions:

  dividing       4,
  nondividing    4,
  empty          4.

The remaining four antiholomorphic maps have order four and are not real
involutions. The original eight antiholomorphic maps have census

  dividing 2, nondividing 4, empty 2.

The eight new maps contribute

  dividing involutions 2, empty involutions 2, order-four maps 4.

The two new dividing involutions are exactly S and hS. They fix all four
branch marks. The two old dividing OS forms hQkappa and hQ^3kappa fix no
branch mark. Thus the original OS circle real forms and the marked-equator
real forms must not be identified.

1. Algebraic lift r
===================

The map r preserves the curve. Put

  x'=1/x,
  y'=i y/x^2.

Then

  y'^2=-y^2/x^4
      =-(x^4-1)/x^4
      =1/x^4-1
      =x'^4-1.

Applying r twice gives

  x''=x,
  y''=i(i y/x^2)/(1/x)^2=-y,

so r^2=h exactly. This is the decisive distinction from a base reflection:
the base inversion has order two, while its chosen lift has order four and
squares to the elliptic deck map.

Use the square-torus uniformization E=C/(Z+iZ) and write z=t+i s. In the
normalization used by the existing elliptic calculation,

  Q(z)=i z,
  r(z)=i z+1/2,
  h(z)=-z+(1+i)/2,
  kappa(z)=-i bar z+(1+i)/2.              (1)

The last formula is the affine torus expression of algebraic conjugation in
this uniformization. Direct composition gives

  S=r kappa:     z -> bar z+i/2,
  hS:            z -> -bar z+1/2.         (2)

No adjustable phase or translated real structure was inserted.

2. Why the full equator pullback is a graph
============================================

Let pi(x,y)=x. Since S lifts x -> 1/bar x, a point p lies over the unit circle
precisely when

  pi(S p)=pi(p).

Away from a branch point the fiber of pi is {p,hp}. Therefore

  S p=p  or  S p=h p.

The second alternative is equivalent to hS p=p. At branch points the two
alternatives coincide. This proves

  pi^(-1)(|x|=1)=Fix(S) union Fix(hS).     (3)

From (2),

  Fix(S):   2s=1/2 mod 1,
            s=1/4 or 3/4,

  Fix(hS):  2t=1/2 mod 1,
            t=1/4 or 3/4.                 (4)

Thus Fix(S) is two horizontal circles and Fix(hS) is two vertical circles.
Their intersection consists of

  (t,s) in {1/4,3/4} x {1/4,3/4}.        (5)

These are exactly the fixed points of h, because

  h(z)=z  iff  2z=(1+i)/2 mod Z+iZ.

They are therefore the four ramification points upstairs, lying over
x^4=1. Each of the four circles is cut by two vertices into two edges. Hence

  V=4,
  E=4 circles times 2 arcs=8.

The graph is connected. Its incidence matrix has rank V-1=3, so

  b1=E-rank(partial)=8-3=5.               (6)

Since the ambient surface is a torus, V-E+F=0 and therefore F=4. The checker
constructs the eight edges and its incidence matrix explicitly. It does not
insert V,E,F or b1 as a census table.

The normalization of (3) is four disjoint circles. The unnormalized inverse
image is the connected V4/E8 graph. Neither object is the single smooth circle
on which the older D_Gamma and its alpha-twisted H1 domain were defined.

3. Exact group closure
======================

Let

  H0=<Q,h>.

Because Q has order four, h has order two, and h commutes with Q, direct
closure gives

  |H0|=8,
  H0 isomorphic to C4 x C2.                (7)

Its element-order distribution is

  order 1: 1,
  order 2: 3,
  order 4: 4.

This group is abelian, so it is not D4. After adjoining kappa, the old group

  G0=<Q,h,kappa>

has order 16, with eight holomorphic elements and an antiholomorphic coset of
eight real involutions. Strictly speaking, those eight real maps form a
coset, not a subgroup.

Now adjoin r. Set t_x=r Q^(-1), the half translation z->z+1/2. Conjugation by
Q gives t_y:z->z+i/2. The four half translations form V4, and

  H=<Q,r>=V4 semidirect C4,
  |H|=16.                                  (8)

The deck h is central. The lifted dihedral relation is

  r Q r^(-1)=h Q^(-1),                    (9)

so after quotienting by <h>, relation (9) becomes the ordinary D4 reflection
relation. Hence H is a central deck extension of the base D4 group, not D4
itself. This is the required scope correction: the old canonical H0 of order
eight is a proper abelian subgroup of the full lifted action.

Finally

  G=<Q,r,kappa>

has order 32. Orientation separates it into 16 holomorphic and 16
antiholomorphic maps.

4. Real-form census computed from fixed equations
==================================================

Every antiholomorphic affine map on the square torus is represented in real
coordinates by

  v -> M v+d,  det(M)=-1.                 (10)

The checker first computes its order in the finite affine group. For an
involution it solves

  (I-M)v=d mod Z^2.                       (11)

This is a rank-one torus equation. A primitive integer vector in the left
kernel of I-M detects whether (11) is soluble. If it is, the nonzero Smith
invariant

  gcd(entries of I-M)

is the number of fixed circles. Thus the program classifies an involution as

  0 circles: empty,
  1 circle: nondividing,
  2 circles: dividing.

This procedure independently yields for the old antiholomorphic coset:

  nondividing 4, empty 2, dividing 2.      (12)

For the eight maps added by r it yields:

  dividing 2, empty 2, antiholomorphic order four 4.     (13)

Combining (12) and (13), the full set of 16 antiholomorphic maps contains

  4 nondividing real involutions,
  4 empty real involutions,
  4 dividing real involutions,
  4 antiholomorphic order-four maps.       (14)

Consequently the phrase "16 real forms" would be wrong. There are 16
antiholomorphic affine symmetries, but only 12 are involutions and hence real
structures.

5. Old OS forms versus marked-equator forms
============================================

The two dividing members of the old coset are

  rho_+=hQkappa,
  rho_-=hQ^3kappa.

In affine coordinates their fixed circles lie at

  t=0,1/2  or  s=0,1/2.

They avoid all four branch points (5). These are the previously classified
dividing Schottky/OS forms whose halves are smooth annuli.

The new dividing members are

  S=rkappa,
  hS.

Their fixed circles are (4), and each real structure fixes all four branch
points. Together they give the full marked pullback (3). The distinction is
not cosmetic:

  rho_+/-: smooth dividing OS boundaries, no marked branch point fixed;

  S,hS: marked-equator lifts, all four branch points fixed, union is a graph.

The old NS spin character on E can still be restricted to these circles, but
that does not identify the complete graph with one old NS circle. A boundary
Dirac operator on one selected component and a graph Dirac operator with
vertex conditions are different closed operators and have different domains.

6. Consequence for the source bridge
====================================

The earlier source audit remains valid but its target now needs one sharper
typing condition. A proposed P1-to-elliptic map must say whether the original
smooth seam is transported to

  a. one old OS oval of rho_+ or rho_-,
  b. one selected component of Fix(S) or Fix(hS),
  c. the normalization of all four marked ovals, or
  d. the full V4/E8 graph with explicit vertex conditions.

These targets are not interchangeable. In particular, identifying the old
smooth circle with (d) is false before any field or state question is asked.
Selecting (b), (c), or a smoothing of (d) adds boundary data and must be
derived from the original source rather than hidden in a cover dictionary.

This does not invalidate the elliptic annulus, its dividing OS cuts, or the
quarter boundary calculation. It separates two real structures that were
previously close enough to confuse:

  the OS/Schottky cut rho_+/- produces a smooth annulus boundary;
  the lifted marked equator S produces the ramified seam graph.

The smallest next test is therefore no longer a comparison of group orders.
It is the domain-level question whether the original P1 seam trace lands on
one smooth rho oval, or whether the original theory supplies graph vertex
conditions at the four ramification points. Without that relation, the full
marked pullback cannot be used as the original one-circle source.

Scope
=====

PROVED: the r extension, the 32-element affine group, the 12-real-involution
census, the distinction between old OS forms and new marked-equator forms,
and the V4/E8/F4, b1=5 seam graph.

REFUTED: the direct identification of the full marked equator pullback with
the old smooth one-circle NS source.

OPEN: which smooth oval or graph domain the original P1 boundary trace
actually selects. That is an operator-domain source question, not a missing
finite group calculation.
