OLD SOURCE MAP AUDIT: P1 CIRCLE, COLLAR, AND SEAM.EQUIV
========================================================

26 October 2026. Read only source audit. No Atlas, paper, ledger, or prior
contract was changed. Atlas snapshot used for navigation:
4a17a404b58c05f460121df8, 327 reviewed findings.

Question
========

Do the older TFPT papers already contain the missing marked source map from
the primitive P1 circle kernel to the later collar and elliptic annulus, so
that B, u, reflection, state, and local support are one original object? The
relevant chain was checked in its historical order:

  fullv21 A4 / circle gluing
    -> tfpt-31 closure transfer
    -> tfpt-42 master / operational seed
    -> TFPT45 boundary kernel
    -> SEAM.EQUIV.MMST.01.

Verdict
=======

No such source map is stated in the checked originals. The sources contain
two strong but different pieces:

  1. fullv21 installs an explicit one-particle circle pair. After the known
     orientation correction it is

       H_alpha=L2(S1),
       B_alpha=-i partial_theta on the alpha-twisted H1 domain,
       u=M_z,
       B_alpha u=u(B_alpha+1).

     It has winding and spectral flow one and a canonical Hardy defect line.

  2. tfpt-42 and TFPT45 define a full operational seed and a product collar.
     Their input already contains the local algebra, Euclidean dynamics,
     reflection, state, seam class, and collar generator. From an object of
     that declared category they construct B_Sigma and the one-sided boundary
     datum.

The missing arrow lies exactly between these two pieces. There is no original
formula B_Sigma=D_Gamma, no unitary taking u=M_z to the collar seam operator,
and no intertwiner carrying the fullv21 circle domain, reflection, state, and
local algebra to the later operational seed. A repository-wide literal search
for B_Sigma=D_Gamma and its reversed/spaced forms finds the equality only in
the 25 October audit as a proposed repair, never in the checked paper sources.

This is smaller than a new field model. One marked collared spin/source
isomorphism would transport the analytic boundary data functorially. It must,
however, include the clutching operator and the state/local net marks. A bare
isometry of unmarked manifolds or equality of winding classes does not do so.

  249-285

The v42 "Master closure roadmap" is likewise downstream. It is explicitly a
roadmap/corollary from T_bound and canonical doubling, rather than an input
proof; see 022-section-closure-theorems-from-the-admissibility-complex.tex:

2. Smallest decisive sewing theorem
====================================

There is one useful compression. The analytic boundary entries B and P_C do
not need independent guesses once a genuine marked collared spin isomorphism
has been supplied.

Let

  F:(M_1,S_1,D_1) -> (M_2,S_2,D_2)

be a unitary spin-bundle isomorphism of manifolds with boundary that preserves
the oriented normal, collar coordinate, boundary trace, and the full Dirac
operator:

  F D_1 F^(-1)=D_2,
  Tr_2 F=W Tr_1.

Writing D_j=gamma_n(partial_n+B_j) in the transported product trivialization
gives

  W B_1 W^(-1)=B_2.                                      (A)

Moreover F maps harmonic interior solutions bijectively, so it maps their
boundary traces. The orthogonal projections onto those Cauchy-data spaces
therefore satisfy

  W P_C,1 W^(-1)=P_C,2.                                  (B)

Equations (A) and (B) are direct consequences of intertwining and require no
new model. They agree with the standard construction of Calderon projectors
from the product collar, invertible double, and Poisson operator in
Booss-Bavnbek, Lesch and Zhu, "The Calderon Projection: New Definition and
Applications", J. Geom. Phys. 59 (2009), arXiv:0803.4160. That paper gives a
canonical Calderon construction for a supplied elliptic first-order operator;
it does not identify two previously unrelated physical sources. Guillarmou,
Moroianu and Park, arXiv:1009.3179, likewise construct the natural orthogonal
Calderon projector for a supplied spin Dirac operator. Neither theorem creates
the missing F.

The scope is exact:

  * B and P_C follow from F.

  * The seam multiplier requires the extra marked relation

      W u_1 W^(-1)=u_2.                                  (C)

    Winding one alone does not imply (C). Many inequivalent unitary
    representatives have the same K1 class.

  * Reflection follows only when F is equivariant for the relevant reflected
    half and its Pin/Real lift. The Schottky reflection rho, free Moebius deck
    delta_A, and quarter transport Q are distinct operations.

  * A quasifree covariance or OS state follows only if it is defined from the
    same transported full Cauchy graph or is separately required to satisfy

      omega_2 = omega_1 o Ad(W^(-1)).                    (D)

    The Calderon projector alone is not a general interacting Schwinger
    functional.

  * Local support follows only when F maps regions and intertwines the local
    algebras. A Hilbert-space unitary can satisfy (A) while being nonlocal.

Thus there are not six unrelated gaps, but there is also not a bare
one-particle shortcut. The minimal sufficient datum is one marked local
collared spin/source isomorphism

  I_source=(F,W; seam faces, deck, Pin lift, Q, u, local net, state),

whose Dirac part automatically yields (A) and (B), while its marks impose (C)
and (D).

5. Smallest decisive original-source test
==========================================

No further finite spectrum or character calculation is needed. The first
decisive test is whether the original P1 construction supplies a marked local
collar map W satisfying simultaneously

  W Dom(B_P1)=H1_NS,
  W B_P1 W^-1=2 pi B_cov,
  (W direct-sum W) P_C,P1 (W direct-sum W)^-1=P_C,ann,
  W u_P1 W^-1=e^(2 pi i s),

with the original reflected local algebra and state transported as in (D).

Success criterion: these are consequences of the original P1 circle kernel
and its canonical double, with elliptic annulus modulus 1/2 or an explicit
operator/conformal transport from the old modulus.

Abort criterion: the source yields only [u]=1, a matching group order, a
boundary spectral resemblance, or a separately chosen QWZ/Hardy state. Any
one of those leaves I_source open.

Final classification
====================

PROVED IN THE ORIGINALS: an explicit old circle B_alpha,u pair; a later
abstract operational seed; canonical Calderon reconstruction from a supplied
collared Dirac problem; downstream heat and MMST/QWZ constructions.

PROVED BY THIS AUDIT: the analytic B and P_C entries collapse functorially to
one supplied marked collared spin isomorphism. The old paper chain contains no
such isomorphism and does not identify the state or local net.

OPEN: the actual P1-to-elliptic marked local source map I_source. This is the
single load-bearing original relation. It is more precise than a request for
a generic "field dictionary" and smaller than adding a new CAR model.
