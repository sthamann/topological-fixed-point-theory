ETA PHASES DO NOT BY THEMSELVES IDENTIFY THE SOURCE BOUNDARY LINE
2026-10-27

Scope: a convention and type check, not a no-go for a TFPT anomaly theory.
The new positive Hardy cable construction is a separate result. This note
prevents a generic eta theorem from being used as its missing physical
operator identification.

1. Fix lambda(E)=det H0(E) tensor det H1(E)^(-1). The positive theta
   determinant line is lambda(E)^(-1), not lambda(E).

2. Dai and Freed define their graded determinant as det(V-) tensor
   det(V+)^(-1), their equation (2.7). For a Dolbeault boundary operator
   this is lambda(E)^(-1). Their exponentiated invariant

       tau=exp(2 pi i xi),   xi=(eta+dim ker D)/2,

   takes values in the inverse of that determinant line, hence in lambda(E)
   in the present convention. Merely reading the word 'inverse' without
   translating conventions reverses the conclusion.

3. The original primitive Euclidean action includes +(i pi/2) Delta eta.
   Its contribution to exp(-S) is therefore

       a=exp(-i pi Delta eta/2).

   Even when zero modes vanish and the compared eta invariants are those
   in the Dai-Freed setting, the numerical relation is

       a^2=tau_rel^(-1),

   not a=tau_rel. If zero modes are retained, the missing factor is
   exp(i pi Delta dim ker D). Any square-root determinant or Pfaffian
   interpretation needs its actual real structure and gluing data.

4. As a conditional diagnostic, if the boundary operator pair were
   E(-Dq) relative to E, its cohomology determinant ratio would be Bq^(-1).
   Under precisely that dictionary the square a^2 would be in Bq, not
   automatically in Bq^(-2) as a=Nq^(-1) would require. This comparison is
   not a claim that the original D_rel/D_ref is this Dolbeault pair.

5. The original D_ref and D_rel are even and odd parts of a given operator
   under the geometric involution. This definition supplies neither the
   boundary Dolbeault operator nor the bundle pair (S0 tensor L,L) required
   by the relative Hardy norm construction. A genuine application must
   identify the boundary family, coefficient bundles, orientation, kernel
   correction and determinant/Pfaffian convention on the actual source.

Verdict: no eta-only shortcut closes that physical identification. The
finite clock phase and the correctly constructed Hardy norm line remain
valid. This note makes no claim that a suitably derived real or relative
anomaly theory cannot realize the needed line.
