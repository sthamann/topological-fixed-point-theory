SAME SPIN REFERENCE SIMPLIFICATION
The original seam shift recovers the ungraded native complex module class on one spin Hardy space
2026-10-27

VERDICT

The proposed simplification is correct at the ordinary complex Fredholm line
and ungraded finite module levels.  Work on the same Hardy space with
coefficient

  E=S_0 tensor P_spin.

Compare multiplication by the original seam coordinate z with multiplication
by 1.  The identity operator is invertible, while the z shift is injective
with cokernel E|D_q.  Therefore

  delta(E,M_z) tensor delta(E,M_1)^(-1)
    =det H^0(D_q,E|D_q)^(-1)
    =N_q(S_0)^(-1) tensor B_O^(-1).                            (1)

The extra line B_O^(-1) is trivial under c and beta and has Q character -1.
It changes alpha to -alpha and changes no other finite relation.  The accepted
native classification already retains exactly this global sign ambiguity.
Thus (1) produces the full ungraded native complex module class without
introducing a second, unspun physical reference space.

To obtain the literal signed representative W_q=H_Theta tensor N_q^(-1), one
must still remove the ordering line B_O or choose the unordered norm
normalization.  The present theorem does not identify those two signed
representatives.  It shows that this distinction is already contained in the
allowed alpha sign at the ungraded complex module level.  It does not identify
their super determinant or Pfaffian sewing functors.


1. THE SAME SPACE RELATIVE FREDHOLM LINE

Let H_E be the direct sum of the three Hardy spaces of E over the components
of the inverse image of the open z disk.  The source half form supplies the
Hardy norm, and a unitary flat metric on P_spin supplies the coefficient norm.
The four boundary ramification points have measure zero and do not contribute
an interior evaluation term.

Multiplication by 1 is the identity on H_E.  Multiplication by z satisfies

  ker M_z=0,
  coker M_z=H_E/zH_E=H^0(D_q,E|D_q).

With

  delta(T)=det ker(T) tensor det coker(T)^(-1),

one has the canonical identities

  delta(E,M_1)=C,
  delta(E,M_z)=B(E)^(-1),                                     (2)

where B(E)=det H^0(D_q,E|D_q).  No comparison between two Hilbert spaces or
two coefficient bundles occurs in (2).


2. THE SPIN NORMALIZATION REMOVES THE MOVING COEFFICIENT

For three line fibers, the ordered evaluation determinant factors as

  B(S_0 tensor P_spin)
    =Nm_Dq(S_0) tensor Nm_Dq(P_spin) tensor B_O.                (3)

The accepted spin relative Poincare normalization proves, equivariantly over
the full Picard family,

  Nm_Dq(P_spin)=O.                                             (4)

Using N_q(S_0)=Nm_Dq(S_0), equations (2)-(4) give

  delta(E,M_z) tensor delta(E,M_1)^(-1)
    =N_q(S_0)^(-1) tensor B_O^(-1),                            (5)

which is (1).  This is familywise.  It applies to the actual sign line and its
c orbit and needs no fixed coefficient fiber.

The separate spin versus scalar cable construction proves the more rigid
identity N_q^(-1) itself because its two ordering determinants cancel.  The
same spin construction proves the ungraded module class with one fewer physical
premise: its reference is the identity on the same source Hardy space.


3. THE ORDERING LINE IS EXACTLY THE ALLOWED ALPHA SIGN

The actions on the scalar ordering line are determined only by the
permutation of D_q:

  c|B_O=+1,
  beta|B_O=+1,
  Q|B_O=-1.                                                    (6)

Indeed, c is a three cycle, Q fixes q_0 and exchanges q_1,q_2, and beta=Q^2
fixes all three scalar fibers.  Taking the inverse does not alter these signs.

On N_q^(-1), beta acts by +i and c acts trivially.  Tensoring with B_O^(-1)
therefore keeps

  gamma^3=I,
  alpha^2=iI,
  alpha gamma alpha^(-1)=gamma^(-1),                           (7)

but replaces alpha by -alpha.  It acts trivially on the N64 Heisenberg group
and hence does not change its central character or Pauli quotient action.
The spin rigidified theta theorem proves that alpha and -alpha are precisely
the two lifts over each accepted quotient action and belong to the same
classified ungraded native complex module class.

Consequently B_O^(-1) is not an additional central phase fit.  It records the
difference between an ordered three point determinant and the unordered norm
line, already visible as the unavoidable sign of the Quarter lift.


4. THE SUPER DETERMINANT PARITY DOES NOT CANCEL ON ONE CABLE

A Fredholm determinant line has super parity equal to its index modulo two.
Here

  index(M_z)=-3=1 mod 2,
  index(M_1)=0 mod 2.                                         (8)

Thus the same spin relative defect in (1) is an odd superline.  In the
factorization (5), B_O^(-1) is also odd.  Its Q character can be absorbed by
alpha -> -alpha after forgetting the grading, but its odd parity cannot be
removed by that sign change.

The two cable construction has a different graded type.  Its spin shift and
scalar shift both have index -3 and hence both determinant lines are odd.
Their relative tensor product is even.  Therefore the equality

  spin cable / scalar cable =N_q^(-1)

is an equality of even relative superlines, while (5) is only the same
ordinary complex character after forgetting one odd ordering line.  The two
statements are not equal as graded sewing functors.

The primitive base shift has index -1 and is likewise odd.  Pulling it to the
three sheet cable changes the integer index from -1 to -3 but preserves its
parity.  This is compatible with an odd defect; it does not by itself derive
fermionic statistics, a Pfaffian orientation or the physical source
quantization rule.


5. WHAT THIS REMOVES AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

After forgetting the super grading, the theorem removes the need to assume
that the physical reference operator lives on an unspun scalar cable.  It is
enough, for the finite native complex module class, that the original source
uses the relative determinant line of its actual spin shift M_z against the
canonical identity M_1 on the same Hardy space.

It does not yet prove that the physical P1 source action evaluates this
relative Fredholm line.  The remaining physical statement is now narrower:

  the original seam determinant or boundary amplitude must use
  delta(E,M_z) relative to delta(E,M_1),

with the Hardy polarization induced by the actual Calderon and reflection
data.  No separate scalar coefficient bundle, state or Hilbert space is
needed for this statement.

Nor does (5) select a fixed signed alpha representative.  A later observable
that distinguishes alpha from -alpha would still require the unordered norm
normalization or an equivalent orientation datum.  All finite N64, gamma,
beta and projective Quarter relations already agree after forgetting the
grading.

For a physical graded or Pfaffian sewing statement, the scalar cable or an
equivalent odd reference remains material because it cancels the Fredholm
parity.  The source must decide whether its amplitude is the odd one cable
defect or the even two cable relative defect.  This choice cannot be dismissed
as alpha gauge and is not fixed by the finite complex module classification.
