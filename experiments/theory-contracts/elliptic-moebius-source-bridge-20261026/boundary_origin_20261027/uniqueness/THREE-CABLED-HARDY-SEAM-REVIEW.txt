INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE THREE-CABLED HARDY SEAM
2026-10-27

VERDICT

The proposed Hardy construction supplies the previously missing mathematical
one-copy line.  On the existing source curve, the original coordinate z is a
degree-three sewing function with one simple zero on each of the three sheets
over the disk.  Multiplication by z has a rank-three cokernel.  Comparing its
spin defect line with the corresponding three-cabled scalar reference defect
gives exactly

  N_q(S_0)^(-1)

once, with the correct c, Q, alpha and beta actions.  The scalar i in
Q M_z Q^(-1)=i M_z does not add a determinant-line phase: multiplication of a
Fredholm operator by a nonzero scalar leaves its kernel and cokernel
unchanged.  The induced action on the quotient is the relevant one.

This closes the mathematical sewing origin of the one-copy norm factor on
the specified Hardy half of the source curve.  It does not yet prove that the
physical P1 seam, state, reflection and Calderon domain are this Hardy
realization.  It also does not construct N64 anew; it supplies the missing
one-dimensional line that the already accepted theta module tensors with.


1. THE ORIGINAL FUNCTION HAS THE REQUIRED DIVISOR AND DEGREE

On

  C: eta^2=1-w^6

define

  z=eta/(1-w^3).

Then

  z^2=(1+w^3)/(1-w^3).                                       (1)

At q_a, where w^3=-1, eta is a local coordinate and the denominator is
nonzero.  Thus z has a simple zero at every q_a.  At p_a, where w^3=1, the
same identity shows a simple pole.  Therefore

  div(z)=D_q-D_p.                                              (2)

There are three poles, so z:C->P1 has degree three.  Solving (1) gives

  w^3=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1).

The only branch values are z=+1,-1,+i,-i, all on the unit circle.  Hence over
the open disk D={|z|<1}, the map is an unramified three-sheeted cover.  Since
D is simply connected, its inverse image is the disjoint union of three
disks, and each restriction maps biholomorphically to D.  The boundary branch
points are measure-zero endpoints for the Hardy traces and do not alter the
interior shift index.


2. THE HARDY SHIFT HAS THE FULL q EVALUATION QUOTIENT

Let

  H_S=H^2(C_D,S_0),

where C_D is the three-component inverse image of D.  Point evaluation at the
interior zeros q_a is bounded on this Hardy space.  On each disk,
multiplication by z is the ordinary unilateral shift, so M_z is injective,
has closed range, and has a one-dimensional cokernel.  On all three sheets,

  index(M_z|H_S)=-3,
  H_S/zH_S isomorphic direct_sum_a S_0|q_a.                    (3)

The quotient isomorphism is evaluation at the three simple zeros.  Therefore
the Fredholm defect line, in the convention det ker tensor det coker^(-1), is

  delta_S=det(H_S/zH_S)^(-1)=B_q^(-1).                        (4)

Apply the same three-cabled shift to the trivial line:

  H_O=H^2(C_D,O_C),
  delta_O=B_O^(-1),
  B_O=det H^0(D_q,O_Dq).                                      (5)

Taking the spin defect relative to this scalar reference gives

  delta_S tensor delta_O^(-1)
    =B_q^(-1) tensor B_O
    =N_q(S_0)^(-1).                                           (6)

This identity is familywise, rather than confined to the trivial coefficient
line.  Let L be any degree-zero line and use the same multiplier and the same
three Hardy components for S_0 tensor L and for L.  Their cokernels are

  direct_sum_a (S_0|q_a tensor L|q_a),
  direct_sum_a L|q_a.

With one common ordering of the q points,

  det direct_sum_a(S_0|q_a tensor L|q_a)
    =Nm_Dq(S_0) tensor det direct_sum_a L|q_a.

Consequently

  delta(S_0 tensor L,z) tensor delta(L,z)^(-1)
    =Nm_Dq(S_0)^(-1)                                           (7)

for every L.  All coefficient fibers and all sheet-permutation signs cancel.
The equality is equivariant over the complete L family, including the actual
q sign line and its c orbit.  It does not require L itself to be a c fixed
point and it does not require a Poincare rigidification merely to cancel the
coefficient line.  The spin-relative Poincare construction remains the
stronger standalone trivialization of the individual norm family.

Equations (6)-(7) are the decisive improvement over an arbitrary evaluation
restriction.  The same original degree-three sewing function produces both
operators, and the ratio removes the ordering determinant of the three-point
scheme.  It also explains why the old base shift has index -1 while its
actual three-sheet pullback has index -3: these are the base operator and its
three-cabled lift, not two operators asserted to be invertibly identical.


2A. HIGHER WINDING AND WHY INDEX ONE SELECTS ONE COPY

For n>=1, multiplication by z^n has index -3n and quotient supported on the
length-n divisor nD_q.  At each q_a its jet filtration has graded pieces

  E|q_a tensor (T_q_a^*)^j,        j=0,...,n-1.

Apply this once with E=S_0 tensor L and once with E=L.  The L fibers, the
cotangent jet factors and their permutation signs cancel degree by degree.
Every level retains one S_0|q_a.  Hence

  delta(S_0 tensor L,z^n) tensor delta(L,z^n)^(-1)
    =Nm_Dq(S_0)^(-n).                                         (8)

This proves the exponent, including all jets; it is not a phase inference.
On the base circle the same multiplier z^n has index -n.  Therefore an
already specified oriented primitive seam with index -1, equivalently one
positive winding, selects n=1 inside this cable functor.  The finite quarter
characters alone only see n modulo 4 or 8 and could not make that selection.
This conclusion uses the original integer index as part of the sewing data;
it does not identify an unrelated physical D_rel or D_ref with these Hardy
operators.


3. THE Q SCALAR DOES NOT CREATE AN EXTRA LINE PHASE

The source automorphisms act on z by

  c z=z,
  Q z=i z.                                                     (9)

Thus c commutes with M_z, while in the stated pullback convention

  Q M_z Q^(-1)=i M_z.                                        (10)

Equation (10) maps the Fredholm operator to its nonzero scalar multiple.  The
canonical identification of their determinant lines is the identity on
kernel and cokernel, since

  ker(iM_z)=ker(M_z),
  coker(iM_z)=coker(M_z).                                    (11)

Equivalently, to write a strictly commuting chain square one may multiply
the domain action by i.  Because the kernel is zero, this domain scalar does
not enter the Fredholm defect line.  The induced Q action on the cokernel is
the quotient action on H/zH and agrees with the action obtained from the
three evaluations.  No additional factor i^(index) belongs to the Fredholm
line.  Such a factor would concern a separately normalized determinant
section or a regularized determinant, neither of which is used in (4)-(8).

The same chain scalar occurs for the spin and scalar three-cabled shifts and
would cancel in their relative ratio in any case.


4. THE FULL FINITE LINE CHARACTER IS CORRECT

At each q point, beta acts by +i on the S_0 fiber.  It acts trivially on the
corresponding scalar reference fiber.  Hence

  beta|B_q=i^3=-i,
  beta|B_O=1,
  beta|B_q^(-1) tensor B_O=+i.                                (12)

This is exactly the beta character of N_q^(-1).  At the Q level, both B_q
and B_O contain the sign of the transposition of q_1 and q_2.  Their ratio
cancels that common permutation sign and retains precisely the spin norm
character.  Thus the alpha square and the alpha sign convention agree with
the already proved marked-divisor line, rather than merely matching beta.

The cubic c cycles the three points.  Its permutation is even, and the
normalized spin lift has product one on N_q.  Since c also commutes with
M_z, equation (6) carries the accepted trivial gamma character.  Therefore
the relative Hardy defect supplies all line characters used in the
spin-rigidified theta-module theorem.

The one-dimensional c-invariant summand of the quotient must not be confused
with this relative line.  Its undualized fiber has beta=+i, whereas its
directed Fredholm defect is the inverse fiber and has beta=-i.  The correct
+i character arises from the inverse determinant of all three q fibers,
relative to the equally cabled scalar reference.

N64 itself continues to act on H^0(O(2Theta)); the Hardy quotient line is
one-dimensional and does not independently derive that Heisenberg action.
Tensoring the accepted theta module with the line (6) gives the already
proved full finite package.  This distinction prevents a line calculation
from being overstated as a new derivation of the theta representation.


5. EXACT SCOPE OF THE POSITIVE CONNECTION

The construction proves a canonical mathematical arrow

  three-cabled spin Hardy shift / three-cabled scalar Hardy shift
      -> N_q(S_0)^(-1).                                       (13)

It uses only the existing source curve, its original z coordinate, the
selected spin line and the disk bounded by the original four branch values.
It does not choose an arbitrary point-evaluation operator after the fact.
Together with the original primitive index-one seam, the higher-winding
calculation selects this literal one-copy power within the mathematical
Hardy sewing contract.

Three physical identifications remain outside this theorem:

  * the actual P1 seam circle and its polarization must be identified with
    the z-boundary Hardy polarization used here;

  * the original state, reflection and Calderon domain must induce the same
    spin and scalar relative defect ratio;

  * the source action must use this relative line, rather than a separate
    zeta determinant or Pfaffian normalization.

The construction is therefore a substantially stronger positive source
candidate than the earlier abstract evaluation sequence.  It is not yet a
claim that the real physical domain or OS state has been identified.
