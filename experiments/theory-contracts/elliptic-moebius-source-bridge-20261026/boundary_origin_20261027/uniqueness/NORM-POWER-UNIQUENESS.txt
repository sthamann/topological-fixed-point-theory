VERDICT

The accepted spin-rigidified theta-module theorem remains valid, but its
finite relations do not select the literal exponent k=1.  For

  W_(q,k)=H^0(Jac(C),O(2 Theta)) tensor N_q(S_0)^(-k),

the complete native central relation alpha^2=+i I holds exactly when

  k=1 modulo 4.                                                (1)

Gamma and the N64 action do not refine this congruence.  With the actual

invariant trivialization, so k is intrinsically periodic modulo eight.  The
two surviving residues are k=1 and k=5.  They have identical N64, gamma and
beta actions; their alpha operators differ by the unresolved global sign
alpha -> -alpha.

Consequently there is one class after quotienting by the spin-lift sign that
the source itself leaves unselected, but there is no theorem that the source
uses literally one inverse norm rather than five, minus three, or another
representative of the same class.  If alpha is treated as a fixed signed
operator, k=1 and k=5 are distinct marked tuples.  A physical or functorial
one-copy statement therefore still requires the missing boundary, sewing or
determinant insertion rule.  Minimal positive exponent is a convention or an
extra simplicity rule, not a consequence of the finite relations.


1. ALL CENTRAL PHASES ARE EXACTLY PERIODIC

Write

  H_Theta=H^0(Jac(C),O(2 Theta)),
  N=N_q(S_0).

The accepted marked-divisor calculation gives

  beta|H_Theta=+I,
  beta|N=-i,
  gamma|N=+1.                                                  (2)

Since beta=alpha^2, the inverse kth power contributes

  beta|N^(-k)=(-i)^(-k)=i^k.                                  (3)

The N64 Heisenberg group acts only on H_Theta, so tensoring by N^(-k) does
not change that action.  Gamma is also unchanged because its normalized
action on N is one.  The conjugation relation

  alpha gamma alpha^(-1)=gamma^(-1)                            (4)

therefore holds for every k.

The native q-oriented central relation is beta=+i I.  Equation (3) gives

  i^k=i  iff  k=1 modulo 4.                                   (5)

For these and only these exponents, alpha^4=(i^k)^2=-I and alpha has order
eight.  Thus the full displayed finite relations, rather than the central
phase alone, give the same condition (5).  There is no further restriction
from gamma or N64.


2. ADDING FOUR COPIES IS PRECISELY THE SPIN-LIFT SIGN

Let a be the scalar by which alpha acts on N.  Compatibility with (2) gives

  a^2=-i.                                                      (6)

Hence a is an eighth root of unity of exact order eight.  The two possible
values differ by a sign, reflecting the two projective spin lifts alpha and
-alpha.

On W_(q,k), the line contributes a^(-k) to alpha.  Increasing k by four
changes this contribution by

  a^(-4)=-1,                                                   (7)

while (3), gamma and N64 remain unchanged.  Therefore

  W_(q,k+4) has alpha_(k+4)=-alpha_k                            (8)

with every other finite operator unchanged.

This sign is mathematically visible if the full source tuple contains a
fixed signed alpha and the N64 and gamma markings are held pointwise.  By
irreducibility, an intertwiner commuting with all of N64 is scalar, so it
cannot conjugate alpha to -alpha.  Equivalently, a Pauli conjugation that
also fixes gamma would need a Pauli label fixed by the fixed-free cubic
action, hence must be central and cannot change alpha.

The source spin construction, however, determines alpha only up to this
global sign.  The accepted native census likewise retains alpha and -alpha
as the unavoidable sign pair.  Under that stated equivalence, k=1 and k=5
represent the same finite source-to-native class.  This is an equivalence of
marked constructions with the unresolved spin sign, not a conjugacy of two
pointwise fixed operator tuples.


3. THE MARKED CURVE CANONICALLY TRIVIALIZES N^8

The period eight is not only a character-table coincidence.  On

  C: eta^2=1-w^6

the function eta is a local parameter at every q_a.  Define the nonzero
cotangent product

  Omega=product_(a=0)^2 d eta|q_a in Norm_Dq(K_C).             (9)

The cubic c leaves eta unchanged and only cycles the three factors, so it
fixes Omega.  At every q_a one has w^3=-1.  For

  Q:(w,eta) -> (1/w,-i eta/w^3),

the pullback of d eta at a branch point is i d eta.  Hence

  Q Omega=i^3 Omega=-i Omega,
  Q Omega^4=Omega^4.                                          (10)

The marked equation fixes eta up to its harmless sign.  Replacing eta by
-eta changes Omega by -1 and leaves Omega^4 unchanged.

The spin isomorphism S_0^2 isomorphic K_C gives

  N^8=(Norm_Dq S_0)^8 isomorphic (Norm_Dq K_C)^4.              (11)

Together, (9)-(11) give a c and Q invariant nonzero vector and therefore an
equivariant trivialization of N^8.  This construction is functorial in the
spin object.  Rescaling the chosen square-root isomorphism rescales both the
map in (11) and the induced identification of N^8 by the same factor; the
resulting trivialization is unchanged under isomorphism of spin objects.

No smaller positive power is equivariantly trivial.  Equation (6) says that
alpha has exact order eight on N.  In particular, N^4 carries the nontrivial
alpha sign -1.  The identity O(D_q)=S_0^3 supplies useful line isomorphisms,
but it cannot trivialize this nontrivial character.  Its scalar ambiguity
also does not disappear when applied directly to the constant norm line:
the cancellation by degree zero that made the spin-relative Poincare family
canonical is absent here.

Thus

  W_(q,k+8) is canonically equivalent to W_(q,k)                (12)

for the marked source geometry, while k and k+4 differ exactly by the spin
sign.


4. WHAT IS UNIQUE AND WHAT IS STILL OPEN

The result has three distinct levels.

  * With only the native central character fixed, k=1 modulo 4.

  * With the full signed alpha operator fixed, there are two residues modulo
    eight, k=1 and k=5, and they are distinct marked tuples.

  * With the actual source equivalence alpha equivalent to -alpha, these two
    residues form one finite-module class.  The canonical N^8
    trivialization removes all further representatives.

This proves uniqueness of the finite N64, gamma, beta and projective-alpha
package in the already stated equivalence class.  It does not prove a unique
tensor exponent in the boundary functor.  Tensor powers N^(-1) and N^(-5)
are different constructions before the spin-sign equivalence is imposed,
and the finite module forgets which construction produced its operators.

The smallest additional statement that would select literal k=1 is an
original source rule saying that the boundary or sewing operation contributes
one inverse evaluation norm.  An exact sequence, anomaly grading or local
gluing formula could supply such a rule.  None of the finite theta, N64 or
clock relations supplies it by itself.  No physical distinction between the
remaining representatives is asserted without that rule.
