ORIGINAL BOUNDARY NORM ORIGIN
The exact three-cabled seam determinant
2026-10-27

QUESTION

The accepted finite source construction has

    H_Theta = H^0(Jac(C),O(2 Theta)),
    W_q     = H_Theta tensor N_q^(-1),

where N_q is the permutation-free norm of the source-selected spin line S_0
over D_q=q_0+q_1+q_2.  Why should the original boundary operation contribute
exactly one inverse N_q, rather than a fitted line power?

RESULT

There is an exact answer at the relative Fredholm-line level.  It uses the
original seam multiplier itself.

On the already reconstructed genus-two source

    C: eta^2=1-w^6

one has the original covering coordinate

    z=eta/(1-w^3),            z^2=(1+w^3)/(1-w^3),      (1)

and therefore

    div(z)=D_q-D_p,           c*z=z,       Q*z=i z.      (2)

The old fullv21 seam gluing is exactly multiplication by
U_Gamma(theta)=exp(i theta), hence by z.  Over the open unit z-disc the
three-sheeted map C->P1_z is unramified and splits into three discs, centered
at q_0,q_1,q_2.  Pulling the original shift to the selected spin line S_0
gives an injective three-cabled Hardy shift whose cokernel is

    K_q = direct_sum_a S_0|q_a.                          (3)

Its Fredholm determinant line is the inverse evaluation determinant

    Det_Fred(z:S_0)^{} = det(K_q)^(-1)=B_q^(-1).         (4)

More generally, for every degree-zero family line L, compare the spin
cable S_0 tensor L with the otherwise identical reference cable L.  If B(E)
denotes the ordered evaluation determinant of E at D_q, their relative line is

    B(S_0 tensor L)^(-1) tensor B(L)
      = Nm_Dq(S_0)^(-1)
      = N_q^(-1).                                        (5)

All L fibers and all permutation signs cancel between the two determinant
lines.  For L=O_C this is B_q^(-1) tensor B_O.  Equation (5) is exactly one
inverse norm, fiberwise over the full Picard family; it does not require a
c-fixed L.
The exponent is the integer winding.  For multiplication by z^n the same
relative construction gives N_q^(-n).  The original scalar seam spectral
flow is one, so it selects n=1 and hence exactly one inverse norm.  This is
stronger than the finite quarter-clock test, which sees only a residue of n.
It does not turn the three-dimensional cokernel into three independent
physical insertions.

This closes the algebraic sewing question.  One physical-source premise
remains: the actual relative boundary field must be the S_0 cable in (3),
and the declared physical reference D_ref must induce the same degree-zero L
cable without the S_0 twist, as used in (5).  The original texts supply
that seam multiplier and a relative operator split, but they do not yet prove
this common-L spin-twist identification.  Thus (5) is an original-operation
reconstruction with a sharply stated field/reference premise, not yet an unconditional
identification of the full P1 operator.

1. THE ORIGINAL MAP AND ITS DIVISOR

The algebra in (1) is immediate from eta^2=(1-w^3)(1+w^3).  At
q_a=-omega^a, eta is a local parameter and 1-w^3 is nonzero, so z has a
simple zero.  At p_a=omega^a, eta is again a local parameter and
1-w^3 has a double zero, so z has a simple pole.  Hence

    div(z)=q_0+q_1+q_2-p_0-p_1-p_2.

The two source automorphisms act by

    c(w,eta)=(omega w,eta),
    Q(w,eta)=(1/w,-i eta/w^3).

Substitution gives c*z=z and Q*z=i z exactly.  Thus z is simultaneously

  * the cyclic quotient coordinate for c;
  * the original quarter-clock coordinate for Q;
  * the old seam multiplier exp(i theta) on |z|=1.

The branch values of w^3=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1) are z=1,-1,i,-i.  They lie on the
unit circle.  The open unit disc is therefore unramified.  Since it is simply
connected, its inverse image is the disjoint union of three discs, one about
each q_a.

This is the missing geometric reason that the old one-circle shift and the
three q evaluations belong to the same map.  It does not yet say which
coefficient bundle the physical field carries.

2. HARDY DEFECT AND THE ONE INVERSE LINE

Let H_q be the direct sum of the three holomorphic Hardy spaces of S_0 on
the lifted discs.  Multiplication by z is an isometry after the standard
boundary normalization and satisfies

    H_q / z H_q  ~=  direct_sum_a S_0|q_a              (6)

by evaluation at the three simple zeros.  No boundary Sobolev trace theorem
is needed for (6): on each disc it is the elementary exact sequence
0->zH^2->H^2->fiber_at_0->0.

2.1 THE HARDY NORM IS FIXED BY THE HALF-FORM

This Hardy realization does not require a fitted measure.  On each open lifted
disc, dz has no zero and the theta characteristic S_0 has a holomorphic
half-form e with e^2=dz.  For psi=f(z)e, and likewise after tensoring with a
unitary flat frame of L,

    integral_boundary |psi|^2
      = integral_|z|=1 |f(z)|^2 |dz|.

Thus the source spin structure supplies the ordinary Hardy norm, while the
unitary coefficient metric is the same in the relative and reference cables.
Changing e by its other square root changes no norm or determinant line.

At each of the four branch points on |z|=1, a local coordinate u has
z-z_0=a u^3.  Hence dz has a zero of order two and e has a zero of order one.
Those boundary points have measure zero in the Hardy norm and create no
additional interior evaluation defect or H^1 point term.  This proves the
natural half-form Hardy realization used in (6).  It does not identify that
Hardy polarization with the physical Calderon polarization of D_rel and
D_ref; that remains the operator premise stated in section 7.

The shift has no kernel and three-dimensional cokernel, hence

    index(z|H_q)=-3.

With the determinant-line convention

    Det_Fred(T)=det ker(T) tensor det coker(T)^(-1),

(6) gives B_q^(-1), exactly as in (4).  This is one line.  Its inverse power
is fixed by the cokernel orientation in the Fredholm determinant functor.

For any degree-zero line L, apply the same shift to S_0 tensor L and to
L.  Their cokernel determinants obey

    B(S_0 tensor L)=Nm_Dq(S_0) tensor B(L).

Consequently the relative Fredholm line is

    Det_rel(L)
      = Det_Fred(z:S_0 tensor L) tensor Det_Fred(z:L)^(-1)
      = B(S_0 tensor L)^(-1) tensor B(L)
      = Nm_Dq(S_0)^(-1)=N_q^(-1).                       (7)

Both ordered determinants acquire the same sign when the sheets are
permuted, so the ratio is the permutation-free norm automatically.  All
three L fibers cancel.  Thus (7) holds over the full Picard family, not only
at a c-fixed fiber.  The spin-relative Poincare normalization from the
accepted construction then makes the remaining N_q line constant over that
family.

2.2 INTEGER WINDING FIXES THE NORM POWER

For n>=1, multiplication by z^n has cokernel equal to the length-n jets at
each q point:

    H_q(E)/z^n H_q(E) ~= H^0(nD_q,E|nD_q).

Its natural filtration has graded pieces

    gr_j ~= direct_sum_a E|q_a tensor (T_q_a^* C)^j,
    j=0,...,n-1.                                      (8)

Take E=S_0 tensor L and compare with E_ref=L.  In the ratio of the two
Fredholm determinant lines, every L fiber, every cotangent/jet factor and
every sheet-permutation sign in (8) cancels.  Each value of j leaves one
copy of Nm_Dq(S_0)^(-1).  Therefore

    Det_rel(z^n; S_0 tensor L,L)=N_q(S_0)^(-n),
    index(z^n on each cable)=-3n.                       (9)

Downstairs the scalar shift by z^n has spectral flow n and index -n.  The
fullv21 source chooses the primitive class SF=1, so n=1.  Thus the original
integer winding fixes the literal power in (7).  The earlier finite
relations could only constrain n modulo 4, or modulo 8 after retaining the
Spin-lift sign; the Fredholm winding removes that residual ambiguity.

The executable audit is

    check_three_cabled_seam.py

with checked output three-cabled-seam.json.  It verifies (1), both symmetry
actions, and, for n=1,...,8, the explicit rectangular matrix of z^n on three
truncated polynomial sheets.  It computes rank, kernel, the 3n-dimensional
jet quotient, its c character, and the Q and beta determinant characters
zeta_8^(-3n) and i^n.  This finite calculation checks the algebraic exact
sequences and symmetry bookkeeping.  The Hardy/Fredholm passage and the
determinant-line identity are the analytic argument above, not a conclusion
drawn from truncation.  Normal Python and python -OO give byte-identical
output.

3. INDEX MINUS ONE IS NOT THE INVERSE NORM DEFECT

The old scalar circle shift has index -1.  The pulled-back S_0 cable has
three sheets and index -3.  These are different operators, and their
determinant orientations prevent a phase-only identification.

The quotient by c makes the distinction exact.  Set x=w^3.  Then

    C/<c>: eta^2=1-x^2,

and q_0,q_1,q_2 map to one unramified point qbar with x=-1.  The pushforward
F=rho_*S_0 has rank three and

    F|qbar=F_1 direct_sum F_omega direct_sum F_omega2,

its regular C3 representation.  Modifying the whole fiber drops the index by
three.  Modifying only the invariant character F_1 drops it by one.

But beta acts by +i on every q fiber.  Hence

    beta|F_1=+i,             beta|F_1^(-1)=-i,
    beta|B_q=-i,             beta|B_q^(-1)=+i.          (10)

The directed Fredholm line of the rank-one modification is F_1^(-1), so it
has the wrong beta and alpha-square character for N_q^(-1).  The undualized
F_1 has the desired phase, but it is the opposite determinant orientation.
The full three-fiber determinant succeeds precisely because
(i^3)^(-1)=+i.

Therefore the base index-minus-one seam defect is not already the inverse q
norm.  The positive construction is the full three-cabled pullback followed
by the relative determinant quotient (7).  This is a degree-three
pushforward, not an invertible identification of the old scalar operator
with the full defect.

The exact check is check_c3_quotient_defect.py, with checked output
c3-quotient-defect.json.  It verifies both indices and the determinant
orientation under normal Python and python -OO.

4. THE ORDINARY THETA EVALUATION SHORTCUT HAS THE OPPOSITE SIGN

This result must be distinguished from modifying one of the two positive
theta determinants by vanishing at D_q.  With

    D(E)=det H^0(E) tensor det H^1(E)^(-1),
    O(Theta)=D(E)^(-1),

and

    0 -> E(-D_q) -> E -> E|D_q -> 0,

one has

    D(E(-D_q))=D(E) tensor B_q^(-1).

Therefore

    D(E)^(-1) tensor D(E(-D_q))^(-1)
      = O(2 Theta) tensor B_q,                         (11)

which is N_q, not N_q^(-1), after removing the ordering line.  Its beta
phase is -i.  Conditioning both theta factors gives N_q^2 and phase -1.

The one inverse norm in (7) therefore comes from the determinant line of the
original multiplication shift and its relative reference.  It does not come
from imposing q vanishing inside one positive theta factor.

At D_p the analogous vanishing operation gives O(2Theta) tensor N_p and has
the desired scalar beta phase +i, but N_p is a different typed line.  The
source selects the q packet for the (4,3,2) triangle.  A scalar phase match
does not identify N_p with N_q^(-1).

The exact convention audit is check_boundary_norm_conventions.py, with
checked output boundary-norm-conventions.json.

5. RELATION TO ETA AND PFAFFIAN SEWING

The three-cabled proof above is a relative Fredholm determinant statement.
The general Dai--Freed theorem independently explains why a genuine Dirac
bordism amplitude contains one inverse boundary determinant line.  For an
odd-dimensional spin Dirac operator on X with boundary Y,

    tau_X in Det_Y^(-1),

and cutting introduces a pair L_Y tensor L_Y^(-1) which gluing contracts
once.  See Daniel S. Freed, Determinant Line Bundles Revisited,
https://arxiv.org/html/dg-ga/9505002, equations (2)--(3) and Theorems 5/8.

The primitive TFPT action contains +(i*pi/2) Delta eta_Sigma, suggesting the
real/Majorana square-root, hence Pfaffian, refinement.  That physical
application remains conditional because the September source itself treats
the same-chirality real Spin--Dirac symbol and Pfaffian structure as a premise,
not a completed identification of the raw seam operator.  Yonekura likewise
states that the Majorana case requires a square-root refinement but does not
carry it out in his Dirac derivation: https://arxiv.org/html/1607.01873,
sections 1.2, 3.2 and 4.3.

Accordingly, two levels must remain separate:

  * (7) is an exact complex relative Fredholm line for the original
    multiplier on the source-selected three-sheet cable;

  * identifying this line with the physical eta/Pfaffian boundary amplitude
    still requires the actual Spin--Dirac/Pfaffian field map.

6. ORIGINAL SOURCE CHECK

The relevant original passages are precise.

1. fullv21 section001, lines 225--250, cuts the double cover along the seam,
   declares the unitary seam gluing U_Gamma(theta)=exp(i theta), and computes
   one scalar spectral-flow crossing.  After the already reviewed orientation
   correction, its Hardy compression has index -1.

2. The September genus-two reconstruction gives
   w^3=(z^2-1)/(z^2+1), eta=z(1-w^3), eta^2=1-w^6 and the original Q lift.
   These printed equations are exactly (1)--(2), so the three-cabled map is

7. VERDICT AND NEXT DECISIVE TEST

PROVED FROM THE EXISTING SOURCE GEOMETRY:

  * the old seam multiplier is the same function z whose zeros are exactly
    D_q and whose poles are D_p;

  * its pullback to the three unramified q sheets has cokernel
    direct_sum S_0|q_a, index -3 and Fredholm line B_q^(-1);

  * the half-form identity e^2=dz gives the standard Hardy norm on every open
    sheet without a fitted measure; boundary ramification adds no point term;

  * relative to the otherwise identical cable L, with the spin twist S_0
    present only in the relative member, winding n gives exactly N_q^(-n)
    for every degree-zero L;

  * the original primitive spectral flow one sets n=1 and therefore fixes
    the literal single inverse norm, beyond the finite clock congruence;

  * the c-invariant rank-one quotient channel has index -1 but its directed
    Fredholm line has the opposite beta phase, so it is not N_q^(-1);

  * the ordinary mixed theta-determinant construction gives the opposite line
    N_q and therefore is not the origin.

REMAINING PHYSICAL PREMISE:

Prove on the actual boundary Cauchy-data space that

    (D_rel coefficient cable, D_ref coefficient cable)
       ~= (S_0 tensor L, L) on pi^(-1)(disc) for one common L,     (12)

with multiplication by the original z, and with the c,Q actions preserved.
Equation (12), not another line-power choice or a rank-one character
projection, is now the smallest decisive operator test.

If (12) holds, (7) derives the single inverse N_q factor directly from the
original boundary operation.  If D_ref induces a different coefficient line,
the relative factor is its actual defect determinant and need not be N_q^-1.
This is a concrete falsifiable distinction.
