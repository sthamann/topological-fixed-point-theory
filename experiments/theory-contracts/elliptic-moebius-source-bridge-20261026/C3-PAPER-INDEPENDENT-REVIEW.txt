INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE PRINTED C3 PUNCTURE PACKAGE
=====================================================

26 October 2026

Verdict
-------

The defect reported in EXISTING-C3-SOURCE-DICTIONARY.txt is real and occurs
in the displayed TFPT45 construction itself. It is not removable by reading
P as a different orientation convention.

Its scope is archival. The same defective display occurs in archived TFPT42
section 017, but the active TFPT2 source does not reuse its rigidity proof.
TFPT2 section 008, lines 87-114 explicitly counts the bare Z4 character
variety as positive dimensional, says that Z4 alone is not cohomologically
rigid, and requires additional full-D4 and determinant-branch data. The
finding below must therefore not be reported as an error in the current
TFPT2 rigidity statement.

The printed formula can be repaired with the already existing exact v117
pair, without introducing a new physical dictionary:

  M=M0_EXACT,
  U=U_EXACT=diag(1,i,-i),
  rho(gamma_(i^k))=U^k M U^(-k),     k=0,1,2,3.          (R)

This gives four monodromies in the required cusp conjugacy class and their
ordered product is exactly I. It recovers the exact algebraic v117 packet.

What it does not recover is the printed rigidity theorem as stated. Formula
(R) proves an explicit marked representation once (U,M) is fixed. The
displayed argument does not show that local spectrum, determinant triviality
and an abstract square symmetry select that pair uniquely. Nor does v117
turn its numerical ODE match into a symbolic analytic identification. The
minimal honest paper repair is
therefore an existence/construction theorem conditional on the fixed marked
v117 pair. A uniqueness theorem needs the additional anchor, residue and
resonance hypotheses from the later verification chain.


1. What the original actually requires
---------------------------------------

TFPT45 source extract 03, section 004, lines 6-13 chooses four positively
oriented puncture loops with

  gamma_1 gamma_i gamma_-1 gamma_-i=1.

Lines 35-44 then state literally

  rho(gamma_1)  =D,
  rho(gamma_i)  =P D P^(-1),
  rho(gamma_-1) =P^2 D P^(-2),
  rho(gamma_-i) =(rho(gamma_1)rho(gamma_i)rho(gamma_-1))^(-1),

where lines 15-31 define

  P=[[0,1,0],[0,0,1],[1,0,0]],
  D=diag(1,omega,omega^2).

Lines 65-91 explicitly interpret the same P as the fiber implementation of
the geometric quarter turn

  1 -> i -> -1 -> -i -> 1.

Thus the questioned formula is genuinely required by the original theorem;
it is not an inference added by the new report.


2. The exact internal failure
-----------------------------

For the displayed matrices,

  P D P^(-1)=omega D,
  P^2 D P^(-2)=omega^2 D.

Consequently

  D (PDP^(-1)) (P^2DP^(-2))
    =omega^3 D^3
    =I,

and hence

  rho(gamma_-i)=I.                                      (1)

This contradicts the theorem's own assumption that every puncture has local
spectrum {1,omega,omega^2}. It also shows why an orientation reversal does
not help: it can exchange omega and omega^2, but the product remains I.

There is a second, independent order mismatch. The base quarter turn has
order four, whereas P has order three. If P is interpreted merely as a
cyclic relabelling of the three eigenspaces, then it may explain the first
three displayed conjugates, but it no longer implements the fourth base
rotation. If it is interpreted as the actual fiber lift of the quarter turn,
P^3=I contradicts that role and Ad_P does not return D after four steps.
Therefore this is a mathematical defect, not harmless notation.

The proof at lines 95-107 only invokes the puncture product relation after
the first three assignments. It never checks that the resulting fourth
matrix remains in the prescribed local conjugacy class. Equation (1) is the
first failed implication.


3. Minimal exact correction from v117
--------------------------------------

The unchanged v117 file defines

  M = [[0,          -(1+i)/2, (1-i)/2],
       [-(1+i)/2,   -i/2,     -1/2    ],
       [(1-i)/2,    -1/2,      i/2    ]],

  U = diag(1,i,-i).

It verifies exactly

  U^4=I,
  M^3=I,
  spec(M)={1,omega,omega^2},
  (MU)^4=I.

Define

  M_k=U^k M U^(-k),             k=0,1,2,3.              (2)

Every M_k is unitary, has determinant one and has the required local
spectrum. Because U^4=I,

  M_0 M_1 M_2 M_3
    =M(UMU^(-1))(U^2MU^(-2))(U^3MU^(-3))
    =(MU)^4
    =I.                                                   (3)

In particular,

  M_3=(M_0 M_1 M_2)^(-1)=U^3 M U^(-3).                 (4)

Equations (2)-(4) are the precise minimal correction to the printed
monodromy formula. They preserve the same four labelled loops and the same
ordered flatness relation. Exact enumeration in v117 further gives

  <U,M> congruent to W(A3)=S4,       |<U,M>|=24.

This correction is entirely internal to the already supplied mathematical
package. It introduces no new field, state, geometry or physical map.


4. What must be weakened in the printed theorem
-----------------------------------------------

Replacing (P,D) by (U,M) repairs existence and all four local classes. It
does not prove the sentences "is forced" and "unique" in TFPT45 lines 33-47
and 111-160 from the premises printed there. This review establishes a proof
gap at that step; it does not claim a universal non-uniqueness theorem for
every strengthened D4 moduli problem.

The active source already makes this distinction correctly. In

  documents/tfpt_2_standard_model/sections/
  008-section-the-setup-parabolic-data-on-the-family-curve.tex:87-114

it gives dimensions 8 for the four-class character variety and 2 after the
bare Z4 reduction, explicitly rejects Katz rigidity for that bare system,
and names full D4 plus the determinant-trivial branch as extra input. Lines
115-172 further distinguish the parabolic and global holomorphic data from
single-fibre monodromy. The archived D/P defect therefore motivates the v117
existence correction; it does not overturn the active theory's scoped
non-rigidity statement.

The project's own later source distinguishes the needed extra information:

  * tfpt_research_contracts section 005, lines 270-293 records a
    two-parameter D4-fixed flat cusp branch before the anchor selection.

  * Lines 296-325 add the anchor residue and initially retain a numerical
    gauge-orbit uniqueness statement.

  * Lines 328-350 add the exact resonance condition a_13=0 and the fixed
    anchor moduli to obtain one gauge orbit inside that narrower ansatz.

  * v117 proves the exact algebraic properties of M and U. Its source header
    and status at lines 36-43 and 184-200 retain the identification with the
    analytic A0* monodromy as numerical.

The narrow corrected statement supported without further work is:

  Fix the simultaneous SU(3) conjugacy class of the marked pair (U,M) from
  v117. Then (2) defines a flat unitary four-puncture representation with the
  required local spectra, and it is the unique tuple attached to that fixed
  marked pair.

To claim selection from the original geometric premises, the theorem must
instead import and state the exact anchor/resonance hypotheses, and it must
retain the analytic identification status. Full D4 equivariance also needs a
marked reflection operator; (2) by itself supplies the C4 rotation part.


5. Scope relative to the new C3 dictionary report
--------------------------------------------------

The main negative conclusion of EXISTING-C3-SOURCE-DICTIONARY.txt survives:
the native E8 family permutation is only a bare C3-module match to M. The
corrected tuple (U,M) does not create a Q-equivariant or quarter-equivariant
intertwiner with the fixed native representatives, and it does not identify
the Fermat deck carrier with the v117 local system.

One sentence should be read narrowly: "restating the construction using the
actual v117 matrices" repairs the explicit monodromy package, but does not by
itself repair the paper's rigidity/uniqueness proof.


6. Independent spin-trace scope correction
-------------------------------------------

SPIN-TRACE-LOCAL-KERNEL.txt previously moved too quickly from a holomorphic
Szego half-form pushforward to transport of a positive CAR covariance. The
local calculation proves the bilinear holomorphic kernel and its determinant
Wick expressions. It does not establish a bounded or unitary one-particle
trace operator on an equal-time Hilbert space.

That report has now been corrected. A positive base CAR covariance would
additionally require a real slice, the one-particle measure and inner
product, a closed branch-domain trace T, and

  C_base=T C_E T^*,   C_base=C_base^*,   0<=C_base<=I,

together with the appropriate isometry or coisometry statement for T. The
square-root Jacobian proves the correct half-form transformation and the
local quarter principal part; it does not alone prove these Hilbert-space
properties.
