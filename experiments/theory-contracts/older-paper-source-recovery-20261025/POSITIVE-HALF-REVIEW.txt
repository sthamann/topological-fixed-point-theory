1. Spectrum and trace-norm convergence
--------------------------------------

Let

  psi_n(theta)=exp(i(n+alpha)theta),
  B_alpha psi_n=(n+alpha)psi_n.

For 0<alpha<1,

  P_+=1_(0,infinity)(B_alpha)

projects exactly onto psi_n with n>=0, and

  Pi=P_+-uP_+u*=|psi_0><psi_0|.

Define, for t>0,

  T_t^+=P_+ exp(-t|B_alpha|)P_+,
  R_t^+=exp(t alpha)T_t^+,
  s=exp(-t).

On P_+H their eigenvalues are

  T_t^+: exp[-t(n+alpha)],       n=0,1,2,...,
  R_t^+: 1,s,s^2,s^3,... .                              (1)

On the complementary subspace (1-P_+)H both compressed operators vanish.
Therefore, as operators on the whole H,

  ||R_t^+-Pi||_1=sum_(n>=1)s^n=s/(1-s),                 (2)

and the operator-norm error is s. The infinite zero block adds no trace norm
and no nonzero Fredholm eigenvalues. Thus (2) is exact on H, not only after
silently replacing H by P_+H.

2. Fredholm determinant and the null block
------------------------------------------

For every t>0, T_t^+ and R_t^+ are positive trace-class operators. If z is a
Fredholm parameter, then

  det_H(I-zT_t^+)
    =det_(P_+H)(I-zT_t^+|_(P_+H)).                       (3)

Indeed the complementary zero eigenvalues of T_t^+ contribute factors one
to I-zT_t^+. They do not create extra zeros or divergent multiplicities in
the Fredholm determinant.

Equation (2), together with continuity of the Fredholm determinant in trace
norm, gives

  det(I-zR_t^+) -> det(I-zPi)=1-z                       (4)

for fixed z. Hence the same canonical projector obtained from the positive
Toeplitz defect is also the ground-mode limit of the positive heat
compression.

3. Quarter products and the origin of the two gradings
------------------------------------------------------

Now set alpha=1/4 and

  q=exp(-t/4).

Since exp[-t(n+1/4)]=q^(4n+1),

  D_+(q):=det(I-T_t^+)
         =product_(m>=0)(1-q^(4m+1)).                    (5)

The negative spectral half has eigenvalues

  |n+1/4|=m+3/4,        n=-m-1,

and therefore

  D_-(q)=product_(m>=0)(1-q^(4m+3)).                     (6)

The full circle determinant factors exactly as

  D_full(q)=D_+(q)D_-(q)
           =product_(k>=0)(1-q^(2k+1)).                  (7)

Thus the congruence-one and congruence-three products are the positive and
negative spectral halves of the same quarter circle. The factor 1-q^3, which
caused the q^4 correction in the preceding full-circle review, is absent from
D_+.

4. First positive-half correction
---------------------------------

Let b=5/4. Compare

  Phi_+(q)=q D_+(q)^(-b)

with its rank-one truncation

  Phi_1(q)=q(1-q)^(-b).

The first omitted positive-half factor is 1-q^5. Hence

  D_+(q)^(-b)
    =(1-q)^(-b)(1+bq^5+O(q^9)),

and consequently

  Phi_+(q)-Phi_1(q)=bq^6+O(q^7)
                   =(5/4)q^6+O(q^7).                    (8)

An exact formal expansion confirms equality of all coefficients through q^5
and gives at q^6

  [q^6]Phi_+=24163/8192,
  [q^6]Phi_1=13923/8192,
  [q^6](Phi_+-Phi_1)=5/4.                               (9)

This does not modify the previously proved full-circle formula

  Phi_full(q)-Phi_1(q)=(5/4)q^4+O(q^5),                 (10)

because (10) retains D_- and its leading factor 1-q^3.
