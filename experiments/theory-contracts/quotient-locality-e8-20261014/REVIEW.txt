INDEPENDENT SCOPED REVIEW OF PROOF.txt
14 October 2026

VERDICT

PASS, with the scope qualifications already stated in PROOF.txt and sharpened
below.  The operator, common-time, Weyl-transport, and microscopic gap
obstructions are mutually consistent.  They rule out the proposed E8
completion on the unchanged charged-CAR/QWZ source Hilbert space while
preserving its state and time.  They do not prove a universal P1/P2 or TFPT
no-go.

1. KLM REFERENCE NET VERSUS THE ACTUAL QUARTER SOURCE

The KLM statement is correctly restricted to the vacuum-reference rational
net A0=V_(2Z).  In that setting the sector group is Z4, the eight-interval
canonical-endomorphism labels obey sum r_i=0 modulo 4, and the count/index is
4^7=16384.  The example r=(1,3,0,...,0) has spin 1/4 modulo 1 and correctly
shows that the full dual label set is not itself a bosonic local extension.
The uniform-parity intersection has 128 even plus 128 odd labels and gives the
abstract D8 union (s+D8)=E8 candidate.

No proof in the supplied material transports that index or canonical
endomorphism to the actual quarter-twisted source representation.  The phase
G_a can remove the twist on a proper interval after choosing a cut, so local
CAR relations and individual interval algebras away from that cut can be
identified.  This is the exact positive local correction.  It does not give
one periodic global gauge/unitary compatible with the whole complement, its
commutant, the vacuum modular data, and the transported source state.  Since
the multi-interval dual uses A(E')', those missing global data are precisely
what would be needed to identify the actual quarter-source index.  PROOF.txt
therefore correctly presents 4^7 only as a vacuum-reference theorem.

2. ACTUAL CARTAN CHARACTER OBSTRUCTION

After diagonalizing the cyclic fold, each actual component number generator
has integer spectral differences; a normal-ordering offset only multiplies
exp(2 pi i N_k) by a scalar and hence leaves its adjoint action equal to the
identity.  A spinor field with the required Ward charge p_k=+/-1/2 would obey

    Ad exp(2 pi i N_k)(S_p)=exp(2 pi i p_k) S_p=-S_p.

On the original Hilbert space the left side is S_p, so a nonzero such operator
cannot exist there.  This argument is valid for bounded operators directly and
for charged fields on the invariant finite-energy domain stipulated in the
proof.  A multi-interval intertwiner with total source charge zero is not thereby
an operator with the eight half-Cartan Ward characters.  This is the correct
operator-level distinction.

3. COMMON-CURRENT TIME AND THE EXTRA GROUND STATE

For alpha=1/4 and 3/4 the listed delta vectors are correct, every
|delta_k|<1/2, and sum |delta_k|=2.  For

    E(p,N)=||p||^2/2 + delta.p + N,   N>=0,

each nonzero integer coordinate contributes strictly positively.  Thus the
D8 integer coset has the unique zero p=0.  In the even spinor coset,
coordinatewise strict convexity uniquely selects

    p_*=(1/2,1/2,1/2,1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2,-1/2).

It has ||p_*||^2=2, sum p_*=0, delta.p_*=-1 and zero energy.  Hence the
stipulated multiplicity-one lattice E8 extension has exactly two ground states,
whereas the original even CAR module has one.  The factor of 8 between the
folded conformal normalization and the physical source normalization is
positive and does not affect this ground-space conclusion.  Simultaneously
reversing the charge and delta conventions sends p to -p and leaves the
spectrum, neutrality and degeneracy unchanged; no signed Q/H_a dictionary is
being inferred beyond that convention-invariant statement.

Since sum p_*=0, delta.p_*=mu.p_*=-1 for both alpha values.  Therefore its
cyclic clock character is exp(2 pi i mu.p_*)=1.  A uniform holonomy shift also
has zero pairing with p_*.  It cannot lift the partner.  A sector-dependent
constant could lift it only by changing the common spinor-current time law.

5. MICROSCOPIC QWZ GAP

For the stated free QWZ cylinder, mass 1 and width 8, the transverse Fourier
block satisfies exactly

    H(k)^2=diag(sin(k)^2 I+B*B, sin(k)^2 I+BB*)
          >= sin(k)^2 I.

At quarter-twisted momenta k=2 pi (j +/- 1/4)/N, the minimum absolute sine is
sin(pi/(2N)).  With the recorded physical scaling,

    g_N >= N sin(pi/(2N))/(2 pi) >= 1/(2 pi),
    liminf g_N >= 1/4.

Thus every finite-N one-particle operator is invertible, and its normal-ordered
Gaussian Fock sea is unique.  The six recorded calls to the original builder
(sectors 1 and 3; N=8,16,32; full dimensions 128,256,512) have no numerical
zero modes and scaled gaps

    0.24839671278605,
    0.24959859825891,
    0.24989961328741,

up to the displayed sector-symmetric roundoff.  These runs agree with the
analytic bound; the analytic block inequality, rather than the tolerance,
establishes absence of zeros.

The limit statement is valid for a common-Hilbert, source-state/time preserving
limit in which the same second-quantized positive-energy form, or equivalently
the uniform spectral inequality with the transported vacuum projection, passes
to the limiting generator.  Under that condition a composite/nonlinear word
cannot create an additional orthogonal zero-energy state.  Local field
convergence without preservation of this generator inequality would not by
itself prove the claim.  A different representation, interaction, source,
scaling, added sector, or sector-specific time is outside the obstruction.

6. GEOMETRY AND TEST ARTIFACTS

The original one-sided reflection double and a free half-period deck action
remain distinct geometric inputs.  Even after granting the free deck,

from an additional observable restriction or gauging rule; P1/P2 do not select
such a rule in the cited passages.

The three exact checker blocks pass.  In particular the corrected global

The six microscopic data records are internally consistent with the original

FINAL SCOPE

The proved negative is precise: the proposed multilocal E8 completion cannot
be realized on the unchanged known charged-CAR/QWZ source while retaining its
actual Cartan action, Hilbert space, quarter state, normal ordering, and common
physical time.  The result neither excludes a separately derived primitive
source with the required neutral carrier partner nor establishes a universal
P1/P2/TFPT impossibility.
