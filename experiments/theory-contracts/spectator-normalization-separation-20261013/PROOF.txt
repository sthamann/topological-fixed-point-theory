Exact finite result
Let H be a finite Hermitian operator, S the declared seam seed subspace,
P its projector, and H_c=H+cI for real c. The binomial identity and its inverse
show span{H^n S:n>=0}=span{H_c^n S:n>=0}. Also [X,H_c]=[X,H], so the joint
commutant of H and P is identical. Thus both seam-generatedness and the
relative-commutant criterion are insensitive to c. Meanwhile
Z_c(beta)=exp(-beta*c) Z(beta), rho_c(beta)=rho(beta), and
exp(-it H_c)=exp(-it c)exp(-it H). Hence all instrument histories built from
the same fixed instruments and normalized thermal state are unchanged:
scalar phases cancel at each density-matrix update. Absolute energy and
unnormalized partition response remain different. These identities hold for
every finite H, not just the two numerical samples.

Connection and next derivation
The no-spectator condition addresses additional inaccessible operator sectors.
The scalar-normalization condition addresses absolute response of the same
operator sector. They cannot be substituted for one another. A reconstruction
route must prove both minimality/cyclicity and its source-defined absolute
geometric normalization. Adding more normalized future observations alone
does not supply the latter. Before any geometric use, derive the common
off-shell operator/reference/domain family from the original source and check
whether an actual source principle fixes its reference. An arbitrary scalar
or metric-only term is not proposed as an allowed TFPT deformation.
