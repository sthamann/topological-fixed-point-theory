1. The first operator identification
-----------------------------------

Use the actual negative-energy-filled quarter representation, with CAR modes
a_r, r in Z, and occupation r<=-1.  The orientation of the original QWZ top
edge is transported by r=-j, so r+1/4=1/4-j.  This retains the physical
one-particle generator and the physical charge; it is not a replacement of
the state by an independently selected bosonic vacuum.

For a residue k=0,...,7 and integer n>=1 put

  h_(k,-n) = sum_(r congruent k mod8) :a*_(r+8n) a_r:.

The actual source stress mode, in folded normalization, is

  K = Lcal_-8/8
    = sum_r c(r) :a*_(r+8) a_r:,
  c(r)=(r+alpha+4)/8.

On any fixed finite-energy vector these raising sums have only finitely many
nonzero terms.  The CAR bilinear commutator therefore gives, without a
cutoff boundary term,

  [K,h_(k,-n)]
    = sum_(r congruent k mod8) [c(r+8n)-c(r)]
                              :a*_(r+8n+8) a_r:
    = n h_(k,-n-1).                                      (1)

There is no central term in (1), since both indices are raising indices.
On the actual sea, only r=k-8 crosses from an occupied to an empty level
in the first stress step.  Hence

  K Omega = sum_k delta_k h_(k,-1) Omega,
  delta_k=c(k-8)=(k+alpha)/8-1/2.                         (2)

Equations (1)--(2) derive the shifted oscillator recursion directly from the
original CAR operators.  They do not infer it from a matching dimension or
from a free choice of a Cartan vector.  With y_n=sum_k delta_k h_(k,-n),

  K^s Omega = s! [z^s] exp(sum_(n>=1) y_n z^n/n) Omega.  (3)

This follows by induction using [K,y_n]=n y_(n+1).  Every oscillator partition
at each level is retained.  In particular, the pure mode-minus-one term of
K^8 Omega has coefficient one and is y_1^8 Omega.
