1. Convention and energy-transfer signs
---------------------------------------

Use the mathematical CAR convention in which the Hilbert-space scalar product
is anti-linear in its first entry and a(f) is anti-linear in f:

  {a(f),a(g)^*}=<f,g>,  {a(f),a(g)}=0.                   (4)

If Kf=epsilon f, (1) gives

  alpha_t(a(f))=a(exp(i t epsilon)f)
               =exp(-i t epsilon)a(f),                  (5)

where anti-linearity supplies the minus sign. Thus a(f) has energy transfer
-epsilon. Taking adjoints gives

  alpha_t(a(f)^*)=exp(+i t epsilon)a(f)^*,               (6)

so a(f)^* has energy transfer +epsilon.

Let

  P_+=1_(0,infinity)(K),  P_-=1_(-infinity,0)(K).

Because zero is outside the spectrum, these two projections sum to one. From
(2), no bounded operator with strictly negative energy transfer can create a
nonzero vector from Omega. Equations (5)--(6) therefore give

  a(f_+) Omega=0       for f_+ in P_+ h,                 (7)
  a(f_-)^* Omega=0     for f_- in P_- h.                 (8)

For pure point vectors this is immediate: a nonzero vector on the left would
be an H-eigenvector of energy -epsilon<0. For general K, smear (1) with a
Schwartz function whose Fourier support lies inside the positive or negative
spectral half-line. The resulting bounded CAR operator has Arveson spectrum
strictly below zero. Spectral approximation inside P_+h and P_-h then yields
(7)--(8). The gap prevents a zero-energy remainder.

The requested signs are therefore exactly the stated ones:

  positive K mode:     a(f_+) annihilates Omega;
  negative K mode:     a(f_-)^* annihilates Omega.

2. Covariance and Wick rule follow from the CAR
------------------------------------------------

Define the ground-adapted annihilator

  b(f)=a(P_+ f)+a(P_- f)^*.                              (9)

Equations (7)--(8) say b(f)Omega=0 for every f. The original a and a^*
operators are linear combinations of b and b^*. Repeatedly move every b to
the right by the CAR. Every term with a b at the far right vanishes, and every
commutation step produces only a two-point contraction. Induction on the word
length gives:

  * all odd moments vanish;
  * every even moment is the signed Wick/Pfaffian sum of its two-point
    contractions.

The two basic contractions are, in the convention (4),

  omega(a(f)^* a(g))=<g,P_- f>,                          (10)
  omega(a(f) a(g)^*)=<f,P_+ g>.                          (11)

The anomalous contractions omega(aa) and omega(a^*a^*) vanish. Hence omega is
the gauge-invariant quasifree state with one-particle density P_-. Since every
CAR polynomial has now been fixed and such polynomials are norm dense in the
CAR algebra, the state is unique.

This is the zero-temperature ground-state form of the standard quasifree CAR
and Bogoliubov-automorphism framework. Primary source:

  Huzihiro Araki,
  "On Quasifree States of CAR and Bogoliubov Automorphisms",
  Publ. RIMS 6 (1970/71), 385--442,
  DOI 10.2977/prims/1195193913.

Araki supplies the state-symbol and Bogoliubov framework and characterizes
the corresponding quasifree equilibrium states. The short argument above is
included so that the present ground-state reduction does not rely on silently
replacing a finite-temperature KMS statement by its zero-temperature limit.

3. Why the full graded algebra is essential
-------------------------------------------

Take one CAR mode with {a,a^*}=1 and K=kappa>0. On the full algebra M_2,

  alpha_t(a)=exp(-i kappa t)a.

Positive energy forces a Omega=0, so the full-CAR ground state is the empty
state.

The even subalgebra is

  CAR_even=C 1 + C n,  n=a^*a,                           (12)

which is isomorphic to C direct-sum C. Its restricted dynamics is trivial,
because alpha_t(n)=n. Every state

  omega_lambda(n)=lambda,  0<=lambda<=1,                 (13)

is therefore a ground state of the restricted even system. In particular the
empty state, the filled state and all mixtures have the same even positive
time. The missing odd operator a is exactly what detects that the filled state
would allow a negative-energy transition.

Consequently an embedding of only the neutral current algebra, the even CAR
algebra or the F8 composite does not imply (3). The source bridge must contain
the odd local CAR fields and both adjoints.

The operator comparison is K_seed iota_1=iota_1 K on a one-particle core,
followed by its CAR-induced many-body time relation. It is not an equation

The smallest decisive source test is therefore:

Passing (i)--(iii) removes the independent state premise automatically by the
proof above. Failure already at (i) leaves only the even-algebra counterexample
