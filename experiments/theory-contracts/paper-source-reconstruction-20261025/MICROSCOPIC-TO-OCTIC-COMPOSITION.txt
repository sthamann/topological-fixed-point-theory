2. One-channel eightfold fold and the quarter convention
---------------------------------------------------------

On the continuum one-particle space define

  (F f)_r(x)=f((x+2 pi r)/8)/sqrt(8),  r=0,...,7.          (4)

This is a unitary reindexing of one field.  If

  f_(n,alpha)(theta)=exp(i(n+alpha)theta)/sqrt(2 pi),
  n=8l+k,  mu_k=(k+alpha)/8,

then exactly

  F f_(8l+k,alpha)
    =U_alpha(:,k) exp(i(l+mu_k)x)/sqrt(2 pi),

  U_alpha(r,k)=exp(2 pi i(k+alpha)r/8)/sqrt(8).            (5)

Consequently the folded covariance is

  U_alpha^* F C_alpha F^* U_alpha
    =diag_k C^-_(mu_k)                                    (6)

in the eigenchannel chart.  In the eight-arc chart the off-diagonal
correlations U_alpha diag(C^-_(mu_k)) U_alpha^* remain. The diagonal
eigenchannel representation has the derived graded tensor-product
factorization of this fixed state. It adds no freely chosen source copies.

The apparent alpha mismatch is only the stated occupation and orientation
change.  The QWZ edge uses modes exp(i(j-1/4)theta) filled for j>=1.  With
n=j-1 this is the positive-filled alpha=3/4 chart.  Reversing the boundary
orientation and writing m=-n-1 gives

  exp(-i(n+3/4)theta)=exp(i(m+1/4)theta),  m<=-1,           (7)

which is the negative-D alpha=1/4 sea used by the native Ward calculation.
Since m=-j, its one-particle generator is

  m+1/4=1/4-j=epsilon_j,

exactly the QWZ limiting energy appearing in (3), including its sign.
The channel label reverses k -> 7-k, and the point-split counterterms obey

  -delta_k^(3/4)=delta_(7-k)^(1/4),
  delta_k^(alpha)=(k+alpha)/8-1/2.                         (8)

The minus sign in (8) is accompanied by the creator/annihilator order and
the current-charge convention.  Flipping only the displayed c-number would
not define the same representation.  With this complete transport, the QWZ

4. Strong composition and the ordered ultraviolet limit
---------------------------------------------------------

Take N along compatible multiples of eight.  On every finite mode window
Pi_M, apply (5) to the residue classes j=8l+k and then transport those modes
through the exact polarized isometry I_N.  Equivalently, smear the actual
top-edge fields at the eight preimage arcs with the inherited QWZ seam
phases.  This is the finite restriction of the unitary F, within the single
microscopic one-particle space.  Its second quantization introduces no new
Fock factor.  The unitary fold and the fixed finite matrices in (9) preserve
the strong convergence (1), including adjoints and timed words.

A nonzero point split does not by itself turn an unsmeared fermion field
into a bounded CAR operator. The first draft of this section incorrectly
used the elementary finite-word theorem at that step. The independent review
identified the gap. SMEARED-OCTIC-ENERGY-BOUND.txt supplies its repair.

For the actual shifted current polynomial and its actual adjoint it proves

  ||F8_m P_E|| <= C(1+E+|m|)^11,
  ||F8(rho) P_E|| <= C_rho(1+E)^11,                       (11)

on the true positive-source-energy cutoff. Smooth rho makes the mode sum
absolute in Hilbert norm, and the adjoint estimate proves closability.
Every fixed output/internal/fermion mode cutoff is a finite original CAR
polynomial. Replacing its elementary Fourier modes by the actual QWZ fields
and first taking N->infinity therefore gives a strong transported limit;
removing those cutoffs afterwards yields the full smeared F8 and its
adjoint. These Fourier approximants have global support.

The local route instead separately smooths every elementary fermion with
a compactly supported mollifier of scale sigma. The original subtractions
must first be combined into the finite Wick/divided-difference expression,
including the derivative counterterms. At fixed sigma,epsilon>0 the smooth
CAR kernel has an absolutely summable Fourier-word expansion. The proof
then gives, for the field and its adjoint, the ordered local limit

  first N->infinity at fixed sigma,epsilon,
  then sigma->0 at fixed epsilon,
  finally epsilon->0.                                     (12)

The finite Wick expression has at most sixteen elementary fermions and
total conformal weight eight. The corresponding mode bound has polynomial
degree at most fifteen, absorbed by smooth rho. Fixed-regulator support lies
in shrinking neighborhoods of the same eight original preimage arcs.
No simultaneous lattice/ultraviolet limit or bounded point field is asserted.
