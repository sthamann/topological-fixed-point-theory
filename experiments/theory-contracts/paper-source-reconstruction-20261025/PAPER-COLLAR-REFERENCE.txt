PAPER SOURCE RECONSTRUCTION: COLLAR, REFERENCE AND LOCAL CAR IMAGE
=================================================================

25 October 2026. Read only audit of the current archived TFPT 4.5 sources.
Verdict: EXACT CATEGORICAL CORRECTION, ONE CONCRETE EMBEDDING PREMISE OPEN.
No Atlas, ledger or paper source was changed.

1. Result
---------

The previous requirement for one global unitary W identifying the complete
CAR source with a separately fixed P1 presentation is stronger than the
written operational seed definition.

An object of Seedop is specified directly by

  S=(A_loc,tau_t,Theta,omega,[u_Sigma],D_coll)

and by the four object conditions in the source. Unitary intertwiners occur
only as morphisms between complete objects. Therefore a global unitary to a
pre-existing P1 presentation is required to prove equivalence of two complete
seed presentations. It is not an object-membership condition.

The source also explicitly defines the physical admissible local net by
restriction to an invariant admissible Hilbert subspace. This supports a
local boundary-subsystem route for the even CAR source. The relevant map is a
state, Euclidean-time and reflection covariant local *-monomorphism, followed
by the induced isometry of OS/GNS spaces. It need only be unitary onto the
closed cyclic image generated by the CAR observables.

The archived paper does not construct the missing monomorphism from the
actual quotient CAR fold into its P1 admissible local net. Nor does it prove
that the resulting cyclic image carries the required elliptic collar and
finite relative action. This is the first remaining additional premise.

2. Exact source statements
--------------------------

Operational seed:

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/002-section-intrinsic-closed-branch-and-canonical-rigid-obje.tex
  lines 103--143

Lines 105--119 define Seedop objects and then say separately that morphisms
are unitary intertwiners. Lines 122--143 define the forward functor

  C_seed : Seedop -> SBord^adm.

The theorem starts from a complete seed. It is not an inverse reconstruction
of a seed from a boundary algebra or from a selected field.

The continuation at lines 146--248 essentializes bordisms, defines the defect
filtration and pulls it back by

  D_seed(S)=D(C_seed(S)).

The corollary at lines 227--243 applies only after a defect-minimal nontrivial
Seedop object has been supplied. It does not prove that C_seed is faithful,
that every minimal boundary datum has one seed preimage, or that the actual
CAR fold lies in that preimage. Physical operationality remains an axiom at
lines 245--248.

Admissible boundary category:

  same file, lines 250--306.

Its objects have an elliptic one-sided Dirac collar, reflection positivity,
finite relative action and nontrivial seam. Its morphisms are again unitary
intertwiners. Hence the category distinguishes direct admissibility from
unitary equivalence.

Physical admissible net:

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/001-section-primitive-core-and-carrier-interface-handoff.tex
  lines 302--342.

The source says that P_adm A P_adm is only shorthand for matrix elements.
The actual local net is

  A_adm(O)={ A|H_adm : A in A_M(O),
                         A H_adm subset H_adm,
                         A* H_adm subset H_adm }.

Thus the paper itself uses a reducing local subsystem. It does not require a
global identification of every operator on the ambient Hilbert space.

One-sided collar:

  same file, lines 42--80.

The primitive one-sided datum contains D_+ and B_Sigma in product form

  D_+=gamma_n(partial_n+B_Sigma).

The doubled reference/relative split is downstream of this one-sided datum.
The operational seed definition therefore requires a collar and finite
relative action, but not a separately displayed D_ref intertwiner as an
independent seed entry.

Here

and the reference sector is a fixed positive normalizing denominator. On a
Theta-compatible admissible sector, reflection positivity is inherited from
the admissible numerator. This does not establish finite spectral relative
action, but it shows that field-level OS positivity does not require global
operator equality with an independently presented D_ref.

OS/CAR reconstruction:

This theorem reconstructs the Hilbert space, positive-energy semigroup and

with P_adm by itself.

3. Boundary-subsystem proposition
---------------------------------

Let A_CAR^+(O) be the bounded even positive-time local CAR algebra of the
actual fold. Let A_P^+(O) be the positive-time algebra of one complete
operational seed S_P. Assume a compatible family of injective unital
*-homomorphisms

  iota_O : A_CAR^+(O) -> A_P^+(O)                         (1)

with the following properties:

  iota respects isotony and locality;
  omega_P(iota(A))=omega_CAR(A);
  iota(tau_CAR,t(A))=tau_P,t(iota(A));
  iota(Theta_CAR(A))=Theta_P(iota(A));
  iota carries the given seam action/class to the restricted P1 seam.

Write q_CAR and q_P for the OS sesquilinear forms on positive-time histories.
Define on the OS quotient of the CAR histories

  V_iota [F]_CAR := [iota(F)]_P.                          (2)

State and reflection covariance give

  q_P(iota(F),iota(G))=q_CAR(F,G).                        (3)

Consequently every CAR null vector maps to a P1 null vector, (2) is well
defined and extends to an isometry

  V_iota : H_CAR -> K_CAR,
  K_CAR=closure{iota(F) Omega_P}.                          (4)

Time covariance gives

  V_iota exp(-t H_CAR)=exp(-t H_P)V_iota                 (5)

on the reconstructed cyclic subsystem. Reflection and the seam action obey
the analogous restricted intertwining relations. This proof uses the OS
forms themselves. It does not assume that an arbitrary unbounded field acts
by left multiplication on the OS quotient.

If K_CAR is invariant under every local image operator and its adjoint, its
restricted net has exactly the form used in the paper's definition of
A_adm(O). Equivalently, the orthogonal projection onto K_CAR reduces the
represented image net. Equations (1)--(5) are sufficient for the local CAR/F8
boundary mechanism and its state, Euclidean time and reflection histories.

They do not yet make the CAR tuple a complete Seedop object. For that stronger
claim one must additionally exhibit a collar realization on the same image
such that:

  * K_CAR and the relevant collar domain are reducing for D_coll,P;
  * the restricted operator is still a first-order elliptic collar generator
    with reflection-regular positive side, rather than merely a self-adjoint
    compression;
  * the declared reference, eta/domain data and spectral profile give finite
    relative action on that restricted seed;
  * the nontrivial seam class is the same class carried by (1).

Under these extra hypotheses the restricted data can be checked directly as
a new Seedop object. This is a direct object proof. The written Seedop
category has only unitary morphisms, so the inclusion iota itself is not a
Seedop morphism unless it is onto a complete equivalent object.

4. Application to the supplied fold
------------------------------------

The first leg is already exact. For

  (F f)_j(x)=f((x+2 pi j)/8)/sqrt(8)

the fold audit proves that F is unitary on the one-particle space, preserves
the CAR and transports the state and excitation Hamiltonian by second
quantization. See

  experiments/theory-contracts/v16-operator-pullback-20261014/
  FOLD-AUDIT.md:10--86.

Thus Gamma(F) identifies the original cover CAR presentation with its
eight-component quotient presentation, including the fixed transported state
and Euclidean time. The octic field is already an affiliated even current
composite of that bounded CAR net, with the fixed point-split prescription.

What is not supplied is the second arrow

  quotient even CAR net --iota--> original P1 A_adm net. (6)

The paper names A_E, A_M and P_adm abstractly, but none of the cited source
theorems defines (6) on the actual fold generators or checks its state,
reflection and collar domains. C_seed cannot construct (6), because its input
already contains A_loc, tau, Theta, omega, the seam and D_coll.

The smallest decisive next test is therefore not a search for a global W.
It is one explicit bounded generator test for (6): choose an original even
CAR Weyl operator or bounded function of a smeared current and verify

  iota(A*)=iota(A)*,
  omega_P(iota(A*)iota(A))=omega_CAR(A*A),
  iota(tau_t A)=tau_t iota(A),
  iota(Theta A)=Theta iota(A),                              (7)

together with invariance of its cyclic image under A, A*, P_adm and the seam.
If (7) fails, the proposed source identification fails before any collar
analysis. If it passes on a generating bounded net, the next and only stronger
test is the elliptic collar/domain and finite-relative-action condition above.

5. Corrected hierarchy of claims
--------------------------------

Field mechanism:
  (1)--(5) on the bounded even local net are sufficient. A global unitary onto
  the whole P1 Hilbert space is unnecessary.

Complete operational seed:
  The same local image must carry a reflection-regular elliptic collar and
  finite relative action. This remains open for the supplied fold.

Equality with the canonical defect-minimal P1 seed:
  Only this final claim requires unitary equivalence of complete displayed
  structures plus the applicable minimality/classification theorem. Neither
  the local CAR map nor C_seed alone proves it.

Accordingly, the previous three global equations for Theta, D_coll and D_ref
are valid as a test of full presentation equivalence. They are not the first
necessary test of the CAR boundary mechanism and must not be used as the
definition of Seedop membership.

6. Scope and excluded shortcuts
--------------------------------

This audit does not claim that the actual quotient CAR fold is already the
P1 admissible net, or that its collar is already the archived P1 collar. It
does not choose a new product collar or a diagnostic zeta reference. It does
not use the disputed identification of Calderon polarization with geometric
reflection, and it does not use the false general claim that every bounded
coefficient first-order perturbation is infinitesimally operator bounded.

The exact advance is the categorical reduction from a global equivalence
problem to the local map (6), followed by one explicit collar admissibility
test. This is the strongest source-backed reconstruction statement present in
the current papers.
