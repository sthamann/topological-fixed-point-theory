PAPER-LOCAL-NET-DICTIONARY
==========================

Purpose
-------

This note records the strongest source-to-field map that is actually proved in
the existing TFPT papers, and separates it from the still conditional E8 local
net reconstruction.  It is a source audit.  It changes no Atlas or ledger
status.

Verdict
-------

The papers already contain a genuine microscopic-to-boundary field map.  It is
the charged scaling limit of one complex chiral CAR channel of the original
filled QWZ strip.  It preserves the filled-sea vacuum, both field adjoints,
integer charge, the microscopic time evolution, positive excitation energy,
and sampled spatial support on a common finite-energy core.

The papers do not contain a map from the scalar P1 datum itself to that QWZ
source, nor a same-source construction of the odd Z4 simple-current fields
which add the 128 spinor currents and generate the E8 extension.  The local E8
net theorem is therefore a rigorous conditional theorem whose remaining
antecedent is the support-preserving microscopic exhaustion condition ALG-EXH.
No global CAR to P1 bulk isomorphism is needed for the boundary theorem, but a
boundary source identification is still needed.

1. The proved microscopic CAR boundary map
-------------------------------------------

The active paper source

  paper-source:7296b452273b7022082ac2e2
  tex-artefacts/toe_research_20260909.tex, lines 16-96

fixes one concrete source, rather than an abstract free-field model:

  * the number-conserving QWZ strip with width 8, mass 1 and holonomy r=1;
  * its negative-energy projector P_N of rank 8N;
  * its actually filled sea Omega_N;
  * H_N = dGamma(N h_N/(2 pi)) - Tr(P_N N h_N/(2 pi));
  * Q_N = Number - 8N.

For a smooth periodic boundary test function g it defines the top-row vector

  (R_N g)(x,y) = N^(-1/2) exp(-i pi x/(2N)) g(x/N)
                 delta_(y,7) chi_+

and the actual microscopic field

  Psi_N(g) = a_N(R_N g).

These operators satisfy the exact CAR, Psi_N has charge -1, its adjoint has
charge +1, and both are supported on the sampled support of g.  They are not
auxiliary charge-register operators.

The paper constructs a one-particle isometry J_N from the chiral current modes
to projected source edge modes, with

  P_N J_N = J_N P_cur

exactly and an explicit O(rho_j) mode error.  Second quantization relative to
the filled sea maps the target vacuum, particles and holes into the same source
Fock space.  On every fixed finite-energy core it proves convergence of the
fields and their adjoints, charge intertwining, and the transported time law

  g_t(x) = exp(i t/4) g(x - t/(2 pi)).

The source commutator and transport estimates are uniform in N for each smooth
g.  Consequently every fixed finite field word and every corresponding vacuum
moment converges.  This is the existing local field, state and time
intertwiner.  Its target is one complex chiral CAR channel with integer charge.
It is not the compiler's sixteen real Majoranas and is not yet an E8 spin
field.

2. What the neutral multipoint result adds
------------------------------------------

The same source and filled projector give neutral four-word limits without a
change of dynamics:

  paper-source:cd8ffe37854d065f0df6c453
  tex-artefacts/toe_research_20260909.tex, lines 97-114.

Alternating words cancel the outer history ramps and normal ordering is the
finite-sea trace subtraction.  The result includes collision control in L1
for the stated four-point family.  The paper explicitly warns that pair-Gram
positivity is not Osterwalder-Schrader positivity.

A later synthesis derives, for eight declared identical and equally oriented
copies with product vacuum, the distributional weight-one pair kernel

  (1 - exp(2 pi i (b-a) - 0))^(-2).

Source:

  paper-source:10db59c23f40c37375a6f46d
  TFPT_Gesamtsynthese, lines 174-258.

This is a real common-source pair response.  The eight copies remain a stated
input.  The kernel does not determine zero-mode charge, cocycle, spinor
chirality, local exchange products or all mixed currents.  It therefore does
not by itself construct the odd E8 sectors.

The newly derived native octic F8 response belongs on this neutral/current
side of the dictionary: it is an even CAR current composite in the already
available source.  It neither creates the two odd 64-dimensional grades nor
proves microscopic exhaustion.

3. The strongest conditional local E8 theorem
----------------------------------------------

The charged MMST memorandum states the exact implication:

  paper-source:b09cb15941959108aaa64faa
  mmst_charged_scaling_limit_en, lines 45-100.

Let A be the neutral current net and let Psi_(k lambda,N), k in Z4, be the
charged candidate generators.  If the full local scaling algebra is exhausted
by A and these four grades, then the fields converge on a common finite-energy
core, the local algebras converge to a local conformal extension B, finite Z4
crossed products and expectations commute with the limit, and

  B is the strongly local lattice net A_(V_E8) = (E8)_1.

The logical split inside this implication is precise.

Proved pieces:

  * graph convergence and energy control for the edge-projected charged
    candidate fields;
  * relative locality against the neutral D5 plus A3 current net, from the
    integral pairings <lambda,alpha> in Z
    (paper-source:d83d12cab2865e92155c8a1c, lines 1-42);
  * the finite-group crossed-product and conditional-expectation limit;
  * preservation of compatible invariant states and their GNS expectations
    (paper-source:792aea785e4efca243045914, lines 308-366);
  * the fixed marked C4 extension and its E8 identification once a local,
    irreducible, common-stress-tensor extension exists.

Open antecedent:

  ALG-EXH requires, interval by interval,

    limsup_N B_N(I)
      subset W*(A(I), overline(Psi_(k lambda)(f)))
      subset liminf_N B_N(I),

  or equivalently the two phase-space and finite-energy approximation
  estimates written in equation (5.31) of
  paper-source:fa103420115caae1fcee2ec6, lines 83-212.

The paper supplies an exact one-mode counterexample to the earlier
Vandermonde shortcut: full CAR compression can be M_2 while the even/code
generated algebra is only diagonal.  Thus finite-sector counting cannot prove
the upper inclusion.  FE-GEN or an exact gauge/code-projected microscopic
algebra theorem is independently required.

Once L1/L2 hold, the endpoint is rigid.  For

  A = A_(D5,1) tensor A_(A3,1),  mu_A = 4*4 = 16,

the diagonal isotropic subgroup has order 4, so KLM gives

  mu_B = 16/4^2 = 1.

The common stress tensor, finite-dimensional L0 spaces and the lattice

  union_(k=0)^3 (D5 plus A3 plus k lambda)

then identify B with the unique rank-8 even unimodular lattice net E8.  This
argument is in paper-source:8f22ae2e47e6a9e2b2f0d308, lines 1-114.  It is
an endpoint theorem, not the missing microscopic realization.

4. Reflection, state and time are present in two different constructions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The filled-QWZ source has the actual source vacuum and time dynamics, but its
neutral paper proves pair-Gram positivity only and explicitly does not prove
OS positivity.

The WOIT beta_2 finite free seam model separately constructs a positive OS
quotient, a fixed vacuum, a positive self-adjoint transfer step and a compact

anti-unitary reflection.  Sources:

  paper-source:fbdc575f08b751529cc80783, lines 139-390.

That result is restricted to the finite free/quotient seam model.  The same
source says that the interacting holomorphic algebra is untouched and all
seven kill tests remain live there.  It therefore cannot be composed with the
QWZ-to-CAR field map as if the two source representations had already been
identified.

5. Exact relation to P1
-----------------------

In the architecture paper P1 is the scalar normalization

  c3 = 1/(8 pi),

given the geometric reading one half times the S2 Gauss-Bonnet coefficient:

  paper-source:7b58a2b8767cacd98fa2feb4, lines 201-243.

It does not specify a local algebra, a covariance, a vacuum, a Hamiltonian or
a field insertion.  The older claim that P1 forces the chiral edge is marked
conditional and explicitly leaves SEAM.EQUIV.01 open:

  paper-source:4b317ed30cf21a13d786c49d, lines 1-52.

The later P1/KMS statement is also conditional.  Even after the E8 net and its

additive transitive four-channel response:

  paper-source:fa1bb1505a5a15659ba5956c, lines 115-175.

The paper states explicitly that the net theorem does not make this
identification.

6. The smallest constructive closure target
--------------------------------------------

A global isomorphism between an entire 3+1-dimensional P1 bulk theory and a
free CAR theory is stronger than the boundary result needs.  The smallest
useful closure is one boundary functor on the existing collar, with four
simultaneous properties:

  (S) Source selection.  Derive from the original P1/P2 boundary data either
      the fixed QWZ collar and filled-sea covariance used above, or a local
      covariance in the same scaling class.  This must also derive the eight
      equally oriented copies if the eight-copy current construction is used.

  (C) Charged extension.  In that same source Hilbert space construct a
      support-preserving, renormalized inter-sector field for the odd Z4
      grades, with both adjoints, charge, cocycle, Clock/mark action, energy
      bound and time transport.  The paper identifies this exact missing
      source-to-source bridge in
      paper-source:0437442fcf03b22a6f32088b, lines 193-215.

  (E) Exhaustion.  Prove the two ALG-EXH estimates, so no bounded local
      ultraviolet sequence survives outside the neutral currents and the four
      charged grades.

  (R) Reflection on the same representation.  If the OS/KMS conclusion is
      required, prove reflection positivity and descent for this same QWZ and
      charged representation, rather than importing the finite free seam OS
      quotient.

Under (S), (C) and (E), the already proved conditional theorem supplies the
local 1+1-dimensional E8 boundary net, its vacuum, conformal Hamiltonian and
Z4 expectation.  Adding (R) supplies the desired same-representation OS/KMS
dictionary.  None of these steps implies a 3+1-dimensional bulk source or a
physical detector process.
