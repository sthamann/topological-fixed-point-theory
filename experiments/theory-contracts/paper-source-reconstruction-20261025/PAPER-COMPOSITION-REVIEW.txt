1. QWZ positive sea and the negative alpha=1/4 sea
--------------------------------------------------

This identification passes the smallest exact test.

The QWZ source modes are

  exp(i(j-1/4) theta),      j>=1 occupied.

With n=j-1 these are the positive-filled alpha=3/4 modes

  exp(i(n+3/4) theta),      n>=0 occupied.

Let R reverse the circle coordinate, (Rf)(theta)=f(-theta). With

  m=-n-1=-j

one obtains

  R f_(n,3/4)=f_(m,1/4),   m<=-1,
  m+1/4=1/4-j.

Thus the occupied positive half-line is mapped exactly to the negative-D
alpha=1/4 half-line, and the one-particle eigenvalue is the QWZ limiting
eigenvalue epsilon_j. Writing n=8l+k gives

  m=8(-l-1)+(7-k),

so the fold channel reverses as k -> 7-k. Direct rational evaluation gives

  delta_(7-k)^(1/4)=-delta_k^(3/4)

for all eight channels. This confirms equations (7) and (8), including the
time eigenvalue. The charge Q=N-N_sea is unchanged by the one-particle
orientation unitary, so annihilators retain charge -1 and creators +1.

One convention should nevertheless be made explicit in the proof. If the

  Gamma(R) :psi^* psi:_(C+)(theta) Gamma(R)^*

2. What the microscopic theorem actually proves
------------------------------------------------

For every fixed smooth L2 test function g, the elementary operators

  Psi_N(g)=a_N(R_N g),        Psi_N(g)^*

are bounded CAR operators. The source proves, along the isometries V_N,

  Psi_N(g)V_N psi -> V_N a(g)psi,
  Psi_N(g)^*V_N psi -> V_N a(g)^*psi

on each finite-excitation vector. It also proves the corresponding generator
limit on every fixed continuum energy cutoff. Because the elementary fields
are bounded for fixed g, the result extends by the ordinary telescoping
identity to a fixed finite word of fixed smooth smearings and to its adjoint.

This is genuine transported strong vector convergence. It is stronger than a
statement about compressed matrix elements. It is not standard strong
operator convergence on one fixed Hilbert space, because the microscopic
spaces vary with N; the displayed V_N formula is the correct precise meaning.

The quantifiers are essential. The theorem says:

  for each fixed finite list g_1,...,g_r of smooth functions,
  first take N -> infinity.

It does not cover N-dependent delta sequences, unsmeared point fields,
normal-order subtractions diverging with a regulator, or an unbounded current
polynomial obtained after a diagonal ultraviolet limit.

The full analytic statement is in

  experiments/theory-contracts/microscopic-charged-car-limit/README.md:
  41--68,70--117,119--226.

The registered paper excerpt states the same restriction as "fixed smooth
finite field words". Its checker supplies finite diagnostics; the all-N
statement rests on the analytic estimates in that README.

3. Decisive counterexample to the fixed-split bounded-word claim
----------------------------------------------------------------

Let eta_delta,x be a smooth approximate delta function on the circle with
integral one. Then

  ||a(eta_delta,x)||=||eta_delta,x||_2,

and for the usual scaling

  ||eta_delta,x||_2 proportional to delta^(-1/2).

The norm diverges as delta -> 0. Translating the field to x+epsilon leaves
this norm unchanged. Therefore epsilon != 0 separates two singular supports
from each other, but it does not make either psi(x+epsilon) or psi(x) a
bounded operator.

The same issue survives for currents. A smoothly smeared U(1) current is a
well-defined closable operator on the finite-energy core, but it is generally
unbounded. Its powers and the degree-eight current polynomial are likewise
unbounded. Calling the fixed-split expression a "fixed finite even CAR
polynomial" confuses the bounded CAR algebra generated by a(f), f in L2,
with the unbounded operator-valued distributions obtained after point
localization and normal ordering.

This invalidates the inference at MICROSCOPIC-TO-OCTIC-COMPOSITION.txt:
201--212 as written. Equation (11) does not follow directly from the finite
word theorem when the fields in its definition are point fields and only one
common centre smearing rho is displayed.

Smooth centre smearing may define the separated bilocal or multilocal field
as an operator-valued distribution after a separate continuum argument. It
does not turn that integral into one finite product of fixed elementary
smearings. One must prove the distributional integral or approximate it by
finite sums with a uniform estimate.

4. Minimal repair: three ordered limits
---------------------------------------

Choose one smooth mollifier eta and put

  eta_(delta,x)(y)=delta^(-1) eta((y-x)/delta),
  psi_delta(x)=a(eta_(delta,x)).

Every occurrence of a fermion field in the point-split formula must receive
its own translated mollifier. Define

  F8_(N,epsilon,delta)(rho)

by the same ordered fermion word, the same sea contractions, the same finite
derivative counterterms and the same matrices A,b, but with all elementary
fields separately mollified. The adjoint must use conjugated mollifiers,
reverse the word order and conjugate the subtraction constants.

The clean limit order is

  1. N -> infinity with epsilon>0 and delta>0 fixed;
  2. delta -> 0 with epsilon>0 fixed;
  3. epsilon -> 0 in the prescribed current-normal-order extension.

At stage 1 every integrand is a bounded finite CAR word of fixed smooth
smearings. The microscopic theorem applies. If the centre variable is
integrated rather than replaced by a finite sum, one also needs a uniform
fixed-regulator bound in x or an explicit Riemann-sum argument.

Stage 2 is a continuum operator-valued-distribution statement for separated
points. Stage 3 is the actual composite-field renormalization. Neither stage
follows from bounded-product convergence at stage 1.

For strong field and adjoint convergence on the common smooth-energy core,
the missing estimate should have the form

  ||F8_(N,epsilon,delta)(rho) psi||
   +||F8_(N,epsilon,delta)(rho)^* psi||
    <= C_(epsilon,delta,rho) ||(1+H)^s psi||

at stage 1, followed by continuum energy bounds that control the two regulator
limits in a common graph topology. The exponent s may be high for a
weight-eight field. It must not be replaced by the elementary bounded-field
estimate.

If only Wick kernels and fixed finite-energy matrix elements are controlled,
the valid conclusion is weaker:

  the ordered microscopic matrix elements converge to the established
  continuum current-normal-ordered distribution.

That statement is still useful and is enough for specified correlation
functions. It is not strong operator or strong graph convergence of F8 and
F8^*.

The continuum literature cited by the existing contract uses polynomial
energy bounds precisely to pass from formal vertex fields to closable smeared
operators and conformal nets. It cannot be invoked without checking that the
present F8 composite satisfies the required bound and without transporting a
compatible bound through the microscopic N limit. Relevant primary sources:

  Carpi, Kawahigashi, Longo, Weiner,
  From vertex operator algebras to conformal nets and back,
  arXiv:1503.01260;

  Carpi, Tanimoto, Weiner,
  Local energy bounds and strong locality in chiral CFT,
  arXiv:2103.16475.

5. Energy 64 and the Ward amplitude
-----------------------------------

This part survives the ultraviolet objection because it can be formulated
entirely with finitely many modes.

The continuum excitation energies are |j-1/4|. On H<=64 the possible labels
are exactly

  -63 <= j <= 64.

There are 128 one-particle labels, and the fermionic energy cutoff contains
only finitely many occupation patterns. Since

  M_N=min(floor(N/8),floor(sqrt(N))),

Gamma(Pi_M) is the identity on this continuum cutoff for N>=4096. The
polarized isometry I_N then intertwines every required truncated elementary
mode and its adjoint exactly. Replacing polarized modes by the actual top-row
modes contributes the already proved residual, which tends to zero for each
of the finitely many labels.

The fold relation

  Lhat_-1=Lcal_-8/8

therefore puts both the degree-eight F8 oscillator state and
(Lhat_-1)^8 Omega_d in original source excitation energy 64. The corresponding
matrix element is a fixed finite-mode calculation. It may be expanded into a
finite sum of elementary particle-hole CAR words before applying the QWZ
limit. In that form the fixed-word theorem does apply, including the adjoint.

Two scope qualifications should remain explicit:

  * H<=64 here is the finite-dimensional continuum top-edge excitation
    cutoff on which V_N is eventually isometric. It is not a claim that the
    entire microscopic QWZ energy-64 spectral subspace contains no bottom-edge
    or other states.

  * Convergence of this one finite-mode Ward amplitude proves a microscopic
    source matrix element. It does not by itself prove existence or strong
    convergence of the whole local smeared F8 field.

The exact arithmetic in equations (13)--(16) is consistent with the existing
native_folded_octic_origin.py certificate. No new E8 field construction is
needed for this retained amplitude.
