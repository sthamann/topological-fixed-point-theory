PAPER PRIMITIVE SOURCE SELECTION
================================

25 October 2026. Targeted original-source audit. No Atlas, ledger or paper
source was changed.

Verdict
-------

CONDITIONAL POSITIVE, WITH THE FIRST EXTRA PREMISE IDENTIFIED.

The full operational seed is much stronger than the scalar P1 statement. It
already contains

  S=(A_loc,tau_t,Theta,omega,[u_Sigma],D_coll).

Therefore, once a complete seed object is fixed, its local net, state, time,
reflection, seam class and collar operator are not still waiting to be chosen
from c3. This is the useful positive correction: a QWZ Hamiltonian may serve
as a regulator representative of that already supplied source. Its numerical
mass and holonomy parameters need not become new fundamental P1 constants.

The current papers do not yet prove that the fixed QWZ strip is such a
representative. The first additional premise is the full graded one-particle
source and time identification

  the local seed contains a(f), a(f)^* and the full CAR relations,

  the one-particle boundary generator induced by D_coll
       intertwines the free CAR one-particle generator K,

  alpha_t(a(f))=a(exp(i t K)f),

together with the already isolated local, time and reflection covariant
embedding of the quotient CAR net into the operational seed. On the same
reconstructed representation, H>=0 and H Omega=0 then force

  omega restricted to CAR = omega_P,
  P=1_(-infinity,0)(K).

Thus the quasifree state is a consequence, not a second premise. The direct
proof and the essential full-CAR qualification are recorded in
GROUND-STATE-SOURCE-REDUCTION.txt. Without the operator and time
identification, neither unit spectral flow nor APS/Calderon nor the Quillen
determinant line selects that state.

This distinguishes two statements which had been conflated:

  * fixed K -> one pure gauge-invariant quasifree ground-state symbol P;
  * gap + chirality + reflection + SF=1 -> a unique K.

The first is valid. The second is not established by the cited paper or by
v464.

1. What the original operational seed actually supplies
-------------------------------------------------------

The current archived source defines Seedop objects at

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/002-section-intrinsic-closed-branch-and-canonical-rigid-obje.tex
  lines 103--143.

Lines 105--119 put A_loc, tau_t, Theta, omega, [u_Sigma] and D_coll in the
object itself. D_coll is required to be first-order elliptic and reflection
regular. Lines 122--143 then construct the forward collar-completion functor
from a complete seed. Thus this theorem does not reconstruct a seed or a
quasifree covariance from SF=1.

The same source, lines 269--306, claims a defect-minimal boundary datum. Its
proof obtains existence of a minimum from well-foundedness and identifies the
first positive defect as |SF|=1. It does not classify all minimizers or prove
that the minimized stratum has one unitary-equivalence class. The sentence at
lines 304--305 invokes the boundary reconstruction after the full datum has
already been supplied. Hence it cannot turn SF=1 into a unique D_coll.

The one-sided boundary theorem is nevertheless useful. In

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/001-section-primitive-core-and-carrier-interface-handoff.tex
  lines 65--170,

the fully specified D_+=gamma_n(partial_n+B_Sigma) has a Calderon projector
P_C, and iC=2P_C-1 gives its Cauchy-data polarization. This is canonical
relative to the complete elliptic boundary problem. It does not say that P_C
is the covariance of omega, that A_loc is a CAR net, or that D_+ was selected
by the seam class.

The provisional APS role is stated even more explicitly in

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/002-section-intrinsic-closed-branch-and-canonical-rigid-obje.tex
  lines 33--42 ("APS roles on the admissible branch").

APS_dom^prov is an analytic starting representative. The later Calderon
upgrade produces APS_dom only after the doubled operator D_b is known. APS
therefore fixes a boundary realization for a supplied operator; it does not
choose the operator or its CAR state from SF=1.

2. P1, spectral flow and Quillen carry less information
--------------------------------------------------------

The registered P1 source is

  paper-source:7b58a2b8767cacd98fa2feb4
  documents/tfpt_1_architecture_e8/sections/004-source.tex:201--243.

It fixes the one-sided Gauss--Bonnet normalization c3=1/(8 pi). It does not
specify A_loc, a one-particle Hilbert space, K, P, omega or a field insertion.
The paragraph immediately preceding the P1 box calls the identification
"single scalar two-point kernel = quasifree seam state" a premise merge which
is recorded, not claimed.

Likewise SF=1 is an integer homotopy invariant of an operator family. It gives
net eigenvalue crossing, but not the eigenvalues, eigenvectors, localization
kernel or negative-energy projector of one representative. The operational
source itself consequently carries D_coll in addition to [u_Sigma].

The relative APS source states exactly what its package remembers:

  documents/archive--paper-latex--tfpt-42/sections/
  041-section-relative-aps-and-superconnection-setup.tex:1--12.

It starts with a declared pair (D,D_ref), seam boundary conditions and an eta
term for residual asymmetry. These are data about an already declared operator
pair. They do not invert the index and recover a unique pair.

The Quillen material does not add the missing inverse. The original
determinant-line discussion at

  documents/tfpt_1_architecture_e8/sections/007-source.tex:70--99

packages a U(1) determinant variation, counterterm and seam response. It is a
functional of an operator family after that family has been supplied. The text
also keeps the exact seam Quillen functional as ALPHA.QUILLEN.EXACT.01. A
determinant line records determinant curvature, phase and index information;
it does not determine the full spectral projector P or the local two-point
kernel. Thus APS plus Quillen plus SF=1 cannot replace the full CAR operator
and time identification. Once that identification is available, positive
energy removes the previously listed independent state premise.

3. The v463/v464 rigidity claim under the smallest decisive test
----------------------------------------------------------------

The changelog statement is registered as

  paper-source:209c76fdd7ea6b363778966e
  documents/changelog/sections/010-source.tex:272--308.

It says v464 reduces the realization to one-particle data. That reduction is
correct: a quasifree CAR state is determined by its symbol P. The stronger
claim that the original invariants fix P does not survive inspection of the
actual checker

  verification/v464_seam_oneparticle_rigidity.py.

The smallest test is simply to trace the checker's inputs and operator:

  * it accepts no D_coll, B_Sigma, P_C, SF class or QWZ Hamiltonian;
  * lines 60--69 choose the separate critical chain h(k)=sin(k) and define
    P_N by the occupation rule sin(k)<0;
  * lines 103--111 verify only that this chosen 0/1 occupation matrix is a
    Hermitian projection;
  * the exact dispersion has zeros at k=0 and k=pi, so this chosen chain is
    not the gapped K whose uniqueness the prose asserts;
  * lines 136--150 compute a Hilbert--Schmidt norm on one fixed finite window.
    Local-window finiteness is not the global Shale--Stinespring condition
    P-Q Hilbert--Schmidt.

Most decisively, v464 cites v426 for the assertion that K is fixed by gap,
chirality and reflection. But

  verification/v426_seam_lto_rp_kms.py:1--35,56--117

constructs one finite-dimensional K satisfying reflection oddness, mu4
commutation and a gap. It labels the result "constructively satisfiable" and
states twice that it does not prove the continuum realization. It contains no
classification or uniqueness theorem for K.

The earlier kernel checker is honest about the same boundary:

  verification/v113_quasifree_kernel.py:33--46,161--170

proves that one supplied polarization determines one quasifree vacuum, while
marking the premise that the seam state is quasifree as recorded, not claimed.

Therefore v464 establishes properties of one chosen symbol and illustrates
the Araki reduction. It does not prove that D_coll or SF=1 canonically selects
that symbol. The v463 holomorphic c=8 endpoint classification is unaffected;
it classifies the endpoint after the source realization has been furnished.

4. What remains positive for the QWZ source
-------------------------------------------

The microscopic theorem

  paper-source:7296b452273b7022082ac2e2
  tex-artefacts/toe_research_20260909.tex:16--96

is a valid source theorem for its declared regulator. It fixes the width-eight,
mass-one, holonomy-r=1 QWZ strip, its negative-energy projector P_N and filled
sea Omega_N, then proves the charged CAR field, adjoint, charge, support and
time limit. None of the criticism above changes that theorem.

Its exact QWZ numbers have two possible roles:

  A. Fundamental selection claim. P1 uniquely chooses width=8, mass=1 and
     r=1. No cited original theorem proves this.

  B. Regulator representative claim. The operational seed already supplies a
     source, and this QWZ strip is a local quasifree representative of the same
     polarized collar class. In this role its exact lattice coordinates do not
     need to be P1 constants. This claim becomes proved once the full graded
     local source and time embedding is proved on the same reducing subsystem;
     the ground-state reduction then supplies its covariance.

The existing paper supports B as the right target but has not completed its
antecedent. The source audit in PAPER-COLLAR-REFERENCE.txt narrows the missing
map to a local state, time and reflection covariant *-monomorphism from the
quotient CAR net into the operational seed, followed by the elliptic
collar/domain check. The refined test is that this map contains the odd fields
and both adjoints and intertwines the original reconstructed time with the
Bogoliubov dynamics of the same gapped K. Positive energy then derives

  iota_1 P_QWZ = 1_(-infinity,0)(K_seed) iota_1

or the corresponding covariance identity on the cyclic image. This equation
is now a consequence and diagnostic, not a separate state premise. The direct
argument is in GROUND-STATE-SOURCE-REDUCTION.txt. This is the smallest
decisive test of the actual charged field source and does not ask P1 to
reproduce a lattice mass value.

The transverse strip width eight must also not be confused with the later
eightfold quotient fold. The latter is a unitary residue-class reindexing of
one continuum CAR channel. Equality of the two integers is not a source
selection theorem.

5. Exact claim hierarchy
------------------------

PROVED:

  * A fixed gapped one-particle K determines the pure gauge-invariant
    quasifree ground-state symbol P=1_(-infinity,0)(K).
  * A supplied complete operational seed contains net, state, time,
    reflection, seam class and D_coll.
  * A supplied elliptic D_+ determines its Calderon projector.
  * The fixed QWZ representative has the charged CAR scaling limit stated in
    paper-source:7296b452273b7022082ac2e2.

CONDITIONAL:

  * QWZ mass, holonomy and finite-width choices are regulator coordinates
    rather than fundamental P1 data, conditional on the full graded CAR/time
    intertwiner and collar admissibility.

NOT PROVED BY THE CITED ORIGINALS:

  * SF=1 uniquely fixes D_coll or its covariance;
  * gap, chirality and reflection uniquely fix K;
  * the archived source establishes the full graded CAR/time embedding needed
    to conclude that omega equals the spectral-projector quasifree state;
  * APS or Quillen reconstructs the QWZ source;
  * P1 selects width=8, mass=1 and r=1.

The first actual additional premise is therefore not a numerical QWZ
parameter and no longer an independent state choice. It is the full graded
CAR operator-and-time identification on the actual local boundary subsystem.
Positive energy then forces the covariance. Passing that test would make QWZ
a genuine regulator representative of the original seed and would remove the
demand to derive its microscopic coordinates as new P1 constants.
