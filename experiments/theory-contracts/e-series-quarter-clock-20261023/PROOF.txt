1. Root slices and E7

For alpha in Phi define Phi_alpha = Phi intersect alpha-perp. This is E7.
The root inner-product distribution is {-2:1,-1:56,0:126,1:56,2:1}.
At Lie-algebra level the associated root grading is
  e8 = 1[-2] + 56[-1] + (e7 + C)[0] + 56[1] + 1[2].
This is the standard E7+A1 branching, not a new physical identification.
The signs alpha and -alpha define the same E7 slice, giving120 slices.

Orthogonality and J^2=-I give (alpha,Jalpha)=0. Hence J interchanges
Phi_alpha and Phi_Jalpha. They differ since Jalpha belongs to the first
but not the second. Thus there are60 exchanged pairs and no J-fixed
root-normal E7 slice. Independently, no J-invariant E7 root subsystem
can exist: its real span has odd dimension7, while J^2=-I forces every
invariant real subspace to have even dimension.

Any orthogonal root pair in E8 is Weyl equivalent to e0+e1,e0-e1.
Indeed Weyl(E8) is transitive on roots, and the Weyl(E7) stabilizer of
the first root is transitive on the roots perpendicular to it. In this
chart the common perpendicular roots are exactly +/-ei +/-ej with
2<=i<j<=7, giving D6 and60 roots. The normal plane contains only
the four roots +/-alpha,+/-Jalpha, hence A1+A1.

2. Literal bridge to the existing60 Gaussian reflections

For norm-two alpha the real root reflection is
  s_alpha(v)=v-(v,alpha)alpha.
Since alpha and Jalpha are perpendicular,
  r_alpha=s_alpha s_Jalpha
         =I-alpha alpha^T-(Jalpha)(Jalpha)^T.
It is -I on the normal real plane and +I on its common perpendicular.
It commutes with J and is an order-two complex reflection on C4.
With h(v,alpha)=(v,alpha)+i(v,Jalpha), linear in the first argument,
  r_alpha(v)=v-2 h(v,alpha)/h(alpha,alpha) alpha.
This is term by term the original v634 S1 Gaussian reflection formula.
The identity of operators, rather than equality of group orders, identifies
the generated group with the existing G31 realization.

The following sets are in explicit bijection:

  the60 pairs {Phi_alpha,Phi_Jalpha};
  the60 common D6 root subsystems;
  the60 operators r_alpha.
Each plane has exactly4 roots, so there is no extra multiplicity. The
complex-linear reflections recover the plane as their -1 eigenspace;
the common D6 span recovers the plane by orthogonal complementation.
All240 root shells and all60 operators are checked in exact doubled

3. E6 slices and the quarter-clock obstruction

For alpha,beta with (alpha,beta)=-1, their6 roots form A2. Its orthogonal
root complement in E8 is E6 with72 roots. All1120 A2 root subsystems
are enumerated without random sampling. None is J invariant; J forms
560 pairs. The corresponding E6 intersections have12 roots in640
subsystem cases and24 roots in480 cases. Those counts alone are not
used to claim an intersection type or an isometry classification.

A stronger all-root-subsystem obstruction needs no enumeration. If J
preserved an E6 root subsystem, it would preserve its root lattice K,
its dual, and its discriminant group K*/K=Z3. Every automorphism of Z3
squares to identity. But J^2=-I induces nontrivial inversion on Z3, a
contradiction. The same argument applies to A2. Thus no E6 ROOT subsystem
can be fixed by this J, including any other root-subsystem embedding.
This says nothing about arbitrary non-root Lie-subalgebra embeddings.

8. Intrinsic square curves, covers and the precise descent theorem

Each Gaussian root line ell=C alpha has exactly four roots. The function
  f_ell(v)=(v/alpha)^4-1
is independent of the root representative alpha, since its replacements
are i^k alpha. Thus ell with its point at infinity carries an intrinsic
square four-mark set and the cover w^2=f_ell(v). This construction is
functorial for actual lattice isometries commuting with J. It gives60
embedded charts of the SAME marked modulus, not60 different moduli.
The60 original reflections act transitively on these lines, verified
from their actual permutations. Therefore the unmarked(E8,J) data do
not equivariantly select one chart. Retaining the full family is valid
and needs no added unique-chart axiom.

Now separately SUPPLY the existing base model X=CP1 minus mu4 and its
rho(gamma_i)=J representation. Its C2 parity character has a connected
degree2 cover Y:y^2=x^4-1, with the four branch points removed. The
compactification has genus1 by Riemann-Hurwitz and j1728 from harmonic
cross ratio. Its restricted fibre holonomy is{I,-I}. Every CHOSEN real
E7 or E6 root subsystem is invariant under that remaining holonomy.
On X the chosen E7 descends as the unordered pair of exchanged slices;
Y orders the pair, and the common D6 already descends individually.
This repairs any inference that lack of a fixed J-sector forbids a
transported reduction. It does NOT make real rank7 into a complex subspace.

The removed points matter. A peripheral loop upstairs projects to
gamma_i^2 and has fibre holonomy-I; this flat bundle does not extend
across them as an ordinary flat bundle on the compact elliptic curve.
The full kernel cover z^4=x^4-1 has compact genus3 and trivial restricted
holonomy. For w^n=x^4-1,
  g=((n-1)*3-gcd(n,4)+1)/2,
so n=2,3,4 gives1,3,3. Infinity ramifies for n=3, not n=2 or4.
The existing TFPT w^3=x^4-1 cover explicitly retains infinity as an
extra branch point in v617 and its original TeX account. It is not the
degree4 kernel cover just because both have genus3.

The algebraic deck is d(x,y)=(x,-y). The two lifts of base x->ix are
R_pm(x,y)=(ix,+/-y), with square(-x,y), not d. Likewise, an odd path
lift representing fibre J must not be equated to constant deck action:
canonical deck equivariance on a pullback bundle squares to identity,
whereas J^2=-I is compensated by the restricted loop holonomy.

The parity cover alone admits eight C4 monodromy lifts on the ordered
four-punctured base: gamma_i->J^(1+2b_i), with sum b_i even. Requiring

these describe the SAME original closed seam must specify and intertwine
its actual loop and peripheral markings. A cover degree or genus alone
