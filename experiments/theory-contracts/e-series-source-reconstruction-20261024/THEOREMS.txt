Canonical reconstruction from the fixed marked C4 glue

0. Verdict and exact scope

Retain as proved input, without changing or rederiving it, the native compiler

  K = D5 + A3 subset L,
  H = <(s,f)> subset K*/K,
  L = union_(a in H) (K+a) = E8,

the fixed sector labels, the original epsilon convention, the compiler and
cover actions, and

  phi_base = (4/3)c3 = 1/(6 pi).

The reconstruction class considered here is the following precise one. Put

  U = V_D5 tensor V_A3 = V_K.

An object of Ext_H(U) is a connected local unitary VOA extension W of U,
with the same conformal vector, whose underlying labelled U-module is

  W = direct_sum_(a in H) M_a                              (0.1)

with every M_a occurring exactly once. An isomorphism in Ext_H(U) restricts
to the identity on U and preserves each labelled summand M_a. It may multiply
a simple summand by a scalar.

THEOREM. Ext_H(U) has exactly one isomorphism class. It is represented by
V_E8. The corresponding normalized connected commutative Q-system has
exactly one unitary equivalence class. Its residual automorphisms over U are

  Hom(H,U(1)) = Hom(C4,U(1)) = C4.                         (0.2)

Thus the four residual choices are character gauge, not four extensions.
Within this exact class the vacuum, conformal vector, L0, unitary structure,
local products and vacuum correlation functions are fixed up to the stated
cocycle and character gauges. No spectator is left, and the extension is
generated over U by the sector M_(s,f), equivalently by its weight-one space.

This is a mathematical reconstruction theorem from the already established
marked C4 glue. It does not prove that primitive P1 data furnish U, its
positive-energy representation, its local fields, or this reconstruction
class. No 3+1 dimensional or physical source identification is asserted.


1. The pointed category and the fixed Lagrangian subgroup

The irreducible modules of the positive definite lattice VOA V_K are indexed
by the discriminant group

  A = K*/K = (D5*/D5) x (A3*/A3) = C4 x C4.

All sixteen modules are simple currents and have categorical dimension one.
Choose the spinor generator s of D5*/D5 and the fundamental generator f of
A3*/A3. Their discriminant quadratic forms, written as lowest conformal
weights modulo Z, are

  q_D5(0)=0, q_D5(s)=5/8, q_D5(2s)=1/2, q_D5(3s)=5/8,
  q_A3(0)=0, q_A3(f)=3/8, q_A3(2f)=1/2, q_A3(3f)=3/8.       (1.1)

Let h=(s,f). The fixed diagonal subgroup is

  H = {0,h,2h,3h} = C4.

Equation (1.1) gives actual lowest weights, not only residues,

  Delta(M_0)=0,
  Delta(M_h)=5/8+3/8=1,
  Delta(M_2h)=1/2+1/2=1,
  Delta(M_3h)=5/8+3/8=1.                                  (1.2)

Consequently q restricted to H is zero modulo one. The pointed modular
category is nondegenerate, |A|=16 and |H|=4. Hence

  |H-perp|=|A|/|H|=4,

and isotropy H subset H-perp implies H=H-perp. Thus H is Lagrangian. This is
also the lattice statement that the index-four overlattice associated with H
is even and unimodular. The fixed input identifies it with E8.


2. Exact sector content and the 248 weight-one states

Use the standard level-one labels for D5 and A3. The four H sectors and their
lowest finite-dimensional representations are

  sector   U-module                              Delta   dimension at Delta
  0        (1,1)                                  0      1
  h        (16,4)                                 1      64
  2h       (10,6)                                 1      60
  3h       (bar(16),bar(4))                       1      64.              (2.1)

The vacuum sector contributes at weight one

  (M_0)_1 = (45,1) + (1,15),

of dimension 45+15=60. Combining this with (2.1) gives the exact branching

  (V_E8)_1 restricted to so(10) + sl(4)
    = (45,1) + (1,15) + (16,4) + (10,6)
      + (bar(16),bar(4)),

  248 = 60 + 64 + 60 + 64.                                (2.2)

This is sector by sector. It is not an inference from the number 248.


3. The associator obstruction and why it vanishes on H

First note that every multiplication channel in an object of Ext_H(U) is
nonzero. The invariant Frobenius pairing of a connected unitary extension is
nondegenerate, so the component M_a tensor M_(-a) -> M_0 is nonzero for every
a. If some component M_a tensor M_b -> M_(a+b) vanished, associativity after
multiplication by M_(-b) would give zero in the bracketing (M_a M_b)M_(-b),
whereas the bracketing M_a(M_b M_(-b)) contains the nonzero unit action on
M_a. This is a contradiction. Hence each one-dimensional fusion space below
is used by a nonzero intertwiner.

For a,b in H choose a unit-norm fusion isomorphism in the unitary pointed
category (hence a unitary basis intertwiner)

  I_(a,b): M_a tensor M_b -> M_(a+b).

Every relevant fusion space is one dimensional. Comparing the two triple
products and comparing exchanged products gives scalars

  F(a,b,c) in U(1),  Omega(a,b) in U(1).

The pentagon and the two hexagons say precisely that (F,Omega) is a normalized
abelian 3-cocycle. This datum must not be discarded. The full pointed category
on A has nontrivial quadratic trace, for example

  Q(s,0) = exp(2 pi i 5/8) != 1,

so its associator and braiding cannot be made globally trivial.

The Eilenberg-Mac Lane trace theorem sends an abelian 3-cocycle class to

  Q(a)=Omega(a,a).

For a VOA module category this trace is the conformal spin

  Q(a)=exp(2 pi i q(a))                                    (3.1)

in the standard convention. Using the inverse convention changes no
conclusion below. The trace map is a bijection from abelian 3-cohomology to
quadratic forms. By (1.2), Q(a)=1 for every a in H. Therefore the restriction
of the abelian class [(F,Omega)] to H is zero. This conclusion uses the full
abelian trace theorem. It is not the false statement that ordinary
H^3(C4,U(1)) vanishes; in fact

  H^3(C4,U(1)) = C4.                                      (3.2)

Because the restricted abelian class is zero, there is a normalized
2-cochain mu:H x H -> U(1) such that replacing

  I_(a,b) by mu(a,b) I_(a,b)

makes both F and Omega equal to one on H. Thus associativity and locality are
obtained simultaneously. The explicit E8 lattice vertex operators furnish
one such trivialization, so existence does not depend on an abstract
obstruction calculation alone.

Suppose mu and mu' are two coherent trivializations. Their quotient eta is a
normalized ordinary 2-cocycle. Two quotients differing by a coboundary give
isomorphic algebra objects, by rescaling M_a with a 1-cochain. For arbitrary
nonzero VOA multiplication constants use C* coefficients; for unitary
normalized trivializations use U(1). In both cases

  H^2(C4,C*) = H^2(C4,U(1)) = 0.                         (3.3)

An elementary proof of (3.3) is useful. A projective lift T of a generator
has T^4=zeta for some zeta in U(1). Choose rho with rho^4=zeta^(-1). Then
(rho T)^4=1, and the projective cyclic action linearizes. Hence every
2-cocycle is a coboundary. The same proof works for C*, since a nonzero
complex number also has a fourth root. Thus algebraic uniqueness does not
assume that an arbitrary VOA multiplication was already normalized to unit
modulus. Section 4 supplies the unitary equivalence of normalized Q-systems.

These scalar coefficients concern only the local C4 extension algebra.
They do not replace Hom(L,Z2), its nontrivial group action, or the fixed
character-valued G31 lift extension. No global sign-extension splitting
follows from (3.3).

It follows that there is one algebra isomorphism class. The 1-cocycles that
stabilize its multiplication are the four characters of H. They act by

  v in M_(kh) |-> chi(kh) v,

and give precisely (0.2).


4. VOA and Q-system classification

The hypotheses of Huang, Kirillov and Lepowsky apply to U=V_K:

  * U is simple and of CFT type, with U_0=C1.
  * Positive definite lattice VOAs are regular, hence their modules are
    completely reducible, and their module category has the required vertex
    tensor category structure.
  * All irreducible module weights are nonnegative, and only U has weight
    zero.
  * The object direct_sum_(a in H) M_a is haploid because M_0 occurs once.
  * Its twist is trivial because all four conformal weights are integral.

HKL Theorem 3.2 therefore identifies the required VOA extensions with
haploid commutative associative algebra objects of trivial twist. Section 3
proves that there is exactly one such algebra on the fixed labelled object.

Existence and identification are concrete. For the even overlattice L=E8,
the standard lattice decomposition is

  V_L = direct_sum_(a in H) V_(K+a)                       (4.1)

as a V_K-module, and the lattice vertex operators give the multiplication.
Thus the unique extension is V_E8, as an extension of the marked U.

The unitary statement is equally exact. The unitary representation category
of a positive definite lattice VOA is a C*-tensor category. The algebra in
(4.1) is rigid, connected and commutative. Carpi, Gaudio, Giorgetti and
Hillier, Theorem 3.9, proves that algebra equivalence classes of rigid haploid
algebras correspond one to one to unitary equivalence classes of normalized
haploid Q-systems, and that an equivalence between normalized haploid
Q-systems is automatically unitary. Hence the single algebra class above is
also exactly one normalized local Q-system class.

For the isomorphism over the fixed base U, use their Theorem 4.7 directly.
Positive definite lattice VOAs and their tensor products are regular and
completely unitary (their Table 1 and Corollary 4.11). Theorem 4.7 says that
a VOA isomorphism of simple unitary extensions restricting to the identity
on such a base is unitary; the extending scalar product is unique. Thus the
algebraic isomorphism fixing U obtained above really is an isomorphism in
the stated unitary extension class. Their Theorem 4.10 and Corollary 4.11
also identify these normalized local Q-systems with the corresponding
irreducible local conformal-net extensions over A_U.


5. No spectator, generation and holomorphicity

No spectator is an automatic conclusion only inside Ext_H(U). Equation
(0.1) fixes the entire underlying U-module and its multiplicities. An extra
tensor factor, extra sector or repeated sector would change that object and
leave the stated class.

The Q-system multiplication has nonzero components

  M_(ah) tensor M_(bh) -> M_((a+b)h).

In the explicit lattice representative these are the nonzero lattice
intertwiners. Uniqueness transports this property to every object of the
class. Therefore U together with M_h generates M_2h, M_3h and M_0 and hence
the whole extension. Since U is generated by its weight-one currents and the
three nontrivial sector tops all have weight one, V_E8 is generated by
(V_E8)_1.

There is also a categorical completeness check. The pointed category has
global dimension 16 and the algebra has categorical dimension 4. The local
module category has

  Dim(C_A^local) = 16/4^2 = 1.                            (5.1)

Thus the extension is holomorphic. Neither (5.1) nor the no-spectator
statement follows if one merely asks for an extension containing the four
sectors without fixing the whole module object and multiplicities.


6. Canonical state space, vacuum, conformal vector and L0

For the now forced lattice L=E8, the standard state space is

  V_L = M(1) tensor C_epsilon[L],                          (6.1)

where M(1) is the rank-eight Heisenberg Fock space and C_epsilon[L] is the
twisted group algebra

  e^alpha e^beta = epsilon(alpha,beta)e^(alpha+beta).

The normalized lattice cocycle satisfies

  epsilon(0,alpha)=epsilon(alpha,0)=1,
  epsilon(alpha,beta)/epsilon(beta,alpha)
    = (-1)^(<alpha,beta>).                                (6.2)

Two normalized cocycles with the same commutator (6.2) differ by a
coboundary. Rescaling e^alpha by the corresponding 1-cochain gives a VOA
isomorphism. Thus epsilon is gauge data, not another extension choice. The
original supplied epsilon is one fixed representative and remains unchanged.

The unit of the connected algebra forces the vacuum

  1 = 1_M(1) tensor e^0.                                  (6.3)

For an orthonormal basis u_1,...,u_8 of L tensor R, the full-rank inclusion
K subset L forces the same Heisenberg conformal vector on U and V_L:

  omega = (1/2) sum_(j=1)^8 u_j(-1)u_j(-1)1.              (6.4)

It has central charge 8. On a monomial state

  u_(i1)(-n1)...u_(ir)(-nr)e^alpha,

the conformal Hamiltonian is

  L0 = n1+...+nr + <alpha,alpha>/2.                       (6.5)

Equations (6.3)-(6.5) leave no independent vacuum, conformal vector or L0
choice in the marked lattice reconstruction class.


7. Exact vacuum n-point functions

Let alpha_1,...,alpha_n be lattice charges in L and take the radial domain

  |z_1| > |z_2| > ... > |z_n| > 0.

For the exponential ground fields the vacuum function is

  <1, Y(e^alpha_1,z_1)...Y(e^alpha_n,z_n)1>
    = delta_(alpha_1+...+alpha_n,0)
      C_epsilon(alpha_1,...,alpha_n)
      product_(i<j) (z_i-z_j)^(<alpha_i,alpha_j>),         (7.1)

where the ordered cocycle factor is

  C_epsilon(alpha_1,...,alpha_n)
    = product_(r=1)^(n-1)
        epsilon(alpha_r,alpha_(r+1)+...+alpha_n).          (7.2)

If a bimultiplicative representative is used, (7.2) becomes

  product_(i<j) epsilon(alpha_i,alpha_j).

Charge conservation in (7.1) is exact. Since L is integral, all exponents
are integers and mutual locality has no branch ambiguity. All ground fields
in all four H sectors are covered by taking alpha_i in the corresponding
cosets K+kh. Linear combinations follow by linearity. Correlators with
Heisenberg oscillator descendants follow uniquely by Wick contractions and
derivatives. Therefore (7.1), the Heisenberg two-point function and the VOA
identities determine every vacuum n-point function; there is no additional
state functional in this class.


8. Unitarity, radial reflection and the strong-local net

The positive definite lattice construction has its normalized invariant
Hermitian form and PCT operator Theta. In a compatible cocycle gauge

  Theta(e^alpha)=kappa(alpha)e^(-alpha),  |kappa(alpha)|=1.

For a primary lattice vector e^alpha of weight

  d=<alpha,alpha>/2,

the radial adjoint relation is

  Y(e^alpha,z)^dagger
    = (-1)^d conjugate(z)^(-2d) kappa(alpha)
      Y(e^(-alpha),1/conjugate(z)).                       (8.1)

The gauge-independent general identity is the invariant adjoint formula

  Y(a,z)^dagger
    = Y(exp(conjugate(z)L1)
        (-conjugate(z)^(-2))^L0 Theta(a),
        1/conjugate(z)),                                  (8.2)

interpreted on the finite-energy domain. For any radially ordered product F
of fields supported in the unit disk,

  <1,F^dagger F 1> = ||F1||^2 >= 0.                       (8.3)

This is the precise radial-reflection condition. Raw complex correlator
values are not individually required to be positive.

V_E8 is generated by its weight-one currents, as proved in Section 5.
Carpi, Kawahigashi, Longo and Weiner, Theorem 8.3, states that a simple
unitary VOA generated by weight-one fields, with the allowed Virasoro
generators, is strongly local. Hence V_E8 produces its canonical irreducible
strongly local conformal net A_(V_E8). This is a chiral conformal net result;
it is not a 3+1 dimensional field theory statement.


9. Naturality for the original sign-extension lifts

Let ghat be any of the original epsilon-preserving lattice-field lifts. Its
action has the defining form

  ghat[h_1(-n_1)...h_r(-n_r)e^alpha]
    =(gh_1)(-n_1)...(gh_r)(-n_r)
      eta_g(alpha)e^(g alpha),                            (9.1)

with eta_g(0)=1 and with the original character carry retained under
composition. Formula (9.1) fixes the vacuum (6.3). Since g is a lattice
isometry, it fixes the quadratic tensor in (6.4), hence

  ghat omega=omega,
  [ghat,L_n]=0 for every n,
  [ghat,L0]=0.                                             (9.2)

Every character correction D_chi fixes the neutral Heisenberg sector,
the vacuum and omega, so (9.2) also holds for every corrected lift. Therefore
the transition functors and the associated-source construction of
MATH.SEAM.ESERIES.JOINTSOURCE.05 remain functorial after adjoining the now
canonical vacuum, conformal vector and L0. No new cocycle or section is
needed.

The earlier E6 nonselection also survives. The existing involutive lifts of

  d=diag(1,1,1,1,1,1,-1,-1)

with masks 126 and 127 are lattice VOA automorphisms commuting with the fixed
Jhat and chat. By (9.2) they also fix the newly explicit vacuum and conformal
vector and commute with L0. Their action on the four E6 vertices remains
fixed-point-free. Thus adding the canonical state and conformal dynamics does
not manufacture a preferred E6 corner.


10. The compiler clock is not conformal time rotation

On the complexified weight-one Cartan space

  H_C = {h(-1)1 : h in L tensor C},

the fixed Gaussian lattice operator Jhat acts through J. Its spectrum is

  Spec(Jhat|H_C) = {+i with multiplicity 4,
                    -i with multiplicity 4}.              (10.1)

By contrast, conformal rotation acts on every homogeneous weight-m space as

  exp(i theta L0)|V_m = exp(i theta m) I.                 (10.2)

In particular its restriction to H_C is the single scalar exp(i theta), for
every theta. Equations (10.1) and (10.2) prove

  Jhat != exp(i theta L0) for every theta.                 (10.3)

Both operators preserve conformal grade, and Jhat commutes with L0, but they
have different actions within weight one. A base or peripheral quarter turn
must therefore not be identified with conformal time evolution merely from
its order. This is an exact mathematical distinction, not a physical no-go
claim.


11. Independent c=8 uniqueness route and its hypotheses

There is a second, less marked route to the same abstract VOA. Dong and Mason
prove that a strongly rational holomorphic VOA of CFT type with central
charge 8 is isomorphic to V_E8. Here strongly rational includes simplicity,
rationality, C2-cofiniteness and the usual CFT-type/self-contragredient
hypotheses, while holomorphic means that the VOA itself is the only
irreducible module.

This theorem is a useful cross-check after holomorphicity and c=8 have been
established. It does not follow from central charge 8 alone. It also does not
recover the fixed embedding U=V_D5 tensor V_A3, the marked sector labels or
the character gauge. The C4 proof above is therefore the direct uniqueness
theorem for the supplied compiler object.

13. Final boundary

The established D5+A3 compiler plus its fixed diagonal C4 glue removes the
lattice VOA, vacuum, conformal vector, L0, local product, normalized unitary
Q-system and vacuum-state functional as independent mathematical choices
inside Ext_H(U). It leaves only cocycle representative gauge and the four
sector characters.

The earliest additional premise remains the choice of Ext_H(U) itself: a
positive-energy local unitary chiral VOA realization of the compiler with
exactly the four marked sectors and multiplicity one. The primitive P1
boundary kernel has not been proved to supply that premise. The result is
therefore a complete conditional reconstruction from the already derived C4
glue, while the derivation of that chiral realization from primitive P1
remains a separate origin problem.
