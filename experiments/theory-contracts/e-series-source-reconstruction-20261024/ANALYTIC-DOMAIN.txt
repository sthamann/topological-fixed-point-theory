MATH.SEAM.ESERIES.SOURCE-RECONSTRUCTION.06 / analytic-domain audit and repair
24 October 2026

Verdict

The universal infinitesimal-boundedness assertion in the original
thm:well-posed-primitive-dynamics is FALSE. A valid same-domain theorem
requires the actual relative bound to be smaller than1, or a verified
bounded zeroth-order perturbation. No conclusion is made here that the
intended special TFPT operator has no valid realization.

1. The original assertion and the invalid step

Boundary-source section001, lines353-376, chooses a compact self-adjoint
Dirac anchor A with a self-adjoint elliptic modified APS boundary condition.
It asserts that every symmetric bounded-coefficient differential B of
order<=1 is infinitesimally A-bounded. The proof continues at section002,
lines1-30. Its valid estimates are

  ||psi||_H1 <= C(||A psi||+||psi||),
  ||B psi|| <= C1 ||psi||_H1+C0||psi||.

They imply only

  ||B psi|| <= C1*C ||A psi||+(C1*C+C0)||psi||.

The coefficient C1*C is fixed. It cannot be replaced by an arbitrary
epsilon>0. Same differential order gives graph boundedness, not relative
bound zero.

2. Counterexample within the stated modified APS boundary class

On H=L2([0,2*pi]), let

  A=-i*d/dx,
  Dom(A)={f in H1([0,2*pi]): f(0)=f(2*pi)}.

Integration by parts shows that the adjoint has exactly the same periodic
trace condition, so A=A*. Its orthonormal eigenbasis is

  e_n=(2*pi)^(-1/2) exp(i*n*x),  A e_n=n e_n, n in Z.

The tangential boundary operator in this one-dimensional example is0 on
the two endpoints. Its kernel is the complete C2 boundary space. The
periodic trace line L={(z,z)} is Lagrangian for the Green boundary form,
so this is precisely a self-adjoint modified APS condition. It is also
the elliptic transmission condition obtained by cutting the circle.

Take B=-A on the same domain. It is symmetric, first order, and has
constant bounded coefficients. Its exact A-relative bound is1: any bound
with coefficient a gives |n|<=a|n|+b, so a>=1, while a=1,b=0 works.
In particular the estimate with epsilon=1/2 is impossible.

Moreover A+B is zero on the proper dense domain Dom(A), whereas its
adjoint is zero on all of H. It is not self-adjoint on the original
domain and is not closed there. Thus the theorem's general same-domain
conclusion also fails, not just the proof of relative bound zero.

This is a counterexample to the generic mathematical theorem. It is not
presented as a full P1/P2 physical model.

3. Reflection oddness does not lower the differential order

The original split is D_rel=(D-U_tau D U_tau^-1)/2. Once a unitary bundle
lift of the geometric reflection is actually specified, its principal
symbol satisfies

  sigma1(D_rel)=1/2(sigma1(D)-sigma1(U_tau D U_tau^-1)).

Thus D_rel has order zero iff the two first-order symbols agree. Projecting
onto the reflection-odd part alone proves no such equality.

For example, on the doubled circle let D=-i*d/dx and U_tau f(x)=f(-x).
Then U_tau D U_tau^-1=-D, so D_ref=0 and D_rel=D is first order. The fixed
set is{0,pi} and the quotient is an interval. A different specified bundle
or Pin lift may change the symbol parity. The source does not specify
that lift or a principal-symbol equivariance identity; the actual intended
special case therefore needs this check.

Formal symmetry also does not fix the operator domain. If a reflection is
used to compare two closed realizations, it must preserve or explicitly
transport the APS boundary subspace, including the kernel Lagrangian.

4. Correct conditional replacement

Let A be a self-adjoint compact elliptic anchor with the chosen self-adjoint
D-elliptic boundary condition. Suppose Dom(A) is contained in Dom(B), B is
symmetric on Dom(A), and

  ||B psi|| <= a ||A psi||+b||psi||,  a<1.

Then A+B is self-adjoint on Dom(A) by Kato-Rellich. The two graph norms are
equivalent, hence the compact inclusion of Dom(A) into H persists and the
sum has compact resolvent as well.

A reflection-specific sufficient condition is:

  (i) a specified unitary reflection lift preserves the chosen domain;
  (ii) sigma1(D)=sigma1(U_tau D U_tau^-1);
  (iii) the resulting order-zero D_rel has bounded self-adjoint coefficients.

Then B=D_rel is bounded symmetric on H, so a=0 and the same-domain result
follows. If the first-order symbols do not cancel, an actual a<1 estimate
must instead be derived. The graph estimate by itself is insufficient.

There is a second notation check before applying any replacement: the
carrier-source section006, lines271-273, later defines D_geo=D_ref+D_rel=D.
Thus D_geo+D_rel is not simply the reconstructed D. One must name whether
the anchor is D_ref, D_geo, or a separately declared operator, and apply
the estimate to that actual pair. Renaming the operator cannot remove the
domain and relative-bound requirement.
