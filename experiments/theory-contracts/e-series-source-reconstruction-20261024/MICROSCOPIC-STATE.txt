Question. In the ORIGINAL finite v988 collar, do its particle-hole
symmetric covariance and its half-filled occupied frame define the same
state? This is a necessary bookkeeping check for a proposed state-preserving
microscopic map, not a substitute for constructing that map.

Object and scope. Use the unchanged qwz_cylinder, occ_frame and fermi_cov
functions at Nx=16, Ny=6, M=1 and twists r=0,1,2,3. The algebra is the
finite complex matrix CAR algebra on192 one-particle modes. No P1 origin,
continuum vacuum, edge-sector selection or physical time is inferred.

1. Exactly two periodic zero modes

For r=0, on vectors constant in x, the x hoppings cancel the on-site
term: M*SZ+TX+TX*=0. The two y-boundary vectors are

    lower boundary: (1,-1),     upper boundary: (1,1),

constant in x, zero on every other y. They obey TY (1,-1)^T=0 and
adjoint(TY) (1,1)^T=0.
The checker uses the unambiguous matrix equations and verifies both
real and imaginary parts over integers, with Gram32*I2.

The doubled matrix2H has entries in Z[i]. Reduction modulo65537 with
i mapped to256 is a ring homomorphism, because256^2=-1 modulo65537.
Its modular rank190 proves rank over Q(i) at least190. The explicit
two-dimensional kernel proves rank at most190. Thus rank(H)=190 and
nullity(H)=2 exactly. For each r=1,2,3 modular rank192 proves exact
invertibility. These are exact rank certificates, not eigenvalue tolerances.

For periodic H, S=(I tensor sigma_x) obeys S conjugate(H) S=-H.
More generally, for EVERY twist the real orthogonal matrix
R=I_x tensor(reflection_y tensor i*sigma_y) obeys R H R^T=-H.
The checker verifies these identities separately over Gaussian integers.
Thus the periodic190 nonzero eigenvalues pair in opposite signs, giving
95 negative,95 positive and2 zero eigenvalues; each other twist has
96 negative and96 positive eigenvalues exactly.

2. Two distinct state prescriptions

The original fermi_cov uses

    C=1_(H<0)+(1/2)1_(H=0).

The original occ_frame instead chooses the96 lowest orthonormal
eigenvectors and gives a projection P. At r=0 it occupies all95 negative
modes and one of the two zero modes. Let Pi0 be the rank2 zero projection
and Q a rank1 projection in it. Then exactly

    C-P=(1/2)Pi0-Q,
    ||C-P||_HS^2=1/2,
    ||C-P||_op=1/2,
    C-C^2=(1/4)Pi0,
    ||C-C^2||_HS^2=1/8.

Thus C and P define different finite gauge-invariant quasifree states.
C uses the maximally mixed zero-mode occupations, whereas P makes a pure
Slater choice. There is no numerical eigenbasis convention that makes
these two covariances equal. At the three nonperiodic twists, the exact
negative count96 and exact absence of zero modes make these prescriptions
agree. The numerical diagnostics match the displayed exact periodic
formulas within1e-10.

3. Consequence for the current source task

The original code ALREADY distinguishes these functions. This result is
not a bug claim against fermi_cov or occ_frame. It rules out silently
replacing the stipulated periodic half-zero-mode covariance by an occupied
half-filled frame in a purported state-preserving dictionary.

The earlier energy diagnostic used the r=1 Slater state, where both
prescriptions agree; its result remains unchanged. A continuum construction
can retain the mixed state, choose and justify a sector, or derive another
appropriate representation. Which procedure the original B_N uses is part
of its required state/sector identification. This finite distinction is
not a nonexistence theorem for B_N and not a disproof of the mathematical
E8 extension.

Verdict: PASS for this exact finite state comparison; fundamental source
