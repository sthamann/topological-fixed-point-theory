The current architecture document, documents/tfpt_1_architecture_e8/sections/

2. Correct minimization theorem

Let C:S -> B be any functor and d:B -> N^k an isomorphism-invariant defect,
with the original lexicographic order on N^k.
Write d_seed=d o C. The unconditional conclusion is

  s minimizes d_seed iff C(s) minimizes d on im(C).

Proof. Both comparisons are exactly comparisons of values d(C(s')).
Passing from the image to all B needs an extra condition: at least one
global minimizer must lie in im(C). Even then, recovering s uniquely needs
classification of the relevant fiber of C, not just its image.

Consequently well-foundedness proves existence of a minimum defect vector
in any nonempty allowed image. It does not prove that its fiber has one
isomorphism class. A nonempty subset of the positive integers has a least
element k>=1; it need not contain1. Unit spectral flow requires either its
own axiom or an admissible unit-flow witness.

The manuscript makes the first statement correctly at section002,
lines194-216: uniqueness is reduced to classification of the last stratum.
It does not provide that classification in the later proof at269-306.
The proof at238-243 also replaces minimization over im(C_seed) by all
admissible bordisms without proving the necessary image condition.

This proof audit makes no independence claim about the full P1 axioms.

3. Conditional spectral-rescaling obstruction

Assume the boundary class is closed under positive constant rescaling of
its metric, reference pair, finite-block definition and dimensional cutoffs.
Assume a compact self-adjoint elliptic realization, or a derived B_Sigma
with a first positive absolute eigenvalue, is part of the object and is
preserved by its isomorphisms. Then define for lambda>0

  B_lambda=(A_+,H_+,lambda D_+,J,Gamma,lambda B_Sigma).

Using g_lambda=lambda^-2 g and r_lambda=r/lambda gives

  lambda D_+=gamma_n(partial_(r_lambda)+lambda B_Sigma).

The Hilbert spaces for the rescaled constant densities are identified by
the natural constant unitary. Ellipticity and the collar identities persist.
Positive rescaling preserves spectral-flow crossing signs, kernels, the
structural finite ranks, determinant-line degrees and the Calderon Cauchy
solution space. Under the stated cutoff/reference covariance assumptions,
all four defect entries and essentialization are therefore unchanged.

Let mu_1>0 be the first positive absolute eigenvalue of the chosen compact
realization. A unitary isomorphism B_lambda -> B_nu would preserve its
spectrum and imply lambda*mu_1=nu*mu_1, hence lambda=nu. Thus any minimum
belongs to a continuum of equal-defect, pairwise nonisomorphic objects.

The original chi_seed does not normalize this family: it is defined by
chi_seed=(lambda_1^+(|B_Sigma|))^-1 and therefore scales as lambda^-1.
The dimensionless c3 and winding remain unchanged. The spectral action
is scale-sensitive, since D_lambda/chi_lambda=lambda^2 D/chi, but the
boundary category asks only for its finiteness, not a specified value or
a stationarity equation.

The rescaling conclusion is CONDITIONAL, not an unconditional counterpair
of complete P1 models. The text does not fully define rank_ess H_prim^fin,
the action domain, a global external reference, or covariance of the
freely chosen epsilon_adm spectral window. An absolute fixed cutoff or
fixed reference could prevent the rescaling, but would have to be declared
and analyzed. Nor does the proof sketch for C_seed establish that a time-
rescaled operational seed is mapped to B_lambda. No assertion about that
operational image is made here.

Original sources: section001 lines42-55,192-261,302-311,353-376;
section002 lines166-183,252-306; section003-subsection-relative-objects.tex
lines1-16, all in the same boundary-source directory.

4. What an actual reconstruction theorem can recover

The complete supported graded Schwinger hierarchy, with the hypotheses of
Osterwalder-Schrader II, reconstructs its Wightman theory up to unitary
equivalence. It supplies all moments, their support and covariance, not
only a scalar kernel or a spectral tuple. Complete truncated functions
determine the moments by the partition expansion with the required graded
signs. This is the data actually used in the QFT-source sections005 and008.

In this paragraph R denotes linear geometric reflection on the graded word
algebra, with the prescribed fermionic signs. The antilinear OS reflection
is j(F)=R(F)^*. This R is distinct from the antilinear VOA PCT operator in
THEOREMS.txt. On the positive-time word algebra the reflection form is

  (F,G)_OS = omega(j(F) G) = omega(R(F)^* G),
  N_OS={F:(F,F)_OS=0},
  H_OS=completion(A_+/N_OS), Omega=[1].

With the translation/regularity/contraction hypotheses of OS reconstruction,
the induced positive-time translations extend to T_t=exp(-t H), H>=0.
The remaining OS hypotheses and analytic continuation reconstruct the
Wightman distributions and their common field domain. Arbitrary positive-
time multiplication is NOT asserted to act on the quotient: N_OS need not
be a left ideal for such multiplication.

Alternatively, Schlingemann's algebraic reconstruction starts from a full
Euclidean net with Euclidean-group action, a regular reflection-positive
functional, and the time-zero condition. It then constructs a Haag-Kastler
net using the controlled time-zero representation. A net with only tau_t,
Theta and omega as listed in Seed_op has not yet supplied these additional
hypotheses.

Neither reconstruction automatically recovers an arbitrary unreduced
Euclidean presentation, its collar generator or seam class. It recovers
the theory generated by the supported correlation data after the OS null
reduction. Claiming an inverse to C_seed needs a separate faithfulness and
localization theorem.

5. The exact remaining source identification

THEOREMS.txt completely reconstructs the canonical local chiral extension
of V_D5 tensor V_A3 with the prescribed four C4 sectors, once each. To
identify that mathematical completion with the original primitive source,
one must prove that the source has this supported unitary chiral realization
and this generated sector content, and intertwines the actual products,
reflection, state and specified time action. The compiler's lattice result
alone does not state such an operator/localization map.

This is a single conjunctive identification problem. It cannot be replaced
by declaring a lattice, a preferred vector, or a convenient Hamiltonian to
be the source. The canonical conformal L0 also does not fix an absolute
physical time unit; on a circle of radius R the scale is L0/R.
