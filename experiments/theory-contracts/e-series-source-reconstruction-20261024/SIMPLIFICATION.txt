2. Finiteness is a consequence

U is regular. Therefore W, regarded as a weak U-module, decomposes as a
direct sum of the sixteen irreducible lattice modules M_a, with possible
multiplicities n_a. The common conformal vector identifies their L0 action
with that on W. If Delta_a is the fixed lowest conformal weight of M_a,
each copy contributes its nonzero ground space to W_(Delta_a), giving

  n_a dim(M_a)_(Delta_a) <= dim W_(Delta_a) < infinity.

If Delta_a is nonintegral this W weight space is zero. Thus every n_a is
finite. There are only sixteen module labels, so W is automatically a
finite U-module. No separate finite-index assumption is needed.

By the standard VOA-extension/algebra correspondence, this finite unitary
extension defines a connected commutative separable algebra B in the
unitary pointed category C=Rep(U), with trivial twist. The normalized
Q-system supplies separability and the corresponding semisimple module
category. These are consequences of the unitary extension hypotheses,
not new free algebra data.

3. Short general multiplicity lemma

Let B be a connected separable algebra in a pointed fusion category C.
The category C_B of right B-modules is semisimple. The regular right
module B is simple because

  End_B(B) = Hom_C(1,B) = C.

For each invertible simple object M_a, left tensoring by M_a is an
autoequivalence of right B-modules, with inverse M_(-a). Thus M_a tensor B
is also simple. The free-module/forgetful adjunction now gives

  n_a = dim Hom_C(M_a,B)
      = dim Hom_(C_B)(M_a tensor B,B)
      in {0,1}.                                         (3.1)

Moreover n_a=1 exactly when M_a tensor B is isomorphic to B as a right
module. Consequently

  S={a:n_a=1}

is the stabilizer subgroup of the regular module under the invertible
objects. This proves both subgroup closure and multiplicity one without
assuming a pairing on repeated labels or choosing multiplication constants.

The lemma uses standard semisimplicity and adjunction. Its application
eliminates the redundant assumptions in the existing TFPT reconstruction.

4. One marked generator saturates the allowed support

In the native discriminant chart

  q(a,b)=(5a^2+3b^2)/8 mod1,
  b((a,b),(c,d))=(5ac+3bd)/4 mod1.

The common conformal vector and the integer VOA grading give trivial
twist on the support, hence q restricted to S is zero. Since S is a
subgroup, polarization then gives b(S,S)=0.

The hypothesis M_h subset W gives H=<h> subset S. Isotropy implies
S subset H-perp. But the native lattice already gives

  H-perp={(a,b):5a+3b=0 mod4}
        ={(a,a):a in C4}=H.

Therefore

  H subset S subset H-perp=H,

so S=H. Equation(3.1) gives each summand once. The coherent-product and
fixed-U unitary uniqueness proof in THEOREMS.txt applies immediately.
Thus the existing marked generator forces the full E8 extension, its
canonical vacuum, all vacuum correlation functions and conformal dynamics.

The local realization and the occurrence of M_h are still hypotheses.
This theorem does not derive their realization by the primitive P1 kernel.

5. Complete classification before choosing the generator

The same lemma classifies all connected local unitary same-conformal
extensions of this U. The only isotropic support subgroups are

  S0={0},
  S2={0,(2,2)},
  Splus={(a,a):a in C4},
  Sminus={(a,-a):a in C4}.                              (5.1)

Completeness is elementary: q=0 has exactly the six labels

  (0,0),(2,2),(1,1),(3,3),(1,3),(3,1).

Its only nonzero order-two label is(2,2). Every order-four label generates
either Splus or Sminus; these cannot occur together in an isotropic subgroup.
This exhausts every subgroup of the zero set. The checker enumerates all
subsets of that six-element set and confirms precisely(5.1).

Each subgroup is cyclic. The even lattice overlattice gives existence,
and the existing abelian-coherence argument gives one multiplication class
for each support (H2(C_n,C*)=0 for these cyclic groups). Thus there are
exactly four extension classes over the fixed U:

  support     lattice realization     dimension of weight-one space
  S0          V_(D5+A3)               60
  S2          V_D8                    120
  Splus       V_E8                    248
  Sminus      V_E8                    248.              (5.2)

The two E8 entries have different labelled embeddings over fixed U. They
become equivalent after allowing the appropriate A3 diagram reversal.
The original marked h=(1,1) selects Splus. It does not belong to Sminus.
No variational choice of a preferred extension is needed once that marked
generator is present in the stated local conformal realization.
