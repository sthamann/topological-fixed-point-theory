quarter CAR source. Can their original P1 insertion be generated using only
the additive scalar density/stress net on their eight proper preimage arcs?
Success for this route requires invariance under all phases constant separately
on these arcs. A nonzero native highest-degree commutator aborts this route.
This tests an original insertion algebra, not a proposed new dynamics.

3. Exact top-degree coefficient calculation
For each native polynomial f_i, expand f_i(V^t t) in four distinct commuting
nilpotent occupations t_k. Let C[K,i] be its coefficient, |K|=4.
C has rank5 and has 70 rows. This is coefficient bookkeeping for the highest
CAR degree, not a physical identification of the full source with 70 states.
The corresponding creator/annihilator tensor is diagonal on exterior^4 C8.
A phase on original arc r acts there by dGamma(E_r),
E_r=U_alpha* |r><r| U_alpha. If K,L differ by replacing one index,
the matrix element has modulus1/8. All other offdiagonal entries vanish.
Thus the exact Hilbert-Schmidt Gram of these principal commutators is
G_ij=(1/64) sum_{ordered K~L} conj(C[K,i]-C[L,i]) (C[K,j]-C[L,j]).
There are1120 directed edges. Direct wedge replacement signs independently
reproduce the same Gram, without floating eigendecomposition.
Result: rankG=5, detG=498159/262144.
With original norms(96,8,8,8,1), detG_normalized=166053/4294967296.
All leading principal minors are positive. This holds for each of eight arcs
and both quarter phases. Every nonzero native quartic responds to each
individual infinitesimal original arc phase.
Normal ordering contractions, b/z shifts and derivative counterterms have
lower CAR degree. They cannot cancel this nonzero degree8 tensor.
The total-density coefficient vector C[K]=1 has zero commutator, a control.

5. Precise algebraic consequence
Let E=union_r I_r have disjoint closures, and let
B_add(E)=join_r B(I_r), where B(I_r) is generated by the original scalar
density and its geometric stress supported in the individual original arc.
Choose one smooth source phase h constant on a neighborhood of the entire
closure of each I_r, with independent constants on the eight components and
transitions in the complementary gaps. Disjoint closures make this possible.
Its Weyl implementer fixes each local density/stress generator: derivatives
of h, including anomalous terms, vanish on these supports. It therefore
fixes the additive net pointwise. The degree8 calculation proves the native
quartic field distributions are not fixed, and hence are not generated by
that additive density/stress net. This is not inferred merely from support.
For a smoothly smeared closed operator realization on a common invariant
core, noninvariance also excludes affiliation with that additive von Neumann
algebra: a unitary fixing the algebra pointwise fixes its affiliated closed
operators and their domains. Existence/domain hypotheses for that closed
realization are explicit; the finite coefficient test alone proves no such
analytic theorem. The unconditional result here is the field-tensor statement.
The older odd-fermion support commutator establishes multilocal support;
the new even charge commutator establishes a different, stronger boundary.

6. Positive source route retained
The full original CAR algebra on E already contains bilocal neutral products
eta_r* eta_s, r!=s. They transfer relative arc charge while preserving total
charge. They are not in general in the additive scalar density/stress subnet.
The actual native currents use A_a=U_alpha diag(V[:,a]) U_alpha*,
which has such offdiagonal entries. Their quartic products give the native
fields. This does not add particles, a new E8 extension or an interaction fit.
The offdiagonal Gram for positive native Cartan versus arc diagonal scalar
potentials is V*V - V* ones(8,8) V/8 and has rank4.
No nonzero positive native Cartan direction is an arc diagonal potential.
This classifies the failed scalar insertion route, not all original P1 maps.

7. Modular diagnostic without replacing the state
Restrict a single half-filled Fourier sea with quarter phase to eight equal
arcs on finite rings N=64,128. Keep all occupied Fourier modes of each finite
ring regulator. These two regulators do not reconstruct the infinite Hardy
sea. No independent arc vacua and no eigenvalue clipping are used.
For the retained covariance0<C_E<I set h_E=log((I-C_E)/C_E).
Its interarc norm is nonzero; so is[h_E,N_arc0]. Removing crossarc
covariances makes this commutator zero, but that control changes the state.
Both quarter choices reproduce the same gauge-invariant norms.
[h_E,A_a tensor I_per] vanishes to numerical precision for all four actual
native Cartan matrices. The equal-arc covariance is Fourier diagonal in the
arc label, explaining this result. These are integrated channel matrices,
not pointwise local fields. Position mixing inside each channel remains.
Two regulators prove neither continuum convergence nor a selected native
quartic dynamics. Modular parameter is not identified with physical time.
General disjoint-interval vacuum modular mixing and charge splitting are
known results: Longo,Martinetti,Rehren arXiv:0912.1106v2, section3,
https://arxiv.org/html/0912.1106v2 . This literature motivates the diagnostic;
it does not select the TFPT quarter state, seam map or physical time.

The exact result excludes native quartic insertion using only the separately
supported additive original scalar density/stress algebra on these eight arcs.

The full CAR source supplies the required algebraic structures already.
Neither the charge tensor nor a modular covariance matrix derives which

Hardy reference. No claim that the full CAR source or arbitrary P1 is excluded.
