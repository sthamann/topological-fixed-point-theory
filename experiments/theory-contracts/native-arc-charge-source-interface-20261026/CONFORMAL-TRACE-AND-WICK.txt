2. General exact conformal trace theorem
Let M be a smooth compact spin manifold of bulk dimension d with smooth
boundary, fixed spin structure, and g_sigma=exp(2sigma)g for smooth real
sigma. Use the canonical conformal spin-bundle identification. With this
identification the physical massless spin Dirac operator satisfies
 D_sigma=exp(-(d+1)sigma/2) D exp((d-1)sigma/2).             (1)
This is the usual conformal Dirac law. A fixed twisted connection is allowed
when it obeys this law; varying a connection or adding a mass is NOT covered.
Smooth harmonic spinors therefore correspond bijectively by
 psi_sigma=exp(-(d-1)sigma/2) psi_0.                       (2)
The induced boundary density is
 dSigma_sigma=exp((d-1)sigma_boundary)dSigma_0.
Consequently the geometrically specified boundary L2 unitary is
 V_sigma: H_boundary,sigma -> H_boundary,0,
 V_sigma f=exp((d-1)sigma_boundary/2) f.                  (3)
It is the positive scalar density correction for the canonical spin lift;
no additional phase or fitted field redefinition is introduced. In particular
 V_sigma Tr psi_sigma=Tr psi_0.                          (4)
Thus V_sigma sends the smooth Cauchy-data spaces EXACTLY onto each other.
Since it is unitary, the same holds for their L2 closures. Uniqueness of
orthogonal projection onto a closed subspace proves
 V_sigma P_sigma V_sigma^*=P_0.                          (5)
Here P means the ORTHOGONAL Calderon projector. This proof is about its
range, not an arbitrarily chosen nonorthogonal Seeley projection.
Positive smooth multiplication and its inverse preserve the relevant
Sobolev spaces on compact M. The smooth harmonic-space argument and the
unitary closure avoid extrapolating a finite Fourier regulator to(5).
No product collar is required after the conformal change.
Chirality is preserved, so the same argument applies to the original
chiral Cauchy space. No APS or other self-adjoint boundary condition is
imposed before computing that space.

For u_sigma=U_sigma psi_sigma, equation(2) becomes

The actual fixed-boundary-space trace is NOT the unweighted raw trace:
