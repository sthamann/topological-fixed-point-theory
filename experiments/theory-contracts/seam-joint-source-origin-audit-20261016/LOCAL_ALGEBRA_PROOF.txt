  A0 = C_infty(M) I,

3. What the unmarked exterior carrier DOES determine

This section reuses the no-selector result already in
raw-carrier-origin-gate-20260920/PROOF.txt:35-112,178-184, rather than rerunning
an old U(5) search. For supplied Hermitian E of rank g define the INTERNAL
exterior space F=Lambda E and its canonical operators

  c*(v) psi = v wedge psi, c(v)=(c*(v))*.

They obey CAR and act between degrees k and k+/-1. Let P_vac=product_i(1-c_i*c_i)
with c_i the annihilator in an orthonormal basis. Creation words, P_vac and
annihilation words give all rank-one matrix units |I><J| (with the appropriate
ordering signs). Hence the represented INTERNAL CAR algebra is End(Lambda E).
Its even part is End(F_even) direct_sum End(F_odd). Its degree-at-most-two even
vector space is

  C I + Lambda^2 E + End(E) + Lambda^2 E*,

represented by c*c*, c*c and cc. Quadratics generate the entire even algebra
ASSOCIATIVELY; their quadratic vector space is not the entire even algebra.
The shifted quadratic commutator algebra is so(E direct_sum E*).

All these formulas are basis covariant. A Hermitian metric and unit complex
volume leave SU(g) acting naturally. A projector canonically selected from
these data must commute with this action. The fundamental representation on E
is irreducible, so such a projector is0 or I. In particular rank five alone
does not select any proper subspace. Its rank-k projectors form Gr(k,5).
For any1<=k<=4 the trace-balanced primitive integral generator

  X_k=(5-k)P_k-k(I-P_k)

has trace0. The k=1 case has spectrum4,-1 and the k=2 case3,-2; trace balance
does not eliminate the1+4 option. This does not exclude an originally derived
interaction selecting rank2 up to gauge. It shows that CAR closure or trace
balance alone supplies no such selection. No physical gauge group is inferred
from this natural internal covariance group.

Crucial type distinction: the exterior even/odd grading is internal half-spinor
chirality. It is NOT automatically physical fermion parity or Lorentz/weak
chirality. If Lambda_even E is the16-dimensional one-particle multiplet, its
physical CAR quantization and its space-time localization are separate data.
The internal creation operators above are therefore not presented as the local
physical field algebra requested by the user. This avoids the known
state-versus-field mistake in raw-carrier-origin-gate:136-153.

There IS additional positive structure on this supplied unmarked carrier.
Its anti-linear Hodge operator J_H obeys, on Lambda^k C5,

4. Can a known reconstruction theorem supply the missing selection?

Connes, arXiv:0810.2088, assumes a commutative algebra inside a spectral
triple; it does not select a missing noncommutative insertion algebra from
an operator spectrum. Doplicher--Roberts, CMP131(1990)51-107,
doi:10.1007/BF02097680, reconstruct charged fields from an already specified
observable net and its superselection structure under its AQFT hypotheses.
Neither theorem supplies the absent original net/charged sector data here.

The more directly relevant finite classification is Chamseddine--Connes,
arXiv:0706.3688, sections2-4: order zero, a separating vector, irreducibility
of (A,J), KO6 and additional quaternion-linear graded data lead, with minimal
size and the maximal-subalgebra/nonzero-center-mixing condition, to the familiar
finite SM algebra. These are not consequences of the source excerpts above.
In particular section3 explicitly adds a quaternion-linear graded W. A complex
linear quarter-turn U with U^2=-I is not that anti-linear isometry I_H with
I_H^2=-I. No identification follows from their equal squares. The classification
requires a compatible sector/bimodule map, not matching dimensions alone.
For a scalar quarter-turn U=iI one even has (UJ)^2=J^2 for every antiunitary J;
the quarter phase creates no new negative square. The whole-space Hodge
structure above also does not by itself supply the classification's right-H
module on its separate W factor or select a4-by4 factorization of a16-space.
It is a conditional comparator, not a new source derivation in this audit.
Its particle module has dimension16, but its rank-one reduction inside the
four-dimensional complex factor is not the TFPT3+2 carrier split by itself.
An equivariant identification preserving action, reality, grading and charges
is still needed; the shared dimension16 supplies no such map.
