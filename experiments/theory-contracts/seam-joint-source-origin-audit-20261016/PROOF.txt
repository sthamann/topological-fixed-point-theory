4. Decisive new control: invariant-ring primitiveness does not fix b=3

S2:2410-2547 defines the graded algebra of invariant trilinears and says its
primitive element is indecomposable under lower carrier-degree invariant factors.
The proof of the lowest-generator lemma says that r>=4 factors through a
spectator bivector; it therefore forces p=1,r=2, and the next theorem reads b=3.
Here is a counterexample to that LOCAL invariant-ring inference.

Strictly, the printed direct sum of trilinear Hom spaces has no multiplication
defined; multiplying two trilinears is no longer trilinear. The invariant-ring
and ideal wording therefore needs an algebraic completion. The counterexample
below uses its natural covariant polynomial-invariant interpretation and the
printed positive carrier-degree criterion, not an invented replacement for the
stronger Lean contraction-isomorphism predicate.

Let V=C^5, W=C^2, G=S(U(V) x U(W)). Define
  A=V tensor W, F=Lambda^4 V, H=W.
The two fermion legs A and F belong respectively to exterior degrees2 and4
of Lambda_even(V direct_sum W), hence obey the stated evenness requirement.
The map
  B((v tensor w),xi,h)=(v wedge xi) tensor(w wedge h)
      in det V tensor det W = C
is nonzero and G-invariant: a group element multiplies its value by
det(g_V)det(g_W)=1. Its carrier type is (p,q;r,t)=(1,1;4,0), not (1,1;2,0).

Why it is primitive in the relevant covariant carrier grading:
for an invariant of total V/W fundamental degrees(d_V,d_W), the SU centers
require 5|d_V and2|d_W. The primitive central subgroup
  (exp(-2it) I5, exp(5it) I2)
requires -2d_V+5d_W=0. Thus(d_V,d_W)=(5m,2m), m a nonnegative integer.
There is no invariant of positive carrier degree below7, and B has degree7.
No product of strictly smaller positive carrier-degree invariants can equal B.
Carrier-degree-zero constants or singlet scalar factors do not change this fact.
In particular, a noninvariant spectator bivector is not an invariant-ring factor.

On the three field variables(A,F,H), the central charges are(3,-8,5).
No monomial of polynomial degree1 or2 has zero charge; B is a nonzero cubic.
It is therefore polynomial-indecomposable as well. Its explicit coefficient
tensor has100 entries,10 nonzero. The exact checker annihilates it under all
20 off-diagonal sl5 generators,4 diagonal differences,2 sl2 roots and1 sl2
diagonal generator:2700 integer residuals, all zero. A b=3 positive control
has36 entries,6 nonzero and396 zero residuals.

The wedge formula and determinant transformation are the proof; these finite
controls check the implementation. The same formula works for every odd b>=3
with F=Lambda^(b-1)V and W=C^2. No numerical dimension matching is used.

What is NOT excluded: a stronger source-derived restriction on field support,
a source-derived degree bound, or an independently justified minimum over all
admissible sources/ranks could select b=3. The b=5 control is outside given P2;
it is not a counterexample to P1+P2, source minimality with all its conditions,
the full physical theory, or the accepted conditional SM algebra.
