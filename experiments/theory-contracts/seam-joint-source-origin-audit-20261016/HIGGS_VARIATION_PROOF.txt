4. Complete smooth-potential classification, including fixed quarter holonomy

Put vbar=(2pi)^-1 integral v. Let lambda be a real primitive of v-vbar; it is
periodic and smooth. Multiplication by exp(i lambda) is unitary on L2 and
preserves the H1 domain. Direct differentiation gives
  exp(-i lambda) B_(alpha+vbar) exp(i lambda)=B_alpha,v.
The same statement works on the actual twisted boundary domain in its
equivalent nonperiodic trivialization. In particular
  exp(2pi i B_alpha,v)=exp(2pi i(alpha+vbar)) I.
Keeping the original quarter transition therefore requires vbar in Z.
For vbar=k in Z the additional periodic large multiplication exp(i k theta)
relates B_(alpha+k) to B_alpha. A half-integer mean flips the quarter branch
and is not an allowed same-branch solution.

This proves a one-particle operator classification, not physical gauge
equivalence of fixed vacua, determinant-line triviality, cancellation of a
chiral anomaly, or a new physical time identification. A transported spectral
covariance is conjugated with the same operator; holding the old reference sea
fixed is a different question. Large gauge maps can carry charge. Likewise a
four-dimensional interpretation as gauge-Higgs unification would require an
actual spacetime/representation map and is not established by a circle label.

Any additional original reflection/state/admissibility constraints restrict
this local one-form space; they do not turn its unchanged U(1) representation
into a new SU(3)xSU(2) fundamental carrier. This exclusion does not cover a

5. The tempting Calderon enlargement does not fix the missing coupling

One might try to obtain matrix-valued scalar directions by including C itself
as a local algebra element. On the actual bilateral source basis e_n, define
  P0=C-U C U*,
  E_mn=U^m P0 U^(-n), m,n in Z.
P0 e_n=delta_(n,0)e0 and E_mn e_j=delta_(j,n)e_m. Thus the algebra generated
by U,U*,C contains all finite matrix units. Every central bounded element must
commute with them, hence is scalar. The center is not C_infty(S1).
Here use the algebraic generated star-algebra (or a specified smooth algebra
with bounded commutators), not all bounded operators. A norm closure alone
does not establish the domain of the unbounded commutator with B_alpha.

Because U*[B_alpha,U]=I still holds, every element a of that enlarged algebra
is also a represented one-form a U*[B_alpha,U]. In particular, arbitrary
selfadjoint finite matrix perturbations become available. This permits desired
couplings to be INSERTED; it does not select one. C is not a pointwise local
multiplication: it is a nonlocal Cauchy/spectral projection.

Therefore this enlargement cannot silently serve as the missing physical local
source algebra while retaining the original smooth geometric center. A genuine
noncommutative boundary net could be a different justified source, but its field
action, state/time and center/locality relation must be derived, not supplied
by declaring a projector to be a new local field. No general noncommutative
Higgs mechanism is excluded.

7. Variation of the original relative action does not supply the missing field map

For any genuinely specified path D(s), at fixed actual reflection R and fixed
chi, write D_rel=(D-RDR)/2 and D_ref=(D+RDR)/2. On a finite regulator, or where
the indicated derivatives satisfy the trace-class/relative-trace conditions
on a common operator/form domain and a differentiable spectral chart, exact
first variation is
  delta S_prim = chi^-1 Tr[f'(D_rel/chi) delta D_rel]
              - chi^-1 Tr[f'(D_ref/chi) delta D_ref]
              + i pi delta(Delta_eta)/2,
  delta D_rel=(delta D-R delta D R)/2,
  delta D_ref=(delta D+R delta D R)/2.
No commutativity of D and delta D is needed for this TRACE first derivative.
If chi varies as prescribed by the source, add its chain-rule contribution:
  -(delta chi)/chi^2 (Tr[D_rel f'(D_rel/chi)]
                      -Tr[D_ref f'(D_ref/chi)]).
If R itself varies, its derivatives must also be included. An absolute Eta
term and any reference-dependent response cannot be discarded by normalization.
Explicitly, delta(RDR)=(delta R)DR+R(delta D)R+RD(delta R), with any moving
operator-domain or APS-boundary terms handled separately. A chi selected from
lambda1+(|B_Sigma|) is differentiable only on an isolated stable-multiplicity
spectral branch; eigenvalue crossings require a one-sided or spectral-projection
treatment. The displayed delta Eta is an ordinary derivative away from zero
crossings; spectral-flow jumps must not be erased by differentiating through them.

These formulas differentiate a supplied operator path; they do not define
which delta D is a physical Higgs field. In particular, scalar seam-evenness
of Phi alone does not determine parity of a putative spinor operator delta D:
the actual Clifford and carrier factors must be supplied. The source's smooth
test function f is also a declared realization choice. Neither replacing f nor
postulating delta D=Phi Y with the desired Y counts as the requested derivation.
