2. Spectral response sees what static Cauchy data lose
----------------------------------------------------
For z != 0, D_f u_f = z u_f is equivalent, with u_f=e^{f/2}u, to

  D u = z e^f u.

The fixed spectral parameter is therefore weighted differently in the
interior. A mere zero-energy boundary trace does not include this information.

There is a more direct boundary observable. Define the Green boundary form

  B(a,b) = (Du,v)_M - (u,Dv)_M,
  a=u|Sigma, b=v|Sigma,

with a consistent inner-product and normal convention. Let A_B be an invertible
self-adjoint elliptic realization of D. For a smooth harmonic u, an explicit
spectral solution chart for |z|<gap(A_B) is

  u_z=u+z(A_B-z)^{-1}u.

The resolvent maps L2 to H1, this expression is analytic, and direct substitution
gives (D-z)u_z=0 and u_0=u. Its H^{1/2} boundary trace is analytic. Take Dv=0
and real z. Green's identity gives

  B(a_z,b) = z (u_z,v)_M,
  B(d_z a_z|0,b) = (u,v)_M.

This first-jet form is chart-independent: the difference between the derivatives
of two charts solves D w=0 and its Green pairing with any harmonic v is zero.

For D_f the boundary form is identical because its collar is identical. The
zero-energy solutions with the same traces are e^{f/2}u and e^{f/2}v, so the
corresponding first spectral jet has Gram form

  G_f(a,b) = integral_M e^f <u,v> dvol_g,
  G_f(a,b)-G_0(a,b) = integral_M (e^f-1)<u,v> dvol_g.

In particular if f>=0 is smooth and nonzero, and u is a nonzero harmonic
spinor for which the chart exists, unique continuation prevents u from
vanishing on the entire open set where f>0. Thus

  G_f(a,a)-G_0(a,a) > 0.

This is an exact positive discriminator for this class of interior profiles.
