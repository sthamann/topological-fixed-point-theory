Exact joint action of the marked cover clock and the native full E8 lattice fields

Verdict

PASS in the stated mathematical scope.  There is a canonical associated
lattice-VOA local system carrying the peripheral all-J monodromy, and the
forced C12 cover carries an exact marked order-twelve compiler action on that
same object.  The peripheral J and compiler J are not identified as cover
automorphisms.  They have the same fixed fibre lift Jhat but different base
components.

For the fixed generator-normalized Jhat there are exactly four character
representatives chat over c.  They form one orbit under the character gauges
that preserve Jhat.  Thus there is one cyclic compiler lift up to that gauge.
The induced sigmahat is necessarily character-corrected.  No representative
has both Jhat and the separately generator-normalized sigma section fixed.

1. Fixed object and conventions

Let

  B° = P1 minus {1,i,-1,-i,infinity},
  C_n : u_n^n=x^4-1,
  Delta_n(x,u_n)=(x,zeta_n u_n),
  Q_n(x,u_n)=(i x,u_n).

The finite meridians are positively ordered.  The original peripheral C4
cover is C4, and the forced common cover is C12.  On C12 the marked compiler
cover clock is

  R12=Q12 Delta12^4,
  R12^4=Delta12^4,
  R12^9=Q12,
  R12^12=1.

Retain the original even E8 lattice L, the literal ordered cocycle epsilon,
and its lattice VOA

  V_L = M(1) tensor C_epsilon[L].

For an isometry g of L, S_g denotes the existing generator-normalized lift.
For a character a in A=Hom(L,F2), put

  D_a E_lambda=(-1)^a(lambda) E_lambda.

Every lift over g is S_g D_a.  On a full lattice field it acts by

  S_g D_a [h1(-n1)...hk(-nk) E_lambda]
    =(g h1)(-n1)...(g hk)(-nk)
      eta_g(lambda)(-1)^a(lambda) E_(g lambda),

where eta_g is fixed by the unchanged epsilon and ordered basis.  This is the
existing full lattice-field action, including charged sectors and every
oscillator descendant.  No new carrier or product is introduced.

The lattice operators are the existing commuting matrices

  J^4=1,  sigma^3=1,  c=J sigma,
  c^4=sigma,  c^9=J,  c^12=1.

Fix Jhat=S_J, the generator-normalized lift already used by the marked seam
study.

2. Canonical associated local system

Use C12 as a principal refinement of C4 and let its deck generator act on the
fibre through Jhat.  Define

  Vper = (C12 times V_L)/equivalence,

with

  (Delta12 p,v) equivalent to (p,Jhat v).                 (2.1)

Equivalently, the diagonal action is

  k.(p,v)=(Delta12^k p,Jhat^(-k)v).

Because Jhat^4=1, this representation of the C12 deck group factors through
C4 and has kernel <Delta12^4>.  Therefore (2.1) is canonically the same local
system as

  (C4 times V_L)/((Delta4 p,v) equivalent to (p,Jhat v)).

A positive finite meridian lifts to an endpoint Delta12 p, so its parallel
transport on the fibre is exactly Jhat.  This realizes the existing
peripheral all-J monodromy without turning it into a base rotation.

The three roles are now explicit:

  deck:  Delta12 on the marked principal cover;
  base:  Q12, covering x -> i x;
  fibre: Jhat, sigmahat and chat on V_L.

Deck and base commute.  A fibre automorphism descends through (2.1) precisely
when it commutes with Jhat.

3. Complete cyclic field-lift classification

Write every candidate over c as

  chat_a=S_c D_a,  a in A.

Bits below refer, least significant bit first, to the dual of the predecessor's
ordered integral E8 basis.  In doubled standard coordinates its column matrix

      [-1  2  2 -2  0  0  0  0 ]

The ninth-power equation is

  chat_a^9=Jhat

Here b is defined by the exact fourth power

  sigmahat_a=chat_a^4=S_sigma D_b.                  (3.2)

For every row the following identities hold on all lattice fields:

4. The exact joint action

For any of the four representatives define the marked action

  Chat[p,v]=[R12 p,chat v].                           (4.1)

It is well defined on Vper.  Indeed R12 commutes with Delta12, and chat
commutes with Jhat because Jhat=chat^9.

Using the diagonal relation (2.1), equations (3.3), and the exact cover
powers gives

  Chat^4[p,v]
    =[Delta12^4 p,sigmahat v]
    =[p,sigmahat v],                                  (4.2)

  Chat^9[p,v]
    =[Q12 p,Jhat v],                                  (4.3)

  Chat^12=1.                                          (4.4)

Equation (4.2) retains the oriented sigma deck motion in the marked C12
presentation.  It becomes vertical sigmahat on the associated local system
because Delta12^4 lies in the kernel of its peripheral C4 representation.
Equation (4.3) is the joint compiler J: it covers the nontrivial base quarter
rotation and acts by Jhat on the fibre.

The peripheral J is instead

  Jper[p,v]=[Delta12 p,v]=[p,Jhat v].                 (4.5)

It covers the identity on B°.  Therefore

  base(Jper)=0,
  base(Chat^9)=1 in C4,
  Chat^9=Q12 Jper.                                    (4.6)

This is the precise resolution of the two roles called J.  They share the
same fibre lift because the associated local system was built from the
peripheral J monodromy.  They remain different total automorphisms because
their base components differ.  No phase or character gauge can remove that
base-component distinction.

The compiler factorization also holds on the joint object:

  Chat=(Chat^9)(Chat^4)=Jcomp Sigma,

where Jcomp is (4.3) and Sigma is (4.2).  This is the existing c=J sigma
relation with cover, base and fibre roles all retained.

5. What this decides and what it does not identify

The restriction of the original character-valued lattice-lift extension to
the cyclic compiler subgroup <c> splits.  Fixing Jhat leaves one character
gauge class, represented by (3.4).  This does not split the full G31
extension.  The predecessor's explicit nonsplitting certificate remains
unchanged.

No Burau lattice is identified with geometric H1 or with the E8 lattice.  The
existing integral Phi12 intertwiner remains an exact unpolarized comparison
between its two stated modules.  Its complementary spectra and alternating
form types remain different.  The present construction instead puts the
existing cover and the existing V_E8 fibre into their standard associated
local system, so no carrier identification is needed.

No physical seam, state, time, coupling or field-selection rule is asserted.
This is a complete mathematical joint action for the supplied marked cover,
the unchanged lattice cocycle and the cyclic compiler subgroup.
