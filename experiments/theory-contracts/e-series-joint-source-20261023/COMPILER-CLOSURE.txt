Minimal joint closure of the native D5+A3 compiler cuts

1. Result

Let Phi be the existing E8 root system in the standard real eight coordinate
chart. Let

  H = W(D5) x W(D3),

where D5 uses coordinates 0,...,4 and D3=A3 uses coordinates 5,6,7. Retain the
actual native quarter rotation

  J(x0,x1,...,x7)=(-x1,x0,-x3,x2,-x5,x4,-x7,x6).

The two exact transition group closures are

  <H,J> = W(D8),                         |W(D8)| = 5,160,960,

and, for the original 60 G31 pair reflections,

  <H,G31> = W(E8),                       |W(E8)| = 696,729,600.       (1.1)

The first equality closes the four original D5 compatible E6 cuts to exactly
224 integer A2 normal E6 cuts. They form 56 coordinate three block frames,
with four tetrahedral cuts in every frame. On the entire 128 dimensional half
root current module, every frame gives four literal orthogonal projectors of
rank 32 whose sum is the identity. For each projector P,

  rank P = 32,       rank(1-P) = 96,
  Tr(I128)/Tr(1-P) = 128/96 = 4/3.                              (1.2)

This is the chirality free form of the previous rank 16 plus rank 48 result.
It uses both native chiral blocks together and therefore introduces no chosen
64 dimensional block.

The second equality enlarges the orbit to all 1120 regular A2 normal E6 cuts.
The fixed 128 dimensional half root sector is not invariant under this larger
group. The W(E8) covariant replacement is the complete unordered family of
1120 E6 projectors on the full E8 root or adjoint carrier.

These are transition groups on transported compiler flags. They are not
stabilizers of one fixed object carrying the original D5+A3 split and J.

2. Why H and J close to W(D8)

Every element of H is an even signed coordinate permutation. J is also an
even signed coordinate permutation, so <H,J> is contained in W(D8).

The coordinate permutation of J is

  (01)(23)(45)(67).

The product (01)(23)(67) already lies in S5 x S3 inside H. Therefore the
permutation image of <H,J> contains the crossing transposition (45). Together
with the adjacent transpositions internal to the two original blocks, this
generates all of S8.

H already contains a sign reversal on any pair within either original block.
Conjugating one such pair reversal by the now available S8 image gives every
two coordinate sign reversal. These vectors span the seven dimensional even
sign space. Hence the kernel is the complete group 2^7 of even sign changes.
Thus

  <H,J> = 2^7 : S8 = W(D8).

The checker verifies the crossing transposition explicitly, verifies rank 7
of the pair sign span over F2, and verifies that the resulting action has the
two expected root orbits: 112 integer D8 roots and 128 half roots.

3. The 224 cut closure and the 56 projector frames

A regular A2 inside D8 is supported on exactly three coordinate axes. A fixed
triple carries a D3=A3 root system, and that D3 contains four A2 subsystems.
Therefore

  number of integer A2 subsystems = binomial(8,3) times 4 = 56 times 4 = 224.

The checker constructs these subsystems from the exact root relation

  A(alpha,beta)={plus or minus alpha, plus or minus beta,
                 plus or minus(alpha+beta)},
  (alpha,beta)=-1,

and proves that one W(D8) orbit contains all 224. This is exactly the union of
the four integer normal orbit rows in the previous H classification:

  40 + 120 + 60 + 4 = 224.

For an integer A2 subsystem A, define a diagonal operator on the literal half
root basis M128 by

  P_A e_lambda = e_lambda  if (lambda,A)=0,
                 0         otherwise.                            (3.1)

Every P_A has rank 32. If A1,...,A4 are the four A2 faces in one coordinate
D3 frame, then

  P_Ai P_Aj = delta_ij P_Ai,
  P_A1 + P_A2 + P_A3 + P_A4 = I128.                              (3.2)

The reason is literal. Restrict a half root sign vector to the selected three
coordinates. Its projective sign axis is exactly one of the four tetrahedral
axes. Equation (3.2) follows without a fitted embedding.

The complement of one face is the sum of the other three projectors, so it
has rank 96. Equation (1.2) is therefore structural and is independent of a
chosen face. Relative to each transported five plus three coordinate split,
every half root also has the unchanged squared projection norms

  ||lambda_D5||^2 = 5/4,       ||lambda_A3||^2 = 3/4.            (3.3)

Thus the projector ranks, the ratio 4/3, and the glue norms 5/4 and 3/4 belong
to one common carrier dictionary.

J acts by exact conjugation,

  J P_A J^-1 = P_(JA).

It permutes the 56 frames and their four faces. In particular it sends the
original coordinate frame {5,6,7} to {4,6,7}. It sends all four original
D5 compatible cuts outside the originally fixed family. The closure retains
the entire transported dictionary and supplies no preferred face.

4. Why the original G31 pair operations force W(E8)

The preceding E7 pair certificate proves that every original G31 reflection
is literally

  g_alpha = s_alpha s_(J alpha),                                (4.1)

where alpha and J alpha are orthogonal E8 roots. Hence every g_alpha belongs
to W(E8). The native v634 certificate also proves that J itself belongs to the
group generated by the 60 reflections. Consequently it is enough to determine
what one actual pair reflection does to the two W(D8) root orbits.

In doubled integral coordinates, take

  2 alpha = (-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1),
  2 beta  = (-2,-2,0,0,0,0,0,0).

The exact original operation (4.1) gives

  2 g_alpha(beta) = (-1,-1,1,1,1,1,1,1).                       (4.2)

Thus an actual G31 pair reflection maps an integer D8 root to an E8 half root.
Since W(D8) is transitive on each of the 112 and 128 root sectors, adding this
one operation makes the joint group transitive on all 240 E8 roots.

Now W(D8) already contains the root reflection s_delta for every integer D8
root delta. For any E8 root gamma, choose a joint group element k with
k delta=gamma. Then

  k s_delta k^-1 = s_gamma.

The joint group therefore contains all 240 E8 root reflections. Those
reflections generate W(E8). Conversely H, J, and every operation (4.1) lie in
W(E8). This proves both inclusions and hence the second equality in (1.1).
No enumeration of the 696,729,600 group elements is used.

5. The larger closure scope

W(E8) is transitive on the 1120 regular A2 subsystems. Therefore the minimal
full closure of any original E6 cut is the complete family

  { C_A = Phi intersect span(A)^perp : A is a regular A2 in E8 },
  |{C_A}| = 1120.                                                (5.1)

On the 240 root current basis, the literal cut projector has rank 72. Adding
the six dimensional Cartan complement of the normal A2 plane gives the usual
rank 78 E6 adjoint projector. This complete 1120 member family is exactly
W(E8) covariant.

The coordinate tetrahedra also enlarge. The 56 disjoint D3 frames close to
7560 regular A3=D3 root subsystems. Each contains four A2 faces. The incidence
identity is

  7560 times 4 = 1120 times 27 = 30240.                          (5.2)

Thus every E6 cut belongs to 27 transported A3 tetrahedra. Unlike the 56
coordinate frames, the 7560 full frames overlap and do not partition the cut
family.

The witness (4.2) proves at the same time that the integer plus half root
polarization is not W(E8) invariant. Therefore the uniform rank 32 projector
description on a fixed M128 is the exact intermediate W(D8) structure. It
must not be advertised as a fixed W(E8) module. Full W(E8) covariance uses
the 240 root current or 248 adjoint carrier and transports the complete flag.

6. Canonical content and retained mark

The mathematical canonical objects are:

  * under W(D8), the unordered 224 cuts, their 56 four projector resolutions,
    and the transported five plus three polarizations;
  * under W(E8), the unordered 1120 E6 cuts and 7560 overlapping A3 frames.

An individual E6 cut, one tetrahedral vertex, one chiral 64 block, and the
original fixed D5+A3 chart are marks. They are not selected by either closure.
In particular H does not centralize J. Equations (1.1) describe allowed
mathematical transitions between transported marked flags, not an assertion
that every group element preserves the original fully marked compiler object.

No physical preparation, P1 event rule, state, or measurement is derived.
The result removes the arbitrary chiral choice from the ratio identity and
classifies the exact finite closure. It does not choose which transported mark
is physically instantiated.
