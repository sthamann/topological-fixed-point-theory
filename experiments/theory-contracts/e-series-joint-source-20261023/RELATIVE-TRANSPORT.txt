  Hom_clock = Q[A] = Q(zeta12), dimension4 over Q.

  H -> D H J^-1 = A^4 H A^-9 = A^-5 H = A^7 H.          (3.1)

  ker(H -> D H J^-1-H)=0.

ordinary local-system comparison is therefore decided negatively; a constant
