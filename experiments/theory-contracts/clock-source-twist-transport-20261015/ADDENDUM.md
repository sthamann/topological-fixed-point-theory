    C_delta(p,t)/C_delta(-p,t)=exp[-2t delta.p].

    y_i=-(1/(2t)) log[C_delta(b_i,t)/C_delta(-b_i,t)].

    delta=(B^T)^(-1) y.

Equivalently, the exact spectral energy differences give
`y_i=(E_delta(b_i)-E_delta(-b_i))/2`. B is the existing original basis,
