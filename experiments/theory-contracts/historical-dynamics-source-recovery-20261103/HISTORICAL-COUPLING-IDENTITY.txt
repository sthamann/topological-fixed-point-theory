2. SMALLEST DECISIVE IDENTITY TEST
Allow every real nonzero constant scale s and an overall nonzero
polynomial multiplier A, more freedom than the stated s=2*pi.
To express an actual change of variable at the same fixed nonzero L,
one needs the polynomial identity
 I_alpha(s*k,L)=A*I_k(k,L).
Its difference is exactly
 (s^3-A)*k^3+(2*A*c3^2-2*c3^3*s^2)*k^2
                  +8*b1*c3^6*L*(A-1).
The nonzero constant term in k forces A=1.
The cubic coefficient then forces s=1 over the reals.
The quadratic coefficient then forces c3=1, contrary to c3=1/(8*pi).
Thus NO real nonzero constant rescaling gives this claimed identity.
This also excludes the inverse interpretation of the stated arrow.
At L=0 the constant-term argument changes; that is not the specified
phi0 in (0,1) source. Rescaling L or b1 would change the given invariant
dictionary and requires its own actual source map.

3. WHAT A GENUINELY SHARED ROOT WOULD REQUIRE

 k^3-2*c3^2*k^2=alpha^3-2*c3^3*alpha^2.
This is a nonlinear relation of two different dimensionless couplings,
not the claimed units conversion. It can relate particular roots,
but it does not transport a full action, its field variations, measure,
state, physical clock or mixed response functions.
