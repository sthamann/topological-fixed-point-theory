1. THE EARLY AND LATE FIELD SYMBOLS DO NOT HAVE THE SAME TYPE
GS25-A Sec2.1 assumes totally antisymmetric contorsion K_ABC and T=2K.
GS25-M p1 Eq2.1, p6 glossary explicitly defines V_a := T^b_ab,
the TRACE vector, although the abstract still invokes antisymmetric torsion.
For any totally antisymmetric T_abc,
 V_a = g^{bc} T_cab = 0,
because symmetric g^{bc} contracts antisymmetric b,c indices.
The axial dual S^d in T_abc=epsilon_abcd S^d can be nonzero when V=0.
Consequently V is not the old axial field S with a renamed normalization.
An axial kinetic Hessian cannot be assigned to the late trace field merely
by reusing the letter T. Preserve each original action and its field map.
This also prevents identifying V with the P25 matter-corrected S_hat.

3. THE SUPPLIED MULTIPLIER CONDITION HAS TO BE VARIED IN THE WHOLE ACTION
GS25-M p2 Eq2.4 actually adds integral lambda*div(V), and claims both
 div(V)=0 and d(lambda)=0.
It is therefore incorrect to describe THIS later source as lacking a local
transversality constraint. The earlier GS25-B caller is a different case.
At the source's flat quadratic order, variation of its actual C.1 plus this
written multiplier term gives

Taking the divergence yields Box(lambda)=0, not d(lambda)=0.
For an explicit local counterexample, take a nonconstant harmonic sigma,
Box(sigma)=0, V_mu=partial_mu(sigma), lambda=-a*sigma/kappa^2.

No global boundary/gauge quotient is inferred from this local example.

transported; they are not supplied by the variation asserted in Eq2.4.
