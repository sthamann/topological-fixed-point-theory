1. The actual source diffeomorphism and its one-particle jet
-------------------------------------------------------------

Let phi_t be a smooth orientation-preserving diffeomorphism of the original
cover circle, with lifted periodicity

  phi_t(theta+2 pi)=phi_t(theta)+2 pi,
  phi_0(theta)=theta,
  dot phi_0(theta)=v(theta).

For a real smooth profile g on the fold circle, the actual eight-preimage
lift is

  v(theta)=g(8 theta)/8.

Thus a properly supported fold interval lifts to eight separated intervals
on the original one-channel circle.  It is local in the quotient c=8 fold
net, not point-local in the original c=1 localization.

In the quarter-twisted chart the canonical half-density transport is

  (U_t^tw f)(theta)=sqrt(phi_t'(theta)) f(phi_t(theta)).       (1)

It is unitary on L2(S1,dtheta), preserves the original quarter boundary
condition, and maps H1_alpha onto itself.  With

  A_v^tw=-i(v partial_theta+v'/2),

equation (1) has dot U_0^tw=i A_v^tw.  Hence, for the active convention

  B_t^tw=U_t^tw B^tw U_t^tw*,       B^tw=-i partial_theta,

the exact first jet is

  dot B_0^tw=i[A_v^tw,B^tw]
            =i(v' partial_theta+v''/2).                        (2)

For v=g(8theta)/8 this is

  dot B_0^tw=i(g'(8theta) partial_theta+4 g''(8theta)).        (3)

The periodic chart is related to the twisted chart by

  (Q_alpha f)(theta)=exp(i alpha theta) f(theta),
  B^per=Q_alpha* B^tw Q_alpha=-i partial_theta+alpha.

The transport itself must be gauge conjugated.  It is therefore

  U_t^per=Q_alpha* U_t^tw Q_alpha,
  (U_t^per f)(theta)
    =exp(i alpha(phi_t(theta)-theta))
       sqrt(phi_t'(theta)) f(phi_t(theta)),                    (4)

not the plain half-density pullback on periodic functions.  Its generator is

  A_v^per=Q_alpha* A_v^tw Q_alpha
         =-i(v partial_theta+v'/2)+alpha v,
  dot U_0^per=i A_v^per.

Thus the same transported operator in the periodic chart has first jet

  dot B_0^per=i[A_v^per,B^per]
             =i(v' partial_theta+v''/2)-alpha v'.              (5)

For v=g(8theta)/8 equation (5) is

  dot B_0^per
    =i(g'(8theta) partial_theta+4 g''(8theta))
       -alpha g'(8theta).                                     (6)

The signs in (2) and (5) follow from dot U_0=iA_v in their respective
charts.  Choosing instead U_t=exp(-itA_v) reverses both the diffeomorphism
convention and these first-jet signs.

The finite operator is the already classified scalar first-order source
fiber.  Put x=phi_t and a_t=1/phi_t'.  In the twisted chart,

  B_t^tw=-i(a_t partial_theta+a_t'/2).                          (7)

Gauge conjugation gives the equivalent periodic-chart formula

  B_t^per=Q_alpha* B_t^tw Q_alpha
         =-i(a_t partial_theta+a_t'/2)+alpha a_t.              (8)

which is exactly the half-density normal form in
SCALAR-FIRST-ORDER-SOURCE-FIBER-AND-SEAM-WARD.txt.  Thus this is a canonical
transport of the original source, not a fitted scalar or matrix deformation.

2. All source and P1 comparison data must move together
--------------------------------------------------------

  C_t=U_t C_alpha U_t*,
  Dom(B_t)=U_t Dom(B_alpha),

and the same transport must be applied to the bulk/collar operator,
reflection, reference, relative operator and their domains.  Here U_t and
B_t are taken together in either the twisted chart or the gauge-equivalent
periodic chart above.  Mixing plain periodic pullback with the twisted-chart

  D_t=U_tilde D U_tilde*,                 R_t=U_tilde R U_tilde*,
  D_ref,t=U_tilde D_ref U_tilde*,         D_rel,t=U_tilde D_rel U_tilde*.

  tau_s,t=Ad_Gamma(U_t) o tau_s o Ad_Gamma(U_t)*,
  omega_t=omega o Ad_Gamma(U_t)*,

3. Passive kernel transport and active preparation are different diagrams
------------------------------------------------------------------------

Let V_t=Gamma(U_t).  Passive covariant transport sends

  (source data, omega, O)
      | Ad(V_t) on every datum
      v
  (transported source data, omega_t, O_t),

where omega_t=omega o Ad(V_t*), O_t=V_t O V_t*.  Consequently

  omega_t(O_t)=omega(O).                                      (9)

Equation (9) is the isospectral kernel/action statement.  Calling it a new
prepared response would count a coordinate change as an intervention.

An active preparation at a fixed detector instead keeps the source chart and
O fixed and changes only the vector/state:

  Omega_d -> Omega_d(t)=V_t Omega_d,
  omega_t^active(O)=omega(V_t* O V_t).                        (10)

Equations (9) and (10) are related by covariance, but they answer different
questions.  The first transports the detector as well and is response
invariant.  The second compares the transformed state with a fixed detector
and can have a nonzero octic response.

4. The first source jet is stress; the octic response is eighth order
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The fold relation

  Lhat_n=Lcal_(8n)^alpha/8+21/64 delta_n0

maps the cover vector v(theta)=g(8theta)/8 to the supplied shifted fold
stress

  A_d(g)=sum_n g_(-n) L_n^(d),
  L_n^(d)=L_n+d.h_n+||d||^2/2 delta_n0.

Thus (2), equivalently (5), is the one-particle source jet and A_d(g) is its
Fock stress implementer, up to the fixed projective scalar.  Let c_-1 denote the
coefficient of L_-1^(d) in A_d(g).  With the displayed Fourier convention
c_-1=g_1=conjugate(g_-1) for real g.

The normalized original octic ray is

  F8hat=I8/(96 sqrt(140)) in Im M.

The unshifted Sugawara part L_n has polarization degree zero, while the two
linear pieces of d.h_n have degree plus or minus one.  F8hat has pure positive
degree eight.  It follows exactly that

  <F8hat,A_d(g)^k Omega_d>=0,                    k<8.          (11)

At k=8 every factor must be the positive linear shift.  Positive modes
annihilate Omega_d, and total oscillator weight eight forces all eight modes
to be -1.  Therefore

  <F8hat,A_d(g)^8 Omega_d>
     =8! conjugate(F8hat(b)) c_-1^8,                           (12)

where b=U_+^T O^T d is the actual native M/O transport.  Both original
quarter sectors have F8hat(b) nonzero.  Consequently

  <F8hat,exp(i t A_d(g)) Omega_d>
     =t^8 conjugate(F8hat(b)) c_-1^8+o(t^8).                  (13)

The projective scalar phase cannot change the first nonzero F8 coefficient.
A real smooth bump in any proper fold interval can be chosen with c_-1 nonzero,
and the existing affine energy bounds define its smeared stress unitary.

5. Correct origin criterion
----------------------------

The earlier test

  <F8hat,P_8 dot D Omega> != 0

is a valid test for a DIRECT LINEAR F8 coupling.  It is not a necessary test
for origin from a geometric one-parameter source family.  In the actual
transported family the first jet is the linear stress generator and its F8
matrix element vanishes by (11), while the eighth ordered product is nonzero
by (12).  The correct criterion is the first nonzero jet of the supplied
family:

  P_(C F8hat) P_(Im M) P_8 d^k/dt^k|_0 Omega_d(t) != 0

for some source-derived k. Here the minimal k is exactly eight when
c_-1 != 0, the local profile class established in section4.

an experimental profile and pulse strength is an intervention specification,

OPE coefficient with dot B or dot D is needed for this geometric route, and
none is inferred.
