REVIEW OF THE PAIRED-QUARTER / LOWEST-BLOCK SOURCE CANDIDATE
29 October 2026

Purpose and verdict
The external Claude candidate is not an original-source derivation. Its
explicit quarter-pair conjugation is valid. Its claimed original choice of
the lowest spectral block reads the actual gap selector backwards and changes
the operator being filtered. The full two-face selfdual extension has the
standard opposite sign on its two conjugation summands. These are source
corrections and reusable conditional algebra, not a physical P1 no-go.

1. Exact original source, not an inferred low-energy convention

The actual displayed formula is

 P_(prim,gap) = 1_[epsilon_adm^2/4,infinity)(T),

The cutoff retains HIGH squared singular values of this compressed operator.
It is not a projection onto the first eigenvalue of |B_Sigma|. Neither the
operator identity T=|B_Sigma|^2 nor Hcar=ker(|B_Sigma|-lambda1) is supplied by

Even if the proposed operator identification is granted for a diagnostic,

2. What the quarter-pair conjugation actually proves
For A_a=-i partial_theta+a on periodic H1(S1), with 0<a<1, define

 B_a = 2(A_a directsum A_(1-a)),
 j(f,g)(theta) = exp(-i theta) (conj(g(theta)),conj(f(theta))).

Multiplication by exp(-i theta), conjugation, and summand exchange preserve
the full H1 domain. On each Fourier basis vector j exchanges (n,a) with
(-n-1,1-a); their B_a eigenvalues are opposite. Thus j^2=I and j B_a j=-B_a
as equalities of closed operators, not only inside a finite Fourier window.
For P=1_(B_a>0), j P j=I-P. For a=1/4 the absolute minimum is 1/2 in each
paired copy. Equal internal multiplicity d gives positive and negative
minimum eigenspaces of complex dimension d each, if that block is chosen.
The choice of that block, its multiplicity and its physical field map have
not followed from section 1.
On a single quarter operator A_(1/4), no antiunitary j with j A j=-A exists,
because its spectrum Z+1/4 is not invariant under negation. This excludes
that specified selfdual dynamical realization. It does NOT exclude a CAR
algebra on that Hilbert space, the original complex charged source, or an
alternative properly derived field conjugation.

3. The full two-face graph and its oriented conjugation
Let B be selfadjoint and j an antiunitary involution with jBj=-B. For ell>0,

 a=(I+exp(-2ell B))^-1, c=(2cosh(ell B))^-1,
 G=[[a,c],[c,I-a]].

The bounded spectral calculus gives c^2=a(I-a) and hence G^2=G=G^*.
It also gives jaj=I-a and jcj=c. Therefore the naive doubled conjugation
j directsum j obeys

 (j directsum j) G (j directsum j) = [[I-a,c],[c,a]],

whose difference from I-G is [[0,2c],[2c,0]]. For finite spectral values c
is nonzero. Correct separate face covariances therefore do not certify this
unmodified full-graph conjugation. With the oriented choice

 C = j directsum (-j)

one has C^2=I and CGC=I-G exactly, including both off-diagonal correlations.
This is the standard purification of a selfdual CAR covariance, not a new
general theorem. See H. Araki, On Quasifree States of CAR and Bogoliubov
Automorphisms, Publ. RIMS 6 (1970/71), 385-442, Definition 4.4 and Lemmas
4.5-4.6, p.391. DOI 10.2977/prims/1195193913.
Primary full text: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/41673
The one-face operator a is a mixed covariance: a^2-a=-c^2. It is not a
basis projection at finite ell and finite spectral values. Its purification
G is a basis projection for C. Applying the existing projector-state theorem
here does not add an independent quasifree premise to a given pure source.
But identifying this selfdual CAR functor and C with the physical original
field/adjoint map remains a separate actual-source identification.

4. No internal 3+2 no-go from a changed field type
If an invariant finite-dimensional complex space H has an involution F and
an antiunitary j with jFj=-F, then j bijects its two F eigenspaces. Their
dimensions are equal. This standard lemma applies to that full selfdual
one-particle space. It does not apply automatically to an internal P2
coefficient factor, a positive-polarization space E, or a spinor Fock module.
In particular an independent 3+2 internal involution on E need not equal F.
The D8 real vector (10,1)+(1,6) and its spinor sectors remain possible. No
new particle multiplicity is proved by a formal selfdual doubling.

5. Necessary next original-source step
The original J, normal Clifford multiplication, domain, reference and
reflection must be transported together to the actual two-face field map.
The algebraic C above is not by itself an OS reflection, a physical time
generator, or a proof that the original gap selects a P2 carrier. A common
operative source already includes its state; another arbitrary measure is
not demanded universally. Stationarity or absence of a displayed map alone
does not prove general underdetermination: any claimed full countermodels
must satisfy all the original operator, domain and action conditions.
This review retains both the useful conjugation repair and these limits.
