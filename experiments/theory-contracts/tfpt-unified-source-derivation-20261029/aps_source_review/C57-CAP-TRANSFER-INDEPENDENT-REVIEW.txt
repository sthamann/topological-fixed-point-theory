1. The OS unit cannot produce the nontrivial cap determinant
The original OS construction defines

  (F,G)_OS=<Theta F,G>_rel,
  T_ell[F]=[tau_ell F].

The algebra unit is translation invariant: tau_ell 1=1. Therefore

  T_ell[1]=[1],
  ([1],T_ell[1])_OS=||[1]||_OS^2=omega_rel(1).          (1)

For a normalized source, equation(1) is1. If an unnormalized convention is
kept, it is the fixed vacuum norm. Within ONE fixed reconstructed OS theory it
is independent of the semigroup time ell. This is the positive OS unit check,
not a nontrivial transfer amplitude.

This statement must not be extended across a family in which ell changes the
collar geometry, the functional omega_ell, the admissible projection or the
theory itself. In such a family ||[1]||_ell may carry an ell-dependent
unnormalized scalar. A separately supplied identification could then make that
scalar equal to a cap factor pointwise. The unit calculation neither forbids
such an extra source identity nor proves it. It blocks C57's inference of the
cap determinant from translation of the unit inside the SAME OS semigroup.

The fixed-cap determinant is different. FIXED-CAP-STATE.txt proves

  det_F C=<Omega0,exp(-ell H_exc)Omega0>,               (2)

where Omega0 is the old sea vector represented in the coupled-sea Fock space.
For nonzero coupling and finite ell it is not stationary under H_exc. Hence it
cannot be the OS vacuum vector [1], which is fixed by the reconstructed
semigroup. Equating

  Z_rel[0]=([1],T_ell[1])_OS=det_F C

therefore fails at its first identification. It also contradicts the explicit
ell dependence of det_F C unless the cap is trivial.

The correct OS-shaped insertion is a cap vector [F_cap] representing Omega0:

  ([F_cap],T_ell[F_cap])_OS
       =<Omega0,exp(-ell H_exc)Omega0>.                 (3)

Equivalently, if U_0h is the already implementable sea-rotation operator with
Omega0=U_0h Omega_h, then the right-hand side is

  <Omega_h,U_0h* exp(-ell H_exc) U_0h Omega_h>.

This is an inserted relative boundary amplitude. It is not the empty OS word
and does not determine the absolute original partition scalar, determinant-line
phase, reference subtraction or Z_adm. This is exactly the distinction retained
in FIXED-CAP-DETERMINANT.txt and FIXED-CAP-STATE.txt.

2. General finite-dimensional cap formula
Let h=h* be the coupled one-particle operator, Ph=1_(-infinity,0)(h), and P0
the fixed old occupied projection. On a finite mode block let Omega_P0 be its
old Slater vector and set

  E0=Tr(h Ph),
  H_exc=dGamma(h)-E0.

Then exterior functoriality gives the exact cap amplitude

  Z_cap(P0,h;ell)
    =<Omega_P0,exp(-ell H_exc)Omega_P0>
    =exp(ell Tr(h Ph))
       det_RanP0(P0 exp(-ell h) P0).                    (4)

The whole-circle version is the Fredholm/determinant-line limit already proved
for the stated fixed-cap source. Formula(4) displays both indispensable data:
the signed coupled operator h and the fixed occupied subspace P0. It is not a
class function of the eigenvalues of h^2 alone.

For the earlier constant scalar-quarter class,

  h0=p gamma,  h=h0-i gamma Phi,  {h0,h-h0}=0,

one has h^2=E^2 and the singular blocks canonically identify old and moving
occupied spaces. Formula(4) then reduces to the previously proved mode factor

  C_p=exp(-ell E) P0 exp(-ell h)P0
     =(I+|p|E^-1)/2
       +(I-|p|E^-1)exp(-2ell E)/2.                     (5)

Equation(5) is a special anticommuting reduction of (4), not the general cap
rule.

3. Why the real weak connection does not obey the old reduction
For the actual weak source reviewed in
ACTUAL-WEAK-CONNECTION-AND-JOINT-SOURCE.txt,

  h(t)=(pI+tQ)gamma-i gamma D_F,
  Q*=Q, [Q,gamma]=0.

Relative to h0=p gamma, the weak term does not anticommute:

  {p gamma,tQ gamma}=2ptQ.                              (6)

Consequently

  h(t)^2=(pI+tQ)^2+D_F^2-it[Q,D_F],

and neither the compression P0 exp(-ell h(t))P0 nor its determinant is fixed by
the unmarked spectrum of h(t)^2. The exact exponential contains the signed term

  exp(-ell h)=cosh(ell |h|)-h |h|^-1 sinh(ell |h|),    (7)

with the continuous value on ker h. Compression by P0 retains the second term.
Replacing (7) by a spectral function of h^2 deletes precisely the old/moving
cap orientation.

The smallest counterexample uses no new source construction. Take one existing
two-dimensional cap block, P0=P_-(sigma_z), and compare

  h1=E sigma_z,  h2=E sigma_x,  E>0.

Both have h_i^2=E^2 I and therefore identical h^2 spectra. Formula(4) gives

  Z_cap(P0,h1;ell)=1,
  Z_cap(P0,h2;ell)=exp(-ell E)cosh(ell E)
                  =(1+exp(-2ell E))/2.                 (8)

Thus even the complete h^2 operator is insufficient without P0 and the signed
compression. This two-dimensional pair is a general cap-algebra counterexample
to the proposed h^2-only inference. It is not asserted to be two complete
original v252 profiles with every source datum held fixed. The actual weak
connection is more structured; equation(6) proves only that its old
anticommuting shortcut is unavailable. Its original cap value must be evaluated
from formula(4) with the actual P0 and h(t).
