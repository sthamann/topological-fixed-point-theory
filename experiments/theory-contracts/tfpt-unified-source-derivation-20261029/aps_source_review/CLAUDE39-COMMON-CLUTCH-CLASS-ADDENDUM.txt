ALGEBRAIC CLOSURE OF THE COMMON LEADING-DISTANCE CLUTCH CLASS
==============================================================

Exact addendum, 30 October 2026.  The previous Claude39 review is unchanged.
No Atlas or original source was changed.

VERDICT
-------

The original three diagonal length spectra exclude EVERY common three-channel
leading-distance clutch in each of the following two classes.

Single leg:

  L_f=a_f P_f x+b_f 1,      a_f>0.                       (1)

Equal-weight two legs with the same q/p distance list:

  L_f=a_f(P_f x+Q_f x)+b_f 1,   a_f>0.                  (2)

Here x is any common real three-component list and P_f,Q_f are arbitrary
permutations.  Sector-dependent positive scale and offset are allowed.  The
result is therefore stronger than the earlier test of
x={1,3,5}/12.

It is scoped exactly to a COMMON list x and equal left/right magnitude in
(2).  Different q and p distance spectra, unequal left/right scales, extra
C6 windings, non-leading mode sums or operator-history dependent readouts
leave this class and are not excluded.


1. SINGLE-LEG CLASS
-------------------

For a sorted nondegenerate list x_0<x_1<x_2 define its gap quotient

  r(x)=(x_1-x_0)/(x_2-x_1).                              (3)

Permutation does not change the sorted spectrum.  Positive scaling and a
common offset do not change r.  Thus (1) forces the same r in every sector.

The original sorted rows and their quotients are

  L_u={0,3,7}: r_u=3/4,
  L_d={2,5,7}: r_d=3/2,
  L_e={3,5,8}: r_e=2/3.                                  (4)

They are not equal.  Therefore (1) is impossible for every common x.


2. COMPLETE SIX-PERMUTATION TWO-LEG CLASSIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------

If x has repeated entries, every spectrum in (2) is degenerate for the only
relative permutations that could otherwise give three values, so it cannot
produce the three nondegenerate rows (4).  Assume x has three distinct
entries.  An affine normalization, absorbed into a_f and b_f, puts

  x=(0,1,t),   t>1.                                      (5)

Apply the inverse of P_f to all three output coordinates.  The spectrum then
depends only on the relative permutation

  sigma=P_f^(-1)Q_f in S3

through x+sigma x.  The six possibilities are exhaustive:

  sigma                 spectrum multiset      result
  ---------------------------------------------------------------
  identity              {0,2,2t}              r=1/(t-1)
  (012)                 {1,t,t+1}             r=t-1
  (021)                 {1,t,t+1}             r=t-1
  (01)                  {1,1,2t}              repeated
  (02)                  {2,t,t}               repeated
  (12)                  {0,t+1,t+1}           repeated.          (6)

At t=2 the middle transposition becomes fully degenerate; it never supplies
a third nondegenerate form.  Thus every nondegenerate spectrum in (2) has
gap quotient

  r in {t-1,1/(t-1)}.                                   (7)

All sectors must consequently have the same quotient or its reciprocal.
The original d and e rows satisfy this relation:

  r_d=3/2,   r_e=2/3.

But the u row has

  r_u=3/4,   1/r_u=4/3,

which is neither 3/2 nor 2/3.  No t>1 can therefore produce all three
original rows.  This proves the exclusion of (2) without a scan or a choice
of particular clutch phases.


3. PHYSICAL AND SOURCE SCOPE
----------------------------

For a reflected q/p collar whose two legs carry the same leading magnitude
dist(alpha+kappa_a,Z), equation (2) is precisely the most generous class
obtained by allowing arbitrary family relabelings, sector scales and sector
offsets while retaining equal left/right decay weights.  The obstruction
above is therefore a complete algebraic exclusion of that proposed common
leading-distance mechanism.

The original source is not limited to this class.  It has sector-dependent
residue packets and actual C6 winding histories:

  R_u={0,1,3}, R_d={1,2,5}, R_e={2,3,5},

followed by one length-six winding in each first-generation direction.
Those data can create the three different gap patterns in (4), but they are
not consequences of a common leading distance x.  Likewise a full Schwinger
sum over all modes, unequal two-leg weights, or a further source operator may
produce another class.  None is ruled out here, and none may be called a
derivation of the original lengths until its actual operator map reproduces
the rows (4).

The established cover pi:C->X_f, sign-line local system and whole B_mu
endpoint response remain unchanged.
