2. THE NINE SECTORS AND THE DECISIVE SIX-CYCLE

Let Laq=O(q_a-q_(a+1)), Lbp=O(p_b-p_(b+1)). The existing direct sum
Jac(C)[2]=Vq+Vp implies Laq tensor Lbp is nontrivial for all nine (a,b).
Riemann-Roch and degree zero nontriviality give

The actual normalized joint Kummer lift from the six-line completion has
order six and gives the pullback permutation

 R:(a,b)->(b+1,a+1),

with orbits

 (0,0),(1,1),(2,2),
 (0,1),(2,1),(2,0),(1,0),(1,2),(0,2).

It follows that R^k has no fixed summands for k=1,2,4,5. R^3 fixes the
three diagonal summands. Its restriction there is an involution, so each
fixed summand contributes +/-1 to the trace. This proves that THESE full
nine-sector traces are zero or real, exactly as claimed. It does not prove
that every trace with arbitrary insertions, restrictions or eigenprojectors
has the same property.

Now restrict to V_off=direct_sum_(a!=b) V_ab. The six nonzero transition
maps form one monomial six-cycle. Their product is one because the chosen
geometric lift satisfies R^6=I, including its natural action on canonical
differentials. Consequently

 det(tI-R|V_off)=t^6-1.

This conclusion does NOT assume that every transition multiplier equals
one. In particular V_off contains a one-dimensional rho eigenspace.
For any nonzero v in one summand an explicit nonzero eigenvector is

 v_rho=sum_(j=0)^5 rho^-j R^j v,   R v_rho=rho v_rho.

Nonvanishing follows because its six components occupy distinct summands.
Equivalently the canonical spectral projector

 p_rho=(1/6)sum_(j=0)^5 rho^-j R^j

has rank one on V_off. Hence tr(p_rho R)=rho even though tr(R|V_off)=0.
This is an actual coherent eigenline in the stated mixed-sector system.
It is not an extra physical permission to prepare or measure that line.

A small exact enumeration independently returned orbit sizes [3,6] and
fixed counts [9,0,0,3,0,0] for powers zero through five. For the unweighted
permutation the characteristic polynomial is (t^3-1)(t^6-1). Only the
six-cycle factor is needed for the counterargument, and it remains valid
for the compatible geometric multipliers just described.

Even a real three-cycle matrix already has the complex eigenvalues

are different statements. Moreover the substitution on Kummer generators

4. PRECISE REMAINDER OF THE CP ARGUMENT

One correct special assertion survives: if an ENTIRE Yukawa matrix is
multiplied by one scalar phase, YY^dagger is unchanged. A factor entering
both complete Yukawa sectors only in that way cannot change their CP-odd
commutator invariant. That statement does not classify the mixed-sector
operator algebra or all allowed family-resolved output functionals.

Likewise a genuinely CP-invariant full configuration has vanishing CP-odd
invariants. To apply that fact here requires the actual induced Yukawa and
state maps. A sufficient finite matrix condition is

with one common unitary left-family map L and the appropriate right maps.
Then H_x=L overline(H_x)L^dagger and the CP-odd invariant vanishes.
Invariance of a geometric set of points/paths alone is not that condition;
all labels, contractions, reference data and source maps must transform.

It therefore does not follow that every original Real structure must be
broken as an oriented triangle before a physical phase can occur, nor
that the magnitude of a physical delta is calculable from Psi alone.
These conclusions need the original map from open transports to the two
Yukawa operators. The proposed regularized three-mark calculation cannot
be promoted to a source derivation by simply naming its output Y_ij.
