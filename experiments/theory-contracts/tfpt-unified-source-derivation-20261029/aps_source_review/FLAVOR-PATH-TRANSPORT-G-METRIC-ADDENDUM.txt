1. FIXED SOURCE METRIC

ORIGINAL-FUCHS-ALGEBRAIC-FRAME.txt gives an explicit fundamental matrix G(z)
for the same plus-sign equation Psi'=A Psi on the actual negative q Kummer
cover.  Its right deck matrices are unitary.  Hence

  H(z)=G(z)^(-dagger) G(z)^(-1)

is the fixed flat positive metric, unique up to an irrelevant positive
scalar.  No fit to P, Q or T is involved.

For x=1/2 let P:E_x->E_2 and Q:E_2->E_x be the actual outputs of
integrate_path_transport and put

  C_pair=diag(G_x,P G_x),
  H_pair=diag(H_x,H_2).

The signed three-cycle on the chosen lift is

  C_s=[[0,0,1],[-1,0,0],[0,-1,0]].

With the actual h signs h0=h1=-omega and h2=+omega, the algebraic G and the
numerically integrated path obey

  G_x^(-1) Q P G_x=C_s,
  C_pair^(-1) T_raw C_pair=[[0,C_s],[I,0]],
  C_pair^dagger H_pair C_pair=I.

Thus T_raw is unitary for the source metric even though it is not unitary for
the raw Euclidean coordinate metric.

only for the raw Euclidean coordinate adjoint.  The source adjoint is

  T_raw^sharp=H_pair^(-1) T_raw^dagger H_pair=T_raw^(-1).

operator.  After B it is exactly the adjoint used in

Consequently the original path, its source metric, T^dagger, D, Green and the
positive inverse square root now form one concrete finite operator chain.
The earlier report must not be read as leaving the metric or its
