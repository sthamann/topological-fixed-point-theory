CLAUDE29 CHIRAL E8 LIFT: INDEPENDENT v252 AND F5 TYPE REVIEW
29 October 2026

Scope

This is the bounded check of the proposed chirality dependent continuation in
CLAUDE29-K-E8-CHIRAL-LIFT-UNGEPRUEFT.txt.  It uses the actual 96 dimensional
full_D constructor, its KO6 grading and real structure, and the already proved
F5 graph typing.  It does not certify the candidate's separate assertion that
its family diagonal preserves every original K mark.  In fact
CLAUDE29-FIRST-K-MARK-REVIEW.txt has already found a line order error in that
assertion.

VERDICT

The proposed Yukawa formula is correct.  The proposed Majorana formula is
wrong by one power of omega.  For

  D=omega^2 S,  S=diag(1,-1,1),  omega^3=1,

and the proposed chiral continuation, the exact v252 result is

  Y'_f = omega S bar(Y_f) S,
  M'_R = omega^2 S bar(M_R) S.                         (1)

The correction does not destroy the proposed torus absorption.  It makes its
mechanism precise.  After the family sign S is removed, the actual Spin(10)
weight pattern from lambda_5 together with the stated common A3 family shift
acts on all Yukawas by omega^2 and on the Majorana term by omega.  Therefore it
maps the corrected pair (omega bar(Y_f),omega^2 bar(M_R)) exactly to
(bar(Y_f),bar(M_R)).  Lambda_5 alone does not do this.  The A3 shift is
essential.

This is an exact finite matrix identity in the displayed v252 field chart.  It
is still conditional as an original K/clock equivalence because the proposed
K mark identification is independently false in the current line ordering,
and the complete v252 to E8 weight dictionary is being checked separately.

1. THE MAJORANA BLOCK FIXES THE PHASE

The original constructor is

  Phi=[[S_Y,T_MR],[T_MR^*,bar(S_Y)]],

where T_MR is the top right map from particle nu_R to antiparticle nu_R.  Let
D_K act by

  D on particle L and antiparticle R,
  bar(D) on particle R and antiparticle L.

For the finite coefficient part of K_E=D_K kappa P_E one therefore has

  Phi^K=D_K bar(Phi) D_K^*.

On a particle L to particle R Yukawa block this gives

  D bar(Y_f) (bar(D))^* = D bar(Y_f) D^T
                         = omega S bar(Y_f) S.

On the particle nu_R to antiparticle nu_R Majorana block it gives instead

  bar(D) bar(M_R) D^* = bar(D) bar(M_R) bar(D)^T
                       = omega^2 S bar(M_R) S.

The second formula is forced by the actual position of T_MR.  Reusing the
Yukawa row and column types for this block produces the candidate's erroneous
common omega.

A targeted replay imported verification/v252_full_finite_triple.py and used
deterministic Gaussian integer matrices with seed 2929.  It found

  ||Phi^K-Phi(equation (1))|| = 3.26e-14.

The same calculation with the candidate's omega Majorana formula differs by a
macroscopic nonzero norm.  This is a structural block error, not numerical
roundoff.

3. THE ACTUAL SPIN(10) TORUS PATTERN

The original weight source v109 constructs the chiral spinor as the sixteen
weights (epsilon_1,...,epsilon_5)/2 with even sign parity.  In the standard
even exterior chart, for an even subset A of {1,...,5}, the lambda pairing is

  P(A)=sum_(i in A) lambda_i - (1/2)sum_i lambda_i.

For

  lambda_5=(1/6,1/6,1/6,1/2,1/2)

the six Standard Model weight classes have the following values:

  nu^c : -3/4        empty subset,
  u^c  : -5/12       two colour indices,
  Q    : -1/12       one colour and one weak index,
  e^c  :  1/4        two weak indices,
  d^c  :  7/12       four indices, missing colour,
  L    :  1/4        four indices, missing weak.

Add the common A3 family shift +1/12 on the selected family triplet, with
-1/4 on the fourth slot so that the A3 trace remains zero.  Relative to Q the
resulting phases are exactly

  Q:0,
  u^c,d^c:-1/3,
  L,e^c,nu^c:+1/3                         modulo 1. (4)

Thus the corresponding complex linear unitary T in the v252 particle chart
acts as

  Q_L:1,  L_L:omega,
  u_R,d_R:omega,  e_R,nu_R:omega^2,

and by conjugate phases on antiparticles.  Direct block multiplication gives

  T Phi(Y_f,M_R) T^* = Phi(omega^2 Y_f,omega M_R).     (5)

The common A3 shift is not cosmetic.  Lambda_5 without it gives factor -1 on
both Y_f and M_R.  Its opposite contributions to Dirac and Majorana bilinears
are what change these into the two different factors in (5).

Let L_S apply S to the three family slots on particles and antiparticles.
Combining (1) and (5) yields the exact identity

  T L_S Phi^K L_S^* T^*
     = Phi(bar(Y_f),bar(M_R)).                         (6)

4. WHAT EQUATION (6) DOES AND DOES NOT ESTABLISH

Equation (6) repairs the finite coefficient part of Claude29 more strongly
than the printed common omega formula: the corrected unequal phases are
exactly the phases required by the actual Spin(10) weight action.

It does not by itself prove that T L_S is an allowed element of the original
marked K action.  The candidate identifies its S with iota_mq(-I5), but the
actual G column order is (L0,L2,L1).  The independent first K mark review
shows that iota_mq(-I5) puts the minus sign on the wrong line and has a
different commutator with the quarter coefficient.  Therefore the statements
"D_K lies in K", "it commutes with Q4 and M_q", and "all marks are preserved"
cannot be inherited from the candidate as written.

The weight calculation proves the relative Spin(10) torus phase, and the A3
shift is an abstract trace zero family torus element.  The remaining finite
dictionary question is whether that combined torus action, with the corrected
line permutation and native clock mark, is the same operation on the actual
v252 states.  No equality of dimensions or labels settles that point.

Finally, (6) is a covariance from Phi to bar(Phi).  It becomes a fixed source
antiunitary only when the given controls are invariant under this conjugation
up to an independently allowed source equivalence.  A CKM invariant can
obstruct such a fixed generalized CP symmetry, but the finite block
calculation does not prove the candidate's unrestricted "if and only if"
claim.  It also gives no global CP or OS obstruction: physical Theta, J_F,
C0 and the E0 family reflection are distinct typed operations.
