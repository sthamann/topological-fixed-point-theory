VERDICT

The proposed annulus operator is an exact whole-source covariance to the
already derived q-to-p image source. Put

  K_F=Kswap,  T=K_F R_x,
  Theta_-=P_r R_x tensor Z tensor gamma_F K_F.                 (1)

On the smooth constant-frame annulus it carries

  D_q=N partial_r+S A_q+Z Phi_q

to D_p with A_p=-R_x A_q R_x^-1 and
Phi_p=-bar(Phi_q) composed with inv. It needs no second freely chosen
background. On the boundary it sends B_q to -B_p; the radial face swap then
turns the whole two-face graph into the standard target graph G(B_p).

The Real/type calculation is more precise than Claude33's phase conclusion.
Theta_- is OLD-linear and NEW-antilinear, squares to -I, reverses C0 on each
face and preserves the oriented C_faces after exchanging the faces. It
anticommutes with the finite antiunitary J_F. The operator i_old Theta_-
instead commutes with J_F and squares to +I. Both have the same conjugation
action on D, B and G, but they are different maps on odd CAR fields.

Consequently the geometric spin condition r^6=-I selects Theta_- only after
an actual spinframe-to-F5 field intertwiner fixes the remaining i_old phase.
The operator and graph identities alone do not fix it. Exact commutation with
the displayed J_F would select i_old Theta_-, whereas allowing J_F to go to
-J_F retains Theta_-; the two signs implement the same opposite-algebra
action but differ as fixed reference operators. C_faces does not distinguish
the two because i_old is OLD-complex-linear and commutes with C_faces.

The actual circle evolution is transported with the SAME parameter t. The
quarter domain is carried from alpha to -alpha modulo one. The smooth annulus
gluing is preserved when the radial and tangential inversions act together,
but the original Fuchs corner/reference domain still requires its explicit
endpoint transport. No P1 execution or physical source choice follows.

1. WHOLE COLLAR OPERATOR

Use the actual normal matrices

  N=[[0,-1],[1,0]],  Z=diag(1,-1),  S=N Z.                    (2)

The radial pullback P_r reverses partial_r. The tangential pullback obeys

  R_x A_q R_x^-1=-A_p.                                      (3)

Conjugation by the normal Z reverses N and S and preserves Z. On the finite
coefficient, the actual v252 matrices give

  K_F gamma_F K_F^-1=-gamma_F,
  K_F Phi_q K_F^-1=bar(Phi_q),
  (gamma_F K_F) Phi_q (gamma_F K_F)^-1=-bar(Phi_q).          (4)

The last equality uses oddness of Phi_q. The three terms therefore transform
as

  N partial_r -> N partial_r,
  S A_q       -> S A_p,
  Z Phi_q     -> Z[-bar(Phi_q) composed with inv].           (5)

This proves

  Theta_- D_q Theta_-^-1=D_p.                               (6)

The plus lift with finite factor K_F would instead target +bar(Phi_q). Both
are algebraically valid family maps, but only (1) reaches the negative target
already independently derived from Ucal. That agreement fixes the target
sign for this source map; it does not by itself fix the field-lift phase.

2. F5 TYPES AND THE TWO REAL STRUCTURES

On one boundary face retain

  Gamma=Z tensor gamma_F,  Ical=i_old Gamma,
  C0=i_old N tensor I.                                      (7)

Let W=R_x tensor Z tensor gamma_F K_F denote the boundary part of Theta_-.
Direct multiplication gives

  W Gamma W^-1=-Gamma,
  W Ical W^-1=-Ical,
  W C0 W^-1=-C0,
  W^2=-I.                                                    (8)

Thus W is NEW-antilinear. On both faces use

  C_faces=diag(C0,-C0),
  Theta_faces=face_swap tensor W.                            (9)

The face swap reverses the two diagonal signs while W reverses C0, hence

  Theta_faces C_faces Theta_faces^-1=C_faces.                (10)

This is the relevant selfdual CAR condition. It proves that Theta_faces
defines a star-preserving order-preserving map on the complete selfdual field
algebra. C0 alone is not preserved on one face, so calling Theta_- a
one-face Majorana symmetry would be wrong.

The finite KO6 antiunitary is J_F=K_F kappa_split. All matrices in (1) are
real in the checked chart. Since K_F anticommutes with gamma_F,

  J_F Theta_- J_F^-1=-Theta_-,
  J_F(i_old Theta_-)J_F^-1=i_old Theta_-.                    (11)

The second equality uses anti-linearity of J_F. Thus the question left open
by Claude33 has a definite answer: Theta_- preserves C_faces but reverses the
chosen phase of J_F; i_old Theta_- preserves both C_faces and the displayed
J_F. Replacing J_F by -J_F changes neither J_F^2 nor its opposite-algebra
action. Whether its phase is fixed by the original reference is separate
source data and cannot be inferred from the square of Theta_-.

As in the preceding q-to-p review, K_F also transports the displayed finite
algebra representation to pi_p(a)=K_F pi_q(a)K_F^-1; it does not commute with
generic pi_q(a). Thus (6) is a covariance of finite source triples. A
same-marked-algebra symmetry still needs the original algebra automorphism
which identifies those two bimodule actions.

3. RELATION TO R_GEO Ucal AND THE FIELD/ADJOINT MAP

The actual normal identity is

  S(i_old N)=i_old Z.                                       (12)

With R_geo=face_swap tensor S tensor gamma_F and
Ucal=i_old N tensor K_F R_x, it follows exactly that

  R_geo Ucal=i_old Theta_faces.                             (13)

The old scalar i_old cancels from operator conjugation, which explains why
both sides give the same D, B and graph transport. It does not cancel from
the one-particle field map or its square.

This can be seen in the actual charged chart. Put

  P_plus/minus=(I plus/minus gamma_F)/2,
  E v=(P_plus v,i_old P_minus v),
  T=K_F R_x.                                                (14)

Then

  W E=-C0 E T,
  W C0 E=E T.                                               (15)

Hence W exchanges field and adjoint slots. In representative notation the
complete T_faces map is

  (v0,v1,bar(w0),bar(w1))
    ->(-T w1, T w0, overline(-T v1), overline(T v0)).        (16)

The signs in (16) include the fixed opposite C0 convention on the second
face. This is a genuine Bogoliubov particle/adjoint exchange, not Ucal acting
separately on two unchanged charged entries.

Because Theta_faces commutes with C_faces, its induced CAR map sends a field
c(u) to c(Theta_faces u), preserves the star and preserves product order.
Reordering the images of two odd fields into a preferred q-before-p order
still produces the forced Koszul minus from the preceding CAR/U6 review.
Moreover

  alpha_Theta^2=fermion parity,

whereas the lift i_old Theta has square +I on one-particle fields. Thus the
phase ambiguity is invisible to B and G but visible on odd fields and their
ordered products. An explicit spinframe/F5 field intertwiner is required
before the geometric r^6=-I can decide between them.

4. FACE-SWAPPED GRAPH AND STATE

The boundary operator is

  B(A,Phi)=Z tensor A-S tensor Phi.                           (17)

Equations (3)-(4) give

  W B_q W^-1=-B_p.                                          (18)

For the graph functions a(-x)=I-a(x) and c(-x)=c(x). Therefore W on both
face fibers changes G(B_q) into the face-swapped matrix of G(B_p). The
explicit face swap in Theta_faces changes it back to the target's standard
label order:

  Theta_faces G(B_q) Theta_faces^-1=G(B_p).                  (19)

Geometrically the two annulus boundaries have been exchanged; algebraically
(19) is written in the target source's ordinary face order. The already
defined pure graph covariance is therefore transported, including both
crossing blocks. No second covariance or state is added.

A targeted actual96 replay with generic distinct complex full_D values at
two tangential points gave zero residual for (6), (8), (10), (11), (15) and
(18). The full graph residual in (19) was 4.88e-14.

5. DOMAIN, REFERENCE AND ACTUAL TIME

The tangential pullback sends the periodic quarter data as

  alpha_p=-alpha_q mod 1,  U_p=U_q^-1.                      (20)

Thus it exchanges the 1/4 and 3/4 blocks. This is transport, not a fixed
quarter claim.

For a smooth constant-frame annulus domain f(ell)=u_q f(0), radial exchange
alone inverts the gluing orientation. If in addition

  R_x u_q R_x^-1=u_q^-1,                                   (21)

then the two inversions cancel and Theta_- preserves the displayed glued
domain. Equation (21) is the precise condition behind Claude33's domain
sentence. It does not identify the tangential quarter holonomy (20) with the
radial clutch u_q.

For a variable frame, the original endpoint factors H(0),H(ell) must be
inserted in (21). At the Fuchs corners z=plus/minus1 the smooth pullback does
not by itself prove preservation of the closed corner domain or its selected
reference subspace. The reference is transported to its Theta image; it is
fixed only if the original reference data satisfy that extra equivariance.
These are the remaining domain checks, not reasons to introduce another p
background.

Finally, (8) and (18) give

  Theta_faces (Ical B_q) Theta_faces^-1=Ical B_p.            (22)

Therefore

  Theta_faces exp(t Ical B_q) Theta_faces^-1
    =exp(t Ical B_p).                                        (23)

The actual circle parameter is +t. A replay of (22)-(23) gave zero generator
residual and time residual 2.25e-16. This statement concerns the existing
circle evolution; it does not identify it with positive radial OS time.

FINAL SCOPE

Theta_- gives a complete conditional whole-source map on the smooth annulus:
operator, q-to-p image coefficient, selfdual field/adjoint chart, both faces,
full graph state and circle time all transport together. The exact remaining
source question is narrower than a second-background problem: prove that the
original geometric spin inversion uses the same field lift and old-i phase,
and verify the variable-frame Fuchs corner/reference domain.

The spin equality r^6=-I is relevant only after that intertwiner is supplied.
Until then it cannot be used to upgrade equality of operator conjugations to
equality of whole CAR field maps or to declare a physical P1 execution.
