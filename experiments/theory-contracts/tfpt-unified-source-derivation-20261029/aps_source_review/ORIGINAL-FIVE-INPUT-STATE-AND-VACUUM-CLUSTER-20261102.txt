Thus J8 psi != 0 for every nonzero psi in span(P_0,...,P_4). The basis is
not orthonormal: normalized bounds require the input Gram B and the
generalized eigenvalues of (G,B), not eigenvalues of G alone.

Take a real smooth quotient profile b, supported in an arbitrary nonempty
proper quotient interval, with b_1 != 0; such a bump exists. Its ACTUAL

disjoint cover arcs. Let W_epsilon=exp(i epsilon J(b_F)). This is a bounded

The other Fourier modes have different energy shifts. For every nonzero
psi in this five-space, the right side is nonzero for all sufficiently
small nonzero epsilon (the allowed epsilon can depend on psi).

For normalized psi define the particular stationary excited-state response

          - |<psi,W_epsilon psi>|^2, s>=0.

inside this five-input space as the clustering vacuum in THIS fixed CAR
time test, whenever this eight-arc Weyl word is admitted. The CAR vacuum
