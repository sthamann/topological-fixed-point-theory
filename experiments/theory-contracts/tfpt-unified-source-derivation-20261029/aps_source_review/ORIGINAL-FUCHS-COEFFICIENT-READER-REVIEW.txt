ORIGINAL FUCHS COEFFICIENT READER REVIEW
Independent narrow source review, 2026-10-30. No Atlas or original source changed.

QUESTION

The stored geometric Spin(2) whole-source arrow leaves a sharp final reader
question.  The geometric rho line has c-character rho^2=omega.  Total deck
descent therefore retains it only with a coefficient leg of inverse character
omega^2.  Does the actual original v252/P2 Yukawa and Pi_triangle consumer
already contract precisely that coefficient channel?

VERDICT

No such contraction is present in the checked original reader.  The exact
Fuchs matrix does contain the required omega^2 coefficient eigenspace, but it
contains all three Pc eigenspaces.  Its deck matrix Pc is a cyclic permutation
with spectrum {1,omega,omega^2} and trace zero.  It is not the native scalar
family centre Z_F=omega^2 I3.  The original open hard holonomies use the full
transport matrix, and the closed phase consumer is exactly

  Pi_triangle=Hol_12 Hol_23 Hol_31,
  delta_CKM=arg tr Pi_triangle.

Neither formula inserts the rank-one omega^2 coefficient projector.  The
positive K_f^str kernel also does not provide it.  Thus the geometric rho line
and the original CKM reader are not yet connected by an actual source
operator.  This is a scoped statement about the displayed original reader,
not an exclusion of a relative phase in a larger open-leg evaluation.

1. ACTUAL ORIGINAL CONSUMER

The primary source formula is

  (Y_f)_ij=<e_i,Hol_GD4(nabla_F^star) K_f^str e_j>,

and, after diagonal reduction,

  (Y_f)_ij=lambda_Y^(L_i^L+L_j^R)
             <e_i,Hol_Gamma_ij^min(nabla_F^star)e_j>.

The phase consumer then closes three full holonomies and takes their ordinary
trace.  These are Hom(F,F) operators.  No geometric coefficient line, inverse
character evaluation, or one-sided coefficient projector occurs in the
displayed operator formula.

The structural kernel is

  K_f^str=(P_str D_f^dagger D_f P_str)^(-1/2),

with D_f=y I-delta U6.  It is a positive, orientation-even function of the
hexagonal transport.  It cannot by itself supply the oriented one-dimensional
Pc character required below.

2. EXACT FUCHS COEFFICIENT ACTION

The actual algebraic fundamental matrix satisfies

  G(c y)=G(y) Pc,
  Pc=[[0,0,1],[1,0,0],[0,1,0]],
  Pc^3=I.

Thus Pc is a right action on the coefficient columns.  For ordinary open
transport on a common lifted sheet,

  H_ij=G(y_i)G(y_j)^(-1),

the simultaneous right action cancels:

  (G_i Pc)(G_j Pc)^(-1)=G_i G_j^(-1).

For a closed path carrying deck c, the normalized monodromy is instead

  M_c=G_base Pc G_base^(-1).

It has the same character as Pc.  In particular

  spec(Pc)={1,omega,omega^2},   tr(Pc)=tr(M_c)=0.

The exact projector onto the needed inverse-character line is

  P_(omega^2)=(I+omega Pc+omega^2 Pc^2)/3,
  Pc P_(omega^2)=omega^2 P_(omega^2),

and has rank one.  This projector is absent from the original Y_f and
Pi_triangle formulas.  Even under the strongest direct identification of the
canonical family basis with the three coefficient slots, the displayed e_i
are not individual Pc eigenvectors.  Their use therefore does not silently
perform this projection.

3. SMALLEST DECISIVE COUNTEREXAMPLE

The tempting identification is

  Pc  ?=  Z_F=omega^2 I3.

It fails before any dynamics or state is considered:

  tr(Pc)=0,               tr(Z_F)=3 omega^2,
  minpoly(Pc)=x^3-1,      minpoly(Z_F)=x-omega^2.

Hence Pc and Z_F are not conjugate.  Replacing one by the other changes the
actual closed consumer.  Moreover, in the Hom(F,F) Yukawa action the scalar
centre cancels by conjugation,

  Z_F H Z_F^dagger=H.

It becomes observable only if the source places it on a single open leg before
the final evaluation.  No such one-sided insertion occurs in the checked
Pi_triangle definition.

This also separates two labels that must not be merged.  The q/p labels of the
geometric Kummer cover are source-sheet labels.  The quark/lepton split in the
positive P2 module is a physical carrier grading.  The established target
operator Z_F S_col has the desired rho/rho^4 eigenvalues, but that fact does not
define an intertwiner from the Fuchs coefficient line to the P2 family leg.

4. FIRST MISSING TYPED ARROW

The smallest remaining source statement is an open-leg coefficient reader,
for example an intertwiner J_coeff with

  J_coeff : im P_(omega^2)(Pc) -> H_family,
  J_coeff Pc=Z_F J_coeff

up to the inverse convention chosen for the geometric leg.  It must occur
before the Hom(F,F) closure and trace.  The existing geometric whole-source
map proves that this is the correct inverse character; it does not provide
J_coeff.  The checked original v252/P2/Yukawa sources likewise do not display
it.

This is the first missing operator, not a request to introduce a new rho line,
a second p source, a freely chosen state, or another lift.  A future positive
identification must point to an original insertion or evaluation leg that
satisfies this intertwining equation in the actual source basis.
