INDEPENDENT REVIEW OF THE ALL-SIX HARDY HOM FRAME AND COEFFICIENT ALGEBRA
30 October 2026. Read-only review of the existing source calculation.

VERDICT
The two-end frame formula, the six common-frame matrices and the M3
coefficient-algebra conclusion are correctly typed in the stated scope.
I found no material mathematical error.

The result is restricted to the already given Phi=0, family-blind NS
Hardy source and its induced finite reader. It does not identify ordinary
matrix multiplication with multiplication of source fields, CAR operators
or physical insertions, and it does not make this response the whole
Yukawa matrix or a source-selection law.

1. THE COMMON HOM FRAME IS FORCED BY BOTH ENDPOINTS

ORIGINAL-FUCHS-ALGEBRAIC-FRAME.txt fixes

  G=[g,c*g,c^2*g],             c:G -> G Pc,
  Pc=[[0,0,1],[1,0,0],[0,1,0]],

with column j carrying h_(-j). Therefore the input chart whose column zero
carries h_a is exactly

  G_a=G Pc^(-a).

This is a column transport in the existing differential frame. It is not
an independently chosen identification of three equal-dimensional fibers.
Since Pc is real orthogonal,

  G_a^(-*) G_a^(-1)=G^(-*) G^(-1).

For the fixed Phi=0 source, the tangential operator and V_h are family
blind. Hence the constant right factor preserves the transported H1_NS
domain, the Hardy projection, the graph isometry and the defect embedding:

  E^(a)=E Pc^(-a).

This step would not follow for an arbitrary fixed family-coupled Phi.

GEOMETRIC-R-SPIN-COLLAR-REVIEW.txt fixes R*h_a^q=h_(a+1)^p. Thus p_b is
read by J E^(b-1), and a scalar cable matrix and a matrix in the one common
G frame obey

  Mscalar_ab=Pc^(b-1) Mcommon_ab Pc^(-a),
  Mcommon_ab=Pc^(1-b) Mscalar_ab Pc^a.

Both factors are necessary. Replacing this formula by a one-sided cycling
or by Ad(Pc) on one fixed matrix would lose the Hom(F_q,F_p) typing.

2. SIX RESPONSES

The inspected replay records 54 entries: 12 signed continuous-root periods
and 42 support or eta-parity zeros. After the two-end frame transport its
six matrices are

  mu01 -> F,             mu12 -> omega^2 F,   mu20 -> omega F,
  mu10 -> N,             mu21 -> omega^2 N,   mu02 -> omega N,

where

  F=alpha E02+beta E21,
  N=-i(alpha E12+beta E20).

The maximum common-frame residual in the 65-digit result is
5.30818374111834e-66. The two reverse periods were also independently
evaluated directly in ROOT-TWO-REVERSE-MU10-PERIODS-CHECK.json and agree
with -i times the corresponding old coefficients. Both alpha and beta are
nonzero; their ratio is the previously proved real nonunit value
I21/I02=-3.347597664025180155... . No phase fit enters this conclusion.

3. EXACT COEFFICIENT ALGEBRA

For alpha beta nonzero set

  A=F^2/(alpha beta)=E01,
  B=-N^2/(alpha beta)=E10.

Then AB=E00 and BA=E11. With E22=I-E00-E11 one recovers

  E02=E00 F E22/alpha,       E21=E22 F E11/beta,
  E12=i E11 N E22/alpha,     E20=i E22 N E00/beta.

Their products give the remaining matrix units. Therefore the unital
coefficient algebra generated by F and N is M3(C), and its common complex
commutant is scalar.

The generating statement concerns F and N together. Their two squares
alone give only the embedded upper two-by-two block and do not generate
M3(C). HARDY-BMU-ALL-SIX-INPUT-READER.txt states the stronger conclusion
with the correct generators and does not make the squares-only error.

4. EXACT SCOPE

The six matrices are responses to six already existing off-diagonal
induced inputs in the fixed finite reader. The M3 result is algebraic
accessibility inside their common coefficient chart. It is not a proof
that the original source supplies response words as composable physical
operations. Such a claim would require an original consumer that composes
the corresponding field or CAR insertions. The existing WEDGE, Clifford
and KO consumer applies linearly to each computed coefficient, which is
all that is used here.
