OFFDIAGONAL HOM, THE ORIGINAL K BUNDLE, AND THE ACTUAL E8 PRODUCT TEST
Independent bounded source review, 30 October 2026.
No Atlas mutation and no change to a registered report.

VERDICT

After the geometrically fixed p-to-q pullback, the six off-diagonal
components of Hom(Fq,Fp) do admit an explicit normalized A4-equivariant
unitary bundle isomorphism

  OffDiag End(F)  ~=  F direct-sum Lambda^2 F
                    = Lambda^2(O direct-sum F),                 (1)

where F=L0 direct-sum L1 direct-sum L2 is the triplet of nontrivial sign
lines. This is more than a dimension match: it uses the old square
trivializations beta_a, L0 L1 L2=O, the fixed product-one normalization,
and it intertwines the actual V4 signs and the cubic permutation c.

Equation (1) is nevertheless NOT the family map induced by bracketing the
two given triplet fields. Both coefficient legs land in the same
triplet F inside Kfam=O direct-sum F. Their actual family wedge has image only
Lambda^2 F, of rank three. The symmetric off-diagonal triplet in (1)
would have to land in O wedge F and therefore requires one genuine
singlet input. The selected closed spin source has

  H^0(C,S0 tensor O)=0,

so no singlet zero mode can be inserted silently. The full local field
bundle may contain an O leg, but using it requires an actual original
field insertion and the matching D5 spinor bilinear. Neither is supplied
by the six q/p products alone.

There is a second sharp boundary. The map (1) is A4-equivariant in the
fixed two-torsion chart, but it is not an SU4 intertwiner and it already
fails the original quarter generator on its symmetric triplet. Thus it
identifies the restriction of the family six to the actual A4 sign-line
system; it does not identify the full native (10,6) field slot.

  mu_ab in H^0(K_C tensor Hom(L_a^q,L_b^p)),

This notation separates the rank-three triplet F from reports that call

2. THE s AND R COMPARISON HAS NO NEW PHASE

The normalized Kummer lift already fixes

  s^*h_a^p=h_a^q,  s^*h_a^q=h_a^p,
  c^*h_a^(q/p)=h_(a-1)^(q/p).                                 (3)

Thus s=R^3 pulls the p endpoint back to the corresponding q endpoint in
the fixed frames. The other inversion representative is

  R=s c^2,  R^2=c,  R^3=s.

Its spin lifts satisfy

  Rhat=shat chat^-1,  Rhat^2=-chat,  Rhat^6=-I.                (4)

Therefore R and s do not define two freely adjustable comparisons. Their
ratio is the existing c transport, including its fixed spin lift. In a
two-field product the common central spin signs cancel, while the residual
c^2 label shift is handled by c-equivariance. Equations (3)-(4) justify
the following comparison on one q chart. They do not create the missing
singlet family leg.


3. EXPLICIT NORMALIZED A4 BUNDLE ISOMORPHISM

Choose unit flat frames e_a of L_a and the product-one orientation
e_0 e_1 e_2=1. Put e_inf=1 in O. For each cyclic triple

  (a,b,c)=(0,1,2),(1,2,0),(2,0,1),

write E_ab=e_a tensor e_b^vee and define

  f_c=e_inf wedge e_c,
  g_c=e_a wedge e_b,

  T(E_ab)=(f_c+g_c)/sqrt(2),
  T(E_ba)=(f_c-g_c)/sqrt(2).                                  (5)

Both f_c and g_c have line type L_c: beta identifies L_b^vee with
L_b, and L_a L_b=L_c follows from L0 L1 L2=O. Consequently (5) is a
typed bundle map, not a map between bare six-dimensional fibers.

The six displayed target vectors are orthonormal, so T^*T=I. Equivalently,

  (E_ab+E_ba)/sqrt(2) |-> f_c,
  (E_ab-E_ba)/sqrt(2) |-> g_c.                                (6)

The actual V4 sign matrices and c are determinant-one signed
permutations of (e0,e1,e2). Direct substitution in (5) gives

  T Ad(g)=Lambda^2(1 direct-sum g) T

for the two V4 generators and c, hence for A4=V4 semidirect C3. The
normalizations beta and product h0 h1 h2=1 remove any extra relative
phase. This proves (1) as a normalized A4 bundle statement.

An exact six-by-six fiber audit gives T^*T=I and intertwiner residual
ranks zero for c and both V4 generators. For the quarter matrix in (7)
the residual has rank three. These ranks are consequences of (5), not a
numerical fit.

It also keeps E_ab and E_ba distinct. Holomorphic multiplication of their
underlying scalar line sections is commutative, but the Hom arrows remain
separate; their sum and difference in (6) are precisely the two target
triplets.


4. FIRST COUNTERTESTS BEYOND A4

The original quarter action on the triplet contains the determinant-one
signed permutation

  Qe0=-e1,  Qe1=e0,  Qe2=e2.                                 (7)

Conjugation sends E01+E10 to its negative. Formula (6) sends this vector
to f2=e_inf wedge e2, which is fixed by Lambda^2(1 direct-sum Q).
Thus T does not intertwine Q on the symmetric triplet. The antisymmetric
combination E01-E10 and g2=e0 wedge e1 are both fixed, so the failure is
exactly the additional O wedge F half, not the old wedge half.

A generic diagonal SU3 element gives the same obstruction without relying
on (7). If U=diag(t0,t1,t2), t0 t1 t2=1, then

  Ad(U)E_ab has weight t_a/t_b,
  e_inf wedge e_c has weight t_c,
  e_a wedge e_b has weight t_a t_b=t_c^-1.                    (8)

These weights agree for the retained order-two signs, but not generically.
Therefore (1) cannot be promoted to an SU3 or SU4 intertwiner. The full
SU4 family six remains Lambda^2 Kfam with its original marking; (1) is
only its A4-restricted sign-line chart.


5. THE DECISIVE E8 PRODUCT TEST

For two same-chirality D5 spinors the relevant E8 bracket uses

  Sym^2(16) -> 10

on the carrier factor and

  Lambda^2(4) -> 6

on the family factor. The two parities combine to the antisymmetric Lie
bracket. Now insert the actual q and pulled-back p triplet fields. Their
family coefficients x,y both lie in F subset Kfam. Hence

  x wedge y in Lambda^2 F,                                  (9)

and the family image has dimension at most three. On an off-diagonal
ordered pair, antisymmetrizing the two family legs keeps

  E_ab-E_ba |-> e_a wedge e_b

and kills the symmetric combination. It cannot turn that symmetric
combination into e_inf wedge e_c. Formula (5) is therefore not
product-preserving for the given two-triplet bracket.

To populate the other triplet O wedge F, one input must carry the O
coefficient and the other an F coefficient. The actual selected closed
source gives

  H^0(C,S0 tensor L_a)=1 for a=0,1,2,
  H^0(C,S0 tensor O)=0.                                     (10)

Thus the six mixed triplet zero-mode products do not contain the required
singlet field. One may not manufacture it from the symmetric scalar
product L_a L_b=L_c: that equality identifies line types, whereas the E8
bracket still wedges two distinct family vectors.

Finally, the family calculation alone does not supply the D5 vector 10.
An actual realization of the remaining half of (10,6) needs both an
original O-valued field insertion and the source-specified symmetric
Spin(10) bilinear into 10, with the transported marking and domain. The
existence of the full local O bundle does not by itself provide that
operator or a nonzero global spin zero mode.


6. PRECISE SCOPE AND NEXT ORIGINAL QUESTION

Established:

  * the p-to-q comparison is fixed by the old Kummer and spin lifts;
  * OffDiag End(F) and Lambda^2(O+F) are explicitly, isometrically and
    A4-equivariantly isomorphic in the two-torsion chart;
  * the old six off-diagonal arrows therefore carry the same restricted
    A4 bundle type as the family six.

Excluded for the present source inputs:

  * derivation of the full family six by bracketing the two triplet fields;
  * full SU4 covariance of (5), and even covariance under the tested
    original quarter generator on the symmetric triplet;
  * identification of the coefficient six alone with the physical
    E8 (10,6) Higgs/current field.

The smallest remaining positive source question is now sharply typed:
does an already original full-field insertion provide one S0 tensor O
leg together with a (16,4) carrier field and the actual Sym^2(16)->10
bracket? If such an insertion exists, its bracket with the known triplet
can populate O wedge F. It is not supplied by the closed zero-mode sector
and is not introduced here.
