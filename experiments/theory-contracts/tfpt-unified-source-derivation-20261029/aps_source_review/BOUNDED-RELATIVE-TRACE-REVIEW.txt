1. Exact operator/profile premises
Let H be the original infinite-dimensional source Hilbert space. Let D_ref
be selfadjoint elliptic on a compact geometric realization with compact
resolvent and its actual closed domain. Let V=V* be BOUNDED on H, and P an
orthogonal projection with infinite-dimensional RanP. Absorb the fixed positive
chi into D_ref,V. For the original even profile notation this means
  f(D_rel/chi)=F((D_rel/chi)^2), V=D_rel/chi.

Take a nonzero positive Laplace profile
  F(x)=integral_[0,infinity) exp(-s x) dmu(s), mu>=0,
finite on spec(V^2), so that the ordinary bounded operator F(V^2) is defined.
For example every finite nonzero positive measure gives such a profile.
Assume separately that K=F(D_ref^2) is trace class. Compact resolvent alone
does not imply this for every possible positive Laplace mixture; the
trace-class condition is an explicit premise, satisfied by ordinary heat
profiles of a compact elliptic realization.

2. Whole bounded-operator proof
Put M=||V||. Since spec(V^2) is contained in[0,M^2], positivity of the measure
gives monotonicity and the strict finite lower bound
  F(x)>=F(M^2)=c>0, 0<=x<=M^2.
Strict positivity follows because exp(-s M^2)>0 at every finite s and mu
is a nonzero positive measure. Functional calculus therefore gives
  F(V^2)>=c I,
  P F(V^2)P>=c P.
No commutation of P and V, or of P and F(V^2), is used in this inequality.

Choose any infinite orthonormal sequence e_j in RanP. Then
  <e_j,P F(V^2)P e_j>>=c.
A compact bounded operator sends such a sequence to norm0. Thus the
compressed operator is not compact, and its positive ordinary trace on
RanP is infinite. Since P K P is trace class, hence compact,
  P[F(V^2)-F(D_ref^2)]P
cannot be compact either: otherwise adding P K P would make P F(V^2)P
compact. In particular the displayed operator difference is not trace class.
Combining the terms before tracing therefore does not repair this contract.

The result does not require V to be constant, finite rank, multiplication by
a scalar, or diagonal in the original Fourier modes. Even an extremely large
finite bound M only makes c small; it never removes infinitely many diagonal
contributions of at least c. The retained rank, rather than the geometric
compactness of the base, is decisive.

If a proposed profile is singular at part of spec(V^2), its actual unbounded
functional-calculus/form domain must instead be specified. An undefined or
unbounded term is not a finite ordinary trace. The present bounded-operator
argument must not silently assume a new extension for such a singular term.
Whenever that positive form is well defined on the retained sector and has
the same strict lower bound, it likewise cannot have a finite positive trace.

3. When the geometric relative operator is actually bounded
For a LOCAL geometric reflection with its actual bundle/spin lift, preserving
the common closed domain and the full principal symbol of D, the conjugate
R D R^-1 is another first-order differential expression with the same
principal symbol. Their difference is order0. Smooth coefficients on a
compact base are bounded; its selfadjoint closure/extension supplies precisely
the V above for D_rel=(D-R D R^-1)/2. This is an explicitly CONDITIONAL
geometric contract and must be checked for the original lift and domain.

No such conclusion follows just because R is an involution. A Pin lift which
reverses the principal symbol can leave a first-order D_rel. A nonlocal
Calderon/spectral reflection is not automatically in this local differential
argument. Neither case is excluded by this result. No reflection is freely
constructed from the Cauchy projector or substituted for the physical deck.

The positive alternative can be proved on exactly the same data. Let
  D=D_ref+V, V=V* bounded, DomD=DomD_ref.
The already recorded bounded zero-order domain repair in

The closed square forms have the SAME form domain DomD_ref and satisfy
  ||D psi||^2>= (1/2)||D_ref psi||^2-||V||^2||psi||^2.
For example this is immediate from the scalar norm inequality
||a+b||^2>=(1/2)||a||^2-||b||^2. The min-max principle therefore gives, with
eigenvalues of the nonnegative squares listed with multiplicity,
  lambda_k(D^2)>=(1/2)lambda_k(D_ref^2)-||V||^2.
Consequently for EVERY t>0,

It is an eigenvalue/TRACE bound, not a claim that exponentiation preserves
