D8 NATIVE K AND GLUE ESCAPE REVIEW
==================================

30 October 2026.  Independent exact source review.  No Atlas, index, ledger
or pre-existing report was changed.

Question
--------

The restricted original seed

  C_a, X_i,a and adjoints,  a in F={1,2,3},

closes to a particular D8 with 112 roots.  Does an operation already present
in the original P1/P2 and E8 glue chain cross its 128-root complement and
supply a missing C0, Xi,0 or the missing half of (10,6)?

Verdict
-------

YES at the given P2 current algebra level, by an operation smaller than the
spinor seam extension.

The original D5 current algebra contains twenty roots outside the restricted
D8.  One such ordinary grade-zero D5 root current sends an already generated
(10,6) current to the missing carrier half in one bracket.  The exact word is

  [ E_(-e0-e1), [ X_(1,3)^dag, C_2^dag ] ] .            (1)

In doubled current coordinates the two actual Chevalley brackets are

  [X_(1,3)^dag,C_2^dag]
       = - E_(0,2,0,0,0,-2,0,0),

  [E_(-2,-2,0,0,0,0,0,0),
   E_(0,2,0,0,0,-2,0,0)]
       = + E_(-2,0,0,0,0,-2,0,0).                      (2)

The last root is one of the thirty previously unreachable (10,6) roots.
Adding the full original D5 root action to the restricted seed gives the
exact closure

  76 -> 172 -> 232 -> 240 roots.                         (3)

Thus the old factor-two carrier gap is closed inside the already given
compact E8 current source.  No O fermion and no freely inserted full 16
spinor input is needed.  The input beyond the restricted three-family seed
is precisely the original D5 zero-mode action.

This is more economical than the seam module switch for the carrier task.
It does not say that primitive P1 dynamics selects or executes this zero-mode
word.  The mathematical P2 carrier and E8 current contracts provide the
operator action.  A physical P1 pulse, response rule or preparation selecting
it is not derived here.

1. Why the native D5 word is an original operation
--------------------------------------------------

The original compiler starts with (D5)_1 times (A3)_1 before the mu4
extension.  Therefore E_(-e0-e1) is a base current, not a newly chosen seam
field.  Independently, v474 constructs the ten carrier Clifford generators
on Lambda* C5 and proves that their 45 bilinears span so(10), preserve the
even 16 dimensional carrier and act as the D5 algebra.  The actual current
implementation then evaluates (1) with the original Chevalley table and its
fixed signs.

The distinction from mere abstract covariance is therefore:

  * on the finite P2 carrier, the D5 action is an explicit matrix action;
  * in the marked affine current source, the D5 roots are weight-one current
    operators and their zero modes act through the checked Chevalley bracket;
  * primitive P1 has not separately derived a source history which applies
    this particular zero mode to the state.

The first two bullets prove an available operator in the given P2/E8 source.
They do not imply the third.

2. Clock and cocycle do not need repair
---------------------------------------

Use the original internal glue grade

  gr(r) = -sum(r_5,r_6,r_7) mod 4.

For the three roots in (2) the grades are

  gr(E_(-e0-e1))=0,  gr(Y)=2,  gr(output)=2.

The D5 step is neutral under the original quarter clock and preserves the
grade exactly.  Both bracket coefficients in (2) come from the actual compact
Chevalley table.  Hence no new cocycle sign, Weyl dictionary or clock fit has
been introduced.

The original mu4 grade is not the Z2 coset parity of the newly found D8.
Both the 112-root D8 and its 128-root complement contain grades 0,1,2,3.  The
exact D8 grade census is

  D8:         26,28,30,28 for grades 0,1,2,3,
  complement: 26,36,30,36 for grades 0,1,2,3.

This prevents a false statement that the old quarter clock itself is the new
D8 module switch.

3. The old marked glue generator is also an exact escape
--------------------------------------------------------

The independent seam route is positive at its stated lattice and conformal
net scope.  v983 fixes

  lambda=(omega_s,omega_f),
  omega_s=(1/2)^5,
  omega_f=(3/4,-1/4,-1/4,-1/4).

The original A3 to D3 isometry maps omega_f to (1/2,1/2,1/2).  Therefore in
the current table

  2 lambda=(1,1,1,1,1,1,1,1)=root(C0^dag),             (4)

with the table using doubled coordinates.  Its adjoint is exactly C0.  Thus
the already marked glue vertex is not merely Weyl conjugate to a useful
escape.  In this fixed chart it is the omitted minimal C0 pair itself.

Relative to the new D8, lambda has integral pairing with every root.  The
pairing numerators in doubled coordinates are -4,0,+4 with multiplicities
28,56,28.  Adding C0 and C0^dag to the closed D8 gives

  114 -> 170 -> 240 roots.                               (5)

The original quarter grades of lambda and its adjoint are 1 and 3.  These are
retained rather than redefined.

4. What the D8 vacuum VOA cannot do
-----------------------------------

Let L be the root lattice of the new D8.  Every state and every vertex mode
in V_L has momentum in L.  Products add lattice momenta and oscillator modes
carry zero lattice momentum.  Consequently arbitrary words and all modes of
the D8 vacuum VOA stay in L.  They cannot produce e^lambda in the other coset
lambda+L.

The E8 lattice VOA is the index-two extension

  V_E8 = V_L direct-sum V_(L+lambda).

The vertex Y(e^lambda,z) is precisely the sector-changing operation.  Once it
and its adjoint are admitted, D8 current modes generate the full 128-root
module and (5) recovers E8.  v459 and the local Z2 crossed-product route
support this extension at lattice/net level.

What remains open is narrower.  The primitive one-channel P1 quarter source
has not been proved to produce Y(e^lambda,z) as a localized microscopic
operator with its GSO choice, cocycle, state and common time.  The existing
source_spin_lift audit explicitly records that the formal quarter-field OPE
and the neutral eight-copy kernel do not prove this operator limit.  This
open P1 realization does not undo the positive native D5 completion (1),
which only uses the already given P2/E8 current operation.
