1. Exact loop theorem, requiring no stochastic search
Let g:S1->U(n) be smooth, k=wind det g, with the unnormalised Hilbert-Schmidt
matrix norm and the fixed coordinate phi of period2pi. Put
  E(g)=(1/2pi) integral tr(g'(phi)^*g'(phi)) dphi.
Write g(phi)=sum_j A_j exp(i j phi). Parseval and unitarity give
  E=sum_j j^2 ||A_j||_HS^2,
  k=(1/2pi i) integral tr(g^*g')=sum_j j ||A_j||_HS^2.
For k>=0 use j^2>=j, and for k<=0 use j^2>=-j. Therefore E>=|k|.
For 0<k<=n, equality forces A_j=0 except j=0,1. Thus g=A+zB.
The Fourier coefficients of g^*g=gg^*=I imply
  A^*B=B^*A=AB^*=BA^*=0,
  A^*A+B^*B=AA^*+BB^*=I.
Set h=A+B=g(0), P=h^*B=B^*B. Then h is unitary and P=P^*=P^2,
  g=h[(I-P)+zP], rank P=k.
For negative k the correct expression uses z^-1 and rank P=-k.
For k=0 equality means g is constant. The restriction |k|<=n is needed for
attaining this bound; the inequality itself holds for every integer k.
Thus for n=2,k=1, minimisers of THIS metric loop energy modulo h form CP1.
Claude's displayed equality formula omitted sign(k) for negative winding.
No Morse existence claim or floating point loop sample is needed.

acts on that internal factor. A smooth based unitary loop g has g(0)=g(2pi)=I,
