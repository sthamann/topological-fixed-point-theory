SPECTRAL HEAT MIXTURE AND GEOMETRIC TIME: TARGETED ORIGINAL REVIEW
29 October 2026. Independent analytic review; no new source/model choice,
large numerics, original edit or Atlas mutation.

Verdict
The original proof names a Euclidean COVARIANCE and reflection tau->-tau,
but never specifies its actual heat-mixture variable or covariance operator.
A positive Laplace mixture of spectral heat operators does NOT by itself
establish this geometric-time RP. A single heat covariance has an explicit
negative reflected2x2 minor. If 'reflection-stable' is intended to mean that
each component is already OS positive, positive superposition is valid, but
that stronger property is a premise needing its actual kernel proof.

2. Smallest decisive spectral-heat covariance test
Fix a single spatial frequency with omega>=0 and geometric time tau on R:
  K=-partial_tau^2+omega^2,
  C_s=exp(-s K), s>0.
This is a positive covariance operator with a positive point Laplace measure
at spectral heat parameter s. Its translation kernel is real, positive and
invariant under tau reflection:
  c_s(u)=exp(-s omega^2)(4pi s)^(-1/2)exp(-u^2/(4s)).
The reflected covariance on positive times a,b is c_s(a+b), not c_s(a-b).
For any distinct a,b>0 its2x2 Gram determinant has ratio
  c_s(2a)c_s(2b)/c_s(a+b)^2
    =exp(-(a-b)^2/(2s))<1.
Thus the determinant is strictly negative despite covariance positivity and
reflection invariance. For example s=1,a=1,b=2, set
  d=exp(-omega^2)/(2sqrt(pi)),
  f=delta_1-exp(7/4)delta_2.
Then its reflected quadratic form is exactly
  q(f)=d[exp(-1)-exp(-1/2)]<0.
The deltas can be replaced by smooth real mollifiers supported in small
neighborhoods of1 and2 inside tau>0; smoothness of c_s preserves the strict
negative sign. Hence this is a genuine supported-test-function obstruction,
not an artifact of allowing delta insertions.

This one atom already disproves sufficiency of an arbitrary positive Laplace
measure for time RP of f(K). It does NOT assert that every mixture fails:
integrating heat kernels with suitable weights can yield an RP covariance.
It is not a complete P1/P2 or admissibility countermodel.

3. The whole continuous smooth scalar covariance class
There is an elementary stronger class test, not a new general mathematical
theorem. Let c:R->R be continuous, even, ordinarily positive definite, with
c(0)>0. Assume its positive-time Hankel matrices [c(t_i+t_j)] are PSD and
c is C1 at0. Then c is constant.

Proof: continuity extends the Hankel positivity to a time0 entry and gives
  c(0)c(2t)>=c(t)^2, t>=0.
Ordinary PD gives |c(t)|<=c(0). Put d(t)=1-c(t)/c(0)>=0. The displayed
Hankel inequality becomes
  d(2t)<=2d(t)-d(t)^2<=2d(t).
Iterating yields d(t)<=2^n d(t/2^n). Evenness and C1 at0 imply c'(0)=0,
hence d(u)=o(u). For each fixed t the right side therefore tends to0,
so d(t)=0 globally. Evenness covers negative t. Conversely a nonnegative
constant covariance plainly meets both positivity conditions.

Thus no nonconstant continuous scalar translation-invariant time covariance
that is differentiable through0 can have both ordinary PD and time RP. In
particular this applies to any genuinely time-smoothed Gaussian covariance
with these hypotheses; no scan over individual positive heat weights is
needed. It does NOT apply to distribution/contact kernels, infinite c(0),
nontranslation-invariant kernels, nontrivial reflection matrices or graded
fermionic kernels. Smoothing only spatial coordinates does not imply C1
through geometric time0. The usual free covariance exp(-E|t|)/(2E) has
precisely a time cusp at0 and is not excluded. Positive heat mixtures which
produce such a cusp remain compatible with the sufficient criterion below.

4. Strong valid statements, with the two parameters kept separate
An actual positive ENERGY semigroup has a different reflected kernel. For
H>=0 and vectors v_i, times tau_i>0,
  M_ij=<v_i,exp(-(tau_i+tau_j)H)v_j>
      =<exp(-tau_i H)v_i,exp(-tau_j H)v_j>.
This is a positive Gram matrix. Positive integrable superpositions of such
already RP kernels remain RP. The heat covariance counterexample does not
touch this statement, the existing HS heat-word construction, the positive
coupled excitation semigroup, or their given full Clifford source.

A useful sufficient covariance criterion in the scalar translation-invariant
class is a positive Stieltjes/resolvent representation
  F(z)=integral_(mu>=0) d rho(mu)/(z+mu), rho>=0,
with the required convergence and positive frequencies. For
K=-partial_tau^2+omega^2 it gives
  c(u)=integral d rho(mu) exp(-sqrt(omega^2+mu)|u|)
                             /[2sqrt(omega^2+mu)].
The reflected c(tau_i+tau_j) is a positive integral of rank-one Gram matrices.
This is stronger than arbitrary positive Laplace measure in spectral heat
parameter s. For example the full resolvent integral of exp(-sK) is RP even
though each individual heat covariance above fails. No choice of such a
representation for the original source is made here.

5. Relation to the old finding and exact original obligation
The Atlas finding positive-spectral-reweighting-does-not-establish-original-
source-rp and source_sewing_20261029/PROOF.txt:1-184 were reread. Its actual
counterexample concerns a Boltzmann/Schur reweighting of a reflected measure
by exp[-Tr f(D^2)]. It does not contain the heat COVARIANCE minor above.
These are distinct mathematical inferences; neither is a new all-P1 no-go.

For the original lemma, either 'reflection-stable' denotes only covariance
under reflection, in which case the generic heat-mixture inference is false,
or it includes reflected positivity itself, in which case that part is an
explicit hypothesis rather than a derivation from Bernstein weights. The
first decisive original input is the actual bosonic covariance and its
reflection, followed by a positive-time kernel factorization or direct RP
test. The fixed positive Z_ref denominator preserves an already RP form; it
cannot change the sign of the negative minor. Full joint boson/fermion
positivity also retains its separate source assumptions.

Primary context, checked directly: Arthur Jaffe, Stochastic PDE, Reflection
Positivity, and Quantum Fields, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.2964 , sectionIII.
That paper distinguishes auxiliary stochastic time from physical reflection
