TWO-FACE RESPONSE OF THE GIVEN FREE QUARTER GRAPH
2 November 2026. A conditional source calculation, not a physical F1 closure.

The source is the original geometric Cauchy graph used in R074, on two
labelled faces, G(p)=[[1-C(p),X(p)],[X(p),C(p)]], p=n+alpha, n in Z,
C(p)=1/(1+exp(beta*p)), X(p)=1/(2 cosh(beta*p/2)). The graph is a
projection. Its identification with a full physical covariance remains
an additional premise. It is not the Higgs-coupled matrix graph.

For two scalar face functions a,b, let A=diag(M_a,M_b). Fourier conventions
are normalized so that Parseval has no extra 2pi factor. The full response
Q(a,b)=||[A,G]||_HS^2 is sum_k (a_k,b_k)^* N_k (a_k,b_k). For k!=0,

 N_k=|k| [[coth(beta|k|/2), -csch(beta|k|/2)],
          [-csch(beta|k|/2), coth(beta|k|/2)]].                 (1)

Proof for k>0; k<0 follows by shifting the summation index. Put
C=C(p), C'=C(p-k), X=X(p), X'=X(p-k). Direct block expansion gives

 N11=sum_n[(C-C')^2+X^2+X'^2], N12=-2 sum_n X X'.          (2)

Using X^2=C(1-C), the two exact pointwise identities are

 (C-C')^2+X^2+X'^2=coth(beta*k/2)(C'-C),
 2XX'=csch(beta*k/2)(C'-C).                                (3)

They hold for every beta>0, real alpha and k>0. The total sum telescopes:
sum_n(C(n+alpha-k)-C(n+alpha))=k, since the Fermi tails are 1 and 0.
All summands in (2) decay at both ends. Thus (1) is an infinite-mode
identity; finite numerical windows in the accompanying checker are controls.

For k=0, N_0=d[[1,-1],[-1,1]], d=2 sum_n C_n(1-C_n).       (4)
The previously derived R066 quarter charge variance gives sum C_n(1-C_n)
=1/(8pi) at beta=4pi and alpha=1/4 or 3/4. Therefore d=1/(4pi) there.
This special identity is not asserted for arbitrary alpha or beta.

The harmonic Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator of a flat strip of width
L=beta/2, with outward derivatives and the same boundary normalization,
has (1) for k!=0, and N_strip,0=L^-1[[1,-1],[-1,1]]. At the original
beta=4pi its zero mode is twice (4). Thus, with the normalized orthogonal
projector Pi_0,-=1/2[[1,-1],[-1,1]] on the constant relative face vector,

 N_source = N_strip - (1/(2pi)) Pi_0,-,
 (1/2) N_strip <= N_source <= N_strip.                      (5)

The inequalities are quadratic-form inequalities: nonzero-mode matrices
are identical and positive; on the zero mode the common direction is zero
for both, and the positive relative eigenvalue is halved. For a selfadjoint
generator, its normal-ordered variance is Q/2, not Q. This does not assert
that the physical action equals either form. It reuses R066 and R074, does
not provide a new independent prediction or derive a spatial dimension.

Coupled diagnostic and tail control. Use the given block h_p=p sigma_z+
m sigma_x, E_p=sqrt(p^2+m^2), beta=4pi, alpha=1/4, and set
C_p=(I-h_p tanh(beta E_p/2)/E_p)/2, X_p=I/(2 cosh(beta E_p/2)).
This defines another two-face projection through G=[[I-C,X],[X,C]].
It is a fixed-background comparison, not a derivation of the shared
physical source, its state, its Higgs measure or its carrier map.

For two internal components, the very same (2) is evaluated with traces
over the internal 2x2 matrices. At |n|<=80 the checker gives:

 k   N11(m=0)       N11(m=.3)       N12(m=0)         N12(m=.3)
 0   .159154943092  .029293149215   -.159154943092   -.029293149215
 1  2.000013949418  1.545807121543  -.007469796977   -.002239511278
 2  4.000000000097  3.640706254598  -.000027898739   -.000012860350

These include both internal components. To bound the omitted diagonal
tail, let j0=N-|k|>0 and beta=4pi. On each omitted path interval,
|p| >= j0 and
 ||C'(p)||_HS <= (m/|p|^2+4 beta exp(-beta|p|))/sqrt(2).
The sum of all omitted N11 terms is bounded by
 2 k^2 [m^2(j0^-4+1/(3 j0^3))
        +16 beta^2 exp(-2 beta j0)/(1-exp(-2 beta))]
 +8 exp(-beta j0)/(1-exp(-beta)).                           (6)
For m=.3 the displayed N11 tail bound is 1.26e-7 for k=1 and
5.25e-7 for k=2; it is exponentially small for k=0 and m=0. Since each
summand is nonnegative and the differences are much larger, the nonzero
mode response changes as well as the zero mode. Calculations are ordinary
floating point, not interval-certified arithmetic. This compares source
graphs with the same explicitly declared scalar face operations; it does
not evaluate the concurrently transported clock or its physical locality.
