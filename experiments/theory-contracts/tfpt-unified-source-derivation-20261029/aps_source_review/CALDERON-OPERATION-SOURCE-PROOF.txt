is sufficient). Its Fourier coefficient A_m obeys [M_A,G]=0 iff
 G(p+m) A_m=A_m G(p) for every r,m.                    (2)
Taking r to +infinity in(2) gives [diag(1,0),A_m]=0.

3. Entire smooth unitary multiplier implementability class

G-Fock representation iff [G,V] is Hilbert-Schmidt. This is the charged
CAR form of the standard criterion (Araki1970,Theorems6/7); it does not
assert physical availability, locality of an implementer or primitive
selection. Pure-state equivalence and unchanged state are different.

In the folded periodic frame, the EXACT smooth multiplier class is
 V(x)=V_L(x) direct_sum V_R(x), V_L,V_R in U(8).        (5)
Necessity: for each fixed Fourier coefficient V_m, the matrix entries of
[G,V] along n -> +infinity tend to [P_face,V_m]. A nonzero off-face block
therefore gives an infinite sum of nonzero squared entries. HS forces all
off-face Fourier coefficients to vanish. This proves necessity on the
whole unitary matrix class, not merely an example.
Sufficiency: for block-diagonal V the asymptotic projections commute with
each V_m. Outside O(1+|m|) modes, G_(n+m,k)-its same-tail limit and G_(n,l)
minus its limit decay exponentially. Uniformly over the finite k,l,

infinite-dimensional proof, without a finite-CCR construction.
