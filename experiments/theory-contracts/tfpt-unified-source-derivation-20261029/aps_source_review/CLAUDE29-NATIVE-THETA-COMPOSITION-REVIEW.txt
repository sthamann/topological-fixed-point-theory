VERDICT

The existing native product reflection does not turn the new chiral E0 map
into a same-Phi symmetry for generic complex Yukawa and Majorana controls.
The two antiunitary maps compose to a NEW-complex-linear Bogoliubov map, but
its target is still the transported source:

  raw chiral map:          G(B_Phi) -> G(B_(Phi^K)),
  torus-compensated map:   G(B_Phi) -> G(B_(bar(Phi))).

The native reflection is a symmetry inside every already fixed graph.  It is
not an additional coefficientwise conjugation Phi' -> Phi.  Therefore a
second antilinearity does not cancel the source transport.

The composition also reverses the circle parameter between the two source
families and squares to minus one on the one-particle selfdual space.  Its
square is fermion parity on the CAR algebra and the identity only after
restriction to even observables.  No CPT name is justified by these facts.

1. ORIGINAL THETA, READ IN ITS ACTUAL TYPE

The existing source uses

  F=S_normal tensor gamma_F,
  R_geo=face_swap tensor F,
  C_faces=diag(C0,-C0).

On the NEW complex one-particle space R_geo is antiunitary, commutes with
C_faces and obeys, for every allowed selfadjoint odd Phi,

  F B_Phi F=-B_Phi,
  R_geo G(B_Phi) R_geo^-1=G(B_Phi).                    (1)

The corresponding cyclic antiunitary in the thermal Hilbert-Schmidt
representation is

  J_R=Q_Gamma^* J_HS.

It implements the same geometric reflection on the bounded CAR fields and
fixes the graph state.  It is not J_F and it is not a map which replaces the
external constructor parameters by their complex conjugates.  In particular,
(1) remains a same-Phi identity even when Y_f and M_R are generic complex
matrices.

The GNS formula is source specific: J_R acts in the Hilbert-Schmidt
representation built from h_Phi.  Thus the clean composition test is first
performed with R_geo on the common one-particle selfdual chart.  The existing
graph-to-HS intertwiner then gives the same conclusion for J_R.

2. SMALLEST COEFFICIENT BLOCK TEST

Let Jcal_E be the chiral E0 antiunitary from the preceding review.  In OLD
complex coordinates it has the form

  Jcal_E=Ical D_K kappa,

with the base pullback understood.  It satisfies

  Jcal_E B_Phi Jcal_E^-1=B_(Phi^K),

where the actual v252 constructor gives

  Y'_f=omega S_f bar(Y_f) S_f,
  M'_R=omega^2 S_f bar(M_R) S_f.                       (2)

The first decisive block is already enough.  Since the native F in (1)
changes B to minus B without changing its coefficient,

  (F Jcal_E) B_Phi (F Jcal_E)^-1=-B_(Phi^K).           (3)

Equation (3) is not -B_Phi for generic complex controls.  The native
reflection has preserved Phi^K exactly as it preserves every other fixed
coefficient.  It has not applied a second copy of bar(.) to (2).

3. ALL FACES AND THE TORUS-COMPENSATED MAP

Extend Jcal_E diagonally to the two faces and put

  C_raw=R_geo Jcal_E,faces.

Both factors are NEW antiunitary, hence C_raw is NEW complex linear.  Both
commute with C_faces, so C_raw is a correctly typed selfdual Bogoliubov map.
Functional calculus and (3) give the complete graph identity

  C_raw G(B_Phi) C_raw^-1=G(B_(Phi^K)).                (4)

This includes both diagonal face blocks and both crossing blocks.

Now reuse the corrected torus result, rather than Claude29's erroneous common
omega Majorana phase.  Let U_K be the already checked combination of the
family sign and the lambda_5 plus A3 phase.  On the displayed v252 chart,

  U_K Phi^K U_K^*=bar(Phi).

The compensated antiunitary

  Jhat_E=U_K Jcal_E

is again an involution and transports B_Phi to B_(bar(Phi)).  Its composition
with the native reflection is

  C_hat=R_geo Jhat_E,faces,
  C_hat G(B_Phi) C_hat^-1=G(B_(bar(Phi))).             (5)

Thus the new torus absorption is fully retained.  It sharpens the target from
Phi^K to bar(Phi), but it does not make (5) a same-Phi identity.  Such an
identity requires Phi=bar(Phi), or a separately allowed equivalence which
identifies those two fixed backgrounds.  The existence of the native Theta
does not supply that equivalence.

The same conclusion holds in either order.  Applying the native reflection
first leaves G(B_Phi) fixed; applying it second leaves the transported target
fixed.

4. SQUARE AND TIME PARAMETER

Direct matrix multiplication gives for both versions

  C_raw^2=C_hat^2=-I                                  (6)

on the two-face one-particle space.  Therefore the induced CAR map squares to
the sign automorphism c(u)->c(-u), namely fermion parity.  It becomes an
involution on the even algebra used by the proven reflection-positive form,
but not on odd fields.

The two antiunitary time rules must also be kept separate.  The existing J_R
obeys

  J_R L_HS J_R^-1=-L_HS,
  J_R exp(i t L_HS) J_R^-1=exp(i t L_HS).

The equality with the same parameter in the second line uses the
antilinearity of J_R.  The chiral E0 antiunitary transports h_Phi to its
target and consequently sends its one-particle circle group to the target at
-t.  Their linear composition therefore obeys

  C_raw U_Phi(t) C_raw^-1=U_(Phi^K)(-t),
  C_hat U_Phi(t) C_hat^-1=U_(bar(Phi))(-t).            (7)

The composition reverses the parameter as an ordinary complex-linear
intertwiner.  This is not the same statement as the source-internal native
Theta relation and does not identify circle time with radial OS time.

6. EXACT SCOPE

intertwiner between the displayed source family members.  It does not select
