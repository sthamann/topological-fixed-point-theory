Exact general conclusion in this stated six-generator real linear span
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
Assume at least one Yukawa entry and at least one Majorana entry are nonzero.
Let lambda = a(3B)+b L+c(3Q_em)+d(6Y)+e C1+f C2. Require [lambda,V(H)]=0
for every H in the original whole Higgs family, keeping the fixed Majorana
term, and [lambda,gamma_F]=0. Then b=c=d=0, and the admissible generators
in this class are exactly a(3B)+e C1+f C2. Their weighted trace is zero:

                   tr_96(lambda gamma_F) = 0.

Proof. On an allowed nonzero neutral Yukawa link the B, L, Q and colour
charges are identical on the connected L/R entries, whereas 6Y differs.
Thus d=0. An original charged Higgs direction connects the corresponding
weak partners; the Q_em eigenvalues differ by one unit, while B, L and each
colour charge still agree. Thus c=0. The original Majorana block connects
nu_R to its charge-conjugate nu_R.  Its 3B and colour charges vanish and its
L charges differ, so b=0 when that block is nonzero. Conversely the explicit
one-form construction is colour-diagonal and connects equal B charges, and
the Majorana block is neutral under both. This proves sufficiency for all
given Yukawa matrices, all four real H components and arbitrary MR.

The weighted trace vanishes by pairing each left particle of a given species,
colour and generation with the corresponding right particle of the same
surviving charge; the antiparticle block cancels in the same way. This result
holds also when Yukawa matrices have kernels: no invertibility assumption is
used. For any Hermitian odd V and commuting lambda, the kernel-weighted trace
equals tr(lambda gamma_F), because V pairs nonzero eigenvalues across chirality
within each lambda eigenspace. Thus in the selected class the weighted kernel
trace is 0, subject to those joint commutation conditions. An index of any
particular boundary operator or a relative determinant additionally requires
its own domain, seam, covariance, orientation and implementability proof.

Key scope distinctions
----------------------
L and B-L commute with the Higgs family but not a nonzero fixed Majorana
block. Electromagnetic charge commutes with a neutral H direction yet not
with both charged H directions. Hypercharge does not commute even with the
neutral Yukawa link. These are fixed-H internal commutators: the full gauge
action simultaneously transforms H, so the Q/Y result is not a claim that
electroweak gauge invariance is broken by the underlying field theory.

The six-generator span is not the entire commutant. Chiral or flavour
generators supported on Yukawa/Majorana null spaces, non-diagonal colour,
field-dependent transformations, and clocks from the seam or E8 are not
excluded. The matrix theorem does not give a dynamical original clock nor
physical masses. It does not replace the nonzero selected 18-channel index
or the full96 unweighted index: those use their own domains and restrictions.
