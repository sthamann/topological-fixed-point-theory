CLAUDE44 QUARTER CHARACTER AND ACTUAL CONSUMER REVIEW
Independent exact review, 30 October 2026

Question

Claude44 correctly found an odd quarter character in the four scalar trace
readings of the certified Hardy coefficient.  It then used the state-vector
phase of the native clock kappa_nat=-i gamma_F as a compensator and called the
result the first closed original source action.  The decisive type question is
whether the clock acts on a vector, on one leg of a charged Hom, or by
conjugation on the actual operator-valued Hardy coefficient.

Verdict

The four-family trace and Fourier table is exact.  The asserted native-clock
closure is not.  On the actual H_F=96 coefficient the relevant action is the
adjoint action on End(H_F).  It fixes B_02 and gives the weakly lifted block
A_a=[X_a,B_02] the sign -1, not the phase -i or +i.  The proposed combined
adjoint quarter average therefore has no invariant component.  A left-only
clock phase would require a separately specified one-legged charged Hom
consumer.  It is not the present Hardy endomorphism, the original HolK family
matrix, or the already checked primitive wedge contraction.

The positive common marked-frame result remains stronger and unchanged:
Bstar sends the actual E_02 open word directly to M0 P0 while simultaneously
intertwining KQ^-1 with U.  The original primitive wedge consumer reads this
directed contribution as +kappa, with the fixed KO pairing -kappa, when all
legs are transported.  Claude44 does not close the still separate coefficient
insertion into the specific original HolK branch.


1. Exact quarter character calculation

Use the already fixed matrices

 KQ=[[0,1,0],[-1,0,0],[0,0,1]],
 Cs=[[0,0,1],[-1,0,0],[0,-1,0]],
 Ck=KQ^(-k) Cs KQ^k,
 B_02=kappa E_02 P_gamma,+.

Then KQ^2=diag(-1,-1,1), and Ad(KQ^2)E_02=-E_02.  Direct exact
calculation gives, after suppressing the common nonzero kappa P_gamma,+,

 Tr(Ck^2 E_02)             = (1,0,-1,0),
 Tr((Ck+Ck^2) E_02)        = (1,1,-1,-1).

With Claude44's Fourier convention sum_k i^(m k) f(k), the corresponding
characters m=0,1,2,3 are

 even word: (0,2,0,2),
 K word:    (0,2+2i,0,2-2i).

Thus every external weight satisfying chi(k+2)=chi(k) cancels this isolated
coefficient, while the two odd characters survive.  This is a correct
statement about that scalar four-mark average.  It is not yet a statement
about which average the original source evaluates.

At operator level, the four Ad(KQ) images of E_02 decompose into nonzero m=1
and m=3 components and zero m=0,m=2 components.  This is the precise family
content behind the trace table.


2. The actual native clock acts by conjugation on the coefficient

On the existing finite Hilbert space,

 kappa_nat=-i gamma_F,
 B_02=kappa E_02 P_gamma,+,
 P_gamma,+=(1+gamma_F)/2.

Because E_02 commutes with gamma_F,

 kappa_nat B_02 kappa_nat^(-1)=B_02.                 (1)

Left multiplication alone does give kappa_nat B_02=-i B_02, but the right
action gives B_02 kappa_nat^(-1)=+i B_02.  The phases cancel in (1).  This is
the elementary distinction between a vector phase and an endomorphism
covariance.  It also shows that P_gamma,+ does not acquire a separate +i
phase merely by calling its labels antiparticles; -i gamma_F is -i on every
vector in Ran P_gamma,+.

For each of the two actual weak Clifford creators X_wa, the already proved
operator is

 A_a=[X_wa,B_02]=kappa E_02 X_wa gamma_F,

of rank16.  It is gamma_F odd, hence

 kappa_nat A_a kappa_nat^(-1)=-A_a.                  (2)

The full KO6 completion is gamma_F odd as well and has the same adjoint
character -1.  Equation(2) is quarter character m=2, not m=1 or m=3.  When
combined with the family Ad(KQ) orbit it only exchanges the two odd family
characters.  The invariant adjoint average is exactly zero:

 (1/4) sum_k (-1)^k Ad(KQ)^k(E_02)=0.                (3)

Consequently the expression sum_k kappa_nat^k (Q^k)* can reproduce the
Claude44 scalar table only if kappa_nat^k is deliberately retained as a
one-sided state or Hom-leg factor.  It is not the conjugation law of the
actual End(H_F) coefficient.


3. Covariance is not a scalar invariance prescription

The original flavor coefficient is an open family matrix

 (Y_f)_ij=<e_i,Hol_G_D4(nabla_F*) K_f^str e_j>.

Under a frame or quarter transport, its two endpoints and the operator are
transported together.  That is covariance of an open Hom/End matrix.  Taking
Tr(W(Ck)B_02), and then averaging its four numbers with a chosen character,
is an additional scalar evaluation.  Claude44 establishes the values of this
evaluation if it is made; it does not identify it as the original HolK or
Pstr call.

The same distinction applies to the original primitive wedge consumer

 C_prim(s_Q,s_uc,H)=vol_E(s_Q wedge s_uc wedge H).

It is a multilinear invariant after all carrier and endpoint legs are
transported.  Its checked directed input reads +kappa, with the fixed compact
KO convention -kappa.  The original formula does not replace that multilinear
transport by a sum of left clock phases over four marks.  Therefore no new
whole charged-Wedge compensation follows from the Fourier table.  Conversely,
the result is no no-go for a future original one-legged charged Hom: such a
typed consumer could use an odd character, but it has to be exhibited in the
source rather than inferred from the eigenvalue of kappa_nat on a vector.


4. The actual positive marked open word remains available

The fixed source-derived unitary

 Bstar=(1/sqrt2)[[0,0,-1+i],[1,i,0],[i,1,0]]

already satisfies exactly

 Bstar KQ^(-1) Bstar*=U=diag(1,i,-i),
 Bstar Cs Bstar*=M0,
 Bstar E_02 Bstar*=M0 P0,
 Bstar E_22 Bstar*=P0,
 P0=(I+U^2)/2.

This transports the Hardy coefficient, its marked input endpoint and the
quarter generator in one common frame.  It needs no scalar frame phase and no
native-clock phase inserted on only one side.  It proves the original open
word channel kappa M0P0.  It does not by itself assert kappa P0 is the entire
positive Kstr or that the original Zadm/HolK source executes this coefficient.
