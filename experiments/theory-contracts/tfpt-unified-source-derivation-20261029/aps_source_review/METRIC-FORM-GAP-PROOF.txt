CONSTRUCTIVE SUFFICIENT BRIDGE
Let H0>=0 be selfadjoint, with simple eigenvalue 0 and spectral gap Delta.
Suppose the actual symmetric form perturbation v is bounded by N0+1, and
on the SAME Hilbert and compatible form domains Q(H0) subset Q(N0),
  N0+1 <= a H0+b I,  a,b>=0, va<1.
Assume the conditions of the minmax principle; e.g. compact resolvent.
The perturbation defines a semibounded selfadjoint form sum by KLMN and
  (1-va)H0-vb I <= H0+V <= (1+va)H0+vb I.
Ground-state trial gives E0<=vb. Monotone minmax gives
E1 >= (1-va)Delta-vb. Thus, if the right side is positive,
  gap(H0+V) >= (1-va)Delta-2vb.

sufficient route, not a claim that the original N0=H0 or that this comparison
is necessary. One may instead establish an independent genuine norm bound or
another actual source-specific theorem.
