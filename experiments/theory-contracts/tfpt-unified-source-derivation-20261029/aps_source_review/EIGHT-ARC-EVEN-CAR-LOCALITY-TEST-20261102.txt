EIGHT ARC SOURCE LINE: AN EXACT EVEN CAR LOCALITY TEST
=====================================================

2 November 2026. Scoped mathematical consequence of the existing source
fold and closed smooth line. This is not a derivation of the P1 net.

Assumptions and original source
-------------------------------
Let I be a proper connected open interval on the quotient circle. Its
preimage under F(theta)=8 theta mod 2 pi consists of eight disjoint open
cover arcs I_0,...,I_7. The existing smooth half-kink construction gives
T=W(-b_F), b_F=b o F, for a real smooth periodic quotient profile b.
The original Weyl convention is

  W(phi) psi(theta) W(phi)^* = exp(-i phi(theta)) psi(theta),

so T acts by multiplication with u(theta)=exp(i b_F(theta)) on the
annihilation field, with the corresponding conjugate phase on the adjoint.
These are exactly the pre-existing fold and Weyl operations; no new field,
measure, or dynamics is selected. The argument holds for alpha=1/4 and
3/4, since the local test uses support away from the circle cut and u is
single-valued. It does not replace or remove the quarter holonomy.

Hypothesis: exp(i b) is nonconstant on I. This is essential for the
observable test below. A constant phase, including a global parity action,
is not covered by that test. A smooth nontrivial line is not automatically
nonconstant on an arbitrarily chosen interval; I must contain its variation.

Exact even-observable test
--------------------------
Fix any one of the eight arcs I_j and select a different arc I_k. Since
F maps I_k bijectively onto I and exp(i b) is nonconstant on I, there are
two open sets U,V with disjoint closures inside I_k such that their phase
images u(U),u(V) lie in disjoint neighborhoods on the unit circle. Choose
nonzero normalized one-particle test functions f,g supported in U,V.
They belong to the existing charged CAR one-particle space, with the
quarter boundary condition; local support makes their definition insensitive
to the position of the cut. Define the bounded even local observable

  B = a^*(f) a(g) in CAR_even(I_k).

Under T, the associated one-particle rank-one kernel |f><g| is transformed
by multiplication with u and its adjoint, up to the standard convention for
whether the annihilation smearing is linear or antilinear. It cannot remain
the same: equality of two nonzero rank-one operators would require u to
act by the same scalar on f and g, contrary to the disjoint phase images.
The CAR map of a rank-one kernel to a^*(f)a(g) is injective: for example,
its commutator with an arbitrary creation operator recovers the action of
that kernel on the one-particle test space. Therefore

  T B T^* != B,  equivalently [T,B] != 0.                  (1)

Any element of CAR_even(I_j), and likewise of its represented local von
Neumann closure, commutes with B because I_j and I_k are disjoint and the
CAR grading makes even operators commute at disjoint supports. Hence

  T is not an element of CAR_even(I_j)''                  (2)

in the given faithful source representation. The conclusion holds for
any chosen j because there is always a distinct k. The proof uses a bounded
even test observable on the actual cover, not a sharp endpoint or an odd
observable as the claimed physical measurement. Strong continuity of
multiplication and locality extend the commutation statement to the local
von Neumann closure.

Scope and necessary next comparison
----------------------------------
Equation (2) concerns the DIRECT pullback T=W(-b o F), using the existing
cover net and its literal eight preimage arcs. It is not a theorem against
an abstract isomorphism or embedding iota_I, a different P1 localization,
a nontrivial compression or quotient, or a transported state and domain.
Any such proposed map must instead be exhibited and tested together with
its action on the relevant even observables, state, reflection, original
OS time, source coefficients, reference and relative normalization. The
argument does not rule out closed neutral lines on the quotient, where this
operator is already available, or assert that every neutral line has this
specific profile. It also does not decide the 30-current sewing rule or
prove the E8 source and F1.
