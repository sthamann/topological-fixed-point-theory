VERDICT
The new check_current_odd_reader.py follows the actual typed chain

  B_s -> G(B_s) -> E* G_22(B_s) E -> v258 logit -> Pi_odd^gamma

and its formulas are correct. Its strongest natural continuation is an exact

is the scalar-mode instance of the original doubled coefficient. The displayed

  E*G_22(B_s)E=(I+exp(2 ell h_s))^-1,
  h_s=p gamma-i gamma Phi_s.

The script does not commute a logarithm through a nonreducing face map. It

The exact SymPy checks retain symbolic momentum p and source parameter s.
The numerical route then executes the actual B192, G384 and v258 induce call.
The recorded maximum reader residual 1.90e-13 and central-difference jet

Two scope points should remain visible. The numerical run is a finite set of

2. Exact smooth operator class
Let

  H=L2(S1,C^96),
  gamma=gamma*=gamma^-1,
  A=-i partial_theta+a(theta)

with A selfadjoint on H1, a smooth bounded Hermitian coefficient and
[A,gamma]=0. Let Phi(theta)=Phi(theta)* be smooth and bounded with
{gamma,Phi}=0. Put

  h=A gamma-i gamma Phi.                                (1)

The bounded Phi term is selfadjoint, so Kato-Rellich makes h selfadjoint on
Dom(A)=H1. The constant gamma preserves this domain. No commutator condition
[A,Phi]=0 is used here. That commutator first matters in h^2, not in the
reader.

For ell>0 define the actual second face

  C(h)=(I+exp(2 ell h))^-1.                             (2)

C and I-C are injective. Their inverses are generally unbounded on the whole
circle. Borel functional calculus nevertheless gives on Dom(h)

  L(C):=log((I-C)C^-1)=2 ell h.                         (3)

With Pi_even(T)=(T+gamma T gamma)/2 and
Pi_odd(T)=(T-gamma T gamma)/2, equations (1)-(3) give

  Pi_even L(C)=2 ell A gamma,
  Pi_odd  L(C)=-2 ell i gamma Phi.                      (4)

The odd difference in (4), initially written on H1, extends to the displayed
bounded multiplication operator. This remains true for [A,Phi] nonzero.

3. The precise lossless maps
Fix gamma, ell>0 and a nonzero declared mu_geo. Define the original odd reader

  R_Phi=mu_geo Pi_odd L(C).

Then

  R_Phi=-2 ell mu_geo i gamma Phi
       =2 ell mu_geo U_gamma Phi U_gamma*,              (5)

where U_gamma=exp(-i pi gamma/4). Its inverse is exact:

  Phi=(i gamma R_Phi)/(2 ell mu_geo)
     =U_gamma* R_Phi U_gamma/(2 ell mu_geo).             (6)

Thus Phi <-> R_Phi is one-to-one for fixed ell and mu_geo. This statement
does not select their values.

The reader R_Phi intentionally deletes A. With A retained as known source
data, one reconstructs

  h=A gamma+R_Phi/(2 ell mu_geo),                        (7)

then C(h), the complete two-face G(h), and through E plus the conjugate C0
chart the original G(B_Phi). Without the retained A, equation (7) is not a
reader-only reconstruction.

The full covariance contains still more information than R_Phi. From C alone
one obtains L(C), and (4) reconstructs both coefficients:

  A=(Pi_even L(C)) gamma/(2 ell),
  Phi=i gamma Pi_odd L(C)/(2 ell).                       (8)

Hence, within this declared operator class, the full face C, h and the pair
(A,Phi) are equivalent data once gamma and ell are fixed. The complete pure
graph G is also equivalent because its second face is C and its other blocks
are fixed spectral functions of h.

Finally, the given G is a quasifree covariance. It determines all finite CAR
word expectations by Wick's rule, and the already checked unitary carrier and
selfdual charts transport those words. This is mathematical losslessness for
the given quasifree source. It is not an original theorem selecting this
source, Phi, A, ell or mu_geo, and it adds no interaction or physical field
coupling.

4. The direct reconstruction loop selects no Yukawa pattern
If one first removes the fixed U_gamma comparison but does not divide by the
known scale, feeding the reader output back as a new coefficient gives simply

  F(Phi)=c Phi,  c=2 ell mu_geo.

Its fixed points are all odd Phi when c=1, and only Phi=0 when c is not 1.
The latter need not lie in the affine family Phi_s=(1+s)D_Y+D_MR when the
fixed Majorana contribution is nonzero. If instead one uses the exact inverse
(6), the feedback is the identity and again fixes every Phi. Therefore this
lossless reconstruction loop supplies no preference among Yukawa matrices and
no useful genetic search objective by itself. This conclusion is restricted
to direct feedback through this reader. External original constraints, other
fields or an actual action may still select a coefficient; no claim that a
noncommuting compression is the only possible generation mechanism follows.
