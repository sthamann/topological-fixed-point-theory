2. CLOSEDNESS, FREDHOLM PROPERTY AND THE ACTUAL ADJOINT

U*AU=A+I, U gamma=gamma U. The comparison expression

 Lb=partial_r+(A-r/ell)gamma

defines a smooth elliptic first-order operator on the compact clutched
torus bundle: at the seam (A-1)U=UA. Its principal symbol is
i xi_r+gamma xi_theta, invertible away from the zero covector. Consequently
its closed elliptic domain and its adjoint domain are H1 sections, which
are precisely D_U in the cut chart. This is a comparison for the domain
proof, not a replacement of the given L0 or its spectrum.

In the cut chart L0=Lb+(r/ell)gamma. The latter multiplication is bounded
on L2 (it is allowed to have a bounded jump at the seam), so closedness,
the H1 adjoint domain and Fredholmness remain valid. Adding the bounded V
has the same properties. The formal Green boundary expression is

 <LPhi psi,chi>-<psi,(-partial_r+A gamma+V)chi>
   =<psi(ell),chi(ell)>-<psi(0),chi(0)>,

up to conjugation according to which Hermitian argument is linear. Since
U is unitary, it vanishes for both traces in D_U. Conversely the pairing
with every clutched trace forces chi(ell)=U chi(0). Elliptic regularity
supplies H1 for the maximal adjoint domain as above. Thus

 Dom(LPhi*)=D_U,
 LPhi*=-partial_r+A gamma+V.

Neither U^-1 on the outgoing trace nor an extra independent domain for the
conjugate coefficient slot is inserted here.

3. THE TWO EXACT ENERGY IDENTITIES

The finite algebra is

 {A gamma,V}=0, V^2=Phi^2, partial_r V=0, U*VU=V.

The anticommutator uses [A,Phi]=0 and [A,gamma]=0. For smooth clutched
fields, integration in theta gives

 2 Re <A gamma psi,V psi>=0.

Integration in r gives

 2 Re <partial_r psi,V psi>
   =<psi(ell),V psi(ell)>-<psi(0),V psi(0)>
   =<psi(0),(U*VU-V)psi(0)>=0.

Density and boundedness of V extend the result to every psi in D_U:

 ||LPhi psi||^2=||L0 psi||^2+||Phi psi||^2.

Replacing partial_r by -partial_r changes the sign of the boundary
term, still zero, and gives the corresponding adjoint identity

 ||LPhi* psi||^2=||L0* psi||^2+||Phi psi||^2.

No commutation of U with A was used or is true. In fact the massless
identity, initially on smooth clutched fields and then in form sense, is

 ||L0 psi||^2=||partial_r psi||^2+||A psi||^2
                 +<psi(0),gamma psi(0)>.

For L0* the last term has a minus sign. This follows from U*AU=A+1.
Discarding that boundary term would be an error. Keeping the complete
||L0 psi||^2 in the mass-difference identity is exactly what avoids it.

4. KERNELS, COKERNELS AND THE BALANCED INDEX

Since both right-hand summands in the mass-difference identity are
nonnegative,

 ker LPhi=ker L0 intersect ker Phi,
 ker LPhi*=ker L0* intersect ker Phi.

The ranges are closed by Fredholmness, so coker LPhi is identified with
ker LPhi*. These are identities for the actual common clutch domains.
For invertible finite Phi they give zero kernel and zero cokernel.

The massless scalar Fourier recurrence independently checks the index.
In a gamma eigenspace sigma=+1 or -1, homogeneous solutions have

 psi_n(r)=exp(-r sigma(n+alpha))a_n,
 a_n=exp(ell sigma(n+alpha))a_(n-1).

Hence a_n=C exp(ell sigma[n(n+1)/2+alpha n]). Square summability for
all integer n forces C=0 for sigma=+1, and gives one smooth Gaussian
Fourier solution for sigma=-1. For the adjoint the roles are reversed.
Thus, writing dplus/dminus for the full grading dimensions,

 dim ker L0=dminus, dim ker L0*=dplus,
 index LPhi=index L0=dminus-dplus.

The full v252 grading under review has dplus=dminus=48, so its index is
zero. The finite mass homotopy is bounded and preserves Fredholmness.
For constant odd Phi the surviving kernels are respectively the negative
and positive grading parts of ker Phi; no bare right-channel reduction
has been made. A claim that a particular supplied numerical Phi is
invertible still requires that actual coefficient's rank, rather than
an assumption that all Yukawa inputs are nonzero or physical.
