VERDICT

action are correct. The displayed Gaussian orbit frame is not horizontal

1. THE SAME CLOSED DEFECT FAMILY

On H=l2(Z), retain B e_n=(n+alpha)e_n, alpha=1/4 or3/4,
R=exp(-ell B), ell>0, and U e_n=e_(n+1). Put

 T_phi=R-exp(i phi)U, Dom(T_phi)=Dom(R),
 L_phi=ker T_phi*.

Its one-dimensional adjoint kernel is generated by

 y_n=exp(-ell[n(n-1)/2+alpha n]),
 y(phi)_n=exp(i phi n)y_n.

Indeed T_phi*=R-exp(-i phi)U*, and its recurrence is
y(phi)_(n+1)=exp(i phi)exp(-ell(n+alpha))y(phi)_n.
The Gaussian lies in Dom(R), and all its n moments are finite.
The norm is independent of phi. With e_phi=y(phi)/||y||, the frame
is smooth and periodic with e_(2pi)=e_0.

2. THE ORTHOGONAL-PROJECTOR CONNECTION

The family lies in the specified trivial ambient Hilbert bundle. Its
natural induced unitary connection is nabla_proj=P_phi d, where
P_phi=|e_phi><e_phi|. With the Hermitian product linear in the second
argument, set

 mu=<n>=sum_n n |y_n|^2 / sum_n |y_n|^2.

Then <e_phi,partial_phi e_phi>=i mu. A section s=c(phi)e_phi is
projector-parallel precisely when c'+i mu c=0. Therefore

 s_parallel(phi)=exp(-i mu phi)e_phi,
 Hol_proj=exp(-2pi i mu).

This fixes the sign convention explicitly. Reversing the loop orientation
or defining holonomy by inverse transport conjugates the result; it does
not make the numerical value a quarter phase.

3. THE EXISTING DIAGNOSTIC SCALE IS A DIRECT COUNTERCHECK

For alpha=1/4 and ell=4log2 the weights are exactly

 |y_n|^2=2^(-4n^2+2n).

A direct sum gives

 mu=0.1855335532291087...,
 -2pi mu=-1.1657416956379574...,
 Hol_proj=0.3940689714100713-0.9190808700934909 i.

These are not alpha=1/4 or the circle character +i. The inverse holonomy
is not a quarter phase either. The arithmetic is tightly controlled:
sum over -4<=n<=4 is a sum of rational powers of2; the first omitted
positive weight is 2^-90 and successive ratios are at most2^-42.
The first omitted negative weight is2^-110, with ratios at most2^-46.
The resulting absolute error in mu is below6e-27; the printed decimal
is only rounded floating-point output of that rational finite sum.

The old seam-full-collar-reconstruction-20261015/PROOF.txt:153 expressly
calls ell=4log2 a DIAGNOSTIC choice making transfers rational. It is not
being promoted here to a physically derived collar width. It is already
an allowed concrete case of the claimed formula and suffices to test the
purported general identification. For alpha=3/4 the weights reflect
n->-n, so mu and the projected phase angle change sign.

4. WHAT THE CIRCLE ACTION REALLY COMPUTES

The exact given action obeys

 exp(i t B)y(phi)=exp(i alpha t)y(phi+t),
 exp(2pi i B)=exp(2pi i alpha)I.

It transports the defect family and has the declared quarter central
character. Relative to the projected parallel section, its phase is
exp(i(alpha+mu)t), rather than a statement of projector horizontality.
These are entirely compatible facts about the same original operators.

In the periodic e_phi frame three different valid unitary connections
illustrate precisely which extra identification would be needed:

 nabla_proj=d+i mu dphi has holonomy exp(-2pi i mu);
 nabla_frame=d declares e_phi parallel and has holonomy1;
 nabla_orbit=d-i alpha dphi declares exp(i alpha phi)e_phi parallel
             and has holonomy exp(2pi i alpha).

Every connection on a one-dimensional base has zero curvature. Calling a
connection flat thus does not choose its holonomy or identify it with
the orthogonal projection connection. The equation defining e_phi is a
particular frame normalization, not a derivation of nabla_orbit. Ordinary
periodic frame gauge changes leave a fixed connection's holonomy unchanged.
