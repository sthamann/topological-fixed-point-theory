SPIN PRODUCT, CAR U6 AND WHOLE SOURCE TRANSPORT REVIEW
Independent exact type review, 30 October 2026. No Atlas or prior report edit.

VERDICT

The six geometric mixed spin products and the six ordered CAR pair fields
carry equivalent C6 representations, but they are not literally the same
object in their natural charts. The holomorphic product map is ordinary
commutative multiplication and carries +U6. The ordered fermionic pair
acquires the forced Koszul sign and carries -U6. Because the orbit length is
even, the alternating orbit map intertwines -U6 with +U6 exactly. It is
unique up to one common scalar only within the orbit-linewise diagonal
comparison that preserves the six fixed summands. Without that extra
restriction the full intertwiner space is D times the six-dimensional
commutant of the regular cycle P; starting label and orientation alone do not
make it one-dimensional.

The annihilation pair B_ab=a(q_a)a(p_b) is the adjoint/dual chart, not the
holomorphic product line itself. A star closed identification therefore needs
Voff direct-sum bar(Voff), with pair creation on the holomorphic factor and
pair annihilation on its conjugate. The canonical Hodge metric supplies that
antilinear Riesz identification and makes the geometric U6 unitary; it is not
by itself the physical CAR star operation. It does not embed these
six modes into the existing F5/96 CAR source, select its covariance C, or fix
its Hamiltonian h.

No existing reviewed map identifies the p packet with the v252 antiparticle
slots, the T_faces adjoint slots, a hole polarization, or the second boundary
face. The prior q-column comparison uses only the three q lines as generation
slots. Consequently the finite CAR lift below is exact as a conditional
algebraic construction on the given six one-particle modes, but it is not yet
an operation of the already fixed whole source.

The reviewed graph/HS state is the exact mathematical source object used
below. Its further identification with an operational P1 Seed remains the
same stated premise as in the graded source reports; this review does not
promote that premise.

For the existing graph source, same-object covariance requires r C r^*=C;
with C=(1+exp(2 ell h))^(-1), this is equivalent to [r,h]=0. A source-family
transport may instead obey r h_Phi r^*=h_(Phi^R) and carry the state and time
to that target. Neither identity has been derived for Rhat. The geometric
Hodge L2 metric alone cannot supply it.

1. Exact CAR star automorphism and its sixth power

Let

  K6=direct-sum_a (Aq_a direct-sum Ap_a)

be the six-dimensional one-particle zero-mode space and assume the stipulated
unitary spin transport Rhat on K6. Its actual spin lift obeys

  Rhat^6=-I,  Rhat^12=I.                                  (1)

Every unitary on a one-particle Hilbert space defines a unique gauge
preserving CAR star automorphism

  alpha_R(a(f))=a(Rhat f),
  alpha_R(a(f)^*)=a(Rhat f)^*.                            (2)

Here a(f) is antilinear in f and a(f)^* is linear. Equation (2) preserves
the CAR, multiplication and the star operation. From (1),

  alpha_R^6=gamma_CAR,  alpha_R^12=id,                    (3)

where gamma_CAR is fermion parity. Hence alpha_R^6 is the identity on the
even pair algebra while it is minus the identity on odd fields. This is the
correct whole-algebra statement; calling Rhat an order-six field operation
would drop its central fermion parity.

2. Pair fields versus ordinary holomorphic products

Use the product report's orbit convention

  (a,b)->(b+1,a+1),  a!=b,                               (4)

and write B_ab=a(q_a)a(p_b). Since Rhat exchanges q and p, direct use of the
CAR gives, with all already fixed spin/Kummer phases retained in the
transition maps,

  alpha_R(B_ab)=- B_(b+1,a+1).                            (5)

The minus sign is forced by moving the transformed p annihilator past the
transformed q annihilator. It is absent from the geometric formula because
mu_ab is ordinary multiplication of complex line-bundle sections.
Using the inverse pullback convention reverses P in both formulas below and
does not alter the relative minus sign or the alternating intertwiner.

Let e_j be the normalized geometric orbit basis of the mixed product report,
so R_V e_j=e_(j-1), and let B_j have the same ordered orbit labels. Then

  R_V=P,  alpha_R|pair=-P.                                (6)

Put D e_j=(-1)^j e_j. Exact 6 by 6 calculation gives

  D(-P)D^-1=P,  D^2=I,                                   (7)

and both P and -P have characteristic polynomial t^6-1. Thus

  I_pair(B_j)=s (-1)^j e_j                                (8)

is an equivariant isomorphism for every nonzero common scalar s. The
recursion imposed by equivariance makes (8) unique up to s among diagonal
orbit-linewise maps that preserve each of the six named summands. The
starting summand (0,1) and the positive R orientation then fix the
alternating sign sequence; the scalar contains the remaining relative
normalization. For arbitrary linear comparisons the complete solution is

  I_pair = D Q,  with QP=PQ,                              (8a)

so the intertwiner space is D times the six-dimensional commutant of P.
Equivariance alone permits summand mixing and does not select D uniquely.

Equation (8) proves equivalence of the two finite representations. It does
not make ordinary holomorphic multiplication equal to the graded CAR product.
That equality would require the explicitly signed comparison (8).

3. Adjoint pair sector and the rho line

The holomorphic product section is naturally linear in its two spin
sections. It therefore matches a pair-creation chart before taking the star.
With C_ab=a(q_a)^*a(p_b)^*, its CAR transform also carries -P. The proposed
annihilation operator is related by

  B_ab^*=a(p_b)^*a(q_a)^*=-C_ab.                          (9)

Consequently annihilation pairs transform in the conjugate/dual
representation. If a coherent creation pair has eigenvalue rho, its adjoint
annihilation pair has eigenvalue bar(rho). A star closed finite object is

  P_pair = Voff direct-sum bar(Voff),                     (10)

not Voff alone. A Hermitian quadrature C_rho+C_rho^* spans a two-dimensional
real rotation plane; it is not a rho eigenoperator.

This distinction does not remove the positive result. The rho and bar(rho)
lines are both present and are exchanged by star. It prevents an untyped
claim that the holomorphic rho line itself is already the annihilation field
or a selfadjoint physical insertion.

4. Why a hole or dual relabeling is not yet an existing source map

There are three different dualities in the reviewed whole source:

  * J_F is the finite KO6 particle/antiparticle antiunitary on the actual
    96-dimensional v252 coefficient;
  * C0 and the bar(w) entries of T_faces provide the selfdual CAR adjoint
    completion of one charged Hilbert space;
  * the negative-energy sea of h_Phi turns some charged annihilators into
    hole creators in its Fock presentation.

None has been identified with the geometric p packet. The q/p rotation R is
complex linear and holomorphic, covers z->1/z, and exchanges two distinct
coefficient/spin packets. J_F is antiunitary and exchanges the already given
finite particle and antiparticle labels. T_faces duplicates the same charged
space into field and adjoint slots and into two boundary faces. A hole label
depends on the spectral polarization of h_Phi. These types cannot be equated
from their dimensions or from the common occurrence of a conjugate space.

The existing full real P2 dictionary verifies the actual action of J_F,
gamma_F, compact conjugation and all D5 generators. The existing declared
q-column comparison maps the three horizontal q columns to the three v252
generation slots. It supplies no image for the three p columns and no unitary

  iota_6: K6 -> H_charged                              (11)

which intertwines Rhat with an operation of the F5/96 source. Assigning p to
antiparticles, holes, adjoints or the second face would be four different new
maps, each with different charge, anti/linearity and time behavior.

The numerical resemblance Rhat^6=-I to other parity-square maps in the F5
reports does not give (11). In particular the known complex-linear
composition C_raw has square -I but transports Phi to Phi^K and reverses the
circle parameter. The native product reflection R_geo is antiunitary and
preserves each fixed graph. J_F has its separate KO6 action. None has the
same base, coefficient packet and source target as Rhat.

Map (11) is therefore a sufficient direct six-mode comparison, not a
necessary form of every possible source-family realization. A different
possibility is an original pullback which carries the q source over z->1/z
to a distinct p source, with the basis-inversion sign compensated by an
already represented chiral or dual swap. Such a map need not place q and p
as six independent modes inside one fixed 96-dimensional coefficient. It
must still be tested on the actual operator h_Phi, state, time and face
charts; none of those identities follows from the product U6 alone.

5. The actual given state and time test

In the reviewed graded whole source the charged one-particle generator is

  h_Phi=A gamma_F-i gamma_F Phi,                            (12)

and the finite-collar graph/HS state has

  C_Phi=(1+exp(2 ell h_Phi))^-1,
  X_Phi=(2 cosh(ell h_Phi))^-1.                            (13)

The real-time source automorphism is implemented by exp(i t h_Phi), and the
graph state is invariant under that actual group. If a unitary r on the same
charged Hilbert space is to act on the same source object, it must satisfy

  r C_Phi r^*=C_Phi.                                       (14)

For finite ell>0 the scalar function x->(1+exp(2 ell x))^-1 is injective on
the real line. Functional calculus therefore makes (14) equivalent to

  r h_Phi r^*=h_Phi,  or [r,h_Phi]=0.                      (15)

Then the cross covariance X_Phi is preserved as well and the CAR
automorphism fixes the graph state. If instead

  r h_Phi r^*=h_(Phi^R),                                   (16)

it transports the whole state and time to another member of the source
family. This is a valid covariance, but not a symmetry of one fixed source.

The exact E0 review provides an example of (16): it transports the complete
F5 graph, T_faces chart and circle group to explicitly transformed
coefficients. It becomes a same-source operation only for appropriately
fixed controls or after a separately allowed commutant equivalence. No
analogue of (15) or (16) has been shown for Rhat. The direct embedding (11)
is absent, and the alternative q-to-p source-family pullback has not yet been
checked on h_Phi and the whole F5 chart.

The Hodge inner product on the degree-sixteen Kummer cover proves that R_V
is unitary on Voff. It is not the covariance (13), does not define h_Phi,
and does not select a Fock sea. Extending the six-mode Rhat by the identity on
an arbitrarily chosen orthogonal complement would create a new source
operation rather than derive the original one.

There is also a sharp consequence if a future same-source map satisfies
(14). For a nontrivial eigenoperator O with alpha_R(O)=rho O, invariance gives

  omega_Phi(O)=omega_Phi(alpha_R(O))=rho omega_Phi(O)=0.    (17)

Thus source symmetry alone does not prepare a nonzero rho one-point
amplitude. Relative insertions, two-point functions or a covariant source
family can still see the character. Their consumer must be the existing
source operation, not a state chosen from the Hodge metric.

6. Smallest missing whole-field alternatives

A sufficient next theorem for a direct six-mode embedding is the explicit
isometric map (11), not another U6 spectrum calculation. It would make the
following data commute together:

  q+p spin zero modes --Rhat--> q+p spin zero modes
          | iota_6                    | iota_6
          v                           v
  actual charged H  ------r------> actual charged H or H_(Phi^R),

and it must additionally verify:

  1. its q restriction agrees with the already declared q-to-generation
     comparison, while its p restriction has an original charge/dual label;
  2. r respects gamma_F, the represented finite algebra and J_F in their
     actual types;
  3. r extends selfdually through C0 and all four T_faces slots;
  4. the pair-product square commutes with mu_ab and the CAR pair operation
     through the forced alternating intertwiner (8), including (9)-(10);
  5. either the fixed-source equation (15) or the explicit source-family
     covariance (16), with the already given graph state and circle time;
  6. the original reflection and reference data, rather than only the Hodge
     target norm.

This diagram is not a universal necessity. The other concrete route is a
q-to-p pullback between two source-family members. Its first decisive test is
the actual action of the original spinframe pullback, including its basis
inversion sign, together with the represented linear chiral/dual swap on

  h_Phi=A gamma_F-i gamma_F Phi,

followed by C_Phi, circle time and all T_faces slots. Success would establish
(16) without embedding K6 into one fixed charged space; failure at that
operator identity would rule out this source-family route in the tested
chart.

Until either source map is supplied, the strongest exact statement is:

  * the original curve gives a canonical geometric mixed-product U6;
  * the stipulated six one-particle modes admit a canonical CAR star
    automorphism and an equivalent signed pair U6 module;
  * the full star closed pair module is Voff plus its conjugate;
  * no reviewed source identifies this module with the existing F5/96
    state/time object.

These are two precise missing source maps, not a proposal for a new six-mode
Gaussian source. The direct iota_6 diagram is the stronger embedding claim;
the q-to-p pullback alternative asks only for whole-family covariance.
