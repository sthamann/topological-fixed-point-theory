CLAUDE39 FN DISTANCE CANDIDATE AGAINST THE ORIGINAL LENGTH DATA
================================================================

Independent narrow source review, 30 October 2026.  No Atlas or registered
source was changed.

VERDICT
-------

Claude39's proposed identification

  L_a proportional to dist(alpha+kappa_a,Z)               (1)

fails the first original arithmetic test.  With the proposed

  alpha=1/4,  kappa={0,1/3,2/3},

the three distances are

  x={1,3,5}/12.                                           (2)

Every common positive scaling and offset of (2) is an arithmetic
progression.  None of the three original diagonal rows is one:

  sorted L_u={0,3,7}, differences 3,4;
  sorted L_d={2,5,7}, differences 3,2;
  sorted L_e={3,5,8}, differences 2,3.                    (3)

The mismatch remains if the candidate is read in its two-leg form.  The
original matrix exponent is L^L_(f,i)+L^R_(f,j).  If both legs use the same
three distances (2), with the common collar scale ell and arbitrary
permutations, the centered diagonal sums can only have one of the forms

  (-4,0,4), (-2,0,2), (-4,2,2), (-2,-2,4), (0,0,0)

up to a common positive scale.  The centered original triples are

  L_u: (-10,-1,11)/3,
  L_d: (-8,1,7)/3,
  L_e: (-7,-1,8)/3,                                      (4)

so none matches.  Separate leg scales, sector-dependent channel weights or
non-affine readouts could evade (3)-(4), but they are extra data and are not
the Claude39 distance candidate.


1. WHAT THE ORIGINAL LENGTHS MEAN
---------------------------------

The original hard-holonomy formula is

  (Y_f)_ij=lambda_Y^(L^L_(f,i)+L^R_(f,j))
             <e_i,Hol_(Gamma_ij^min)(nabla_F^star)e_j>.   (5)

It defines D_L,f and D_R,f as diagonal word-length factors, but the displayed
source does not give a separate numerical decomposition of the diagonal
totals into L^L and L^R.  What it fixes numerically is

  L_u^diag=(7,3,0),
  L_d^diag=(7,5,2),
  L_e^diag=(8,5,3).                                      (6)

These totals come from the sector-dependent residue packets

  R_u={0,1,3}, R_d={1,2,5}, R_e={2,3,5},                 (7)

plus one complete winding of length six in each first-generation direction.
The same source explicitly interprets them as graph distances on C6.  Thus
the original already carries channel information that is not contained in a
single universal distance triple (2).

The missing separate L/R split cannot rescue (1): under the candidate's own
common x_a and ell assumptions the complete permutation calculation gives
the obstruction (4).  Treating L^L and L^R as independently fitted triples
would abandon the claimed derivation from alpha and kappa.


2. THE KAPPA VALUES ARE NOT YET THE ORIGINAL CIRCLE CLUTCH
---------------------------------------------------------

The actual source fixes three different objects that must not be collapsed:

* the scalar charged CAR quarter alpha=1/4 or 3/4;
* the order-three coefficient/deck action Pc, whose Fourier eigenvalues are
  1,omega,omega^2;
* the v117 quarter coefficient matrix KQ with eigenvalues 1,i,-i.

The local Fuchs exponents 0,1/3,2/3 describe puncture monodromy.  They do not
by themselves prove that the tangential collar operator has spectrum

  n+alpha+kappa_a,

or that Pc is its clutch.  The existing source review states the correct
conditional form: a nontrivial family clutch MAY produce such shifts, in
which case the full covariant cross kernel must be used.  No checked original
identity currently equates the Fuchs exponents with the scalar circle clutch.

Therefore (2) is an assumed diagnostic case, not an already fixed original
boundary spectrum.  Even under that favorable assumption, (3)-(4) reject the
proposed FN identification.


3. SCOPE
--------

The exact mode formula

  c_(a,n)=1/[2 cosh ell(n+alpha+kappa_a)]                 (8)

is valid for a collar operator that actually has the displayed shifted
spectrum.  Its large-ell leading term is exp[-ell dist(alpha+kappa_a,Z)].
The full sum over n is not this leading exponential, and the original exact
FN powers are not derived by a leading-mode approximation.

This review rejects only the identification (1), including its common
two-leg version.  It does not reject the already established cover
pi:C->X_f, the global sign-line dictionary, the whole B_mu endpoint kernel,
or a future response using the actual full clutch and all modes.  Such a
response would be a different calculation and must reproduce the
sector-dependent data (6)-(7), rather than relabel the Frobenius exponents as
the original word lengths.
