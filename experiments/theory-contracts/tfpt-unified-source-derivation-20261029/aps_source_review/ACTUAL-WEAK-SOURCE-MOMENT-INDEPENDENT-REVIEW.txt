1. Operator types and signs
The checker uses the actual v252 representation. With

  T=pi(0,i sigma_x,0)

the particle block is i tx. Since J_F is antiunitary,

  J_F T J_F^-1=-i J_F tx J_F^-1.

Therefore the infinitesimal real gauge lift and its Hermitian coefficient are

  L=T+J_F T J_F^-1=i(tx-J_F tx J_F^-1),
  Q=-iL=tx-J_F tx J_F^-1.                              (1)

The minus sign on the antiparticle block is correct. The exact checker verifies
Q=Q* and [Q,gamma_F]=0. For A(t)=pI+tQ,

  h(t)=A(t)gamma_F-i gamma_F D_F

is selfadjoint because A commutes with gamma_F, D_F is selfadjoint and
{D_F,gamma_F}=0. Direct multiplication gives

  h(t)^2=A(t)^2+D_F^2-i[A(t),D_F].                    (2)

Thus the construction retains the noncommuting weak response. In the code,
H0+tH1=4h at p=1/4 is exact:

  H0=gamma_F-4i gamma_F D_F,
  H1=4Q gamma_F.

The polynomial update multiplies these matrices in their actual order and does
not replace them by commuting symbols.

2. Independent factor check
Suppress colour and the conjugate block for one moment. On one particle colour
block order the states as (u_L,d_L,u_R,d_R). Q is sigma_x on the left doublet
and zero on the right singlets. Exact block multiplication gives for two down
matrices with the same singular values

  Delta [t^2] Tr h^6
    =6 Delta Tr((Y_dY_d*) (Y_uY_u*)).                 (3)

The factor6 is the cyclic sixth-word multiplicity. The actual v252 quark sector
has three identical colours, and its J_F conjugate block gives the same real
trace. Consequently

  Delta [t^2] Tr_96 h^6
    =36 Delta Tr((Y_dY_d*) (Y_uY_u*)).                (4)

For the checker's data the family traces are440 and280. Equation(4) yields

  36(440-280)=5760,

which independently reproduces the saved symbolic result. The identical,
nonzero Ye, Ynu and MR blocks contribute equally to both controls and cancel in
the difference. They were nevertheless retained in the exact96 construction.

The checker also obtains Delta Tr h^2=Delta Tr h^4=0 and

  Delta Tr h^6=5760 t^2

as a polynomial identity, not at a selected numerical value of t. The down
singular data agree exactly. I found no sign, order or normalization error in
this calculation.

3. Cross-face coefficients
For the actual finite cross block

  X=(2 cosh(ell h))^-1

the exact even expansion is

  X=I/2-ell^2h^2/4+5ell^4h^4/48-61ell^6h^6/1440+O(ell^8).

Using the moment differences above gives

  Delta Tr X
    =-(61/1440) 5760 ell^6 t^2+O(ell^8)
    =-244 ell^6 t^2+O(ell^8).                         (5)

Moreover

  log det X=-96 log2-Tr log cosh(ell h),
  log cosh z=z^2/2-z^4/12+z^6/45+O(z^8),

so

  Delta log det X=-(5760/45)ell^6t^2+O(ell^8)
                 =-128 ell^6t^2+O(ell^8).             (6)

Both reported coefficients are correct. These are finite p=1/4 asymptotics;
they do not exchange the ell expansion with an infinite circle-mode sum.

4. Stronger statement for every fixed positive width
For t nonzero, equation(4) says that the third power sums of the eigenvalues of
h(t)^2 differ. Hence the two h(t)^2 eigenvalue multisets differ. On u>=0 the map

  u -> 1/(2 cosh(ell sqrt(u)))

is injective for every fixed ell>0. Functional calculus therefore sends the two
different h^2 multisets to different X eigenvalue multisets for every such ell.
An individual scalar trace can coincide at an isolated ell, but the complete
cross-block spectra cannot.

The full unlabelled two-face G remains an orthogonal projection of the same rank
in both cases, so its spectrum is still only0 and1. The conclusion concerns its
labelled face and crossing blocks, equivalently the retained logit data. The
root report states this distinction correctly.
