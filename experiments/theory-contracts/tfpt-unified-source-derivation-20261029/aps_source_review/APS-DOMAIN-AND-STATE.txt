3. Exact inverse and the smallest state comparison
For this constant B the claimed inverse is correct:
  G_APS(r,s)=1_(r>s) exp(-(r-s)B)P_+
             -1_(s>r) exp(-(r-s)B)P_-.
Away from r=s it solves L G=0; its jump is P_++P_-=I. Its r=0 range is H_-
and its r=ell range is H_+. A homogeneous solution obeying both endpoint
conditions is zero. The formula is therefore the inverse, not just a formal
parametrix. Spectral calculus gives bounded decaying exponentials on each
indicated half. The kernel's Hilbert operator norm is <=1 pointwise; a safe
L2 inverse bound is ||L^-1||<=delta^-1. An unqualified inverse norm <=1 does
not follow from the quarter gap. Generic Fredholmness alone is not invertibility.

Given charged CAR quantization, this is the time-ordered vacuum propagator
with sea P_-(h), and the usual particle/hole Fock construction has excitation
Hamiltonian sum |lambda| n_exc >=0. This is a correct source realization for
the given h. The analytic realization theorem alone does not identify its
field operators, state or radial translations with the original Seed.

It also does not replace the already established whole finite-collar graph.
Its labeled face occupation is C(h)=(1+exp(2ell h))^-1, with nonzero cross
X=(2cosh(ell h))^-1. On any positive eigenmode lambda, the APS vacuum has
