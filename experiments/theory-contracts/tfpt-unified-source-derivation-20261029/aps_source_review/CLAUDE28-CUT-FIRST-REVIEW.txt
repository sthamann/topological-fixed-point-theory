1. COMPLETE FINITE CLASS, NOT JUST THE k=1 EXAMPLE

Let M={1,i,-1,-i}, and retain its original circle |z|=1. All holomorphic
Mobius maps preserving M are precisely

 r_k(z)=i^k z, s_k(z)=i^k/z, k=0,1,2,3.

Their restrictions have the eight D4 permutations of the marked circle.
A circle-preserving Mobius map preserves or reverses cyclic order, hence
has at most these eight possible permutations; three points determine
the map uniquely, and the displayed maps realize all eight.

Composing with ordinary conjugation gives every anti-Mobius map:

 sigma_k(z)=i^k bar(z), rho_k(z)=i^k/bar(z).

There are sixteen sphere maps in total, with only eight distinct circle
restrictions: the central equatorial involution rho_0 acts identically
on the circle. Equivalently the full sphere stabilizer is D4 x C2.
Every map taking the four marks to themselves already preserves their
unique generalized circle. Thus no further anti-Mobius maps are omitted.

All four sigma_k are antiinvolutions. Their fixed line is
arg z=k pi/4 modulo pi. For k=0,2 this line hits two marks. For k=1,3
its circle intersection consists of two unmarked points. In particular

 sigma_1=i bar(z), sigma_3=-i bar(z)

are TWO distinct mark-avoiding real cuts. Rotation r_1 conjugates
sigma_1 to sigma_3. In particular they are equivalent under the already
supplied original quarter symmetry if no further marking breaks it.

For completeness rho_k^2=r_(2k). The only antiinvolutions of this form
are rho_0 (fixed circle containing all marks) and rho_2 (no fixed points).
rho_1,rho_3 have order4, so they are not real involutions defining cuts.
The holomorphic s_1,s_3 have the same unmarked fixed-point pairs on the
circle, but are not antiinvolutions of the sphere.

2. q COMPATIBILITY AND WHETHER ZERO MUST BE FIXED

The original curve has div z=Dq-Dp. Maps r_k and sigma_k fix 0 and
infinity separately and preserve the q/p packets; maps s_k and rho_k
exchange 0 and infinity and exchange the packets. Consequently retention
of the selected q packet leaves four holomorphic rotations and four
antiinvolutions sigma_k. Of their real cuts exactly sigma_1 AND sigma_3
avoid the marks. There is no surviving unique candidate.

Fixing0 is not forced by the four marks or the circle alone: s_k,rho_k
are explicit counterexamples. It follows here from the ADDITIONAL
retained q-versus-p marking. This is legitimate when that ordered packet
is part of the original source data; it must not be advertised as a
consequence of the unlabelled four-point set.

On C the two anti maps are Q tau0 and Q^3 tau0. They differ by the
hyperelliptic involution Q^2, which fixes all branch points. Both fix
each q_a and preserve the divisor classes S0 and L_a. Mere preservation
of these classes, even with the flat coefficient lifts below, still
does not identify a graded Pin or physical CAR reflection.

Retain the real rational original A from ORIGINAL-FUCHS-ALGEBRAIC-FRAME:

For rho_0(z)=1/bar(z) the anti-pulled coefficient is

 -z^-2 A(1/z)=A(z)^t-diag(2,1,1)/z.

Every entry of this identity was checked exactly. This is a dual-type

D4-CONNECTION-LIFT.txt.

Now specify the convention rather than infer a sign from a label. For

Equivalently, on sections the anti-pulled coefficient before multiplication

The known algebraic fundamental matrix G supplies an independent
