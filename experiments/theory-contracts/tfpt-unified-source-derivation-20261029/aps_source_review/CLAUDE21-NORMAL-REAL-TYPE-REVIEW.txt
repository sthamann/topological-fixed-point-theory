1. Verdict
Lemma P is false as a general prohibition of an antilinear reflection
covering orientation reversal of the normal sphere. Its fourth-slot equation
m_4=-1-m_4 does not follow for an antilinear Real bundle map.
The linear dual formula used there and a Real structure are different types.

2. Smallest global counterexample in the existing carrier class
Take X=CP1 with sigma_X([z0:z1])=[bar z0:bar z1] and
  E=O_X(1) direct-sum O_X^4, D=det E=O_X(1).
This is the same ordinary rank-five normal bundle representative already
used in the September22 joint-lift proof, not a new physical source.
Conjugation of homogeneous coordinates gives the canonical antilinear
involution on the tautological O(-1), its dual O(1), and the four trivial
lines. Hence
  j_E:E_x -> E_sigma(x), j_E^2=1,
  E ~= sigma_X^* bar E.
On degree-two cohomology sigma_X reverses orientation, while conjugating
a complex bundle reverses c1. Therefore
  c1(sigma_X^* bar D)=(-1)(-c1(D))=c1(D)=1.
Keeping only the first of these two sign changes produces Claude's error.

Exterior powers, duals and integer tensor powers are Real functors.
For EVERY integer tuple with sum m_j=-2,
  W_m=Lambda-even E tensor direct-sum_j D^{m_j}
has the explicit global map
  j_W=(Lambda-even j_E) tensor direct-sum_j j_D^{m_j}.
It is antilinear, involutive and covers sigma_X. Each m_j is UNCHANGED.
In particular the fourth flat-trivial slot D^{m_4} is preserved for integer
m_4, without requiring m_4=-1/2. Standard Real Hermitian metrics and their
Chern connections provide compatible connections in this same example.

By contrast, over a FIXED E the complex-linear duality identity is
  W_m^* ~= Lambda-odd E tensor direct-sum_j D^{-1-m_j}.
It uses Lambda-even E^* ~= Lambda-odd E tensor D^-1.
That identity does not describe j_W, which preserves exterior parity and
covers sigma_X antilinearly. It cannot be applied as the reflection rule
without an additional, different identification.

3. Extension to all E8 branches and Lie products
The counterexample is not confined to an arbitrary standalone 64-space.
Use the complexification
  K_C=(Spin(10,C) x SL(4,C))/diagonal mu4
and the actual natural iota_m from the September22 proof.
In the compatible wedge/contraction realization of the spinor,
  s(g,z)=z^-1 Lambda-even g, z^2=det g,
the family factor is diag(z^{2m_j+1}). These maps, their Clifford
contractions and the integer-cocharacter quotient are defined over R.
Thus the split Real involution r_K satisfies
  r_K(iota_m(g))=iota_m(bar g).
The local root change z->-z still cancels by (-1,-I4); conjugation
preserves this quotient kernel. No square-root line is introduced.

A concrete full-E8 realization uses a real Chevalley chart. Write compact
conjugation as
  c_c(H)=-H, c_c(e_alpha)=-e_-alpha, anti-linearly,
and the linear Chevalley involution as
  theta(H)=-H, theta(e_alpha)=-e_-alpha.
With compatible Chevalley signs these preserve the Lie brackets and square
to one. Their composition r_s=theta c_c is coefficient conjugation:
  r_s(H)=H, r_s(e_alpha)=e_alpha, anti-linearly.
It preserves every bracket since the Chevalley structure constants are real.
The linear involution induces -I on the eight-dimensional root chart,
an E8 Weyl transformation normalizing D5+D3. It exchanges the two 64
branches; c_c also exchanges them. Their composition preserves each branch.
In the real wedge chart its action on W_m is the j_W construction above.

Therefore the transition rule r_s iota_m(g) r_s^-1=iota_m(bar g)
constructs a genuine antilinear Real automorphism of the whole associated
complex E8 Lie-algebra bundle. All five K branches and Lie products are
included. This uses SPLIT/Chevalley conjugation, not compact conjugation
alone. It supplies an existence counterexample to Lemma P, not a selection
of the physically intended TFPT reflection or its real form.
