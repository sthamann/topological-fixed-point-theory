HARDY B_MU WEAK CLIFFORD BLOCK: ACTUAL KO6 AND FOUR-SECTOR COMPLETION
30 October 2026

Scope

This is a separate supplement to HARDY-BMU-WEAK-CLIFFORD-ODD-LIFT-REVIEW.txt.
It does not change that frozen report.  It answers one narrower question on the
actual v252 finite Hilbert space H_F=C^96: what the adjoint and KO6 completions
of the nonzero Hardy/weak-Clifford block are, and whether the completed matrix
already has the type of the existing finite scalar coefficient.  It does not
derive an operation which applies the weak Clifford creator to the P1 source.

Inputs and conventions

The exact Hardy entry is

  B_02=kappa E_02 P_gamma,+,
  kappa=(4 X_*/pi) I !=0.

Here I is the already computed non-real Hardy integral.  E_02 is the same
family matrix on particle and antiparticle sectors.  For a=1,2 let X_a be the
actual family-blind weak exterior creator in the signed H_F<->3 copies of
Lambda(C^5) dictionary.  The previous exact commutator is

  A_a=[X_a,B_02]=kappa E_02 X_a gamma_F.                 (1)

The actual finite real structure is J_F=K_swap followed by coefficient
conjugation.  It obeys J_F^2=1, J_F gamma_F=-gamma_F J_F and epsilon'=+1 in
KO dimension 6.  These are the definitions and checks in
verification/v252_full_finite_triple.py, especially lines 19-31 and 119-132.
The real inner-fluctuation prescription is

  D -> D+A+epsilon' J_F A J_F^-1,

as stated in verification/v254_inner_fluctuations.py, lines 6-12.

1. Adjoint completion is not yet KO-real

The signed H_F dictionary gives

  K_swap X_a K_swap = X_a^T=X_a^dagger,
  K_swap gamma_F K_swap=-gamma_F,
  [E_02,X_a]=[E_02,gamma_F]=0.

Consequently

  J_F A_a J_F^-1 = -bar(kappa) E_02 X_a^dagger gamma_F,
  A_a^dagger      = -bar(kappa) E_20 X_a^dagger gamma_F. (2)

The different family matrix in the second line is decisive.  Therefore

  D_adj=A_a+A_a^dagger

is selfadjoint and gamma_F-odd, but

  J_F D_adj J_F^-1 != D_adj.                             (3)

For both weak components its exact rank is 32.  Thus selfadjointness does not
replace the finite KO condition.

2. The actual KO6 completion is nonzero and rigid

With epsilon'=+1 the completed block is

  D_KO = D_adj+J_F D_adj J_F^-1
       = kappa S_02 X_a gamma_F
         -bar(kappa) S_02 X_a^dagger gamma_F,
  S_02=E_02+E_20.                                       (4)

It obeys exactly

  D_KO^dagger=D_KO,
  {D_KO,gamma_F}=0,
  J_F D_KO J_F^-1=D_KO,
  D_KO^2=|kappa|^2 (E_00+E_22) tensor I_32.              (5)

Hence rank(D_KO)=64 for each a.  The completion cannot cancel for the actual
nonzero kappa.  Equation (5) also shows that no phase or frame adjustment was
used: the omitted positive real factor 4 X_*/pi changes only the common scale.

3. Complete particle, antiparticle, lepton and quark form

The family coefficient is the same symmetric matrix S_02 in every carrier
sector.  The carrier coefficient is not identical in the four sectors.  In
the ordered weak bases (nu,e) and (u,d), the following are the LR matrix
blocks, meaning rows L and columns R.  The RL blocks are their adjoints as
required by (4).

For X_1:

  particle lepton  LR: [[0,-kappa],[-bar(kappa),0]],
  particle quark   LR: [[0, kappa],[ bar(kappa),0]],
  antiparticle lepton:   [[0,-bar(kappa)],[-kappa,0]],
  antiparticle quark:    [[0, bar(kappa)],[ kappa,0]].

For X_2:

  particle lepton  LR: [[bar(kappa),0],[0,-kappa]],
  particle quark   LR: [[-bar(kappa),0],[0,kappa]],
  antiparticle lepton:   [[kappa,0],[0,-bar(kappa)]],
  antiparticle quark:    [[-kappa,0],[0,bar(kappa)]].

Thus there is one common family matrix S_02, accompanied by fixed carrier
signs and conjugations.  Quark blocks are minus the corresponding lepton
blocks.  Antiparticle blocks are the J_F conjugates of particle blocks.  This
is a constrained P2/SO(10) carrier pattern.  It is not four freely chosen
matrices Y_nu,Y_e,Y_u,Y_d, and it must not be reported as their equality.

The block is colour diagonal.  Its holomorphic component A_a carries the
already checked weak-doublet weight and hypercharge +1/2; the adjoint supplies
the conjugate component.  It has no particle-antiparticle Majorana block.
It therefore fits the algebraic support and quantum numbers of a finite
Higgs-doublet coefficient, not the sigma direction.  The old v254 statement
that finite gamma_F-odd one-forms are Lorentz-scalar Higgs coefficients is the
relevant type comparison.  Membership in the old one-form space still does
not follow: that space is generated from a supplied D_F, whereas (1) uses the
Hardy block and an additional weak Clifford operation.

4. C0, Lorentz type and the two meanings of parity

The geometric operator in the collar contract is

  C0=i0 N tensor I_96.

It commutes with D_KO as an old-complex-linear normal operator.  It neither
changes E_02 into E_20 nor supplies the missing terms in (4).  The explicit
separation of C0 from J_F is already part of FINITE-COUPLED.txt, lines 53-76,
and charge_transport/DERIVED-PAIRING.txt, lines 32-60.  J_F supplies the
finite KO completion; C0 supplies the field/adjoint slots of the selfdual
collar chart.  Combining their roles would double count the completion.

As a bounded selfadjoint gamma_F-odd finite coefficient, D_KO can occupy the
same internal slot Phi in the product-collar operator

  B_Phi=Z tensor A-S tensor Phi,
  h_Phi=A gamma_F-i0 gamma_F Phi.

This is the exact operator type in FINITE-COUPLED.txt, lines 10-40.  The weak
Clifford creator X_a acts on the internal finite carrier.  It is not a
spacetime Clifford generator.  Therefore its gamma_F oddness is finite
chirality parity.  After second quantization the quadratic background term
is CAR-even and bosonic.  It is not an odd CAR field insertion.  These two
parities are independent.

5. What remains open at the original source

The full KO6 completion removes no source gap and introduces no new one.  The
previous source test remains exact:

* all 45 D5 current zero modes are even Clifford bilinears and commute with
  P_gamma,+, so they cannot produce (1);
* a genuine mixed E8 current has the additional family-six leg, whereas X_a
  in (1) is family blind;
* the old NCG sewing operation a[D_F,b] requires a supplied finite D_F.  At
  Y=MR=0 every such one-form vanishes while (1) and (4) remain nonzero.

The completed matrix is therefore a valid nonzero finite scalar candidate if
the source applies the weak Clifford operation after the Hardy reader.  No
examined original P1, D5-current or old inner-fluctuation rule performs that
application.  The first missing statement is still that concrete source
operation, not adjointness, KO reality, carrier support or nonvanishing.

Verdict

The naive adjoint block A+A^dagger is insufficient.  The unchanged KO6 rule
requires the J_F image.  The resulting block is selfadjoint, gamma_F-odd,
J_F-real and rank 64, with one common symmetric family matrix S_02 and fixed
sector signs/conjugations.  It has the internal Lorentz-scalar Higgs-doublet
type of the finite Phi slot.  This establishes the complete operator typing,
but not its execution by the original source.
