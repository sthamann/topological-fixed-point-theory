E0 REFLECTION ON THE WHOLE v252 FINITE SOURCE: TYPE AND COVARIANCE REVIEW
Independent operator review, 29 October 2026.
No original source or Atlas record was changed.

RESULT

The actual E0 coefficient reflection extends to the entire original
96-dimensional v252 finite coefficient and to the complete F5 two-face graph.
It is a covariance of the already given source family.  For a generic v252
Yukawa/Majorana coefficient it is not a symmetry of one fixed Phi.

The existing finite charge J_F does not already contain the E0 flavour
matrix.  The two antiunitaries instead commute.  In the F5 complex structure
their correctly typed combination transports the whole operator, its pure
graph covariance, all charged/conjugate slots and the circle group.  The
circle parameter is reversed.  No new covariance or state is selected.

For real constructor controls there is an additional exact commutant unitary
which turns the family covariance into a same-Phi antiunitary.  This is a
unitary equivalence of the finite triples,
but it is not an inner gauge transformation of the displayed algebra A_F.
Its availability in the original physical source action menu, with the
native K/clock marking fixed, is not established here.

1. ACTUAL E0 MATRIX AND THE 96-DIMENSIONAL EXTENSION

In the checked horizontal G-column frame the original algebraic Fuchs
solution gives

  D_E = Gbase^-1 U_F bar(Gbase) = omega^2 S,
  omega^2+omega+1=0,  S=diag(1,-1,1).

Thus D_E bar(D_E)=I.  Let P_E be the actual intrinsic NS reflection
theta -> pi/2-theta and kappa pointwise OLD complex conjugation.  On v252,
let Dcal act by D_E on the three generations of every particle field and by
bar(D_E) on every antiparticle field.  Define

  K_E = Dcal kappa P_E.

The particle/antiparticle distinction is essential.  Directly from the
blocks one obtains

  K_E^2=I,  [K_E,gamma_F]=0,  [K_E,J_F]=0,
  J_F=K_swap kappa.

The last equality uses

  Dcal=diag(D_E,bar(D_E)),
  K_swap bar(Dcal)=Dcal K_swap.

Therefore E0 is not inserted by composing two unrelated copies of complex
conjugation.  Literal composition of two antiunitaries would be OLD linear
and would answer a different KO/type question.  Here K_E and J_F remain two
commuting antiunitaries on the same actual 96-dimensional space.

2. THE FULL v252 PHI AND THE MAJORANA TRANSPOSE

Write the actual constructor exactly as

  Phi(Y_f,MR)=[[S_Y,T_MR],[T_MR^*,bar(S_Y)]],

where T_MR occupies the particle-to-antiparticle nu_R block and MR is
symmetric.  Then

  K_E Phi(Y_f,MR) K_E^-1 = Phi(Y'_f,MR') =: Phi^E,
  Y'_f = D_E bar(Y_f) D_E^* = S bar(Y_f) S,
  MR'  = D_E bar(MR) D_E^T = omega S bar(MR) S.       (1)

The transpose in the Majorana formula is forced because its second leg lies
in the antiparticle block.  Replacing D_E^T by D_E^* would be a type error and
would lose the phase omega.  Symmetry of MR implies symmetry of MR', so the
transformed matrix is again an input of the original full_D constructor.
Since K_E commutes with J_F, the transformed Phi^E still obeys the original
KO6 reality relation J_F Phi^E=Phi^E J_F.

For generic complex v252 controls, Phi^E is not Phi.  A targeted replay using
the actual full_D, grading and Kswap with seed 2904 Gaussian-integer controls
gave

  ||K_E Phi K_E^-1-Phi^E|| < 4e-15,
  ||K_E Phi K_E^-1-Phi|| = 54.0053.

The nonzero second number is only a concrete generic counterexample; equation
(1) is the exact result.  For real diagonal Yukawas, Y'_f=Y_f.  The actual
finite-typing control has a real tridiagonal MR, however, so its precise
image is omega S MR S, not omega MR.  The shorter omega MR formula applies
only when [S,MR]=0, for example for diagonal MR or MR=0.  Thus the actual
finite-typing control is a family covariance before both parts of the extra
unitary in section 5.

3. F5 COMPLEX STRUCTURE, C0 AND THE COMPLETE GRAPH

Use only the already established whole coefficient data

  Gamma=Z tensor gamma_F,
  Ical=i0 Gamma,
  C0=i0 N tensor I_96,
  B_Phi=Z tensor A-S_normal tensor Phi,
  A=-i0 partial_theta+a(theta).

The actual E0 pullback preserves the intrinsic NS A, with its background
transported if a is not E0 invariant.  Since K_E commutes with Gamma and is
OLD antilinear,

  K_E Ical=-Ical K_E,  K_E C0=-C0 K_E.

Thus K_E is also antilinear for the NEW F5 complex structure, but it has the
wrong sign with C0 for the oriented selfdual chart.  The existing complex
structure supplies the correctly typed involution

  Jcal_E=Ical K_E.

It satisfies

  Jcal_E^2=I,
  [Jcal_E,C0]=0,
  [Jcal_E,C_faces]=0,
  Jcal_E B_Phi Jcal_E^-1=B_(Phi^E).                 (2)

There is no identification here of coefficient-real J_F, the NEW real
structure C0 and the physical CAR/OS word reflection.  They are three
different typed operations.  In particular J_F anticommutes with gamma_F
and is NEW complex linear after extension to Ical, whereas C0 and Jcal_E are
NEW antilinear.

Real functional calculus applied to (2) gives the whole two-face identity

  Jcal_E G(B_Phi) Jcal_E^-1=G(B_(Phi^E)).             (3)

This includes both diagonal and crossing blocks.  The pure quasifree graph
state therefore transports to the graph state for Phi^E.  No second state,
Gaussian fit or selected finite channel is introduced.  Equation (3) becomes
a symmetry of one graph exactly when Phi^E=Phi, or after an independently
allowed equivalence which identifies the two backgrounds.

4. CHARGED T_FACES CHART AND TIME

Let

  E v=(P_F,+ v,i0 P_F,- v),
  h_Phi=A gamma_F-i0 gamma_F Phi.

The exact pullback of Jcal_E through E is

  Jcal_E E=E j_E,  j_E=i0 gamma_F K_E,
  j_E^2=I,
  j_E h_Phi j_E^-1=h_(Phi^E).                         (4)

The same j_E acts on the two charged face entries; the conjugate entries in
T_faces carry the corresponding conjugate action.  Consequently (4)
transports the already existing decomposition

  G(h_Phi) directsum (I-bar(G(h_Phi)))

to the same complete expression with Phi^E.  This is the concrete
charged-plus-dual covariance which remains when Phi is not fixed.

Because j_E is antiunitary, it reverses the unitary circle parameter:

  j_E exp(i0 t h_Phi) j_E^-1=exp(-i0 t h_(Phi^E)).    (5)

Likewise Jcal_E exp(t Ical B_Phi) Jcal_E^-1 is the minus-t group for
B_(Phi^E).  A plus-time equality would silently count the anti-linearity
wrong.  This is source-family time reversal.  It does not identify circle
time with a positive radial OS Hamiltonian.

5. THE omega MAJORANA PHASE AND THE ACTUAL GAUGE BOUNDARY

For any phase t define the actual 96-dimensional unitary

  V_t=diag(t I_48,bar(t) I_48).

It commutes with the represented A_F, gamma_F and J_F.  It leaves every
particle Yukawa block unchanged and sends MR to t^2 MR.  Thus t=omega
removes the common omega in MR', but it does not remove the two S factors.
V_omega alone identifies Phi^E with Phi only for an S-real background,
namely S bar(Y_f) S=Y_f and S bar(MR) S=MR.

To test the actual real finite-typing control, let L_S apply S to generations
on both particle and antiparticle blocks.  It too commutes with A_F, gamma_F
and J_F.  Put

  W_E=V_omega L_S.

Then the exact general identity is

  W_E Phi^E W_E^*=Phi(bar(Y_f),bar(MR)).

In particular every fully real constructor input, including the actual real
tridiagonal MR in check_finite_typing.py, obeys

  W_E Phi^E W_E^*=Phi.                                (6)

Moreover Jcal_E W_E Jcal_E^-1=W_E^*, so

  Jcal'_E=W_E Jcal_E

is again an antiunitary involution.  It preserves the full B_Phi, graph and
charged source for real controls.  If only V_omega is composed, its effective
coefficient matrix is S rather than omega^2 S and only the S-real subclass is
fixed.  With W_E the effective coefficient matrix is the identity.  The
omega Majorana phase is therefore not by itself a physical same-source
obstruction, and the actual off-diagonal MR also requires the explicit L_S.

These extra steps are more than a common change of the three Fuchs solution
columns: V_omega rephases particle and antiparticle sectors oppositely, while
L_S is generation dependent.  They give a unitary equivalence in the
commutant of the displayed finite representation.  They are not generated
by the inner gauge group of the shown A_F.  Indeed an
inner gauge unitary pi(u)J_F pi(u)J_F^-1 acts trivially on particle nu_R:
the left factor is lambda and the opposite factor is bar(lambda).  V_t acts
there by t, so t!=1 cannot be such an inner gauge transformation.

Whether the original full source admits W_E, or either of its factors, as a
physical global action compatible with its native K, clock and cut marking
is a separate original action-menu question.  No SU4, E8 or G31 centre is
inferred merely from these commutant unitaries.  For generic complex inputs
W_E leaves the coefficientwise conjugation in (6), so it does not reduce
Phi^E to Phi.

6. VERDICT AND SCOPE

The smallest decisive test is positive at the family level and negative at
the generic fixed-background level:

* the entire original v252 operator, not only a selected channel, has an
  exact E0 covariance;
* the existing J_F does not contain the E0 flavour matrix, but it commutes
  with the actual E0 antiunitary and retains its KO6 role;
* the full F5 Ical, C0, T_faces and graph G transport without a new state;
* generic Phi goes to the explicitly reflected Phi^E of (1);
* real controls can be made same-Phi by the additional W_E=V_omega L_S
  commutant equivalence; V_omega alone suffices only for S-real controls,
  and the physical source permission for either factor is still open;
* the transported circle evolution has reversed parameter.

This establishes a whole-operator/state/time family transport on the given
F5 source.  It does not prove a physical P1 insertion, choose the classical
Yukawa/Majorana background, derive the extra W_E action from the native
clock, or prove a universal OS symmetry for every Phi.
