1. Exact conditional determinant formula

Let the normal double be an antiperiodic circle of length 2 ell, let the
tangential coefficient be constant, and put

  T=h^2+mu^2 >=0,  E=sqrt(T).

For the scalar second order operator -partial_n^2+T, with all finite internal
multiplicities retained in Tr, zeta functional calculus gives, up to a
T-independent normalization,

  F_AP(T)=log det_zeta,AP(-partial_n^2+T)
         =Tr[2 ell E+2 log(1+exp(-2 ell E))]
         =Tr[2 log(2 cosh(ell E))].                    (1)

For an arbitrary selfadjoint matrix direction Q=dot(T), including
[T,Q]!=0, trace cyclicity and the Frechet trace identity give

  dot(F_AP)=ell Tr[E^-1 tanh(ell E) Q].                 (2)

The split in C59 is therefore algebraically correct:

  dot(F_bulk)=ell Tr(E^-1 Q),

  dot(F_glue)
    =-2 ell Tr[E^-1 exp(-ell E) X_E Q],                (3)

  X_E=(2 cosh(ell E))^-1.

No commuting approximation is used in (2) or (3). Operator level derivatives
still require divided differences; only after the trace may they be written
with f'(T)Q. Equations (2) and the sum of the two split terms have their
continuous limit at E=0. The bulk and gluing pieces separately contain E^-1
and require a gap or a common limiting prescription; their singular parts
cancel in the total AP determinant derivative.

The full Clifford typing remains necessary. The original normal operator is

  D=N(partial_n+B),

and the complex linear charged leg is obtained by the already fixed
multiplication -ND=partial_n+B. Formula (1) is the second order determinant.
It does not by itself fix the phase and multiplicity of the first order
determinant or Pfaffian on the complete selfdual source.

2. The gluing derivative is not the cross operator X

Equation (3) contains X_E as one factor. It is not X_E itself. It is a scalar
pairing of Q with

  K_glue=-2 ell E^-1 exp(-ell E) X_E.                  (4)

The distinction is already decisive in dimension two. Take

  T=diag(a,b),  0<a!=b,
  Q=[[0,1],[1,0]].

For every scalar spectral function f,

  d Tr f(T)[Q]=Tr(f'(T)Q)=0,                            (5)

because f'(T) is diagonal. But for

  x(u)=(2 cosh(ell sqrt(u)))^-1

the actual boundary variation is

  D x_T[Q]=x[1](a,b) Q !=0,                            (6)

since x is strictly decreasing on u>0. Thus one determinant derivative can
vanish while the mixed matrix source response is nonzero. The scalar master
consumer cannot be identified with the whole two face operator response.

There is a second exact boundary. The existing source has

  X_h=(2 cosh(ell h))^-1=(2 cosh(ell |h|))^-1.

At mu=0 this is X_E. For the master regulator mu!=0, (3) instead contains

  X_mu=(2 cosh(ell sqrt(h^2+mu^2)))^-1,

which is a different kernel unless the source covariance is changed by the
same regulator. C59's phrase that the master gluing term and the source cross
block are exactly the same object is therefore valid only after the explicit
conditions mu=0 and common AP source have been imposed, and even then only X
as a factor of the scalar weight (4).

3. The exact positive cross identity is first order

For the antiperiodic first order operator partial_n+h on the circle of length
2 ell, its Green branch from one boundary to the opposite boundary is

  S_AP(ell,0)
    =exp(-ell h)(1+exp(-2 ell h))^-1
    =(2 cosh(ell h))^-1
    =X_h.                                               (7)

This is an operator identity by functional calculus. It remains true for the
actual constant full96 weak coefficient h_p(t), without anticommutation or a
singular pair reduction. Its source derivative is the existing Frechet
response

  dot(X)=D x_(h^2)[dot(h^2)]                            (8)

in the unregulated mu=0 source chart. Equation (7), rather than the scalar
determinant derivative, is the direct Green to mixed face map.

The AP determinant remains compatible with it: its gluing derivative is the
trace pairing of the already supplied direction Q with the weighted cross
kernel (4). If the original source provides enough independent Q directions,
the family of all such pairings can recover that weight by trace duality. The
single actual radial D_Y direction supplies one genuine scalar response, not a
reconstruction of the full matrix kernel.

4. AP is source data, not a consequence of matching

The original homogeneous collar fixes the Poisson range
graph(exp(-ell h)). Together with the positive boundary metric it fixes the
orthogonal Calderon graph

  G=[[A,X_h],[X_h,1-A]].

This does not select a closed normal spin structure. An invertible periodic
Green realization has the same Poisson range and the same orthogonal graph,
while its raw midpoint kernel uses sinh rather than cosh. The statement

  demand that master and source agree, therefore choose AP

is a consistency condition added to the two formulas. It is not a derivation
of AP from either formula.

AP is already genuine data in the named elliptic NS source and period two NS
cover. Within that scoped source, (7) is original and no target kernel has
been inserted by hand. The primitive doubled manifold in the original
theorem at lines 94 to 170 supplies the deck involution and Calderon data, but
does not state an AP clutch on the closed normal circle. The normal coordinate
also does not by itself identify its Euclidean translation with reconstructed
physical time.

The projected master term at paper tfpt-42 section 027 lines 229 to 248 is a
definition on Ran(P_adm). Applying (1) to it additionally requires:

  * an exact homogeneous product collar of normal length 2 ell;
  * the AP normal clutch;
  * reduction of the operator by P_adm;
  * the same charge legs, multiplicities and determinant line;
  * a stated treatment of mu matching the boundary covariance.

Those bindings are not printed in that master definition.

5. The claimed moving domain does not follow from the reused symbol

The first primitive theorem defines D_ref and D_rel as the even and odd parts
of a doubled D under tau. The later selfadjointness theorem starts with a
Dirac anchor D_geo, chooses a modified APS condition from an adapted boundary
operator A_partial, and proves that a bounded symmetric D_rel perturbation is
selfadjoint on that same domain.

The repeated symbol D_rel does not prove D_geo=D_ref as a family identity,
nor that A_partial moves with every constant Phi. C59 also needs an explicit
tau action to show that the full constant Phi coefficient is even. Therefore
the original text does not select the moving APS(h) sea and does not exclude
the already reviewed fixed reference APS source. Fixed and moving domains
remain distinct source contracts until an original family map binds them.

6. Strongest actual conclusion and continuation

For the named AP NS source, the full constant weak h_p(t) has an exact mixed
face operator X by (7), and the existing actual inner fluctuation has the
noncommuting response (8). This is the strongest operator result.

For an AP product realization of the projected master with mu=0, its existing
zeta determinant has the exact scalar mixed response (3) along the same
source direction. It can therefore serve as a real scalar consumer of the
already given weak source without adding a new kernel. It neither chooses the
weak coefficient nor reconstructs dot(X).

The next decisive original check is small: identify the actual closed normal
spin clutch and P_adm reduction used by D_xi in the master determinant. If it
