Source result, paraphrased:
For genus two, an even spin is represented by a partition of six branch
points into two triples. Choosing square roots sA,sB of the corresponding
cubic polynomials with sA*sB=s gives the normalized Szego kernel
 [sA(x)sB(y)+sB(x)sA(y)]/[2(x-y)] * sqrt(dx/s(x))*sqrt(dy/s(y)).
The unwanted opposite-sheet pole cancels. The diagonal residue is one.
The paper also distinguishes the six odd spin structures from the ten even
ones. This is established primary mathematics, not a new kernel formula.
