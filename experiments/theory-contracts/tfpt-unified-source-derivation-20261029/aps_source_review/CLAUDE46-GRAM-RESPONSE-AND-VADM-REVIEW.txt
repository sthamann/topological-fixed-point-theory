CLAUDE46 GRAM RESPONSE AND V_ADM TYPE REVIEW
Independent targeted audit, 30 October 2026

Result

Claude46's Fréchet calculation contains a correct conditional resonance, but
it does not prove that the Hardy coefficient is the original V_adm insertion.
For the declared three-dimensional direction V=-kappa M0P0 on the positive
C6 branch, the real and imaginary differential numbers reproduce exactly.
The quoted 62.33 and 8.25 are Frobenius norms, not operator norms.  More
importantly, the actual Hardy direction has phase -5pi/12.  Its full first
variation is rank3 and only 89.03 percent of its Frobenius amplitude lies on
the fixed resonant line, rather than the quoted 99.3 percent for the purely
real test direction.  The remainder after the g^(-2) fixed-line term is
O(g^(-1)), not O(1).  A purely imaginary kappa has no g^(-2) term but still
diverges like g^(-1).

The original EM5 contract permits an unspecified V_adm satisfying two
conditions.  It does not set V_adm=-kappa M0P0.  The condition
[Pstr,V_adm]=0 has not been checked because Pstr=P_Seam,+ P_sing P_Theta is
not the positive C6 branch selector and none of these projectors is P0.  The
existing spine/fold report explicitly leaves the coefficient-fiber pair
distinct from the physical F5 face pair.  Hence neither the original source
choice nor structural admissibility follows from the Gram response alone.


1. Original contract

Original EM5, 005-source.tex:42-94, states

 D_f=D_0+V_adm,
 [Pstr,V_adm]=0,
 ||V_adm||<epsilon_f/2,
 epsilon_f=2/3-delta_ph.

EM3:91-97 defines

 Pstr=P_Seam,+ P_sing P_Theta,
 P_Seam,+=(1+iC)/2,
 P_Theta=(1+Q_Theta)/2,
 P_sing=(1+Z_c+Z_c^2)/3.

The source gives a class of gap-stable perturbations and proves positivity
for every member of that class.  It supplies no formula identifying the
new Hardy matrix moment with V_adm.  In particular, the positive/negative
three-dimensional C6 blocks of diag(M0,-M0) are not the P_Seam eigenspaces.
The statement that a block-supported matrix commutes with a block selector
therefore does not verify the printed commutator condition.

The marked family endpoint P0 is likewise not Pstr, P_Theta or P_sing.  Even
the forbidden substitution Pstr=P0 would fail:

 ||[P0,M0P0]||=1.                                      (1)

Thus the commutation condition must be checked only after an actual original
map into the Pstr Hilbert space has been exhibited.  P0=P_Theta must not be
inserted by notation.


2. Exact spectrum and Fréchet derivative

Set

 y=2/3,
 delta=0.593277280133224001394066545420926568...,
 g=y-delta=0.07338938653344262...,
 D0=yI-delta M0,
 H0=D0*D0.

The spectrum is

 spec(H0)={g^2,h,h},
 g^2=0.005386002055755119...,
 h=1.191940562322203... .

Let f=(1,-1,1)/sqrt3, so M0f=f and |f_0|^2=1/3.  For the declared
direction V=-z M0P0, the derivative of K=(D*D)^(-1/2) is obtained from the
standard divided differences of x^(-1/2).  On the fixed line it gives

 <f,delta K_Re f>=1/(3g^2)=61.888824007625...,
 <f,delta K_Im f>=0.                                    (2)

This proves Claude46's singular leading term.  It is a leading component,
not the whole derivative.  The full numerical values per unit direction are

 real direction:
   Frobenius norm 62.3267060810,
   operator norm  62.3198583277,
   rank3,
   fixed-line amplitude fraction 0.9929744069;

 imaginary direction:
   Frobenius norm 8.24850950745,
   operator norm  5.83257700740,
   rank2,
   fixed-line coefficient zero.

Therefore 62.33 and 8.25 reproduce only when double bars are interpreted as
Frobenius norms.  The 99.3 percent statement is an amplitude ratio for the
artificially real direction; its squared Frobenius share is 98.60 percent.

Claude46's displayed expansion

 delta K=Re(kappa)/(3g^2) ff*+O(1)

has the wrong remainder.  Put Q=ff* and R=I-Q.  The divided difference of
x^(-1/2) between the two Gram eigenvalues is

 c(g)=[1/g-1/sqrt(h)]/[g^2-h]
     =-1/(3y^2 g)+O(1),
 h=y^2+(y-g)^2+y(y-g).

For V=-kappa M0P0 one has

 Q delta H R
   =-y bar(kappa) QP0(M0*-I)R+O(g),
 ||QP0(M0*-I)R||=sqrt(2/3).

Consequently, at y=2/3,

 ||Q delta K R+R delta K Q||_op
      =|kappa|/[sqrt(6)g]+O(1),
 ||Q delta K R+R delta K Q||_F
      =|kappa|/[sqrt(3)g]+O(1).                         (3)

The fixed-line identity Re(kappa)/(3g^2)Q is exact.  For nonzero
Re(kappa) it remains the leading singularity, with (3) as the subleading
term.  For purely imaginary kappa, (3) is the leading divergence.  Thus the
imaginary response is not uniformly O(1) as the gap closes.  A direct replay
at g=10^(-4) gives g times the cross operator norm 0.40827417 and g times its
Frobenius norm 0.57738688, converging to 1/sqrt6 and 1/sqrt3.

HARDY-BMU-FIRST-NONZERO-ENTRY.txt fixes

 kappa=(4X_*/pi) I,

For the unit direction z=exp(-5pi i/12), real linearity of the Fréchet derivative
gives

 ||delta K||_F=17.9916735432,
 ||delta K||_op=17.8809143219,
 rank(delta K)=3,
 |<f,delta K f>|=16.0180063322.

The fixed-line Frobenius amplitude fraction is 0.8903010770 and its squared

resonant component, but the whole response is neither rank1 nor 99.3 percent
fixed-line.  Its mixed blocks obey the g^(-1) law (3).  At the fixed physical
gap they form the finite nonresonant part that is numerically important for
this phase.


4. Conditional norm threshold

The exact source value gives

 |kappa|=0.3096133626901069/cosh(ell_NS/2).

Since ||M0P0||=1 and EM5 itself defines epsilon_f=g, the norm inequality for
this DECLARED direction would be

 |kappa|<g/2

and hence

 ell_NS>5.644618151351245.                               (4)

Claude46's rounded 5.65 is correct.  The phrase "if epsilon_f is the
resonance gap" is unnecessary: EM5 defines it that way.  Equation(4) remains
conditional for a different reason.  It applies only if an original source
map first identifies -kappa M0P0 as V_adm on the actual Pstr space and the
commutator condition is verified.  No current source fixes an ell_NS value
and proves those two statements.


5. Infinitesimal and finite responses are different

The values in sections2-3 are derivatives at kappa=0.  The exact finite response
is

 [(D0-kappa M0P0)^dagger(D0-kappa M0P0)]^(-1/2)-H0^(-1/2),

and is generally not kappa times the derivative.  As a dimensionless control
inside the EM5 norm bound, take |kappa|/g=0.49 with the actual Hardy phase.
Then

 finite response Frobenius norm =0.5456360675,
 linear response Frobenius norm =0.6469949632,
 relative linearization error   =0.2264717958.

The derivative is a correct local diagnostic.  Near the admissibility
boundary it is not an accurate finite replacement.  A claimed physical
finite K therefore has to use the exact inverse square root after ell_NS and
the actual source insertion are known.


6. Relation to the completed spine/fold result

SPINE-ORIGINAL-PQ-FOLD-KERNEL-ADDENDUM.txt proves the actual coefficient

Final scope

The smallest supported conclusion is positive and conditional: if the actual
source inserts -kappa M0P0 as a Pstr-preserving V_adm, the original Gram

and the conditional norm threshold (4).  Existing sources prove neither that
insertion nor its structural commutator.  The Hardy coefficient remains a
nonzero directed contribution already read by the primitive wedge consumer;
it is not thereby the entire physical V_adm or Yukawa source.
