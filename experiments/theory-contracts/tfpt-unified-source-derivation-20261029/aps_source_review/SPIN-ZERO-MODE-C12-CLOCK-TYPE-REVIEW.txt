Use the existing notation

 Jg^2=-I, Jg^4=I, sigmag^3=I,

curve map c_geo:(w,eta)->(omega w,eta). On the curve the base maps are

In particular Jg exchanges Aq and Ap. Its +/-i eigenspaces each have
dimension three and are coherent q/p combinations, not Aq and Ap.

3. WHAT THE PROPOSED MATCHED 96-SPACE WOULD REQUIRE

Consider the specifically proposed carrier

 Hmatch=(S+ tensor Aq) direct_sum (S- tensor Ap).

Acting by r on the family factor while leaving S+ fixed sends the first
summand to S+ tensor Ap, which is not in Hmatch. A linear endomorphism
implementing the q/p exchange must ALSO exchange S+ and S-.
The same applies to Jg. This is a necessary representation condition,
not an argument from 16=16.

There is no Spin(10)-equivariant complex-linear isomorphism S+->S-:
the half-spin representations are inequivalent. More directly, any
linear T exchanging the matched chiral summands obeys

 T gamma_F T^-1=-gamma_F,
 T kappa_native T^-1=kappa_native^-1.

Every element of the given compact K commutes with its central kappa.
Such a T therefore cannot be an element of that fixed K. A D5 outer
normalizer or Pin-type flip may implement the outer action instead;
it would change the marked chiral/charge action and must be supplied
with the corresponding family and full E8 action. The previous explicit
root-lift results are not discarded by this test.

The known signed whole96 wedge map proves the actual SM representation,
gamma_F and J_F dictionary. It does not prove that Ap is the pointwise
dual or conjugate of Aq over C. In fact the q and p degree-zero line
packets lie in disjoint Jac(C)[2] planes. Tau0 preserves each plane;
the holomorphic side exchange s carries one to the other. Their actual
open source transport is already available, but it is not an identity
of flat family bundles over an unchanged base point.

The global determinant factors must also be retained: the existing
conjugate charged branch is Lambda_odd E tensor D^(-1-m_j), not a
second untwisted 16-space. A local signed wedge matrix does not cancel
these global line powers or provide the missing base/connection map.

4. THE REAL-STRUCTURE TEST

The existing normalized geometric spin Real structure j0 over tau0 can
be chosen with j0^2=I and

 j0 r j0^-1=r^-1.

It preserves the q/p packets and sends their index a to -a-1. Consequently

 j0 Jg j0^-1=Jg^-1, j0 sigmag j0^-1=sigmag^-1.

These are genuine coefficient/spin dihedral relations. The actual
compact J_F on BOTH conjugate charged branches instead commutes with
the represented compact gauge elements kappa_native and sigma0.
For kappa this follows explicitly from anticommutation with gamma_F
and antilinearity:

 J_F(-i gamma_F)J_F^-1=(+i)(-gamma_F)=-i gamma_F.

Thus the geometric j0 cannot simply be renamed J_F while both clocks
are renamed their native counterparts. Ordinary kappa_split does
invert native kappa because it conjugates -i and preserves gamma_F;
this is the other, split Real structure in the actual96 dictionary.
The distinction is exactly the previously established compact-versus-
coefficient type distinction, now tested on these concrete section lines.

An actual additional D5 flip and a base/source anti-map could change the
comparison. Their relations must be checked together; multiplying two
unrelated antilinear operators or ignoring the base is not that check.
No new OS/CAR lift or physical reflection is asserted here.
