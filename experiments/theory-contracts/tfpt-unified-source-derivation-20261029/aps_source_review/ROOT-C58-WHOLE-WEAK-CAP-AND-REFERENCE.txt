2. An explicit whole-circle bound, independently checked
Write g=|p| and v=||V||. For g>2v the coupled negative projection Ph
has the same rank r as P0 by the minmax gap bound. For any normalized
negative eigenvector u with eigenvalue lambda<=-g+v, projection on
the positive h0 space yields
 (g-lambda) Pplus u = -Pplus V u,
 ||Pplus u|| <= v/(2g-v).
Summing over an orthonormal negative eigenbasis gives
 d_p=Tr(Ph Pplus)=Tr(P0(I-Ph)) <= r v^2/(2g-v)^2.
The squared old/coupled ground overlap is
 w_p=det_RanP0(P0 Ph P0).
All eigenvalues of this compression lie in[0,1]. The elementary product
inequality 1-product a_j<=sum(1-a_j) proves
 0<=1-w_p<=d_p.
The spectral resolution of H_exc,p now gives Z_p(ell)>=w_p. Hence
 0<=1-Z_p(ell)<=r v^2/(2g-v)^2,
uniformly in ell>=0 on the high modes.

The right side is summable over the original quarter circle. Outside
finitely many p it is <1/2, so -log Z_p<=2(1-Z_p). Therefore
 Z_cap(ell)=product_p Z_p(ell)>0
exists for every finite ell>0; every finite low-mode factor is positive.
This closes the previously finite-only weak cap on the given source.
It does not assert the older scalar C_p matrix formula for noncommuting Q.

At zero modes the negative spectral projection does not choose a unique
ground vector. Nevertheless E_p is unchanged by adding any subprojection
of ker h_p, because h_p vanishes there. Thus H_exc,p and the cap above
with fixed P0 are independent of that zero-mode occupation. No new
zero-mode state choice is required to evaluate this cap. A particular
vacuum ray or Thouless chart can require such data; distinguish them.
A large-ell cap limit can vanish if an old low mode is orthogonal to the
whole ground space. Strict positivity here is for finite ell.

Constant weak fields preserve Fourier p. What fails is reduction into
the earlier anticommuting scalar singular pairs, not Fourier factorization.
