VERDICT

The family Hodge statement is exact, but it is not by itself a proof that
the restricted C/X input spans the full compact real (10,6). The compact
adjoint complements the family pair AND reverses the D5 vector weight.
Consequently the directly checked C/X triplet bracket has real rank 30,
whereas the full compact (10,6) has real dimension 60.

The missing factor two is supplied algebraically if the input really is a
complete 16 tensor F. The ten Spin(10) components omitted by the C/X start
then produce the opposite five D5 vector weights. Those ten components are
indeed generated at depth two by the previously checked E8 Lie closure of
the original 24 C/X currents and their adjoints. They are not members of
the initial C/X input, however. Thus the Lie closure proves availability
inside the full current algebra, not that the geometric q/p product or a
given source insertion already excites the complete spinor input.

Accordingly, every claim of a full 60 dimensional real (10,6) from the
restricted C/X tensor Lambda^2 F bracket must be read as conditional on
this full carrier completion. No additional O family fermion is required
once a complete 16 tensor F input is actually supplied. The verified C/X
subset alone is insufficient.


1. EXACT TARGET DIMENSIONS

For the complexified subgroup D5 times A3,

  (10,6)=10 tensor Lambda^2(4)

has complex dimension 60. Its compact real form also has real dimension
60. Choose family label 0 for O and labels 1,2,3 for F. Then

  dim_C Lambda^2 F=3,
  dim_C O wedge F=3.                                      (1)

The antilinear family Hodge operator exchanges these two summands. On the
D5 vector it simultaneously exchanges the five negative and five positive
weights. Therefore the real completion of a complex subspace W in

  W5_negative tensor Lambda^2 F

has real dimension twice dim_C W. It does not retain one D5 weight while
filling an independent six dimensional family fiber.


2. THE ACTUAL C/X RESTRICTION HAS RANK 15 COMPLEX, 30 REAL

The starting current dictionary is

  C_a in 1 tensor 4,
  X_i,a in bar5 tensor 4,
  [C_a,X_i,b]=Y_i,(a wedge b).                              (2)

Restrict a,b to the triplet labels 1,2,3. There are five carrier labels
i and three unordered family pairs 12,23,31. The exact Chevalley root
enumeration gives

  5 times 3 = 15 complex root directions.                  (3)

All 15 have the negative D5 vector sign. Their compact adjoints have the
positive D5 sign and the complementary family pairs 03,01,02. The root
spaces and their opposites are independent, so selfadjoint combinations
have

  rank_R(C/X triplet bracket plus dagger)=2 times 15=30.    (4)

This is the exact span generated by

  h=sum_i,ab z_i,ab Y_i,ab,  ab in {12,23,31},
  H=h+h^dagger.

It is a valid compact real 30 dimensional subspace of (10,6), not the
whole 60 dimensional sector. Suppressing the carrier index made the
earlier family-only description look like a full real six for every
fixed carrier. That suppression is invalid because dagger changes the
carrier weight at the same time.


3. A COMPLETE 16 TENSOR F DOES GIVE THE FULL 60

Use the actual odd-minus Spin(10) half-spinor weights in doubled signs.
Their census is

  one weight with five minus signs,
  five weights with one minus sign,
  ten weights with three minus signs.                       (5)

These are the U5 components 1, bar5 and 10, up to the retained naming
convention. C and X use only the first two rows of (5), namely six of the
sixteen spinor weights. The ten three-minus weights are absent from the
starting C/X input.

For two COMPLETE 16 tensor F inputs, the exact root bracket over the
three family pairs has

  15 target roots with negative D5 vector sign,
  15 target roots with positive D5 vector sign.             (6)

Thus its holomorphic target has complex rank 30. Its compact adjoint has
the complementary family pairs and gives

  rank_R(full 16 tensor Lambda^2 F plus dagger)
       =2 times 30=60.                                     (7)

Equations (6) and (7) are a direct root enumeration, not a dimension
match. The explicit Spin(10) gamma calculation independently confirms
that Sym^2(16) maps onto the vector 10.


4. WHICH SPINOR BILINEARS SUPPLY THE OTHER CARRIER HALF

The same sign enumeration distinguishes the U5 channels. Vector roots
arise as follows:

  1 times bar5:   five witnesses, all negative vector weights;
  10 times bar5:  twenty witnesses, all positive vector weights;
  10 times 10:    thirty witnesses, all negative vector weights.  (8)

Hence the carrier half missing from the direct C/X bracket is supplied
by the cross channel 10 times bar5. The 10 times 10 bilinears are part of
the complete Spin(10) realization but land in the same negative vector
half as 1 times bar5. They do not alone repair the missing positive half.

This is consistent with symmetry: the vector lies in Sym^2(16), while
the family target lies in Lambda^2(4). It also shows why adding only a
family Hodge complement cannot repair the carrier deficit.


5. WHAT THE ORIGINAL E8 SOURCE CLOSURE PROVES

The original source starts with the 24 positive currents C4 plus X20 and
their 24 compact adjoints. Its exact iterative Chevalley closure has root
counts

  48 -> 140 -> 240.                                        (9)

The forty positive half-spinor roots in

  10 tensor 4

that are missing from C/X all appear at closure depth two. Their forty
adjoints appear with them in the full closure. Consequently the already
verified full E8 current algebra contains the complete 16 tensor 4 and
can form the full (10,6) through its actual brackets.

This is an algebraic current-generation statement. It does not show that
the two geometric q/p triplet fields are already complete 16 tensor F
inputs, nor that the source evaluation inserts the depth-two generated
currents with the required carrier coefficients. Invoking the closure is
therefore a genuine additional source step, although it introduces no new
representation or freely chosen field.
