CLAUDE42 GENERAL FAMILY MATRIX: KO6, FOUR SECTORS AND WEAK SU2
Independent exact review, 30 October 2026

Scope and corrected source premise

This report tests only the first algebraic identification in the Claude42
proposal.  It uses the actual signed H_F=C^96 exterior dictionary, gamma_F,
J_F and the already verified original primitive wedge consumer.  The latter
is recorded in ORIGINAL-PRIMITIVE-WEDGE-CONSUMER-HARDY-REVIEW.txt: the
original carrier map vol(s_Q wedge c_H s_uc) really applies the weak exterior
creator and reads the directed Hardy contribution.  Therefore there is no
remaining missing-X gate in this report.

The open source question is narrower: whether one or more Hardy moments are
the actual matrix element, variation or summand of the original coefficient
Hol(nabla_F*) K_f^str.  No equality to the whole original Yukawa tensor is
assumed below.

1. General complex family matrix

Let M be any complex 3 by 3 matrix, lifted identically to every actual
particle and antiparticle carrier slot, and put

  B=M P_gamma,+.

For either marked weak exterior creator X=X_wa the already checked Clifford
identity gives

  A=[X,B]=M X gamma_F.                                   (1)

The actual finite real structure is J_F=K_swap followed by coefficient
conjugation.  Since K_swap X K_swap=X^dagger and
K_swap gamma_F K_swap=-gamma_F,

  A^dagger             =-M^dagger X^dagger gamma_F,
  J_F A J_F^-1         =-bar(M) X^dagger gamma_F,
  J_F A^dagger J_F^-1  = M^T X gamma_F.                 (2)

The v254 KO6 prescription has epsilon'=+1 and no factor one half.  Hence

  D_KO=A+A^dagger+J_F(A+A^dagger)J_F^-1
      =S X gamma_F-S^dagger X^dagger gamma_F,
  S=M+M^T.                                               (3)

Thus the completed coefficient is the complex symmetric part S.  The
antisymmetric part of M cancels.  There is no universal factor two: a
symmetric input doubles, while a single directed E_ij produces the two
directions E_ij+E_ji with their original coefficient.  Adding a half would
be a different normalization from the stated v254 formula.

The exact 96 dimensional replay verifies for a generic nonsymmetric complex
M that D_KO is selfadjoint, gamma_F-odd and J_F-real.  This directly rules
out the proposed replacement of the KO completion by adjoint completion.

2. The two-entry Claude42 matrix

Use the stated candidate

  M=a E_02+b E_21,
  a=0.1258739334853158906973
       -0.4697679151157443151336 i,
  b approximately -0.421375+1.572594 i.                 (4)

The displayed decimals give

  b/a approximately -3.347597561089+0.000000580585 i.

Thus the quoted moments have a common phase up to the uncalibrated real
sign; the tiny imaginary remainder is set by the six printed decimals of b.
Changing that sign does not change any result below.  Equation (3) gives

  S = a(E_02+E_20)+b(E_21+E_12)
    = [[0,0,a],[0,0,b],[a,b,0]].                         (5)

Put r=sqrt(|a|^2+|b|^2).  Then exactly

  singular_values(S)=(r,r,0),
  r approximately 1.699157120653                         (6)

for the displayed normalization.  On the full H_F the completed D_KO has
singular value r with multiplicity 64 and zero with multiplicity 32.  Its
rank is therefore 64.

For comparison, the directed matrix M itself has singular values
(|b|,|a|,0).  Moreover

  M M^dagger=diag(|a|^2,0,|b|^2),
  M^dagger M=diag(0,|b|^2,|a|^2).                       (7)

So even the naive pair Up proportional to M, Down proportional to M^dagger
has only coordinate eigenvectors, at most a permutation, and zero CP
Jarlskog invariant.  After the required KO completion it is not the actual
pair at all: both directions belong to the single real operator (3), and
the two nonzero singular values are degenerate.  A relative matrix V^dagger
U extracted inside that two dimensional degenerate space is basis dependent.
It is not a CKM prediction.

Because (5) is a common phase times a real symmetric weighted path, that
phase can be removed by the allowed common left/right rephasing.  There is
no intrinsic CP phase in this two-edge coefficient.  A physical CP invariant
would require two independently sourced, noncommuting sector matrices with
their actual endpoint and carrier maps.  Neither adjunction nor KO reality
supplies the second matrix.

3. Exact four-sector Yukawa form

Write S for (5).  In the ordered weak bases (nu,e) and (u,d), the LR matrix
blocks, meaning rows L and columns R, are:

For X_w1:

  particle lepton:     [[0,-S],[-S^dagger,0]],
  particle quark:      [[0, S],[ S^dagger,0]],
  antiparticle lepton: [[0,-S^dagger],[-S,0]],
  antiparticle quark:  [[0, S^dagger],[ S,0]].

For X_w2:

  particle lepton:     [[S^dagger,0],[0,-S]],
  particle quark:      [[-S^dagger,0],[0,S]],
  antiparticle lepton: [[S,0],[0,-S^dagger]],
  antiparticle quark:  [[-S,0],[0,S^dagger]].

Every displayed entry is itself a 3 by 3 family block; the RL blocks are
their adjoints.  The pattern is colour diagonal, contains no Majorana block
and retains the fixed quark/lepton signs and particle/antiparticle
conjugations of the actual P2 dictionary.  It is one constrained real
Higgs-doublet coefficient.  It is not a common unrestricted matrix Y for
all four species, nor four independently fitted Yukawa matrices.

4. Weak SU2 covariance

The actual weak raising operator in the same dictionary satisfies exactly

  [T_+,X_w2]=X_w1,   [T_+,X_w1]=0.                       (8)

A family matrix is a spectator of this action.  Therefore

  [T_+,M_2 X_w2 gamma_F]-M_1 X_w1 gamma_F
     =(M_2-M_1)X_w1 gamma_F.                             (9)

Attaching unrelated matrices to the two bare components X_w1 and X_w2 of
this one fixed doublet violates (8), unless they agree.  This does not ban
the usual sector-specific Yukawa intertwiners.  Distinct up and down matrices
can be SU2-covariant when the original source supplies the corresponding
right-handed carrier projectors, H versus its conjugate, or separate HolK
readers.  Those extra typed maps must be shown.  Merely assigning one matrix
to X_w1 and another to X_w2 does not provide them.

Likewise a genuine E8 (10,6) insertion may carry a nontrivial family-six leg,
but naming that representation does not select two desired matrices or prove
their source coefficients.  This review makes no global E8 exclusion.

5. Reproducibility and verdict

check_claude42_general_family_ko.py imports the actual v252 grading, Kswap,
signed whole-96 exterior dictionary and weak creators.  It checks (1)-(3)
for a generic complex nonsymmetric M, (5)-(7), all displayed four-sector
blocks and the exact SU2 raising identity (8)-(9).  Its JSON output retains
the rounded b phase residual explicitly rather than upgrading it to an exact
source value.

The Claude42 adjoint-CKM inference fails at the first required typing step.
KO6 replaces M by S=M+M^T, yielding two equal nonzero singular values and no
unique relative mixing matrix.  The quoted two moments share only a removable
common phase and generate no CP invariant.  Different matrices on the two
bare components of one weak doublet violate the actual SU2 action.  A positive
continuation remains possible through genuinely sector-specific original
HolK readers or a fully typed (10,6) source leg; those are source questions,
not consequences of adjunction.
