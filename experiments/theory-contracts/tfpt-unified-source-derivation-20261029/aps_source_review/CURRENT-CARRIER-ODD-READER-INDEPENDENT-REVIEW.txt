INDEPENDENT REVIEW: CURRENT CARRIER ODD READER ON THE WHOLE SOURCE
30 October 2026. Read-only review of the original and the new source chain.

VERDICT
The operator identity in CURRENT-CARRIER-ODD-READER-WHOLE-SOURCE.txt is
correct with its stated scope. The v258 odd projection is the finite
chirality projection for gamma_F. After the already checked reducing map E,
it acts on

  h_s=A gamma_F-i gamma_F Phi_s,

not on the total boundary grading Gamma_tot. For the actual second face

  C_s=(I+exp(2 ell h_s))^-1

functional calculus gives, on Dom(h_s)=H1,

  log((I-C_s)C_s^-1)=2 ell h_s,
  Pi_odd^gamma log((I-C_s)C_s^-1)=-2 ell i gamma Phi_s.   (1)

Thus the reader extracts the complete bounded finite coefficient without a
Fourier projection. It does not derive Phi_s or identify the still unnamed
paper symbol P_F with E, p_E or the face map.

1. The grading and carrier types agree
v258_dirac_covariance_induction.py:24-27 defines Pi_odd using gamma_F. Its
implemented diagnostic uses the same left/right chirality convention. The
coupled source imports grading() from v252_full_finite_triple.py, so there is
no second hidden parity. On the doubled normal fiber the total grading is

  Gamma_tot=Z tensor gamma_F,

and B_s is even for Gamma_tot. The constant isometry

  E v=(P_+v,i P_-v)

has EE*=(I+Gamma_tot)/2 and reduces B_s. Only after this reduction does the
finite gamma_F odd projection in (1) remove A gamma_F and retain the Phi_s
term. Applying Pi_odd to B_s with Gamma_tot would be the wrong operation.

The second-face map is separately nonreducing for the whole two-face graph,
because its crossing block is nonzero. The reviewed report keeps this
distinction and does not move a logarithm through an arbitrary compression.

2. Exact comparison unitary, charge and gauge type
For

  U_gamma=exp(-i pi gamma_F/4)=(I-i gamma_F)/sqrt(2)

and {gamma_F,Phi_s}=0,

  U_gamma Phi_s U_gamma*=-i gamma_F Phi_s.              (2)

Combining (1) and (2) gives exactly

  mu_geo Pi_odd log((I-C_s)C_s^-1)
       =2 ell mu_geo U_gamma Phi_s U_gamma*.             (3)

The charge statement also has the right antilinear sign. Since J_F is
antilinear and J_F gamma_F J_F^-1=-gamma_F, its conjugation changes both i
and gamma_F, hence J_F U_gamma J_F^-1=U_gamma. The actual represented finite
algebra is even and commutes with gamma_F, so it also commutes with U_gamma.
This makes (2) a fixed charge-compatible comparison. It does not make
U_gamma a physical gauge event.

3. Scale audit
Equation (3), with 2 ell mu_geo left explicit, is the strongest justified
normalization. In v258 the general modular dictionary is

  C=(I+exp(D/mu_geo))^-1,
  D=mu_geo log((I-C)C^-1).

For the present face exponent 2 ell h_s, the choice mu_geo=(2 ell)^-1 would
normalize (3) to U_gamma Phi_s U_gamma*. That equality of scales has not
been derived here and the reviewed report correctly does not impose it.

There is a small implementation limit worth recording. v258's executable
scale test sets mu_geo=1. Its line125 passes Dmass/mu_geo into kms_cov and
also passes mu_geo as the function scale, so that line would divide the
exponent twice away from the unit convention. It therefore checks only the
mu_geo=1 convention and is not evidence for a general scale relation. The
analytic formula above is unaffected.

4. Domain and smallest independent actual-96 check
C_s and I-C_s are injective but have no bounded inverse on the whole circle.
The logarithm in (1) is the spectral logarithm on Dom(h_s)=H1. Since gamma_F
preserves H1 and Phi_s is bounded, the odd difference initially defined on
H1 extends to the bounded multiplication operator in the right side of (1).
This is the precise sense in which its finite96 coefficient is read on the
whole circle.

An independent actual-v252 diagnostic used a generic complex full_D,
symmetric Majorana block, ell=.73 and scalar A=.41. The residuals were

  ||Phi-Phi*||                         0,
  ||{gamma,Phi}||                     0,
  ||logit(C)-2 ell h||                2.44e-12,
  ||Pi_odd(logit C)+2 ell i gammaPhi||1.86e-12,
  ||U Phi U*+i gammaPhi||             5.39e-15,
  ||J_F U J_F^-1-U||                  0,
  ||[pi(a),gamma]|| and ||[pi(a),U]|| 0.

These numerical residuals only replay the exact algebra above; they add no
source choice. The resulting statement is a positive whole-source reader
for an already supplied Phi_s, not a derivation of its Yukawa values, a
literal finite-rank circle compression, or a proof of the physical P_F
binding in the current paper.
