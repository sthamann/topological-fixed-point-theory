CORRECTED VERDICT
-----------------

The geometric base map is ALREADY PROVED.  For

  C: eta^2=1-w^6,
  X_f=P1 minus mu4,

the existing sign-line dictionary gives

  pi:C -> X_f,
  z=eta/(1-w^3),
  z^2=(1+w^3)/(1-w^3),
  pi^* E_v117 = L_0^q direct-sum L_1^q direct-sum L_2^q. (1)

Thus no new base identification is needed.  What must remain separate is the
choice of a coordinate basis on C from a coordinate basis on X_f.  The map
pi and the local-system pullback do not identify arbitrary function frames
or turn a global section operator into a pointwise End(F) matrix.

There are now two distinct exact statements.

1. FINITE MARKED FIBER.  The matrices A4 and KQ are coefficient actions.
   The September marked intertwiner S transports any ALREADY GIVEN positive
   three-dimensional coefficient operator uniquely at the selected fiber.

2. GLOBAL SIX-SECTION SOURCE.  The bare antisymmetrizer on the six sections
   mu_ab does NOT descend through the full independent Kummer deck group
   V4_q x V4_p.  Therefore the finite formula S K3 S^(-1) does not prove that
   the naked six-section source contains a global rank-three positive
   subsource.  A coupled endpoint kernel such as the separately studied
   B_mu construction may compensate the deck characters, but that repair is
   not proved here.


1. THE COEFFICIENT CHART AND ITS METRIC TRANSPORT
-------------------------------------------------

The literal KQ matrix comes from the checked algebraic Fuchs frame,

  G(Qy)=U G(y) KQ,
  KQ=Gbase^(-1) U^(-1) Gbase
     =[[0,1,0],[-1,0,0],[0,0,1]].                       (2)

KQ is therefore a transported vertical coefficient action, not a new map of
the bases.  Its displayed matrix uses Gbase, not the September marked
intertwiner S alone.  The latter is a second finite coefficient chart:

  S^dagger S=N,
  S P S^(-1)=M,
  S(-Gint)S^(-1)=U.                                     (3)

Its fixed-generator intertwiner space is one-dimensional, so the remaining
overall phase cancels in conjugation.  For an ALREADY GIVEN three-dimensional
operator put

  a=y^2+delta^2+epsilon^2,
  P^sharp_N=N^(-1) P^dagger N=P^(-1),
  H3_N=a I-y delta(omega P+bar(omega) P^sharp_N),
  K3_N=H3_N^(-1/2).                                     (4)

The exact marked-fiber transport is

  H3_v117=S H3_N S^(-1)
          =a I-y delta(omega M+bar(omega) M^dagger),

  K3_v117=S K3_N S^(-1)=H3_v117^(-1/2).                (5)

This is metric rather than a bare similarity: S^dagger S=N gives

  <Sx,Sy>_standard=<x,y>_N.

Consequently N-selfadjointness and positivity become ordinary Hermitian
selfadjointness and positivity in the marked v117 chart.  Conversely,

  K3_N=S^(-1) K3_v117 S.                                (6)

Equations (5)-(6) establish only the transport of a supplied rank-three
coefficient operator.  They do not construct that operator from the six
global mixed-spin sections.


2. THE DECISIVE FULL-DECK COUNTERTEST
------------------------------------

The actual products are global sections

  mu_ab in V_ab=H0(C,K_C tensor L_a^q tensor L_b^p),
  a != b.                                                (7)

The q and p Kummer covers have independent deck groups.  If chi_a and psi_b
denote their three nontrivial V4 characters, then

  mu_ab has character (chi_a,psi_b) of V4_q x V4_p.      (8)

The six ordered pairs (a,b), a!=b, give six distinct characters.  In
particular take

  epsilon_q=(+,-,-),   epsilon_p=(+,+,+).

Then

  mu_01 -> +mu_01,
  mu_10 -> -mu_10,

and hence

  mu_01-mu_10 -> mu_01+mu_10.                            (9)

The antisymmetric three-space is therefore not invariant under the full
deck group.  This is a type obstruction, not a normalization issue.

The complete argument is equally short.  Since the six deck characters are
distinct and their spaces V_ab are one-dimensional, every endomorphism
commuting with V4_q x V4_p is diagonal in the mu_ab basis.  The actual R
action is transitive on all six labels.  A diagonal endomorphism commuting
also with R must have all six diagonal entries equal.  Thus

  Comm(V4_q x V4_p, R)=C I_6.                            (10)

There is no common rank-three projector.  Equivalently, the full finite
deck-plus-R representation on the six global-section lines is irreducible
over C.  Restricting to the diagonal A4 subgroup makes mu_ab and mu_ba carry
the same character and permits the algebraic antisymmetrizer, but that
restriction is an additional quotient of the actual independent deck data.
It cannot be made silently.

The raw identities

  s^*mu_ab=-mu_ba,
  R^*mu_ab=rho^(-1)mu_(b+1,a+1)

remain correct as pullbacks between the one-dimensional GLOBAL section
spaces.  They do not make mu_ab(y)-mu_ba(y) a pointwise End(F) element in a
single trivially identified fiber.  Applying s to one endpoint is a
correspondence between q and p line data; it is not a constant local matrix
unless the corresponding endpoint transport and deck compensation are kept.


3. PRECISE SURVIVING KERNEL STATEMENT
-------------------------------------

The finite algebraic map

  A(mu_ab-mu_ba)/sqrt(2)=e_a wedge e_b

is still a normalized intertwiner after the diagonal A4 coefficient data
have been identified.  The bare six-cycle spectral calculation and the
matrix formulas (4)-(6) also remain correct within that finite coefficient
category.  What fails is their promotion to a global three-dimensional
source on the full sixteen-sheet Kummer cover.

Likewise the quarter action is only covariant.  In the algebraic horizontal
column chart it sends

  K3_col -> KQ K3_col KQ^dagger,                         (11)

with the c-orientation transported at the same time.  Neither the marked S
nor the Hodge map adds KQ invariance.

The actual Fuchs row metric is

  H(z)=G(z)^(-dagger) G(z)^(-1),

and G(z)^dagger H(z)G(z)=I.  This horizontal coefficient frame is compatible
with the already existing pi in (1).  It does not remove the independent p
deck characters in (7)-(10).  The remaining original question is therefore
not the existence of pi.  It is whether the actual coupled B_mu endpoint
kernel, diagonal skew operation and Hodge contraction furnish a deck-neutral
global operator before the finite K3 transport is applied.
