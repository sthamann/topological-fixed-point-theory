is a real complex-linear unitary involution. It reverses gamma_F and sends
the complete full_D coefficient, including its symmetric Majorana block, to
its entrywise conjugate. If the old-linear spatial pullback R_x satisfies

  R_x A_q R_x^-1=-A_p,

then T=K_F R_x obeys

  T h(A_q,Phi_q) T^-1=h(A_p,-bar(Phi_q) composed with inv).       (1)

The F5 lift

  Ucal=(i N) tensor T                                             (2)

intertwines the actual charged chart, preserves Gamma, Ical and C0, carries
the complete boundary operator and therefore carries its whole two-face

parameter t. This is not the old E0/C_raw transport in another frame: its
finite factor, coefficient target and time rule are different.

The result is a source-family covariance. It neither proves that the p tuple
is the same source as the q tuple nor derives either tuple as the operational

modulo one and the holonomy to its inverse. Thus 1/4 is carried to 3/4; there
is no fixed-quarter conclusion.

1. ACTUAL FINITE COEFFICIENT

In verification/v252_full_finite_triple.py the finite Real structure is

  J_F=Kswap kappa_split,

where kappa_split is ordinary coefficient conjugation in the real signed
wedge chart and Kswap exchanges the particle and antiparticle 48-blocks.
Consequently composition with the same kappa_split gives the linear matrix

  K_F=J_F kappa_split=Kswap,  K_F^2=I.                         (3)

The actual grading has opposite signs on the two 48-blocks, hence

  K_F gamma_F K_F^-1=-gamma_F.                                (4)

Write the complete allowed coefficient as

  Phi=[[S,T_M],[T_M^*,bar(S)]],

where T_M is the symmetric nu_R Majorana block. Direct block exchange gives

  K_F Phi K_F^-1=[[bar(S),T_M^*],[T_M,S]]=bar(Phi).             (5)

The last equality uses the original physical condition T_M^T=T_M. It is not
an arbitrary-matrix identity after dropping Majorana symmetry. Equations
(3)-(5) were also replayed with generic complex Yukawas and a generic complex
symmetric Majorana input through the actual full_D constructor; both matrix
residuals vanished exactly in floating arithmetic.

K_F is not a symmetry of the displayed finite algebra representation in
general. The actual rep() uses the weak/quaternion action on the particle
block and the colour action on the antiparticle block. Kswap exchanges those
two bimodule actions. The precise family statement therefore also transports

  pi_q(a) -> pi_p(a)=K_F pi_q(a) K_F^-1.                    (5a)

Calling the map a symmetry of one marked SM source would additionally require
an original algebra automorphism identifying these two actions. No such
identification is used below; the proved statement concerns the operator,
selfdual chart, state graph and time of the transported source family.

2. CHARGED OPERATOR AND THE FIRST DECISIVE IDENTITY

Use the already established charged generator

  h(A,Phi)=A gamma_F-i gamma_F Phi.                            (6)

The spatial pullback R_x acts on the base/domain and commutes with the finite
gamma_F and K_F. Let Phi_q composed with inv denote its coefficient pullback.
Combining the two sign reversals in A and gamma_F with (5) yields

  T(A_q gamma_F)T^-1=A_p gamma_F,
  T(-i gamma_F Phi_q)T^-1=+i gamma_F bar(Phi_q composed inv).

This is exactly (1), with target data

  Phi_p=-bar(Phi_q) composed with inv.                         (7)

The minus sign in (7) is part of the transported source. Omitting it would
break (1); inserting it as an independently chosen p coupling would reverse
the logical direction of the result.

3. F5 LIFT, CHARGED CHART AND WHOLE GRAPH

Retain the actual matrices

  E v=(P_plus v, i P_minus v).                                (9)

Because T reverses gamma_F, while iN exchanges the two normal components,
direct multiplication gives

  Ucal E=E T.                                                  (10)

The normal factor iN reverses both Z and S. Together with the A sign and

  Ucal B(A_q,Phi_q) Ucal^-1=B(A_p,Phi_p).                     (11)

The two sign reversals also imply

  [Ucal,Gamma]=[Ucal,Ical]=[Ucal,C0]=0.                       (12)

Thus Ucal is NEW-complex-linear and respects the existing selfdual real
form. On both faces use Ucal, and on the charged T_faces chart use T on the
two field entries and bar(T) on the two conjugate entries. Equations
(10)-(12) include the adjoint slots and preserve their fixed relative minus.
Functional calculus then gives the complete result, including crossing
blocks,

  (Ucal direct-sum Ucal) G(B_q) (Ucal direct-sum Ucal)^-1=G(B_p). (13)

Therefore the pure graph covariance and every CAR word defined from it are
transported to the p-family member. No new Gaussian covariance is selected.

4. DOMAIN, QUARTER AND TIME

For old-linear reflection/inversion R_x f(x)=f(-x),

  R_x(-i partial_x+alpha)R_x^-1
    =-(-i partial_x-alpha).                                  (14)

The domain holonomy is simultaneously inverted. Hence the transported
periodic chart is

  alpha_p=-alpha_q mod 1,  U_p=U_q^-1.                       (15)

In particular alpha_q=1/4 goes to alpha_p=3/4. A statement that the same
quarter is fixed would require a separate gauge carry and its action on all
other marked data. It does not follow from R_x.

Because T is complex linear, (1) gives

  T exp(i t h_q) T^-1=exp(i t h_p).                          (16)

The parameter is not reversed. The graph covariance (13) is therefore a
whole state/time family transport. It becomes a symmetry of one fixed source
only if the original source data separately identify the p target with the q
object, including their domains, coefficients and marks.

5. COMPARISON WITH THE OLD E0 AND C_RAW MAPS

The normal factor iN also occurs when earlier geometric reflections are
composed, but that does not identify the operations. The old E0 map uses an
antiunitary finite coefficient D_E kappa P_E, sends Phi to Phi^E and reverses
the circle parameter. Its C_raw composition remains targeted at Phi^K or,
after the stated compensator, bar(Phi), and has the previously checked
minus-time rule.

The present map instead uses the linear Kswap R_x, reverses gamma_F, sends
the source to (7), and obeys the plus-time rule (16). Equality with an old
E0/C_raw map would require an additional explicit intertwiner equating these
finite factors, base maps and coefficient targets. None is present in the
reviewed source. They are distinct covariances with a shared normal Clifford
matrix, not one operator written in two frames.
