GRADED BOUNDARY CLIFFORD TYPING AND THE EXISTING SCALAR SOURCE
29 October 2026

Result and exact scope
There is a constructive alternative to adding a charge conjugation Jgamma_n
on the original complex Hilbert space. Use the standard geometric Clifford
boundary construction with its changed complex structure. In the existing
free product collar this identifies the ENTIRE oriented normal/two-face
field space and complete geometric selfdual projector with the old scalar
charged two-face CAR data. Under the physical covariance premise in section3
it also transports the full source state. The map retains the circle
evolution and local smearing chart. It does not
postselect a right normal component or add another chiral determinant.
This is a source-typing construction inside the canonical Clifford functor.
It does not prove that the thin P1 tuple chooses this physical quantization,
the P2 coefficient multiplicity, the interacting bosonic state, or OS time.
It is not equivalent to the earlier full COMPLEX CAR quantization of both
normal components as independent charged species; its real structure differs.

1. Original collar data and the primary construction being applied
Let V=L2(S1,C^d), A=-i partial_theta+alpha on periodic H1, with the old complex
unit i0. Use the already studied free product data, with no new matrix fit:

 H0=V directsum V, Gamma=diag(1,-1),
 N=gamma_n=[[0,-1],[1,0]], B=Gamma A,
 D=N(partial_r+B).

N^2=-I, N^*=-N, Gamma^2=I, and NGamma=-Gamma N.
The standard geometric construction changes the complex structure on the
underlying REAL Hilbert space and uses normal multiplication for conjugation:

       Ical=i0 Gamma,           C0=i0 N.

Here C0 is OLD-complex-linear. It is NEW-complex-antilinear, because
C0 Ical=-Ical C0. Confusing these two complex structures would invalidate
the construction. Use the Hermitian metric whose real part is Re h0 and
whose imaginary part is Re h0(Ical u,v). Then C0 is antiunitary, C0^2=I,
and B is selfadjoint and complex-linear for Ical, with C0 B C0=-B.
These are full H1 operator identities; the matrices are constant and do not
change the domain. No assumption on the square of the original charge J is
used to prove them. They do not identify C0 with that original J.

Primary reference: Ludewig and Roos, The Chiral Anomaly of the Free Fermion
in Functorial Field Theory, Ann. Henri Poincare21 (2020),1191-1233,
DOI10.1007/s00023-020-00893-6, section3.3, equations36-38 and Theorem3.12.
Their J is the re-complexifying grading operator, not the TFPT charge J.
The reference constructs the anomaly twist and coherent Clifford bimodule

2. The real field space and the charged chart are explicit
The fixed real form is

 W_R=Fix(C0)={(v,i0 v)/sqrt(2):v in V}.

The displayed map is a real isometry from the realification of V. Thus its
real Clifford algebra has the usual charged CAR(V) complexification. More
explicitly, regarding the left normal copy as a conjugate complex space,

 T(v,bar(w))=(v,i0 w),     T:V directsum bar(V) -> (H0,Ical)

is unitary and new-complex-linear, and transports the canonical antiunitary
flip (v,bar(w))->(w,bar(v)) to C0. It transports B to

                 A directsum (-bar(A)).

Both normal entries are retained as field and adjoint test slots. They are
not two independent charged fermion copies in this functor. Conversely,
quantizing CAR(H0) using the OLD complex structure is a different statistical
assignment. The old full-complex-vector cancellation theorem retains exactly
that premise and is not contradicted by the new typing.
This map does not identify a finite spinor STATE module Lambda_even C5 with
elementary field labels. Its odd Clifford maps still require the full parity
module and change parity. No physical species count is inferred from it.

3. The entire two-face covariance, including its crossing blocks
Let ell>0. On the two labeled faces the already given graph is

 G(B)=[[a(B),c(B)],[c(B),I-a(B)]],
 a(x)=(1+exp(-2ell x))^-1, c(x)=(2cosh(ell x))^-1.

Opposite outward normals give the real structure
C_faces=C0 directsum(-C0), not C0 directsum C0. Therefore
C_faces G(B) C_faces=I-G(B), on both complete boundary Hilbert spaces.
The charged chart, with particles first and holes second, is

 T_faces(v0,v1,bar(w0),bar(w1))=(v0,i0 w0,v1,-i0 w1).

It is constant, preserves supports and H1 domains, and transports G(B) to

             G(A) directsum (I-bar(G(A))).                 (1)

Indeed on right normal slots the graph is G(A). On left normal slots it is
G(-A), while the opposite factors +i0,-i0 turn its off-diagonal +c(A) into
-c(A); this is exactly I-bar(G(A)) on the conjugate slots. Equation(1) is
the selfdual presentation of the OLD full scalar charged CAR covariance,
not just equality of individual face occupations. The second labeled face
retains n(A)=(1+exp(2ell A))^-1; it has not been changed into a vacuum.
If the Clifford graph is used as the actual quantum covariance, its pure
projector state is uniquely determined by the old positive-projector theorem.
Equation(1) then preserves every CAR word and every defined composite on the
same supports, without another quasifree-state or independent-face premise.
This last physical use of the functor is an explicit premise, not a theorem
deduced merely by naming the classical Calderon projector.

4. Circle time, gauge action and geometric reflection
The new-complex one-particle unitary generator obeys the exact identity

               Ical B = i0 A I_normal.                     (2)

Thus exp(t Ical B) is precisely the old scalar circle evolution exp(i0 t A)
on both normal slots. It commutes with C0. The canonical CAR map therefore
preserves the original scalar circle action; it does not use exp(i0 t B)
as if it were the same group. That latter group generally fails to preserve
this real form. Gauge maps exp(i0 Q f(theta)) with internal Hermitian Q and
real scalar f commute with C0 and Ical and have their old charged action on
the particle chart. They need not leave the fixed background covariance
invariant; gauge covariance transports the background as usual.
For the explicitly given source state the GNS implementation of the preserved
circle group, fixed on its cyclic vector, transports with the CAR map. This
does not equate tangential circle time with a positive radial OS Hamiltonian.

In the free collar, put S=NGamma and R=face_swap tensor S. One has R^2=I,
R Ical R=-Ical, R C_faces R=C_faces, and R G(B) R=G(B).
It is the covariant geometric reflected field map in this collar chart.
Its compatibility with the particular original domains/reference and its
identification with physical OS reflection still need that actual map.
No reflection-positive interacting reconstruction is claimed from a symbol
calculation. The unbounded exp(-ell B) is not called a Hilbert contraction.

5. What this changes in the origin search
Within this standard graded Clifford boundary construction, an additional
freely chosen charge conjugation or separate right-fermion source is not
needed to obtain the old scalar CAR source: the above chart constructs it
from Gamma,N and the complete graph. This is a more concrete sufficient route
than arbitrary field/state matching, using the original product operator.
The remaining question for this route is whether the original TFPT source
implements this functor with its full charge, marks and interactions. The
complex retyping is not silently declared equal to the old independent full
complex CAR assignment, and the finite Yukawa extension is not assumed from
the free calculation. Neither original 3+2 selection nor E8 charged completion
nor the physical whole-theory endpoint follows from equations(1)-(2) alone.
