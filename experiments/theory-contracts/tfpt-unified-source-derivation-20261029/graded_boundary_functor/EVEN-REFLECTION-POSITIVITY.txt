3. SAME-FACE CONTRACTIONS ARE REMOVED BY AN INVERTIBLE WICK BASIS

Write X(f)=c(j_+f) for f in W and keep the ACTUAL same-face contraction

For an ordered list define its Wick polynomial W_K(f_1,...,f_n) by

  W_K(f_1,...,f_n)=X(f_1) W_K(f_2,...,f_n)

In particular W_K(f,g)=X(f)X(g)-K(f,g)I. This is triangular, with leading
term the original word and lower terms differing in length by even integers.

Thus the even Wick polynomials span the WHOLE even face algebra in that

For lists f=(f_1,...,f_n) and g=(g_1,...,g_m) the result is zero if n!=m.
