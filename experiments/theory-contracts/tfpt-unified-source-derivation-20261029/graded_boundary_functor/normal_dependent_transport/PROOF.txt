Purpose and scope
The existing coupled dictionary need not require a normal-independent
classical background. The same constant chart transports the entire
normal-dependent Cauchy graph, including its crossing blocks. This removes
the restriction in FINITE-COUPLED.txt:7-8 for the stated product class.
It does not construct a physical bosonic measure, identify OS time, or prove
that P1 chooses this Clifford quantization. No pinned source is changed.

1. Fixed geometric data, varying actual coefficients

Retain the product collar and its fixed normal matrices

  Z=diag(1,-1), N=[[0,-1],[1,0]], S=NZ,
  Gamma_tot=Z tensor gamma_F, Ical=i0 Gamma_tot, C0=i0 N tensor I.

Let gamma_F be fixed. At each r let A(r)=A(r)^* be even and let
Phi(r)=Phi(r)^* be odd: [A(r),gamma_F]=0, {Phi(r),gamma_F}=0.
Allow smooth dependence on r and theta, with the same original finite
Dirac/Higgs coefficient type. Any additional original J_F-even condition on
Phi is retained; it is not needed for the following transport identity.
Put

  B(r)=Z tensor A(r)-S tensor Phi(r),
  E v=(P_F,+ v, i0 P_F,- v),
  h(r)=A(r) gamma_F-i0 gamma_F Phi(r).

Then E is independent of r AND of the background, E^*E=I, and pointwise

  [B(r),Gamma_tot]=0, C0 B(r) C0=-B(r), B(r)E=E h(r).

C0 is complex-antilinear for Ical; it is linear for the old i0 structure.
The fixed one-face unitary chart is

  T(v,bar(w))=Ev+C0Ew,
  T^-1 B(r) T=h(r) directsum(-bar(h(r))).                 (1)

No derivative of E occurs. A moving grading, moving normal frame, or moving
metric trivialisation would require the corresponding connection terms.
They are outside this fixed product assertion.

2. Exact finite regulated transfer, without a commutativity premise

Work first in any finite regulator preserving the displayed constant maps
and adjoints. B(r) is then a smooth selfadjoint matrix for the new Hermitian
metric. Let K(0)=I and

  K'(r)=-B(r)K(r).

Finite evolution is invertible. Set M(r)=C0 K(r) C0. Since r is real,

  M'=B(r)M, M(0)=I.

Also (K^*)'=-K^*B(r). Consequently

  ((K^*)^-1)'=B(r)(K^*)^-1.

Uniqueness for this finite ordinary differential equation proves

  C0 K(r) C0=(K(r)^*)^-1.                               (2)

No commutation of B(r) with B(s) is assumed. In general the right side is
NOT K(r)^-1. This is the transport version of the oriented Green pairing.

3. Entire two-face projector in the charged chart

Write L_K=Graph(K) in H_boundary directsum H_boundary. Its orthogonal
projection is

  P_K=[I;K](I+K^*K)^-1[I,K^*].

For C_faces=C0 directsum(-C0), equation (2) gives

  C_faces(v,Kv)=(C0v,-C0Kv) in Graph(K)^perp.

Indeed set w=-C0Kv. Then -K^*w=C0v, so the displayed vector is (-K^*w,w),
the general form of a vector in Graph(K)^perp. Dimensions, or invertibility,
give equality of the two subspaces. Thus

  C_faces P_K C_faces=I-P_K.                             (3)

Let K_+'=-h(r)K_+, K_+(0)=I. Equation (1) and the constant E imply

  T^-1 K T=K_+ directsum overline((K_+^*)^-1).

Use the same oriented two-face chart as before:

  T_faces(v0,v1,bar(w0),bar(w1))
    =(Ev0+C0Ew0, Ev1-C0Ew1).

The first charged sector satisfies v1=K_+v0. The second satisfies

  T_faces^-1 P_K T_faces

All face-diagonal and crossing correlations are retained. Constant B is

4. Small exact ordering check in the same coefficient type

For a purely algebraic finite check take gamma_F=diag(1,-1) on one even/odd
pair and X=[[0,1],[1,0]]. Set

  B1=Z tensor I, B2=-S tensor X.

They have precisely the displayed even-A/odd-Phi form, B_j^2=I, and
C0 B_j C0=-B_j. Choose ordered disjoint smooth normal bumps f1,f2, each
with integral t>0, and B(r)=f1(r)B1+f2(r)B2. The exact transfer is

  K=exp(-t B2)exp(-t B1).

Then

  C0KC0-K^-1
    =exp(t B2)exp(t B1)-exp(t B1)exp(t B2)
    =sinh(t)^2 [B2,B1]
    =-2 sinh(t)^2 N tensor X != 0.

This is an ordering counterexample to the wrong inverse formula, not a new
physical source or a replacement for the actual 96-dimensional coefficient.
It uses smooth normal dependence; no suite or numerical evolution is needed.

5. Existing joint bosonic insertions can pass through the fixed chart

Suppose a common bosonic measure mu(db), its original insertions q(b), and
the conditional fermionic functionals omega_b are already given, with the
required integrability. At each background b use precisely its actual B_b
and graph, not a fitted covariance. If these functionals use the Clifford
graph as quantum covariance, (4) transports every conditional CAR word
through the SAME background-independent algebra map alpha. Hence

  integral q(b) omega_b(F) mu(db)
     = integral q(b) omega'_b(alpha(F)) mu(db).            (5)

The same holds for arbitrary existing integrable bosonic functionals q and
finite linear combinations of joint words, including distinct insertion
points. Bounded CAR words allow the usual dominated integration when q is
integrable and the conditional states are normalized. Any determinant or
Pfaffian factor already included in the original common measure must be
retained on both sides. This identity does not supply that factor or measure.
It concerns conditional classical-background integration, not an automatic
identification of an arbitrary noncommuting bosonic operator algebra.

For a positive normalized mu and conditional states, the integrated fermion
state is generally a mixture. With a complex eta or Pfaffian density, (5)
remains an integrable algebraic identity and asserts no positive mixture.
The averaged two-point function need not be a projector, and higher moments are integrals
of the actual conditional moments. They are not obtained by applying Wick
to the averaged two-point function. No projector-rigidity or Gaussian
assumption for the integrated source is made here. Likewise no bosonic
reflection positivity follows just from this intertwiner.

6. Unbounded operators and provenance

The finite proof asserts no well-posed arbitrary Hilbert-space Cauchy problem
for an unbounded elliptic generator. In the continuum, use the actual closed
