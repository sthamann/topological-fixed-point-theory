2. Count and trace
Applying any subset of m such pairs to the target vacuum gives 2^m distinct
normalized occupation vectors. They are orthogonal exact energy eigenvectors,
have Q=0, and have energies <=m^2. The positive trace therefore satisfies
  Tr_(Q=0)(1+H)^(-p) >= 2^m/(1+m^2)^p
for every m and p>0. The right side is unbounded for every finite p.
The conclusion already holds on the neutral/even sector. It survives a
positive affine change of energy. This is an infinite limiting-source result;
it does not assert divergence on any fixed finite microscopic Fock space.

4. Scope
A usual elliptic first-order Dirac on a compact finite-dimensional manifold
with finite-rank coefficient and standard elliptic selfadjoint realization
has (1+|D|)^(-p) trace class for sufficiently large finite p. It consequently
cannot be unitarily identified with THIS full H while preserving the stated
time generator. This says nothing against a one-particle extraction, a
different D_coll, an abstract Hilbert-space unitary, or all possible P1 seeds.
In particular compact resolvent or heat trace finiteness are not excluded by
the argument; the old finite-temperature source construction is preserved.
The report correctly retains these distinctions and does not infer a universal
positive-generator requirement for a thermal GNS Liouvillian.
