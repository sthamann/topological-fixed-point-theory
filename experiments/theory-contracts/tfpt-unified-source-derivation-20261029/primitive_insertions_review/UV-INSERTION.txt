RESULT
The algebraic CAR insertion does not automatically have a Fock generator on
the old sea domain. Nevertheless the full coupled one-particle evolution is
implementable in that same one-dimensional sea representation. The two facts
are compatible and must not be turned into a physical exclusion.

1. Exact existing class
Let K be the given finite internal coefficient space, gamma=gamma_F,
gamma*=gamma, gamma^2=1. On L2(S1,K), with period2pi and periodic H1 domain,
  A=-i partial_theta+alpha, alpha=1/4 or3/4,
  h0=A gamma, Phi=deltaPhi=Phi* constant, {gamma,Phi}=0, Phi!=0,
  V=delta h=-i gamma Phi, h=h0+V.
These are precisely the existing finite-coupled chart and an actual constant
odd insertion type. V*=V, {gamma,V}=0, V^2=Phi^2. There is no spatial or normal
profile change in this calculation. Bounded V preserves the one-particle H1
domain. None of this alone is a statement about a many-body vacuum domain.

Sources: graded_boundary_functor/FINITE-COUPLED.txt and
charge_transport/DERIVED-PAIRING.txt:17-30,83-95; the actual v252/v254
one-form is followed in original_insertions/PROOF.txt:25-51. The latter's
section4 already warns against differentiating a massive Fock Hamiltonian
on a fixed massless vacuum. The present appendix evaluates that precise
constant-insertion condition, rather than reopening the field chart.

2. Smallest obstruction: the insertion alone
For Fourier momentum p=n+alpha, n in Z, put s=sign(p). The old negative
projector is P0(p)=(I-s gamma)/2. Since V anticommutes with gamma,
  [P0(p),V]=-s gamma V,
  ||[P0(p),V]||_HS^2=Tr_K(Phi^2)>0.
Summing over all n gives infinity. The off-diagonal particle-hole block
has half this positive squared norm on each mode. Thus its formal pair
creation action on the old sea vacuum is not a square-summable two-excitation
vector. The normally ordered quadratic insertion is not supplied as an
operator with the old vacuum in its domain. Subtracting a scalar cannot
change this off-diagonal divergence.

One can prove a stronger group statement here without inferring it from
the divergent infinitesimal norm alone. On every Fourier block,
  exp(iu V)=cos(u |V|)+i V sin(u |V|)/|V|,
with the continuous value on ker V. Its off-diagonal block is nonzero and
has the same finite positive norm for infinitely many n whenever
  0<|u|<pi/||V||.
Therefore [P0,exp(iu V)] is not HS for all such u. The CAR automorphism of
this PURE INSERTION group is not unitarily implementable in the old sea
Fock representation. This rules out treating it as an already constructed
selfadjoint Fock generator of that whole group, not the algebraic derivation
delta(a^dag(v))=a^dag(Vv) on the polynomial CAR algebra.

3. The full coupled polarization has the opposite HS verdict
Here the Fourier scalar p commutes with Phi, so
  h(p)=p gamma+V, E_p=(p^2+Phi^2)^(1/2), h(p)^2=E_p^2,
  P(p)=1_(negative)(h(p))=(I-h(p) E_p^-1)/2.
All p are nonzero, so these formulas have no zero-mode convention ambiguity.
The exact trace identity is
  ||P(p)-P0(p)||_HS^2
       =1/2 Tr_K[I-|p|(p^2+Phi^2)^(-1/2)]
       <=Tr_K(Phi^2)/(4p^2).
To check it directly, split gamma into its plus/minus spaces and write
Phi in its odd block form. The nonzero squared singular values occur once
on each grading. Cross traces vanish. Each scalar contribution uses
  0<=1-(1+x)^(-1/2)<=x/2, x>=0.
Since sum_(n in Z)1/(n+alpha)^2 converges,
  P(h)-P(h0) is Hilbert-Schmidt in this one-dimensional circle class.
No small-mass assumption is required. Zero internal singular values simply
give zero contribution. The selfdual conjugate test slots of the EXISTING
whole chart give the same HS criterion; they add no physical species.

Consequently the pure quasifree sea representations are unitarily equivalent.
This does NOT say their vacuum vectors or their generators are identical.
The coupled sea is the state determined by P(h), not an independently fitted
new state. Its particle/hole Fock time has a positive excitation generator.
Transport through the representation equivalence gives a genuine strongly
continuous implementation of the full exp(it h) CAR evolution in the old
Fock Hilbert space, with its own transported domain and coupled ground vector.

4. Direct check of the full evolution
The same conclusion is visible without replacing the generator by V:
  U_t(p)=cos(t E_p)+i(p gamma+V) E_p^-1 sin(t E_p),
  [P0(p),U_t(p)]=i[P0(p),V] E_p^-1 sin(t E_p).
Hence
  ||[P0,U_t]||_HS^2
      <=Tr_K(Phi^2) sum_n 1/(n+alpha)^2 < infinity.
The bound and dominated convergence also give HS continuity at t=0.
The full kinetic evolution produces the inverse-momentum suppression absent
from the pure insertion group. A non-HS infinitesimal commutator therefore
cannot be used to reject the full exp(it h) evolution.

Indeed the old vacuum need not lie even in the coupled energy form domain.
In this nonzero constant class its coupled excitation energy, obtained as
the increasing sum of the positive finite-mode energies, is
  1/2 sum_n Tr_K[(p^2+Phi^2)^(1/2)-|p|I].
Its summand is asymptotic to Tr_K(Phi^2)/(4|p|), so the sum diverges.
The corresponding coupled Hamiltonian exists, but its old vacuum is not
in Dom(H_coupled^(1/2)), hence not in Dom(H_coupled). This is why writing
H_coupled=H0+an already defined mass operator on the entire old finite-particle
vacuum core is unjustified. This domain fact does not obstruct its unitary
time group or the pure-state representation equivalence above.
