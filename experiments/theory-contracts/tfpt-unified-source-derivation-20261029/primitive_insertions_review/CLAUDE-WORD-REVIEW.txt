2. EXACT WORD IDENTITY AND ITS ACTUAL TYPE

Write K for the complex-bilinear symmetric invariant E8 form, avoiding
confusion with the different operator kappa. Let superscript t denote the
K-transpose, not the Hilbert adjoint. Invariance gives

  (ad_u)^t=-ad_u, (Ad_g)^t=Ad_(g^-1).

For any finite product w of such letters, transpose reverses their order.
If n letters are adjoint actions and rev reverses all letters and inverts
each group letter, then w^t=(-1)^n w_rev. Consequently, on ANY subspace
where the indicated bilinear is evaluated,

  B_w(x,y)=K(x,w y),
  B_w(y,x)=(-1)^n B_(w_rev)(x,y),
  Sym B_w(x,y)=K(x,[w+(-1)^n w_rev]y)/2.                (1)

This proves the all-orders vanishing of Sym K(x,(ad_h)^(2m+1)y), without
numerical tests or a vacuum-invariance assumption. If every h lies in g2
and all group letters preserve the C4 grading, an even number of ad_h
letters also gives zero on g1 x g1, because K pairs g1 with g3, not g1.
The latter grade statement is a separate hypothesis from transpose(1).

Equation(1) is a necessary and sufficient coefficient criterion WITHIN
this explicit bilinear word ansatz, after restricting to the actual input
fields. Nonpalindromic syntax alone is insufficient: the restricted
matrix of w+(-1)^n w_rev can still vanish. Even h1!=h3 is insufficient
for the asserted full structure: h3=2h1 gives
  ad_h1 ad_h2 ad_h3=2 ad_h1 ad_h2 ad_h1,
whose symmetric bilinear is zero by(1).

Most importantly this is transpose, not a theorem about physical time.
Different matrices in an ordered product can all be static operators.
Neither noncommutativity nor the word rev introduces a time parameter,
reflection-positive physical dynamics, or a map from g1 currents to Weyl
fermions. The latter is a field-type assumption additional to(1).
