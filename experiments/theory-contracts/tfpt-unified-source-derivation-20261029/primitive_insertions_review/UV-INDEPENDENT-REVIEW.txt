4. OLD VACUUM AND THE COUPLED ENERGY FORM

Because E also commutes with P0, the coupled particle/hole excitation energy
in the old quasifree vacuum is the increasing positive finite-block sum

  sum_n Tr_K[E(P-P0)^2]
    =(1/2)sum_n Tr_K[E-|p|I].                            (2)

Equivalently a finite block gives Tr_K[h(P0-P)], with its cross trace zero.
On one2-by2 singular pair this is E_m-|p|, consistent with the two excitation
energies times their Bogoliubov occupations. It is not2(E_m-|p|).
The summand in(2) is Tr_K Phi^2/(4|p|)+O(|p|^-3). Hence it diverges for
nonzero Phi. The old vacuum is not in the form domain of this positive
coupled excitation Hamiltonian, although the new ground vector belongs to
the same Fock Hilbert space and the time group exists on that Hilbert space.

This invalidates a derivative on the OLD vacuum core. It does not invalidate
the whole coupled generator or the exact bounded-field CAR automorphism.
For a group derivative convention the factor i must be kept:
d/du a^*(e^(iuV)f)|0=i a^*(Vf). The appendix's delta without i can be read
as its commutator-generator convention; it does not affect the UV verdict.
