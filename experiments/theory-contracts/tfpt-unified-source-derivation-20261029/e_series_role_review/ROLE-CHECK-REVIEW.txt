3. Ordinary horizontal modes commuting with the retained H8

On the common algebraic finite-energy domain,

   =(-8n-rho.alpha+(2a-1)Q(alpha)/2) J_(alpha,n).
Hence for Q-neutral ordinary n=0 modes the condition is rho.alpha=0.
No integer root e_i-e_j satisfies it because all eight rho entries differ.
The neutral half roots have four plus signs, and the four chosen entries
of (7,5,3,1,-1,-3,-5,-7) must sum to zero. There are exactly eight choices:
six choose two complete opposite pairs, and two choose the complementary
balanced selections 7-5-3+1=0 and its negative. Their four root lines are
mutually orthogonal and span rank4. Thus their root system is4A1.

For both quarters the additional charged-root test is exhaustive. Every
rho.alpha is integral; Q=+/-2 would require a half-integer cancellation.
The two Q=+/-4 roots are +/-q/2, have rho pairing0 and have nonzero quarter
coefficient. Therefore the same eight roots are ALL commuting ordinary
horizontal root modes among the240 roots, not just within the126 neutral
ones. The Cartan zero modes also commute. If describing the full finite
horizontal Lie centralizer, it is4A1+u1^4, not only4A1.

The counterexample alpha=e0-e1 has rho.alpha=1 and gives the saved
commutator -J_(alpha,0). This checks actual operator action rather than
equating charge neutrality with energy symmetry.
