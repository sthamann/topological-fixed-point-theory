2. Completeness of the SM-commuting Spin10 list

An element commuting pointwise with the specified SM must commute with Y.
In SO10 the two nonopposite, nonzero Y frequencies have multiplicities
three and two. The centralizer of this circle is U3 x U2. Commuting also
with its SU3 and SU2 subgroups leaves their scalar block rotations.
Thus the entire centralizer lifts to the two-dimensional Spin torus
  t=(x,x,x,y,y).
This is the whole pointwise centralizer, not only an arbitrary chosen
Cartan subsection. An equivalent root argument is that no D5 root
restricts to zero on the rank-four SM Cartan; commuting with the color
and weak root groups then imposes the scalar block phases.

There is no missing disconnected lift: the preimage of the scalar U1^2
in Spin10 is connected. A scalar loop in its first block winds three
vector planes and reaches the nontrivial Spin sheet. Explicitly its
periods are
  (Delta x,Delta y) in Z^2, 3 Delta x+2 Delta y even,
so x has period2 and y has period1. Integer differences among individual
slot angles only change the Spin central sign; x -> x+1 already includes
that sign. They do not supply further components or representatives.

For g5^4=-1_Spin, vector triviality requires a=4x,b=4y integral, and
the Spin sign is (-1)^(3a+2b). Thus a is odd. Complete distinct Spin-torus
representatives are
  a=1,3,5,7 modulo8; b=0,1,2,3 modulo4;
  t5=(a,a,a,b,b)/4.
This gives sixteen covering representatives. They are not claimed to be
sixteen inequivalent E8 conjugacy classes.

3. Family lift and quotient condition

A four-cycle permutation C4 has determinant-1. A scalar lift eta C4 lies
in SU4 precisely when eta^4=-1. The four choices all have the same four
eigenvalues, the four odd eighth roots. Consequently all are SU4-conjugate
and have fourth power -I4. A unitary conjugator can be made determinant1
by multiplying by a commuting scalar, so SU4 conjugacy is sufficient.

For the actual D3 tetrahedral spinor weights
  (+++),(+--),(-+-),(--+) divided by2,
t3=(1/2,-1/4,0) gives phases (1,-1,3,-3)/8, the same unordered list.
Its vector6 is Lambda^2(4) and has eigenvalues {-1,-1,i,-i,1,1}.
In particular Tr_4(g3)=Tr_6(g3)=Tr_4(g3^2)=0,
Tr_6(g3^2)=2 and Tr_15(g3)=Tr_15(g3^2)=-1.

The actual subgroup is (Spin10 x Spin6)/Z4. In particular its kernel
contains (-1_Spin,-I4). Necessity of g5^4=-1_Spin can also be seen without
choosing a generator of this Z4: the E8 branching contains (16,4).
Since g3^4=-I4, triviality of g^4 on that summand requires g5^4=-I on
the faithful Spin10 chiral spinor. This is exactly -1_Spin. Thus the
fourth-power restriction used to enumerate the class is both necessary
and sufficient. The vector6 factor already has exact order4, so the
resulting E8 element does not accidentally have order1 or2.

This covers arbitrary conjugates of the four-cycle lift and all pointwise
SM-commuting Spin10 factors. A continuous connected SM cannot acquire
additional commuting elements by quotienting by the finite kernel: any
commutator lying in that kernel is continuously constant and equals1.

5. General Spin10 factor without SM commutation

Every compact Spin10 element is conjugate into its torus. The condition
g5^4=-1 means n=4t is integral with odd sum. Modulo4D5, a complete set is
  n in {0,1,2,3}^5 with odd sum,
  together with each such n+4e_1.
There are512 base residues and two Spin sheets, hence1024 distinct
Spin-torus representatives. This is a covering list for E8 conjugacy,
not1024 distinct E8 conjugacy classes.

Let k count odd n_j and d=count(n_j=0)-count(n_j=2) in the base residues.
Then k is1,3 or5, and the same branching gives
  Tr_248(g)=2(d^2+k-3).
Thus native trace0 is possible exactly when k=3 and d=0: of the two
remaining even planes one has residue0 and the other residue2. For this
case Tr(g^2)=-8, and the full character is exactly(60,64,60,64).
The old complete native-clock theorem, not a general assumption that
characters suffice, then identifies these candidates' inner E8 class.

For every native-matching Spin10 representative the only fixed D5 roots
are the six roots among its three odd planes. Sign changes put them in
the A2 form; the even0 and2 planes have no common fixed root and no fixed
root connects an odd plane to an even plane. Therefore the Lie centralizer
is A2+u1^3. It cannot contain commuting su3 and su2: an su3 image uses the
entire A2 factor, whose remaining Lie centralizer is abelian. This excludes
an SM subgroup INSIDE THIS Spin10 centralizing that g5, even if its SM
embedding there is allowed to move. It does not constrain SM groups that
mix the Spin10 and SU4 factors or lie in a fully transported E8 subgroup.

6. Exact scope and conclusion

The proof covers a specified family four-cycle SU4 class and Spin10 factor
with exact E8 quarter order. With fixed SM commutation there are no native
Jhat matches. Removing that commutation admits320 matching torus points,
but their Spin10 centralizers have only A2 as a semisimple factor.
No statement excludes all clocks, arbitrary family actions, a transported
SM embedded across the full E8 centralizer, or the already established
native kappa/glue conjugacy. A geometric rotation of four marked points
can be a different operation until its entire original field action is
identified. No physical source, state, Yukawa field or P1 selection has
been derived by these character tests.
