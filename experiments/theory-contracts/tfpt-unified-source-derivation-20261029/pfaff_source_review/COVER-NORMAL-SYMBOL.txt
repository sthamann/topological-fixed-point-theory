1. Positive tangential identity is already present
The actual old NS cover decomposes unitarily, including H1 domains, as
  B_cov=2(A_(1/4) directsum A_(3/4)),
  V=T_pi=diag(i,-i),
  u_cov=[[0,u_base],[I,0]], u_base=exp(i x).
This is the longitudinal one-boundary source tuple. Its two summands are
the two reducing NS mode-parity subspaces under
  W0 f(theta)=exp(i theta/2) f(2theta),
  W1 f(theta)=exp(3i theta/2) f(2theta).
They are not introduced there as two normal spinor components or the two
geometrically labeled radial faces.

The tangential antiunitary J'=swap M_(exp(-ix)) K has J'^2=I and sends
(n,1/4) to(-n-1,3/4), so J' B_cov J'^-1=-B_cov. This is a valid exact
identity. It was already proposed in CLAUDE-ORIGINALABGLEICH-UNGEPRUEFT.txt:
section2 and is not a new result of followup12. It does not identify J'
with old-complex-linear normal Clifford multiplication gamma_n.

2. Minimal principal-symbol test
Both summands have the SAME old-complex principal symbol:
  sigma_1(B_cov)(x,xi)=2xi I.
For any old-complex-linear local zero-order bundle map N(x),
  sigma_1(B_cov N+N B_cov)=4xi N(x).
Thus {B_cov,N}=0 on all smooth modes forces N=0. Derivatives of N only
contribute lower-order terms and cannot cancel this principal symbol.
A nonzero local normal Clifford map, which must have N^2=-I, therefore
cannot be obtained by reinterpreting this SAME tangential two-sector
coefficient space as the complete old-complex normal spinor space.

There is no conflict with J': it is anti-complex-linear and conjugates the
factor i in the derivative. A linear operator containing a base reflection
could instead reverse xi by changing support. Neither is the local linear
normal Clifford multiplication required in the product Dirac expression.

The supplied deck map itself satisfies [B_cov,V]=0. Its matching square
V^2=-I on the NS cover does not make it a normal Clifford generator: the
required ANTICOMMUTATION is absent. The winding obeys [B_cov,u_cov]=u_cov,
also not the proposed normal anticommutation. These are operator action
tests, not deductions from the orders of the maps.
