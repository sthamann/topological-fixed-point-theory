4. EXACT PROFILE TEST IN THE ACTUAL BERNSTEIN CLASS

Equation(2), on any open interval of variable x>0 in this direct comparison
class, forces f(x)=-(1/2)log x+C. This does not equal a regular original
positive heat profile with finite moments. Such f is nonnegative and, when
the zeroth moment is finite, bounded by mu([0,infinity)); the logarithmic
profile diverges at0 and becomes negative at sufficiently large x for
every finite C.

This conclusion applies even if equality is proposed only on an open
positive spectral interval. A positive Laplace heat profile finite for
all x>0 is real analytic there. Identity on an interval extends to the
positive axis, where the boundedness/positivity conflict remains.
It is not a claim about equality at one isolated spectral value.

The smallest allowed-profile discriminator needs no spectrum or simulation:
take the original allowed heat measure delta_1, so f(x)=exp(-x^2). On the
full paired block the variation residual against minus log Pf is

cannot change this real derivative. A different positive finite moment
profile changes this residual but does not become the exact logarithm on
an open interval.

The LATER version[C] permits other declared profiles, so it does not ban
