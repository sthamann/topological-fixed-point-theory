P1 SPIN STRUCTURE, GSO, AND LOCAL E8 SOURCE AUDIT
27 October 2026

Scope

This note asks one narrow question. Does the original P1 source already contain
an operation that selects the local bosonic E8 extension of the D8 current net?
The qualifying operation could be either

  (a) a sum over spin structures, equivalently fermion parity gauging with its
      twisted sectors and relative Arf phases, or
  (b) a source derived local maximality or multi interval Haag duality rule
      that forces the holomorphic extension.

The audit keeps the known marked D5+A3 lattice and its C4 glue, the finite CAR
collar, and the compiler maps as established inputs. It does not ask whether an
E8 extension can be constructed once its sector support is chosen. That
conditional extension theorem is already available.

DECISION

SCOPED NEGATIVE.

The original P1 operational seed does not specify a spin structure functor, a
sum over the four torus spin structures, a fermion parity orbifold including
its twisted sector, an Arf phase, a localized Ramond spin field, or a source
rule of bosonic maximality. It also does not prove the continuum local net
hypotheses from which multi interval Haag duality with mu=1 would follow.
Therefore it does not force

  (A+B+C+D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_s

or

  (A+B+C-D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_c

for the four spin torus blocks in the standard D8 convention.

There is, however, a genuine positive P1 descendant that should be retained.
The v522 analysis derives the only reflection compatible subgroup of the
finite NS clock tower: its two torsion {1,(-1)^F}. It also proves reflection
positivity after restriction to the even observable algebra. This derives the
fermion parity gauge datum and the D8 or even CAR side. It does not perform the
full gauging operation that adds twisted sectors and chooses their relative
phase. Calling both operations “GSO” hides the remaining step.

The precise remaining datum is small and constructive. One must derive from
P1 either

  1. four compatible source amplitudes on a common spin torus together with
     the parity gauging and Arf weight, or
  2. one normalized local spin or simple current field satisfying the energy,
     relative locality, braiding, and Q system closure conditions, or
  3. the actual P1 scaling limit as a completely rational local net with a
     source derived holomorphic completion, mu=1.

Any one of these closes the already classified extension to one of the two
labelled E8 embeddings. The original h=(1,1) marking selects the plus embedding
only after the marked field identification is transported into that local
extension.

1. WHAT A FULL SPIN SUM DOES

Let the four fermionic torus blocks be written, in one standard convention, as

  A = Z_NS^NS = chi_0 + chi_v,
  B = Z_NS^R  = chi_0 - chi_v,
  C = Z_R^NS  = chi_s + chi_c,
  D = Z_R^R   = chi_s - chi_c.

Then

  (A+B+C+D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_s,
  (A+B+C-D)/2 = chi_0 + chi_c.

These are the two labelled E8 extensions of D8. The sign is the Arf or spin
SPT choice, equivalently the labelled s versus c chirality after all field
labels are retained. The unrefined q series cannot distinguish chi_s and
chi_c.

This formula contains two logically separate operations:

  i. projection in an untwisted sector, for example (A+B)/2=chi_0;
  ii. addition of the twisted Ramond sector with a specified relative phase.

The first operation alone produces the parity invariant observable algebra.
It does not produce chi_0+chi_s or chi_0+chi_c.

This distinction is explicit in the primary literature. Smith, Lin,
Tachikawa, and Zheng state that a bosonic theory is obtained by summing spin
structures, T_B=T_F/(-1)^F, and that the inverse fermionization carries an
Arf quadratic refinement. Their torus formula (5.1) displays all four defect
blocks and the independent Arf sign. In their E8 example the untwisted blocks
are chi_0 plus or minus chi_s and the twisted blocks are chi_c plus or minus
chi_v. Thus the existence or parity of four sectors is necessary input. It is
not itself the gauging map.

5. HAAG DUALITY IS NOT A HIDDEN MAXIMALITY RULE

There are three different statements that must not be merged.

First, ordinary interval Haag duality is a locality completeness property of a
net in a specified representation. A completely rational net can satisfy it
and still have nontrivial DHR sectors. D8 at level one is such a sectorful
net. Thus ordinary Haag duality does not imply mu=1 and does not force E8.

Second, KLM identify the multi interval inclusion index with the global sector
index under substantive hypotheses: a local irreducible net, split property,
Haag duality, conformal covariance or the stated replacement, and finite
index. Under complete rationality, mu=1 means that only the vacuum sector
remains. That is a theorem about a net already satisfying these hypotheses.

Third, one may impose “take a maximal local bosonic extension” as a new rule.
For D8 this leaves the two labelled E8 extensions 0+s and 0+c. It gives E8 up
to labelled chirality, but the rule and its label transport are extra source
data. No such rule occurs in the audited P1 seed definition.

The v501 calculation is a finite lattice witness for the two interval index.
It measures mu_gauged=4 and verifies the conditional condensation arithmetic
16 to 4 to 1. The same passage explicitly leaves continuum Haag duality, local
normality, and split for the quotient net open. It therefore cannot be used as
the missing maximality theorem.

8. MODULAR SEWING, THE HOLOMORPHIC KMS THEOREM, AND THE SINGLE VACUUM LINE

The newer finite modular sewing result is exact: in the already classified
extension class of U=V_D5 tensor V_A3, a one dimensional chiral vacuum line
fixed by cycle exchange forces a Lagrangian support of order four, hence one
of the two E8 extensions. This is an efficient sufficient test.

It does not follow from a unique Hilbert space vacuum vector. It requires an
actual labelled torus conformal block and its cycle exchange action. A scalar
paired partition function, a determinant line, or a state at one fixed torus
does not supply that datum.

The holomorphic KMS theorem likewise starts by assuming an inclusion

  A = B^Z4 subset B,
  [B:A]=4,
  A(I)' intersect B(I)=C,
  mu_B=1,
  c_B=8.

It then proves uniqueness of the KMS state extension. It cannot be reversed to
construct B or to infer mu_B=1 from the finite CAR KMS state. Its own final
section says that the concrete TFPT scaling limit, nonbilinear spin fields,
relative locality, and commutative Q system remain to be constructed.

9. SMALLEST DECISIVE COMPLETION TEST

A future claim that P1 itself selects E8 should pass the following test before
any character or dimension comparison is used.

Input constructed from P1:

  * four source amplitudes A,B,C,D on the same torus, with explicit spatial
    and temporal spin labels;
  * a common state and time normalization;
  * the action of fermion parity on each amplitude;
  * the Arf or quadratic refinement phase;
  * the retained D8 charge marking.

Required identity:

  * prove that the P1 operation is exactly the normalized sum
    (A+B+C+/-D)/2;
  * prove that its output has the refined character chi_0+chi_s or
    chi_0+chi_c;
  * prove sewing under S and T, locality of the added spin fields, and
    compatibility with the original reflection and adjoint.

Kill controls:

  * parity projection only must stop at the even D8 observable algebra;
  * changing the Arf sign must exchange the labelled s and c completions;
  * deleting the Ramond blocks must not yield E8;
  * a scalar determinant or unrefined character match must not count as a
    labelled field identification.

Equivalent local net completion:

  Construct one normalized spin or simple current field in the P1 scaling
  limit and prove the four G1 to G4 properties in the active contract. The
  existing extension uniqueness then supplies all 128 spinor currents and the
  holomorphic E8 net. This route avoids adding a separate arbitrary GSO rule,
  but it still requires a source derived local field.

FINAL STATUS

PROVED OR SOURCE VERIFIED:

  * P1 descendants canonically identify fermion parity as the reflection
    compatible two torsion of the NS clock tower.
  * The even finite observable algebra passes the stated reflection positivity
    test.
  * The sixteen layer torus census is anomaly compatible in all four spin
    structures and has mu_pre=4.
  * Once a D8 spinor current or the marked C4 glue extension is chosen, the
    local extension is holomorphic E8.
  * Within the fixed extension class, an S stable single chiral vacuum line
    selects the two E8 supports, and the marked field map selects the label.

NOT DERIVED FROM ORIGINAL P1:

  * the sum over spin structures;
  * the Arf sign or s versus c labelled completion;
  * a localized Ramond spin field in the P1 scaling limit;
  * crossed product continuity and Q system locality in that limit;
  * a source derived mu=1 or maximal local extension rule.

The exact obstruction is therefore not “no source map at all.” The even CAR
source, parity datum, reflection positive state, and D8 current side are
present. The missing arrow is the source derived passage from parity invariant
D8 observables to one labelled local spinor extension. Without that arrow, the
formula (A+B+C+/-D)/2 and the E8 vacuum line remain a mathematically valid
completion, not an original P1 consequence.
