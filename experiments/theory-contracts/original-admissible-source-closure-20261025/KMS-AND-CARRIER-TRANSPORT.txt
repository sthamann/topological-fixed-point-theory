3. Exact stability theorem
--------------------------

The following is an exact multiplication identity:

      (u A_P u* A_P^(-1))
      (A_P A_C^(-1)).

Every factor is I plus trace class. By unitary invariance and
multiplicativity of the Fredholm determinant, the first and last determinants
cancel. The middle factor is I-zPi because P psi_0=0. Therefore

  det_F R_C(z)=1-z.                                      (1)

class. No empty or filled eigenvector of C itself is required. For winding

  det_F[u^m A_C u^(-m) A_C^(-1)]=(1-z)^m.                (2)

For m>0 the reference adds m orthogonal modes; for m<0 it removes them.
Unitary transport of the entire tuple C,P,u preserves (1). This permits the
already established change to the actual D8 CAR frame; it does not turn the

6. Why ordinary finite compression loses the factor
----------------------------------------------------

C_F has eigenvalue zero and C'_F has eigenvalue one. Mere rank one does not
