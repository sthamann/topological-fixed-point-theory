Why the eta scalar does not finish the line construction
---------------------------------------------------------
Dai and Freed, “Eta-Invariants and Determinant Lines”, arXiv:hep-th/9405012,
https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9405012, formulate the exponentiated eta invariant
on a manifold with boundary as an element of an inverse determinant line and
prove its gluing and variation laws. This is the relevant typing test: an eta
number in a fixed presentation and a positive determinant modulus do not by
themselves provide a global determinant-line section, its connection, or its
holonomy over the space of gauge/source backgrounds. The original TFPT formulas
would need the missing identification theorem, not merely matching eigenvalues.

Smallest decisive missing interface
------------------------------------
No new projector or dynamics is needed for the test. Starting from the already
displayed graded block, the original chain must provide one theorem with these
objects and equalities:

1. A charged Fredholm family with actual domains and representations,

     D_A^+ : Dom_A^+ subset H^+ -> H^-

   for complex Weyl fields, or an explicitly real skew family A_A for a
   Pfaffian. The theorem must state how J, Gamma, the APS/Calderon domain,
   P_adm and the SM charge representation act. Geometric D_+ cannot substitute
   for this typing.

2. A relative determinant/Pfaffian line with a chosen reference and connection,
   for example Det(D_A^+) tensor Det(D_ref^+)^{-1}, together with a section whose
   logarithmic norm reproduces the stated real zeta-determinant response and
   whose phase/holonomy reproduces the eta/inflow term.

3. The Grassmann source identity

     Z_ferm[A;eta,bar eta] / Z_ferm[A_ref;0,0]

   with its two-point kernel equal to the declared inverse/Green operator on the
   same domain and with the same charge action. This decides whether the measure

4. Reflection positivity for that constructed kernel under the original

scalar, or a formal eta/bar eta alphabet is available, then the measure type and
line connection remain unselected.

Appendix: exact carrier compression test
========================================

Status: PROVED analytically under the stated operator assumptions.

Duplicate check
---------------
The Atlas already contains two nearby but different results:

* `finding:old-quarter-heat-transfer-has-controlled-rank-one-limit-and-odd-product`
  proves the controlled heat-transfer limit to a rank-one ground-mode factor.
* `finding:historical-em-rank-one-and-full-heat-traces-have-different-contracts`
  separates the historical scalar rank-one response from the full finite heat
  determinant.

Neither finding states the carrier-compression equivalence below or its
faithfulness consequence. The strict chart used for the active covariance
calculus is recorded separately in
`premise:faithful-differentiable-covariance-chart`, with 0<C<I and
h(C)=log(I-C)-log C. The following test links these scopes without identifying
them.

Assumptions and compression
---------------------------
Let H be a complex Hilbert space. Let C=C*=C^2 be the old projective circle
covariance. Let psi_0 be a unit vector in its empty complement,

  C psi_0 = 0,
  Pi = |psi_0><psi_0|,
  C' = C+Pi.

Let P_F=P_F*=P_F^2 be an orthogonal carrier projection, F=Ran(P_F), and define
operators on F by

  C_F  = P_F C P_F |_F,
  C'_F = P_F C' P_F |_F.

Put v=P_F psi_0 and w=||v||^2. Then

  C'_F-C_F = |v><v|.

Consequently its rank is zero when w=0 and one when w>0. For w>0 it is

  |v><v| = w |v/sqrt(w)><v/sqrt(w)|.

Its only nonzero eigenvalue is w. Thus it is a normalized orthogonal projector
exactly when w=1. Merely knowing “rank one” does not justify replacing the
compression by a unit rank-one projector.

Relative determinant
--------------------
For every z for which I_F-z C_F is invertible,

  (I_F-z C'_F)(I_F-z C_F)^(-1)
    = I_F-z |v><v|(I_F-z C_F)^(-1).

The difference from the identity has rank one, so its Fredholm determinant is
well defined even when F is infinite dimensional. The matrix determinant lemma
gives

  D_F(z)
   := det_F[(I_F-z C'_F)(I_F-z C_F)^(-1)]
    = 1-z <v,(I_F-z C_F)^(-1)v>.

No commutation of P_F with C is needed for this identity.

Exact 1-z criterion
-------------------
Under the assumptions above, the following are equivalent:

  (i)  D_F(z)=1-z on a nonempty open subset of the resolvent domain which is
       analytically connected to z=0;
  (ii) D_F(z)=1-z throughout that connected resolvent domain;
  (iii) psi_0 lies in Ran(P_F).

For a positive contraction C_F the singular z values lie on the positive real
axis at reciprocals of nonzero spectral values, so the natural resolvent domain
containing zero is connected. In finite dimension the same statement follows
immediately from rational-function identity. Equality on a real open interval
with an accumulation point also suffices by real analyticity and continuation.

Proof. Set

  m_F(z)=<v,(I_F-z C_F)^(-1)v>.

If D_F(z)=1-z on an open set, then m_F(z)=1 there away from z=0. The identity
theorem extends this to z=0. Hence

  1=m_F(0)=||v||^2=w.

Because P_F is an orthogonal projection and ||psi_0||=1, equality
||P_F psi_0||=1 holds exactly when P_F psi_0=psi_0, equivalently
psi_0 in Ran(P_F).

Conversely, if psi_0 in Ran(P_F), then v=psi_0 and

  C_F psi_0=P_F C psi_0=0.

Therefore (I_F-z C_F)^(-1)psi_0=psi_0, so m_F(z)=1 and D_F(z)=1-z wherever the
relative determinant is defined. This proves the equivalence.

The projectivity of C ensures that C and C'=C+Pi are pure covariances and makes
the physical endpoint interpretation immediate. The algebraic equivalence
itself uses only C psi_0=0, unit normalization of psi_0 and orthogonality of P_F.

Faithfulness and log-odds consequence
-------------------------------------
If the exact factor 1-z is retained, the criterion gives P_F psi_0=psi_0. On the
same vector,

  C_F psi_0=0,
  C'_F psi_0=(C_F+|psi_0><psi_0|)psi_0=psi_0.

Thus C_F has an eigenvalue 0 and C'_F has an eigenvalue 1. Neither covariance is
strictly faithful on F. For

  h(C)=log(I-C)-log C,

the psi_0 eigenvalue is +infinity for C_F and -infinity for C'_F. In finite
regularization this is precisely the divergence of the log odds at occupation
0 and 1; in the operator formulation the logarithms are singular on that mode.

The other compression regimes are also exact:

* w=0: the carrier erases the added mode, C'_F=C_F and D_F(z)=1.
* 0<w<1: the perturbation is rank one but not a normalized projector. Its
  determinant begins D_F(z)=1-w z+O(z^2), and in general contains the full
  compressed resolvent m_F(z). It cannot equal 1-z on an open z range.
* w=1: the factor is exactly 1-z, but the two endpoint covariances are
  nonfaithful as just proved.

Consequent scope separation
----------------------------
The pure old-circle update and the active strict KMS covariance cannot be
literally the same covariance on the same compressed carrier if the exact
rank-one determinant factor 1-z is demanded. The former has an exactly empty
mode before the update and an exactly filled mode after it. The latter requires
0<C<I so that the finite log-odds chart is defined.

This is not a contradiction between the two original results. It identifies the
missing map between them. A heat or KMS regularization may approach the pure
rank-one factor, and a different faithful covariance may encode a finite
response, but then the object is a limit or a changed covariance. It is not the
same strictly faithful compression of the projective circle state. The present
test adds no new dynamics, carrier projector or source choice.

Appendix: faithful relative-determinant continuation
====================================================

Status: PROVED as a sufficient operator theorem. Conditional only at the final
source-identification step described below.

Purpose
-------
The preceding appendix showed that a literal compression of the pure projector
covariance cannot simultaneously retain the exact factor 1-z and be strictly
faithful. The following construction resolves that mathematical tension without
changing the circle operator, the shift, or an existing carrier projection. It
uses the relative determinant of the full carrier-plus-complement operator.

Circle assumptions and orientation
----------------------------------
Let B be the self-adjoint old circle operator and

  P=1_(B<0),
  u=M_z.

Assume the orientation actually used in the old circle dictionary,

  u P u* = P+Pi,
  Pi=|psi_0><psi_0|,
  P psi_0=0.

For B=-i partial_theta on the twisted Fourier domain with spectrum n+a,
0<a<1, multiplication by z sends n to n+1. Hence uPu* adds exactly the unique
mode with eigenvalue a in [0,1), proving this orientation. Reversing B or the
shift reverses the index and gives (1-z)^(-1), so the orientation is an essential
hypothesis rather than a notation-free fact.

Let C be any covariance satisfying

  0 <= C <= I,
  C-P in L^1(H),

and for |z|<1 put

  A_C=I-zC,
  A_P=I-zP,
  R_C=u A_C u* A_C^(-1).

All inverses exist because ||zC||<1 and ||zP||<1.

Trace-class control
-------------------
First,

  uCu*-C
   = u(C-P)u*-(C-P)+(uPu*-P)

is trace class: the first two terms are trace class by assumption and unitary
invariance of L^1, while the last term is Pi of rank one. Therefore

  R_C-I = -z(uCu*-C)A_C^(-1)

is trace class and det_F(R_C) is defined.

The factorization

  R_C
   = (u A_C A_P^(-1) u*)
     (u A_P u* A_P^(-1))
     (A_P A_C^(-1))

is an exact multiplication identity. Each factor is I plus trace class. Indeed,

  A_C A_P^(-1)-I = -z(C-P)A_P^(-1),
  A_P A_C^(-1)-I =  z(C-P)A_C^(-1),

and the middle factor differs from I by rank one.

Exact determinant invariance
----------------------------
Fredholm-determinant multiplicativity and unitary invariance give

  det_F(u A_C A_P^(-1)u*) = det_F(A_C A_P^(-1)),
  det_F(A_P A_C^(-1))     = det_F(A_C A_P^(-1))^(-1).

Thus the first and third determinants cancel. For the middle factor, the old
orientation gives

  u A_P u* = I-z(P+Pi)=A_P-zPi.

Since Ppsi_0=0, A_P^(-1)psi_0=psi_0, and hence

  u A_P u* A_P^(-1)=I-zPi,
  det_F(u A_P u* A_P^(-1))=1-z.

Therefore, for every |z|<1,

  det_F R_C = det_F R_P = 1-z.

The exact factor is an index/relative-determinant invariant of the trace-class
polarization class. It does not require C itself to contain an empty or filled
eigenvector.

Faithful thermal representative from the same B
-----------------------------------------------
For beta>0 define by functional calculus

  C_beta=(I+exp(beta B))^(-1).

Every spectral value lies strictly between zero and one, so C_beta has no 0 or 1
eigenvectors. Moreover

  log(I-C_beta)-log C_beta = beta B

on Dom(B). The log-odds operator is therefore densely defined and self-adjoint;
it is unbounded because B is unbounded, but it is not singular through a 0 or 1
covariance eigenvector.

On the twisted circle, lambda_n=n+a. For lambda_n>0,

  0 <= C_beta(lambda_n) <= exp(-beta lambda_n),

while for lambda_n<0,

  0 <= 1-C_beta(lambda_n) <= exp(-beta |lambda_n|).

These exponential tails are summable over n in Z. Hence

  C_beta-P in L^1(H).

The determinant theorem applies and yields

  det_F[u(I-zC_beta)u*(I-zC_beta)^(-1)] = 1-z

for all |z|<1, while C_beta remains faithful. This resolves the endpoint problem
of the literal compressed projector calculation.

Carrier-plus-complement Schur identity
--------------------------------------
Let F be any existing finite-dimensional orthogonal carrier projection and
Q=I-F. No commutation of F with C or u is assumed. Set

  A=I-zC,
  A'=uAu*=I-z uCu*.

For |z|<1, A and A' are invertible. Their Q-compressions

  A_QQ=Q A Q |_Q,
  A'_QQ=Q A' Q |_Q

are also invertible because QCQ and Q uCu* Q are positive contractions and the
Neumann bound applies. Define the Feshbach/Schur complements on F,

  S_F =A_FF -A_FQ A_QQ^(-1) A_QF,
  S'_F=A'_FF-A'_FQ A'_QQ^(-1)A'_QF.

They are invertible because A,A' and their QQ blocks are invertible. Standard
block Gaussian factorization gives

  A = L diag(S_F,A_QQ) U,

where L and U are triangular unipotent operators. Since F is finite, L-I and
U-I are finite rank and their Fredholm determinants are one. The same holds for
A'. Also A'-A is trace class by the calculation above, so
A'_QQ-A_QQ is trace class. Multiplicativity then gives the exact identity

  det_rel(A',A)
   = det_F(S'_F S_F^(-1))
     det_rel(A'_QQ,A_QQ).

Since det_rel(A',A)=det_F R_C=1-z,

  1-z
   = det_F(S'_F S_F^(-1))
     det_rel(A'_QQ,A_QQ).

Thus every finite carrier projection admits an exact carrier-plus-complement
factorization of the old circle index. The factor assigned to F alone is the
Schur complement, not the naive compression I_F-z FCF. The naive compression
can lose or attenuate the factor because it discards the complement term

  A_FQ A_QQ^(-1) A_QF.

No new carrier choice appears in this statement; it holds for every existing
finite orthogonal F for which the displayed block decomposition is taken.

Independent review verdict and remaining source condition
---------------------------------------------------------
The proposed continuation is mathematically sufficient:

* every inverse used above exists for |z|<1;
* every Fredholm determinant is applied to I plus trace class;

Section 6: universal finite-rank compression loss
-------------------------------------------------
For the thermal circle covariance,

  D_beta=u C_beta u*-C_beta

finite-rank orthogonal projection and Q=I-F. Since the circle Hilbert space is
infinite dimensional, Q is nonzero. Trace cyclicity for a trace-class operator
and the orthogonal decomposition give

  Tr D_beta=Tr(F D_beta F)+Tr(Q D_beta Q).

The second compression is a nonzero positive trace-class operator: for every
nonzero q in Ran Q,

  <q,D_beta q>>0.

Hence Tr(QD_beta Q)>0 and therefore

  0<=Tr(FD_beta F)<1.

No commutation between F and D_beta is used. If F is nonzero, the lower bound is
also strict; allowing the zero projection explains the report's weak lower
bound.

For the finite-dimensional compressed relative determinant,

  D_F(z)=det[(I_F-z F C'_beta F)(I_F-z F C_beta F)^(-1)],
