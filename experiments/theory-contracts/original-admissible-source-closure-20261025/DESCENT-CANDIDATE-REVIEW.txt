INDEPENDENT REVIEW: THE FIXED PIN DESCENT CANDIDATE A = u_cov C
================================================================

26 October 2026

Verdict
-------

The composite gives a valid positive one particle statement in the same flat
Moebius geometry.  With

  D = -i(sigma_1 partial_x + sigma_2 partial_w),
  C = sigma_1 K,
  u(x) = exp(i pi x),
  A = u C,

and c = plus or minus i, A is an antiunitary involution of the single fixed
domain Dom D_(c,b).  It preserves the chosen Pin-minus lift, the physical
boundary sign b, H1 regularity and supports.

It is not a symmetry of the supplied Dirac realization.  The exact identity is

  D_(c,b) A = - A D_(c,b) + pi sigma_1 A,                 (1)
  A D_(c,b) A = -D_(c,b) + pi sigma_1.                   (2)

Consequently it neither preserves nor exactly complements the original
positive spectral polarization.  It is not an intertwiner of the original
Dirac solution operator, heat semigroup or positive time.  Its action on the
Calderon boundary range requires a separate boundary trace calculation and
does not follow from the bulk relation alone.  It is therefore not yet the
P1 reflection Theta.  It closes the narrower descent question that u alone
left open: the lift-changing winding can be combined with the sector-changing
charge conjugation to obtain a support-preserving antiunitary on one fixed Pin
and boundary domain.

1. Exact domain calculation
---------------------------

The primary source uses

  Psi(x+1,-w) = c sigma_1 Psi(x,w),
  sigma_1 Psi(x,plus or minus 1) = b Psi(x,plus or minus 1).

Proposition 8.3 proves that C maps (c,b) to (bar c,b).  For c = plus or
minus i one has bar c = -c.  If Phi belongs to the -c sector, then

  (u Phi)(x+1,-w)
    = -u(x) (-c sigma_1) Phi(x,w)
    = c sigma_1 (u Phi)(x,w).

Thus u maps (-c,b) to (c,b), and A maps (c,b) to itself.  Multiplication by
u and C both preserve H1.  Since sigma_1 is real and u bar(u)=1,

  A^2 = u sigma_1 K u sigma_1 K = I.                    (3)

The boundary condition is retained because C preserves b and u is scalar.
Both operations are pointwise, so A preserves support in the cover chart.

2. The derivative term is decisive
------------------------------------

Direct differentiation gives

  D u = u D + pi sigma_1 u.

Combining this with D C = - C D proves (1).  Since A commutes with the real
matrix sigma_1, the centered operator

  D_cen = D - (pi/2) sigma_1                              (4)

does satisfy

  D_cen A = - A D_cen.                                   (5)

Equation (5) cannot be silently assigned to the original source.  The gauge
v(x)=exp(-i pi x/2) obeys

  D(v Psi) = v D_cen Psi,

and changes the deck multiplier from c to -i c.  Hence for c=plus i or
minus i the centered problem is gauge equivalent to the c=plus 1 or minus 1
Pin-plus realization.  It is the original differential expression with an
additional flat connection, not the supplied fixed Pin-minus operator.

This also agrees with the primary spectral table.  A strict particle-hole
symmetry inside one c=plus or minus i realization would force symmetric
spectrum, while Corollary 8.4 gives eta plus or minus one half.

3. Original polarization and Fock type
---------------------------------------

Let P_- be the negative spectral projection of the actual D_(c,b).  A
particle-hole lift on its quasifree CAR representation would require

  A P_- A^(-1) - (I-P_-)

to be Hilbert-Schmidt.  This is not true for the full two dimensional
realization.

For every nonzero transverse mode, the primary source reduces D to the
two by two blocks

  H(q,mu) = b q sigma_1 + mu sigma_3,
  mu = n pi/2,  n >= 1.

Equations (2) and functional calculus reduce the covariance mismatch to the
difference of the positive projectors of

  H(q,mu)                    and
  H(q,mu) - pi sigma_1.

Put x=bq.  The squared Hilbert-Schmidt distance of these rank one projectors
is exactly

  1 - [x(x-pi)+mu^2]
      / [sqrt(x^2+mu^2) sqrt((x-pi)^2+mu^2)].            (6)

For n >= 4, restrict to the longitudinal frequencies with |q| <= mu and the
appropriate sign of x=bq.  There are at least n/4 such lattice points.  The
exact two by two projector formula gives on each of them

  ||Delta P||_HS^2 >= pi^2/(13 mu^2) = 4/(13 n^2).       (7)

Their contribution at transverse level n is therefore at least 1/(13n).
The harmonic sum diverges, so the full mismatch is not Hilbert-Schmidt.

Accordingly A has no Shale-Stinespring particle-hole implementer in the
standard full D sea representation.  Ordinary second quantization Gamma(A)
would in any event be the wrong type because A is antiunitary.

The one dimensional zero-transverse branch is different.  There sigma_1=b
and A sends a longitudinal eigenvalue lambda to b pi-lambda.  The failure to
exchange positive and negative modes is only one unpaired quarter mode, so
the reduced circle particle-hole map is implementable with a finite defect.
That is consistent with the already proved quarter-domain/Fock construction
in seam-partner-original-action-20261015 and FOCK-DEFECT-REVIEW.txt.  It does
not promote that reduced branch to the full collar.

4. Time, reflection and Calderon data
-------------------------------------

For the original realization,

  A exp(-t |D|) A^(-1)
    = exp(-t |-D + pi sigma_1|),                         (8)

which is not the original positive semigroup.  Likewise antiunitarity gives

  A exp(i t D) A^(-1)
    = exp(i t (D-pi sigma_1)),                           (9)

not the original unitary time.  The normal derivative makes sigma_1 fail to
commute with D, so the extra term cannot be removed from these formulas by a
scalar phase.

The original P1 construction distinguishes geometric deck reflection,
Calderon polarization and finite carrier involution.  A moves no base point;
it is pointwise conjugation followed by a phase.  It therefore does not by
itself exchange the positive and negative collar regions.  If a local spinor
solves D Psi=0, equation (1) gives

  D(A Psi)=pi sigma_1 A Psi,

so A is not a bulk D-solution intertwiner.  This fact alone does not prove
that its boundary trace fails to preserve the Calderon range: another
D-solution could have the same transformed boundary trace.  Deciding that
question requires the actual Poisson or Calderon boundary operator and the
identity

  A_boundary Ran(P_C) = Ran(P_C),

or a counterexample to it.  Neither is supplied by the present calculation.
Preserving the local physical edge condition b is strictly weaker than
preserving the P1 boundary polarization.

The composite can decorate a separately supplied geometric reflection, but
reflection positivity, the state, the collar reference and their common
intertwiner still have to be proved on that same representation.

5. Relation to the earlier source results
------------------------------------------

The registered earlier work contains the two ingredients separately:

  * C exchanges the alpha=1/4 and alpha=3/4 source domains and gives their
    state, charge and positive excitation energy conjugation.
  * u_cov exchanges the two quarter character spaces on the cut NS cover and
    has the existing CAR winding implementer.

In the registered sources inspected here the two ingredients are stated
separately; I found no stated full two dimensional composite as a fixed
Pin-minus endomorphism and no occurrence of the correction term in (1).  The
composite therefore supplies a useful local conclusion for the domain descent
test.  The same correction term prevents the present calculation from being
called the already required Theta arrow.
