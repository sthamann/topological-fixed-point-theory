3. Relative spectral determinant, including its actual norm

Define theta(z|tau)=sum_n exp(pi i tau n^2+2pi i n z), tau=i.
With the reference z=0, the relative determinant is exactly

 N(z)=det_zeta L_z / det_zeta L_0
     =exp(-2pi u^2)|theta3(z|i)/theta3(0|i)|^2.             (3)

Equivalently the holomorphic section in a local frame is theta3(z|i)/eta(i),
with frame metric h(z)=exp(-2pi u^2). Constant eta/reference factors cancel.
We use the positive theta line convention. Passing to a dual line reverses
its degree and connection, not the need to specify that convention.

The standard flat torus determinant calculation is given in Chakraborty
and Mathai, J.Noncommut.Geom.3(2009)559-578, Section5, pp576-577,
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/30461 . Its commutative
torus case and translation by the NS spin characteristic give(3).
This is an application of known determinant mathematics, not a new theorem
of fundamental physics. Squared norm, chiral modulus and doubled Dirac
modulus are kept separate: norm_chiral^2 is proportional to det L_z;
for D_z in(1), |det D_z| is proportional to det L_z as well, whereas
|det K_z| is its square root. Reference ratios remove fixed constants.
