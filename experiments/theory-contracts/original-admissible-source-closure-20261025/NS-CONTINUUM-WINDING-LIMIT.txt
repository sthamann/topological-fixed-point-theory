NS CONTINUUM WINDING LIMIT
==========================

Question
--------

Does the positive finite NS CAR source of v519/v524/v526 carry the original
fullv21 winding operator u=M_z, and can its finite ordinary determinant converge
to the old polarized factor 1-z without deleting a UV contribution by hand?

Verdict
-------

There is an exact, parameter free one-particle map. At NS twist a=1/2, sample
the old twisted Fourier basis on the N=2M sites theta_j=2 pi j/N. The original
operator u=M_z becomes the diagonal site phase

  (u_N f)_j=exp(i theta_j) f_j.                              (1)

In the sampled Fourier basis it is the cyclic mode shift. The v519 covariance
Q_N=(I+i C_N)/2 is exactly the projection onto the M modes n=0,...,M-1.
Thus the same finite CAR space contains both the v519 state and the sampled old
u. No identification of the Euclidean site translation S_1 with u is used.

The exact finite pair has two crossings. For the negative-sea convention
C_N^-=I-Q_N, which matches the old C=1_{(-infinity,0)}(B_{1/2}),

  u_N C_N^- u_N^* - C_N^-
    = |psi_0><psi_0| - |psi_{-M}><psi_{-M}|.                (2)

The first term is the old spectral-flow defect. The second is its UV alias at
the cyclic cutoff. Hence, for every finite N and z != 1,

  det[(I-z u_N C_N^- u_N^*)(I-z C_N^-)^{-1}]
    = (1-z)_IR (1-z)_UV^{-1}=1.                            (3)

Under the canonical embedding of the sampled modes into the old infinite
twisted circle, the UV vector psi_{-M} escapes every fixed Fourier core. The
defect in (2) therefore converges strongly to Pi_0, but it does not converge in
trace norm:

  D_N -> Pi_0 strongly,       Tr D_N=0,
  ||D_N-Pi_0||_1=1.                                         (4)

Consequently the finite determinants in (3) cannot be passed to the limit by
Fredholm determinant continuity. On the original infinite polarized circle the
ordered relative operator is defined directly and has determinant 1-z. This is
a different limit topology and a different operator category from ordinary
finite determinants. The UV factor may not be removed from the finite OS model.
It is absent only after returning first to the original infinite polarization,
where there is no cyclic alias and the relative perturbation is trace class.

The existing v519/v524/v526 OS prescription does not select that relative
Fredholm limit. It retains the complete finite circle, including the wrap mode,
and defines no normal ordering, spectral cut determinant, or UV subtraction for
(3). If one forms the natural ordinary finite determinant from its supplied
covariance and the sampled u_N, the result is the finite cancellation 1. The
factor 1-z is selected by the separately supplied fullv21 polarized continuum
pair (B_{1/2},u) and its directed relative Fredholm determinant. Combining the
two sources gives a canonical local strong-limit map, but not an OS derivation
of the continuum determinant prescription.

Status: PROVED for the one-particle sampling map, the two finite crossings, the
determinants and the limit topology. SOURCE NEGATIVE for an OS rule choosing the
continuum relative determinant. No free kernel, state, generator or cutoff
counterterm is introduced.

Exact sampling dictionary
-------------------------

Let N=2M and

  H_N=span{psi_n : n=-M,...,M-1},
  psi_n(theta_j)=N^{-1/2} exp(i(n+1/2)theta_j),
  theta_j=2 pi j/N.                                      (5)

The sampling matrix in (5) is unitary. A finite geometric sum gives

  sum_{n=0}^{M-1} psi_n(theta_a) conjugate(psi_n(theta_b))
    = 1/2                                                if a=b,
    = i/[N sin(pi(a-b)/N)]                              if a-b odd,
    = 0                                                  otherwise. (6)

By the v519 definition, the right side is precisely

  Q_N(a,b)=[delta_ab+i C_N(a-b)]/2.                      (7)

This proves, for every even N rather than only N=8 or16,

  Q_N=sum_{n=0}^{M-1}|psi_n><psi_n|,
  C_N^-=I-Q_N=sum_{n=-M}^{-1}|psi_n><psi_n|.              (8)

Sampling the original u=M_z gives

  u_N psi_n=psi_{n+1},                    -M<=n<M-1,
  u_N psi_{M-1}=psi_{-M}.                                (9)

The last equality is aliasing: the ratio of the two sampled functions is
exp(iN theta_j)=1. Equations (8) and (9) immediately give (2).

For the v519 covariance itself, rather than its negative-sea complement,

  u_N Q_N u_N^*-Q_N
    = |psi_{-M}><psi_{-M}|-|psi_0><psi_0|,                (10)

and its same ordered determinant is again 1. After the strong limit its
directed continuum determinant is (1-z)^{-1}; reversing the ordered pair gives
1-z. This is only the occupation and orientation convention. The finite UV
cancellation is invariant under that convention.

No exact finite equivariant embedding
-------------------------------------

There is no nonzero isometry J_N:H_N -> L^2(S^1) satisfying simultaneously

  J_N u_N = u J_N                                      (11)

with u=M_z. Indeed, the finite unitary u_N has an eigenvector. Equation (11)
would map it to an L^2 eigenvector of multiplication by z. Multiplication by z
has no nonzero L^2 eigenvector, because each level set z=lambda has measure
zero. Thus an exact finite cyclic subsystem of the bilateral winding shift does
not exist.

There is nevertheless a canonical asymptotic embedding. Map every sampled
psi_n in (5) to the old continuum psi_n. It intertwines the covariance exactly
on H_N. The sampled u_N differs from the compressed old u only on the top mode:

  J_N u_N J_N^* - P_{H_N} u P_{H_N}
    = |psi_{-M}><psi_{M-1}|.                             (12)

This rank-one wrap has operator norm one for every N, but annihilates every
fixed finite Fourier core once N is large. Hence the map is locally exact and
converges strongly, while it cannot converge in norm as a full finite cyclic
representation.

The same distinction controls the determinant. Under the continuum embedding,

  |psi_{-M}><psi_{-M}| -> 0 strongly,

but every such projector has trace norm one. Therefore (4) is exact. The trace
cannot jump from zero to one under trace-norm convergence, so no theorem about
Fredholm determinant continuity can turn (3) into 1-z. The continuum factor is
obtained by the old directed relative pair

  (I-z u C u^*)(I-zC)^{-1}=I-zPi_0,
  det_F=1-z,                                             (13)

not by taking the numerical limit of the finite ordinary determinant.

Three cancellations that must remain distinct
---------------------------------------------

1. Finite NS cyclic cancellation

The two factors in (3) live on one finite chiral circle. The second factor is
the Fourier alias at the cutoff. It escapes strongly as N grows, but it is
present with unit trace weight for every finite N.

2. Finite carrier compression of an infinite source

KMS-AND-CARRIER-TRANSPORT.txt:194-220 begins with the infinite source. A naive
finite compression loses part of its trace-class response. The exact Schur
complement plus the unchanged complement restores the original factor 1-z.
This is not the cyclic determinant (3), whose winding has index zero at every
finite N.

3. Reflected face or full Dirac cancellation

RELATIVE-PAIRING-REVIEW.txt:66-115,466-475 has two continuum faces with inverse
directed factors (1-z) and (1-z)^{-1}. That cancellation survives the continuum
limit and the Quillen norm. Its partner is a reflected physical sector, not the
UV alias in (2). Removing the finite alias therefore does not remove the
full-Dirac partner.

  import numpy as np

Consequence for the requested forward map
-----------------------------------------

The old and new sources now have an explicit common operator dictionary:

  old twisted circle at a=1/2

  old spectral polarization               -> Q_N or I-Q_N by convention,

therefore sharply typed: does an original continuum OS or admissible functional

v519/v524/v526 construction itself answers no.
