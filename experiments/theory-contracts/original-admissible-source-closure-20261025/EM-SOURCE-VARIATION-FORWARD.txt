ORIGINAL EM SOURCE VARIATION: FORWARD REVIEW
============================================

25 October 2026. Read only source review followed by this report. No Atlas,
index, paper or existing contract was changed.

Question
--------

The faithful one sided circle KMS calculation gives the exact relative
Fredholm determinant

  det_F[(I-q u C_beta u*)(I-q C_beta)^(-1)] = 1-q.        (1)

This report asks whether the original electromagnetic construction already
contains an operator family which turns this directed covariance response into
the source variation q(alpha)=delta_top exp(-2 alpha), while avoiding the
spectral invariance of a fully transported source. It also checks the proposed
D5 spinor conformal block explanation of the exponent 5/4 against the actual
original readout.

Verdict
-------

The sources contain genuine variations which are not merely unitary changes
of frame, but they do not yet identify one common operator family

  alpha -> (D_alpha, domain(D_alpha), state_alpha, reference_alpha)          (2)

whose relative determinant is the electromagnetic q(alpha) and whose
variation gives the stated seam Ward functional.

Three different original structures must be kept distinct.

  1. The twist torus family H_L(b), and the demanded continuum family D_b^+,
     is genuinely nontrivial. Its determinant line can have nonzero first
     Chern class and therefore cannot be one globally unitary transported
     operator with transported domain and frame. Its parameter is the flat
     connection b, however. The sources give no canonical map alpha -> b and
     no theorem equating its relative determinant with 1-delta_top exp(-2alpha).

  2. The historical modulus sigma changes the Maxwell kinetic function
     f(sigma), with alpha(sigma)=1/(4 pi f(sigma)). This is a genuine coupling
     variation. It is explicitly introduced as an axiom. The inspected text
     does not define D_sigma or D_alpha, their domains, their boundary state,
     or their relation to the circle covariance and to (1).

  3. The old defect reweighting adds the relative action alpha d SF. For
     d=2 and SF=1 it gives exp(-2alpha) exactly only when the residual defect to
     trivial partition ratio is alpha independent. The displayed old lemma
     does not prove this residual Ward identity for the complete same source.

The proposed carrier spinor reading gives an exact and useful compression:
the D5 spinor primary has h=5/8, hence its normalized radial two point block
has exponent 2h=5/4. The glued E8 current decomposes into D5 spinor times A3
fundamental, with weights 5/8 and 3/8, so the full exponent is 2 and the A3
factor has exponent 3/4. Dividing or conditioning on the A3 factor leaves the
D5 exponent 5/4. This identity is already realized more strongly in the
existing charged sector response contract. What is not present in the
original EM definition is the source map which says that tr_adm performs this
D5 channel readout or this A3 conditional quotient. Consequently the equality

  B gamma = 5/4 = 2 h_(D5 spinor)                          (3)

is exact, but its right hand side is not yet the original definition of the
left hand side. In the original carrier text B gamma is derived instead as
(3/2)(5/6), and tr_adm is applied to a separately declared rank one primitive
seam projector.

1. The known unitary invariance already excludes the naive identification
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

For a fully transported source

  C' = u C u*,       h(C') = u h(C) u*,
  D' = u D u*,       domain(D') = u domain(D),             (4)

every ordinary unitary invariant spectral functional is unchanged. In
particular, whenever the relevant regularisation is defined and unitary
covariant,

  Tr f(h(C')^2) = Tr f(h(C)^2),
  ||det D'||_Q = ||det D||_Q.                              (5)

This is not a new observation. P1-COVARIANT-INSERTION.txt:67-88 already proves
the stronger source statement: transport B, D, algebra, state, J, grading,
reflection, domains and time together, and spectra, chi, eta, invariant defect
values and correlation functions remain unchanged. Its equations (1) and (2),
at lines 90-135, remove tangential frame motion from the covariant source jet.

Therefore (1) is a directed relative covariance determinant with the old
reference C held in the comparison. It cannot at the same time be read as an
ordinary spectral action or positive Quillen norm of the completely
transported pair (4). The shift u is a source winding operator in the proved
circle construction. No inspected original identifies it by itself as the
physical electromagnetic gauge variation.

2. The nontrivial determinant line is real, but its alpha pullback is absent
---------------------------------------------------------------------------

The strongest original operator family is recorded in
documents/tfpt_research_contracts/sections/020-source.tex:179-231.

On the finite side it specifies a smooth family of gapped collar Hamiltonians
H_L(b), occupied projectors P_L(b), gauge identifications and the Berry
determinant line over the twist torus B of flat U(1) connections. Its nonzero
Chern number rules out a single global frame in which every member is just
unitarily transported with a globally chosen ground state vector.

On the continuum side, however, lines 207-230 say to define independently a
chiral Fredholm family D_b^+, including domains, coupling data and gauge
descent, and then construct its comparison with the finite occupied line.
These are acceptance requirements, not an already supplied D_b^+.
Lines 232-269 likewise state as the open T2 target that the exact Bismut Freed
variation equal the electromagnetic Ward expression. The same section keeps
the continuum comparison and ALPHA.QUILLEN.EXACT.01 open.

This family is the correct kind of object to evade (5), since flat holonomy
and moving domains over a nontrivial line bundle are physical source data, not
only a moving basis. The missing first map is

  alpha -> b(alpha) in B,
  together with D_alpha := D^+_(b(alpha))                 (6)

and a proof that the pullback connection, norm and reference subtraction give
the stated alpha response. Choosing (6) now would be an additional coupling
and path choice. The twist torus construction does not select it.

3. The historical kinetic modulus is a genuine added coupling
--------------------------------------------------------------

The older sources do contain a variation which is plainly not of the form
(4). The fullv21 source at lines 363-387, repeated in fullv27 at lines 60-85,
introduces

  L contains -(1/4) f(sigma) F_mu_nu F^mu_nu
                +(1/2)(partial sigma)^2,
  alpha(sigma)=1/(4 pi f(sigma)),
  delta Gamma_eff/delta sigma = 0.                         (7)

Changing sigma changes the kinetic coefficient and hence the action. This can
overcome unitary spectral invariance. But (7) is labelled an axiom and does not
define the boundary operator family, its collar domain, its state, or an
operator identity relating partial_sigma D to the charged current. Using (7)
as the desired bridge therefore introduces f(sigma) and its source coupling as
the first extra physical input.

The later master package does not fill in that input. In
documents/archive--paper-latex--tfpt-42/sections/
002-section-big-picture-what-tfpt-actually-claims.tex:70-110 it writes

  S_TFPT = Tr f((D_rel+A_Sigma^seed)/chi_seed)
           -Tr f(D_ref/chi_seed) + i pi Delta eta/2,
  A_Sigma^seed = B_rel direct_sum nabla_(chi_seed),         (8)

and separately imposes stationarity in alpha. It does not display
A_Sigma^seed(alpha), partial_alpha D, or a current insertion generating the
alpha dependence. Thus (8) is a package in which such a family could live, not
the required forward identity.

4. The defect action gives the exponential only with its residual Ward law
----------------------------------------------------------------------------

The original reweighting statements use

  S_def(alpha)=S_def(0)+alpha d SF,
  d=2, SF=1.                                               (9)

For complete sector partition functions the exact algebraic consequence is

  q(alpha)=exp(-2alpha) R(alpha),                           (10)

where R is the ratio after removing the displayed topological term. The target
q(alpha)=q(0)exp(-2alpha) is equivalent to R(alpha)=R(0), or under suitable
differentiability to partial_alpha log R=0. This exact distinction and the
measure score terms are proved in REWEIGHTING-REVIEW.txt:40-149. The inspected
historical lemma asserts the intended relative normalisation but does not
derive that Ward identity for all remaining actions, measures and determinant
factors of the same source.

Equation (1) cannot supply the missing cancellation by itself. Substituting
q=delta_top exp(-2alpha), or setting its determinant variable equal to that
expression, names the desired source dependence but does not derive (9) or the
constancy of R.

5. The carrier spinor block: exact identity and exact selection boundary
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The conformal embedding data are exact. The current research contract records

  h_D5 = (0,5/8,1/2,5/8),
  h_A3 = (0,3/8,1/2,3/8),                                 (11)

and the diagonal glue has integer weights (0,1,1,1). The simple current
generator is lambda=(omega_s,omega_f) with squared norms 5/4 and 3/4 and total
conformal weight one. These are stated in
documents/tfpt_research_contracts/sections/010-source.tex:203-227 and
017-source.tex:28-48.

For normalized chiral primaries at radial separation q, the factorized
component of the glued field therefore has

  <Phi_s Phi_s*>_D5 proportional to (1-q)^(-5/4),
  <Phi_f Phi_f*>_A3 proportional to (1-q)^(-3/4),
  <Phi_lambda Phi_lambda*>_E8 proportional to (1-q)^(-2).  (12)

Thus a specified D5 component, equivalently a specified quotient by the A3
factor in this factorized block, gives exactly the v42 transfer exponent.
There is no fractional Grassmann determinant in this statement. It is an
ordinary primary block exponent.

This connection is not new and must be deduplicated against
experiments/theory-contracts/charged-sector-readout-clock-20261013/PROOF.txt.
That contract uses the actual E8 charge beta with

  beta^T P5 beta=5/4,  beta^T P3 beta=3/4, beta^2=2,

and proves the normalized marked generating function

  G_u(q5,q3)
   = [(1-u)/(1-u q5)]^(5/4)
     [(1-u)/(1-u q3)]^(3/4).                              (13)

At q3=1 its reference ratio is exactly (1-u q5)^(-5/4); at q5=q3 it has
the full exponent two. Lines 102-124 of that proof then identify the first
extra choice precisely: u=48 c3^4, q5=exp(-2alpha), the charged state, and the
D5 grade readout are not supplied by the neutral original port. The original
neutral operator has P5 R=0 and is not this marked grade observable.

The v42 electromagnetic definition makes the same selection gap visible. At
lines 13-47 it derives B gamma=(3/2)(5/6)=5/4 from carrier invariants, declares
U_Sigma^min=|psi_Sigma><psi_Sigma|, inserts
q(alpha)=delta_top exp(-2alpha), and defines

  W_Sigma=-B gamma tr_adm log(1-q U_Sigma^min).            (14)

No field Phi_s, A3 conditional expectation, coset quotient, or equality of
tr_adm with the marked grade functional occurs in that definition. The later
generic determinant bound in
029-section-frw-reduction-seam-transfer-and-the-scalaron-bra.tex:96-117 defines
tr_adm through the admissible eigenvalues of a positive trace class seam
transfer and treats rank one as an additional special case. It supplies no D5
module or conformal block selector. The later
architecture calls 5/4 the D5 discriminant norm but explicitly keeps the
physical boundary Ward origin open in
documents/tfpt_1_architecture_e8/sections/007-source.tex:53-99.

The current source flavor correlator also does not add the selector. Its exact
original mark port proves a determinant 1-4 epsilon c3 log(2), while
tfpt_explorer/source_flavor_correlator.py:278-297 explicitly says that matching
it to 1-48 c3^4 exp(-2alpha) requires an alpha dependent epsilon and still
needs the boundary to vertex map and reference contraction.

Hence (12) and (13) establish a precise positive candidate for the origin of
the exponent. They do not prove that the original susceptibility B gamma or
tr_adm reads that block. Dividing out the A3 factor without an original
conditional expectation, state or source insertion would be an additional
channel selection.

6. What the charged current and hypercharge data do supply
----------------------------------------------------------

The U(1)_Y current normalization fixes the embedding index and b1 coefficient.
It supplies the a4 content of the proposed Ward functional. The charged EW2
seam projector in the v42 charged current section is a downstream scalar
pullback of an external scheme remainder. Neither construction defines the
family (2), the map (6), or the channel readout in (12).

This distinction matters because coefficient matching and operator source
variation are different statements. The exact equality 5/4=2h_s shows that
the desired power is native to the carrier module. It does not make alpha a
radial separation, identify the KMS determinant variable with the conformal
cross ratio, or turn tr_adm into a D5 conformal block functional.

7. Smallest forward theorem which would close this step
--------------------------------------------------------

A complete positive result now has a narrow form. On the same original one
sided admissible source, construct a family D_alpha, or equivalently a family
of covariances and insertions, with all four properties:

  A. Intrinsic variation:
       dot D_alpha - [G_alpha,D_alpha] is nonzero after transporting domains,
       state, reference and field frame. Equivalently its covariant source jet
       is not pure tangential frame motion.

  B. Original parameter law:
       the family derives q(alpha)=delta_top exp(-2alpha), rather than receiving
       this expression as its determinant variable or radial coordinate.

  C. Original channel law:
       tr_adm, a conditional expectation, or a specified source insertion is
       proved equal to the D5 marked response in (12) or (13), with the A3
       factor and reference normalization treated by that same map.

  D. Complete relative Ward law:
       the residual ratio R(alpha) is constant, including moving domains,
       measures, Jacobians and determinant normalizations, and the resulting
       variation agrees with the Maxwell, Calderon and transport decomposition.

The smallest decisive test is C. Evaluate the original alpha tangent of the
declared source functional on the glued spinor state and show, as an operator
or normalized matrix element identity,

  partial_alpha W_original
    = partial_alpha[-(5/4) log(1-delta_top exp(-2alpha))],  (15)

while the A3 contribution is removed by an already defined original
conditional expectation or reference factor. If the only way to obtain (15)
is to prescribe q as a radial coordinate, to choose the D5 projector, or to
divide by the A3 block, then that prescription is the first new coupling and
the source derivation remains open.
