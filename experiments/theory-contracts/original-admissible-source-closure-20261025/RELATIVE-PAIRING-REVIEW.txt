INDEPENDENT REVIEW: TWO-FACE RELATIVE PAIRING
=============================================

25 October 2026. This is a source review and an exact operator calculation.
It changes no original paper, Atlas entry, ledger, contract index, or other
contract file.

Question
--------

Can the doubled source

  B_pair=diag(A,-A),
  C_0=diag(P_-,P_+),
  U=diag(u,u)

turn the previously proved single-CAR factor 1-z into an original two-face
relative determinant-line response? In particular, do the original grading,
deck involution, primitive eta term, Quillen line, or cover normalization
already select the sheet ratio and its factor 1/2?

Verdict
-------

The finite-rank operator calculation is exact:

  * the ordinary full-pair relative covariance determinant is 1;
  * a separately introduced sheet superdeterminant is (1-z)^2;
  * one half of its logarithm is log(1-z).

The last two bullets are not yet an original TFPT identification. The actual
primitive grading Gamma commutes with the deck involution. A sheet sign
Gamma_sheet=diag(1,-1) for a deck operation that exchanges the two faces would
anticommute with that deck operation. Therefore Gamma_sheet is not the
displayed original Gamma and must not be inserted under its name.

The Quillen norm does not repair the cancellation. For the actual inverse
pair R=diag(1-z,(1-z)^(-1)), determinant-line multiplicativity gives a trivial
relative line and

  ||s_R||_Q=|1-z| |1-z|^(-1)=1.

Taking one half of the logarithm still gives zero. A positive squared norm
(1-z)^2 would require a different operator, for example two copies of the same
oriented one-face deformation. Neither the original filtration nor the written
gluing formulas construct that operator.

There is nevertheless an exact positive one-face continuation already using
the recovered circle KMS covariance. Its winding defect is positive trace
class with trace one, and its directed relative Fredholm determinant is exactly
1-z at every finite inverse temperature. This removes the nonfaithful endpoint
problem of the pure-sea rank-one formula. The original electromagnetic formula
is singly directed and starts from one-sided boundary data, so no physical
full-pair determinant is required. Its admissible trace is nevertheless not
defined literally as a trace on H_+. The remaining missing equality identifies
the admissible OS boundary marginal with the recovered circle KMS covariance.

The original d=2 weight remains a separate positive cover-degree statement:
two identical sheet contributions are added. It is compatible only with a
separately defined positive-pair norm, not with the actual inverse pair. It is
not the sheet ratio and does not derive the factor 1/2. Thus a hypothetical
positive determinant-line amplitude and the topological weight d SF=2 can
coexist as two typed pieces of one source contract; they cannot yet be called
the same original operation.

1. Exact two-line calculation
-----------------------------

Assume the established one-face defect line Pi satisfies

  P_- Pi=0,
  P_+ Pi=Pi.

On the paired polarization put

  C_1=C_0+diag(Pi,-Pi).

For |z|<1 define the directed relative Fredholm operator

  R(z)=(I-z C_1)(I-z C_0)^(-1).

It is the identity away from the two defect lines. On the first line C_0=0 and
C_1=1, hence R_+(z)=1-z. On the reflected line C_0=1 and C_1=0, hence

  R_-(z)=(1-z)^(-1).

Consequently

  det_F R(z)=(1-z)(1-z)^(-1)=1.                 (1)

This cancellation is correct for the ordinary vector determinant of the full
occupation-covariance pair. It does not say that a real electromagnetic
susceptibility cancels. Particle and hole have opposite signed charge, while a
quadratic gauge response depends on charge squared and adds their
contributions. Equation (1) is therefore an occupation-response statement,
not an F^2 response theorem.

If one defines an additional face grading

  Gamma_sheet=diag(1,-1),

then its superdeterminant is

  sdet_Gamma_sheet R(z)
    =det R_+(z)/det R_-(z)
    =(1-z)^2,                                      (2)

and on the holomorphic branch fixed by Log(1)=0,

  (1/2) Log sdet_Gamma_sheet R(z)=Log(1-z).        (3)

Equations (2) and (3) are exact algebra. They do not establish that
Gamma_sheet is an original grading or that this superdeterminant is the
physical response.

4. The actual Quillen norm and the different positive-pair candidate
---------------------------------------------------------------------

For a chiral Fredholm family D_z^+, a relative determinant-line section s_z
has a Hermitian Quillen norm. With the convention that its squared norm is
given by the zeta determinant of the positive Laplacian, the elementary
identity is

  Delta log ||s_z/s_0||_Q
    =(1/2) Delta log det_zeta((D_z^+)^*D_z^+).             (5)

For the actual cancelling pair, multiplicativity gives

  Det(R)=Det(R_+) tensor Det(R_-)
        =Det(1-z) tensor Det((1-z)^(-1)) congruent C,       (6)

and its Quillen norm is

  ||s_R||_Q=|1-z| |1-z|^(-1)=1.                           (7)

Equivalently, the positive operators associated with the two inverse relative
sections have reciprocal determinant ratios. Formula (5) therefore gives zero,
not Log(1-z). The same conclusion holds for complex z after the inverse phase
is included: the full determinant-line section is canonically the product of a
line and its dual.

A different positively adding pair could be written abstractly as

  R_pos(z)=diag(1-z,1-z),
  det R_pos(z)=(1-z)^2.                                  (8)

For real 0<=z<1 its Quillen norm would be 1-z, since the norm is the
square root of the squared norm. But R_pos is not R. It replaces the reflected
inverse update by a second copy of the same oriented update. The factor one
half is then only the standard conversion from norm squared to norm of this
different object. It is not a normalization that can be applied to the
cancelling pair.

The required original identification is absent at three exact points:

  1. The v42 master functional writes

       Re log det_zeta,Padm(D_xi^2+mu^2)
       +(1/2) eta_Sigma,Padm(D_xi)

     with coefficient one on the positive determinant, not one half:

       documents/archive--paper-latex--tfpt-42/sections/
       027-subsection-primitive-completion-from-the-boundary-kernel.tex:
       229-248.

     That source does not define D_xi as the covariance-pair deformation.

  2. The active Quillen contract defines the continuum line from a chiral
     family D_b^+ and requires a future comparison of its metrics and
     connections with the occupied Berry line. It writes the proposed
     functional with log det_zeta Delta_U(1), while explicitly retaining the
     continuum comparison and first-principles response identity as open:

       documents/tfpt_research_contracts/sections/020-source.tex:180-240,
       296-318.

  3. The already reviewed direct-defect contract records as open the equality
     between the original electromagnetic logarithmic response and the
     directed relative covariance determinant. Replacing that determinant by
     a Quillen norm changes the candidate but does not prove the missing source
     map.

Thus neither the Quillen metric nor Delta eta turns the actual inverse pair
into the single factor. To use (8), the source must first construct a positive
pair family and then identify its electromagnetic observable with that norm.

7. Exact faithful KMS continuation on the existing one-face circle
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Let psi_l, l in Z, be the twisted circle basis, 0<a<1, and let

  B_a psi_l=(l+a)psi_l,
  u psi_l=psi_(l+1),
  C_beta psi_l=n_l psi_l,
  n_l=(1+exp(beta(l+a)))^(-1),  beta>0.

The proposed collar specialization sets beta=2 ell, but the proof is independent
of that value and holds for every beta>0. Since n_l is strictly decreasing,

  D_beta:=u C_beta u^*-C_beta

is diagonal with eigenvalues d_l=n_(l-1)-n_l>0. Moreover

  sum_l d_l=lim_(L->infinity)[n_(-L-1)-n_L]=1.          (12)

Thus D_beta is positive trace class, Tr D_beta=1. Both C_beta and
u C_beta u^* are faithful contractions: no eigenvalue is zero or one. For
|z|<1 the relative operator

  R_beta(z)=(I-z u C_beta u^*)(I-z C_beta)^(-1)

differs from the identity by trace class. Its determinant exists, and the
symmetric finite products telescope:

  product_(l=-L)^L (1-z n_(l-1))/(1-z n_l)
    =(1-z n_(-L-1))/(1-z n_L)
    ->1-z.                                               (13)

Trace-norm convergence of the diagonal perturbations justifies passage to the
Fredholm determinant. Therefore

  det_F R_beta(z)=1-z                                  (14)

exactly at every finite beta. As beta tends to infinity, D_beta tends in trace
norm to the rank-one projector Pi and (14) recovers the pure-sea formula. This
proves a faithful KMS continuation, not a regularization chosen to fit the
answer.

The ingredients were already present separately: the actual twisted-circle
thermal product and source winding are recorded in

  experiments/theory-contracts/mixed-current-origin-20261014/
  ATLAS-KMS-LOCALITY-AND-SOURCE-WINDING.txt:75-121,

and the active source contracts use Fermi-Dirac covariances
C_beta=(1+exp(beta K))^(-1). The targeted source and Atlas search found no
previous calculation of the telescoping relative determinant (13). In
particular, the earlier conclusion that an exact faithful factor 1-z would
force a rank-one endpoint perturbation applies only to a rank-one compressed
ansatz; (12)-(14) are its explicit trace-class countercase.

On the reflected face take C_beta^ref=I-C_beta. Then

  u C_beta^ref u^*=C_beta^ref-D_beta,

and the same telescoping calculation gives determinant (1-z)^(-1). Hence the
ordinary full two-face determinant and its Quillen norm remain one. The KMS
continuation therefore closes only the one-face faithfulness gap. It does not
select R_pos and does not prove the missing marginal equality (11). Once (11)
is supplied, the written one-sided EM domain is already the correct type for
the directed determinant (14).

Taking one half of the hypothetical positive paired log determinant returns a
base-normalized amplitude, whereas the original d=2 rule retains the cover
integral as twice the base response.

The two statements can consistently coexist only with separate types:

  topological source action:       J_top=2 SF,

No checked source equates J_top with log A_det or multiplies the first by the
second. In particular, the one half must not be used to cancel d=2 silently.

9. Smallest decisive source contract
------------------------------------

No new geometry is needed. The missing theorem can be stated on the existing
one-sided datum:

  (A) Circle-to-boundary marginal.
      Construct the state-preserving boundary map V into the admissible
      boundary marginal and prove, on its common invariant core,

        V u = u_Sigma V,
        V B_a = B_Sigma V,
        C_adm,Sigma=V C_beta V^*.

      Here u_Sigma must be the sourced boundary winding operator, not the
      rank-one projector U_Sigma^min and not the heat transfer customarily also
      denoted U_Sigma. If no such operator is displayed, constructing and
      typing it is part of the missing theorem. An equivalent equality of all
      quasi-free two-point functions on the boundary marginal is sufficient.
      Preserve the declared time, state, adjoint, APS domain, and reference
      sector. No equality Gamma=P_C and no new chirality projector is part of
      this test.

  (B) Existing electromagnetic observable.
      Keep the already written singly directed quantity (10). Prove the exact
      response identity

        det_F R_beta(q)=det(1-q U_Sigma^min)=1-q.          (15)

      Equality of the two scalar determinants alone is not enough. At finite
      beta the KMS jump D_beta is trace class of infinite rank, whereas
      U_Sigma^min is rank one, so no unitary V can identify those two operators.
      A literal rank-one identification is available only in the limit beta
      tends to infinity after proving V Pi V^*=U_Sigma^min. At finite beta the
      source must instead prove that its EM functional reads the relative
      Fredholm determinant of the marginal in (A).

  (C) Optional doubled norm route.
      If a genuinely positive two-face Quillen response is still intended,
      separately construct R_pos and prove that the original source reads its
      norm. This cannot be inferred from the actual inverse pair, whose
      determinant and norm are one.

  (D) Topological coupling.
      Retain J_top=d SF=2 as the separately sourced alpha reweighting and prove
      the previously identified residual-sector Ward constancy. Do not infer
      this coupling from a determinant norm.

The original formula (10) already fixes a singly directed admissible EM
functional, and the KMS identity (14) supplies the exact one-face factor on a
faithful circle covariance. It does not by itself identify tr_adm with H_+ or
with that covariance. The first unsupported implication is (A), followed by
the response identification in (B). A physical full-pair determinant is not
required by the cited EM formula. Merely naming a Quillen line, BFK gluing, or
an eta phase does not pass the test.
