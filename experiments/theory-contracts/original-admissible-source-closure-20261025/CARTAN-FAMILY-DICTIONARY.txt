3. The original Gaussian four is a Vinberg Cartan subspace

its original Heisenberg Cartan in weight one, and let theta=Jhat. On h_C,

  h_C=H+ direct-sum H-,  H+=ker(J-i), H-=conjugate(H+), dim H+=4.

commute because h_C is abelian. No real E8 root alpha restricts to zero

The finite union of its root hyperplanes therefore does not fill H+.
A point x outside it is regular semisimple, with Z_g(x)=h_C.

If an abelian semisimple subspace of g_i contains H+, it lies in
Z_g(x)=h_C. Its intersection with g_i is exactly H+. Thus H+ is maximal:
it is a Cartan subspace of the theta representation. This is a proof of
maximality, not an inference from the dimension four.
