----------------------------------------------------

3. The exact deck/quarter commutator in the invariant even spin class
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Let O=(0,i), T=(0,-i), and u=(y+i)/x^2. Then

  div u = 2T-2O,   delta* u=-1/u,   q* u=-u.

The unique q-fixed nontrivial spin class is alpha=O(T-O). For the twist
L=p*O(1)=O(O+T), the kernel line bundle is

  alpha tensor L=O(2T) isomorphic to O(2O),
  H0(alpha tensor L)=span(1,u).

One genuine deck spin lift acts by D f=u(f composed delta), so in that
basis

  D = [[0,-1],[1,0]],       D^2=-I.

Up to its required scalar phase, a quarter lift acts by

  Q = a diag(1,-1).

The spin square-root convention fixes a fourth-power sign, but the following
relation is independent of every scalar choice:

  Q D = -D Q,      Q D Q^-1 D^-1 = -I.

Thus q exchanges the two D eigenlines (eigenvalues +i and -i). No rank-one
kernel subspace is invariant under both operators. Multiplying either lift
by a scalar or changing basis cannot remove this commutator. The half-turn
Q^2 preserves each line. A changed spin class is an additional input.

This result is for the specified normal spin/twist factor only. Another
ACTUAL factor with the opposite projective cocycle could cancel the sign.
The original full Spin/family source needs its real tensor factors and
transport matrices to establish such a cancellation. No artificial two-state
factor is inserted, and no exclusion of the whole TFPT construction follows.
