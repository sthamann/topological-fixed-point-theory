2. Exact coupled conformal derivative on a fixed reflection domain

Assume common transported domains, fixed R, and a conformal Dirac variation
 D_s=exp(-s h/2) D exp(-s h/2), h=h*, deltaD=-{h,D}/2.
This is the standard conformal covariance formula after unitary L2 density
transport; Ginoux, The Dirac Spectrum, Proposition1.3.10.
Set h_+=(h+RhR)/2 and h_-=(h-RhR)/2. Expanding the definitions, without
any assumption that A and B commute, gives
 deltaA=-({h_+,A}+{h_-,B})/2,
 deltaB=-({h_+,B}+{h_-,A})/2.                         (1)
Even profiles change both pieces. Odd profiles mix them. Formula(1) is
kinematic: odd profiles need not belong to the actual doubled geometry.
If B=0 and h is even, deltaB=0; an even conformal probe cannot create an
odd relative component from zero. This is not a conclusion about every P1
source, whose original D and R must first be identified.

Let E_X(t)=exp(-t X^2/chi^2), F(x)=integral exp(-t x^2)dmu(t).
Assume finite dimension or an ACTUAL valid differentiable relative trace
contract. Heat positivity/finite moments alone do not supply that contract.
For fixed chi, cyclic trace differentiation gives
 delta S_real=-(2/chi^2) integral t Tr[B E_B deltaB-A E_A deltaA]dmu.
Inserting(1) yields
 delta_+ S_real=(2/chi^2) integral t Tr h_+[B^2 E_B-A^2 E_A]dmu,
 delta_- S_real=(1/chi^2) integral t Tr h_-[{A,B E_B}-{B,A E_A}]dmu. (2)
The ordered products in the odd term must be retained.
For kappa=delta chi/chi add

4. General finite all-profile stationarity theorem

Assume a finite selfadjoint pair, fixed chi and a permitted common scale path.
If the real spectral action is stationary at one scale for EVERY positive
atomic heat measure, then for every t>0
 sum_(lambda in spec B) lambda^2 exp(-t lambda^2/chi^2)
 =sum_(lambda in spec A) lambda^2 exp(-t lambda^2/chi^2).
Group distinct positive squared eigenvalues x_1<...<x_n. Independence of the
finite functions exp(-t x_j) forces equal multiplicities at every x_j: divide
by exp(-t x_1) and send t to infinity, then iterate. Zero multiplicities are
invisible to this derivative. Thus the action is constant along the ENTIRE
scale orbit, equal only to the zero-multiplicity difference times F(0).
There is no isolated selected scale from this universal stationarity demand.
This theorem does not apply if chi is tied to scale, scale is disallowed,
the quantum measure supplies other terms, or the actual channel fixes F.
