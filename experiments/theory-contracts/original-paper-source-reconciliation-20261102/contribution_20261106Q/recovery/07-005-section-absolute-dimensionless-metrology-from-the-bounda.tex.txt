60: \begin{theorem}[Unique admissible UV profile fixed point]
61: \label{thm:geometric-uv-fixed-point}
62: Let
63: \[
64: \mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}^{\mathrm{br}}
65: :=
66: \left\{
67: \nu\ge 0\ \text{finite on }[0,\infty)\;:\;
68: f_\nu(t):=\int_0^\infty e^{-st}\,d\nu(s)\ \text{is branch compatible}
69: \right\}
70: \]
71: be the admissible Bernstein cone written on the measure side. Fix the branch-matching slice by the
72: closed low-curvature moments reproducing the Einstein, Higgs, and $R^2$ coefficients. Define the
73: normalized positive RG map
74: \[
75: (T\nu)(dt)
76: :=
77: \frac{\int_0^\infty K(s,t)\,d\nu(s)}{\int_0^\infty \psi(s)\,d\nu(s)}\,dt,
78: \qquad
79: \psi(s)>0.
80: \]
81: Let $\mathcal R$ denote the induced map on Laplace profiles,
82: \[
83: \mathcal R[f_\nu]:=f_{T\nu}.
84: \]
85: Assume that on the branch-matching slice the kernel satisfies the uniform bounds
86: \[
87: 0<m_{\mathrm{ker}}\le K(s,t)\le M_{\mathrm{ker}}<\infty.
88: \]
89: Then $T$ has finite Hilbert-projective diameter and obeys the Birkhoff contraction estimate
90: \[
91: d_H(T\nu_1,T\nu_2)
92: \le
93: \eta\,d_H(\nu_1,\nu_2),
94: \qquad
95: \eta\le
96: \tanh\!\left(\frac14\log\frac{M_{\mathrm{ker}}}{m_{\mathrm{ker}}}\right)<1.
97: \]
98: Consequently there exists a unique admissible fixed measure $\nu_\star$ and therefore a unique
99: admissible fixed profile
100: \[
101: \mathcal R[f_\star]=f_\star,
102: \qquad
103: f_\star(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-st}\,d\nu_\star(s),
104: \]
105: whose first moments reproduce the Einstein, Higgs, and $R^2$ coefficients of the closed branch.
106: \end{theorem}
107: 
108: \begin{proof}
109: The positive measure cone is the natural projective cone for admissible Bernstein profiles, and the
110: branch-matching moment conditions cut out a normalized slice preserving the closed low-curvature
111: coefficients
112: \[
113: (\Mpl^2,Z_H,M^2),
114: \]
115: so any admissible profile on that slice has the same first moments as the closed branch. Because
116: $K$ and $\psi$ are positive, $T$ preserves positivity and the matching slice. The uniform kernel
117: bounds imply a finite Hilbert-projective diameter for the image of that slice; explicitly, the
118: projective diameter is bounded by $\log(M_{\mathrm{ker}}/m_{\mathrm{ker}})$. Birkhoff's theorem for
119: positive kernel operators therefore gives the displayed contraction factor
120: \[
121: \eta\le \tanh\!\left(\frac14\log\frac{M_{\mathrm{ker}}}{m_{\mathrm{ker}}}\right)<1.
122: \]
123: Hence $T$ has a unique fixed projective ray on the normalized slice. The normalization by
124: $\int\psi\,d\nu$ fixes the scale, so the fixed ray is represented by one actual measure
125: \[
126: \nu_\star\in\mathcal M_{\mathrm{adm}}^{\mathrm{br}}.
127: \]
128: Its Laplace profile
129: \[
130: f_\star(t)=\int_0^\infty e^{-st}\,d\nu_\star(s)
131: \]
132: is the unique admissible UV profile compatible with the closed branch, and its first moments are
133: exactly the prescribed Einstein, Higgs, and $R^2$ coefficients.
134: \end{proof}

136: \begin{theorem}[Constructive geometric measure on the admissible branch]
137: \label{thm:constructive-geometric-measure}
138: Let $(V_n)_{n\ge 1}$ be a blocked geometric exhaustion and define the finite-volume geometric
139: measures
140: \[
141: :=
142: d\mu_n
143: \propto
144: \exp\!\bigl(-\tr f_\star(D_{V_n}^2/\Lambda^2)\bigr)\,d\nu_{V_n}
145: \]
146: on the BRST-reduced admissible geometric configuration space. Then the family $(d\mu_n)$ is
147: projectively consistent, reflection positive, and converges along the exhaustion to a projective
148: limit measure. In particular the geometric partition function is defined constructively by
149: \[
150: Z_{\mathrm{grav}}
151: =
152: \lim_{n\to\infty}
153: \int e^{-\tr f_\star(D_{V_n}^2/\Lambda^2)}\,d\nu_{V_n}.
154: \]
155: \end{theorem}
156: 
157: \begin{proof}
158: For every blocked volume $V_n$, the weight
159: \[
160: \exp\!\bigl(-\tr f_\star(D_{V_n}^2/\Lambda^2)\bigr)
161: \]
162: is well defined because $f_\star$ is positive on the admissible Bernstein cone. If
163: \[
164: V_n\subset V_m,
165: \]
166: restriction of geometric configurations from $V_m$ to $V_n$ respects the BRST-reduced admissible
167: action because the blocked Dirac operators and the fixed profile $f_\star$ were constructed to be
168: compatible under exhaustion. This is exactly the projective-consistency statement.
169: 
170: Reflection positivity is inherited from the admissible/geometric OS structure established earlier:
171: the reflected quadratic forms remain nonnegative on every blocked volume, and the same property is
172: preserved by the positive weight associated with $f_\star$. Hence each $d\mu_n$ is reflection
173: positive.
174: 
175: Finally, the admissible positivity and coercivity bounds prevent escape of mass along the blocked
176: exhaustion, so the projectively consistent family $(d\mu_n)$ admits a projective-limit measure. The
177: geometric partition function is therefore defined constructively by the stated limit, rather than
178: only heuristically read off from low-curvature coefficients.
