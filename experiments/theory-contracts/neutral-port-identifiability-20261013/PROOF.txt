QUESTION

Can the already declared selected neutral source state R and its rank-one
mark port P0 retain or identify the D5 carrier factor 5/4?

SCOPED VERDICT

No.  This specific route is excluded exactly.  The selected source state is

  B48 = 12 T_D5 + 16 T_A2 + 4 T_U1 = 12 T_E8 + R,
  R   =                4 T_A2 - 8 T_U1.

Thus R has D5 coefficient zero.  With the source oscillator Gram form,
||R||^2 = 48 while ||B48||^2 = 624.  The declared port

  V_R = |R><p0|,       P0 = |p0><p0|

therefore compresses R to V_R^* V_R = 48 P0.  It sees the norm, not the
sector decomposition.

The loss is explicit.  In the sector-stress basis (T_D5,T_A2,T_U1), the
distinct primary contrast

  R' = (16/7) T_D5 - (12/7) T_A2 - 8 T_U1

satisfies the same primary condition 5a+2b+c=0 and the same norm

  (5/2)a^2 + b^2 + (1/2)c^2 = 48,

but has nonzero D5 coefficient 16/7.  Nevertheless its rank-one mark-port
compression is also 48 P0.  Hence the port output does not identify the D5
coefficient, even within the three-sector primary plane.

WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT REJECT

It rejects routing the D5 exponent 5/4 through the already selected R -> P0
operator.  It does not reject carrier-resolved charged insertions, another
source-derived defect observable, or an independently derived quotient.
R' is an information-loss witness in the primary sector plane; it is not
claimed to be another selected 48-root branch.  The finite quantities above
already existed in the implementation.  Their use here is a scoped
identifiability check, not a new general theorem and not a physical gate.

The remaining inputs are the choice R rather than B48 or another primary,
the seam injection scale, the propagation exp(-alpha L0), the rank-one mark
contraction, and the physical response functional.  The susceptibility 5/4
and the epsilon target match are still not derived by this port.

PROPAGATION AND INPUT BOUNDARY

The original conditional heat propagation is scalar exp(-2 alpha) on
this oscillator-level-two subspace. With the declared injection scale
c3^2, both contrasts therefore give the same 48 c3^4 exp(-2 alpha) P0.
This is equality for arbitrary alpha and c3, not a numerical fit. It
shows that this compressed datum alone cannot distinguish the two
sector decompositions. It does not assert that the full original
operator algebra has lost this information. A D5-resolved insertion
would require its own source-derived operator and observable dictionary.
