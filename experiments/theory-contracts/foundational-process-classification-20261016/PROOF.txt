2. Class A: event orders without a background geometry

INPUT: an unlabeled finite strict partial order P=(E,<). A precedence relation,
transitivity and absence of cycles are assumed; causal order is not derived
from consistency in THIS class. There are no rates, amplitudes or metric.

JOINT CONSEQUENCE: cumulative occurrence states are the order ideals J(P).
They form a distributive lattice under union and intersection. Its nonzero
join-irreducibles are exactly principal ideals down(e), recovering the events
and their order. Maximal one-event-at-a-time ideal chains are the linear
extensions. Thus one relational datum specifies events, cumulative states and
allowed histories together. These are occurrence states, not quantum states
or physically stored memories; the event order remains an input.

COUNTERWORLDS: at each ideal I a fixed-P Markov growth law independently
chooses weights on the enabled events. Invariance under relabeling constrains
weights, but need not select them. For a<c with an isolated b, even the first
choice a versus b has a free probability. There is no order automorphism
exchanging them. The full product of invariant Markov simplices has dimension
 beta(P)=sum_[I](number of enabled-event orbits under Aut(P)_I - 1).
The sum runs over Aut(P) orbits of nonterminal ideals. The checker obtains
unlabeled-poset counts1,2,5,16 at sizes1..4. At size3, four classes have beta0
and the chain-plus-isolate class has beta2. At size4, eight have beta0 and
eight retain free parameters. Beta0 is uniqueness ONLY in this fixed-P,
unit-step, invariant Markov class, not uniqueness of durations or a world.

A two-event chain can embed as timelike points separated by different proper
times in continua of different dimensions. Finite precedence therefore does
not reconstruct metric/locality. A chain supplies an ordinal history, not a
physical clock or observer. Classical poset worlds are counterexamples to
claiming complex quantum theory from these axioms.

3. Class B: relational consistency without a prescribed global order

INPUT: finitely many labeled interfaces k with finite nonempty local input
sets I_k and output sets O_k; a deterministic relation-function
 w: product O_k -> product I_k.
There is a local input/output typing and independently selectable local maps
f_k:I_k->O_k. Demand exactly one solution i=w(f(i)) for every tuple of local
maps. This is a substantial full-control/modularity assumption, not a derivation
of laboratories, interventions, space or physical time from nothing.

No-self-signalling follows: fix all other outputs. If w_k depends on its own
output, choose two outputs with distinct returned inputs and a local map
swapping the corresponding choices, mapping all other inputs to one choice.
There is then no fixed point, contradicting consistency. This also justifies
the completeness of the no-self-signalling enumeration reduction.

EXACT BINARY CLASSIFICATION: for three interfaces, each w_k is a Boolean
function of the other two outputs, giving16^3=4096 candidates. Checking every
one of the4^3 local unary function tuples and all8 input tuples yields744
valid processes. Among them488 have an acyclic static dependency graph and
admit at least one fixed total order;192 have a first interface with a
context-dependent reversal between the other pair;64 have no first interface.
An order being compatible is NOT a uniquely selected total order.

An explicit no-first example is the Lugano function
 w(a,b,c)=((1-b)c,a(1-c),(1-a)b).
It has a unique solution for each of the64 independent unary interventions.
Single-copy consistency therefore does not force global acyclicity.
The published single-copy framework/census is Baumeler--Wolf,
https://arxiv.org/html/1507.01714v2, with general process-function analysis in
https://arxiv.org/html/1703.00779v2. We adapt only the finite set/function
mathematics; the latter paper's physical setup also discusses spacetime, which

EXPLICIT NEW PREMISE TO TEST: independent copies may be jointly manipulated
at each corresponding local interface, and the resulting experiment must

It is not implied by the original single-copy axiom or by mere associativity.

4. Class C: stable information under an explicitly supplied loss process

INPUT: finite X, deterministic step f:X->X and observation o:X->A; optionally
an explicit error relation T. Here X and the step are assumed, not created.
One may quotient by identical future observations using partition refinement;
this is known automata minimization, not a new fundamental origin theorem.
The code independently classifies endofunctions up to relabeling at sizes1..4:
1,3,7,19 classes. These are ALL such finite step systems within those bounds.

JOINT CONSEQUENCE: every endofunction graph consists of cycles with transient
trees. Distinct cycles give invariant sector labels; positions on a cycle
give a cyclic phase orbit of the GIVEN step, without a canonical phase origin.
Thus persistence and periods share
one transition structure, without a chosen physical symmetry. They need not
both be nontrivial. A constant map has one terminal sector; identity has many
sectors but no nontrivial periods; one n-cycle has period n but one sector.

Define a record cell as a nonempty forward-closed set. The record quantity
here is the MAXIMUM number of pairwise disjoint, observation-distinguishable
record cells, not an exhaustive invariant record function on every state.
With full state access this quantity equals the number of sink strongly
connected components of the allowed-transition support. With coarse
observation, it is the independence number of the conflict graph of sink
components, where adjacency means overlapping observation sets. Every closed
cell contains a sink component, proving the bound, and choosing those
components attains it. This does not claim every transient state already
carries one of those stable labels: x->a and x->b with a,b absorbing has two
record cells but no two-label invariant record function on all three states.
Error correction is relative to a given error relation: disjoint output sets
are the ONE-STEP zero-error criterion; indefinitely repeated errors require
disjoint reachable closures of the codewords.

For f=(1,0,2), there are two record sectors and a period2 cycle. Adding one
allowed error edge0->2 removes the first closed sector, leaving one. With
constant observation the predictive quotient has one class; with full access
it has three. Stability does not determine the channel or observation.
Iterations already supply a step order. No continuous duration or physical
time is derived from it. A stable label is not a local or conscious observer.
