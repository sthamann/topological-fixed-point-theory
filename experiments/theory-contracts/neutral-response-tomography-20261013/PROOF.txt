1. Exact additional response of the SAME source
------------------------------------------------
Wick contraction gives the two-point coefficient (1/2)Tr(A^2), three-point
coefficient Tr(A^3), and connected four-point function
    <T_A(z1)T_A(z2)T_A(z3)T_A(z4)>_connected
      = Tr(A^4) sum_(three unoriented four-cycles C)
                    product_((i,j) in C) (zi-zj)^(-2).
Self-contractions are excluded by normal ordering. Connected pairings at four
quadratic vertices are cycles. Each of the three cycles has 16 socket pairings;
the factor (1/2)^4 cancels their multiplicity. At three vertices the eight
pairings cancel (1/2)^3; at two vertices two pairings leave 1/2. The checker
enumerates all pairings and verifies these counts independently of matrix traces.
Disconnected two-pair terms must be subtracted before extracting Tr(A^4).
At (z1,z2,z3,z4)=(0,1,3,7), the cycle sum is 67/127008, nonzero.

The actual native matrix yields
    Tr R=0, Tr R^2=96, Tr R^3=-384, Tr R^4=4608.
Only the fourth coefficient and ensuing identifiability argument are new here;
the source's norm48 and cubic-384 were already implemented.

3. A complete reconstruction in the specified three-block class
---------------------------------------------------------------
Restrict the candidate to A=aP5+bP2+cP1 with fixed native projectors and
primarity Tr A=0. Thus c=-5a-2b. Match moments two, three and four to R.
Let Q=5a^2+2b^2+c^2-96, K=5a^3+2b^3+c^3+384,
and L=5a^4+2b^4+c^4-4608. Exact elimination of b yields
    Res_b(Q,K)=23040 a(a-2)^2(35a^3+140a^2-180a-864),
    Res_b(Q,L)=230400 a^2(161a^6-2512a^4+15936a^2-36864).
Their polynomial gcd is 23040 a. Any common solution therefore has a=0.
Then Q=0 gives b=+4 or -4, and K=0 selects b=4, hence c=-8.
Conversely this triple satisfies all equations. The reduced Groebner basis
over the rationals is [a,b-4], providing a second exact elimination check.
No sampling or numerical root-search is used in this uniqueness argument.

Thus the existing two-/three-point response supplemented by the connected
four-point response uniquely identifies the selected R inside THIS class.
Within the hierarchy of these self-responses up to order four, the third order
fails and the fourth succeeds. This is not a claim of minimality over all probes.

For general symmetric A they recover only block traces, not off-diagonal data.
