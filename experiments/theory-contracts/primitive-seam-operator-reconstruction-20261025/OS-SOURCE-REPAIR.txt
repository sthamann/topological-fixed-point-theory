OS SOURCE REPAIR: FULL MOMENTS, GRADED WORDS, AND THE PRIMITIVE SEAM BOUNDARY
================================================================================

Scope and verdict
-----------------

This note repairs one precise mathematical defect in the archived TFPT
Osterwalder--Schrader argument.  It does not construct a new physical source.

PROVED here:

1. The connected or truncated distributions

       kappa_n = delta^n log Z |_{0}

   cannot themselves be used as the full Schwinger moment functional in the OS
   Gram form.  A two-variable Gaussian already gives a 2 by 2 counterexample.

2. The correct input is the full normalized moment functional

       S_n = Z[0]^{-1} delta^n Z |_{0},

   equivalently the graded moment--cumulant expansion of exp(log Z).

3. A unital, reflection-intertwining boundary word map which preserves the
   complete original Z-functional on all reflected word products induces an OS
   isometry.  This is a sufficient repair theorem.  It does not require odd CAR
   fields to belong to the physical observable algebra.

OPEN after the repair:

The cited TFPT source does not construct Z_adm from D_+, B_Sigma, the Calderon
projector, or the relative spectral/Chern--Simons expressions.  It also does not
provide the state-preserving boundary word map required below.  Thus this note
repairs the logical form of the OS step but does not close the primitive source
gap.


1. The defect in the cited source
---------------------------------

The relevant original passages are:

* documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--04_qft_source/sections/
  005-subsection-admissible-schwinger-functions-minkowski-loca.tex:144-161
  defines W_rel = log Z_rel and explicitly calls

       S_n^T = delta^n W_rel |_{0}

  the truncated admissible Schwinger distributions.

* The same file, lines 163-184, then applies the OS axioms directly to
  {S_n^T} and says that the reconstructed theory has these truncated
  distributions as its Schwinger functions.

* documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--04_qft_source/sections/
  008-section-relative-reflection-positivity-with-fermions.tex:181-196 and
  217-254 repeats the same typing: it proves distributional properties of the
  truncated family and supplies that family directly to the stated OS/CAR
  reconstruction theorem.

This is not the standard OS input.  Reflection positivity is a positivity
condition on the full moment functional, or equivalently on the normalized
generating functional Z/Z[0].  It is nonlinear under passage from moments to
connected cumulants.  Positivity of a reflected full Gram matrix is therefore
not positivity of a matrix formed by replacing its entries with cumulants.


2. Small exact counterexample
-----------------------------

Let X be a centered real Gaussian variable smeared at positive Euclidean time,
and write Y = Theta X for its reflected copy.  Choose the covariance

       E[X^2] = E[Y^2] = a > 0,
       E[YX] = c > 0,

with 0 < c <= a, so that the elementary covariance matrix

       [ a  c ]
       [ c  a ]

is positive semidefinite.  Such a centered Gaussian field is reflection
positive on the one-dimensional positive-time test space.  Explicitly, put
rho=c/a and expand a polynomial F in Hermite polynomials for the normalized
variable X/sqrt(a): F=sum_n f_n H_n.  The bivariate Gaussian identity gives

       E[(Theta F)F] = sum_n n! rho^n |f_n|^2 >= 0.

Thus positivity holds on the whole polynomial algebra, not merely at degree
one.

Use the positive-time polynomial basis

       F_0 = 1,
       F_1 = X^2.

The genuine OS Gram matrix is

       G_ij = E[(Theta F_i) F_j].

Wick's rule gives

       E[X^2]       = a,
       E[Y^2 X^2]   = E[Y^2]E[X^2] + 2E[YX]^2
                     = a^2 + 2c^2.

Hence

       G_full = [ 1       a          ],
                [ a   a^2 + 2c^2     ]

and

       det G_full = 2c^2 > 0.

Thus the full moment functional is positive on span{1,X^2}.

Now make exactly the erroneous replacement of a full four-point entry by its
connected four-point cumulant.  For a centered Gaussian,

       kappa_4(Y,Y,X,X) = 0,

while the two-point cumulant is kappa_2(X,X)=a.  The resulting cumulant
substitution is

       G_trunc = [ 1   a ],
                 [ a   0 ],

with

       det G_trunc = -a^2 < 0.

Therefore an RP Gaussian theory becomes nonpositive if truncated functions are
inserted as though they were full OS moments.  Conversely, separate properties
of the cumulants cannot establish positivity of the full reflected word
functional without the moment reconstruction.

This example is only a test of the theorem's mathematical typing.  It introduces
no TFPT field, state, or physical source.


3. Correct bosonic and fermionic reconstruction
------------------------------------------------

Let the mixed source be mathcal J=(J,eta,bar eta), with J even and eta,bar eta
Grassmann odd.  Assume Z[0] is nonzero and define

       W[mathcal J] = log Z[mathcal J] - log Z[0].

Connected distributions are derivatives of W.  Full distributions are

       S_n(z_1,...,z_n)
         = Z[0]^{-1}
           delta_{z_1} ... delta_{z_n} Z[mathcal J] |_{mathcal J=0}.

Equivalently,

       S_n(z_1,...,z_n)
         = sum_{pi in Pi_n} epsilon(pi;z_1,...,z_n)
             product_{B in pi} kappa_{|B|}(z_B).

Here Pi_n is the set of set partitions.  Inside each block and among blocks one
uses one declared canonical order.  epsilon(pi;z_1,...,z_n) is the Koszul sign
of the permutation which moves the odd source derivatives into that block
order; even source derivatives contribute no sign.  This is precisely the
coefficient identity Z/Z[0] = exp(W).

For a purely fermionic Gaussian source, W has degree two.  Thus connected
cumulants above degree two vanish, but the full even moments do not:

       S_{2m}(f_1,...,f_{2m})
          = Pf( K(f_i,f_j) )_{1 <= i,j <= 2m}.

This Pfaffian is the Grassmann-signed sum over pairings.  It is the fermionic
analogue of the a^2+2c^2 term in the preceding bosonic example.  Reconstructing
with exp W therefore retains the complete Wick family which the direct use of
{S_n^T} discards.


4. Minimal repaired OS theorem
------------------------------

Let P_+ be the unital graded polynomial word algebra generated by admissible
Euclidean field insertions with support at positive time.  Let Theta be the
antilinear graded reflection, including the declared order reversal and CAR
sign convention.  Let omega_Z be the normalized full moment functional obtained
from Z/Z[0].

Assume:

  (R1) omega_Z is defined on every word (Theta F)G with F,G in P_+;

  (R2) full reflection positivity holds,

           omega_Z((Theta F)F) >= 0       for every F in P_+;

  (R3) positive Euclidean translations act on P_+, preserve the OS null space,
       and the induced operators satisfy the symmetric contraction bounds and
       strong continuity required for a self-adjoint positive generator;

  (R4) if a full relativistic reconstruction is claimed, the full moments
       reconstructed from Z satisfy the required graded symmetry, Euclidean
       covariance, and the uniform growth/regularity hypotheses of that
       reconstruction theorem. Temperedness of each connected distribution
       separately is not a substitute for these full-family hypotheses.

Then

       <F,G>_OS = omega_Z((Theta F)G)

is positive semidefinite.  Quotienting P_+ by its null space and completing
gives the OS Hilbert space. Under (R3), positive-time translation descends to
a strongly continuous symmetric contraction semigroup exp(-tH), H>=0. The
self-adjointness conclusion uses symmetry, not merely the general
Hille--Yosida theorem for an arbitrary contraction semigroup. In an application
one may prove (R3) from the complete standard OS assumptions instead of
assuming it separately. Truncated
functions may be used to encode Z through exp W, but never as replacements for
the entries of this Gram form.


5. State-preserving primitive boundary transport is sufficient
---------------------------------------------------------------

Let P_boundary,+ be a graded boundary field-word algebra built on the primitive
boundary data, and let P_adm,+ be the positive-time field-word algebra whose
full functional is the original Z_adm.  Assume that this full original
functional is reflection positive.  Suppose there is a unital graded word map

       iota : P_boundary,+ -> P_adm,+

such that

  (B1) iota intertwines reflection and the declared grading;

  (B2) it preserves the complete reflected word functional,

       omega_boundary((Theta F)G)
         = omega_adm((Theta iota(F)) iota(G))

       for all F,G in P_boundary,+;

  (B3) it intertwines positive Euclidean translation on a common invariant
       dense word domain.

Then the boundary OS Gram matrix is the pullback of the original admissible OS
Gram matrix.  In particular it is positive, and

       [F]_boundary -> [iota(F)]_adm

is a well-defined isometry of OS quotient spaces.  By (B3) it also intertwines
the reconstructed positive-time semigroups.  This is the smallest positive
repair path: no independently chosen boundary state is needed if the complete
state-preserving map into the already defined original Z_adm is proved.

Preservation of the two-point function alone is insufficient outside a proven
quasi-free sector.  After a fluctuating bosonic background has been integrated,
a mixture of Gaussian fermion states need not remain Gaussian, so its full
four-point function is not fixed by the averaged two-point kernel.


6. Field algebra and observable algebra
---------------------------------------

The repaired theorem is first a statement about the graded field-word algebra.
Odd CAR generators may live in a charged field algebra F.  The physical
observable algebra is obtained only afterward by the actual gauge-invariant or
admissible restriction used by the theory.  In the cited primitive source,

  documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
  sections/001-section-primitive-core-and-carrier-interface-handoff.tex:319-343

defines A_adm by restriction of operators which preserve Ran(P_adm), together
with their adjoints.  It does not require each odd charged field to be an
element of A_adm.  The boundary map above must therefore map field words into
the graded source algebra before this observable restriction; the restriction
can then be checked on invariant/even words.  No identification of Q_Theta with
fermion parity is used here.


7. Source audit: what the spectral and Chern--Simons data do not supply
------------------------------------------------------------------------

The following statements are source-level facts, not a general impossibility
claim.

1. Primitive relative spectral action.

   documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--01_boundary_kernel_source/
   sections/001-section-primitive-core-and-carrier-interface-handoff.tex:302-317
   defines

       S_prim = tr f(D_rel/chi) - tr f(D_ref/chi) + (i pi/2) Delta eta_Sigma.

   This is a spectral functional.  It contains no Grassmann integration,
   fermionic source coupling, inverse Dirac kernel, or definition of Z_adm.

2. Relative generating functional.

   documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--04_qft_source/sections/
   002-section-closure-theorems-from-the-admissibility-complex.tex:256-316
   defines only

       Z_rel[J,eta,bar eta]
          = Z_adm[J,eta,bar eta] / Z_ref[0,0,0].

   Its reflection-positivity lemma explicitly assumes that the admissible
   fermionic CAR kernel is reflection positive.  It does not derive that kernel
   or Z_adm from D_+ and B_Sigma.

3. Geometric measure.

   documents/archive--tfpt-45--source_extracts--05_metrology_source/sections/
   005-section-absolute-dimensionless-metrology-from-the-bounda.tex:136-178
   constructs a measure weighted by exp[-tr f_*(D_V^2/Lambda^2)] on the
   BRST-reduced geometric configuration space.  It does not include D psi,
   define a Berezin measure, or provide the missing mixed source functional.

4. Chern--Simons material.

   documents/tfpt_research_contracts/sections/005-source.tex:97-108 uses an
   abelian bosonic 2+1-dimensional Chern--Simons/K-matrix theory to type the
   anyon-condensation and holomorphic-edge step.  It is not a definition of the
   local fermionic Z_adm.  The Maxwell/Chern--Simons reading in
   documents/tfpt_1_architecture_e8/sections/007-source.tex:140-150 is itself
   conditional and likewise does not provide Grassmann sources or the full
   Schwinger family.

5. Explicit coupled source remains a demand.

   documents/tfpt_research_contracts/sections/017-source.tex:153-191 asks for

       Z_a[J] = integral DU Dpsi DPhi exp(-S_a + J O),
       S_a = S_Wilson + bar psi D_GW[U,Phi] psi
             + S_Phi + S_seam/top,

   and marks the complete T1--T7 package open.  This confirms where an actual
   fermion-plus-boson source would enter; it is not supplied by the primitive
   spectral action.


Final boundary
--------------

The exact repair is therefore:

       derivatives of log Z  -> connected cumulants
       exponentiation         -> full graded moments
       full Z moments         -> OS Gram form
       state-preserving map   -> boundary OS isometry.

The first three arrows are mathematical identities or the standard OS input.
The fourth is a precise sufficient interface.  The original TFPT sources cited
above do not yet construct Z_adm from the primitive D_+, B_Sigma data and do not
prove that interface.  No claim of complete primitive seam reconstruction is
made.

Primary mathematical references
--------------------------------

K. Osterwalder and R. Schrader, Axioms for Euclidean Green's Functions II,
Communications in Mathematical Physics 42 (1975), 281-305,
doi:10.1007/BF01608978. The corrected theorem and its full-family growth
hypotheses are in Sections III-IV, especially IV.1. This reference supplies
the standard reconstruction framework, not the missing TFPT functional.

P. F. Baum and E. van Erp, The Index Theorem for Toeplitz Operators as a
Corollary of Bott Periodicity, arXiv:2010.02906v2 (2020), Section 6,
Proposition 13; Quarterly Journal of Mathematics 72 (2021), 547-569,
doi:10.1093/qmath/haab008. The Calderon and nonnegative boundary spectral
projections there differ by a compact operator and give the same index.
Equality of that index does not give an exact equality of projections,
an isometric shift, a CAR state, or the time covariance used in the companion
operator theorem.
