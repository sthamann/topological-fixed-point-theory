1. COMPLETE AMBIGUITY OF THE SPECIFIED INTERNAL DYNAMICS
Let two finite-dimensional Hermitian generators H and H' induce identical
Heisenberg derivations on every completed CAR generator and on C. Then
A=H'-H commutes with these operators and their adjoints. On each C block,
the finite full CAR representation generates all M_(2^n)(C). Explicitly,
the vacuum projector is the product of (1-n_j); creation monomials on
its left and annihilation monomials on its right give every occupation
basis matrix unit. The supplied unitary J_s^u transports these matrix
units to each C sector (prior charged-register proof, equation (4b)).
Commuting
with C eliminates cross-block entries. Therefore
                 A = sum_(s=0)^3 c_s P_s, c_s real.
Conversely every such A preserves those specified derivations. Since H
commutes with C, the all-time Heisenberg evolutions on this block-diagonal
algebra also agree. Each separately normalized block Gibbs state agrees.
This does NOT assert that a full Gibbs state with measured weights across
all sectors is unchanged: those weights can themselves constrain offsets.

2. THE NATIVE OPERATOR RECOVERS EXACTLY THREE RELATIVE MODES
For H_c=H+sum_s c_s P_s,
 ([H_c,D]-[H,D]) P_s = (c_(s+1)-c_s) D P_s.
Multiplying on the left by D^dagger gives
 K := D^dagger([H_c,D]-[H,D]) = sum_s b_s P_s,
                  b_s=c_(s+1)-c_s.
Thus a sector-resolved first time response supplies the cycle differences.
Their sum is zero. The C4 oriented incidence matrix has rank3 and kernel
span{(1,1,1,1)}. Integration around a spanning path reconstructs c uniquely
modulo a common constant. The sum-zero relation is a consistency condition
for the scalar-sector-offset model, not a proof of the physical source.

By contrast D squared supplies c_(s+2)-c_s. Its graph has two connected
components, {0,2} and {1,3}; rank2 leaves a separate constant on each.
Keeping the original D therefore matters: the quarter-step operator
contains information absent from its square. This is a relation between
actual operator actions, not a dimension or numerical coincidence.

3. A CONSTRUCTIVE FOUR-PREPARATION PROTOCOL
A full matrix tomography assumption is unnecessary if the supplied D can
be used for preparation and its complex temporal response is accessible.
Choose a fixed normalized vector psi in one C sector, and use the four
preparations D^k psi. Do not replace this preparation by the ground state
of each shifted Hamiltonian. For a prepared vector in sector s, define
 G_s(t;H)=<psi_s|D^dagger exp(itH) D exp(-itH)|psi_s>.
Then for every real t,
 G_s(t;H_c)=exp(it b_s) G_s(t;H), and G_s(0;H)=1.
Hence [G'_s(0;H_c)-G'_s(0;H)]/i=b_s. The initial sector is known; D moves
it around the full cycle. This reconstructs the three relative offsets
from the four first moments, with one dependent consistency equation.

The required original response or specified H baseline and the preparation
menu are inputs of this inverse problem. They are not inferred from a
single ground-state autocorrelator, from CAR alone, or from the existence
of D in an inventory. A scalar change invisible in an unknown channel
cannot simply be made observable by postulating a free control gate;
physical controls require additional structure (see Araújo et al.,
arXiv:1309.7976v2, discussion of global/relative phase). Here the native D
and its four sectors are explicitly present, but their physical P1-derived
realization remains open.
