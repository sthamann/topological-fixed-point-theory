2. The geometric reflection is not the Calderon polarization

Let C be the ORTHOGONAL projection onto the Cauchy data of a symmetric Dirac operator,
and let J_n be its normal Clifford action, J_n*=-J_n and J_n^2=-I. Under the usual
Dirac hypotheses the Cauchy space is Lagrangian for <J_n u,v>. Consequently
J_n C J_n*=I-C. For F=2C-I this gives J_n F=-F J_n.
The algebraic operator R_alg=J_n F is a selfadjoint involution, satisfies
R_alg F R_alg=-F and R_alg F=J_n. This is a reconstructed, generally nonlocal
operator; it is not automatically the local geometric lift of a deck reflection.
If R and F are anticommuting involutions then (RF)^2=-I, whereas R=F would give
(RF)^2=I. The algebraic quarter turn is not a derivation of charged holonomy i.

An explicit Dirac calculation: on a circle let A_alpha=-i partial_theta+alpha,
alpha=1/4, in the periodic frame. On TWO GRADED COMPONENTS OF THE SAME DOMAIN put
B=diag(A_alpha,-A_alpha), J_n=[[0,-I],[I,0]], and R=[[0,I],[I,0]]. Then
J_n^2=-I, R^2=I, R J_n R=-J_n, R B R=-B. The actual differential operator
D=J_n(partial_r+B) commutes with the geometric reflection psi(r)->R psi(-r).
On the half-infinite cylinder C_half=1_(0,infinity)(B), and
R C_half R=I-C_half, not C_half. Thus its Cauchy range is not the +1 eigenspace of R.

There is also a compact version; the conclusion is not an artifact of an infinite
collar. Double [0,ell]xS^1 to the r-circle of length 2ell with constant D as above.
The involution r->-r fixes r=0 and r=ell, and has the local lift R. The Cauchy
projection of the original finite cylinder is G_ell in section 3. On its two
boundary components the deck lift is diag(R,R). On any nonzero B mode it conjugates
G_ell=[[A,Cross],[Cross,I-A]] to [[I-A,Cross],[Cross,A]], which differs from G_ell.
In particular it cannot equal 2G_ell-I, an operator that commutes with G_ell.
Do not confuse this deck operation, which fixes each boundary, with reflection
WITHIN the original cylinder: (u,v)->(Rv,Ru); the latter preserves graph(exp(-ell B)).

More generally, a lift over the fixed boundary is pointwise in the bundle, while
the principal symbol of 2C-I depends on tangential cotangent direction for an
ordinary Dirac boundary. These cannot be identified just from reflection regularity.
If R_tau is instead DEFINED as 2C-I, the algebraic statement is true by definition,
but its identification with a geometric reflection has become an extra hypothesis.
This corrects the stated geometric inference; it is not a no-go for all TFPT seams.

For a generic compact interior, C need not equal the positive spectral projector
of B. The half-cylinder equality used above is exact in its specified realization.
The two-face finite-cylinder calculation below uses the actual solutions, not that
generic replacement. Orthogonalizing a Calderon projection is also explicit here.

3. The complete two-face datum retains the signed operator

Assume a given selfadjoint B on H, a known normal length ell>0, identified/labeled
boundary fibers, and an exact normal-independent product equation
(partial_r+B)psi=0 on the retained interval [0,ell]. Write T=exp(-ell B).
By the spectral theorem T is positive, injective, densely defined and closed,
possibly unbounded. The L2-closed endpoint Cauchy space is
L_ell={(u,Tu): u in D(T)}, where
D(T)={u: integral exp(-2ell lambda) dmu_B,u(lambda)<infinity}.
Finite spectral-cutoff solutions form a core for this closed graph. No claim is
made that every such endpoint pair has an H1 solution up to both boundary faces.

The orthogonal graph projection is
G_ell = [[A,Cross],[Cross,I-A]],
A=(I+T^2)^(-1)=(I+exp(-2ell B))^(-1),
Cross=T(I+T^2)^(-1)=(2cosh(ell B))^(-1).
This follows by projecting each spectral two-vector onto span(1,t), or from the
closed-operator graph projection theorem. All four displayed blocks are bounded.
Both A and I-A are injective, with dense range; 0 and 1 can be spectral limit
points but not eigenvalues. The original signed B is recovered uniquely by
B=(2ell)^(-1) log(A/(I-A)),
D(B)={u: integral_(0,1) |log(a/(1-a))|^2 dmu_A,u(a)<infinity}.
This formula keeps signs and magnitudes and transforms covariantly with the fibers.

Likewise T is the spectral quotient Cross/A on D(T). As a raw composition,
Cross A^(-1) has domain Ran(A)=D(T^2); its closure is T. Omitting this closure
would give the wrong domain in infinite dimension. Example: B e_n=-n e_n,
u_n=exp(-3ell n/2) is in D(T) but not D(T^2).
If ell is not independently known, the reconstruction determines only ell B.
If the face identification is missing, a positive transfer cannot simply be
declared in an arbitrary frame. Recovering a spectral list is not recovering
the matrix/operator action on the given local fields.

4. A common state and time from that same datum, with explicit field premise

Now additionally assume that the actual field algebra is charged CAR(H direct_sum H)
and that its state is the gauge-invariant quasifree state with covariance G_ell.
These are field/statistical premises, not consequences of P1 alone. A projection
covariance defines a pure quasifree state. Restriction to the second labeled face
has occupation n_ell=I-A=(I+exp(2ell B))^(-1), with 0<n_ell<I spectrally.
For the free CAR dynamics a(f)->a(exp(itB)f), this is the quasifree KMS state
at inverse temperature beta=2ell (using the convention where occupation is
(I+exp(beta B))^(-1)). Its one-particle modular logarithm is
log((I-n_ell)/n_ell)=2ell B.
Thus the covariance and time generator are read from the SAME two-face datum;
one does not fit a second Hamiltonian to a prescribed occupation list. In infinite
dimension this is a state on the CAR algebra, not automatically a trace-class
Gibbs density matrix in an arbitrarily chosen Fock representation.

This is the known faithful-covariance inverse from the earlier Atlas contract
seam-state-dynamics-injectivity-20260918, now linked to the exact finite-collar
boundary equation. Neither the logarithm nor quasifree purification is new general
mathematics. The constructive contribution here is the corrected TFPT data path.
As ell->infinity, n_ell tends strongly to 1_(-infinity,0)(B)+1/2*1_{0}(B).
The sign-only limit loses the energy magnitudes that the finite two-face data keep.
On that limit one must not continue using a faithful-state logarithm.

5. Quarter phase, markings and actual field action

quarter transition; they do not select alpha=1/4 from unrestricted holonomies.

-(n+1/4)=(-n-1)+3/4. This is a spectral relabeling. The matrix J_n acts linearly
between graded components of the SAME domain. An anti-linear charge conjugation
of a literal scalar twisted section instead sends H_alpha to H_(1-alpha).
These statements must not be merged into an untyped scalar identity between domains.

For a supplied unitary source transport W, B'=WBW*, G'=diag(W,W)Gdiag(W*,W*)
and n'=WnW*. A supplied marking M must transform M'=WMW*, including
[B',M']=W[B,M]W*. On the CAR algebra the associated field map is a(f)->a(Wf).
The checks transport a noncommuting marking and test the operator action, not
only equal eigenvalues. They do NOT derive the original M, the P2 split, a local
geometric W, or an implementer in every infinite-dimensional Fock representation.
In particular a function of B alone commutes with B. A noncommuting physical
marking cannot be manufactured from spectral data of B alone; local algebra,
winding or another source structure has to supply its actual operator action.
