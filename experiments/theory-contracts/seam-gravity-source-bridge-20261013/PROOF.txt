1. What v323's finite calculation establishes
------------------------------------------------

commutators with L=diag(n), rho=diag(i^n), and C vanish (up to floating point
roundoff), and K is diagonal in this basis. This proves the stated finite

It does not identify the generator K with a geometric horizon boost. The two
neighboring bilinears (-1,0) and (0,1) both have mode gap +1, but their K gaps
are -1 and +1. At modular parameter t=pi/2 their phases are -i and +i, so a
single scalar quarter-turn phase does not implement this action on these two
bilinears. Nor is K an affine function aL+b on this symmetric mode set. This
separates “commutes with the rotation generator” from “is the generator of the
same geometric rotation.” It does not exclude a doubled/chiral geometric action,
another representation, or a real local interval Bisognano–Wichmann theorem;
there is no local net or interval inclusion in the finite helper.

2. Entropy normalization is a separate choice of replica completion
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Take a fixed-metric matter functional Z[g,J] and multiply it by exp(lambda Q[g]),
where Q is independent of J. Then the normalized fixed-background functional
Z[g,J]/Z[g,0] is unchanged: the common factor cancels. Under the declared conical
convention Q[g_n]-n Q[g_1]=4 pi (1-n) A, however, the normalized replica entropy

  S = - d_n (log Z[g_n]-n log Z[g_1]) at n=1

shifts by 4 pi lambda A. Thus matter-normalized correlators do not by themselves
fix this generalized conical coefficient. This is a change to the replica
completion/counterterm, not a change in the entropy of the same microscopic
density-matrix trace. No claim is made that every such lambda is allowed by all
TFPT principles; the diagnostic identifies the additional selection theorem
that a bridge would need.

In four dimensions lambda and the Einstein-Hilbert coefficient have mass
dimension two. Setting lambda=z m^2 leaves the chosen matter mass m fixed while
G m^2 = m^2/[16 pi (k+z m^2)] varies with z. So a unit convention alone does not
remove the freedom. This is consistent with v68: its heat-kernel normalization
is K_EH=f2 Lambda^2 d/(192 pi^2), and its scale ratio depends on
f2 Lambda^2/m^2. v68 itself carries the P1 normalization through the heat-kernel
calculation; it is not a freestanding derivation of that input.
