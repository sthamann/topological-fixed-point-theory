The canonical workflow is `semantic_explorer/skills/tfpt-knowledge/SKILL.md`. In
Exploration mode the Atlas checks ideas after they are written down; in
Continuation mode start with `graph_status`, `theory_overview` and
`research_state`. Ledger and original sources are authoritative; graph paths and
AI annotations are not proof. Keep the specialized RH source catalog and skills.
