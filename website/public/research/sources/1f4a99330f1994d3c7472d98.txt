000063 | 3. The complete two-face datum retains the signed operator
000064 | 
000065 | Assume a given selfadjoint B on H, a known normal length ell>0, identified/labeled
000066 | boundary fibers, and an exact normal-independent product equation
000067 | (partial_r+B)psi=0 on the retained interval [0,ell]. Write T=exp(-ell B).
000068 | By the spectral theorem T is positive, injective, densely defined and closed,
000069 | possibly unbounded. The L2-closed endpoint Cauchy space is
000070 | L_ell={(u,Tu): u in D(T)}, where
000071 | D(T)={u: integral exp(-2ell lambda) dmu_B,u(lambda)<infinity}.
000072 | Finite spectral-cutoff solutions form a core for this closed graph. No claim is
000073 | made that every such endpoint pair has an H1 solution up to both boundary faces.

000076 | G_ell = [[A,Cross],[Cross,I-A]],
000077 | A=(I+T^2)^(-1)=(I+exp(-2ell B))^(-1),
000078 | Cross=T(I+T^2)^(-1)=(2cosh(ell B))^(-1).
