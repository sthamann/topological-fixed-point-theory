000074 | \begin{theorem}[Infrared cosmological constant from seam transfer]
000075 | \label{thm:cosmology-closure}
000076 | \[
000077 | U_\Sigma:=\Padm e^{-\ell_\Seam |B_\Seam|}\Padm,
000078 | \qquad
000079 | S_\Sigma:=-\log\rho(U_\Sigma)=\log\mu_\Sigma(\alpha_\star),
000080 | \]

000083 | \Lambda_{\mathrm{IR}}
000084 | =

000086 | \Bigl[-\log\det\nolimits_{\mathrm{adm}}(1-U_\Sigma)\Bigr]
000087 | \]
000088 | is well defined and positive on the closed branch, and
000089 | \[
000090 | 0<U_\Sigma\le e^{-S_\Sigma}\Padm<\Padm.
000091 | \]
000092 | \end{theorem}

000094 | \begin{proof}
000095 | \Cref{lem:automatic-seam-transfer-convergence} gives trace-class positivity of
000096 | $U_\Sigma$, while \Cref{prop:seam-action-cosmology} fixes the intrinsic seam damping $S_\Sigma$.

000100 | \begin{corollary}[Determinant bounds for the infrared seam transfer]
000101 | \label{cor:ir-seam-transfer-bounds}
000102 | Let $\{\lambda_j(U_\Sigma)\}$ denote the admissible eigenvalues of the positive trace-class
000103 | operator $U_\Sigma$. Then
000104 | \[
000105 | \tr_{\mathrm{adm}}U_\Sigma
000106 | \le
000107 | -\log\det\nolimits_{\mathrm{adm}}(1-U_\Sigma)
000108 | \le
000109 | \frac{-\log(1-\rho(U_\Sigma))}{\rho(U_\Sigma)}\,\tr_{\mathrm{adm}}U_\Sigma.
000110 | \]
000111 | If, moreover, the infrared seam transfer is rank one,
000112 | \[
000113 | U_\Sigma=\rho(U_\Sigma)\,P_\Sigma^{(1)},
000114 | \]
000115 | then
000116 | \[
000117 | \frac{\Lambda_{\mathrm{IR}}}{\Mplunred^4}
000118 | =
000119 | -\log(1-\rho(U_\Sigma))
000120 | =
000121 | \rho(U_\Sigma)+O\!\bigl(\rho(U_\Sigma)^2\bigr).
000122 | \]
000123 | \end{corollary}
000124 | 
000125 | \begin{proof}
000126 | Because $U_\Sigma$ is positive trace class, its eigenvalues satisfy
000127 | \[
000128 | 0<\lambda_j(U_\Sigma)\le \rho(U_\Sigma)<1.
000129 | \]
000130 | For every $x\in[0,\rho(U_\Sigma)]$ one has the scalar bounds
000131 | \[
000132 | x\le -\log(1-x)\le \frac{-\log(1-\rho(U_\Sigma))}{\rho(U_\Sigma)}\,x.
000133 | \]
000134 | Applying this to each eigenvalue and summing gives the determinant bounds. If
000135 | $U_\Sigma=\rho(U_\Sigma)P_\Sigma^{(1)}$ is rank one, then
000136 | \[
000137 | \det\nolimits_{\mathrm{adm}}(1-U_\Sigma)=1-\rho(U_\Sigma),
000138 | \]
000139 | and the Taylor expansion of $-\log(1-\rho)$ yields the displayed asymptotic formula.
